## Mechanisms

The 2026-09-08 re-audit reported an empty engine and shared seam queue
(`docs/audits/EX12-reaudit/REVIEW-NOTES.md`). The two earlier EX12 passes did change engine
and shared seams, and those records are kept here because their source documents are deleted.

### Fortitude ordering (2026-09-05)

Source: `docs/audits/EX12-20260905.md`, commit `7fa33686f`. Implementation commit
`64095d3a0`.

＜Fortitude＞ now joins the ordinary simultaneous deletion trigger pool. Event snapshots
retain keyword eligibility and former digivolution cards after deletion, and a deleted-host
identity map prevents pending own or inherited effects from activating once Fortitude has
replayed that host. Effect deletion, battle deletion, and pooled DP rule checks retain this
provenance; Scapegoat's combat sacrifice path supplies the same Fortitude snapshot. The
obsolete combat replay callback was removed. Rule source: local
`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX12-065` and official Q6866 — the controller chooses among
simultaneously triggered effects, so replaying Kaguyamon first makes its other pending
deletion effects ineligible.

Tests that pin it: `src/engine/effects/fortitudeOrdering.test.ts` (red before the change,
green after); affected deletion mechanisms 13 files / 307 tests covering
`fortitudeOrdering`, `primitives`, `kernel`, `resolution`, `builders`, `ruleCheckPool`,
`deletionDpCluster`, `advancedKeywords`, `controller`, `battleDeletionSnapshot`,
`retaliationOverflow`, `ch15-02-timing-and-resolution`, and
`ch16c-deletion-and-advanced-keywords`. The seam is deliberately internal: ordinary deletion
effects and Fortitude share one resolver pool, and each removal path carries immutable event
eligibility. Future deletion routes must preserve that snapshot.

### Decode host scope (2026-09-01)

Source: `docs/audits/EX12-AUDIT.md`, commit `7fa33686f`.

`applyDecodeHostScope` in `apps/api/src/engine/interpreter/actions/play.ts` scopes every
`playedByDecode` play from digivolution cards to the resolving permanent unless the IR
already scopes the host. Before the fix the replacement's `PlayWithoutCost` had no
`hostFilter`, so `targeting/loose.ts` pooled every stack the controller owned (CR 16-36-1).
Affected EX12 cards: 014, 016, 017, 028, 031, 032, 035, 036, 044, each of which gained a
neighbor-stack negative test. BT19-024, BT19-027, BT22-015, EX11-018, and P-214 relied on the
unscoped default; BT19-024 carries the regression test.

### Granted triggers on unaffected permanents (2026-09-01)

Source: `docs/audits/EX12-AUDIT.md`. Commit `0a716a54a`, seam
`apps/api/src/engine/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts`. Q6740: a granted trigger may be
given to a permanent that is already unaffected by effects; the grant-time resolution dropped
it. Proven by EX12-016.

### Bracketed rule-name aliases (2026-09-01)

Source: `docs/audits/EX12-AUDIT.md`. Commit `8c057337d`, seam
`packages/shared/src/cards/effectiveNames.ts`. The catalog prints `[Rule] Name:` as well as
`(Rule) Name:`; only the latter was parsed. Thirteen cards, EX12-041 among them, gain their
alias in every zone (KB Q759).

### Guard keyword (2026-09-01)

### Nezhamon redirection scope (2026-09-05)
