# Borrowed-effect source ownership

Discord bug 1552448454788124932 (2026-09-23, match `e9fba7b7-b215-4ee8-8bec-e1e419df35cb`): the attacker's BT25-060 Rebootmon had gained "your opponent's Digimon effects don't affect it" from its link reaction. BT25-052 Logimon's link reaction then suspended EX13-023 UlforceVeedramon, BT11-112 Rina Shinomiya paid her suspend cost and activated Ulforce's When Digivolving return, and the production log recorded the return under Rina (`sourceCardId: BT11-112`, no EX13-023 trigger event). Rebootmon went to the deck bottom although the effect was a Digimon's.

Root cause in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/borrowed.ts`: `runActivateForeignEffect` ran every borrowed effect with the activating card as `ctx.source` unless the card opted in with `useLenderAsSource`. BT11-112 had not opted in, so the Tamer was the source and `ctx.effectSourceKinds` stayed `["Tamer"]` (set by the SubTrigger dispatcher). `candidatePermanents` in `targeting/permanents.ts` qualifies the `beAffected` immunity by those kinds, so the Digimon-only immunity never matched. The opt-in branch also kept the activator's `effectSourceKinds`, so even opted-in cards leaked the wrong kinds.

Rules 15-15-7-1 transfers ownership only when the printed text says "as an effect of this Digimon". The interpreter now keeps a battle-area lender as the effect's source, with the lender's kinds, whenever the action carries no `asEffectOf` clause and the lender is not the activating permanent. Security and digivolution-card lenders, and cards with `asEffectOf`, keep running as the activating card's effect. `ActivateForeignEffectAction.asEffectOf` was added to the shared IR so the legacy `ActivateEffect` clause reaches the resolver.

Evidence: `apps/api/src/cards/BT11/BT11-112.test.ts` "delegated [When Digivolving] stays the Veedramon's own Digimon effect" links BT25-072 to Rebootmon, suspends Ulforce by a seat-0 effect, lets Rina choose the return, and asserts Rebootmon stays while a non-immune BT1-013 goes to the deck bottom. It failed before the change (empty battle area) and passes after it. Focused files for BT24-102, BT11-029, EX3-065, BT10-110, BT11-097, EX13-023, BT25-060, BT23-060, EX8-054, P-147, EX5-059, `interpreter.test.ts`, and `securityActivateCluster.test.ts` all pass.

Follow-up: EX3-065 Hina Kurihara was authored with `asEffectOf: "this Tamer"` although its printed text ("activate 1 of that Digimon's [On Play] effects") has no such clause. The clause was removed; `EX3-065.test.ts` "delegated [On Play] stays the Dragon's own Digimon effect" proves an opponent Digimon immune to opponent Digimon effects survives Volcanicdramon's delegated deletion. BT24-102 Homeros ("activate 1 [On Play] or [When Digivolving] effect of 1 of your [Olympos XII] trait Digimon") also has no ownership clause, so the lender-as-source path is the correct reading there and its 15 tests pass unchanged.
