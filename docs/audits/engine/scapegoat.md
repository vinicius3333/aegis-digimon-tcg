prevention contract. The conformance cases use the granted EX8-071 provider,
the resulting EX8-057 holder, and the native EX11-023 provider. They assert

and opponent-effect cases with the exact ally sacrifice and preserved holder;

inherited, or alternate numeric Scapegoat form is present in the reviewed
providers; those are recorded as N/A rather than inferred from catalog counts.
