## Name matching

The printed route says "w/[Belphemon] **in name**", so substring matching is the correct
semantics — `names: ["Belphemon"]` in the module is right, not `namesExact`. The positive
proof is BT13-088 "Belphemon: Sleep Mode", which is legal even though its name is not the
bare token. The `excludeTraits: ["X Antibody"]` half is proved by rejecting another
BT23-070 as the source.
