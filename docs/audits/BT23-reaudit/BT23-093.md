## Card summary

Blue Option, play cost 2, type [Appmon], rarity C. Printed clauses:

- **C1** While you have a Digimon or Tamer with the [Appmon] trait on the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements.
- **C2** [Main] ＜Draw 1＞. Then, place this card in the battle area.
- **C3** [All Turns] When any of your [Appmon] trait Digimon suspend, ＜Delay＞ ・You may link 1 [Appmon] trait Digimon card from your hand to 1 of those Digimon without paying the cost.
- **C4** [Security] Place this card in the battle area.

## Behavioral notes

- **Delay timing.** The once-per-card timing rule ("can't activate the turn this card enters play") is proven on the real turn loop, not by injection: the Option is publicly played during turn 1 Main, the same-turn attack suspension leaves it unpaid and the link card in hand, then after a full opponent turn the same attacker's suspension pays the Delay, trashes the Option and links the exact card instance.
- **No-cost link.** `linkRequirement` on BT21-009 is asserted as "Cost 1", and memory is asserted unchanged at 3 across the link, so `payCost: false` is proven rather than assumed.
- **Recipient identity.** With two suspendable Appmon on board, only the attacker receives the link; the bystander stays unlinked and unsuspended. This proves `isTriggerSource` rather than "any own Appmon".
- **Fixture vocabulary.** [Appmon] is a *form* on BT21-009 / BT22-016, and the engine's trait set is forms ∪ attributes ∪ types, so `match: "trait"` (exact) matches. BT21-009 carries ＜Link＞, BT22-016 does not — that pair is the Q5367 control. BT21-084 Haru Shinkai supplies the Tamer half of Q5366. Security fixtures use main-deck BT1-011.
