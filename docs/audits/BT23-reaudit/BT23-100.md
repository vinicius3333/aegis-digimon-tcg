# BT23-100 Hudie Net Café — re-audit

## Card

White Option, play cost 3, traits [Hudie]/[CS]. The catalog stores `nameEn: "Hudie Net CafxE9"`; see the discrepancy note below.

Printed clauses:

1. Static: "While you have a Digimon or Tamer with the [CS] trait on the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements."
2. `[Main] ＜Draw 1＞ Then, place this card in the battle area.`
3. `[Main] ＜Delay＞ ・You may play 1 Tamer card with the [CS] trait from your hand without paying the cost.`
4. `[Security] You may play 1 level 3 Digimon card with the [CS] trait from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area.`

## Rulings covered

- Q5388 (2025-10-03): "on the field" means the battle area **or** the breeding area. The IR condition uses `zone: ["battleArea", "breeding"]`, and two behavioural tests pin both halves (battle-area source, breeding-only source).
- CR 2-3-2-3: "with the [CS] trait" is an exact trait match, not `traitContains`. The IR uses `match: "trait"` on every clause; the negative tests use a non-[CS] Digimon and a non-[CS] Tamer.
- CR 16-17-3 (＜Delay＞): cannot activate on the turn it is placed; activating trashes the card as the cost.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **`// @ts-nocheck` removed from `BT23-100.ts`.** The module typechecks without it, so real IR type errors (e.g. bad `duration` strings) now surface for this card. No IR content changed, so the card's role as a public colour-waiver fixture for BT23-055 and BT23-059 is untouched.
2. **Wrong memory baseline in the inherited ＜Delay＞ test.** It compared memory across a turn change, so it failed on the turn-start memory reset (expected 5, got 3). Split into two assertions: memory pinned at `8 - 3` for the same-turn refusal branch, and a fresh baseline captured on the next own turn for the free play.
3. **Missing optional-refusal branch for the ＜Delay＞.** Added a decline test proving the Tamer stays in hand while the option is still trashed as the activation cost.

No IR defect was found. The waiver condition already carries `zone`, the `kind` list, and exact trait matching.

## Catalog discrepancy (coordinator action)

`cards.json` stores the card name as `"Hudie Net CafxE9"` — a mojibake of `Café` (`x` + `E9` instead of `é`). The official name is "Hudie Net Café". This is a catalog data issue, not a rules issue, and catalog edits are coordinator-owned, so the test asserts the current stored value. Likely affects other cards with accented names.
