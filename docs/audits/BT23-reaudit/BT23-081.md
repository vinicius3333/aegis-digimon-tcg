## Card

Yellow Tamer, play cost 4, traits Hudie/CS, SR. Catalog text matches the official list character for character.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Start of Your Main Phase] If you have a Digimon with the [CS] trait, gain 1 memory.`
2. `[On Play] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower Digimon card with the [Hudie] trait from your hand without paying the cost.`
3. `[All Turns] When any of your [Hudie] trait Digimon suspend, by suspending this Tamer, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP for the turn.`

## Suspected defect (probe first)

`youHave` in clause 1 carries no `zone`. `countMatching` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:95-101`) scans the battle area **and** the breeding area when `zone` is absent, so a CS Digimon sitting in breeding would wrongly satisfy the condition. Comprehensive §3-4-5-8 says breeding-area cards are not referenced unless the effect names the breeding area. Expected fix: add `zone: "battleArea"`. Prove red before, green after.

## Defects found and fixed

**1. Start-of-Main condition counted the breeding area (fixed in `BT23-081.ts`).**
The `youHave` filter carried no `zone`. `countMatching` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:95-101`) falls through to a scan of the battle area *and* the breeding permanent when `zone` is absent, so a level-3 `[CS]` Digimon parked in breeding granted the memory. Comprehensive rules 3-4-5-8 say breeding-area cards are not referenced unless the effect names the breeding area. Fix: `zone: "battleArea"` on the condition filter.

Red-then-green proof, test `ignores a CS Digimon that is only in the breeding area`:
- Without the fix: `pnpm --filter @aegis/api exec vitest run src/cards/BT23/BT23-081.test.ts --maxWorkers=1 --no-file-parallelism -t 'breeding'` — 1 failed (breeding fixture reached Main with 1 memory, the empty control with 0).
- With the fix: same command — 1 passed; both fixtures reach Main with the same memory.

**2. Removed `// @ts-nocheck` from the module.** It typechecks clean without it, so `toDuration`-style silent duration mistakes now surface. `duration: "forTheTurn"` is a real `EffectDurationRef` member (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/durations.ts:4`), so clause 3's duration is correct.

## Behavioral evidence

All twelve tests drive public intents (`playCard`, `attack`, `endPhase`, `surrender`) or the production turn loop. No test uses `advance().fireTiming` or `advance().fireSubTrigger`; the previous suite drove clauses 1 and 3 entirely through injected timing, which earned no behavioral credit.

- **Clause 1.** Two turn-loop fixtures reach seat 0's Main through `startTurnLoop()`; the CS fixture leaves exactly one more memory than the identical non-CS fixture. A third pair proves the breeding-only case matches the empty board. The helper closes the Breeding phase with a public `endPhase` when a seeded breeding permanent holds it open.
- **Clause 2.** Chitose is played publicly from hand: memory falls 6 -> 2 (her printed 4), the cost-3 Hudie (BT23-037 Tentomon) reaches the battle area, and the cost-8 Hudie (BT23-032) plus the non-Hudie (BT1-028) stay in hand by exact instance id. The `playCostLte: 5` and trait bounds are therefore both exercised in one fixture. A decline branch under `autoDeclineOptional` leaves the eligible Hudie in hand and the battle area at length 1, with memory still 2.
- **Clause 3.** A public attack by an own Hudie (BT23-037) onto a suspended BT1-024 suspends Chitose and drops the target from 10000 to 7000. Three negatives: the decline branch leaves Chitose unsuspended at 10000; a non-Hudie attacker (BT1-028) fires nothing; an already-suspended Chitose cannot pay, so the target stays at 10000. A turn-loop test proves the reduction expires at the real turn boundary — 7000 during seat 0's Main, back to 10000 after a public `endPhase` reaches seat 1's Main, with the same physical permanent id.
- **Clause 4.** A public attack into a security stack topped by Chitose puts her exact security instance into the defender's battle area, not the trash, and removes it from security.
- **IR trace.** A final structural test pins all four compiled clauses, including the new `zone: "battleArea"`, the `playCostLte: 5` bound, the `whenSuspended` source filter, and the self-suspend cost with `abortOnDecline`.

Every endpoint assertion also checks `state.pendingDecision === undefined` and, where a battle ran, `observe(engine).isAttacking() === false`.
