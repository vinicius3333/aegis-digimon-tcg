## Evidence highlights

- Security clause, both battle outcomes. Attacker 3000 DP: Consulmon survives, the attacker's card lands in trash, Consulmon leaves security (0 left), is not in trash, and stands alone on the defender's battle area. Attacker 7000 DP: the `securityChecked` event reports `securityDigimonDeleted: true` and Consulmon is still played onto the board from trash. Memory is unchanged in both cases, and the `cardsMoved` event for Consulmon comes after `securityChecked`, so the play really happens at the end of the battle.
- The security play chains into `[On Play]`: the attacker's untouched second Digimon ends up restricted from attacking players, which also proves the controller framing (the security player's "opponent" is the attacking player).
- Public play costs 4 memory (6 -> 2), hand empties, exactly one of two opposing Digimon is restricted, and the controller's own Digimon is not.
- Restriction meaning and boundary, driven through the real turn loop: on the opponent's turn the restricted Digimon's player attack is rejected (`ok:false`, no security lost, attacker not suspended) while its attack on a suspended Digimon is accepted and deletes that Digimon. After the opponent's turn ends the restriction is gone. This is the printed "until their turn ends" boundary — the engine re-frames `untilOpponentTurnEnd` per target seat in `primitives.ts durationForTarget`.
- Evolution: Black Level 3 (BT2-052) source costs 2 memory, draws the one deck card, keeps the base card in the stack under Consulmon, and applies the restriction. Red Level 3 and Black Level 4 sources are both rejected with no memory or hand change.
- Link: cost 2 (memory 5 -> 3), host DP +3000, host gains both Reboot and Blocker.

## Defects

- No card-module defect. `BT23-052.ts` is unchanged: the restriction target, restriction kind, duration and link IR all match the printed text. The zone-less `kind:["Digimon"]` filter is safe here because permanent targeting defaults to the battle area (`interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts`, `allowsZone`); the breeding test proves it behaviorally.
- Catalog discrepancy (coordinator-owned, not edited): `cards.json` `linkEffect` for BT23-052 reads `"[When Linking] [When Linking] This Digimon gains ..."` — the `[When Linking]` tag is duplicated. `effectText` also carries a double space between the `[Security]` clause and the `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` clause. The test therefore asserts `linkRequirement` but not `linkEffect`.

## Test-fixture note

Under the real turn loop, seat 0's main phase closes on its own once the seat has nothing left to do. The turn-boundary test keeps a spare hand card so the phase stays open after Consulmon is played; without it the engine passes the turn and the following `endPhase` intent is rejected as `not-your-turn`.
