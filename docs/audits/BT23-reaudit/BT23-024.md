## Contract and implementation

Sources: committed `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` BT23-024, local KB Q5246–Q5252 and Q6025–Q6026, and direct `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-024.ts`. Blue/white level 6, play 12, 12000 DP, ordinary blue level-5 evolution for 4, App Fusion Oujamon + Beautymon for 0. Evade and Link +1 apply. When Digivolving/When Attacking optionally links one eligible Appmon from own hand or this Digimon's evolution cards without paying its Link cost. All Turns, once per turn, becoming linked permits paying an unsuspend cost to prevent opposing Digimon from suspending through the opponent's turn, except all Digimon tied for highest play cost.

Production behavior remains exclusively `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. The existing IR already represents the printed contract. The audit corrected the shared `GameEngine.highestPlayCostExemptions` helper: negative play-cost sentinels mean no printed play cost and must be excluded from the maximum calculation. Undefined costs remain excluded and valid zero costs remain eligible. The all-token test failed before this correction and passes after it; the failure was retained until fixed.

## Reproducible behavioral evidence

The colocated suite has 18 passing tests, including three structural checks and these behavioral proofs:

- Public App Fusion consumes the exact physical Oujamon and Beautymon materials, pays zero, draws the evolution bonus, preserves the expected stack and allows entry Link refusal. Shared App Fusion mechanism regressions separately cover modifiers, legality and provenance.
- Public normal evolution from a realistic Musclemon / Coachmon / Oujamon stack pays 4 and draws once. Poseidomon links the own Musclemon source; another friendly stack remains unchanged. The source begins as an evolution card, so the assertion proves the actual source-to-Link transition.
- Public attack links the only eligible Appmon when invalid Poseidomon and non-Appmon hand candidates precede it. Invalid own cards and an opponent's physical control card remain in hand (Q5246).
- Public Link pays the suspended host's unsuspend cost and arms the restriction. An already unsuspended host cannot pay that cost and does not arm it.
- Mixed and all-token boards prove no-play-cost Amon/Umon are restricted. Ordinary highest-cost and tied-highest opponents are exempt, with lower-cost opponents restricted.
- A production turn loop proves actual lower-cost attack rejection and highest-cost attack success. The restriction expires through public turn changes, after which the formerly restricted attacker removes security.
- Public higher-cost play changes the highest-cost exemption. Public Calling From Darkness removes that entrant, recomputation exempts the prior highest again, and its public attack removes security. A separate supplemental direct-state fixture is not the duration proof.
- Public links preserve two links with Link +1; a third link presents a live trim choice. Selecting the first physical link trashes it, retains the second and third, and produces 17000 DP.
- Three public attacks across a real turn cycle prove linked unsuspend once-per-turn consumption and reset. Each newly linked physical material is asserted; the second activation leaves the host suspended, while the next turn's activation unsuspends it.
- Actual Raid combat from EX6-011 tests Evade acceptance and refusal. Acceptance suspends and preserves the exact Poseidomon; refusal moves it to trash after combat resolves.

The suspension mechanism is traced through `applySuspendRestrictionRecompute`, public attack legality and the ordinary suspension primitive. Link costs and watchers resolve through the effect interpreter. The App Fusion backend lifecycle has its own committed report, `APP-FUSION-MECHANISM.md`.
