## Defects found

None in the module. The IR already models every printed clause, the `[Ghost]` condition is a
trait match (not a substring name match), the opponent Delete is mandatory, and the `EndAttack`
is gated on the actual removal count rather than on target selection.

The module has no `// @ts-nocheck` and typechecks; the `duration: "permanent"` on the Execute
grant is a valid `EffectDurationRef`.

## Remaining gaps

- Q5337 is proved by the absence of a skip prompt plus the IR shape. A stronger proof would need
  a decision seam that lets a test refuse a mandatory action, which the engine correctly does not
  offer.
- Two pre-existing tests drive the clause through a hand-built `EffectContext` rather than the
  engine. They are kept for the interpreter-level gate proof but earn no behavioral credit; the
  behavioral equivalents added in this audit cover the same two branches.
