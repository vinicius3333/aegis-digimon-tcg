## Defects

### Fixed (local, in `BT23-057.ts`)

`playCostCeiling.filter` had no `zone`, so `countMatching` (apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:94-101) also counted the controller's breeding-area permanent. A Digimon in breeding raised the deletion ceiling from 6 to 9. Comprehensive rules 3-4-5-8 say cards in the breeding area can't be referenced.

Proof: `does not count a breeding-area Digimon toward the deletion ceiling` failed red before the fix (a cost-7 opponent Digimon was deleted) and passes with `zone: "battleArea"` added to both trigger bodies.

### Interpretation recorded, not changed

"Then, delete 1 ..." resolves even when the player declines the optional token. No KB entry gates the second sentence on the first, and comprehensive rules 15-1-2 only fix the processing order. `pays the full cost when the return cost is declined` and `deletes only up to the base play-cost ceiling of 6 when the token is declined` both assert this branch.
