## Evidence notes

C1 uses four public `digivolve` intents. `BT9-063` (black) and `BT23-044` (green) cannot use either printed ordinary evolution colour, so their cost-3 success proves the two alternate routes rather than the yellow/red `evoCosts`. Every case asserts the source instance id in the stack, memory reaching exactly 0 from the exact cost, the drawn instance id in hand, and no pending decision. The level 4 negative asserts the refusal plus untouched memory, board and hand.

C2 is proved at the printed boundary: a 10000-DP `BT1-024` reduces the cost to 7 (memory 10 to 3); an 8000-DP `BT23-033` leaves the full 12 (memory 12 to 0).

C3 is driven only through public `playCard` (On Play) and public `digivolve` (When Digivolving). Both destination halves of Q5297 are exercised: a `[Leomon]`-name destination (`BT23-044` into `BT4-061`) and a `[CS]`-trait destination (`BT23-031` into `BT23-034`). The level cap is proved with a paired control: `BT23-046` Rosemon (green level 6 `[CS]`) satisfies `BT23-047` Examon's printed green level 6 evolution requirement, so the effect's refusal to offer Examon can only come from the level 6 cap — the companion test digivolves the same pair publicly for cost 5 and succeeds. A second negative uses `ST3-10` Magnadramon: a level 6 yellow card whose requirement the recipient meets but which is neither `[Leomon]`-named nor `[CS]`. `excludeSelf` is proved by playing BanchoLeomon with no other Digimon on the board. The decline branch keeps the destination in hand and the recipient's stack empty.

C4 runs on the production turn loop (`startTurnLoop` plus public `endPhase`). The `＜Raid＞` grant has `forTheTurn` duration and the turn ends as soon as the effect finishes, so the grant is only observable while the "may attack" prompt is still pending. Both branch tests therefore leave that prompt unanswered, assert `hasKeyword(bancho, "Raid")` and the unsuspended attacker live, then answer it with a public `respondDecision`. Q5298's answer is the decline branch: the Digimon stays unsuspended, the opponent keeps all 3 security cards, no `securityChecked` event is emitted, and nothing is attacking. The accept branch consumes one security card, suspends the same Digimon and emits `securityChecked`. Frequency is proved by counting `effectTriggered` events for `BT23-036/ir-3-0` across three real turns: 1 after the first own turn, still 1 after the opponent's turn, 2 after the second own turn.

## Remaining gaps

- The `＜Raid＞` grant cannot be asserted after the end-of-turn window closes, because its `forTheTurn` duration ends with the turn. The tests assert it live at the pending decision instead, which is the strongest available observation; there is no engine defect here.
- The `[Once Per Turn]` marker cannot produce a same-turn refusal for an `[End of Your Turn]` trigger, since the window opens once per turn. The proof is the per-turn trigger count across three real turns.
- The C3 level cap negative depends on `BT23-047` Examon having no compiled alternate requirement; its printed `[Digivolve] Lv.6 w/[CS] trait: Cost 5` is absent from `effects.json`. That is a catalog gap on **BT23-047**, not on this card, and it does not weaken the negative (the ordinary green level 6 route is used instead). The coordinator may want to route it to the Examon lane.
