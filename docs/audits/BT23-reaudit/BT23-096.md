# BT23-096 Comet Hammer — re-audit

## Card

Black Option, play cost 5, trait [CS], rarity C.

Printed clauses (catalog `effectText` / `securityEffectText`):

1. **Static color waiver** — "While you have a Digimon or Tamer with the [CS] trait on the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements."
2. **[Main]** — "＜De-Digivolve 4＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, place this card in the battle area."
3. **[Your Turn] ＜Delay＞** — "When one of your [CS] trait Digimon attacks, ＜Delay＞ ・＜De-Digivolve 4＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon."
4. **[Security]** — "＜De-Digivolve 4＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, place this card in the battle area."

## Rules used

- Q5384 (the card's only KB Q&A): "on the field" = battle area **or** breeding area.
- CR §16-12 ＜De-Digivolve＞: trash cards from the top of the chosen stack; mandatory once activated (§16-12-3); **can't trash from level 3 or lower** (§16-12-4).
- CR §16-17 ＜Delay＞: while the card is in the battle area, trashing it activates the effect; optional (§16-17-2); **can't be activated the turn the card entered the battle area** (§16-17-3).
- CR §2-3-2-3: "with the [CS] trait" is exact trait matching (`match: "trait"`), not `traitContains`.

## Findings

- **No IR defect found.** The compiled IR already matches the printed text: `match: "trait"` (exact, §2-3-2-3), `zone: ["battleArea", "breeding"]` (Q5384), `kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]`, `controller: "opponent"` on every De-Digivolve target, and ＜Delay＞ on the `[Your Turn]` window so `withIntrinsicDelayGate` installs the §16-17 trash cost and the §16-17-3 entry-turn gate. The only module change is removing `// @ts-nocheck`.
- **Prior test coverage was thin.** Four of the six existing tests were structural (`compiled.effects` shape) or leaned on hand-set `placedByEffect`. The [Main] and [Security] clauses had no behavioral proof at all, and the §16-17-3 entry-turn restriction — the only rule that governs when this card's Delay may be used — was untested.

### Engine behaviour relied on

- `subTrigger.ts` ~1105: `delaySource.enterFieldTurnCount === subCtx.game.state.turnCount` implements §16-17-3.
- `primitives.ts` `deDigivolve` (~1795): peels from the top, `levelFloor = stopAtLevel ?? 3` implements §16-12-4.

### Remaining gaps

- **Declared number not modelled (§16-12-1/§16-12-3).** The rules let the player declare any number from 1 to 4; `runAction` always passes the printed maximum to `fx.deDigivolve`. No prompt is opened. This is an engine-wide De-Digivolve simplification, not a BT23-096 defect, so it is reported, not patched here. It only matters when a player wants to stop a peel early (for example to leave a Digimon at level 4).

- **Fixed (local):** removed `// @ts-nocheck` from `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-096.ts`. The module typechecks without it, and the suppression would have hidden a bad `duration:` string or a mistyped action if one were ever introduced (the BT23-047 failure mode).
