# BT23-040 Wormmon re-audit

Sources: committed catalog `BT23-040`; KB Q5302; comprehensive rules 4-6-8 and 8-1-2-1.

## Card summary

Green Rookie Digimon, level 3, play cost 4, DP 3000, traits Larva/Hudie/CS. Printed evolution cost is green level 2 for 1.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[CS] trait: Cost 0` — alternate requirement.
2. `[Start of Your Main Phase] By placing 1 of your [Erika Mishima] as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, this Digimon may digivolve into [Hudiemon] in the hand or trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.`
3. Inherited: `[All Turns] All of your Digimon with the [Hudie] trait get +1000 DP.`

Q&A covered: Q5302 (the Erika placement cost takes her only from the battle area, never from hand or trash).

## Defects found and fixed in the card module

All four are local to `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-040.ts`. No engine or catalog file was touched.

| Defect | Was | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Substring name matching | `match: "name"` for `[Erika Mishima]` and `[Hudiemon]` | `match: "nameExact"` | A printed bracketed card name is an exact reference. Substring mode would also accept any longer name containing it. |
| Erika's own stack rode along | no `shedOwnCards` | `shedOwnCards: true` on the place cost | Erika leaves the battle area when she becomes a digivolution card, so cards under her are trashed (CR 4-6-8). The old IR attached them to the new stack instead. Same shape as BT12-027, BT12-083, BT12-102. |
| Double refusal | `cost.optional: true` plus `optional: true` | `optional: true` only | With both flags the engine asks twice: pay Erika, then decline the digivolution — the controller could throw Erika away for nothing. The printed clause has one "may". |
| Forced alternate requirement | `useAlternateCost: true` | field removed | Hudiemon has both a printed green Lv.3 requirement (cost 5) and an alternate CS Lv.3 requirement (cost 4). CR 8-1-2-1 gives the choice to the player; the interpreter already opens that `chooseOption` when the field is absent. Forcing the alternate silently charged 2 where the controller may want the printed route. |

## How the behavioral cases are driven

Every dynamic case uses public intents and the production turn loop. Wormmon's alternate requirement names a level-2 CS source, and every level-2 card is a Digi-Egg, so the only legal stack starts in breeding: the test publicly digivolves Wormmon over BT23-002 Yokomon in the breeding area for 0, then moves it out with `moveFromBreeding`, then runs `runOneTurn()` and waits for the real Main phase so `[Start of Your Main Phase]` fires naturally. The earlier committed test placed BT23-002 in the battle area, which is not a legal Digi-Egg zone (the turn loop trashes it), and reached the timing with `advance().fire(...)`.

Endpoints asserted in the main positive case: stack instance order `[Erika, Yokomon, Wormmon]` with Hudiemon's exact hand instance on top; Erika's permanent gone from the battle area; the exact card that was under her in trash; memory 3 (5 at Main, +1 from Erika's own start-of-main gain, then 5 - 2 = 3 paid for the printed requirement); hand exactly the draw-phase card plus the single evolution bonus draw; `assertNoLoudGap`.

Memory is read while Main is still open, because ending Main hands memory to the opponent. Erika's own `[Start of Your Main Phase]` memory gain resolves first under the harness's default trigger ordering; each memory assertion states that +1 explicitly.

## Remaining gaps

- No printed card today has a name that contains "Erika Mishima" or "Hudiemon" as a strict substring, so the exact-name regression is asserted at the `matchNameOrTrait` seam rather than through a board fixture. A behavioral fixture becomes possible only when such a card is printed.
- The card placed under Erika in the shed test is laid by the Board Spec. Erika herself prints no effect that stacks cards under her; the engine has a dedicated place-under-Tamer primitive that other cards use, and the assertion under test is the destination of those cards, not how they got there.
- `effects.json` sync for this card is not done here (forbidden for this lane).
