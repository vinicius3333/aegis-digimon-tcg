# BT23-076 Sistermon Blanc — re-audit

## Card

- Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` → `BT23-076`.
- White, Digimon, Lv.3 Rookie, play cost 3, DP 3000, Vaccine, traits Puppet / CS, no printed digivolution costs.
- Printed text:
  1. `[On Play] Add your top security card to the hand. Then, ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞`
  2. `[Your Turn] When this Digimon suspends, 1 of your other Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Huckmon] in its name or the [Royal Knight] or [CS] trait in the hand or trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 1.`
- No inherited effect.

## Rulings

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT23-076` → no knowledge-base entries. `docs/audits/BT23-reaudit/KB-INDEX.md` line 82 also lists 0 Q&A ids. So no Q&A coverage obligation; mechanics were checked against the comprehensive rules instead:

- `[comprehensive §16-6]` ＜Recovery＞: place the stated number of cards from the stated area face down on top of the security stack.
- `[glossary] ＜Recovery +x (Deck)＞`: the cards come from the top of the deck.
- `[comprehensive §3-4-5-3]` breeding-area cards can't be affected by effects that do not name the breeding area, so "1 of your other Digimon" means battle area only.

## Findings

No defect found in `BT23-076.ts`. Every printed clause resolves correctly.

Two things checked and cleared rather than fixed:

- **Breeding area.** The `Digivolve` target filter carries no `zone`, but `resolvePermanentTargets` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:110-124`) defaults a zone-less filter to the battle area, so the breeding Digimon is never offered. Proven behaviorally, not just by reading the code. The `zone`-less pitfall listed in the worker brief applies to `youHave` count filters, not to permanent targeting.
- **`[Huckmon]` in its name.** Printed as "with [Huckmon] in its name", which is substring wording, so `match: "name"` with substring semantics is right; BaoHuckmon is proven to qualify.

`// @ts-nocheck` removed from `BT23-076.ts`. The module typechecks without it.

### Non-defect worth recording

A card that satisfies both its printed EvoCost and its own alternate `[Digivolve]` requirement makes the effect-driven digivolution ask a `chooseOption` ("Printed digivolution requirement (cost N)" / "Alternate digivolution requirement (cost M)") before it resolves — `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolve.ts:440-452`. A test that does not answer `chooseOption` silently sees no digivolution at all. This cost me a diagnosis cycle on Meramon (BT22-010); it is correct behavior, not a bug, and Blanc's reduction is applied to whichever route the controller picks (both branches asserted).
