# BT23-090 Keisuke Amasawa re-audit

Sources: committed catalog `BT23-090`; KB Q5363; comprehensive rules 3-4-5-3 / 3-4-5-8 (breeding area).

## Card summary

White Tamer, play cost 4, traits [Hudie] / [CS]. Printed clauses:

1. `[Start of Your Turn] If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3.`
2. `[End of Your Turn] By suspending this Tamer and returning 1 of your Digimon with the [Hudie] trait to the hand, you may play 1 Tamer card with the [CS] trait from your hand without paying the cost.`
3. `[All Turns] All of your [Hudie] Digimon get +1000 DP.`
4. `[Security] Play this card without paying the cost.`

Q&A covered: **Q5363** — the `[End of Your Turn]` "by" cost is all-or-nothing. You cannot suspend this Tamer and skip the [Hudie] return; if either part cannot be paid, nothing happens.

## Behavioral endpoints asserted

- The `[End of Your Turn]` window is reached through the real turn loop (`startTurnLoop()` + public `endPhase`), never an injected timing call. The prior test file drove `fireTiming(OnEndTurn)` directly; that was replaced.
- Free play proved by memory, not by `ok: true`: an `onEvent` observer snapshots `state.memory` at the instant the Tamer's `cardsMoved` lands in the battle area. It reads `-3` (the turn-pass rule reframes the gauge to the incoming turn player's sign before the End of Your Turn window opens), i.e. seat 0's 3 memory untouched. Paying BT23-080's printed cost of 4 would read `-7`.
- Exact instance ids asserted in the destination zone for the returned [Hudie] (hand, and absent from battle area) and the played [CS] Tamer (battle area, absent from hand and trash), plus `pendingDecision === undefined` after the window settles.
- Refusal branches assert an intermediate state, never a bare await: the Tamer stays unsuspended, the [Hudie] stays on board, the Tamer card stays in hand.
- DP aura: friendly [Hudie] 7000 -> 8000; friendly non-[Hudie] [CS] Digimon (BT23-006) unchanged at 1000; opponent [Hudie] (BT23-101) unchanged at 7000; a [Hudie] arriving mid-test (BT23-020) picks the buff up at the next recompute (5000 -> 6000); the buff still reads 8000 during the opponent's Main, proving `[All Turns]` rather than `[Your Turn]`.
- Security: attacked into a security stack holding BT23-090; the card leaves security, is not in trash, sits in the opponent's battle area, and memory is unchanged.

## Defects found and fixed

- **`// @ts-nocheck` removed from `BT23-090.ts`.** The module typechecks without it, so the suppression only hid future errors (the brief's `EffectDurationRef` pitfall). `pnpm --filter @aegis/api typecheck` reports no error in this file; the only errors in the package are pre-existing and belong to other lanes (`src/engine/effects/immunityAffectation.test.ts:29`).
- **Test file rewritten.** The previous suite proved the End of Your Turn clause only through `engine.fireTiming(EffectTiming.OnEndTurn)` (an injected timing, structural credit only) and had no Security test, no controller boundary, no already-suspended boundary, and no CS-trait negative.

No IR change was needed. Two checks that looked like defects are not:

- The `ModifyDP` and `return` filters carry no `zone`. Permanent targeting defaults to the battle area (`interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts` `allowsZone`, and `matching/permanent.ts`), so breeding-area Digimon are already excluded per CR 3-4-5-3. The `zone: "battleArea"` pitfall applies to `youHave`-style conditions, which this card has none of.
- `duration: "permanent"` is a valid `EffectDurationRef` member, not a silent fallback.

## Remaining gaps

- No test removes Keisuke from the board to prove the +1000 DP aura lapses; no public intent deletes a Tamer on your own turn without adding another card's effect to the fixture.
- The [Start of Your Turn] clause is proven with `state.memory` armed before the loop starts (the same lever `turnEndHarness.test.ts` uses). The turn loop itself does not offer a public way to enter your turn holding a chosen memory value.
- Q5363 is proven from the "cannot pay part of the cost" side (no [Hudie] to return, opponent-only [Hudie], already-suspended Tamer). There is no engine surface that would let a player suspend and then decline the return, so the "partial payment" case cannot be requested at all — that is the correct shape, not an untested branch.
