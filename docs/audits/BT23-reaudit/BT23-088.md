## Rulings

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT23-088` returns **no knowledge-base entries**, matching
`KB-INDEX.md` (`| BT23-088 | 0 | None |`). No Q&A id to cover. The clauses are governed by
comprehensive rules instead:

- §3-4-5-3 — cards in the breeding area cannot be affected by effects that do not name the
  breeding area. Clause 2 says "1 of your Digimon", so the breeding resident is out of reach.
- "Without paying the cost" waives the memory cost only; the digivolution requirement still
  applies (`engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolve.ts::legalIntoCandidates`, and the
  matching comment there).

## Defects found

1. **Fixed (local): the module carried `// @ts-nocheck`.** Removed. The module typechecks
   without it, so a future bad `duration:` or action shape now surfaces at compile time
   (the BT23-047 failure mode named in the brief).
2. **Fixed (local): the old test proved almost nothing behaviorally.** It drove both triggers
   through the injected `engine.fireTiming` cast rather than the real turn loop, asserted
   `s.state.memory === before` after an end-of-turn digivolve, never checked the digivolution
   requirement, the trait gate, the decline branches, the controller boundaries, the digivolve
   draw, or the `[Security]` clause. Replaced with 16 tests, 11 of them behavioral through
   public intents (`startTurnLoop`, `endPhase`, `attack`, `surrender`).
3. **Catalog discrepancy (NOT fixed — coordinator owns cards.json).**
   `cards.json` stores BT23-088's `effectText` with two **non-breaking spaces** (U+00A0) at
   offsets 86 and 270: `"trait from your hand"` and `"trait in the trash"`. The
   official card list prints ordinary spaces at both positions (verified byte-for-byte against
   `logs/official-bt23.html`). Cosmetic only — no parser in the effect pipeline reads
   `effectText` for these clauses — but it will break any exact-text assertion or diff against
   the official list. The test normalizes U+00A0 and records why.

No engine defect found. No retained red, no skip.

## Remaining gaps

- The "without paying the cost" proof is indirect at the memory level: the turn handover
  overwrites memory before the test can read it, so the accepted branch is compared against the
  declined branch reaching the same handover value (3) from the same board, plus the IR's
  `payCost: false`. A direct "memory did not drop by 2 at the instant of the digivolve" reading
  would need an `onEvent` probe inside the resolution.
- The `[Security]` test proves the free play; it does not prove what happens when the security
  play would be the fourth Tamer or otherwise blocked, which is generic play-path behavior, not
  this card's text.
- Clause 2 is not tested with several eligible bases at once (the choice among two of your
  Digimon). The engine's `chooseTargets` path is shared and covered elsewhere; this card adds
  no selection rule of its own.
