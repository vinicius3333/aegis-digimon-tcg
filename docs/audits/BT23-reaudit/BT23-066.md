Printed text:

```
[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[CS] trait: Cost 3

＜Scapegoat＞
[On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon.
Then, if digivolving from the trash, you may play 1 play cost 3 or lower card with
the [Undead] or [CS] trait from your trash without paying the cost.
```

Inherited: `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your other Digimon would leave the battle area, by deleting this Digimon, they don't leave.`

## Printed clauses

1. **C1 — Alternate digivolution route.** Lv.4 with the [CS] trait for cost 3, on top of the printed Purple Lv.4 cost-3 route.
2. **C2 — ＜Scapegoat＞** on the card itself.
3. **C3 — [On Play] / [When Digivolving] delete.** Delete exactly 1 opposing Digimon of level 4 or lower.
4. **C4 — Conditional tail.** Only when this card digivolved *from the trash*: optionally play 1 card of play cost 3 or lower with the [Undead] or [CS] trait from your trash, free.
5. **C5 — Inherited leave-play replacement.** [All Turns] [Once Per Turn], when another of your Digimon would leave the battle area, delete this Digimon instead so they stay.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **`// @ts-nocheck` removed from `BT23-066.ts`.** The module typechecks without it, so IR type errors (for example a bad `duration:` string) will now surface instead of being silenced. No other change to the module — the compiled IR was already correct on every clause tested.
2. **The old test suite proved almost nothing behaviorally.** Three of its four "behavioral" tests drove injected timing (`engine.fireTiming`, `advance().fire`, `advance().verb.deletePermanent`) instead of public intents, and the rest asserted IR shape. All are replaced by public-intent tests: `playCard`, `digivolve`, `attack`.
3. **The old evolution test's negative was vacuous.** It used BT1-025 WarGreymon (Red, Lv.6) — rejected for level and colour before the [CS] trait is ever consulted. Replaced with BT23-052 Consulmon (Black, Lv.4, no [CS]), which isolates the trait gate.
4. **The old evolution test asserted `{ok:true}` with no cost.** Both routes now assert the exact memory delta (3) and the resulting stack identity.

No engine defect found. Nothing retained red; there are no `it.fails` / `it.skip` entries.

- **＜Scapegoat＞ refuses through an empty card selection, not a yes/no prompt.** `combat/controller.ts:1404` calls `selectOptionalInstance`, so `autoSelectCards` always accepts the sacrifice and `autoDeclineOptional` never sees a decision. ## Remaining gaps

- **C5's [Once Per Turn] is not behaviorally observable.** The clause's own cost deletes the source Digimon, so a second use in the same turn is impossible by construction and there is nothing to reset on the next own turn. The `frequency: "OncePerTurn"` field is asserted structurally instead. Reporting this rather than faking a turn-loop test that proves nothing.
- **No Q&A coverage exists to check.** `tools/kb/query.mjs card BT23-066` returns no entries and `KB-INDEX.md` records 0.
- **The tail's non-Digimon reach is untested.** The printed wording is "1 play cost 3 or lower *card*", so an [Undead]/[CS] Tamer or Option in the trash should also qualify; the IR carries no `kind` filter, which is correct, but BT23 has no cost-3-or-lower [Undead]/[CS] non-Digimon card to prove it with.
