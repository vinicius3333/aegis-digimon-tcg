## Defects found and fixed

Both fixes are local to `BT23-080.ts` and were proven red first.

1. **Clause 1 counted breeding-area Digimon.** The `opponentHas` filter had no `zone`, so a lone opponent Digimon in the breeding area granted the memory. Comprehensive rules 3-4-5-8 says information on breeding-area cards can't be referenced. Added `zone: "battleArea"`. Red before the fix (memory 1, expected 0), green after — test `ignores an opponent Digimon that is only in the breeding area`.
2. **Clause 2's replacement had no zone bound.** Same zone-less filter on `sourceFilter`, so a `[CS]` Digimon in the breeding area would have armed the replacement. Comprehensive rules 3-4-5-3 says breeding-area cards aren't affected by effects. Added `zone: "battleArea"`. Structural fix only — see gaps.
3. **Removed `// @ts-nocheck`** from the module. It typechecks clean without it, so future `duration`/filter type errors will surface.

The previous test file was replaced. It proved clause 2 only through `advance().verb.deletePermanent(...)` and clause 1 through an injected `fireTiming` cast, so it earned no behavioral credit and never exercised Q5355.

## Remaining gaps

- The breeding-area bound on clause 2 has no behavioral test: no public intent deletes a breeding-area Digimon, and a verb-driven test would be structural only.
- Simultaneous deletion of two `[CS]` Digimon ("place **1** of those Digimon") is untested. The IR resolves per trigger subject, so the printed choice is not modelled as a single pick across the group. No ruling forces a decision here, so it is recorded rather than changed.
- Clause 2 is not once-per-turn, but Yu leaves play as its own cost, so a repeat is only reachable with a second copy. Not tested.
- Clause 3 does not assert Yu's own `[Start of Your Main Phase]` firing on the turn after it lands from security.
