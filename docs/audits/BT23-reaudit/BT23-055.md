# BT23-055 Cyberdramon re-audit

Sources: committed catalog record `BT23-055`; the official BT23 card list capture in `logs/official-bt23.html`; KB Q5320; `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-055.ts` and its colocated test.

## Card

Black level 5 Digimon, play cost 7, DP 7000, Ultimate, Vaccine, Cyborg/Hudie/CS, evo cost Black level 4 for 3, rarity R.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[CS] trait: Cost 3` — an alternate digivolution route.
2. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost of 5 or less.`
3. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by trashing 1 of your Option cards in the battle area, it doesn't leave.`
4. Inherited: `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with [Cyberdramon] or [Justimon] in its name or the [CS] trait would leave the battle area, by trashing 1 of your Option cards in the battle area, it doesn't leave.`

The catalog text matches the official list byte for byte, including the non-breaking spaces after `[CS]` and `[Justimon]`. No catalog discrepancy.

## How the behavioral tests stay public

Every prevention test reaches an Option card in the battle area through the `playCard` intent on BT23-100 Hudie Net Cafe, paying its cost 3 out of memory and taking its `＜Draw 1＞`. The board is recomputed with `ready()` first, because Hudie Net Cafe's colour waiver is a continuous effect that a hand-laid board has not installed yet; without it the intent is refused with `color-requirement-unmet`.

Removal is driven by real combat wherever possible: Cyberdramon at 7000 DP attacks or is attacked by BT1-024 MetalTyrannomon (10000 DP) or BT1-042 LoaderLeomon (10000 DP), loses the battle, and would leave. The once-per-turn tests hand the turn to the opponent through `advance().runTurn(0)` and a driven `runOneTurn()` for seat 1, then re-arm `isSuspended` after the opponent's Main is authoritatively open, because the Active phase unsuspends the board.

Two tests use `advance().verb.deletePermanent(..., "byEffect")`: the ones about which permanent the inherited clause guards. No player intent deletes a Digimon you control outright, so the effect-driven delete primitive stands in for an opponent removal effect. Those three tests are the only non-intent-driven ones in the file.

## Defect found and fixed

The inherited replacement guarded the wrong permanents.

- File: `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-055.ts`, the `isInherited: true` effect's `sourceFilter`.
- Before: `{ controllerDefault: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"], nameOrTrait: [...] }`.
- Why that is wrong: `runReplacement` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts` computes `protectsSelf` from `action.target?.isSelf` or `protectsFilter.isSelfRef`. With neither set, it installs the board-wide `protects` branch. That branch's seat gate reads `protectsFilter.controller` only — it never reads `controllerDefault` — so the clause protected every CS Digimon on the board, including the opponent's.
- Printed text: "When **this** Digimon ... would leave the battle area". The clause guards its own host and nothing else.
- Fix: add `isSelfRef: true` to that `sourceFilter`. The name and trait gate still applies, because the `protectsSelf` branch runs `permanentMatchesFilter` against the same filter.

Red-then-green proof, run with the fix reverted:

```
RED | does not protect another CS Digimon or the opponent's CS Digimon
RED | compiles both deletion timings and both prevention clauses
passed 12 failed 2
```

With the fix in place all 14 tests pass, including `protects the Digimon carrying it as a digivolution card`, which shows the host is still guarded.

This encoding is not unique to this card. `ST24-06`, `ST24-10` and `BT26-044` carry the same "this Digimon with X in its name or the Y trait" inherited prevention with the same `controllerDefault`-without-`isSelfRef` shape. Fixing them is out of this lane's allowed edits; the coordinator may want a sweep.

## Behaviour confirmed as correct

- The On Play and When Digivolving deletion cannot reach the opponent's breeding-area Digimon. The zone-less target filter does not over-reach here.
- The `playCostLte: 5` boundary is exact: BT1-020 (cost 5) is deleted, BT1-019 (cost 6) is untouched.
- Digivolving from BT23-041 Kabuterimon (Green/Yellow, level 4, CS) succeeds for 3 memory. Because the CS alternate route costs the same 3 as the printed Black level 4 route, `useAlternateCost` does not change the price; the test pins both branches at 3 rather than accepting `{ok: true}` alone. BT1-037 Gorillamon (Blue, level 4, no CS) is refused with `invalid-evolution`.
- The prevention is genuinely once per turn, not once per available Option: with two placed Options, the second removal in the same turn goes through and leaves the spare Option in the battle area.
- The printed clause and the inherited clause do not stack on a host that carries BT23-055 as a digivolution card. The host gets exactly one prevention per turn.

## Remaining gaps

- No retained reds and no skips.
- `BT23-catalog-sync.test.ts` now lists `BT23-055` as drifted, because the module changed and `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` is coordinator-owned. The persisted IR needs a re-sync. `BT23-055` was not in that drift list before this change.
- The `useAlternateCost` branches cannot be told apart on this card: both legal routes cost 3. If the engine ever mispriced the alternate route the test would catch it only against the value 3.
- The two `deletePermanent` tests model an opponent removal effect through the effect-driven primitive rather than a real opponent card, because no player intent deletes a Digimon its controller owns.
