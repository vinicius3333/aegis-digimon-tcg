# BT23-064 Bakemon — re-audit

## Card summary

Catalog record (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`): Purple Digimon, Lv.4 Champion, play cost 4,
DP 4000, evo cost `Purple Lv.3: 2`, attributes `Virus`, types `Ghost` / `LIBERATOR`, rarity C.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] By deleting 1 of your Digimon, delete 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon.`
2. `[When Digivolving]` — same body as clause 1.
3. Inherited: `[On Deletion] Gain 1 memory.`

## Rulings

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT23-064` returns **no knowledge-base entries**. There are no Q&A ids,
errata, or restrictions to cover for this card. The audit therefore leans on the comprehensive rules:

- §3-4-5-3 — cards in the breeding area can't be affected by effects. The IR target filters carry no
  `zone`, and `candidatePermanents` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:110-131`)
  defaults a zone-less permanent filter to `battleArea`, so breeding is already excluded. No IR change needed.

## Defects found

1. **Injected-timing-only proof (fixed).** The previous suite fired `EffectTiming.OnPlay` and
   `EffectTiming.WhenDigivolving` through `fireTiming` / `advance().fire`, and drove the inherited
   clause through `advance().verb.deletePermanent`. None of it proved that a real play or a real
   digivolution reaches the effect, nor that the memory cost is paid. Replaced with public-intent tests.
2. **No cost/level negatives (fixed).** Nothing pinned the `level 4 or lower` gate, the opponent-only
   target scope, or the fact that the By cost is not paid when no legal target exists. Added.
3. **No illegal-source digivolution negative (fixed).** Added a red-source rejection.
4. **`// @ts-nocheck` masked type errors (fixed).** Removed from `BT23-064.ts`; the module typechecks
   without it, so any future bad `duration:` or action shape now surfaces.

Fixture note: the earlier suite stacked `BT23-064` under `BT23-065` Phantomon to prove the inherited
clause. Phantomon has its own `[On Deletion]` that replays a Ghost Digimon from the trash, so that
fixture silently re-played the Bakemon and masked the board endpoint. The stack now uses `BT2-075`
Myotismon (Purple Lv.5, no printed effects, legal Lv.4 Purple route).

No IR change was required: the compiled module already matches the printed text, and the zone-less
filters resolve to the battle area only.

## Remaining gaps

- The inherited `[On Deletion]` is proven through a deletion caused by Bakemon's own By cost. A
  battle-deletion route would exercise the same producer and was not added.
- No test drives the effect from the opponent's seat, so the memory sign for a seat-1 controller is
  unproven. `GainMemory` reads `ctx.source.ownerSeat`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/resources.ts:44-64`), which looks correct by
  inspection only.
