# BT23-098 Unique Emblem: Soul Banquet re-audit

## Card

Purple Option, play cost 3, type LIBERATOR, rarity C. Catalog text (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`) matches the official card list; no discrepancy found.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Main] You may play 1 [Ghostmon] or [Violet Inboots] from your hand or trash without paying the cost.`
2. `Then, place this card in the battle area.`
3. `[Your Turn] When any of your [Violet Inboots] suspend, ＜Delay＞ ・1 of your Digimon with the [Ghost] trait may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Ghost] trait and [LIBERATOR] in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 3.`
4. `[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effects.`

Rulings: Q5386 (2025-10-03) — the ＜Delay＞ target may only digivolve into a card that has **both** the [Ghost] and [LIBERATOR] traits, not either one.

## Defects found and fixed (both local, proven red then green)

1. **The ＜Delay＞ digivolution was free.** The compiled `Digivolve` action carried `reduceCost: 3` but no `payCost`. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolve.ts:247` reads `const pays = action.payCost === true`, so the omission made the whole digivolution cost 0 instead of the printed cost minus 3. Red: the cost test read memory 3 where 2 was due. Fixed by adding `payCost: true`; the same test is now green and pins the exact remainder (Necromon cost 4 - 3 = 1).
2. **Substring name matching.** The previous author had already switched both `[Ghostmon]` / `[Violet Inboots]` filters from `match: "name"` to `match: "nameExact"` and removed `// @ts-nocheck`; that change is kept and pinned by the IR test. It is not behaviorally observable — the catalog holds no card whose name contains "Ghostmon" or "Violet Inboots" as a proper substring — so the proof is structural only.

## Fixture corrections to the partial work

- Four `[Main]` tests played the Option with no colour source and died on `color-requirement-unmet` (CR 4-21-2, `GameEngine.printedColorRequirementMet`). A purple Digi-Egg (BT11-006) in breeding is now the legal colour source.
- The "declines ＜Delay＞" test asserted that Violet Inboots suspends under `autoDeclineOptional`. It cannot: Violet Inboots suspends itself through its own optional `[Your Turn]` cost, which that flag refuses. The test now answers each prompt by hand (accept the suspension, refuse the ＜Delay＞) and asserts the prompt text of each.

## Remaining gaps

- **`[Your Turn]` boundary is untested.** No public route suspends your own Violet Inboots during the opponent's turn — its own suspension cost is `[Your Turn]` too — so "own Violet suspends on the opponent's turn does not fire the ＜Delay＞" has no legal fixture. The opponent-controller case is covered instead.
- **Same-permanent ＜Delay＞.** When the only Ghost is the permanent that is mid-digivolution (the digivolve that suspended Violet Inboots), the effect-driven digivolve resolves to nothing. Not reproduced as a red test: no ruling was found either way, so it is reported rather than blessed. Suspected seam: the target permanent is still inside `digivolveFromInstance`'s own resolution window when the nested ＜Delay＞ runs.
- The free-play clause is not proven against a *deck*-zone candidate or an illegal zone; the printed zones are hand and trash and both are covered.
