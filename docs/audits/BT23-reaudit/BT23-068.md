# BT23-068 GranDracmon — re-audit

## Card

Purple Lv.6 Mega Digimon, Virus, traits [Dark Animal] / [CS]. Play cost 13, DP 13000.
Printed digivolution cost: Purple Lv.5 for 5.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.5 w/[Undead]/[CS] trait: Cost 4` — alternate route; either trait qualifies.
2. `[Start of Your Main Phase] [On Deletion]` 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a level 6 or
   lower [Undead] or [Dark Animal] trait Digimon card in the trash without paying the cost.
3. `[When Digivolving]` You may play 1 level 4 or lower purple Digimon card from your trash
   without paying the cost.
4. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` When any of your Digimon digivolve from the trash, delete all of
   your opponent's lowest level Digimon.

Catalog record matches the official text. No discrepancy to report.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **`// @ts-nocheck` on the module.** Removed. `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-068.ts` typechecks
   clean without it, so the pragma only hid future type errors — exactly the failure mode
   REVIEW-NOTES records for BT23-047's silent `duration` mapping. The module has no `duration:`
   field, so nothing was being mis-mapped.
2. **Structural-only coverage.** Four of the eight prior tests asserted the compiled IR shape
   instead of behaviour, and the [When Digivolving] test drove the engine through the injected
   `fireTiming` seam. All are now behavioural, driven by the `digivolve` intent or the real turn
   loop.
3. **Unpinned alternate cost.** The prior evolution test asserted only `{ ok: true }`, which
   proves nothing about cost 4 (the printed Purple Lv.5 route also returns `ok`). Both branches
   now pin memory: 5 without `useAlternateCost`, 4 with it, and 5 for a Lv.5 base that carries
   neither trait.
4. **No once-per-turn proof.** The `[Once Per Turn]` gate and its reset are now proved through
   the real turn loop, with the intermediate same-turn refusal asserted explicitly.

No card-module logic changes were needed. The IR was already correct on every clause tested.

## Fixture notes for later lanes

Two fixture traps cost time here and are worth recording:

- `Permanent.stack` holds only the cards BENEATH the top card. A digivolution assertion reads
  `stack === [baseInstanceId]` plus `topCard === newInstanceId`, not a two-element stack.
- Purple BT23 Digimon are noisy as bystanders. BT23-065 Phantomon's `[On Deletion]` plays a
  [Ghost] Digimon back from the trash, which silently rebuilt the opponent's board mid-test and
  read as a missing delete. Opponent boards here use vanilla BT1 Digimon (BT1-009 Lv.3,
  BT1-013/BT1-014 Lv.3/Lv.4, BT1-020 Lv.5) so only the audited card moves anything.

  three-way tie is untested.

  reachable.
