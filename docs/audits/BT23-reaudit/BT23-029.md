## Final pass (session 2)

The three gaps left open in session 1 are now closed with public tests.

### 1. Mixed Beast/Beastkin/CS pool, including opponent-controlled cards

`traitPool` drives two matrices over the same six cards: Beast Digimon AD1-010, BT1-049 and BT1-031, Beastkin Digimon BT1-035, CS Digimon BT23-017, and CS Tamer BT23-078.

- "reacts to an own played %s and restricts exactly one opposing Digimon": each own play restricts the first opposing Digimon and leaves the second untouched, so the `count: 1` bound holds for every trait and both card kinds.
- "ignores the same %s when the opponent controls and plays it": the opponent holds and plays the same card on their own turn; neither of their two Digimon is restricted. This pins the `controller: "mine"` source filter against a controller mix-up.

Option cards with the [CS] trait (BT22-099, BT22-100, P-225) are not in the pool. Their play path is a [Main] activation with its own targets and colour-requirement clauses; adding one would test the Option, not Antylamon. The Digimon and Tamer cases already cover both `whenPlayed` shapes.

### 2. Restriction removal at the exact opponent-turn end boundary

New test "keeps the restriction through every opponent phase and drops it at the opponent turn end" reads the `phaseChanged` event log instead of sampling state:

- The restriction is applied on the controller's own Main phase.
- After `endMainPhaseIfOpen(0)` and a wait for the opponent's Main, the log contains `0:End` and the restriction is still active. This rules out a `untilYourTurnEnd` mapping.
- After the opponent's Main ends, the test polls for the first moment the restriction disappears and captures the phase log emitted since. The first entry is `1:End`, and no second opponent phase appears. The clear therefore lands on the opponent's End phase, matching `EffectDuration.UntilOpponentTurnEnd` swept at `opponentTurnEnd` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/modifiers.ts` (`clearsAt`).
- The next own Main phase reports no restriction.

### 3. Inherited clause is now public only

The `advance.fireSubTrigger` smoke test is gone. It is replaced by "publicly reacts only when another own Digimon suspends, not the carrier itself": under a real turn loop the carrier (BT23-035 over BT23-029) attacks first and leaves the 10000-DP opponent untouched, then BT1-035 attacks and the same opponent drops to 6000. `advance.fireSubTrigger`, `fireGlobal` and `fireForPermanent` no longer appear in the file.
