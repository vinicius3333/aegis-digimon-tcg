# BT23-099 The Sistermon Sisters Training Gym

White Option, play cost 2, type [CS].

## Printed clauses

1. **Static colour waiver.** "While you have a Digimon with [Huckmon] in its name the field, you can ignore this card's color requirements."
2. **[Main]** "＜Draw 1＞ Then, place this card in the battle area."
3. **[Your Turn] Delay arm.** "When any of your Digimon digivolve into a Digimon with [Huckmon] or [Jesmon] in its name, ＜Delay＞ ・You may play 1 card with [Sistermon] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost."
4. **[Security]** "You may play 1 card with [Sistermon] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area."

All three name conditions use the "with [X] in its name" wording, so substring matching (`match: "name"`) is correct here. No `nameExact` is warranted.

## Rulings covered

| Q&A | Meaning | Where proved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q5387 | "while you have (card) on the field" = battle area **or** breeding area | `waives the white color requirement from an off-color Huckmon in breeding` (breeding side) and `refuses the off-color play without a Huckmon and allows it once one is on the field` (battle-area side) |

The module's static condition carries `zone: ["battleArea", "breeding"]` for exactly this reason; a structural test pins the zone list so a later edit cannot silently drop breeding.

## Sistermon Ciel and the [Sistermon Noir] alias

BT23-077 Sistermon Ciel carries a `Rule` effect granting the name `[Sistermon Noir]`. For **this** card the alias is not load-bearing: Ciel's printed name already contains the substring "Sistermon", so the Delay and Security filters see it on its printed name alone. The test proves both halves anyway:

- Ciel is free-played out of the trash by the Delay payload (`trash` no longer holds it, it is a battle-area permanent, no play cost paid).
- `observe(engine).grantedNames(ciel)` is `["sistermon noir"]` and `effectiveNames` contains both `sistermon ciel` and `sistermon noir`.

Two findings came out of proving this:

1. **Test defect (fixed).** The file did not import `../index.js`, so only BT23-099's own module was registered and BT23-077's `Rule` grant never ran. The alias assertion failed for that reason, not an engine one. Adding the barrel import (matching the BT23-028 exemplar) turned it green.
2. **Engine note, out of scope.** The universal `Rule` name alias reaches ordinary filter matching only through the continuous ledger (`ContinuousEffectLedger.grantNameTrait`), which works. The separate static path `universalNameAliasesFor` (`engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts`) is wired into `digiXrosNamesOf` only, so an alias would not be visible to a card that looks for a Ciel sitting in **hand or trash** by its granted name. BT23-099 is unaffected because it matches on the printed substring. Flagging it for the engine lane rather than retaining a red here.
