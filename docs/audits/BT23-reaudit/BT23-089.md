## Card

Printed text (official card list, BT-23):

1. `[Start of Your Main Phase]` If your opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory.
2. `[All Turns]` When any of your Digimon with the `[CS]` trait would leave the battle area, by
   suspending this Tamer and trashing 2 same-level cards from 1 of your `[CS]` trait Digimon's
   digivolution cards, they don't leave.
3. `[Security]` Play this card without paying the cost.

Catalog check: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` matches the official list on every
field (name, color, kind, cost, trait, both effect texts). The catalog writes "trash 2 same-level
cards" where the official page writes "trashing"; same clause, no rules difference. No catalog
edit needed, so none was made.

## Rulings

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT23-089` returns no knowledge-base entries, and
`docs/audits/BT23-reaudit/KB-INDEX.md` lists BT23-089 with 0 Q&A ids. There is nothing to cover
from the Q&A set. Mechanics therefore come from the comprehensive rules:

- §3-4-5-3 / §3-4-5-8: cards in breeding areas can't be affected by effects, and their
  information can't be referenced, unless the effect names the breeding area. This settles both
  board clauses: "your opponent has a Digimon" and "leave the battle area" are battle area only.

### Fixture legality

`SAME_LEVEL_STACK` is `[BT2-056 Numemon (Lv.4), BT22-031 GoldNumemon (Lv.4)]` under
`BT23-031 Angewomon (Lv.5, [CS])`. That stack has a real public route: Numemon digivolves into
GoldNumemon ("[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[Numemon] in name: Cost 2" — a rare same-level route), which
digivolves into Angewomon ("[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[CS] trait: Cost 3"). The fixture seeds the
resulting stack rather than replaying the three plays, because the clause under test does not
depend on how the stack formed. It also gives a non-`[CS]` card in the pair, which is what makes
the `hostFilter` fix observable.

Security fixtures use main-deck Digimon (BT1-009/010/011). No Digi-Egg is placed in a deck or
security stack.

## Defects found and fixed (all local, in `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-089.ts`)

1. **The trash cost demanded that the trashed cards be `[CS]` Digimon.** The cost filter carried
   `kind: ["Digimon"]` and the `[CS]` trait alongside `zone: "digivolutionCards"`. For a hosted
   zone, `candidateLooseInstances`
   (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/loose.ts` ~300) applies the plain filter to
   each **stack card** and only `filter.hostFilter` to the host permanent. The printed clause puts
   the `[CS]` requirement on the Digimon whose digivolution cards are trashed, not on the trashed
   cards. Moved to `hostFilter`. Red proof: with the old shape, "pays the compound cost..." fails
   (the pair Numemon + GoldNumemon has only one `[CS]` card, so the cost is unpayable).
2. **`affectsAll` was missing.** The printed text says "they don't leave" — one payment saves
   every `[CS]` Digimon leaving in the same event, exactly as on peer BT19-048
   (`apps/api/src/cards/BT19/BT19-048.ts`). Without it, `consultLeavePrevention` charges once per
   saved permanent. Added `affectsAll: true` and `target.count: "all"`. Red proof: removing it
   alone fails "saves every [CS] Digimon leaving together for a single payment".
3. **Both board clauses reached the breeding area.** `opponentHas` with no `zone` counts breeding
   permanents (`interpreter/scaling.ts` `countMatching`, default branch), and the replacement's
   `sourceFilter`/`target.filter` likewise did not restrict the leave to the battle area. Added
   `zone: "battleArea"` to all three, per comprehensive rules §3-4-5-8. Red proof: removing it
   from `opponentHas` fails "ignores an opponent Digimon that is only in the breeding area".
4. **`// @ts-nocheck` removed.** The module typechecks without it, so the suppression only hid
   future IR type errors (the `EffectDurationRef` class of defect found elsewhere in this set).
   `pnpm --filter @aegis/api typecheck` reports no error in either BT23-089 file.

## Remaining gaps

- No retained reds or skips. No engine seam was hit.
- A non-deletion leave (return to hand or to the deck by an opponent effect) is not covered.
  `wouldLeavePlay` is the right event and `leaveCause` is left at `any`, so the shape is correct,
  but the behavior is untested for lack of a clean single-clause bounce card in the fixture set.
- The `SAME_LEVEL_STACK` route is argued from the printed digivolution requirements rather than
  replayed through three public digivolve intents.
- Simultaneous interaction with another leave replacement on the same permanent (the
  `orderReplacements` path) is not covered; the fixture deliberately uses a host with no
  competing replacement.
