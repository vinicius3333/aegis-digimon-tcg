# BT23-048 Gotsumon — re-audit

## Card

- Lv.3 Rookie, Black/Red, play cost 3, DP 1000, Data. Traits: Rock / Hudie / CS.
- Evolution costs: Black Lv.2 cost 1, Red Lv.2 cost 1.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[CS] trait: Cost 0` — alternate evolution route.
2. `[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Hudie] trait and 1
   Tamer card or Option card with the [CS] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to
   the bottom of the deck.`
3. Inherited: `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower Digimon
   card with the [Hudie] trait from your hand without paying the cost. The Digimon this
   effect played can't digivolve and is deleted at the end of your opponent's turn.`

The catalog text matches the official card list. No discrepancy to report.

## Rulings covered

- **Q5316** — with this card in the digivolution cards of a Digimon with ＜Alliance＞, the
  Digimon played by the inherited effect during that attack may be chosen as the ally.
  Covered behaviorally.
- **Q5567** — the played Digimon is deleted at the end of the opponent's turn. Covered
  behaviorally. The second half ("if it isn't deleted by another effect, it's still prevented
  from digivolving") is a gap — see below.
- **Q5568** — an end-of-turn trigger and this deletion are simultaneous pending processing, so
  the turn player chooses the order. Retained red; the engine never offers the choice.

## What the tests pin

- **On Play**: memory 3 -> 0 through a public `playCard`; hand ends as exactly
  `[BT22-083, BT23-050]`; deck ends as exactly `[BT1-010, BT1-011, BT1-009]`, proving the
  unchosen card goes to the BOTTOM, not the top. No `actionRejected` event.
- **On Play, no match**: hand stays empty and all 3 revealed cards land at the bottom in
  revealed order.
- **Inherited play**: memory is unchanged at 3 (the card is played free), the cost-7 Hudie
  (BT23-055) and the non-Hudie (BT1-009) both stay in hand, and the played permanent carries
  the `digivolve` restriction. No decision is left pending.
- **Digivolution lock**: a public `digivolve` of BT23-041 onto the played Digimon returns
  `ok: false`; the permanent keeps its top card and the evolution card stays in hand.
- **Once per turn**: after the first attack the second copy is still in hand; a second attack
  in the same turn (attacker unsuspended) leaves it in hand; after a full seat 0 turn and a
  full seat 1 turn the third attack plays it. The intermediate state is asserted at each step.
- **Q5567**: the played Digimon survives the whole of my turn and is in the trash after the
  opponent's turn ends. The host permanent is untouched, so the deletion is scoped to the
  Digimon this effect played.
- **Q5316**: the ally the test chooses is the Digimon the inherited effect just played; it ends
  suspended, and the opponent loses 2 security cards (the normal check plus ＜Alliance＞'s extra),
  proving ＜Alliance＞ actually resolved with that ally.

## Defects found

### 1. ＜Alliance＞ never orders against an inherited [When Attacking] (engine, retained red)

`GameEngine.combineAllianceTiming` (apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts ~line 891) decides whether
to pool ＜Alliance＞ into the attacker's [When Attacking] window with:

```ts
effectsOf(EffectTiming.OnUseAttack, this.cardSourceOf(permanent.topCard)).length > 0
```

That reads the TOP CARD only. An attacker whose only [When Attacking] effect is inherited from a
digivolution card — exactly the BT23-048 case — falls back to the legacy path, where ＜Alliance＞
always runs last and no `orderTriggers` decision is raised. Comprehensive Rules §15-4 and KB
Q5257 make the two simultaneous triggers the controller orders.

Q5316's answer still comes out right, because the fixed order (inherited play, then ＜Alliance＞)
happens to be the order the ruling needs. The controller simply loses a choice the rules give
it, and a card that wanted ＜Alliance＞ to resolve first cannot get it.

Retained as `it.fails("orders inherited [When Attacking] against Alliance in one window")`, seam
name `alliance-window-ignores-inherited-when-attacking`. Engine-owned; not fixed here.

### 2. Q5568 ordering is never offered (engine, retained red)

Seam name `end-of-turn-order-with-delayed-delete`. Fixture: seat 1 holds EX9-033 Kaguyamon,
whose `[End of Your Turn]` effect fires at the same moment as this card's end-of-opponent-turn
deletion. Both DO fire — seat 1 receives its optional prompt and the played Digimon is deleted —
but they never meet in one window, so no `orderTriggers` decision is raised for the turn player.
Suspected seam: the `endOfTurn` SubTrigger registration behind the `DelayedDelete` action in
apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts, which does not join the end-of-turn window's
`collectPending` pool that the simultaneous-trigger resolver orders.

Retained as `it.fails(...)`. Engine-owned; not fixed here.

### 3. Fixture defects in the previous test file (fixed here)

The prior BT23-048 test file was largely structural and had two illegal or non-proving fixtures:

- The alternate-evolution test put a Lv.2 Digi-Egg (BT23-002) in the BATTLE AREA. Digi-Eggs live
  in breeding. Now `breeding: { card: "BT23-002" }`, and the negative uses BT1-007, a non-CS egg.
- It asserted `{ ok: true }` on the alternate route without pinning the cost. Both branches now
  assert memory stays at 0 — the implicit alternate match and the explicit
  `useAlternateCost: true` request — and the explicit branch also asserts the resulting stack.

The old file also drove the whole inherited clause through `advance().fire(OnUseAttack, ...)` and
`advance().fireSubTrigger("endOfTurn")` with a hand-set `turnSeat`, which proves the IR wiring but
not the game. Those paths are replaced with a public `attack` and real turns.

No defect was found in `apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-048.ts`. The module is unchanged.

## Remaining gaps

- **Q5567 second half** — "if it isn't deleted by another effect when it would be deleted, it's
  still prevented from digivolving". Reaching this needs a save effect to intercept the delayed
  deletion. The attempt with BT23-073 Eater Bit's "doesn't leave" replacement never fired against
  the `DelayedDelete`, which points at BT23-073 or the deletion path, not at BT23-048. Out of this
  lane's allowed edits and not written up as a BT23-048 red. Recommend it as a follow-up for the
  engine or BT23-073 lane.
- The Q5316 test drives ＜Alliance＞ down the legacy path (see defect 1). When that seam is fixed
  the test will need to answer an `orderTriggers` decision first; the `it.fails` companion is the
  marker for that.
