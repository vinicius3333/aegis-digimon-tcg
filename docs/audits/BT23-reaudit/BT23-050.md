## Evidence details

All behavioral tests drive public intents (`playCard`, `digivolve`, `attack`, `respondDecision`, `respondAlliance`, `endPhase`, `surrender`). No test uses `advance().fire`.

- **-2000 DP.** Playing Ankylomon for exactly 5 memory (6 to 1) reduces one chosen opposing Digimon from 5000 to 3000 and leaves the second opposing Digimon at 5000. Both own Digimon and the Shakkoumon card in hand are unchanged when the optional DNA is declined.
- **DNA accept.** A suspended BT23-027 Angemon plus the played Ankylomon merge into the exact Shakkoumon hand instance. The result is unsuspended, its stack is exactly those two instances, the seat has one permanent left, memory is 6 - 5 = 1 (Shakkoumon's `[DNA Digivolve]` cost is 0), and the hand holds only the DNA bonus draw's exact instance.
- **Refusals.** Three fixtures prove the DP clause still resolves while the DNA does not: one own Digimon, a level-3 partner, and Shakkoumon in trash instead of hand. Each asserts the exact battle-area instance set, the exact hand instance set, the exact trash card ids, and no pending decision.
- **Q5317.** With the target at 2000 DP, the -2000 takes it to 0. The event log shows `cardPlayed` for BT23-032 with `mechanic: "dna"`, then `effectResolved` for `BT23-050/ir-6-0`, and only then the target's `cardsMoved` from battleArea to trash. The DNA therefore resolved while the 0-DP Digimon was still on the board, which is exactly what Q5317 states. Endpoint: the target's exact instance is the only card in the opponent trash and the opponent battle area is empty.
- **Q5318.** BT23-018 Garurumon with BT23-017 Betamon underneath attacks; Betamon's inherited effect plays Ankylomon (cost 5, Hudie) without paying, under a permanent digivolution restriction. The board is then exactly Blue Lv.4 Garurumon plus Black/Yellow Lv.4 Ankylomon, which is the only pair that could satisfy Shakkoumon's `Yellow Lv.4 + black/blue Lv.4`. The DNA is refused: Shakkoumon stays in hand, the seat still has two permanents, and `isRestricted(anky, "digivolve")` is true. A control test plays the same Ankylomon normally onto the same host and the DNA succeeds, so the refusal is caused by the restriction and not by an illegal pair.
- **Q5319.** Hudiemon (BT23-101) with Erika Mishima (BT23-084) underneath stays on the board while Ankylomon's On Play consumes the suspended ally and Ankylomon itself. The result enters unsuspended. Hudiemon then attacks publicly; the `alliancePrompt` event lists the new Shakkoumon permanent among `eligibleAllyIds`, `respondAlliance` suspends it, and the opponent's two security cards are both checked — the extra ＜Alliance＞ security check. This proves the operative half of Q5319 (an unsuspended DNA result is a legal ＜Alliance＞ ally).
- **`if it's your turn`.** Shakkoumon's `[All Turns]` leave-the-battle-area replacement is used as the public route to an `[On Play]` on the opponent's turn: the opponent deletes Shakkoumon in battle, Ankylomon is played from its digivolution cards, the mandatory -2000 lands on the attacker (12000 to 10000), and the DNA clause does not fire even though a second Lv.4 and a Shakkoumon card in hand are available (the same pair the Q5318 control proves is legal on your own turn).
- **Evolution.** Both alternate routes are taken publicly with `useAlternateCost: true` and the matching `alternateRequirementIndex`. BT1-027 Armadillomon pays the printed 2; BT23-037 Tentomon pays 1 because its own `[Your Turn]` effect reduces a digivolution into a `[CS]` card by 1. Each asserts the source and Ankylomon instances inside the resulting Shakkoumon stack, the -2000 endpoint, and the two bonus draws (digivolution then DNA) as exact instances in order. BT1-009 Monodramon (Red Lv.3, no CS) is refused on both alternate indices and on the printed route, with hand, source and memory unchanged.
- **Duration.** Through `startTurnLoop()` and public `endPhase`, the target stays at 3000 during the opponent's Main phase and is back at 5000 on the next own Main.

## Defect found and fixed

`BT23-050.ts` compiled the DNA result filter as `{ tokens: ["Shakkoumon"], match: "name" }`. `matching/definition.ts:352` implements `"name"` as a substring test, while `"nameExact"` (line 354) is the exact test. A printed `[Shakkoumon]` is an exact-name condition, and the peer card BT23-027 already uses `nameExact` for the same clause. Both occurrences (OnPlay and WhenDigivolving) are now `nameExact`. This is local to the card module; no engine or catalog change was needed.

## Exact-name sweep (session 2)

The printed route reads "[Digivolve] [Armadillomon]/Lv.3 w/[CS] trait: Cost 2". The bracketed name with no "in name" wording is an EQUALITY gate, but the module used `names`, a substring gate in `engine/cards/cardData.ts` `matchGatedRequirement` (~line 481). Changed to `namesExact: ["Armadillomon"]`. No released card's name merely contains "Armadillomon", so the near-name negative uses a synthetic level-3 base without the [CS] trait ("BlackArmadillomon") passed straight to `matchingAlternateDigivolutionRequirement`: red before the fix, green after. The positive [Armadillomon] route (BT1-027) and the level-3 [CS] route stay green. The DNA target [Shakkoumon] already used `match: "nameExact"`.
