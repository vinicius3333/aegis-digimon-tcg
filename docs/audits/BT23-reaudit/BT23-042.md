# BT23-042 Waspmon — re-audit

## Card summary

Green/Black Lv.4 Champion, play cost 4, DP 4000. Traits: Cyborg, X Antibody, Royal Base, CS.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.3 w/[Royal Base]/[CS] trait: Cost 2` — alternate digivolution requirement.
2. `[Security] [All Turns] All of your [Royal Base] trait Digimon get +1000 DP.`
3. `[When Digivolving] If you have 1 or fewer Tamers, you may play 1 Tamer card with [Royal Base] in its text from your hand without paying the cost.`
4. Inherited: `[All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP.`

Catalog text matches the compiled IR. No catalog discrepancy found.

## Rulings covered

- **Q5303** — "X in its text" spans name, traits, effects, inherited effects, requirements.
  The IR uses `nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Royal Base"], match: "text" }]`, and
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts` resolves `match: "text"`
  as the union of names, traits and every text field — the ruling's reading.
  The behavioral proof deliberately uses a **text-only** positive: BT23-083 Fei is a Tamer
  with traits `Zaxon`/`CS` (no `Royal Base` trait) whose printed effect names `[Royal Base]`.
  A trait-only positive is not available: the catalog holds no Tamer with the `Royal Base`
  trait, so the trait half of the union is untestable from the card pool.

## Engine behavior confirmed during the audit

- When both the printed EvoCost and the alternate requirement match one source, the
  `digivolve` intent must set `useAlternateCost: true` to pay the alternate cost. This is the
  documented intent contract (`apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolve.ts:65`), not a defect.
  The previous test file never exercised either path through a public intent, so this was
  not visible.
- The permanent's `stack` holds the under-cards only; `topCard` is separate.

## Defects found

- The previous `BT23-042.test.ts` proved the `[When Digivolving]` clause by injecting the
  timing (`advance(...).fire(EffectTiming.WhenDigivolving, ...)`), never digivolved through a
  public intent, and toggled `security[0].faceUp` by hand after setup. It also asserted no
  memory, no stack identity, and no refusal branch. All of that is replaced with public
  intents and exact endpoints. No change was needed in `BT23-042.ts`.

## Remaining gaps

- The `[Security]` aura is proved by DP inspection, not by a resolved battle. No combat case
  shows the +1000 deciding a battle outcome.
- Inherited DP teardown is proved by comparison against a control permanent with no BT23-042
  in its stack, not by removing the source card from an existing stack mid-game.
- The trait half of the Q5303 union cannot be exercised: no Tamer in the catalog carries the
  `Royal Base` trait.
