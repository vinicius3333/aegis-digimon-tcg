# BT23-073 Eater Bit — re-audit

## Card

White Digimon, [Eater] form, [Hudie]/[CS] traits, play cost 3, DP 1000, no evolution costs.

Printed clauses (catalog `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`):

1. `[On Play] Delete 1 of your opponent's level 3 Digimon.`
2. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your other Digimon with the [Eater] or [Hudie] trait
   would leave the battle area, by deleting this Digimon or placing it as the bottom digivolution
   card of your [Mother Eater] in the breeding area, 1 of those Digimon doesn't leave.`
3. Inherited: `[Breeding] [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When any of your Digimon cards with the
   [Eater] trait would be played, you may reduce the play costs by 1.`

Clause 2 carries **no cause qualifier**. Compare BT22-007 [Mother Eater], whose inherited effect
prints "would leave the battle area **other than by your effects**". That difference is the whole
defect below.

## Defect found and fixed

`apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-073.ts` compiled clause 2 with `leaveCause: "otherThanYourEffect"`.
`causeAllows` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts` ~line 249) then
returns false whenever the removal is `byEffect` and the resolving seat is the card's own
controller, so `consultLeavePrevention` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/leavePrevention.ts` line 94)
skipped the replacement entirely. The controller could never save a Digimon from their own effect.

The printed text has no such qualifier, so the field is removed and `causeAllows` stays at `"any"`.
Two earlier lanes flagged exactly this (docs BT23-037 Q5565, BT23-048 Q5567, REVIEW-NOTES "Session 2
engine seam queue").

Red-then-green proof: restoring `leaveCause: "otherThanYourEffect"` fails 2 of the 14 tests
("...with no cause qualifier" and "saves an ally from the CONTROLLER'S OWN delayed deletion...");
removing it passes all 14.

**No engine seam remains.** The replacement *is* consulted on a `DelayedDelete`:
`primitives.delayedDeletePlayed` resolves through `deletePermanent(..., "byEffect")`, which calls
`consultLeavePrevention` with `resolvingSeat` = the controller. Only the cause gate was blocking
it. No `it.fails` reproducer is retained for this card.

## What the behavioral tests pin

- **On Play**: memory 3 -> 0 through a public `playCard`; the opponent's battle area ends as exactly
  the level 4 permanent id; the opponent's trash holds exactly the level 3 instance id; no
  `actionRejected` event.
- **Opponent effect**: the opponent publicly plays their own BT23-073, whose On Play targets my
  level 3 [Hudie] Gotsumon. My board ends as exactly `[ally]`, my trash as exactly the Eater Bit
  instance.
- **Own delayed deletion**: BT23-050 Ankylomon over BT23-048 Gotsumon attacks; Gotsumon's inherited
  [When Attacking] plays BT23-037 Tentomon free and schedules its `DelayedDelete` for the end of the
  opponent's turn. After a full seat 0 turn the played Tentomon is asserted still present; after a
  full seat 1 turn it is *still* present and my trash is exactly the Eater Bit instance.
  Q5565/Q5567 second half: `observe().isRestricted(survivor, "digivolve")` is true and a public
  `digivolve` of BT23-041 onto it is rejected with the top card unchanged.
- **Battle**: my 1000 DP Gotsumon attacks a 3000 DP blocker. My board ends as exactly the attacker
  id, my trash as exactly the Eater Bit instance, and the blocker is untouched.
- **Mother Eater cost**: with `preferOptionIndex: 1`, the ally survives, my trash is empty, and
  `stack[0]` (the bottom of the breeding host's digivolution cards) is the Eater Bit instance.
- **Decline**: with `autoDeclineOptional`, my board ends as exactly the Eater Bit id and my trash as
  exactly the ally instance.
- **Inherited reduction**: memory 5 -> 3 on the first Eater play (printed 3, paid 2), then 3 -> 0 on
  the second in the same turn (full 3). The same stack in the battle area charges the full 3.

Every behavioral test drives public intents (`playCard`, `attack`, `digivolve`) plus
`advance().runTurn()` for real turn boundaries. `s.give` is used once, only to put an evolution card
in hand after the turn loop.
