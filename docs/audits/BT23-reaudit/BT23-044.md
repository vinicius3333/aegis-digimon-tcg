## Defects found and fixed (all local to `BT23-044.ts`)

1. **Breeding area counted toward "if you have ... a [CS] trait Digimon".** The generated `youHave` filter carried no `zone`, and the zone-less scan in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:95-100` walks the battle area *and* the breeding area. Comprehensive rules 3-4-5-8 forbids referencing information on cards in breeding areas. Fixed by adding `zone: "battleArea"`. Red without the fix: the play cost dropped to 4 with only a hatched CS Digimon.
2. **A `[CS]` trait Tamer was offered as a protection target.** The `Restrict` target filter had no `kind`, but the printed text says "1 of your Digimon". With Yuuko Kamishiro (a `[CS]` Tamer) on the board, she appeared in the `chooseTargets` candidate list and could absorb the protection. Fixed by adding `kind: ["Digimon"]`. Red without the fix.
3. **The protection blocked the controller's own return effects.** "their effects can't return" is opponent-scoped. `restriction: "cannotReturnToHandOrDeck"` normalizes to `beReturned` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/restrictions.ts:62-68`, and that path only records the opponent-only scope when the IR action carries `byOpponentEffectsOnly`. Fixed by adding it to both trigger copies. Red without the fix: the controller's own BT4-102 Aqua Viper could not return the protected Digimon.
4. **`[Yuuko Kamishiro]` matched as a substring.** `match: "name"` is a substring match (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:352`); a printed bracket name is exact (KB Q1231/Q1232). Changed to `match: "nameExact"`. This has no behavioral test because BT22-083 is currently the only card whose name contains "Yuuko Kamishiro", so both modes agree today; the structural assertion pins the mode.

## Engine gap reported, not fixed

**Hand-origin gate on `wouldBePlayed` self-reducers is missing.** The printed condition is "when this card would be played **from the hand**". `WouldBePlayedSelfReducer` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/reducers.ts:200-250`) records no origin zone, and `GameEngine.fireBeforePayCost` applies every collected reducer at `apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts:4280` without consulting its `originZone` parameter. The two intent-driven call sites (`GameEngine.ts:5482`, `GameEngine.ts:6615`) pass `"hand"`, but `finalizeEffectPlayCost` (`GameEngine.ts:994-1006`) is reachable with `originZone` `"trash"` or `"security"`, and the reduction would then apply against the printed wording.

No retained red test is attached: a scan of the catalog found no implemented card that plays another card from trash or security *while paying its play cost*, so the wrong branch is currently unreachable through public intents. Every other card with the same printed wording (BT16-089, BT17-068, BT23-031, BT23-067, EX5-034, EX6-039, ST20-12/13, ST21-12/13) shares the gap, so the fix belongs to the engine lane, not this card.
