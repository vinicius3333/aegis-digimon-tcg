# BT23-038 FunBeemon — re-audit

## Card summary

Lv.3 Green/Black Digimon. Play cost 3, DP 1000. Traits: Insectoid, X Antibody, Royal Base, CS.
Printed evo costs: Green Lv.2 cost 1, Black Lv.2 cost 1.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[Royal Base]/[CS] trait: Cost 0`
2. `[Security] [All Turns] All of your [Royal Base] trait Digimon get +1000 DP.`
3. `[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with [Royal Base] in its text and
   1 card with the [CS] trait among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.`
4. Inherited: `[All Turns] This Digimon gets +1000 DP.`

Catalog matches the official text. No discrepancy found. The catalog uses a non-breaking space
before `trait` / `in its text`; the test asserts the exact catalog string including ` `.

## Rulings covered

| Q&A | Subject | Where proved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q5301 | "[X] in its text" spans name, traits, effects, inherited effects, (Rule) and every requirement line | "adds a [Royal Base]-in-text card that has no [Royal Base] trait…" (BT19-084 Winr, trait `LIBERATOR`, only mention is in its `[Main]` effect text) and "spends two distinct physical cards…" (BT23-042, matched through its trait) |

Engine seam: `matchNameOrTrait` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:296`
treats `match: "text"` as the union of effective names, traits (substring) and every printed text
field (`effectText`, `inheritedEffectText`, `securityEffectText`, `linkEffect`, `linkRequirement`,
`dualEffect`, `optionEffect`). Requirement lines live inside `effectText` in this catalog, so the
requirement axis of Q5301 is covered by that union.

Untestable axis: no card in the catalog carries `Royal Base` **only** in `inheritedEffectText`
(checked across all 4388 catalog entries), so the inherited-text axis of Q5301 cannot be
discriminated with real data for this token. Same for a requirement-only card: all four
`Royal Base`-in-text-without-the-trait cards (BT19-084, BT23-083, EX11-063, P-230) carry it in a
`[Main]` / `[On Play]` body, not in a requirement header.

## Defects found

No defect in `BT23-038.ts`. The IR matches every printed clause; `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`.

Defects in the previous test file, all fixed here:

1. The reveal clause was driven by `advance(...).fire(EffectTiming.OnPlay, ...)` — injected timing,
   no behavioral credit. Replaced with the public `playCard` intent, asserting memory paid and the
   permanent on the board.
2. The reveal test never proved the overlapping pools. Its `royalBaseText` and `cs` fixtures were
   disjoint, so a "same card added twice" bug would not have been caught. Two new tests cover the
   overlap (BT23-042 matches both slots) in both directions.
3. The remainder-order assertion was vacuous: exactly one card remained. Now two remain, under an
   untouched fourth deck card, and the exact bottom order is asserted.
4. The digivolution test used `battleArea: [{ card: "EX11-003" }]` — a Lv.2 Digi-Egg placed in the
   battle area, an illegal fixture. It also only asserted `{ ok: true }`, never the cost, so it
   passed while the engine charged the printed cost 1 instead of the printed alternate cost 0.
   Rebuilt as a breeding-area evolution with memory, stack identity and the digivolution draw
   asserted, plus two negatives.
5. The security-aura test set `s.inst(...).faceUp = true` by hand and compared against a captured
   `currentDP` rather than an absolute value, so a zero-delta bug would have been invisible. It also
   had no face-down control and no removal control. All three are now absolute-value assertions.
6. The inherited-DP test preassembled `under: ["BT23-038"]` under BT23-046 Rosemon, a Lv.6 that
   BT23-038 cannot legally sit under, and asserted only a delta. Replaced with a real evolution
   chain into BT23-042 Waspmon plus a real battle and a control.

## Notable engine behavior (not a defect)

`applyIntent(..., { type: "digivolve" })` uses the 0-cost alternate route only when the intent sets
`useAlternateCost: true`. When both a printed evo cost and an alternate requirement match the same
base, the controller chooses (`apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolve.ts:493-503`). Both branches are
now proved, so a regression that silently forces one route goes red.

## Remaining gaps

- The inherited-text and requirement-header axes of Q5301 cannot be discriminated for the
  `[Royal Base]` token with real catalog data (see above). Covered by the shared `matchNameOrTrait`
  union instead of a card-level fixture.
- The aura's reach into the breeding area is not asserted. Whether "all of your Digimon" includes a
  breeding-area Digimon is a general targeting question owned by the engine lane, not by this card.
- The removal control moves the security card to the trash directly and recomputes, rather than
  resolving a real security check. A full security-check route would also fire BT23-038's other
  clauses and would not isolate the aura teardown.
