Printed clauses:

1. `[Hand] [Main] By paying 3 cost and placing this card as the bottom digivolution card of your [King Drasil_7D6] or [Mother Eater] in the breeding area, <Draw 1>`
2. `[All Turns] When any of your Digimon with the [Royal Knight] or [CS] trait are played, by suspending this Digimon, 1 of the played Digimon gains <Rush>, <Raid>, <Reboot> and <Blocker> until your opponent's turn ends.`
3. Inherited: `[Breeding] [Start of Your Main Phase] If this Digimon has 6 or more digivolution cards, you may play 1 Digimon card with [King Drasil] in its name from its digivolution cards without paying the cost.`

Catalog note: `types: ["9000", "CS"]` is correct, not a DP leak. `9000` is the printed trait shared with the BT13-007 Digi-Egg of the same name. No catalog discrepancy found.

## Defect found and fixed

`BT23-072.ts`, `[Hand] [Main]` host filter: `nameOrTrait` used `match: "name"`, which is substring matching in
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts` (`matchNameOrTrait`). The printed names are bracketed
(`[King Drasil_7D6]`, `[Mother Eater]`), so the host must match exactly. Changed to `match: "nameExact"`.

Proof: the "also accepts the [King Drasil_7D6] Digi-Egg as the breeding host" and Mother Eater tests stay green under
`nameExact`, so the fix does not narrow a legal host; the IR-shape test goes red when the mode is reverted to `name`
(verified: 1 failed / 18 passed on the reverted module). The fix is currently latent in behavior: no card in the
catalog has a name that merely contains "King Drasil_7D6" or "Mother Eater", so no near-name negative fixture exists
today. The inherited clause keeps `match: "name"` on purpose, because "with [King Drasil] in its name" is printed
substring wording (it matches both BT13-007 and BT23-072).

## Tests rewritten

The previous suite proved clauses 2 and 3 through injected timing (`advance.fireSubTrigger("whenPlayed")` and
`advance.fireGlobal(OnStartMainPhase)`), which is structural only. Both now run through the real turn loop
(`startTurnLoop()` + `advance.waitForMainPhase`) and public `playCard` / `endPhase` intents. Two fixture rules from
the brief were needed: every turn-loop fixture keeps a neutral playable hand card (`ST1-02`), otherwise Main
auto-passes and the assertions read a state after the turn boundary, where the suspension and the keyword grants
are already gone.

Fixtures use main-deck Digimon for security counts and real Digi-Eggs (`BT22-007`, `BT13-007`, `BT23-003`) only in
breeding.
