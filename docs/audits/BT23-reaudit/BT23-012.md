The printed text reads "[Avian], [Bird], [Beast], [Animal] or [Sovereign] in any of its traits (other than [Sea Animal])", which CR 2-3-2-4 defines as substring containment; the printed "(other than [Sea Animal])" carve-out only exists because "Sea Animal" contains "Animal".
