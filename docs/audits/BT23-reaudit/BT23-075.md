evolution cost.

## Defects found and fixed (local, in `BT23-075.ts`)

1. **Substring digivolve route.** `digivolutionRequirement` used `names: ["Eater Legion"]`.
   `matchingDigivolutionRequirement` (`apps/api/src/engine/cards/cardData.ts` ~479) treats
   `names` as substring — the "[X] in name" wording. The printed `[Eater Legion]: Cost 3` is
   an exact card name. Changed to `namesExact`.
2. **Substring bracketed name in the ceiling filter.** Both ceiling filters used
   `match: "name"` for `[Mother Eater]`; `matchNameOrTrait` treats `"name"` as substring and
   `"nameExact"` as equality. Changed to `nameExact` on both effects.
3. **`// @ts-nocheck` removed.** The module typechecks without it, so future `duration`/field
   errors will surface.

Neither name fix changes behavior against the current catalog (no other card's name contains
"Eater Legion" or "Mother Eater"), so both are proved structurally, plus a near-name
digivolve negative using BT22-082 Eater Adam (same [Eater] trait, different name) which is
refused.

## Retained red (engine seam, do not fix in this lane)

`it.fails("does not stack the breeding [Mother Eater] placement with its own leave effect
(Q5352)")`.

- Setup: seat 0 has a breeding Digimon with BT22-007 [Mother Eater] in its digivolution
  cards, Eater EDEN in the battle area and an [Eater] Digimon in hand. Seat 1 deletes EDEN by
  effect.
- Ruling (Q5352): you may **not** first place EDEN under the breeding Digimon with BT22-007's
  inherited replacement and then also activate EDEN's own [All Turns] replacement.
- Actual: both apply — EDEN is placed under the breeding host **and** the free [Eater] is
  played.
- Seam: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/leavePrevention.ts`, `consultLeaveReplacements`, the
  `repl.mode === "instead"` branch. It stops offering further replacements only when the
  leaving permanent was relocated (`const relocated = host.permanentById(leavingId) ===
  undefined`). EDEN's replacement plays a card from hand and leaves EDEN in place, so it does
  not consume the leave event and the second "instead" replacement runs as well.
- Expected fix: one "instead" replacement per leave event, chosen by the leaving permanent's
  controller, or an explicit exclusivity gate.

## Coordinator probe: BT23-074 Q6706/Q6707

Discriminator used: Erika Mishima (BT23-084) is a Tamer whose **lower** text grants
＜Alliance＞ while the Digimon is [Hudiemon], [Eater Legion] or [Eater EDEN]. Eater EDEN
prints no ＜Alliance＞, so an EDEN stack built through Erika separates the inherited-effect
ruling from EDEN's own printed keywords.

- `gains inherited ＜Alliance＞ on Eater EDEN through an Erika Mishima source (Q6707)`:
  play Erika on the board, digivolve BT23-074 off the Tamer (`useAlternateCost: true`), then
  BT23-075 off Eater Legion. Final stack is `["BT23-084", "BT23-074"]` under EDEN and
  `observe().hasKeyword(perm, "Alliance")` is `true`.
- `has no ＜Alliance＞ on an Eater EDEN stack without Erika Mishima`: BT23-074 (which prints
  ＜Alliance＞ itself) as the base, digivolve EDEN onto it. Stack is `["BT23-074"]` and
  Alliance is `false` — the base's printed keyword correctly does not carry up.

**Result: the engine matches Q6707.** A Tamer digivolution card's inherited (lower) text does
reach the Digimon above it, and only that text does. Q6706 (security effects are *not*
gained) is satisfied by construction here: Erika's `[Security] Play this card without paying
the cost` never activates from the digivolution cards, but this probe does not assert it
directly — it is not observable without a security check on a card already on the board.

## Remaining gaps

- **Ceiling wording is ambiguous.** "For each of your [Mother Eater]'s digivolution cards in
  the breeding area" is modeled as *the digivolution cards under a breeding permanent named
  Mother Eater*. The competing reading is *Mother Eater cards that are digivolution cards in
  the breeding area* — the shape Q5352 describes. The possessive `'s` and BT22-007's own
  breeding effect ("place [Mother Eater]s as **this Digimon's** top digivolution cards", "if
  this Digimon has 10 or more digivolution cards") both support the modeled reading, but no
  ruling settles it. Coordinator may want a KB question.
- The Q5352 red uses `verb.enterEffectResolution` + `verb.deletePermanent` for the
  opponent-effect leave cause (the same seam BT22-007's own suite uses); no printed card in
  the fixture pool produces that cause on demand. The battle-deletion tests for clause 3 are
  fully public.
- The "your own effect" negative for clause 3 also uses the removal verb inside seat 0's
  effect resolution, for the same reason.
- Q6706 is not directly asserted (see probe section).
