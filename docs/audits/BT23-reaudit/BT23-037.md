# BT23-037 Tentomon

## Card

Catalog source: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`. Green/Yellow level-3 Rookie, play cost 3, 1000 DP, Vaccine, traits Insectoid/Hudie/CS. Printed evolution costs: Green level 2 for 1, Yellow level 2 for 1.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[CS] trait: Cost 0` — alternate evolution route.
2. `[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into a Digimon card with the [CS] trait, reduce the digivolution cost by 1.`
3. Inherited: `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 play cost 5 or lower Digimon card with the [Hudie] trait from your hand without paying the cost. The Digimon this effect played can't digivolve and is deleted at the end of your opponent's turn.`

The catalog text uses non-breaking spaces in three places. The test normalises them and asserts both printed strings in full. No catalog discrepancy against the official text was found; the card module needed no change.

## Evidence detail

**Q5299.** The digivolve intent selects its own cost path (`intent.useAlternateCost`, `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolve.ts` step 5), so the table drives both routes and reads the installed reduction through `observe().costReduction("wouldDigivolve", …)` before paying:

| Zone | Target | Route | Reduction | Memory paid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| battle area | BT23-041 (CS) | ordinary | 1 | 2 (printed 3) |
| breeding | BT23-041 (CS) | ordinary | 0 | 3 |
| battle area | BT23-041 (CS) | alternate | 1 | 1 (printed 2) |
| breeding | BT23-041 (CS) | alternate | 0 | 2 |
| battle area | BT23-020 (CS, off-colour) | alternate only | 1 | 1 |
| breeding | BT23-020 (CS, off-colour) | alternate only | 0 | 2 |
| battle area | BT1-051 (non-CS) | ordinary | 0 | 2 |

Each case also asserts the exact top card and single-source stack, and one evolution bonus draw by exact deck and hand contents. The non-CS row separates the printed `[CS]` condition from the route; the off-colour rows make the reduction visible where only one route exists. A further test shows the reduction is 0 while `turnSeat` is the opponent.

**Inherited clause.** The host is `BT23-050` Ankylomon over a level-3 `[CS]` Tentomon, which is a real public evolution route. On a real attack the effect plays only `BT23-020` Seadramon (cost 5, Hudie); `BT23-055` (cost 7, Hudie), `BT1-009` (no Hudie), `BT23-081` (Hudie Tamer) all stay in hand, and `BT23-017` (Hudie, cost 3) stays in the trash, proving the hand-only source. The board ends with exactly two permanents, no pending decision and no loud gap. The catalog has no Hudie Digimon of play cost exactly 6, so the upper boundary is proved at 7.

**Restriction.** The played Seadramon rejects a `digivolve` intent into `BT23-055` on its legal alternate route with 8 memory available; memory is unchanged and the permanent stays. A second Seadramon that this effect did not play accepts the identical intent and pays 3, so the block is bound to the played instance and not to the card id.

**Once per turn.** One host plays a Hudie on its first attack, plays nothing on a second attack in the same turn (asserted before the turn ends, not merely awaited), and plays the second copy on the next own turn after both players end their phases through the real loop. A separate test shows the cap is per Tentomon copy: two hosts, each with its own Tentomon source, each play one Hudie in the same turn.

**Decline.** With `autoDeclineOptional` nothing is played, the candidate stays in hand, and after both turn boundaries the battle area still holds only the host and the trash is unchanged — no deletion was scheduled.

**Deletion boundary.** The played Digimon survives the controller's own turn end and is deleted at the opponent's turn end, with exactly one matching card in the trash. The host and its Tentomon source are asserted intact.

## Retained reds

1. **Alliance DP on the legacy branch.** `it.fails("adds the played ally's DP to the attacking host")`. Q5300's own content — the played Digimon appears in `alliancePrompt.eligibleAllyIds` and is suspended by `respondAlliance` — passes. The DP benefit does not arrive. The host `BT23-041` has no printed `[When Attacking]` effect on its top card, so `combineAllianceTiming` (`apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts`) is false and `CombatController.declareAttack` takes the legacy `!combineAllianceTiming` Alliance loop in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts`. Expected 14000 (6000 base + 3000 from BT23-041's own suspend effect + the 5000-DP ally); observed 6000, so the pre-existing `forTheTurn` grant is dropped as well. `src/engine/combat/allianceDecision.test.ts` still passes, so the combined branch is healthy. The engine lane owns Alliance attack timing, so this is not adjusted here.

2. **Q5566 ordering choice.** `it.fails("offers the turn player the end-of-turn processing order")`. Both processings do land at the same boundary (proved by the passing companion test: BanchoLeomon's ＜Raid＞ attack still sees the played Digimon and redirects onto it, leaving security at 5). But no `orderTriggers` request is ever made. The deletion is a one-shot `endOfTurn` SubTrigger subscription installed by `delayedDeletePlayed` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts` and owned by the non-turn player, while `[End of Your Turn]` is a System-A timing effect owned by the turn player; the two are never combined into one ordering window across controllers, so the deletion always resolves last. `src/engine/effects/delayedDeletePlayed.test.ts` only covers the same-controller collision (Q5564), which is why the shared suite does not prove this card.

## Q5565 closed (session 2)

The prevention half is now green and the `it.fails` is gone.

Two causes, fixed in two lanes:

1. The BT23-073 lane removed `leaveCause: "otherThanYourEffect"` from Eater Bit's `wouldLeavePlay` replacement. The printed text has no "opponent's effect" qualifier, so `causeAllows` must stay at "any" and the prevention now answers a deletion the controller's own card scheduled.
2. The residual failure in this file was the fixture, not the intent shape. Eater Bit's prevention is an alternative cost ("delete this Digimon" or "place it under your breeding [Mother Eater]"), so it raises a chooseOption decision. The fixture ran with `autoAcceptOptional` and `autoSelectCards` but no `autoChooseOption`, so that decision was never answered; the end-of-turn boundary stalled and `waitForMainPhase(0)` threw "Seat 0's Main phase did not become ready". Adding `autoChooseOption: true` closes it. `permanentId` plus `useAlternateCost` on the digivolve intent was not the cause — the same pair digivolves successfully on the untouched twin in the sibling test.

Endpoints, unweakened: the played Digimon is still in the battle area under its exact instance id and not in the trash, Eater Bit is in the trash and off the board, `isRestricted(played, "digivolve")` is true, and a public `digivolve` intent on it is rejected with the card left in hand and memory unchanged at 8.
