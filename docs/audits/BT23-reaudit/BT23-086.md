## Defects

1. **Retained red (engine/IR seam): the end-of-turn attack is not restricted to the player.**
   Printed: "1 of your level 6 Digimon … may attack a player." `AttackAction`
   (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/actions/combat.ts`) exposes only `attackPlayer`, and
   `forceAttack` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts` ~5557-5578) treats it as a WIDENING
   flag: `attackPlayer !== false` adds the player to the candidate list, and the opponent's legal
   (suspended) Digimon are added unless `attackPlayerOnly` is set. `attackPlayerOnly` exists on the
   primitive but has no IR field, so no card can request it. Observed candidate list with one
   suspended opposing Digimon: `["player", "seed-perm-113"]`; expected `["player"]`. Auto-selection
   happens to take the first candidate, so the only visible symptom is the illegal choice offered to
   the controller. Not fixable inside the allowed files (needs a `attackPlayerOnly` field in
   `packages/shared` plus a line in `interpreter/actions/combat.ts`). Retained as `it.fails`.
   The same shape affects every "may attack a player" card in the repo (BT17-081, BT18-088, EX8-054,
   BT20-080, BT22-067), so the fix is one IR field, not a per-card patch.

2. **Fixed locally: `// @ts-nocheck` removed from `BT23-086.ts`.** The module typechecks without it,
   so future duration/field errors will now surface instead of being silently swallowed. No IR change
   was needed — every clause already matches the printed text (`nameOrTrait` trait matching for
   `[Zaxon]`/`[Machine]`, `levels: [6]`, `toTop: false`, `faceUp: true`, `abortOnDecline`).

## Rules note

Paying the end-of-turn suspend cost with no eligible level 6 Digimon is legal: comprehensive rules
15-7-5 lets the controller execute an optional processing condition even when the processing after it
cannot be executed. The negative test asserts Yuugo suspends and no attack happens, rather than
treating the suspension as a bug.
