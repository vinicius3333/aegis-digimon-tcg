## Card summary

Yellow/Black level 5, play cost 8, DP 8000, traits Mutant / Hudie / CS / Angel.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[CS] trait: Cost 3` — alternate evolution.
2. `[DNA Digivolve] Yellow Lv.4 + black/blue Lv.4: Cost 0`.
3. `[When Digivolving]` Until your opponent's turn ends, give 1 of their Digimon
   "[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks."
4. `[When Digivolving]` Then, if DNA digivolving, ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon.
5. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` When this Digimon would leave the battle area other than by your
   effects, you may play 1 level 4 or lower yellow, black or [CS] trait Digimon card from its
   digivolution cards without paying the cost.
6. `[Inherited]` The same [All Turns] clause.

The catalog text matches the official card list, including the two special-evolution headings
restored in commit 91fb49da8. No catalog discrepancy found.

Q5278 and Q5279 both state the pool is "yellow or black, OR [CS]" (an OR, not an AND). The IR
already encodes that as `filter` (colours) plus `orFilters` (trait). Both branches are proven
behaviourally with cards that satisfy exactly one of them.

## Evolution routes proven

- Normal, yellow: BT1-051 Reppamon base, pays 4 to 0, stack is exactly the base instance, one
  evolution-bonus draw, deck reduced by one.
- Normal, black: BT10-062 Golemon, same endpoints.
- CS alternate: BT22-047 Kuwagamon (green level 4, [CS]) pays 3 to 0. Green satisfies no printed
  EvoCost, so this isolates the alternate.
- Alternate rejections: level-3 [CS] (BT23-037) and level-4 non-CS (BT1-051). Both leave the base
  top card, the hand card and memory unchanged.
- DNA: no player intent declares a DNA digivolution, so the public route is BT23-027 Angemon's
  [On Play], which DNA digivolves two of its controller's Digimon into a Shakkoumon in hand.
  Angemon is the yellow level 4 material; the partner is BT23-050 (black) or BT23-018 (blue).
  Endpoints asserted: both material instances in the resulting stack, Shakkoumon on top, exactly
  one battle-area permanent, memory 10 -> 5 (Angemon's play cost 5, DNA cost 0), and both draws
  (Angemon's effect draw and the DNA evolution bonus) in hand in order.
- Illegal DNA pair: BT23-037 Tentomon is level 3. The DNA does not happen, Shakkoumon stays in
  hand, both Digimon stay on the battle area and memory is 5 — that is, no legacy ordinary-
  evolution fallback cost is charged. This is the negative the reconciliation note asked for.

## What replaced the previous suite

The committed `BT23-032.test.ts` was six cases: five pure IR-shape assertions and three
`deletePermanent` replacement cases. The new suite keeps the two shape assertions that state the
printed contract, keeps every behaviour the old cases covered (own-stack scoping against an
unrelated friendly stack, the inherited copy, an ineligible stack card going to trash) and adds
the evolution routes, the DNA proofs, the grant lifecycle, the De-Digivolve condition, the battle
and own-effect leave causes and the refusal branch. No assertion was weakened.

## Defects found and fixed

`BT23-032.ts` carried a file-level `// @ts-nocheck`. It was unnecessary: removing it leaves
`pnpm --filter @aegis/api typecheck` clean. The IR itself needed no change — the OR pool,
the `otherThanYourEffect` leave cause and the granted effect text all match the printed card
and the rulings.

## Retained red

`it.fails("Q6250 plays a source card after ＜Barrier＞ prevents the battle deletion")`.

Expected: with BT23-027 Angemon among its digivolution cards, accepting ＜Barrier＞ keeps
Shakkoumon on the battle area and the controller may still play Angemon from the digivolution
cards with the [All Turns] effect.
Actual: Barrier consumes the security card and Shakkoumon survives, but no source-play choice is
offered and Angemon stays in the stack.
Suspected seam: the Barrier prevention path in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts` resolves
the prevention without re-entering the `wouldLeavePlay` replacement registered by
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts`. This is the same open item
recorded against BT23-027 and owned by the engine lane; no fix attempted here.

## Remaining gaps

- Once-per-turn is proven only as a declaration. A direct or inherited leave removes the
  permanent, so a second occurrence of the same replacement on the same permanent within one turn
  has no observable route; the reset on the next own turn is likewise unobservable. If the
  coordinator knows a card that returns a permanent to the battle area and re-triggers a leave in
  the same turn, that case can be added.
- Q5277 uses `advance().ledgers.continuous.addRestriction` to make the recipient unaffected,
  following the EX12-016 / Q6740 precedent, because no simple card grants an unconditional
  "isn't affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects" at a usable level. The grant handout and
  the missing activation are both asserted through the real turn loop.
- The clause-3 turn-loop case cannot use `advance().runTurn(1)`'s awaited form: the forced attack
  fires before seat 1's Main phase opens for input and the resulting memory swing ends that turn
  immediately, so `waitForMainPhase` cannot succeed. The test runs the production turn and settles
  on `turnEnded`, then asserts exactly one `attackDeclared` by the granted permanent against the
  player, seat 0's security down to 2, and the grant gone once the turn has ended.
- The inherited (Q5279) case is proven with the colour branch only; the trait branch is proven for
  the direct copy.
- The banlist entry (restricted to 1 copy since 2026-04-04) is not exercised; deck validation is
  outside this card's module.
