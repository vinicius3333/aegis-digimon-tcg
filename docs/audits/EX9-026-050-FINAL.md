## Historical residual reconciliation

The historical “multiple physical intrinsic reducer copies in hand” concern is
not introduced by EX9-026..050: only EX9-030, EX9-031, EX9-036, EX9-041 and
EX9-044 use reducer IR in this range, and their focused tests exercise payment,
source counts, route filters, or shared activation limits. The specific reducer
copy reproduction in the checkpoints concerns BT22-076 and its shared intrinsic
registry, outside this range. No shared engine change was made here and no
EX9 card is used as evidence that BT22-076 is resolved.

The historical “direct stack/under-Tamer/linked enumeration” concern does not
apply to an EX9-030 printed payment. Its reducer cost filter is explicitly
`zone: "hand"`; its On Play/When Digivolving placement cost is explicitly
`zone: "trash"` and `kind: "Digimon"`. Neither cost can legally select a card
from a digivolution stack, an under-Tamer stack, or a linked list, and no
EX9-026..050 effect creates a play-from-stack or play-from-linked-card route
that would make EX9-030 the imminent candidate. The focused tests prove the
hand-origin and trash-origin routes, including the effect-play and
security-origin route, while the IR filters rule out those other zones by
construction. This is a scope argument for this card, not a resolution of the
shared engine’s general direct-stack/linked enumeration risk.

The focused tests cover the security-origin and ordering cases exercised by
EX9-030 and EX9-031, along with the other security clauses through public
combat or deletion flows. This report does not generalize those card-specific
fixtures into a claim about every possible security-source engine path.
