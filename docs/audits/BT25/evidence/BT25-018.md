# BT25-018 Apollomon

## Contract and sources

Catalog `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` records Apollomon as level 6, 12000 DP, with TS alternate evolution. Its play replacement reduces the play cost by 5 when the opponent has a Digimon at 12000 DP or more. On Play/When Digivolving, all opponent Digimon get -2000 DP for each of your Digimon for the turn, then one opponent Digimon at or below Apollomon's live DP is deleted. At the end of your turn, two of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into GraceNovamon, then one of your Digimon may attack. Inherited When Attacking is once per turn and deletes one opponent Digimon at or below this Digimon's DP. KB Q6267 requires the zero-DP rule check after the whole entry effect; Q6268/Q6269 require the follow-up attack after accepted or declined DNA.
