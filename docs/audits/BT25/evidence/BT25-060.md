# BT25-060 Rebootmon

## Contract and sources

The committed catalog identifies Rebootmon as a green/white level 6, 12000 DP God/Appmon Digimon with Security Attack +1, Reboot, and Link +1. Its App Fusion requirement is Bootmon plus Shutmon for cost 0. When Digivolving/When Attacking Once Per Turn, it may link one Appmon trait card with a Link requirement from hand or its own digivolution cards without paying, then one of your Digimon may unsuspend. All Turns Once Per Turn, when it links or unsuspends, it gains Piercing, Blocker, and immunity to opponent Digimon effects until your turn ends.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT25-060` returned Q6357 (a linked Appmon must itself have Link) and Q6358–Q6363 (chosen-but-unaffected behavior, immunity timing, and trigger suppression). The focused suite also exercises exact App Fusion link names, link/no-Link boundaries, public attack/security handling, opponent-effect immunity, and real turn-duration/reset paths.
