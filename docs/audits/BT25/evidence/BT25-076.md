# BT25-076 Ghoulmon — integration evidence

The committed catalog identifies Ghoulmon as a black level 6 Mega with ordinary Black Lv5 evolution cost 3, play cost 12, and Rush, Reboot, and Blocker. Its play-time reduction deletes one own play-cost-11-or-lower Digimon with Negamon in its text and a Negamon-named card in its stack, reducing by that deleted card's printed play cost. On Play, When Attacking, and On Deletion each delete the opponent's lowest-play-cost Digimon; if no deletion occurs, the opponent's top security is trashed.
