# BT25-070 Logamon audit evidence

## Contract and sources

- Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` identifies Logamon as a level 4 Black/Purple Digimon, play cost 5, DP 6000, `[App Fusion] [Offmon] & [Hackmon]: Cost 0`, ordinary evolution from Black or Purple level 3 for 3, and Link requirement `[Appmon] trait: Cost 2`.
- Printed Main clause: once per turn, optionally link one Social, Tool, or Game Digimon from the owner's trash or this Digimon's digivolution cards, with the Link cost reduced by 1.
- Printed Your Turn clause: once per turn when this Digimon gets linked, delete one opposing Digimon with play cost 4 or less.
- Printed Link clause: when linking, one opposing Digimon or Tamer cannot unsuspend until their turn ends.
- Local KB query: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT25-070` returns Q6367, which confirms that a card without `<Link>` cannot be selected by the Main effect.
- Direct IR: `BT25-070.ts` uses only `registerIrCard("BT25-070", compiled)`. The Main link is optional, once per turn, cost-reduced, and limited to the owner's trash/host stack and the Social/Tool/Game trait families; the linked restriction and Your Turn deletion are separate once-per-turn effects.
