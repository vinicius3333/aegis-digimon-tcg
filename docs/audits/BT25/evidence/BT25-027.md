# BT25-027 MachGaogamon

## Printed contract

The committed catalog record (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`) identifies MachGaogamon as a blue/black level 5 Ultimate Digimon, play cost 7, 7000 DP, with the Cyborg and DATA SQUAD traits. It has alternate `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[DATA SQUAD] trait: Cost 3`. Its main effects are `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may return 1 of your opponent's level 4 or lower Digimon to the hand. Then, by trashing the bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, this Digimon unsuspends` and `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by trashing the bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, it doesn't leave`. The inherited effect applies the same protection to a Digimon with Gaogamon in its name or the DATA SQUAD trait.

The applicable comprehensive rules are 8-1-2-1 and 8-1-3-1 for evolution requirements, 15-10-2-1 for exact target counts and limits, and the trigger/replacement timing sections governing When Digivolving, When Attacking, and would-leave-play effects.
