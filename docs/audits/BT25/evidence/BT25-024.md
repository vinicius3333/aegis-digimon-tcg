## Printed contract

The committed catalog record (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`) identifies Lekismon as a blue level 4 Champion Digimon, play cost 4, 5000 DP, with the Beastkin, Iliad, and TS traits. Its alternate evolution requirement is level 3 with TS trait at cost 2, independently of color. Ordinary evolution accepts blue or red level 3 at cost 2. The baseline catalog incorrectly said hand. Astra corrected it to trash using the [official English card text](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522036&search=true), and synchronized the BT25 effects after the IR correction. Its effects are `[On Play] [When Digivolving] Draw 1` and `[Your Turn] When your Digimon are played or digivolve, if any of them are red, this Digimon may digivolve into Crescemon in the trash with the cost reduced by 1`. Its inherited effect is Jamming.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT25-024` returned Q6287 and Q6288. Q6287 establishes that the watcher triggers on all own Digimon play/digivolution events but can activate only when the resulting Digimon is red. Q6288 establishes that the digivolution event references the Digimon after it digivolves. The applicable comprehensive rules are 8-1-2-1 and 8-1-3-1 for evolution requirements, 15-8-3-9-1/2 for trigger versus activation conditions, and 15-16-3-1 for the When Digivolving timing.
