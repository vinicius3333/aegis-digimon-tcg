# BT25-028 Dianamon

## Printed contract

The committed catalog record identifies BT25-028 as a blue/purple level 6, 12000 DP, play-cost 12 Dianamon with Shaman, Olympos XII, Iliad, and TS traits. Its alternate evolution is level 5 with TS for cost 3. The play replacement reduces its cost by 5 when the opponent has a level 6 or higher Digimon. On Play/When Digivolving, opposing Digimon with 1 or fewer digivolution cards cannot suspend until the opponent's turn ends, then one unsuspended opposing Digimon is deleted. All Turns Once Per Turn, when any Digimon is played or digivolves, it may trash any four opposing digivolution cards, then two of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into GraceNovamon from hand. The inherited When Attacking Once Per Turn effect prevents one opposing Digimon or Tamer from suspending until the opponent's turn ends.
