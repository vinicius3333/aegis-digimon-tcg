# BT25-037 Pegasusmon

The committed catalog identifies Pegasusmon as a yellow/blue level 4 Armor Form Digimon, 6000 DP, Holy Beast/Iliad/TS, play cost 6. Its alternate evolution routes are Patamon at cost 2 and level 3 TS at cost 2. It has Armor Purge. On Play and When Digivolving add the top security card to hand, then may place an Angel, Archangel, Three Great Angels, or Iliad Digimon, or a TS Tamer, from hand as the top or bottom security card. KB Q6304 confirms the effect still activates at zero security; when no card can be added, the specified hand card is placed as security.

`BT25-037.ts` is IR-only with one `registerIrCard("BT25-037", compiled)` registration. The IR models both alternate routes, both public timings, top-security payment, optional top/bottom placement, and Armor Purge. The stale `@ts-nocheck` and generator guidance were removed.
