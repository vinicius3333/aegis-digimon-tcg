# BT25-026 Crescemon

## Contract and sources

The committed catalog records Blue, level 5, 7000 DP, play cost 6, standard level 4 Blue or Red evolution for 3, and alternate level 4 TS evolution for 3. Printed behavior is On Play/When Digivolving trashing the bottom three evolution cards of one opposing Digimon, then restricting one of their Digimon with no evolution cards from suspending until their turn ends. During the owner's turn, when one of their Digimon is played or digivolves, if any triggering Digimon is red, Crescemon may digivolve into Dianamon in its owner's trash with the cost reduced by 2. The inherited Your Turn text prevents this Digimon's attack target from changing.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT25-026` returns Q6290 (the watcher triggers even for non-red plays/evolutions but can activate only when the subject is red) and Q6291 (the subject is the post-evolution Digimon). Relevant comprehensive rules include 11-2-7 for attack-target changes.
