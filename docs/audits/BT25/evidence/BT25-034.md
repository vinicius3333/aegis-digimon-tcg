# BT25-034 Angemon

The committed catalog identifies Angemon as a yellow level 4 Champion, 5000 DP Angel/Iliad/TS with alternate `[Digivolve] Lv.3 w/[TS] trait: Cost 2`. Its security effect is: when an effect trashes this card from the security stack, you may play one level 4 or lower Angel or Iliad Digimon from hand without paying its cost. It has Ascension, and its inherited effect is Barrier. KB Q6298 states that the effect only activates when the card is directly trashed from security by an effect, not when revealed or searched.

`BT25-034.ts` is IR-only with one `registerIrCard("BT25-034", compiled)` registration. The effect is scoped to `OnDiscardSecurity`, with an optional free play from hand limited to level 4 or lower Angel/Iliad Digimon. The stale `@ts-nocheck` and generator guidance were removed.

Focused behavioral evidence covers:

- exact security discard target filter and free-play semantics;
- public direct security trash and public attack security trash, with the eligible hand card entering play and Angemon entering trash;
- Q6298 negative reveal path, where a security reveal leaves Angemon in security and does not play the hand card;
- Q6298 negative real security-check path, where the revealed Angemon is trashed without activating the effect;
- distinct Angel-only and Iliad-only level-4 positives, with level-5, wrong-trait, and Iliad Tamer candidates rejected;
- legal TS alternate evolution at cost 2, Ascension presence, and rejection of a non-TS level 3;
- real Ascension activation after battle deletion, with an explicit decision selecting the card and exact top-security placement;
- inherited Barrier through a legal BT25-039 over BT25-034 stack, including accepted payment, refusal, and no-security deletion.
