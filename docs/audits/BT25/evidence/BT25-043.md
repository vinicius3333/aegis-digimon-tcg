# BT25-043 Habakirimon

## Contract and sources

The committed catalog identifies Habakirimon/Habakiri as a yellow level 6 Digimon/Option with Shaman, Glowing Dawn, and BEATBREAK traits. Its ordinary evolution is Yellow Lv.5 for cost 4 and its alternate evolution is Lv.5 Glowing Dawn for cost 3. When Digivolving/When Attacking Once Per Turn, it recovers one, trashes the top security card of a player with the most security, then unsuspends if that processing succeeded. Its All Turns Once Per Turn replacement protects all of the controller's Glowing Dawn Digimon by trashing the controller's top security. Its Main Option effect gives one opposing Digimon -8000 for the turn, then optionally trashes the controller's top security for -5000 to all opposing Digimon.

Catalog-verified ordinary-evolution fixtures are BT1-058 (Yellow Lv.5, non-Glowing Dawn) and BT24-076 (Purple-only Lv.5, non-Glowing Dawn); public tests evolve from each at exact cost 4. The purple-only route includes a separate BT25-041 Glowing Dawn permanent to satisfy the card's color-waiver condition. The retained alternate proof uses BT25-041 as a legal Lv.5 Glowing Dawn source at cost 3.
