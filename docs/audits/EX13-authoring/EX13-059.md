# EX13-059 BigMamemon — authoring audit

## Card summary

Black Lv.5 Ultimate Digimon, [Mutant] / Data, play cost 8, DP 8000.
Single printed EvoCost: Black Lv.4 for 3. No [Digivolve], [Assembly], [DNA],
[DigiXros] or [Link] header; no security effect.

Printed clauses:

1. `[When Digivolving] [On Deletion] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You
   may play 1 play cost 7 or lower Digimon card with [Mamemon] in its name or
   the [Mutant] trait among them without paying the cost. Trash the rest.`
2. `[End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your Digimon with
   [Mamemon] in its name, delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest
   play cost.`
3. `[Inherited] [End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] By deleting 1 of your
   Digimon with [Mamemon] in its name, delete all of your opponent's Digimon
   with the lowest play cost.`

## Knowledge-base check

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-059` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`.
**None — pre-release**, as the brief predicts for the whole set.

General rules consulted in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` (via
`tools/kb/query.mjs rules`):

- §4-22-1 card names — "with [X] in its name" is the substring reading. This
  is load-bearing twice over: it admits MetalMamemon / PrinceMamemon to both
  the reveal slot and the deletion cost, **and** it makes BigMamemon itself a
  legal cost for its own clause 2 (the text says "1 of your Digimon", not "1 of
  your *other* Digimon", so there is no `excludeSelf`).
- §4-22-2 traits — "with the [Mutant] trait" is the exact-trait form, so
  `match: "trait"`, never `traitContains`.
- §8-8 "without paying the cost" — a full waiver, i.e. `to: "play"` with no
  `costDelta`.
- §2-6 play cost — the printed cost, which is what both `playCostLte: 7` and
  the `lowestPlayCost` superlative compare.

### Deliberate non-entries

- `allowCostWithoutTarget` is **off**: no ruling permits paying the deletion
  cost with nothing to delete. Proved by "does not pay the cost when the
  opponent has no Digimon to delete" — the fodder survives untouched.
- No `excludeSelf` on the cost target (see §4-22-1 above).
- No `sharedUseKey`: the two [Once Per Turn] budgets are printed separately on
  the battle-area clause and the inherited clause, so they are independent.

## Remaining ambiguity

- **Exact vs. substring trait matching is not separable on this card.** The
  only cards whose traits *contain* "Mutant" without being exactly `[Mutant]`
  are the two AncientWisemon printings (BT12-071, BT18-074) at play cost 12
  and 13, so `playCostLte: 7` already excludes them. The encoding follows the
  printed "with the [Mutant] trait" wording and EX13-046's accepted reading of
  the same phrase, but the test suite cannot discriminate `match: "trait"` from
  `traitContains` here. Stated rather than claimed as proven.
- **The "Digimon card" gate is likewise unprovable.** No non-Digimon card in
  the catalog carries `[Mamemon]` in its name or the `[Mutant]` trait, so
  `kind: ["Digimon"]` has no counter-example to refuse.
- Whether declining a `[Once Per Turn]` "by …" cost window spends the use is
  the open question the coordinator's notes already record for
  `CardEffect.frequency`; this card's tests never assert that half.
