# EX13-045 Examon — authoring audit

## Card summary

Green/Red/Blue Lv.7 Mega Digimon, Data, [Holy Warrior]/[Royal Knight], 15000 DP, play cost 15.
Catalog EvoCosts: Green Lv.6 / Red Lv.6 / Blue Lv.6, memory 5 each. No inherited effect and no
security effect are printed.

Printed clauses:

1. `[DNA Digivolve] Green Lv.6 + Blue Lv.6 : Cost 0`
2. `＜Raid＞`
3. `＜Piercing＞`
4. `＜Security A. +1＞`
5. `＜Blocker＞`
6. `＜Evade＞`
7. `[When Digivolving] If DNA digivolving, this Digimon attacks and all of your Digimon get
   +10000 DP until your opponent's turn ends. Then, this Digimon may battle 1 of your opponent's
   Digimon.`
8. `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon wins a battle, you may play or use 1 play or use
   cost 12 or lower [Dracomon] or [Examon] text card from your hand or its digivolution cards
   without paying the cost.`

## Authoring decisions

- **Action order inside clause 7.** The printed sentence joins the attack and the DP buff with
  "and", so both land in the same step, but the interpreter runs actions sequentially and an
  `Attack` action resolves the whole battle inline. The buff therefore comes FIRST, otherwise the
  +10000 DP would arrive after the very battle it is printed to support. `Then,` keeps the
  `Battle` last, where the printed text puts it.
- **The condition is on every action**, not only the first: "If DNA digivolving" gates the whole
  clause, and a normal digivolve into Examon must not attack, buff, or battle (proven by the
  non-DNA negative).
- **`source: "thisDigimon"`** is what makes "its digivolution cards" self-scoped; it narrows only
  the hosted zones (`targeting/loose.ts:327`), so the hand half of the pool stays open.
- **No `printedTextOnly`.** The REVIEW-NOTES text-self-match seam applies to live battle-area
  permanents whose own stack text widens a `match: "text"` filter. Every candidate here is a loose
  card in hand or under this Digimon, matched against its own definition, so the flag would be
  inert.
- **`allowMultiColor: true`** on the Option branch: the printed sentence carries no single-colour
  restriction and the catalog's [Examon]-text Options include the two-colour EX3-070. The Option's
  own colour requirement is still enforced by `optionColorRequirementMet`.
- **Digi-Eggs excluded by kind, not by cost.** EX13-005 Bebydomon is a [Dracomon]-text Digi-Egg
  whose -1 cost would pass the 12 ceiling; it can never be played to the battle area.
- **The five `Static` keyword entries are load-bearing, not record-keeping.** Mutation-confirmed:
  deleting them turns four behavioural tests red (the two security checks, the ＜Raid＞ switch and
  the ＜Piercing＞ pass-through), so a compiled card's keywords come from its IR and not from the
  `PRINTED_MATCHERS` regex in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/keywords.ts`. This corrects the general
  expectation that a printed keyword needs no IR entry.

## Remaining ambiguity

- The relative order of "this Digimon attacks" and "all of your Digimon get +10000 DP" within the
  same sentence is an authoring reading (buff first), not a ruling. A pre-release Q&A that resolves
  the sentence the other way is a one-line reorder.
- The ＜Raid＞ redirect and the forced attack's own target choice are both engine-side selections;
  the forced-attack candidate list is `[player, ...legal enemy Digimon]` and the harness takes the
  first, so the "may battle" tests pin the behaviour by seeding the opponent's body SUSPENDED
  (no ＜Raid＞ candidate) rather than by answering a prompt by hand.
