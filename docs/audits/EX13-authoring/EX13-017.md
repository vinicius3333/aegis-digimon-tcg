# EX13-017 Veemon — authoring audit

## Card summary

Blue Lv.3 Rookie Digimon, Free attribute, [Mini Dragon] [CS], play cost 3, DP 1000,
printed blue Lv.2 digivolution route at cost 0.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[CS] trait: Cost 0` — an alternate digivolution requirement.
2. `[When Moving] [On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with
   [Veedramon] in its text or the [Royal Knight] trait among them to the hand. Return
   the rest to the bottom of the deck.`
3. Inherited: `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with [Veedramon] in its
   name would leave the battle area by your opponent's effects, by suspending it, it
   doesn't leave.`

## Knowledge-base check

- `node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-017` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. None —
  EX13 is pre-release (street date 2026-10-02), so no card-specific rulings exist.
- General rules consulted instead:
  - `comprehensive §15-15-3-5` (effects that reveal cards; a draw during a reveal comes
    from the unrevealed cards) — not exercised by this card, but it confirms the reveal
    window semantics the shared `RevealAdd` primitive owns.
  - `manual §1` quotes the identical template "Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add
    1 Tamer card or Option card among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of
    the deck", and states the activating player chooses the bottom order. That is exactly
    `rest: "deckBottom"`.
- Peer precedent: BT22-019 Veemon and BT22-022 Veedramon print the SAME inherited clause
  and BT22-019 prints the same `Lv.2 w/[CS] trait: Cost 0` alternate. This module reuses
  their accepted IR shape verbatim, so the Veemon line stays consistent.

Design notes recorded in the module:

- One `add` entry, not two. EX12-009 Wankomon uses two entries because it prints two
  INDEPENDENT trait clauses ("Add 1 … and 1 …"); EX13-017 prints a single "1 card with A
  or B", which is the union reading (KB Q2625), so one entry with two OR-matched
  `nameOrTrait` refs takes exactly one card.
- `match: "text"` is the full card-information union in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts`, so a card merely
  NAMED Veedramon also "has [Veedramon] in its text". Both readings are proved.
- "the [Royal Knight] trait" is the exact trait reading (`match: "trait"`), per the
  brief's names-and-traits rule, which is why [Royal Base] is refused.
- The add is mandatory (no "you may"), so no `optional`/`upTo`.

## Retained reds

None. `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`. No engine seam was needed: every clause maps
to an existing primitive (`RevealAdd`, `Replacement`/`Prevent`, `digivolutionRequirement`).

The REVIEW-NOTES per-card name-exclusion seam does NOT apply here: EX13-017 prints no
`[Rule] Name:` exclusion.

## Remaining ambiguity

- No Lv.3 card with the exact [Royal Knight] trait exists in the catalog, and no trait
  contains "Royal Knight" as a strict substring, so the exact-vs-substring trait
  discrimination is proved against the closest printed near-miss, [Royal Base]
  (BT18-044), rather than against a literal superstring trait.
- Both digivolution routes into EX13-017 cost 0, so `useAlternateCost: true` is proved by
  the base legality it unlocks (an off-color GREEN [CS] Lv.2) rather than by a cost delta.
- `[When Moving]` is reachable publicly only through `moveFromBreeding`; the breeding-area
  digivolve test confirms the timing does not fire while the card is still in breeding.
