pre-release**, so no card-specific rulings exist. General rules consulted in
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- §4-6 / §4-6-9 stacked cards — a card placed face down under a Tamer is one of that Tamer's

## Engine seams observed (no red retained; both were worked around inside the card lane)

1. **`canAttemptDigivolve` does not recognize a placement cost's `bindHostAs` as the
   pre-payment stand-in for `target.fromSelectionRef`.**
   `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolve.ts:156-164` has exactly this
   accommodation, but only for a `moveToBattleArea` cost's `target.bindAs`. A `Digivolve` action
   carrying `cost: compound([…, place with bindHostAs])` and
   `target.fromSelectionRef` therefore preflights against an **unbound** ref →
   `candidatePermanents` returns nothing → the effect is never offered (observed: the `[Main]`
   clause silently did nothing).
   Worked around the way BT26-097/BT26-098 already do — a `CostGatedBlock` owns the cost and the
   nested `Digivolve` runs after the binding exists. No card-side red. A generalization (accept a
   nested `place` cost's `bindHostAs`) would let the simpler single-action shape work, but the
   `CostGatedBlock` shape is the established in-tree idiom, so this is informational only.

2. **`canPayCost` does not evaluate `Target.distinctLevels`.**
   `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/costs.ts` (the `place` branch, ~line 404) counts raw
   matching candidates only. A single two-card component with `levels: [4, 5]` +
   `distinctLevels: true` therefore preflighted as payable on a trash holding **two level-4**
   materials, trashed the three face-down cards, and only then failed inside `pickLoose` —
   a partially paid all-or-nothing cost (§15-8-4-4-1). Observed directly during authoring
   (the "two level 4 Holy Beasts" test caught it).
   Avoided by splitting the printed "1 each of level 4 and level 5" into two level-pinned
   components, which is also the closer reading of the printed sentence and the BT26-098 shape.
   Worth noting for any future card whose placement cost relies on `distinctLevels`/
   `distinctNames` for payability rather than only for selection.

## Remaining ambiguity

- **Cost paid with no reachable [Kentaurosmon].** `CostGatedBlock` pays its cost before running
  the nested digivolve and has no preflight on that nested action, so accepting the optional
  prompt with no Kentaurosmon in hand or trash spends the three face-down cards and the two
  trash materials for nothing. This matches BT26-097's identical printed shape and is
  defensible under §15-8 (the activation cost is paid; the effect then resolves as much as
  possible), and the printed "may" still lets the controller decline. There is no condition kind
  that can gate on "a card matching a filter is in your hand **or** trash", so a tighter gate is
  not expressible today. Flagged rather than approximated.
- **Relative order of the two placed materials.** Each component places at the stack's current
  bottom, so the level-5 card (placed second) ends up below the level-4 one. The printed
  sentence fixes only that both are bottom digivolution cards of the same [Kudamon]; it does not
  order them relative to each other. The test asserts the observed order with that reasoning
  recorded inline.
