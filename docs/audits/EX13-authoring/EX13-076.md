## Knowledge base

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-076` → "(no knowledge-base entries)". None — EX13 is
pre-release. General rules consulted in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- §7-3 / §7-3-2 Assembly: trash-only materials, exact printed count (§7-3-2-4), flat printed
  reduction (§7-3-2-1), never mandatory (§7-3-2-9), leftmost material closest to the played card
  (§7-3-2-6).
- §14 battle: a "have this Digimon battle it" sentence is a direct comparison — no attack
  declaration, no security check; the loser, or both on a tie, is deleted.
- §16-35-1 / §16-35-4 ＜Iceclad＞: the rule that compares digivolution-card counts instead of DP,
  applying when EITHER battler carries it. This is the engine's only implementation of the printed
  last sentence of clause 4.
- §15-6-2: different processes in one effect do not inherit each other's processing conditions, so
  declining the leading "You may suspend" does not skip the "Then, you may …" half.
- §16-30 ＜Evade＞: suspending the Digimon prevents a battle deletion, and only while unsuspended.

### Implementation notes

- "Return all digivolution cards … the Digimon stays" is `ReturnTopDigivolutionCards` with
  `position: "bottom"`, not `Return.returnDigivolutionCardsFirst` (which returns the Digimon too)
  and not `DeDigivolve` (which trashes the top card, promotes a source, and floors at level 3).
  `cardsPerTarget` is a slice ceiling that the primitive further clamps to "always leave one card",
  so any value at or above the longest legal stack spells the printed "all".
- The single printed "may" covering three operations on ONE chosen Digimon is expressed by a
  leading `SelectBind` with `optional` + `abortOnDecline`: `SelectBind` is the only action that
  records a reusable binding for a later `fromSelectionRef`, and `abortOnDecline` is what makes one
  prompt gate all three siblings.

## Retained red

`it.fails("drops the count-comparison grant once the battle it was printed for is over")`.

The ＜Iceclad＞ grant implementing "in this battle" is installed for `untilEndOfBattle` →
`EffectDuration.UntilEndBattle`, which is the narrowest real `EffectDurationRef`. Nothing ever
sweeps that boundary after a DIRECT battle: `GameEngine.sweepCombatDurations()`
(`apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts:1705`) is the only caller of
`continuous.sweep(state, "endBattle", …)`, and it runs solely from `CombatController.cleanup()`
(`apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts:1836`) at the END OF AN ATTACK. A `Battle` action goes
through `forceBattle` → `CombatController.resolveBattle`, which never reaches `cleanup()`.
Expected: no ＜Iceclad＞ on the host once the battle is over. Actual: the grant is still live, so a
later battle in the same turn would also compare digivolution-card counts. Engine lane: sweep the
`endBattle` boundary per battle (or scope the grant to the battle that consumed it). Not fixable
inside a card module. `coverage: "partial"` with one `residual` entry.

## Remaining ambiguity

- §15-6-2 is read as keeping the two "may" halves of clause 4 independent (declining the suspend
  still offers the strip-and-battle). The alternative reading — "Then," chaining the halves — would
  be a one-field change (`abortOnDecline: true` on the `Suspend`). The structural assertion in the
  IR test pins the current reading; no behavioural test mixes the two answers, because the harness
  answers optional prompts with one global flag.
- "You may suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" carries no orientation predicate, matching
  EX13-041 / EX12-051; an already-suspended Digimon is therefore a legal, inert choice.
