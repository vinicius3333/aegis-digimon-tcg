### Filter discrimination

The `match: "text"` reference is proven to discriminate, not merely to fire, with three
fixtures in one reveal:

- `BT20-044` Breakdramon — matches **only** through its printed inherited text
  (`[Dracomon]/[Examon] in their text`); its own name carries neither token. This proves
  the reference reads text fields.
- `BT1-009` Monodramon — the near-match: it carries `dramon` but neither `Dracomon` nor
  `Examon`, and must be refused.
- `BT1-011` Agumon Expert — plain non-match.

A separate test uses `ST1-04` Dracomon (name-side match) alongside `BT20-044` to prove the
singular "Add 1 card" keeps the second match out of hand.

### Evolution-route proof

- Legal alternate route: breeding `EX13-005` Bebydomon (**green** Lv.2 — unreachable via
  the printed Red Lv.2 evoCost) + `useAlternateCost: true` → `{ ok: true }`, memory stays
  at 0 (Cost 0), the egg survives as the single digivolution card, and the bonus draw moves
  the one deck card into hand (deck emptied).
- Illegal source: breeding `BT1-007` Tanemon (same colour, same level, wrong name) +
  `useAlternateCost: true` → `ok: false`; the egg keeps its top card and Dracomon stays in
  hand.
- The alternate route is additive: the printed Red Lv.2 route from `BT1-001` still resolves
  with `useAlternateCost: false`.

### Evolution-stack proof for the inherited clause

Smallest legal stack that reaches clause 3: `BT20-044` Breakdramon with
`[EX13-008, BT12-022, BT20-025]` underneath, a second own Digimon `BT20-027` Slayerdramon,
and `BT20-045` Examon (`[Breakdramon] + [Slayerdramon]`) in hand. After the end-of-turn
firing, the battle area holds exactly one permanent, its top card is Examon, both material
permanent ids are gone, and the merged stack contains `EX13-008`, `BT20-044`, `BT20-027`,
`BT12-022` and `BT20-025` — source identity and inherited cards survive the transition.
Negatives: an opponent-controlled Slayerdramon is not "your other Digimon" (nothing fires),
declining the optional prompt through the real turn loop preserves both materials and the
hand card, and removing EX13-008 from the stack removes the grant entirely.

## Remaining ambiguity

- `EX13-008` is not imported by `apps/api/src/cards/EX13/index.ts`, which the brief forbids
  editing. The module registers itself when imported, and the test imports it directly
  (`import { compiled } from "./EX13-008.js"`), so the behavioural proof is complete — but
  the card will not be registered in a production bundle until the coordinator adds
  `import "./EX13-008.js";` to that index.
- "any of your other Digimon" (EX13-008, BT21-046, BT22-008) versus "another of your
  Digimon" (BT17-007) is encoded identically across the corpus; with `count: 2` the two
  wordings are behaviourally the same, so no distinction was introduced.
- `payCost: true` follows the printed text granting no waiver, matching BT21-046 and
  BT22-008. A pre-release errata could in principle waive it; no KB entry exists to check
  against.
