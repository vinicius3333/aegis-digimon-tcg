## KB check

- `node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-069` → "(no knowledge-base entries)". No card-specific
  rulings available; EX13 is pre-release.
- General rules consulted in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:
  - §6-2-1 / §6-2-1-1 — the turn player unsuspends **all** their Digimon and Tamers
    simultaneously at the start of the unsuspend phase, and start-of-turn processing runs
    before that unsuspend. This is why this Tamer can pay its own "by suspending this Tamer"
    cost off an unsuspend-phase trigger: it is itself already unsuspended at that instant.
  - §3-4-5-8 — breeding-area card information can't be referenced unless an effect says so,
    so "if you have a Digimon" is battle-area only.
  - glossary, ＜Digivolve＞ cost reduction — "the digivolve cost can't be reduced to less than
    zero", so `reduceCost: 2` floors at 0 instead of refunding memory.
- Naming conventions per the brief: "with [X] in its name" is a SUBSTRING match
  (`match: "name"`), not the bare-bracket exact form. Matches BT11-112 (the earlier Rina
  Shinomiya), which prints the identical "[Veemon] or [Veedramon] in its name" sentence.

## Remaining ambiguity

- "When any of your Digimon unsuspend" is plural in the printed text. During the unsuspend
  phase every Digimon unsuspends simultaneously (§6-2-1), and the engine fires the watcher
  once for that batch, which matches the single-use "by suspending this Tamer" cost (the
  Tamer can only be suspended once). A later ruling that the clause triggers once per
  unsuspending Digimon would change nothing observable, because the second instance could
  never pay its cost.
- Whether declining the cost window spends anything is moot here: the clause prints no
  `[Once Per Turn]`, so the decline test only asserts that nothing happened.
