## Knowledge-base check

- `node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-026` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. None — EX13 is
  pre-release, so no card-specific rulings exist.
- General rules consulted instead:
  - `comprehensive §4-3-2` — a card placed under a Tamer that already has cards under it goes
    to the BOTTOM. Matches the interpreter's `placeUnder` behavior, so nothing special is
    encoded.
  - `comprehensive §4-6-9` — a face-down card under a permanent is not public information. This
    is what `faceDown: true` buys, and the test asserts `faceUp === false` on the placed card.
  - `comprehensive §4-6-8` — cards under a Tamer are trashed with it. Not exercised; the clause
    does not depend on it.
  - `comprehensive §16-20 <Save>` — the keyword form of the same placement. EX13-026 spells the
    placement out in prose instead of carrying ＜Save＞, so no keyword is compiled.
- Borrowed rulings (binding on the identically shaped BT19-055 clause, cited in that module):
  KB Q3113 (do as much as possible) and KB Q3114 (with only ONE applicable card revealed it goes
  to the hand and cannot also go under a Tamer). Encoded as `requiresMinRevealed: 2`.

## Remaining ambiguity

- **`requiresMinRevealed: 2` is behaviourally inert for this clause shape.** Both add slots take
  `count: 1`, so when only one applicable card is revealed, slot 1 consumes it and slot 2 finds
  no remaining match either way. The flag therefore records the KB Q3114 intent (and keeps
  parity with BT19-055) but the observable endpoint is identical with or without it; the
  mutation run confirms only the IR-trace test catches its removal. Flagged rather than hidden.
- **`useAlternateCost` is a preference in BOTH directions.** Beyond the coordinator's note, the
  engine also ignores `useAlternateCost: false` when the printed route is illegal and the
  alternate is legal: the blue [DATA SQUAD] egg still digivolves. The route is pinned with
  `alternateRequirementIndex: 0` instead, and that named route is what refuses a
  non-[DATA SQUAD] Lv.2 source. Not an engine gap for this card, but worth carrying into the
  review notes.
- **Alternate and printed costs coincide (both memory 0).** Memory cannot separate the two
  routes; the source pool does. Noted so a future reader does not read the memory-0 assertions
  as proof of the alternate.
- `[When Moving]` fires on the real `moveFromBreeding` intent. The inherited `[When Attacking]`
  window is driven both by a real `attack` intent (one test) and by
  `advance().fireForPermanent(OnUseAttack, …)` (the once-per-turn and scope tests), following
  the EX13-005 precedent for repeated attack windows within one turn.
