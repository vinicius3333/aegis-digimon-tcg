### Decisions worth recording

- **`nameExact` for ＜Decode ([Sistermon Blanc])＞.** The bare bracketed name is the exact
  reading (§4-22-1), and it matters here: this very card and BT7-082 are both named
  "Sistermon Blanc (Awakened)", which a substring read would wrongly pull out of the stack.
  BT24-027 (`＜Decode ([Calmaramon])＞`) is the shape followed; BT24-014's older
  `match: "name"` spelling is looser than its own printed text.
- **Substring `match: "name"` for the Option's "card with [Sistermon]".** The scraped
  `optionEffect` string lost the qualifier. The scrape is not lossy about "in text" — the same
  card's own `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[Huckmon] in text` line keeps it — so the missing qualifier is
  read as "in its name", the substring reading every earlier printing of this sentence uses
  (BT23-099, BT23-013, BT13-017, BT6-016). The exact reading would be dead text: no card is
  named "Sistermon". See Remaining ambiguity.
- **No `kind` on the Option's free play.** The printed word is "card", not "Digimon card"
  (contrast EX13-028), so a [Sistermon] Option such as BT23-099 also qualifies.
- **No `abortOnDecline`.** §15-6-2 and the absence of "if you did": declining the optional play
  must not stop the DP reduction. Proven by the decline test.
- **＜Guard＞ is an explicit `Replacement`, not a bare keyword.** Following the coordinator note
  and EX13-052 / EX12-056 / EX12-072: `combat/keywords.ts` only tokenizes ＜Guard＞.
- **No inherited copies of anything.** The card prints no inherited text.

## Retained reds / gaps

**No engine gap found.** One **catalog** gap, retained as a documented negative-only proof
rather than an `it.fails`:

- **Clause 2 positive is unprovable with today's catalog.** `[Digivolve] Lv.2 w/[Huckmon] in
  text: Cost 1` needs a level-2 card (level 2 is exactly the Digi-Egg kind in this catalog)
  carrying the `[Huckmon]` token anywhere in its printed information. A scan of every
  `level === 2` record in `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` over `nameEn`,
  `effectText`, `inheritedEffectText` and `types` returns **zero** matches — EX13 is only
  60/77 revealed and the set's Huckmon-line Digi-Egg (EX13-004 is an unimported number) has not
  landed. The route is therefore proven only by (a) the published requirement and (b) a Lv.2
  Digi-Egg without `[Huckmon]` being refused. **Coordinator action: re-run the positive leg once
  the missing EX13 Digi-Egg is imported.** No code change is expected — `texts`/`level` on a
  `DigivolutionRequirement` is the already-accepted encoding (EX12-014, EX13-066).

## Remaining ambiguity

- The printed qualifier after "card with [Sistermon]" in the Option text is missing from the
  community-scraped `optionEffect` (the sibling EX13-066 is scraped identically). Implemented as
  the substring **name** reading for the reasons above. If the released card prints "in its
  text" instead, the fix is one field (`match: "name"` → `"text"`) plus flipping the BT13-009
  leg of the discriminator test; nothing structural changes.
- Whether a card merely *named* with `[Sistermon]` but of a non-Digimon kind (BT23-099, an
  Option) is legally "played" free by clause 5a is untested here — the filter deliberately
  carries no `kind`, matching the printed word "card", but no assertion pins Option-kind
  behaviour because the free play of an Option immediately re-enters option resolution and would
  confound the DP endpoint. Flagged, not claimed.
