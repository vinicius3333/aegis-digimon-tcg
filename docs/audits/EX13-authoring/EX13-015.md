General rules consulted via `node tools/kb/query.mjs rules "…"` and
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- **§7-3 / §7-3-2 / §2-3-10 Assembly** — materials come from the **trash** only; the specified
  number must be placed; the play cost is reduced by the printed amount.
- **§7-3-2-6** — "cards placed for Assembly ... are placed in order from top to bottom so that the
  card shown on the left side of the Assembly requirements goes on top". This fixes the expected
  stack order for the Lv.5 / Lv.4 / Lv.3 slots.
- **§11-3 Counter Timing** — a `[Counter]` effect is activated by the **defending** seat inside the
  open counter window, which is why the fourth copy of the delete body is `trigger: "Counter"` and
  is exercised through a real `respondCounter` intent.
- **§16-39 ＜Progress＞** — "this Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's effects while
  attacking", scoped to its own in-flight attack (§16-39-3).
- **＜Raid＞ (engine: `combat/controller.ts`)** — evaluated at declaration; switches the target to
  the opponent's highest-DP **unsuspended** Digimon.

### Cross-card and evolution-stack checks

- Digivolution: the printed EvoCost (Red Lv.5 for 3) is proven through the public `digivolve`
  intent with the source kept as the single digivolution card, DP 12000, and the bonus draw.
  Illegal sources are rejected on **both** the normal and `useAlternateCost: true` routes: a
  **blue** Lv.5 (`BT4-046` WarGrowlmon — right name, wrong color) and a **red Lv.4**
  (`BT2-013` Growlmon — right color, wrong level). No `digivolutionRequirement` is emitted,
  because the catalog row is the printed EvoCost and no `[Digivolve]` header is printed
  (the EX10-058 note).

  family card per slot (`BT2-017` WarGrowlmon — qualifies only because "Growlmon" is a substring,

  (`BT1-014` Kokatorimon), and a level collision (two Lv.3s).

  `ifThisEffectDidNotDelete` pairing follows EX13-013 / BT19-015; `leaveCause:

## Remaining ambiguity

- The printed clause 6 does not say "you may", so the prevention cost is encoded as mandatory.
  Whether the controller may decline to pay (leaving the Digimon to go) is the kind of question a
  card-specific Q&A normally settles; none exists pre-release. The engine's `prevent` path still
  prompts for the cost target, so a controller with several eligible Digimon chooses which one
  pays, which is the uncontroversial part.
- ＜Raid＞'s redirect is offered as an optional prompt by the engine (`selectOptionalInstance`),
  matching its printed "you may" in the keyword's reminder text; no EX13 ruling exists to confirm
  it for this card specifically.
