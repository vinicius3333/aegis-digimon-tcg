Green/Red Lv.5 Ultimate, Virus, [Earth Dragon], 7000 DP, play cost 7, rarity C,
EvoCost Green Lv.4 / Red Lv.4 for 4, max 4 in deck.

Printed main text:

1. `[Digivolve] [Coredramon]: Cost 3`

   Then, 1 of their Digimon or Tamers can't unsuspend in their next unsuspend phase.`

## Knowledge-base check

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-041` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. No
card-specific rulings exist; EX13 is pre-release. General rules consulted in
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- §6-1/§6-2/§6-2-1 — the unsuspend phase unsuspends all of the turn player's
  Digimon and Tamers at once. This is the single window clause 3's lock has to
  survive into and then expire with.
- §15-7-1/§15-7-2 — only an explicit "By X, Y" optional processing condition
  gates the text that follows it. Clause 3's first sentence is a mandatory
  Suspend with no "By", so the "Then" half is not conditioned on it and needs no
  `ifThisEffectActed` guard.
- §4-23-1/§4-23-2 — a Digimon gains its digivolution cards' *effects*, not their
  printed *text*. This is what scopes "with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in their
  texts" to each candidate's own card, and is the basis for `printedTextOnly`.
- Glossary `＜Reboot＞` / `＜Fortitude＞` (§16-27) for the two keyword mechanics
  touched.

### Decisions worth recording

- **Two independent targets, not one.** Every near-twin prints "It can't
  unsuspend" and therefore binds the second target (`sameTarget: true` on
  EX9-037/EX9-038, a selection ref on EX10-019). This card re-opens the choice
  with "1 of their Digimon or Tamers", matching BT20-042 Groundramon's identical
  sentence, so both targets are plain and independent. The behavioural test
  lands the Suspend on a Digimon and the lock on a Tamer to prove it.
- **`unsuspendDuringOwnUnsuspendPhase`, not `unsuspend`.** The printed lock is
  scoped to "their next unsuspend phase", so effect-driven unsuspends and an
  opponent-turn ＜Reboot＞ stay legal. `GameEngine.unsuspendAllForSeat` reads
  exactly this restriction for the phase sweep. EX9-037/EX9-038 — the only other
  printings of this wording — use the same pairing; BT20-042/LM-012/EX10-019
  correctly use the broad `unsuspend` because their wording is the whole-turn
  "can't unsuspend until the end of their turn". The test asserts the broad form
  is absent and that `advance().verb.unsuspend` still works while the lock holds.
- **`printedTextOnly: true` on the inherited `sourceFilter`** — not an engine
  gap (REVIEW-NOTES "NOT a gap — read before flagging a text self-match case").
  This card's own inherited line literally prints "[Dracomon] or [Examon]", so
  without the flag `permanentMatchesFilter`'s live-text branch would read those
  tokens off the carrier's digivolution stack and match every winner
  unconditionally. LM-012 Lamortmon prints the same inherited sentence with a
  different token and is the prior art for this exact flag on a
  `whenDeletesInBattle` sourceFilter.
- **No `isSelfRef` on that sourceFilter.** The clause says "any of your
  Digimon", so the watcher is board-wide over the controller's side. Contrast
  BT20-042, whose own inherited line says "this Digimon".
- **Merged DNA stack order.** `＜Blast DNA Digivolve＞` places the hand-side
  material at the BOTTOM of the merged stack:
  `["BT20-027", "BT20-040", "EX13-041"]` under Examon. Asserted as observed
  (EX13-021's twin test reads the same way with the roles swapped).

## Remaining ambiguity

- Whether the printed lock should also stop an opponent-turn ＜Reboot＞ unsuspend
  is untested here because ＜Reboot＞ fires in the *controller's* unsuspend
  phase, not "their next unsuspend phase", so the two windows never overlap for
  this card. The chosen restriction leaves ＜Reboot＞ legal, which matches the
  printed scope.
- The lock's phase window is proved with `advance().runTurn`, which is the
  production turn machine, but the "survives an intervening own turn" leg relies
  on the seat-relative `UntilNextUntap` sweep behaviour that EX9-037 also
  depends on; if a future engine change makes that sweep seat-agnostic both
  cards fail together.
- No KB rulings exist for this card (pre-release), so the "two independent
  targets" reading rests on the printed English plus BT20-042's identical
  sentence rather than on a ruling.
