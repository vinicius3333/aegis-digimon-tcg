## Knowledge-base check

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-035` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. **None — EX13 is
pre-release**, so no card-specific rulings exist. General rules consulted in
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- **§4-22-1** (card names): "with [X] in its name" is the SUBSTRING reading. ChuuChuumon,
  PlatinumSukamon, KingSukamon, MetalEtemon and this card itself all match, which is why every
  name reference is `match: "name"` rather than `nameExact`.
- **§3-4-7-5 / §3-4-7-8**: cards in a breeding area can neither be chosen by nor have their
  information referenced by an effect that does not name the breeding area. Both C4's count and
  C4's aura target are therefore battle-area scoped.
- **§6-4 / §4-17**: an effect-driven free play still enters the battle area as a play, so the
  played card's own [On Play] window fires — `PlayWithoutCost`'s normal contract.

Peer precedent used in place of rulings: **BT13-076 KingEtemon** (the first printing of this card)
prints the identical `Lv.5 w/[Etemon] or [Sukamon] in name: Cost 4` header and the same
"-3000 DP and ＜Security A. -1＞" debuff vocabulary, so the name references and keyword ref are
copied from it verbatim. **BT11-044 MetalEtemon** prints the same three-name union
(`[Chuumon], [Sukamon], or [Etemon]`) for a cost-budgeted free play. **BT23-057 Gankoomon** is the
precedent for a trash-return activation cost and for "add N to this effect's play cost maximum".
**BT3-040 / BT5-058** are the precedents for an `[All Turns]` board-wide `Aura` with `count: "all"`
and a `while` gate. **EX13-027 Chuumon** (this set, same archetype) fixes the house style for the
substring name union and the "no controller named ⇒ either player" reading.

### Design notes

- **Why a `Modal`.** C2 and C3 describe ONE free play whose maximum is either 6 or 6 + 6. Two
  sequential `PlayWithoutCost` actions would both resolve and play up to four cards, so the two
  maxima are exclusive `Modal` branches with `choose: 1`. The `Modal` needs no
  `optionConditions`: `optionIsAvailable` already preflights each bullet through `canPayCost`, so
  a trash with fewer than 10 matching cards drops branch 1 and the base branch then resolves with
  no choice prompt. A `selfHasMinTrash` condition restating that was proven behaviourally inert in
  a mutation run and was removed rather than carried as an unproven field.
- **`playCostLte` as the per-card half of the maximum.** A single card costing more than the
  maximum can never fit it — the same derivation `resolveTotalPlayCostBudgetTargets` makes
  server-side before it begins summing. Declaring it turns the otherwise-inert budget into real
  enforcement for the single-card case AND makes C3's "+6" behaviourally observable (6 → 12).
- **`zone: "battleArea"` on C4's gate is declarative.** A mutation run showed `countMatching`
  already excludes breeding-area permanents for this filter shape, so dropping the field changed
  no behaviour. It is kept because §3-4-7-8 is what makes the reading correct, and the
  breeding-area test asserts the rules outcome even though it cannot pin the field.

## Retained red (engine seam)

`it.fails("caps the combined play cost of the cards it plays at the printed maximum")`.

- **Seam.** `pickLoose` (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/loose.ts:502`) resolves
  a loose-card play target without ever reading `Target.totalPlayCostBudget`, and never passes a
  `maxTotalPlayCost` to `ctx.ask.selectCards` — even though `decisionApi.selectCards`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/decisions/decisionApi.ts:141`) already accepts one and clamps the answer
  to it (`clampToCostBudget`). Only `resolveTotalPlayCostBudgetTargets`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:305`) honors the field, and
  that path resolves battle-area permanents, not hand/trash cards.
- **Expected vs. actual.** With KingSukamon (play cost 6) and Chuumon (play cost 3) in the trash
  and a maximum of 6, only one of the two may be played. Actual: both are played (controller's
  battle area holds 3 permanents, trash 0), because each clears the per-card ceiling
  independently.
- **Fix shape.** Pass `target.totalPlayCostBudget` through `pickLoose` into the existing
  `selectCards` `maxTotalPlayCost` option. One field, one plumb-through; the IR and the decision
  API already carry it. `PlayMultipleAction` would need the complementary change (it enforces a
  total but has no card-count cap), and the two together would let this card drop to
  `coverage: "full"`.
- Not a repeat of any seam already in `REVIEW-NOTES.md`.

## Remaining ambiguity

- **"up to 6 total play cost" vs. the printed card pool.** Every Chuumon/Sukamon/Etemon Digimon
  card costs at least 3, so at a maximum of 6 the aggregate cap and the 2-card cap coincide and
  the seam above is invisible. It only bites on the raised maximum of 12, where four cost-3 cards
  would fit the sum but the printed count cap allows two. The count cap is enforced, so the card
  is never *more* permissive than printed in card count — only in summed cost.
- **"such cards" in C3** is read as the full preceding description ("Digimon cards with [Chuumon],
  [Sukamon] or [Etemon] in their names"), not merely "cards with those names". A non-Digimon card
  carrying one of those tokens would not fund the return under this reading; the archetype has no
  such card today, so the choice is currently unobservable.
- **C4 counts this card itself** (KingEtemon contains "Etemon"), so only two further named Digimon
  are needed. The printed text gives no reason to exclude the source, and BT13-076 spells out
  "another Digimon" when it means to exclude it — absent here.
