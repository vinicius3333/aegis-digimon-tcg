  available; the set is pre-release.** Nothing invented.
- General rules consulted in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:
  - §4-22-1 — "with XX in its text" is the token anywhere in the card's printed
    information (name, traits, effects, inherited effect, Rule, requirements). Modelled by
    `match: "text"`.
  - §2-3-2-4 — "with [XX] in any of its traits" references cards with one or more traits
    that **include** the bracketed text: the substring reading (`match: "traitContains"`).
    §2-3-2-3 is the exact reading and belongs to the other printed wording, "with the [XX]
    trait". The printed "other than [Sea Animal]" exclusion is only meaningful under the
    substring reading, which independently confirms it.
  - §3-4-5-3 — breeding-area cards are unaffected by effects that do not name the breeding
    area, i.e. the permanent-targeting default; the inherited Aura needs no `zone`.

## Notes for the coordinator (candidates for REVIEW-NOTES.md)

1. **`[Rule] Trait: Has [X] Type.` is honoured from printed text, so its IR entry is
   declarative.** `staticTraitsOf` (`apps/api/src/engine/cards/cardData.ts:299`) regex-parses
   `[Rule] Trait: Has [X]` out of `effectText` for every card. Mutation-confirmed: deleting
   the whole `Rule`/`GrantStatic` effect keeps the behavioural trait tests green, and changing
   its token to `[Mammal]` changes nothing. Kept anyway, matching EX12-026 / EX8-026 / ST18-12,
   so the IR record of the printed clause is complete — but it cannot be proven behaviourally
   and a report should not claim it was.
2. **A per-card test loads only its own module.** A cross-card proof that needs another card's
   effects needs a side-effect import (`import "../BT13/BT13-048.js";`, the
   `_a3/revealAdd-cluster.test.ts` precedent). Without it the other card behaves as a vanilla
   body and the proof silently passes/fails for the wrong reason.
3. **BT13-048 (this card's earlier printing) is likely miscoded** — out of scope here, not
   touched. It is auto-generated and encodes the same printed "[Beast], [Animal], or
   [Sovereign], other than [Sea Animal], in one of its traits" sentence with exact
   `match: "trait"`, which makes the printed [Sea Animal] exclusion dead text and rejects
   [Holy Beast] / [Dark Animal] / [Four Sovereigns] cards. BT23-012's hand-audited
   `traitContains` + branch-scoped exclusion shape (followed here) is the correct reading per
   §2-3-2-4. The same doubt applies to its inherited `selfHasTrait` aura.
4. Residual shared with BT23-012 (not a new seam, no printed card hits it): no filter
   primitive excludes one trait from a substring scan, so a hypothetical Digimon carrying both
   [Sea Animal] and another `[...Animal]` spelling would be rejected on the [Animal] branch.
