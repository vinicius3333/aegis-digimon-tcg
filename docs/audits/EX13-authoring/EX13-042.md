## Reading decisions

- **"in any of its traits" is the SUBSTRING reading** (`match: "traitContains"`), not
  "with the [X] trait". That is exactly why the printed `other than [Sea Animal]` rider exists:
  the catalog's matching trait families are [Beast], [Sea Beast], [Holy Beast], [God Beast],
  [Mythical Beast], [Beast Dragon], [Beast Knight], [Beastkin], [Aquabeast], [ElusiveBeast],
  [Mysterious Beast], [Ancient Animal], [Dark Animal], [Rare Animal], [Sea Animal] and
  [Four Sovereigns] — `Animal` would otherwise drag [Sea Animal] in.
- **The rider** is `excludeNameOrTrait` (the name/trait-spanning exclusion), kept in
  `traitContains` mode so a compound trait carrying "Sea Animal" is excluded too.
- **One `[Once Per Turn]` over two printed timings** ⇒ two windows sharing one
  `sharedUseKey: "EX13-042/play-beast"` (BT26-016 / BT26-031 shape). Plain per-window
  `frequency` would wrongly allow one activation per timing.
- **`＜Alliance＞` printed twice** ⇒ two `Static` keyword windows, the second `isInherited: true`
  (EX13-019's ＜Jamming＞ pair; ST20-04 for the inherited Alliance copy).
- **"without paying the cost"** ⇒ `payCost: false`; **"You may"** ⇒ `optional: true`.
- `playCostLte: 4` is the printed-play-cost gate on a hand candidate
  (`matching/definition.ts:154`). No `totalPlayCostBudget` is involved, so the loose-path budget
  seam recorded for EX13-035 in REVIEW-NOTES.md does not apply.

## Remaining ambiguity

- **The `[Sovereign]` disjunct cannot be proven behaviourally today.** Every catalog card with a
  `Sovereign`-containing trait is `[Four Sovereigns]`, and all eleven printings have play cost 7
  or higher (cheapest: EX5-013/EX5-024/EX5-041/EX5-053 at 7), so none can ever satisfy the
  printed "play cost 4 or lower" gate. The token is encoded and asserted at the IR level, and the
  filter's cost bound is proven behaviourally with EX5-041 as a refused candidate. This is a
  catalog-coverage limit, not an engine gap; a future low-cost [Four Sovereigns] printing (or one
  among EX13's 17 still-unrevealed numbers) would make the leg testable with no code change.
- The printed rider is read as a **trait** exclusion, not a name exclusion. No catalog card is
  named "Sea Animal", so the two readings are behaviourally identical today; the
  `excludeNameOrTrait` + `traitContains` encoding covers the trait reading that the surrounding
  sentence ("in any of its traits") implies.
