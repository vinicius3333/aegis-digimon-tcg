| Play cost / DP | 3 / 2000 |

Printed clauses:

1. `＜Blocker＞`
2. `[On Deletion] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play 1 play cost 3 or lower
   Digimon card with [Chuumon] or [Sukamon] in its name among them without paying the cost.
   trash the rest.`
3. `[Inherited] [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon would leave the battle area other
   than by your effects, by deleting 1 other Digimon with [Sukamon] in its name, it doesn't
   leave.`

No security effect, no `[Digivolve]` header, no Assembly/DNA/ACE/Link text.

## KB check

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-028` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. **None — the set is
pre-release**, so no card-specific rulings exist. General rules consulted in
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` / the rule manual via `query.mjs rules`:

- §4-22-1 card names: "with [X] in its name" is the substring reading, so `PlatinumSukamon`
  qualifies for `[Sukamon]` while a card printing `[Sukamon]` only inside its effect text does
  not.
- §16-36 `＜Blocker＞`: read from printed text by `combat/keywords.ts`; the `Static` IR entry
  keeps the record complete.
- "without paying the cost" (rule manual §5/§11/§33 hits): a full waiver of the play cost, not a
  reduction — hence `to: "play"` with no `costDelta`.

Peer/stack consistency: the reveal clause reuses the BT3-063 / BT11-040 `RevealAdd` shape (this
card's own earlier printings) and EX12-055 / BT26-084's `to: "play"` slot; the inherited clause is
byte-for-byte EX13-027's encoding, which in turn matches BT11-040 / BT13-065's printed sentence and
EX12-003 / EX13-015's `leaveCause` usage. Per the coordinator's EX13 notes the redundant
`oncePerTurnKey` that EX13-015 carries is deliberately omitted — `frequency: "OncePerTurn"` alone
holds the budget, which the once-per-turn test proves.

## Remaining ambiguity

- `kind: ["Digimon"]` encodes the printed "Digimon card" but cannot be proven behaviourally: the
  catalog holds no Option/Tamer/Digi-Egg whose name contains `[Chuumon]` or `[Sukamon]`, so no
  fixture can distinguish its presence from its absence.
- The host's own inherited text prints the token `[Sukamon]` it also filters on, but the filter is
  `match: "name"`, not `match: "text"`, so the `printedTextOnly` seam in REVIEW-NOTES.md does not
  apply here.
