### Fixture discrimination

The `match: "text"` slot is proven to discriminate, not merely fire:

- **BT6-009 Huckmon** — carries the token as its name.
- **BT6-011 BaoHuckmon** — carries the token only as a substring of a longer name
  (the reading EX13-014 / EX13-061 depend on for their "Lv.5 w/[Huckmon] in text"
  headers, whose only catalog answers are SaviorHuckmon and BaoHuckmon).
- **BT6-093 Judgement of the Blade** — an Option whose only `[Huckmon]` mention sits
  inside its printed effect text. It is not named and not "treated as" a `[Huckmon]`,
  so it avoids the `effectiveStaticNames` false-green trap noted in REVIEW-NOTES.md;
  it also proves the slot carries no card-kind restriction.
- **BT1-009 / BT1-012 / BT1-013 / BT1-014** — inert Digimon carrying no token.

## Engine gaps / retained reds

None. No `it.fails` / `it.skip`, no `// @ts-nocheck`, `coverage: "full"`,
`residual: []`. Every printed clause resolves through existing primitives
(`SetMemory`, `RevealAdd`, `PlayWithoutCost`).

Checked against REVIEW-NOTES.md and found not applicable here: the DUAL-card
`printedColorRequirementMet` bug (this card is a plain Tamer, not DUAL); the
`printedTextOnly` self-match seam (the filter runs over deck-card definitions,
never over the host's own live stack); `requiresMinRevealed` (inert on a
single `count: 1` slot, so it is deliberately omitted rather than encoded and
left unproven).

## Remaining ambiguity

- The engine's `match: "text"` resolves name tokens by substring, so BaoHuckmon and
  SaviorHuckmon answer `[Huckmon]`. This is the reading already baked into EX13-014
  and EX13-061 and is consistent with §4-23-1 (the token appears in the printed
  information); a post-release ruling that "in its text" needs a delimiter-anchored
  card reference would narrow it. Recorded, not worked around.
- The card has no printed `[Rule]` line, no inherited text and no evoCosts, so there
  is no evolution-stack route to prove for it; the digivolution assertions the brief
  asks for do not apply to a Tamer with an empty `evoCosts` array.
