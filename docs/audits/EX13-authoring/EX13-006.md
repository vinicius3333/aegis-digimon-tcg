Evolution-stack proof: the public route Digi-Egg → Black Lv.3
(`EX13-006` → `BT13-063` Dorumon, Black Lv.2 requirement, cost 0) is driven
through the real `digivolve` intent from breeding; memory is unchanged (breeding
digivolution is free), the evolution draw lands in hand, `Permanent.stack`
holds exactly `["EX13-006"]` (sources beneath the top card), and the inherited
clause then fires at the natural end of turn after a real attack suspends the
host. Illegal source negative: the Lv.2 egg cannot digivolve into the Lv.4
`BT1-014`; the intent is rejected and neither the stack nor memory moves.

Mutation checks run while authoring (reverted): removing `frequency` fails the
once-per-turn test; widening `controller` to `"any"` and dropping the
`kind` gate fails the opponent-reach and Tamer tests. The negatives are
load-bearing, not decorative.

## Remaining ambiguity

- The `[Chronicle]` branch cannot be isolated behaviourally: the catalog
  contains no Digimon carrying `Chronicle` without `X Antibody` (only the
  Tamers BT20-087 and EX13-072 carry `Chronicle` alone, and Tamers are excluded
  by the clause's "Digimon"). The branch is proven structurally in the IR and
  behaviourally through BT20-048, which carries both traits.
- The engine normalizes whitespace and hyphens in trait references
  (`normalizeTrait` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts`),
  so the catalog's separate `X-Antibody` spelling (BT20-073) matches this
  clause. This matches the real game, where the two spellings are one trait; the
  behaviour is pinned by an explicit test rather than left implicit.
