# EX13-032 Chirinmon — authoring audit

## Card summary

Yellow Lv.5 Ultimate, Vaccine, 7000 DP, play cost 7, catalog EvoCost Yellow Lv.4 for 3,
types [Holy Beast] / [DATA SQUAD]. Rarity R.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[DATA SQUAD] trait: Cost 3` — a colorless alternate route beside the
   catalog's Yellow Lv.4 route.
2. `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By trashing your top security card or the
   bottom face-down card from under any of your Tamers, this Digimon unsuspends. After, 1 of your
   opponent's Digimon can't activate [When Digivolving] effects until their turn ends.`
3. `[All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by placing its top stacked card as
   the top security card, it doesn't leave.`
4. Inherited: `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon with [Kentaurosmon] in its name would
   leave the battle area, by placing its top stacked card as the top security card, it doesn't
   leave.`

No security effect is printed.

## Knowledge-base check

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-032` → "(no knowledge-base entries)". EX13 is pre-release, so
**no card-specific rulings are available**. General rules consulted in
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- §15-7 Optional Processing Conditions — "by X, Y" is an optional processing condition. §15-7-2:
  if the condition is not executed, nothing after it may be executed. §15-7-4: the controller may
  choose to attempt a condition it cannot in fact perform. This fixes the whole card's shape:
  every clause is a cost-gated block (`optional: true` / `abortOnDecline: true`), the printed
  "After, …" restriction sits INSIDE the gated block, and the printed either/or stays a free
  player choice rather than being pre-filtered to the payable half.
- §15-8-4 / §15-8-5 — the leave clauses are immediate-type effects on a "would" event, which is
  the `Replacement` / `wouldLeavePlay` shape.

Related rulings reused from implemented peers (not EX13-specific): KB EX11-043 Q5875/Q5888 — a
permanent's "top stacked card" is its own visible top card, and placing it promotes the stack
while the permanent stays in play; KB BT17-098 Q2892 — such a placement needs a card underneath,
so a stackless permanent cannot pay.

### Why `place` + `detachPermanentTop`, not `placeAsSecurity`

The `placeAsSecurity` cost kind (`costs.ts:1621`, BT19-048/BT26-033) calls `addSecurity` WITHOUT
`detachPermanentTop`, which moves the whole permanent out of the battle area — the opposite of "it
doesn't leave". The `place` cost with `destination: "security"` (`costs.ts:1801`) honours
`detachPermanentTop`, sheds only the visible top card, promotes the stack, and recomputes DP and
continuous effects (`primitives.ts:4895`). Prior art: BT9-044, BT17-098, EX11-041/EX11-043.

### Filter-discrimination proof

The inherited host gate is proven to discriminate, not merely to fire: BT3-043 Kentaurosmon (match)
is kept in play, BT22-037 Chirinmon (prints `[Kentaurosmon]` in its own effect TEXT but not in its
name) leaves, and BT1-009 Monodramon (plain non-match) leaves. Mutating `match: "name"` to
`match: "text"` fails that test, so the reading is behaviourally pinned. Because the gate reads the
host's NAME only, the `printedTextOnly` scoping that REVIEW-NOTES describes for `match: "text"`
self-gates does not apply here — even though this card's own inherited line prints the token.

## Remaining ambiguity

- "After, 1 of your opponent's Digimon can't activate …" is read as a dependent sentence inside the
  same optional processing condition (§15-7-2), i.e. it does not resolve when the cost is not paid.
  Every implemented peer with "Then,"/"After," after a "by X" cost reads the same way.
- The printed main-body leave clause and the inherited one can both be live on one permanent (a
  Kentaurosmon over a Chirinmon is the card's own evolution line). The order the engine offers them
  in is the generic simultaneous-replacement order; the tests assert outcomes that hold either way.
- Whether an official ruling will let the same turn spend the main clause more than once is
  untested by Bandai; the card prints no `[Once Per Turn]` there, so it is implemented unrestricted
  and that reading is asserted.
