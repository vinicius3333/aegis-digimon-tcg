# EX13-018 Coredramon — authoring audit

## Card summary

Digimon, Blue/Red, Lv.4 Champion, [Dragon], Vaccine, play cost 5, 5000 DP, rarity C.
EvoCosts: Blue Lv.3 cost 3, Red Lv.3 cost 3.

Printed clauses (the contract):

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.3 w/[Dracomon] in name: Cost 2`
2. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] By trashing 1 card with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in its text
   from your hand, ＜Draw 2＞`
3. `[Your Turn] When any of your other Digimon with [Dracomon] or [Examon] in their texts are
   played, this Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with [Examon] in its text in the hand
   with the cost reduced by 2.`
4. Inherited: `[Your Turn] This Digimon gets +2000 DP.`

Nothing is printed twice, no clause carries `[Once Per Turn]`, and the card has no security
effect, no keywords, and no DigiXros / link / ace fields.

## Knowledge base check

- `node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-018` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. **No KB rulings
  available; the set is pre-release.** Nothing was invented to fill the gap.
- General rules consulted instead:
  - `node tools/kb/query.mjs rules "in its text"` → comprehensive §4-22-1: a "with XX in its
    text" card is one with the term or icon anywhere in the information printed on that card.
    The official rule manual entry for the same phrase spells the union out: names, traits,
    effects, inherited effects, Link, Rule, digivolution requirements, DigiXros requirements.
    This is exactly the engine's `match: "text"`, so both the trash-cost filter and the
    destination filter use it rather than the narrower `match: "name"`.
  - `[Digivolve] ... w/[Dracomon] in name` is the substring NAME reading, which the IR expresses
    as `DigivolutionRequirement.names` (documented in
    `packages/shared/src/effects/ir/requirements/digivolve.ts` as the substring gate, against
    `namesExact` for the Armor/X-Antibody shape).

## Remaining ambiguity

- `useAlternateCost: true` is a PREFERENCE, not a gate: the engine falls back to the printed
  EvoCost when no alternate entry matches, so the negative test proves the in-name gate by the
  memory charged (3, not 2) rather than by a rejected intent. A Lv.4 base is rejected outright
  on both routes, which is the hard negative.
- No card in the catalog is a Lv.3 carrying `[Dracomon]` in its text but NOT in its name, so the
  `names`-versus-`texts` distinction on clause 1 cannot be separated by a real fixture. It is
  asserted structurally (the IR field) and argued from §4-22-1 in the module comment. If such a
  card ships later it becomes a behavioural test.
- Clause 3 cannot fire twice on one carrier in a single turn even without a `[Once Per Turn]`,
  because the clause leaves the board with the top card the moment it resolves. The absence of
  the per-turn limit is therefore proven structurally plus by the "stops offering" test, which
  shows the limiter is source identity rather than a per-turn ledger.
- Memory is clamped to ±10 (`MEMORY_MAX` in `apps/api/src/engine/MemoryGauge.ts`); seeds above
  that silently clamp, so every fixture stays at 10 or below.
