## Knowledge base

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-058` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`. **None — the set is
pre-release.** General rules consulted in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- **§4-23 Cards "With XX in Their Texts"** — §4-23-1 defines the match as the token anywhere in
  the information printed on that card; §4-23-3 confirms an INHERITED-effect mention still counts
  even when the card is not in digivolution cards. That is what makes BT7-059 DeadlyAxemon (no
  "Knightmon" in its name, [Knightmon] in its effect and inherited lines) a legal source and a
  legal grant recipient, and what rules out `match: "name"`.
- **§4-22-2 / §4-22-3 colour requirements** — a multicolour Option needs every one of its colours
  represented. The printed sentence narrows by text and cost only, so the Option branch carries
  `allowMultiColor: true` and leaves the colour check itself to the engine (BT18-099 waives its
  own colour requirement "while you have a Digimon with [Knightmon] in its text", which this host
  satisfies).
- **§16-12-1 ＜De-Digivolve N＞** — trash cards from the chosen Digimon's stack starting with the
  top card; `DeDigivolve` is that keyword.
- **§3-4-5-8** — breeding-area cards cannot be referenced by effects, which keeps the keyword
  grant scoped to the battle area without an explicit `zone`.

Two corrections came out of this, both applied:

1. `includeLaterEntrants` was dropped. It exists for a grant resolved ONCE out of a timed window
   (EX1-068, BT17-040). This is a resident `OpponentsTurn` clause the continuous pass re-derives
   from the live board, so a mid-turn arrival is covered by the next pass regardless — confirmed by
   mutation. BT24-041, the printed twin, does not carry it either. **Finding worth generalising:
   do not copy `includeLaterEntrants` onto a resident `[Your Turn]`/`[Opponent's Turn]`/`[All
   Turns]` keyword grant; it is inert decoration there.**
2. The `digivolutionRequirement` entry is retained for record completeness with an explicit comment
   saying it is not behaviourally provable.

## Remaining ambiguity / unprovable

- **`[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[Knightmon] in text: Cost 3` is structurally asserted only.** The route
  costs the same 3 as the printed Black Lv.4 EvoCost, so its only observable difference from the
  printed route is dropping the Black colour requirement — and the catalog contains **no** non-Black
  Lv.4 printing with "Knightmon" anywhere in its text (every Lv.4 carrying the token includes
  Black: BT7-058, BT7-059, BT8-062, BT10-061, BT18-062, BT19-058, EX4-040, EX4-042, EX10-026,
  EX10-027, EX13-052, P-115, P-154). Per REVIEW-NOTES, `alternateRequirementIndex` cannot pin
  "printed route only" either, so the proof used instead is a same-cost positive (text-matching
  Lv.4), a same-cost control (non-matching Black Lv.4), and two negatives both preferences refuse
  (wrong colour + no text; right colour + right text but **Lv.3**). This is a catalog-fixture gap,
  not an engine gap. It becomes provable the moment EX13's remaining 17 unrevealed numbers — or a
  future set — print a non-Black Lv.4 with [Knightmon] in its text.
- **Self-match is intended here.** This card's own printed information contains "Knightmon" (its
  name), so it is one of "your Digimon with [Knightmon] in their texts" and grants itself
  ＜Reboot＞/＜Blocker＞. `printedTextOnly` was deliberately NOT set: the REVIEW-NOTES case for that
  flag (EX13-021/EX13-024) is a host-identity gate whose own *inherited* line prints the tokens it
  filters by, whereas here the token is the host's own NAME, which every reading of §4-23-1
  includes. The stack-inclusive reading of `match: "text"` for other permanents is the codebase
  default (EX1-021/Q3208) and matches every Chronomon-archetype peer in BT26.
- No engine seam was found. The `UseOptionWithoutCost` scaled-reduction gap recorded for EX13-043
  is not reachable from this card: it plays/uses "without paying the cost" with no reduction
  arithmetic at all.
