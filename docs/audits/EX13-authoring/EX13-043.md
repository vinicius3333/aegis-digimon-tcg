# EX13-043 Leopardmon — authoring audit

## Card summary

Green Lv.6 Mega Digimon, Data, `[Holy Warrior]`/`[Royal Knight]`, 12000 DP, play cost 12,
printed EvoCost Green Lv.5 for 3. No inherited text, no separate security text.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] [Leopardmon: Leopard Mode]: Cost 1`
2. `[Assembly -5] Lv.5 × Lv.4 × Lv.3, all Green w/[Mammal]/[Beast]/[Beastkin] trait`
3. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon. Then, you may return 1 of your
   opponent's lowest DP Digimon to the bottom of the deck.`
4. `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play or use 1 [Mammal], [Beast],
   [Beastkin] or [Royal Knight] trait card from your hand with the cost reduced by 4. For each
   suspended Digimon, further reduce it by 1.`
5. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your suspended Digimon would leave the battle area
   other than by your effects, by unsuspending 1 of your Digimon, they don't leave.`

## KB check

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-043` → "(no knowledge-base entries)". No rulings available;
EX13 is pre-release. General rules consulted through `node tools/kb/query.mjs rules` and
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- §7-3 / §7-3-2 Assembly — materials come from the **trash** only, the exact printed slot count
  must be placed, and the reduction is the flat printed −5.
- §7-3-2-6 — the leftmost listed material ends up closest to the played card, so the Lv.5 slot is
  last in `Permanent.stack`.
- §3-4-5-8 — breeding-area cards cannot be referenced, which keeps the board filters on the
  battle area.
- §15-8-4-4-1 — an unperformable cost fails the action; the `unsuspend` cost can only be paid by a
  currently suspended permanent, so its target filter pins `suspended: true`.

## Retained red (engine seam)

`it.fails("applies the per-suspended-Digimon reduction to the Option USE branch too")`

- Seam: `UseOptionWithoutCostAction` (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/actions/meta.ts:121`)
  declares `reduceCostBy` and `reduceCostByOpponentMemory` but **no** `reduceCostByScaling`, and
  `runUseOptionWithoutCost`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/borrowed.ts:522`) computes
  `totalReduction = (action.reduceCostBy ?? 0) + dynamicReduction` — it never calls `scaleFactor`.
  The sibling `PlayWithoutCost` path does, in `paidReduction`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/play.ts:104`).
- Expected vs actual: with 2 suspended Digimon and BT13-110 (use cost 6), the printed reduction is
  4 + 2 = 6, so memory should stay 5. The engine charges 6 − 4 = 2 and memory lands on 3
  (confirmed by temporarily asserting 3, which passed).
- `coverage: "partial"`,
  `residual: ["For each suspended Digimon, further reduce it by 1. (Option-use branch only)"]`.
- Fix shape: add `reduceCostByScaling?: Scaling` to `UseOptionWithoutCostAction` and add
  `+ (action.reduceCostByScaling === undefined ? 0 : scaleFactor(ctx, action.reduceCostByScaling))`
  to both `totalReduction` and `canAttemptUseOptionWithoutCost`'s `costDelta`. Not built here — the
  brief forbids engine edits in this lane.
- Checked against REVIEW-NOTES.md first: the listed seams (per-card name exclusion, DP-valued
  scaling unit, `whenSuspended` `sourceFilter`, `totalPlayCostBudget` on loose zones) do not cover
  it, and no existing field on this action expresses a scaled reduction.

## Remaining ambiguity

- "You may suspend 1 Digimon" and "For each suspended Digimon" name no controller, so both are read
  as **either** player's board (`controller: "any"` / `controllerDefault: "any"`), matching the
  EX13-027 coordinator decision and the EX11-032 / BT25-059 encoding of the identical phrase. A
  later ruling narrowing either to "your" would be a one-field change.
- The two printed "you may"s in clause 3 are treated as independent declines (no `abortOnDecline`):
  declining the suspend still offers the bounce. "Then," is read as sequencing, not dependency.
- `[Mammal]`, `[Beast]`, `[Beastkin]` and `[Royal Knight]` are currently carried by no Tamer in the
  catalog, so the play branch's `kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]` states the printed scope ("card", not
  "Digimon card") rather than anything reachable today.
