General rules consulted in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` (via `tools/kb/query.mjs rules`):

- **§4-5 / §4-5-2 / §4-5-6 (DUAL cards).** A DUAL card is declared as either its Digimon
  information or its Option information before use; the Option information is the lower text.
  The engine models that declaration as `useAs` on the `playCard` intent; the Option half of the
  IR is a plain `trigger: "Main"` effect (EX12-033 / EX12-052 / BT26-032 shape).
- **§4-19 / §4-19-2 (Arts Digivolve).** A RULE on DUAL cards, not a printed clause: after a DUAL
  card's Option side resolves, one of the controller's permanents may digivolve into it without
  paying the cost, replacing the trash step. The engine implements this
  (`playCard.ts:456`, `GameEngine.ts:6059`) and it fires in this card's Option-side tests — see
  "Observations" below.
- **§7-1 / §7-1-1 / §7-1-2 (playing a card).** Playing places one card with a play cost onto the
  field. Option cards are USED, not played — `playableCandidates`
  (`interpreter/actions/play.ts:40`) already drops Option-ONLY candidates from a kind-less play
  pool while keeping a DUAL card, so clause 6's filter carries no `kind`.
- **§16-36-1 (＜Decode＞).** Scoped to THAT Digimon's digivolution cards, and it activates only
  when the Digimon would leave the battle area other than in battle.
- **§4-22-1 / §4-23-1 / §4-23-2 ("in its text").** The token anywhere in a card's printed
  information; a Digimon gains its digivolution cards' effects, never their text.
- **§3-4-7-7 / §3-4-7-8.** Breeding-area information is inaccessible to ordinary effects, which
  matches `countMatching`'s default of excluding breeding for clause 7's "your Digimon".

### Reading decisions

- **`[Sistermon Noir]`/`[Sistermon Ciel]` are EXACT references** (`namesExact` /
  `match: "nameExact"`). A substring reading would admit "Sistermon Ciel (Awakened)" (BT7-083,
  BT20-084) — which the printed parentheses do not name — and even this card's own granted alias.
  `effectiveExactNames` still folds in the aliases a card is treated AS, so ST12-13 and BT6-084
  (treated as [Sistermon Noir] per KB Q759 / Q1470) stay legal sources with no special casing.
- **`[Sistermon]` in clause 6 is a SUBSTRING name reference** (`match: "name"`). No card in the
  catalog is named exactly "Sistermon"; an exact reading makes the clause dead. This is the one
  genuinely ambiguous reading on this card — the community text is abbreviated ("card with
  [Sistermon]") and the released wording is most likely "with [Sistermon] in its name". Recorded
  under "Remaining ambiguity".
- **Clause 6 carries no `kind`.** Printed "card", and §7-1 plus `playableCandidates` already
  exclude Option-only candidates (so the Option BT23-099 is unreachable while the Digimon and
  Tamer [Sistermon] cards are reachable). Asserted explicitly in the fidelity test.
- **Clause 7's scaling is a REPETITION count, not a deeper peel.** `actions/digivolution.ts`
  turns a `Scaling` on `DeDigivolve` into N separate ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ passes, with board state
  re-read between them — BT21-061 / Q4568's documented shape, and exactly what the printed
  "＜De-Digivolve 1＞ for each …" says. The count is taken when this action resolves, i.e. AFTER
  the preceding play, so a card the first clause played counts itself (proven: 2 peels with one
  pre-existing Digimon plus the played one).
- **Both `[Digivolve]` headers are `isAlternate: true`** because the catalog carries no printed
  EvoCost. The exact-name entry is listed first so `matchGatedRequirement` returns the cheaper
  route for any base that could answer both.
- **Nothing waives the Option's colour requirement.** "without paying the cost" waives a cost,
  not `optionColorRequirements: ["White"]`, and the card prints no waiver — proven by the
  `color-requirement-unmet` rejection with no white permanent in play.

## Observations for the coordinator (not this card's bugs, nothing changed)

1. **CR §4-19 Arts Digivolve fires in DUAL-card tests and is easy to mistake for a card bug.**
   After the Option side resolves, `autoSelectCards: true` accepts the Arts Digivolve offer (a
   `selectCards` decision with `min: 0`, not an `optional` prompt — `autoDeclineOptional` does
   NOT decline it, matching the existing note about optional `RevealAdd` slots). The DUAL card
   then lands on the board on top of an eligible own permanent instead of going to the trash, and
   its `[When Digivolving]` clauses fire. While debugging, this made an 8-card opposing permanent
   disappear entirely and looked like a runaway De-Digivolve. Two practical consequences for
   anyone writing a DUAL-card test:
   - Pick a white/colour anchor that is NOT a legal Arts Digivolve base for the card under test,
     or the card silently becomes a permanent. Here every Lv.3 [Sistermon] in the catalog prints
     "[Huckmon]" and therefore answers this card's own Lv.3-text route, so the anchor had to be
     the Lv.4 BT20-084.
   - BT26-056's test declines it explicitly with `{ kind: "selectCards", instanceIds: [] }`; there
     is no harness flag for that. Worth adding to REVIEW-NOTES.
2. **`printedColorRequirementMet` applies `optionColorRequirements` to a DUAL card's DIGIMON-side
   play too.** `GameEngine.ts:6137` reads
   `definition.optionColorRequirements ?? (mode === "option" ? definition.colors : [])` — the
   `??` branch is taken regardless of `mode`, so playing EX13-066 as a Lv.4 Digimon from hand is
   rejected with `color-requirement-unmet` unless a white Digimon/Tamer is already in play. That
   looks wrong (a Digimon-side play has no colour requirement in the printed rules), but it is
   shared by every DUAL card in the repo, not specific to this one. The Digimon-side test keeps a
   white permanent on the board, so it is legal under either reading and does not lock the
   current behaviour in. Flagged for an engine lane; not fixed here.
3. **`observe().grantedNames()` returns case-folded names.** Not documented in REVIEW-NOTES; an
   assertion on the printed capitalisation fails.

## Remaining ambiguity

- **"card with [Sistermon]" (clause 6).** Read as a substring NAME match. The two other readings
  are "in its text" (wider — it would also reach a card whose effect text merely mentions
  [Sistermon], e.g. BT7-082/BT7-083's [On Deletion] lines, which are refused here only by the
  play-cost ceiling) and an exact name match (which matches nothing). If the released English
  text turns out to say "in its text", the fix is one field: `match: "name"` → `match: "text"`
  on `sistermonCard`, plus updating the two negative fixtures in the trash/cost test.
- **Clause 7's "to 1 of your opponent's Digimon" under repetition.** The printed sentence names
  ONE Digimon and peels it N times. `runDigivolutionAction` re-resolves the target on each
  repetition, so on a board with several opposing Digimon the controller could in principle be
  asked again and spread the peels. Every test here uses a single opposing Digimon, so the
  behaviour is deterministic and correct for the proven cases; a multi-target board is untested
  and may need a `fromSelectionRef`-style binding if a later ruling pins the single subject. The
  same re-resolution exists on BT21-061, the cited precedent, so this is not a new divergence.
- **＜De-Digivolve＞ depth is only observable above the level floor.** `peelStackTops` stops once
  a Lv.3 card is on top, so the victim stack was built Lv.3 / Lv.4 / Lv.5 / Lv.6 to make each
  successive peel distinguishable.
