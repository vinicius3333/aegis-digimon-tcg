Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play]` Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with
   [Huckmon] or [Sistermon] in its text **and** 1 such Tamer card or Option card
   among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.
2. Inherited: `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn]` When any of your white Digimon are
   played, gain 1 memory.

No security effect, no DigiXros, no ACE, no link, no printed `[Digivolve]`
alternate route — so the single Red Lv.2 evoCost is the ordinary catalog route
and carries no `digivolutionRequirement` IR entry.

## Rules / KB check

- `node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX13-009` → `(no knowledge-base entries)`.
  **No KB rulings available; the set is pre-release.** Not a gap introduced here.
- General rule that the card's vocabulary touches:
  `node tools/kb/query.mjs rules "in its text"` →
  **comprehensive §4-22-1**: a "with XX in its text" card is a card that has the
  "XX" term or icon *in the information printed on that card*; the official
  manual entry expands that to names, traits, effects, inherited effects, Link,
  Rule, digivolution requirements and DigiXros requirements. That is exactly the
  engine's `match: "text"` reference (name ∪ traits ∪ every printed text field),
  so `match: "name"` would be wrong here.
- `reveal.ts` documents KB Q2625 for the "**or**" form of a multi-category add
  (one card from the whole union). This card prints the "**and**" form, so it
  needs two `add` slots, not one slot with `orFilters`.

## Peer alignment

## Remaining ambiguity

- The module is **not** imported by `apps/api/src/cards/EX13/index.ts` (an
  off-limits file for this lane), so the test imports `./EX13-009.js` directly to
  register the IR. The coordinator must add the `import "./EX13-009.js";` line to
  that index.
- `match: "text"` is asserted to include the effect-text field (BT13-019) and to
  include names (BT13-013). Whether the engine's text scope also covers
  digivolution-requirement and DigiXros-requirement printing, as the official
  manual's §4-22 gloss says it should, is not exercised here — no revealed
  fixture in the Huckmon/Sistermon pool distinguishes it. Harmless for this card
  in practice; worth a shared-filter test if another card depends on it.
- Two `selectCards` decisions are raised even when each slot has exactly one
  legal candidate (asserted in the first reveal test). That is the interpreter's
  existing behaviour for a forced numeric `count`, shared with ST20-02, and is
  recorded rather than changed.
