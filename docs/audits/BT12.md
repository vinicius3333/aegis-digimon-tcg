### BT12-001 — Gigimon

**Catalog contract.** Red Digi-Egg, Lv.2, In-Training/Lesser, inherited effect: `[Your Turn] Add 1000 to the maximum your DP-based deletion effects can delete.`

**Implementation trace.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/BT12-001.ts:1-17` compiles one inherited Your Turn effect with `CostModifier { mode: "raiseCeiling", costType: "dpDeletion", amount: 1000 }`, with full coverage and no residual clauses. It registers exactly once with `registerIrCard("BT12-001", compiled)`.

**Rules and seam trace.** KB Q2144 confirms that the modifier raises a numerically printed DP-based deletion ceiling (for example, 3000 to 4000); Q2145 confirms that it does not raise a dynamic ceiling such as “equal to this Digimon’s DP.” Comprehensive Rules §15-15-4 makes the same numeric-ceiling distinction. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/resources.ts:251` applies the owner-wide `dpDeletion` modifier through `ctx.fx.addDeletionMaxDp`, which is the correct scope when no self target is specified. BT9-009 and BT9-011 provide relevant raise-ceiling peers.

**Static proof.** `BT12-001.test.ts:7-25` uses a public ST7-06 removal to prove the owner-turn +1000 path against a 5000-DP target, and `:27-43` proves the effect is inactive outside the owner’s turn. The test source reflects the numeric ceiling and turn clause; it was not executed in this dispatch.

**Verdict.** Direct IR, snapshot, interpreter seam, peer semantics, and source proof align. Ambiguities: 0. Snapshot drift: none. Correction commits in this pass: none. Score: **8/10 provisional** (`2+2+2+2+0`).
