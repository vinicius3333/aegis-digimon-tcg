# EX11-023 Kaguyamon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines yellow/purple level-6 Puppet Kaguyamon with normal level-5 yellow or purple evolution cost 4 and alternate level-5 Puppet cost 3. Printed keywords are Alliance and Scapegoat. Its `[When Digivolving] [End of Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn]` effect deletes one opponent Digimon with the lowest level. Its `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` effect, when another Digimon is deleted, may play one level-4-or-lower Puppet from trash without paying its cost.

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. It shares the once-per-turn key between When Digivolving and End of Opponent's Turn, uses lowest-level targeting, excludes self from the deletion watcher, and gates recursive play to level <=4 Puppet cards from trash. No IR defect was established.
