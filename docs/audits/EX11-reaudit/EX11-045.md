# EX11-045 Metatromon audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines black level-6 Metatromon, play cost 12, 12000 DP, Machine/LIBERATOR, and the alternate Assembly -5 recipe requiring five black Digimon cards with [Maquinamon] in their text. It also has an alternate level-5 [Maquinamon] in text evolution for cost 3 and Blocker. Its On Play, When Digivolving, and When Attacking once-per-turn effects de-digivolve one opposing Digimon by 2, then restrict one opposing Digimon or Tamer from digivolving until that opponent's turn ends. At the end of the owner's turn, once per turn, another Digimon may free-digivolve into a green Maquinamon-text card from hand. The inherited All Turns once-per-turn effect deletes the opposing Digimon with the lowest play cost when an effect adds cards to this Digimon's digivolution cards.

The module is exclusive `registerIrCard`, full coverage, and retains the compiled `onAddDigivolutionCards` SubTrigger for the inherited effect. It encodes exact Maquinamon-in-text matching, the five-card black Assembly recipe, Blocker metadata, the three shared once-per-turn effect windows, green hand evolution, and lowest-play-cost deletion.
