# EX11-074 — Vortexdramon

Vortexdramon is a green level 7 Digimon with `[Bird Dragon]`, `[Vortex Warriors]`, and `[LIBERATOR]` traits. Its catalog clauses are:

- alternate `[Digivolve]` while you have `[Shoto Kazama]`: `[GrandGalemon]` for cost 6;
- `<Piercing>`, `<Vortex>`, and `<Blocker>`;
- `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking]` you may suspend one Digimon; if this effect suspended your Digimon, until the opponent's turn ends their Digimon effects do not affect this Digimon and it gets +6000 DP;
- `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` when any Digimon suspends, this Digimon may unsuspend, then may battle one opposing Digimon.

The local KB covers Q5948–Q5959. Q5948 permits choosing either player's Digimon for the first suspension. Q5949–Q5954 define the opponent-Digimon-effect immunity and its timing. Q5955–Q5959 define the optional direct battle and its interaction with Piercing and attacks. The reset proof uses two legal EX11-026 On Play suspension producers, explicitly declines the producer-turn unsuspend, then accepts the watcher from attacker A and attacker B on separate opponent turns; preferred target IDs keep each direct battle on the attacking Digimon.

## Q&A coverage

Q5948 is covered by the public own-Digimon suspension route. Q5949–Q5954 are partially covered by the public installed restriction and DP result. A production-loop EX11-026 opponent-Digimon suspend attempt reaches the target but the engine still suspends Vortexdramon, so the restriction-to-action seam is unresolved without shared-engine changes. The trace is specific: `runBoardAction` forwards the opponent seat/card to `ctx.fx.suspend`; `suspend` checks only `isSuspended` and `beSuspended`, then calls `access.suspend`. It never calls the existing shared `isRestricted` gate, whose source-kind and opponent-seat logic already handles `beAffected`. The smallest general fix is therefore to replace the `beSuspended` check in the effect-driven `suspend` primitive with `isRestricted(permanentId, "beSuspended")` (or equivalently add the same `beAffected` source-kind/opponent check there), preserving combat's direct `access.suspend` path. The affected regression coverage belongs in the shared primitive/mechanic tests: add a source-kind-qualified opponent `beAffected` restriction case beside `primitives.test.ts`'s existing hand-written mutation test and assert a public Digimon effect such as EX11-026 leaves the target unsuspended while a same-controller effect remains legal. EX11-074 can only retain the legal public characterization until that engine test and fix land. Q5955–Q5959 are covered at the direct-battle/no-security boundary plus one normal Piercing security check; nested direct-battle-during-attack permutations remain unexercised.
