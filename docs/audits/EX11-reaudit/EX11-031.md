# EX11-031 Vespamon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines green/black level-5 Vespamon with 8 play cost, 8000 DP, normal level-4 green or black evolution cost 4, and alternate level-4 Royal Base evolution cost 3. It carries Cyborg, X Antibody, Royal Base, LIBERATOR, and Insectoid traits. Its Security/Opponent's Turn effect gives Blocker to own Royal Base Digimon. On Play/When Digivolving, it suspends one opponent Digimon or Tamer per own face-up security card, then restricts one independently selected opponent Digimon or Tamer from unsuspending until that opponent's turn ends. Its inherited All Turns Once Per Turn replacement flips the top own face-up security card face down to keep an own Royal Base Digimon from leaving by another player's effect.

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. The resident Security effect, face-up security scaling, independent restriction target, exact Royal Base source filter, once-per-turn replacement, and top face-up security payment are encoded. No IR defect was established.

The direct `EffectTiming.OnPlay` checks remain mechanism probes because the available test seam invokes timing directly.

## Q&A and catalog reconciliation

- Q5837 is covered by the public and mechanism tests: the suspended target and the independently restricted target are not required to be the same card. Q5838 and Q5839 belong to EX11-032 and do not apply to EX11-031.
- The EX11-031 catalog text has no clause placing a stacked card on face-up security during a security check. Consequently, no synthetic top-card placement claim is credited. The implemented top-card operation is the inherited replacement's face-up security payment, which is directly asserted.
