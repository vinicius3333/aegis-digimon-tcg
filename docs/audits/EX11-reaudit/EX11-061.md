# EX11-061 — Mirai Kinosaki

Mirai Kinosaki is a yellow/purple LIBERATOR Tamer with play cost 4. Its catalog clauses are:

- `[Start of Your Main Phase]` if the opponent has a Digimon, gain 1 memory;
- `[Your Turn]` when one of your Digimon digivolves into a `[Puppet]` trait Digimon, by suspending this Tamer, you may play one level 3 `[Puppet]` trait Digimon from hand without paying its cost; at turn end, delete the Digimon this effect played;
- `[Security]` play this card without paying its cost.

Q5915 confirms that Mirai deletes the Digimon played by her effect at turn end. Q5916 identifies the turn-end processing-order question; this audit proves the real turn-end deletion but does not claim a complete alternate-order matrix.

## Clause → public test → IR

| Clause | Public evidence | IR evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Start-main condition and memory | A public turn loop with an opposing Digimon reaches seat 0's main phase and asserts memory +1. | `StartOfYourMainPhase` uses an opponent-controlled `Digimon` filter and `GainMemory(1)`. |
| Puppet digivolution watcher | A public digivolution into EX11-021 fires the watcher, suspends Mirai, and plays EX11-020 from hand without cost. | `YourTurn` contains `whenOneOfYoursDigivolves` with an own Digimon source filter requiring the `Puppet` trait. |
| Exact level/trait hand target | A mixed hand puts level 4 Puppet EX11-021 and unrelated BT1-090 before legal level 3 Puppet EX11-020; only EX11-020 is played. | `PlayWithoutCost` filters hand Digimon to `levels: [3]` and `Puppet` trait, with count 1. |
| Optional suspend cost | A public `autoDeclineOptional` digivolution leaves Mirai unsuspended, leaves both hand cards in hand, and leaves the existing Puppet alive. | Play action is optional, has self suspend cost, and `abortOnDecline`. |
| Turn-end deletion / Q5915 | The played EX11-020 is deleted only after a real main-phase transition into the opponent's turn; the evolved EX11-021 remains. | `DelayedDelete` is inside the digivolution watcher after `PlayWithoutCost`, targeting the permanent played by this effect. |
| Security play | A legal public attack checks EX11-061 from security and places Mirai in the battle area without payment. | Security effect is self `PlayWithoutCost` and marked `isSecurity`. |

## Peer and boundary evidence

The digivolution tests use a real EX11-020 Puppet host and EX11-021 Puppet evolution, with a level-4 Puppet and a non-Puppet peer in hand. The decline case proves no suspend, no play, and no delayed deletion when the optional cost is refused. Main-deck cards are used for all fixtures; no Digi-Egg is placed in a deck or security stack.

## Q&A coverage

Q5915 is covered by the public turn transition and exact-card trash assertion. Q5916 is covered by two public turn-end runs with BT13-099's independent End of Your Turn processing ordered before and after Mirai's delayed delete. Both orders delete only the EX11-020 Puppet played by Mirai; the evolved EX11-021 host and BT13-099 remain. No synthetic timing API, injected trigger, `it.fails`, or skip is used.
