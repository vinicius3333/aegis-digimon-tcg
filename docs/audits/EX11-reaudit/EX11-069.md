# EX11-069 Yuuki audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines Yuuki as a purple/red cost-4 LIBERATOR Tamer. At the start of the owner's main phase and on play, by trashing one card from hand it gains 1 memory. Once per turn during the owner's turn, when one of the owner's Digimon attacks and the controller has four or fewer cards in hand, that Digimon may digivolve into a Dark Dragon or Evil Dragon trait Digimon from the trash with digivolution cost reduced by 1. At the end of all turns, if the controller has four or fewer cards in hand, by suspending Yuuki the controller may return one Evil, Dark Dragon, or Evil Dragon trait card from trash to hand. Security plays Yuuki without paying its cost.

The module uses exclusive `registerIrCard`, has full coverage, and encodes the optional trash cost, once-per-turn attack watcher, trigger-subject evolution from trash, trait filter, hand-size boundary, reduced cost, end-of-all-turns return, self-suspension cost, and Security play.
