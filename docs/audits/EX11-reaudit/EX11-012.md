# EX11-012 Medusamon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog prints Rush and Progress, plus `[When Digivolving] [End of Attack] You may delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with as much or less DP as this Digimon. Then, by returning 1 card from your opponent's trash to the bottom of the deck, they play 1 [Petrification] Token.` The token reminder text supplies its own `[Your Turn] This Digimon can't suspend` and On Deletion security behavior. Medusamon also prints `[On Deletion] Trash your top security card` and `[All Turns] When this Digimon would leave the battle area, by deleting 1 Token, it doesn't leave.`

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. It correctly keeps token reminder behavior out of Medusamon, models both When Digivolving and End of Attack, uses opponent-trash → deck-bottom as a paid return cost, places the token as an opponent Digimon, and uses an `isSelfRef` leave-play replacement with token deletion cost. No card IR defect was established.

All deck and security fixtures use main-deck Digimon; no Digi-Egg is used in deck or security. Public play, evolution, attack, and security-related paths are exercised. The explicit deletion/return-to-hand replacement tests use the named testkit leave/delete seam because no ordinary public card action in this fixture creates each required leave event; they are retained as observable engine-seam tests rather than timing claims.
