# EX11-054 — Owen Dreadnought

Owen Dreadnought is a red LIBERATOR Tamer. Its catalog clauses are:

- `[Start of Your Turn]` if memory is 2 or less, set it to 3;
- `[All Turns]` when one of your Digimon is played or digivolves, if it has `[Reptile]` or `[Dragonkin]`, by suspending this Tamer, draw 1, then one of your `＜Progress＞` Digimon gets +3000 DP for the turn;
- `[Security]` play this card without paying its cost.

Q5908 confirms that the part after “after” cannot be processed when the suspend cost is not paid.

## Peer and boundary evidence

The watcher tests use Reptile and Dragonkin peers, plus a nonmatching `Dragon`-trait near-peer. A two-Progress board proves the target count is exactly one, and the memory-3 start-of-turn case covers the threshold boundary. Structural matching asserts the explicit Reptile/Dragonkin union, so near-match traits are not admitted. All hand, deck, and security fixtures use legal main-deck cards; no Digi-Egg is placed in deck or security. The security scenario uses an inert legal attacker and security card while the opponent performs the public check.
