# EX11-022 Karakurumon re-audit

The catalog identifies EX11-022 as the Yellow/Purple level 5 Karakurumon with
the Puppet and LIBERATOR traits. It has alternate Yellow/Purple level-4 Puppet
digivolution for cost 3, Scapegoat, optional On Play/When Digivolving play of
one Puppet Digimon with 4000 DP or less from hand or trash without paying, and
turn-end deletion of the Digimon played by that effect. Its inherited All Turns
once-per-turn replacement deletes one of your Tokens or another Puppet to
prevent leaving play for causes other than your effect.

The card query returned Q5809 and Q5810. Q5809 is covered by the delayed-delete
positive; Q5810 is covered by the public end-turn ordering test.
The rules query returned comprehensive §16-32 for Scapegoat.
