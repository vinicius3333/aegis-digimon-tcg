# EX11-041 — Oblivimon

Oblivimon is a level 5 Black/Blue Cyborg/LIBERATOR Digimon. Its catalog clauses are:

- alternate digivolution over a level 4 `[Cyborg]` or `[Machine]` Digimon for cost 3;
- `[Security] [End of Opponent's Turn]` play this card without paying its cost;
- `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` flip the opponent's top face-down security card face up, De-Digivolve 1 one opposing Digimon, then, if it is the opponent's turn, optionally digivolve this Digimon into an `[Invisimon]` from hand for free;
- `[Your Turn]` when any of your Digimon checks face-up security, optionally place this Digimon's top stacked card face up at the bottom of security;
- inherited `[Your Turn]` attack target cannot change.

## Peer and stack evidence

The positive evolution uses BT12-086, a real level-4 Machine peer, and asserts the exact source stack. BT1-037 supplies the nonmatching level-4 boundary and is rejected. The public security-check stack uses Oblivimon over two inert Digimon and proves that only the top Oblivimon leaves while its stack survives. Fixtures contain no Digi-Egg in deck or security and no injected timing effects.
