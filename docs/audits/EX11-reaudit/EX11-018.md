# EX11-018 Ryugumon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines blue level-6 Aquatic Ryugumon with Evade and Decode (level 5 or lower with Aqua/Sea Animal in any trait). Its entry clause is `[On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] By placing 1 Digimon card with [Aqua] or [Sea Animal] in any of its traits from your hand as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, 1 of your Digimon unsuspends.` Its all-turns clause is once per turn: when effects add cards to this Digimon's stack, return one opponent Digimon with as many or fewer digivolution cards as this Digimon to the bottom deck.

The module is full IR and exclusively uses `registerIrCard`. It uses `traitContains` for Aqua/Sea Animal, places the paid card at the bottom of the self stack, shares the printed once-per-turn use key across On Play/When Digivolving/When Attacking, scopes Decode to the self stack and level <=5, and compares opponent stack counts against the source.

Fixtures use main-deck Digimon for incidental cards and no Digi-Egg in hand, deck, or security. Source-stack cards are deliberately stack materials. The primary On Play proof now uses public play; the remaining timing-specific and leave/add-source cases retain named production testkit drivers because no minimal public fixture creates those events without changing shared infrastructure. They assert observable zones and decisions rather than synthetic timing success.
