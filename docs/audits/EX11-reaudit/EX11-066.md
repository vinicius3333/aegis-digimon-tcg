# EX11-066 Xeno audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines Xeno as a black, cost-4 LIBERATOR Tamer. Its Rule also treats its name as [Zenith]. At the start of its owner's main phase and on play, the controller may trash one card with [Vemmon] in its text from hand to draw 1 and gain 1 memory. During all turns, when one of the controller's Vemmon Digimon is played or digivolves, the controller may suspend Xeno to reveal the top two cards of the deck, place every revealed card with [Vemmon] in its name under that triggering Digimon, and trash the rest. Security plays Xeno without paying its cost.

The module uses exclusive `registerIrCard`, has full coverage, and encodes the Rule alias, both draw/memory triggers, exact Vemmon-in-text matching, both public watcher events, optional self-suspension, reveal/add/rest dispositions, and Security play.
