# EX11-055 Chitose Horaiji audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines a red/purple cost-4 LIBERATOR Tamer. On Play and at the start of the owner's main phase, by trashing one Composite or Wicked God trait card from hand, it draws one and gains one memory. All Turns, when one of the controller's Composite or Wicked God Digimon is deleted, by suspending this Tamer it may play one exact Gazimon or Gizamon from hand without paying the cost. Its Security effect plays this card without paying the cost.

The module is exclusive `registerIrCard` and full coverage. It encodes the coupled trash/draw/memory payment, exact Composite/Wicked God trait alternatives, deletion watcher, optional free Gazimon/Gizamon play with suspension cost, and Security play.
