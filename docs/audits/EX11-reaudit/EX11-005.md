# EX11-005 Yaamon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog prints this inherited clause: `[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon may digivolve into a [Dark Dragon] or [Evil Dragon] trait Digimon card in the trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 1. If this effect digivolved, trash 2 cards in your hand.` The card is a purple level-2 Digi-Egg. Q5791 and Q5792 are the applicable KB entries.

The existing module is IR-only and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. The IR has `StartOfYourMainPhase`, `isInherited`, optional paid `Digivolve` from `trash`, `reduceCost: 1`, exact trait matching, and a conditional hand `Trash` count 2 gated by `ifThisEffectDigivolved`. No module defect was established.

| Clause | Behavioral proof | IR mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Public start-main trigger and legal trash source | Positive tests enter the production turn with `advance.runTurn(0)` and evolve EX11-048 + EX11-005 into EX11-049 from trash | `trigger: "StartOfYourMainPhase"`, `from: ["trash"]`, self target |
| Reduced paid cost and standard draw | Q5791 positive asserts stack identity, final memory `-3` from starting `10` (cost 3 after reduction plus normal turn transition), and the drawn card is then available to the trash count | `reduceCost: 1`, `payCost: true`; ordered `Digivolve` then `Trash` actions |
| Draw-before-trash ordering | Two-card hand leaves one after the bonus draw and trash-2; zero-card hand trashes the bonus draw; one-card hand trashes both the existing card and bonus draw | action order and `ifThisEffectDigivolved` condition |
| Optional refusal | Public turn flow with auto-decline leaves the source in trash, host unchanged, and hand intact | `optional: true`; conditional tail does not execute |
| Exact Dark Dragon/Evil Dragon boundary | BT10-074 Quetzalmon in trash is rejected; a qualifying card in hand is rejected because source is trash-only | exact `match: "trait"`; `from: ["trash"]` |
| Cross-card stack and peer route | A public alternate host stack carrying Yaamon evolves the Evil Dragon peer EX10-053 from trash, preserving the host stack identity and conditional hand cleanup | inherited `StartOfYourMainPhase`, trash source, exact Dark/Evil trait union |
| Controller/timing | Opponent's production main phase does not activate the inherited effect | start-main trigger ownership in production loop |

All fixtures use main-deck Digimon only and contain no Digi-Egg in deck or security.

## Q&A coverage

- Q5791: covered by the public positive flow; the bonus draw is observed before two hand cards are trashed.
- Q5792: covered by zero-card and one-card hand cases. The one-card case proves the card drawn by evolution is also trashed when possible.
