# EX11-028 Galemon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines green level-4 Bird Dragon Galemon with normal green level-3 evolution cost 2 and play cost 4. Its entry effects are `[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may suspend 1 Digimon.` Its all-turns once-per-turn effect triggers when any of your Digimon suspend; with 1 or fewer Tamers, it may play exact-name Shoto Kazama from hand without paying. Its inherited clause is `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon wins a battle, gain 1 memory.`

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. On Play and When Digivolving allow any-player suspension. The all-turns watcher is controller-scoped to own Digimon, exact-name Shoto, hand source, free play, and one-or-fewer-Tamers gating. No IR defect was established.
