# EX11-034 QueenBeemon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines green/black level-6 QueenBeemon with 12 play cost, 12000 DP, normal level-5 green or black evolution cost 4, and alternate level-5 Royal Base evolution cost 3. Its traits are Cyborg, X Antibody, Royal Base, LIBERATOR, and Insectoid. The shared once-per-turn On Play/When Digivolving/When Attacking effect may place one Royal Base trait card from hand or trash face up as the top or bottom own security card, then delete up to 8 opponent play cost total, adding 2 to the budget per own face-up security card. A second shared once-per-turn When Digivolving/When Attacking effect may play one card with Royal Base in its text from hand, reducing its paid play cost by 1 per own face-up security card.

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. It encodes the alternate evolution, exact hand/trash source zones, face-up top/bottom security placement, opponent Digimon deletion budget, face-up scaling, the separate shared OPT key, Royal Base text matching, and paid play-cost reduction. No IR defect was established.
