# EX11-002 Hiyarimon re-audit

## Card and sources

The committed catalog identifies EX11-002 as Hiyarimon, a Blue level 2 Digi-Egg
with inherited text:

> [Your Turn] While your opponent has no Digimon with digivolution cards, this
> [Ice-Snow] trait Digimon can also attack your opponent's unsuspended Digimon.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card EX11-002` returned Q6044: the condition “your
opponent has no Digimon with [the specified property]” is met when the opponent
has no Digimon. The local KB index lists no other EX11-002 Q&A. The rules query
also returned comprehensive §15-11-2-3-3, which supports re-evaluation when an
overall condition changes as board state changes.

## Clause -> test -> IR

| Printed clause | Behavioural evidence | IR evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `[Your Turn]` | `applies only on the inherited source controller's turn` proves the permission is absent on the opponent's turn and present after the turn seat changes. | `trigger: "YourTurn"` |
| `While your opponent has no Digimon with digivolution cards` | `treats having no opposing Digimon as satisfying Q6044`; `does not grant ... when any opposing Digimon has digivolution cards`; and `recalculates ... after a legal opponent evolution` prove empty, stacked, and state-transition cases. | `condition.kind: "opponentHasNone"`, opponent controller, `kind: ["Digimon"]`, `digivolutionCards: "hasAny"` |

| `can also attack ... unsuspended Digimon` | Positive test uses the public `attack` intent against an unsuspended BT1-009 and asserts `{ok:true}`; blocked cases assert `illegal-target`. | `GrantCanAttackUnsuspended`, `count: 1`, `isSelf: true`, permanent duration. |

The compiled-card shape is asserted directly in `compiles the inherited clause
with exact Ice-Snow and opponent-stack filters`. The stack transition test
starts with EX11-014 over EX11-002 and evolves the opponent's BT1-009 into
BT1-014 through the public `digivolve` intent, asserting the resulting source
stack and loss of attack permission.

## Mutation sensitivity

The focused suite is sensitive to the card-specific condition: removing or
changing `digivolutionCards: "hasAny"` makes the legal opponent-evolution test
incorrectly retain attack permission and fail. Removing the exact Ice-Snow
trait filter makes the non-Ice-Snow Agumon host test fail. Removing the
`YourTurn` trigger makes the turn-controller test fail.
