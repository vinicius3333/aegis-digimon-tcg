# EX11-026 Pteromon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines green level-3 Pteromon with a green level-2 evolution cost of 0. Its entry text is `[When Moving] [On Play] You may suspend 1 Digimon. If this effect suspended your Digimon, 1 of your Digimon with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits or the [Vortex Warriors] trait gets +3000 DP until your opponent's turn ends.` The inherited text is `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When this Digimon wins a battle, gain 1 memory.`

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. Both entry timings allow any-player suspension, then gate the +3000 bonus on the effect having suspended the controller's own Digimon. The buff uses partial trait matching for Avian/Bird and exact matching for Vortex Warriors. The inherited effect is a Your Turn once-per-turn battle-win watcher. No IR defect was established.
