# EX11-049 — Punkmon

Punkmon is a level 4 Purple/Red Dark Dragon/LIBERATOR Digimon. Its catalog clauses are:

- alternate `[Digivolve] Lv.3 with [Evil] trait: Cost 2`;
- `[When Attacking]` trash 2 cards from hand, then this Digimon may digivolve into a `[Dark Dragon]` or `[Evil Dragon]` trait Digimon card in the trash with the digivolution cost reduced by 2;
- inherited `[Your Turn]` this Digimon gets +2000 DP.

The local Q&A has one card-specific entry, Q5903, confirming that a card trashed by this attack cost may immediately be used for the trash-based digivolution.

## Peer and stack evidence

The attack fixtures use legal main-deck cards, including an Evil Dragon alternate target and a nonmatching Dragon near-peer. The target is selected from the actual trash created by the same public attack. A zero-card hand boundary proves the effect does not begin without cost material, while a one-card public boundary proves the mandatory sequence consumes the one available card and still permits the follow-up evolution. The inherited test uses a real Punkmon under a BT1-009 host and crosses the turn boundary through the public loop. No Digi-Egg is placed in deck or security.
