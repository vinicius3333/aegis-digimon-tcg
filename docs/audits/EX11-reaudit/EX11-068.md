# EX11-068 — Violet Inboots

Violet Inboots is a purple LIBERATOR Tamer with play cost 4. Its catalog clauses are:

- `[Start of Your Turn]` if memory is 2 or less, set it to 3;
- `[Your Turn]` when one of your `[Ghost]` trait Digimon attacks, by suspending this Tamer, draw 1 and trash 1 card from hand; if attacking by `<Execute>`, that Digimon may digivolve into a `[Ghost]` trait Digimon card in hand with its digivolution cost reduced by 2;
- `[Security]` play this card without paying its cost.

Q5938 defines “if attacking by `<Execute>`” as an attack triggered by the `<Execute>` mechanic. Q6517 concerns a different EX11-062 suspension follow-up and is not applicable to this card.

## Peer and stack evidence

The public tests use BT20-072 (Ghost + Execute), BT20-063 (Ghost without Execute), and EX11-049 (non-Ghost) as meaningful attacker boundaries. The positive Execute flow proves the real end-of-turn Execute attack and Violet's draw/trash response, while observing the evolved EX11-051 in trash after the legal Execute self-deletion. The non-Execute Ghost boundary remains negative.

All fixtures use legal main-deck cards. No Digi-Egg is placed in a deck, security, or battle stack.

## Q&A coverage

Q5938 is covered publicly for the Execute attack's Violet response and positively by the reduced-cost Ghost digivolution. The real BT20-063 non-Execute Ghost attack remains negative. The traced shared seam was `CombatController.resolveAttack`: it already constructed `attackTrigger.attackMechanic = "Execute"`, but omitted that field from `attackSubTriggerPayload`, so `triggerAttackBy` could not see it in prepared or nested `whenAttacking` contexts. The fix copies the field into the shared payload. A focused controller regression asserts the payload preserves `Execute`, while EX11-068 proves the public card outcome. Q6517 is unrelated to EX11-068.
