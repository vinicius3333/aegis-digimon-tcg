# EX11-053 Omekamon audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines white level-4 Omekamon, play cost 5, 5000 DP, Puppet/X Antibody/LIBERATOR, with no evolution cost. On Play, it may place one Royal Knight trait Digimon from hand as the bottom digivolution card of any King Drasil_7D6 on the field, then draw 1. On Deletion, if the controller has one or fewer security cards, it may play Omnimon (X Antibody) from hand or under one of the controller's King Drasil_7D6s without paying the cost, then must place Omekamon as that played Digimon's bottom digivolution card. Its Rule name also treats it as X Antibody.

The module uses only `registerIrCard`, has full coverage, and encodes the field union (battle area or breeding), exact King Drasil_7D6 host scope, Royal Knight placement cost, security threshold, hand/King Drasil source union, bound played-card placement, and Rule name grant.
