# EX11-058 — Yao Qinglan

Yao Qinglan is a blue LIBERATOR Tamer. Its catalog clauses are:

- `[Start of Your Main Phase]` by placing one level 5 or lower card with `[Aqua]` or `[Sea Animal]` in any trait from hand as the bottom digivolution card of one matching Digimon, gain 1 memory;
- `[All Turns]` when one of your Digimon is played or digivolves and has `[Aqua]` or `[Sea Animal]` in any trait, by suspending this Tamer, draw 1; if played by `<Decode>`, one opposing Digimon cannot suspend until its turn ends;
- `[Security]` play this card without paying its cost.

Q5911 defines the Decode provenance condition. Q5912 distinguishes a Decode play trigger from a later digivolution trigger.
