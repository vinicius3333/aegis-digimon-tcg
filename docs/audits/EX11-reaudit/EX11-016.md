# EX11-016 PolarBearmon re-audit

The catalog identifies EX11-016 as the Blue/Yellow level 5 PolarBearmon with
the Ice-Snow and LIBERATOR traits. Its alternate route is level 4 with
Ice-Snow for cost 3. Printed behavior is Iceclad; On Play/When Digivolving,
trash any 2 opposing digivolution cards, then optionally place one of the
opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards as top or bottom security; and
the inherited Your Turn condition grants the Ice-Snow host Piercing and
Security Attack +1 while the opponent has no Digimon with digivolution cards.

The card query returned Q5802 and Q6046. Q5802 is proven by the battle test
where deleting the opponent's final stacked Digimon makes Piercing available
at the same timing and the attack performs the expected checks. Q6046 is proven
by the empty-opponent board in the inherited-grant test. The rules query
returned comprehensive §16-35 for Iceclad.
