# EX11-071 Cool Boy audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines Cool Boy as a white, cost-3 LIBERATOR Tamer. On Play, it reveals the top three cards, adds one exact-name Omekamon or Omnimon (X Antibody) and one Royal Knight or LIBERATOR trait card to hand, and bottoms the rest. Its Main effect may return this Tamer to the bottom of the deck to play a hand Royal Knight or LIBERATOR with play cost at least 4, reducing that play cost by 2. Security plays Cool Boy without paying its cost.

The module uses exclusive `registerIrCard`, has full coverage, and encodes exact bracketed-name matching, two independent add slots, bottom-deck disposition, play-cost floor, folded reduction, self-return cost, optional decline behavior, and Security play.
