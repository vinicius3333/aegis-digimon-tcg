# EX11-035 Zephagamon audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines green level-6 Zephagamon, play cost 11, 12000 DP, normal green level-5 evolution for cost 3, and Magic Knight, Vortex Warriors, LIBERATOR, and Bird Dragon traits. It has Piercing, Vortex, and Blocker. Its When Digivolving effect may unsuspend one Digimon, then may suspend one Digimon, with either player's Digimon legal (Q5857). Its All Turns Once Per Turn watcher is caused by any of the controller's Digimon suspending and may play one green Digimon with 3000 DP or less and Avian or Bird in any trait from hand without cost. The play ceiling gains 2000 for each suspended Digimon.

The module uses only `registerIrCard`. The local correction adds the missing normal green level-5 evolution requirement and changes the Avian/Bird target to `traitContains`, so traits such as Bird Dragon and Giant Bird satisfy the printed partial-trait wording. The unsuspend/suspend targets and suspension scaling remain cross-player (`controllerDefault: "any"`), while the trigger source remains the controller's own Digimon.
