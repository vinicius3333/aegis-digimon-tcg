# EX11-021 Kokeshimon audit

## Contract and IR

The catalog defines yellow/purple level-4 Puppet Kokeshimon with normal level-3 yellow or purple evolution cost 3 and alternate level-3 Puppet cost 2. Its When Digivolving clause may play one Mirai Kinosaki from hand without paying if the controller has 1 or fewer Tamers. Its inherited clause is `[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When one of your opponent's Digimon attacks, by deleting 1 of your other Digimon, end that attack.`

The module is full IR and registers exclusively with `registerIrCard`. The Mirai action is exact-name, hand-only, free, optional, and gated at one or fewer Tamers. The inherited effect is an opponent-turn watcher with an optional abortable delete-own-other cost followed by `EndAttack`. No module defect was established.

| Clause | Behavioral proof | IR mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Alternate/ordinary evolution | Public evolution covers Puppet cost 2, normal yellow/purple cost 3, and rejects off-color level 3; realistic Puppet stack is used | `digivolutionRequirement` plus catalog normal costs |
| Mirai with 0/1 Tamer | Public evolution flows prove Mirai leaves hand and enters play with no Tamer and exactly one existing Tamer | `permanentCount lte 1`, exact `Mirai Kinosaki`, `from: hand`, `payCost:false` |
| Two-Tamer and refusal boundaries | Public evolution with two Tamers leaves Mirai in hand without an optional prompt; a separate public evolution with one Tamer and optional decline leaves Mirai in hand | optional condition and target filter |
| Opponent attack cost and end | Public opponent attack ends before block/counter windows after deleting another Digimon, including an unaffected Progress attacker | `OpponentsTurn`, `whenOpponentAttacks`, `deleteOwn excludeSelf`, `EndAttack` |
| Cost failure | Public attack with deletion restriction leaves fodder and does not end attack | abortable delete cost |
| Q5806/Q5807/Q5808 | Covered by the public attack/end and blocked-cost tests | inherited effect implementation |

Fixtures contain no Digi-Egg in hand, deck, or security. All Mirai branches now use public digivolution intents and the real `WhenDigivolving` window; no timing-driver API is used.

## Q&A coverage

- Q5807: public attack proves transition to end-of-attack before block/counter windows.
- Q5808: the Progress attacker is unaffected by effects yet the attack still ends.
