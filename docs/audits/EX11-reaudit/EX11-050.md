# EX11-050 Loudmon audit

## Contract and implementation

The catalog defines purple/red level-5 Loudmon, play cost 7, 7000 DP, Cyborg/LIBERATOR/Dark Dragon, with purple or red level-4 evolution cost 4. Its alternate evolution is level 4 with Dark Dragon or Evil Dragon trait for cost 3. On Play and When Digivolving, it trashes two cards from hand, then deletes one opposing Digimon with DP no greater than one of the controller's Dark Dragon or Evil Dragon Digimon. All Turns grants Scapegoat to all own Dark Dragon/Evil Dragon Digimon while the controller has four or fewer cards in hand. Its inherited Your Turn effect grants Security Attack +1 to all own Dark Dragon/Evil Dragon Digimon under the same hand-size condition.

The module is exclusive `registerIrCard`, full coverage, and encodes the alternate trait evolution, mandatory two-card hand trash, live relative-DP comparison, both conditional aura effects, exact trait matching, and inherited turn restriction.
