# EX11-051 — Necromon

Necromon is a level 6 Purple Ghost/LIBERATOR Digimon. Its catalog clauses are:

- `＜Piercing＞` and `＜Execute＞`;
- `[On Play] [When Digivolving] [On Deletion]` delete one opponent's lowest-level Digimon, then optionally play a level 4 or lower `[Ghost]` from trash for free;
- a second `[On Deletion]` effect may free-digivolve one of your `[Ghost]` Digimon into a `[Ghost]` card from hand.

Q5904 asks about the order of the two simultaneous On Deletion effects. Q5905 covers a pending On Deletion effect leaving trash through a DemiMeramon interaction.

## Peer and stack evidence

All behavioral fixtures use legal main-deck Digimon. The positive On Deletion flow uses BT20-063 Ghost as the host, BT4-080 Ghost in trash, and BT11-078 Soulmon in hand, proving both Ghost trait routes across trash and hand. The public attack uses a high-DP BT1-080 attacker against a suspended Necromon, so deletion is resolved through battle rather than an injected delete verb. No Digi-Egg is used in deck or security.
