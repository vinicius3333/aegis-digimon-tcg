# BT21-088 Tagiru Akashi

## Source and implementation

Committed catalog `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`: purple/red Hunter/Hero Tamer, play cost 4. Q4602 defines “in its text”; Q4603 puts hand placement at the bottom under this Tamer (`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT21-088`).

Production registers exclusively `registerIrCard("BT21-088", compiled)`. Start Main optionally places one Save-text or Hero Digimon from hand under this Tamer, draws one and gains one memory. Your Turn optionally suspends this Tamer and transfers one card from under any own Tamer to the evolving Digimon’s bottom to reduce a Save-text or Hero evolution by one. Security plays itself free.
