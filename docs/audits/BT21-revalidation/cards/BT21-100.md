# BT21-100 — The Digimon I Designed

## Exact source facts

- `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` identifies BT21-100 as a purple Option, play cost 2, deck limit 4.
- Printed effects: while you have a Digimon or Tamer with `[Takato Matsuki]` in its name on the field, ignore this card's color requirement. Main: Draw 1, trash 1 hand card, then place this card in the battle area. During your turn, when an effect deletes a Digimon, gain Delay; Delay allows one own Guilmon/Growlmon-name Digimon to digivolve into a Growlmon/Gallantmon/Megidramon-name Digimon from trash without paying. Security: gain 1 memory, then place this card in the battle area.

## Implementation and clause mapping

`BT21-100.ts` registers only `registerIrCard("BT21-100", compiled)`. The static waiver is restricted to an own exact Takato Matsuki field card. Main draws one, trashes one hand card, and then places itself. The Your Turn deletion watcher requires an effect-caused Digimon deletion and grants a separate permanent Delay keyword; the optional free digivolution payload restricts both host and trash destination to the printed Guilmon/Growlmon and Growlmon/Gallantmon/Megidramon name families. Security gains one memory and places itself. Coverage is full and residual is empty.
