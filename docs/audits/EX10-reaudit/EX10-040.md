# EX10-040 DemiDevimon — re-audit

## Card summary

Catalog (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`): Purple Lv.3 Digimon, play cost 3,
DP 1000, digivolve from Purple Lv.2 for 0, form Rookie, attribute Virus, type Evil,
rarity C, max 4. No security text, not an ACE, no overflow.

Printed clauses:

1. Main: `[Start of Your Main Phase] If your opponent has 10 or fewer cards in their
   trash, trash the top 2 cards of both players' decks. Then, if they have 10 or more
   cards in their trash, gain 1 memory.`
   Two independently gated steps. The second gate is read after the first step resolves,
   and it is read against the OPPONENT's trash ("they"), not the controller's.
2. Inherited: `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] Trash the top card of both players' decks.`

No catalog discrepancy found: stored text matches the official wording, and every stored
field matches the printed card. `securityEffectText` is absent, as printed.

## Defects and gaps

- No defect in `EX10-040.ts`. The IR already models both gates as separate per-action
  conditions, and the interpreter honours that: `runAction` returns `false` for a failed
  gate (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts`, the "Per-action
  gate" block), and the action loop in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts` treats that return as "do not abort",
  so a later action still runs. Q5121 passes for the right reason. The module is unchanged.
- Defect in the previous test, fixed here: every clause was proved with
  `advance(...).fireForPermanent`, an injected timing. Nothing showed the trigger fires
  from the real turn loop, that it is scoped to the controller's own Main phase, that the
  main clause does not apply from under a host, or that the digivolution route and cost are
  real. All are now covered.
- Retained red: none.
- Remaining gap: the `[Once Per Turn]` SAME-TURN refusal on the inherited clause is not
  asserted. A second attack in one turn needs the attacker unsuspended, and no inert
  fixture card can do that; adding an unsuspend card would put another effect in the mill
  path. The gate is proved by its reset through the real turn loop (attack, hand the turn
  over, attack again on the next own turn), and by the IR `frequency: "OncePerTurn"`.
- Not applicable: security effect, ACE / Overflow, DigiXros, Link, ＜Save＞, ＜De-Digivolve＞,
  Digi-Egg route. This card prints none of them.
- Deck-out interaction is untested: nothing was checked for a mill that runs into an empty
  deck. `TrashTopDeck` reveals up to `amount` and trashes what it gets, so the clause
  itself cannot fault, but the loss condition is engine-owned and out of this lane.
