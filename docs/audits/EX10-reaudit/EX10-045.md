## Defect fixed locally

`save-placeunder-position-default` (REVIEW-NOTES seam 16). The `[On Deletion] ＜Save＞` `PlaceUnder`
carried no `position`, and `runPlaceUnder` reads `belowTop: action.position !== "bottom"`, so the
saved card landed directly beneath the Tamer instead of at the bottom of its stack (comprehensive
4-3). Fixed in `EX10-045.ts` with `position: "bottom"`, matching the accepted shape in
`EX10-026.ts` / `EX10-027.ts`. The behavioural test seeds two cards under the Tamer and asserts the
exact resulting order `[tuwarmon, under0, under1]`, so the line is load-bearing.

No other module change was needed: the DigiXros pair, the shared once-per-turn key, the
`fromSelectionRef` binding of the second keyword, the `[Bagra Army]` `hostFilter` on the cost and
the inherited `isSelfRef` scope all matched the printed text under review.

## Test rewrite

The committed test proved most clauses through the injected timing surface
(`advance.fireForPermanent`, `advance.verb.deletePermanent`, `advance.verb.trashDigivolutionCards`),
which earns no behavioural credit under this brief, and it had no once-per-turn proof at all.
Every clause is now driven by public intents: `playCard`, `digivolve` (with `useAlternateCost` and
`alternateRequirementIndex`), `attack`, `declareBlock`, `playCard` with `digiXros`, plus
`startTurnLoop` for the once-per-turn reset and the duration crossing into the opponent's turn.
`advance` is used only for `waitForMainPhase` / `endMainPhaseIfOpen`, which are turn-loop
navigation, not injected timing.

Fixture notes for reuse:

- There is no inert `[Bagra Army]` Digimon in the catalog. `EX10-026` (SkullKnightmon) and
  `EX10-044` (Damemon) are the quietest: all their printed clauses are `[On Play]` /
  `[When Digivolving]` / `[On Deletion]`, so they sit still on a board and never open a decision.
- `BT12-094` (Yuu Amano) is the neutral ＜Save＞ Tamer: it prints no `[On Deletion]` or battle-time
  clause. `EX10-064` would fire a `[Start of Your Main Phase]` prompt inside a turn loop.
- `EX10-028` (Landramon, `[Mineral]`/`[LIBERATOR]`) is the trait-mix negative for the cost's
  `hostFilter`; it does carry an inherited deletion clause, but that clause never fires here
  because the cost is refused before anything is trashed.

## Corrected premise (not an engine defect)

A first draft asserted that a SECOND copy of Tuwarmon played in the same turn was blocked by the
shared once-per-turn key. It is not, and should not be: `[Once Per Turn]` is per Digimon, so a
second copy has its own use. The test now proves the shared key the right way — one Digimon,
`[When Digivolving]` then `[When Attacking]` in the same turn, then a reset on its next own turn.

## Observation for the coordinator (low priority, not a retained red)

An unblocked seat-0 attack declared against `{ kind: "player" }` while the defender holds a
Digimon that ＜Collision＞ makes eligible to block appears to leave the attack mid-resolution after
its security check: with `turnSeat === 1`, `phase === Main`, no `pendingDecision`, the attacker
unsuspended, and after `settle` on the security endpoint plus a 30-tick drain, seat 1's own
`attack` intent was still refused `{ ok: false, reason: "wrong-phase" }`. `validateAttack`
(`apps/api/src/engine/actions/attack.ts:80`) returns `wrong-phase` for `deps.combat.isAttacking`,
which is the only remaining branch consistent with the observed state. `waitForMainPhase` does not
wait on `combat.isAttacking` (it checks `mainEntryPending`, `activeWindowToken` and the two
resolution depths), so a test crossing turns after such an attack has no seam-level way to wait
for the combat to close. Worked around here by sourcing the grant from `[On Play]` instead of
`[When Attacking]` in that one test; the `[When Attacking]` arm is still proved behaviourally in
the once-per-turn test, where the defender has no battle-area Digimon. Suggested owner: engine or
testkit lane, as either a `combat.isAttacking` clause in `waitForMainPhase` or an
`advance(...).waitForCombatIdle()` affordance.

## Remaining gaps

- ＜Collision＞ is proved through block legality only, which is all the engine exposes
  (`combat/legality.ts hasCollision`); it grants no observable keyword.
- The inherited clause's `[Bagra Army]` `hostFilter` is proved structurally (IR assertion) and
  positively (a Bagra host draws). A behavioural negative would need a non-Bagra host whose
  digivolution cards some effect can trash; Tuwarmon's own cost cannot reach one by construction,
  and no other effect on a legal fixture board does. The `isSelfRef` half of the scope IS proved
  behaviourally (a different card trashed from the same host draws nothing).
