## What this re-audit changed

The committed test proved C6 only through `advance(s.engine).verb.suspend`, which the
brief rates as structural, and left C2, C3 and the `[Once Per Turn]` gate on C4 unproved.
Five tests were added or rewritten; the module (IR) needed no change.

1. `Q5048` was rewritten to run on the real block path: our attack, the opponent's
   `declareBlock`, the Blocker suspending, the link card paying itself as the cost.
   Both halves of `[All Turns]` are now public-intent proof — our turn (block) and the
   opponent's turn (their attack declaration suspending the attacker).
2. New: the `[Once Per Turn]` gate on C4, through the real turn loop. With ＜Link +2＞
   armed on the ledger, two links in one turn play exactly one Tamer even though the
   "1 or fewer Tamers" condition still holds after the first play; a third link on our
   next turn plays the second Tamer.
3. New: ＜Jamming＞ and ＜Retaliation＞ behavioural proofs through real battles.
4. New: a negative for the linked clause while the card is an ordinary battle-area
   Digimon.
5. `PlayWithoutCost` now asserts memory, so a silently paid play cost would fail.
6. Round 2: both ＜Link +N＞ setups moved off the `continuous.addLinkMaxGrant` cast onto
   `advance(engine).verb.grantLinkMax`. The grants now run after `ready()`; all 13 tests
   still pass with every assertion unchanged.
7. Fixture correction from coordinator review: every deck seeded BT1-001, a Digi-Egg,
   which the brief forbids. All seven occurrences are now BT1-013, an inert main-deck
   Lv.3, with the `drawn` / `top` aliases and every assertion unchanged. The
   opponent-turn payoff test also gained a hand-count assertion, so the drawn card is
   provably the effect's draw and not a turn draw step.

Mutation check (`isLinked` removed and the Tamer condition widened to `lte 9`, then
reverted): 6 of 13 tests fail. Note the unlinked negative is not the test that catches
`isLinked` — with the gate removed, the trash cost aborts on its own; ＜Retaliation＞ and
the two payoff tests catch it instead. Reported rather than papered over.

## Remaining gaps

- The `[Your Turn]` restriction on C4 has no negative. No public intent links a card to
  your Digimon during the opponent's turn (`linkCard` is a main-phase action of the turn
  player, and no implemented card links an opponent-turn card onto your Digimon), so the
  gate is unreachable. Proved structurally by the IR-shape test only.
- The `[Once Per Turn]` proof and the Q5049 proof still need ＜Link +N＞ headroom, but they
  now arm it through the Advance Surface verb `advance(engine).verb.grantLinkMax`
  (engine lane seam 7), not a cast into `engine.continuous`. No `as unknown as` engine
  reach remains in the file. No printed ＜Link +X＞ host can replace it: Mienumon links only
  to a host with the [Appmon] trait, and in the OPT proof Mienumon is itself the host whose
  cap must rise, so neither BT21-101 nor BT26-086 is a legal substitute.
- No inert Appmon-trait Digimon exists in the catalog, so link hosts must be
  BT21-009 Gatchmon, which carries its own `whenLinked` clause. It is inert here only
  because [Haru Shinkai] is never in hand. A future host change could make these tests
  noisy.
- App Fusion is proved for one real fusion (Mirrormon top, Kabemon link) plus the full
  cost table for all 9 ordered name pairs. The other 5 legal fusions are not driven end
  to end on a board.
