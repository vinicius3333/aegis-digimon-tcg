## Changes made

Only `EX10-004.test.ts`. The module (`EX10-004.ts`) was already correct and is unchanged.

Defects found and fixed in the test:

1. **The once-per-turn assertion was vacuous.** The fixture had a single hand card, trashed by
   the first (natural) resolution. The injected re-fire then found an empty hand, so the cost
   was unpayable and the "no second memory" assertion would have held even with the gate
   removed. The fixture now keeps a spare payable hand card and a second deck card, and the
   test asserts hand size and trash size as well as memory, so a missing gate is visible.
2. **No natural-origin proof of the substring name gate.** Added a `Lucemon: Chaos Mode`
   (BT7-111) breeding stack driven through the real `moveFromBreeding` intent.
3. **No proof that the watcher is not self-referential.** Added a case with Cupimon under an
   unrelated battle-area Digimon while a different Lucemon moves out of breeding.
4. **No opponent-side negative.** Added the opponent's own breeding move on the opponent's
   turn; nothing is granted.
5. **No Digi-Egg move negative.** Added the lone-Cupimon case, rejected `not-movable`.
6. **Q5010 was implied but not named.** The digivolution-card placement is now labelled with
   the Q&A id.

## Round 3: catalog search and the natural-origin once-per-turn proof

### Catalog search for a second breeding move in one turn

Searched all 4388 catalog entries for printed text mentioning the breeding area together with
move / return / place (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, main, inherited and
security text). 45 cards matched; the ones that move a Digimon out of breeding by EFFECT
rather than by the breeding-phase action are:

| Card | Text | Implemented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EX10-013 Lucemon | `[Breeding] [When Digivolving] This Digimon may move.` | yes — `MovePermanent` `direction: "toBattle"` |
| P-130 Lui Ohwada | `[On Play] You may move 1 of your level 3 or higher Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area.` | yes |
| BT1-089 Mimi Tachikawa | `[Main]` suspend to move a level 3+ Digimon from breeding | module present |
| BT14-088 Gennai | `[Opponent's Turn]` … move 1 of your Digimon from breeding | module present |
| EX9-057 Abbadomon Core | `[Breeding] [Opponent's Turn]` … this Digimon moves to the battle area | module present |
| BT20-095 Fellowship of Hope's Keepers | ＜Delay＞ move a [Chronicle] Digimon from breeding | module present |

`primitives.ts movePermanentZone` fires `whenMovedFromBreeding` (and
`whenOpponentMovedFromBreeding`) for effect moves exactly as
`GameEngine.handleMoveFromBreeding` does for the player verb, and it is explicitly NOT gated on
`Phase.Breeding` or the one-action-per-turn limit. So a second move in the same turn IS publicly
reachable; the rubric-ceiling policy is not needed.

Refilling the emptied breeding area mid-turn also has printed support: P-123 Ukkomon and
BT16-082 Ukkomon both hatch on `whenMovedFromBreeding` (an effect hatch, no phase gate).

### The proof

`refuses the second grant in the same turn: two Lucemon leave breeding through public intents`
drives only public intents:

1. `moveFromBreeding` (the breeding-phase action) moves EX10-013 Lucemon out. EX10-004, sitting
   under a battle-area host, pays the hand trash, draws and gains memory. P-123 Ukkomon's own
   watcher hatches BT1-006 Cupimon into the freed breeding slot. BT1-006 prints only a
   `[When Attacking]` inherited clause, so the new stack adds no competing EX10-004 source.
2. `digivolve` in the breeding area turns that Cupimon into a second EX10-013, whose
   `[Breeding] [When Digivolving]` move carries it to the battle area — the second
   `whenMovedFromBreeding` of the turn, from the same single EX10-004 source.
3. Endpoints: the second Lucemon is in the battle area and breeding is empty (the move really
   happened), the trash still holds exactly the one card paid the first time, the deck lost
   exactly one card (the digivolution draw, §6-2-2) and memory moved by exactly the
   digivolution cost of 5. No second trash, draw or memory.

`resets on the next own turn: a move on turn 1 and a move on turn 3 both grant` runs the real
turn loop: turn 1 uses the breeding-phase move verb, turn 2 is the opponent's, turn 3 hatches
(that turn's breeding action) and moves the fresh Lucemon by EX10-013's effect. Each firing
trashes exactly one hand card, so the trash count (1 after turn 1, still 1 after turn 2, 2 after
turn 3) is the reset signal that survives the loop's own draws.

## Remaining gaps

- **The `[Your Turn]` gate cannot be isolated.** Your own Digimon can only move from breeding
  on your own turn, so no fixture separates the turn gate from the `controller: "mine"` subject
  filter. The opponent-move test exercises both together.
- Observation for the coordinator, not a defect in this card: digivolving in the breeding area
  charges the full digivolution cost from memory (the same-turn test goes 8 -> 3 for EX10-013's
  cost of 5). That matches the printed cost; it is recorded only because the reset test can
  drive memory negative inside the turn loop.
- No engine seam is red. No `it.fails` / `it.skip` retained.
