## What the previous audit missed

The committed suite (base `46810fe1e`) proved every clause with `advance(...).fire(...)`
and `advance(...).verb.deletePermanent(...)`. Under this run's rules that is structural
only, so the prior 10/10 claim had zero behavioural credit. It also had:

- no digivolution at all (no route, no cost, no bonus draw, no illegal-source negative);
- no real attack declaration and no real turn loop;
- no Q5017 and no Q5018 case (deletion prevention was untested in either direction);
- no Q5656 case;
- a colour/DP filter "proved" only by a card that matched it (EX10-007 is Red), with no
  candidate the filter had to reject;
- a suspended-Creepymon fixture (`suspended: true`) for the end-of-turn case that cannot
  prove anything through the real loop, because the turn's own Unsuspend step clears it.

No defect was found in `EX10-009.ts`. The module is unchanged from `HEAD`.

## Retained red

`it.fails("Q5656 loses its When Attacking window when the trash digivolve is ordered first")`.

Q5656 says the two `[When Attacking]` reactions (EX10-009's own clause and BT24-078
Creepymon (X Antibody)'s `{Trash} [Your Turn]` digivolve) trigger simultaneously so the
player chooses the order, and that digivolving first costs you EX10-009's `[When
Attacking]`.

- Seam: the sub-trigger fire path in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/subtriggers.ts` and the
  timing-window queue in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/EffectContext.ts` (`fireTiming` /
  `fireSubTriggers`).
- Expected: an `orderTriggers` decision is raised; with the BT24-078 branch first, breeding
  stays empty and BT1-013 stays in trash.
- Actual: decisions are `["optional","optional","optional"]` — no `orderTriggers` is ever
  offered — and after BT24-078 digivolves over EX10-009 the already-queued `[When
  Attacking]` entry still resolves, putting BT1-013 into breeding. The queue does not
  re-check that the effect's source is still the permanent's top card.
- Not fixable in this lane: both defects live in the engine, and BT24-078 is another card's
  file (it also still carries `// @ts-nocheck`).
