# EX10-066 Akihiro Kurata — re-audit

Purple Tamer, play cost 4, DP 0, no evolution costs, max 4 per deck, rarity U.

## Printed clauses

1. `[Start of Your Turn]` If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3.
2. `[End of Your Turn]` If you have 6 or fewer cards in your hand, by placing this Tamer as
   the bottom digivolution card of any of your Digimon with `[Belphemon]` in their names,
   that Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card with `[Belphemon]` in its name in the
   trash without paying the cost.
3. `[Security]` Play this card without paying the cost.

No inherited effect.

## What the previous audit missed

The committed test file proved every clause with injected timing only:
`advance(...).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnStartTurn | OnEndTurn, ...)` and
`fireForInstance(EffectTiming.SecuritySkill, ...)`. Under this run's evidence standard that
is structural proof and scores 0 behaviourally, so the previous 10/10 claim was not
supported. It also had these gaps:

- No real turn loop anywhere: neither trigger was ever proved to fire on its own.
- The `[Security]` clause was proved by injecting the timing on a face-down security card,
  not by a security check that an attack opens.
- No proof that the Tamer lands at the BOTTOM of the digivolution stack: the host had no
  pre-existing digivolution cards, so `stack[0]` was trivially the Tamer.
- No proof that the digivolution requirement is still checked.
- No proof that the "may" can be declined.
- No negative for the Tamer being inert in the trash or the hand.
- The memory negative used a hand-set gauge with no turn boundary, so it never showed the
  clause is evaluated at the real start of a turn.

The rewritten file uses `s.engine.startTurnLoop()` plus `advance(...).waitForMainPhase` /
`endMainPhaseIfOpen` for every timing, `playCard` for the Tamer, and `attack` for the
security check. It contains no `advance(...).fire*` call.

## Catalog discrepancy (reported, not edited)

`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` stores the EX10-066 effect text with:

- a non-breaking space (U+00A0) after each `[Belphemon]` token (offsets 219 and 299);
- the phrase "in its name" duplicated: "...a Digimon card with [Belphemon] in its name in
  its name in the trash...". The official English text prints it once.

Neither affects behaviour — the compiler already produced the right filters — but the
duplicated phrase is a transcription defect in the catalog. The catalog is the coordinator's
file, so the test asserts the stored string verbatim (including the U+00A0 escapes) and the
defect is reported here.
