## Remaining gaps

1. ~~`[Once Per Turn]` is structural only.~~ **Closed.** The gate is now proved through public
   intents (see "Round 3" below).
2. **Q5139's simultaneity is structural.** The "all, without choosing" reading needs two of my
   Digimon leaving at the same instant; the injected deletion is the only way to arrange it here.
   The single-target prevention and its payment are proved publicly.
3. **Q5169's grant path is out of scope.** Testing it needs EX10-059's "gains this [All Turns]"
   grant, which is another lane's file.
4. **Latent engine seam (not a defect today).** `validateDigiXros`
   (`apps/api/src/engine/actions/digiXros.ts`) computes
   `perMaterialReduction = requirement.count === "∞" ? (requirement.costReduction ?? 1) : requirement.count`,
   so for a numeric `count` the `costReduction` field is **ignored** and `count` doubles as the
   per-material reduction. EX10-055 (and EX10-056 / EX10-058) happen to have `count === costReduction === 2`,
   so the result is right by coincidence. I checked `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`:
   no card currently has a numeric `count` differing from its `costReduction`, so nothing is
   wrong right now — but a future `[DigiXros -1] 2 cards …` recipe would silently overcharge or
   undercharge. Engine file, not mine to change.

### The proof

`"the opponent's two Option plays in one turn: the first departure is prevented, the second is
not, and the gate resets next turn"` runs the real turn loop
(`s.engine.startTurnLoop()` + `advance.waitForMainPhase` / `endMainPhaseIfOpen`) across four
turns. Seat 1 holds a Red source (BT1-013 Muchomon, an inert Lv.3 at 5000 DP — it satisfies the
Option's printed color requirement, checked by `GameEngine.printedColorRequirementMet`, and is
itself above that Option's own 4000 DP bound). Endpoints asserted:

| Step | Intent | Assertion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opponent's turn opens | turn loop | `turnSeat === 1`; Tactimon's stack has 4 cards |
| 1st departure | seat 1 `playCard` BT2-091 | Both allies still on my board (`battleArea` length 3); stack 4 -> 2 (the 2-card payment was made) |
| 2nd departure, same turn | seat 1 `playCard` BT2-091 | `battleArea` length 2 — one ally left the battle area; stack still 2, so the payment was affordable and simply not taken. The gate, not the cost |
| Next own turn, then opponent's turn again | turn loop | `turnSeat === 1` |
| 3rd departure | seat 1 `playCard` BT2-091 | The survivor is still on my board; stack 2 -> 0. The gate reset and paid again |

Because the replacement is `affectsAll` with `count: "all"`, the assertion does not depend on
which of the two equal-DP allies the Option's selection picks: after the second play exactly one
ally is gone, whichever it was, and that survivor is the one protected on the next turn.

The earlier injected-timing test (`advance.verb.deletePermanent`) is retained unchanged as a
structural companion; every assertion in this file is kept.

The coordinator's rubric-ceiling policy was **not** needed — the gate is exercised twice in one
turn by public intents.
