## What the previous audit missed

The committed test file proved both printed clauses only through injected timing —
`advance(s.engine).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnPlay, …)` and
`advance(s.engine).verb.deletePermanent(…)`. Neither earns behavioural credit under this
run's rules, so the prior 10/10 claim rested on structural evidence for every clause.
It also had no negative for the `[Myotismon] in its text` filter, no "you may" refusal for
`[On Deletion]`, no empty-hand path for the optional cost, no memory assertion proving the
Tamer was played *without paying the cost*, and no evolution-route coverage at all.
Its `[On Play]` fixture used EX10-053 / EX10-042 as the opposing stack; both carry their own
`[On Play]`/`[When Digivolving]` text, so the fixture was not inert.

## Defects found

None in the module. The compiled IR matches both printed clauses and the engine implements
them correctly:

- `optional: true` + `abortOnDecline: true` is the right encoding for an optional processing
  condition (CR 15-7-2: declining the condition cancels everything after it).
- `match: "text"` is exactly Q5134's union in `matchNameOrTrait`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts`).
- `excludeSameNameAsOwnTamers` filters against Tamers in the controller's battle area
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/play.ts:490`), which is what "any of your
  Tamers" means.

No test is retained red; no `it.fails` / `it.skip` in this lane.

## Catalog discrepancy (reported, not edited)

`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` stores EX10-051's `effectText` with a
**non-breaking space (U+00A0) at offset 147**, between `[Myotismon]` and `in its text`.
Every other space in the field is a plain space. It does not change behaviour — the compiled
token is `Myotismon` and the phrase is parsed at authoring time — but it makes an exact-text
assertion surprising, and it suggests the scraped text for this card (and possibly its
siblings) carries stray NBSPs. The catalog test encodes it as ` ` with a comment rather
than silently normalizing it.

## Remaining gaps

- No Tamer in the catalog is *named* Myotismon, so the "name" half of Q5134's union cannot be
  exercised through this card's own effect. Covered indirectly by `matchNameOrTrait`'s own
  unit tests.
- ＜De-Digivolve＞'s `levelFloor = 3` (a Lv.3 top is never demoted) is engine-level behaviour
  shared by every ＜De-Digivolve＞ card and is not re-proved here; the fixture uses a Lv.4 top
  over a Lv.3 source, which is the legal case for this card.
- The `[On Deletion]` clause is proved on deletion in battle. Deletion by an opponent's effect
  or by the rule sweep is the same trigger path and is not separately exercised.
