## What the previous audit missed

The committed test proved every clause through injected timing: `advance.fireForPermanent`,
`advance.fireForInstance` and `advance.verb.trash`. Under the re-audit rubric that scores 0
for behaviour. The rewrite removes every `advance.fire*` / `advance.verb.*` call. The file
now uses only:

- public intents `playCard`, `linkCard`, `attack`, `respondDecision`;
- the production turn machine (`advance.runTurn`, `startTurnLoop` + `waitForMainPhase` +
  `endMainPhaseIfOpen`), which are turn-loop drivers, not injected timings.

Three facts the old test could not have caught, now pinned:

1. The link-card trash is driven by a real card (BT25-073 Dragomon's [On Play] cost), so the
   clause is proved against a producing effect rather than a raw primitive call.
2. Q5172 now has a behavioural negative: two `linkCard` intents onto a base-limit-1 host,
   the old link trashed by the rule sweep, and no prompt, no draw, no suspend.
3. The [Once Per Turn] app fusion is proved across three real turns, including the fact that
   the OPPONENT's end of turn fuses nothing.

Also recorded, because it is a non-obvious endpoint: after the fusion, the stack under
EX10-019 Warudamon holds only the consumed link card EX10-043, because Warudamon's own
[When Digivolving] re-links EX10-017 out of the digivolution cards. The old test never
looked at the stack.
