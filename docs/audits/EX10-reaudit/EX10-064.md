## What the previous audit missed

1. **Injected timing.** Both endpoints of clause 1 and clause 3 were proved with
   `advance(...).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnStartMainPhase, …)` and
   `fireForInstance(EffectTiming.SecuritySkill, …)`. Neither earns behavioural credit.
   Clause 1 now runs through `startTurnLoop()` + `waitForMainPhase(0)`; clause 3 now runs
   inside a real security check driven by an `attack` intent at `{ kind: "player" }`.
2. **Vacuous draw assertion.** The old Q5174 test settled on "the drawn card is in hand" and
   asserted nothing else, so a second draw, a lost hand card, or a missing trash payment would
   all have passed. The replacements assert the whole hand, the whole deck, the whole trash and
   `pendingDecision === undefined`, and the three negative variants leave `deckA` on the deck —
   which is what makes the single draw load-bearing.
3. **No trait gate proof.** Nothing proved the `[Bagra Army]`/`[Twilight]` and `kind: ["Digimon"]`
   halves of the place cost. Added: a fixture holding only an off-trait Digimon (BT1-014) and an
   on-trait Digi-Egg (BT26-006) opens no prompt and draws nothing.
4. **No "may" refusal.** Added a decline path: the card stays in hand and no draw happens
   (`abortOnDecline`).
5. **No turn-ownership proof.** Added: the payment armed after my own main phase is untouched at
   the start of the opponent's main phase.
6. **No zone-inertness proof** (coordinator request). Added two negatives: copies sitting in hand
   and trash open no prompt and draw nothing across a real turn loop, and a DigiXros trash
   material is refused (`invalid-material`) while the Tamer is in the trash rather than in play.
7. **Bottom placement was under-specified.** The old fixture had one card under the Tamer, so
   "bottom" and "directly beneath the Tamer" were indistinguishable in a two-element array. The
   fixture now seeds two cards and asserts the exact three-element order.
8. **`Q5177` had no coverage at all.** Now partly covered — see gaps.
9. **`Q6961` had no coverage at all.** Now covered by a control/target pair.

## Findings

- **`cost.position: "bottom"` is inert on this path (engine seam, no behaviour change).**
  Deleting the field from the module, or flipping it to `"top"`, leaves all 20 behavioural tests
  passing; only the IR-shape test notices. `costs.ts` reads `belowTop: cost.position !== "bottom"`
  for permanent-source and security placements, but the loose-card "place under the source Tamer"
  branch never reaches that read, and its default placement is already the bottom of the Tamer's
  cards. So Q5174 holds behaviourally and the field is documentation. Reported, not changed —
  fixing it belongs in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/costs.ts`, not in a card lane.
- **The `playCard` DigiXros route does not consult this module.** `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digiXros.ts`
  reads the static `DIGIXROS_ZONE_EXPANDERS` table in `packages/shared/src/cards/zoneExpanders.ts`
  (`underTamerMax: 1`, `trashMax: 1`, trait gate `[Bagra Army]`/`[Twilight]`). Only the effect-play
  route (`interpreter/actions/play.ts`) reads the compiled `DigiXrosMaterialZoneExpansion`. Both are
  exercised here, but a reader should know that the `playCard` tests grade the shared table, not the
  card module. The registry comment already says so.
- **Q6961 is implemented correctly.** With EX10-044 Damemon trashed from the [Bagra Army] attacker's
  digivolution cards by BT26-006's cost, its pending ＜Draw 1＞ fires when Damemon stays in the trash
  (control) and is lost when EX10-064's expansion takes Damemon as a DigiXros material — exactly the
  ruling. No defect.
- No red tests retained. No engine gap blocked a clause.

## Remaining gaps

- **Q5177 / Q5179 "multiples of the specified cards would be played" is not reachable through a
  public intent.** `playCard` plays exactly one card, so there is no way to have two DigiXros cards
  in one would-be-played window. What is proved instead: `refuses a second activation of an already
  suspended copy in the same turn` shows the suspend cost is real and per-activation, and the two-copy
  tests prove quotas add. The genuine simultaneous case stays unproved.
- The [Security] test asserts memory is unchanged by the play; a hypothetical bug that both charged
  and refunded 4 memory would slip past. The printed cost of 4 is asserted alongside it.
