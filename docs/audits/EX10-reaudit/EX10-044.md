## Defect found and fixed

**＜Save＞ placed Damemon just beneath the Tamer instead of at the bottom of its stack.**
`runPlaceUnder` calls `relocateByEffect(..., { belowTop: action.position !== "bottom" })`
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts:340`). The compiled
`OnDeletion` `PlaceUnder` carried no `position`, so `belowTop` defaulted to true. Q5126 and
Comprehensive Rules 4-3 require the bottom.

Fix: `position: "bottom"` on the `OnDeletion` `PlaceUnder` in `EX10-044.ts`, matching the
accepted shape in `EX10-026.ts` / `EX10-027.ts`.

Revert check (`logs/EX10-044-revert-check.log`): with the line removed, 3 tests fail,
including `expected [ 'inst-22', 'inst-23', 'inst-18' ] to deeply equal
[ 'inst-18', 'inst-22', 'inst-23' ]` — the saved card lands directly beneath the Tamer.
With it restored, all 8 pass.

The `[On Play]` cost already carried `position: "bottom"`, and there it is inert: the
loose-card place-cost branch hardcodes `belowTop: false`
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/costs.ts:2133`). It is kept because it documents
the intent and because a future refactor of that branch would silently change the endpoint.

## Remaining gaps

- **Q5160 is not reachable through the public seam and the rule it states is not
  implemented.** The ruling: an inherited effect pending activation lapses when the card
  leaves the area it was in while activation is still pending (trashed from digivolution
  cards, then played out of the trash before the inherited draw activates). The engine has
  no general "pending activation lapses when the source leaves" gate — a search for such a
  guard finds only per-card notes (`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/AUDIT.md` on Q4285), not an
  engine seam. Reproducing the scenario also needs two effects interleaved inside one trash
  batch (an `[All Turns]` trash plus a play-from-trash), which no single public intent
  produces. Left unasserted rather than guessed; flagged for the coordinator as an engine
  question, not an EX10-044 question.
- The `[On Play]` cost's `from: ["trash"]` branch is not separately proved: with a
  [Bagra Army] card in both hand and trash the engine picks one, and there is no ruling
  fixing which. The negative test does show a trash card that fails the trait gate is not
  taken.
- No `[Tuwarmon]` with play cost above 7 exists in the catalog (BT11-082 and EX10-045 are
  both exactly 7, BT12-064 is 5), so the upper side of the `≤7` boundary cannot be shown
  with a real card. The lower side is shown at exactly 7, and the name gate is shown by the
  no-Tuwarmon negative.
- The inherited positive depends on EX10-045 Tuwarmon's `[When Digivolving]` clause, which
  another lane owns. It passes today; if that card changes, this test is the canary.
