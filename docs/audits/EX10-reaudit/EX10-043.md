## Defects found and fixed

No module defect. The IR already matches every printed clause and every ruling, and the
engine already suppresses `whenLinkTrashed` for rule-driven trashes (`primitives.ts`
`trash`, `opts.byRule`), which is exactly Q5124.

What was wrong was the **evidence**. The committed test file (7 tests) was replaced
(16 tests) because:

1. `[On Play]` was proved with `advance(...).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnPlay, …)` —
   injected timing, no behavioural credit. Now proved by playing the card from hand for
   its 5 memory, with the paid cost, the hand, the board and the trash asserted.
2. `[When Digivolving]` had **no test at all**, in either the structural or behavioural
   sense beyond a shape assertion. Now proved through a real `digivolve` intent from
   ST6-05 (inert Purple Lv.3) for 2 memory, asserting the stack identity and the bonus
   draw, with a Red Lv.3 illegal-source negative alongside.
3. The Q5125 fixture was wrong. It seeded **two** link cards on BT21-009 Gatchmon, which
   prints no ＜Link +X＞ and therefore has a link cap of 1. The attack that drove the test
   also runs CR §4-9-5 rule processing (`GameEngine.trashExcessLinkCards`), so the second
   link card was liable to be trashed by rule rather than by the printed cost — the test
   could pass without the cost ever selecting it. The fixture now uses BT26-086 Dantemon
   (＜Link +6＞), so both link cards are legal and only the cost removes one.
4. Q5124 was not covered at all. The old "Q5124" test trashed a *neighbour's* link card,
   which is the host-scope negative, not the ruling. Q5124 is now proved through a real
   `linkCard` intent that overflows the cap.
5. The link effect had no level boundary test, no decline test, and no assertion that
   the deletion is restricted to level 4 or lower. All three are added.

## Retained reds and remaining gaps

None red. Two rubric ceilings, both fixture-shaped, not card defects:

- **`[Once Per Turn]` same-turn refusal is injected.** Two effect trashes of *this*
  Digimon's link cards in one turn need two link cards on a Sakusimon, and Sakusimon
  prints no ＜Link +X＞. No public intent or Advance Surface verb grants one — engine seam
  queue item 7, `testkit-link-grant-affordance`. The refusal test therefore seeds an
  over-cap board and drives `advance(...).verb.trash`; it runs outside the turn loop so
  rule processing never trims the seeded pair. Structural credit only.
- **The reset half is behavioural.** `[Once Per Turn] resets on the next own turn` runs
  the real turn loop (`startTurnLoop` / `waitForMainPhase` / `endPhase`), re-links a fresh
  EX10-038 through the public `linkCard` intent on the next own turn, and shows the gate
  pay out again. Only the trash itself is verb-driven.

Two observations for the coordinator, neither owned by this lane:

- **Seeded link cards do not contribute their link DP.** A Board Spec `linked: [...]`
  entry with a `linkDp` card leaves the host's `currentDP` at its seeded value; the +3000
  only appears after a real `linkCard` intent (proved in `links only onto an [Appmon]
  host…`). A DP assertion around a seeded link therefore reads the wrong baseline. This
  looks like a harness seeding gap (`harness.ts buildPermanent` calls `linkCard` on the
  state directly and never runs `ledger.recomputeDP`), not a production defect — the
  production link path recomputes correctly. Worth a testkit fix so link-DP assertions
  are possible on seeded boards.
- **An over-cap seeded board cannot enter the turn loop.** `trashExcessLinkCards` raises
  a `selectCards` decision for the excess, and `advance(...).waitForMainPhase` then times
  out on the unanswered decision. Not a defect; recorded so the next lane that seeds
  over-cap links does not lose an hour to it.
