`[Your Turn]` and `[Once Per Turn]` are carried from the effect header onto the installed
watcher by `withSubTriggerTurnScope` / `withSubTriggerFrequency`

## Defect fixed locally

`EX10-042.ts` — the `[Regulusmon]` reference used `match: "name"`, which is a SUBSTRING
match (`matching/definition.ts:352`). A bracketed `[Name]` is exact, so it is now
`match: "nameExact"`. No card in the current catalog is named `<x>Regulusmon`, so this is a
latent-only correctness fix with IR evidence and no behavioural difference today. The four
`Regulusmon` printings (BT21-077, EX10-053, LM-017, RB1-030) all still match.

The neighbouring "with `[Gammamon]` in its name" clause correctly keeps `match: "name"`
(substring), which is what lets GulusGammamon / BetelGammamon / KausGammamon qualify.

## Checked and found correct (previous-audit claims verified, not assumed)

- The inner `Digivolve` action's `optional: true` IS honoured: `runAction.ts:560-590` prompts
  generically for `action.optional` on every action kind except `SubTrigger`/`Replacement`,
  and preflights with `canAttemptDigivolve`, so no prompt is opened when no Regulusmon is
  reachable. Proved by the accept and decline paths.
- `sourceFilter: { isSelfRef: true }` on an `onAddDigivolutionCards` watcher gates on the
  RECEIVER permanent through `subjectMatchesFilter`, the same subject `triggerFilter` uses
  (`actions/subTrigger.ts:802-826`). The two-copy board proves it behaviourally.
- No `byEffect: true` is needed on the watcher: `onAddDigivolutionCards` is only fired from
  `primitives.ts` `placeUnder` / `placeOwnTopAtStackBottom`, both effect-driven. Ordinary
  digivolution does not fire the bus, so "when EFFECTS add" is already exact.

A full-catalog scan of `cards.json` for placement clauses (`place ... as/under ... digivolution
card`) whose receiver filter is unrestricted produced three usable ones:

- **BT9-109 X Antibody** (Option, cost 0) — "[Main] Place this card under 1 of your Digimon
  without [X Antibody] in its digivolution cards as its bottom digivolution card." Its own
  `[Static]` waives the colour requirement while you have a Digimon in play. Usable ONCE per
  host, since the second copy is excluded by its own placement.
- **EX6-065 Mythical Arms of Salvation!** (Option, cost 3, Red/Black) — "[Main] You may place
  1 Digimon card with the [Legend-Arms] trait from your trash as 1 of your Digimon's bottom
  digivolution card." Receiver unrestricted. GulusGammamon covers only the red half of its
  colour requirement, so the fixture keeps an inert P-097 Zubamon ([Legend-Arms]) on the board
  to satisfy the card's own waiver.
- **BT11-088 Bagramon** (Lv.6, cost 12) — "[On Play] ... place 1 of your opponent's Digimon
  under 1 of your opponent's other Digimon as its bottom digivolution card." This is the
  opponent-side placement the `[Your Turn]` negative needs: an OPPONENT effect adding a
  digivolution card to MY Digimon on THEIR turn.

So the rubric-ceiling policy is NOT needed for this card; both gates are exercised for real.

### `[Once Per Turn]` — same-turn refusal and next-own-turn reset

`it("[Once Per Turn]: a second same-turn placement offers nothing, and the next own turn
resets it")` runs the real turn loop with GulusGammamon already on the board:

1. `playCard` BT9-109 → its mandatory `[Main]` placement lands under GulusGammamon → the
   watcher fires → the Regulusmon offer opens → **declined**. Stack `["BT9-109"]`, top card
   still EX10-042.
2. `playCard` EX6-065 → P-097 Zubamon is placed from the trash under the SAME Digimon.
   Stack grows to `["BT9-109", "P-097"]`, so the `onAddDigivolutionCards` event definitely
   fired a second time — and **no second `digivolve` prompt is opened**. The test asserts the
   absence over `s.decisions` sliced from before the second play, not merely a quiet
   `pendingDecision`.
3. `endPhase` → opponent's turn → back to my Main through `advance().waitForMainPhase(0)`.
4. `playCard` the second EX6-065 → ST13-02 Zubamon lands → **the offer is back**, and is
   declined again. Stack `["BT9-109", "P-097", "ST13-02"]`.

Why a declined offer still spends the use: the SubTrigger action itself carries no `optional`
and no `cost`, so `subTrigger.ts:1174`'s decline path never runs; the decline happens on the
INNER `Digivolve` in `runAction.ts:761`, which sets `oncePerTurnActivationDeclined` only when
the action sets `preserveOncePerTurnOnDecline`. This IR does not, so `subtriggers.ts:588`
keeps the provisional `markFired`. That is the rules-correct reading of comprehensive
§15-14-1: the watcher activated, and the player declined the digivolution it offered.

### `[Your Turn]` — the opponent-turn negative

`it("[Your Turn]: an opponent's effect placing cards under it on their turn offers nothing")`
runs the turn loop to seat 1's Main, then seat 1 plays BT11-088 Bagramon. Its `[On Play]`
buries my inert BT1-009 under GulusGammamon (steered with `preferInstanceIds`). The stack
grows to `["BT1-009"]` and **no `digivolve` prompt is opened**.

Bagramon's cost of 12 pushes the gauge past 0, so the turn passes as soon as the resolution
finishes. The test therefore records the turn seat AT the `cardsMoved` event through the
harness `onEvent` hook and asserts the placement of that exact instance happened while
`turnSeat === 1`, rather than reading `state.turnSeat` afterwards.
