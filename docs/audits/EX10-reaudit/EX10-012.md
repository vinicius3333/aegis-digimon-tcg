# EX10-012 MetalSeadramon — re-audit

Worker lane, card only. Branch `audit-ex10-reaudit`.

## Card summary

Blue Lv.6 Digimon, play cost 11, DP 11000, forms Mega, attribute Data, traits Cyborg /
Dark Masters, no digivolution requirements (`evoCosts: []`), rarity R, max 4 per deck.
Catalog matches the official text; no discrepancy found.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Hand] [Main]` If you don't have any Digimon other than Digimon with [Dark Masters] in
   their texts, you may play this card with the play cost reduced by 5.
2. At turn end, delete the Digimon this effect played.
3. `[On Play] [When Attacking]` 1 of your opponent's Digimon and 1 of their Tamers can't
   suspend until their turn ends.
4. `[All Turns]` This Digimon can only digivolve into [Apocalymon].
5. `[On Deletion]` If you have no blue face-up security cards, place this Digimon face up as
   the bottom security card.
6. `[Security]` If this card was face-up, you may play 1 level 5 or lower card with
   [Dark Masters] in its text from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

## Peer / stack evidence

The card prints no digivolution requirement (`evoCosts: []`), so no evolution route builds a
stack *into* it. The reachable stack proof is the route *out of* it, driven through the public
`digivolve` intent:

- "goes silent as a digivolution card once [Apocalymon] sits on top of it" digivolves BT15-102
  Apocalymon over a played EX10-012 and asserts the stack identity
  (`topCard === "BT15-102"`, `stack === ["EX10-012"]` by both card id and instance id), then
  proves all three of its own main-text surfaces go quiet underneath:
  - `[Hand][Main]`: `observe(engine).cardSource(host)` yields no `EX10-012/` effect key at
    `OnDeclaration` or `OnPlay`, and the client-visible `activatableEffects` payload carries none.
  - `[When Attacking]`: the host attacks and no opposing Digimon or Tamer picks up a
    `beSuspended` restriction.
  - `[On Deletion]`: the 20000 DP wall wins the battle, so the stack leaves the battle area and
    the EX10-012 instance lands in the trash — security stays at two face-down cards and never
    receives it.
- The `[Dark Masters]` "in its text" filter is now proved against mixed pools rather than one
  card at a time: a board holding a trait-only match (BT15-031), a text-only match (BT15-072)
  and — in the negative — a non-matching Digimon (BT1-013) at the same time, plus a hand pool of
  three where only the level-4 text-only card is eligible for the `[Security]` free play (the
  level-6 trait match and the no-text card stay in hand, and the engine raises no `selectCards`
  because exactly one candidate is legal).
- The test no longer casts through the engine. `reducedCostPlayEffectKey` and the new silence
  assertions both read `observe(s.engine).cardSource(...)` from
  `apps/api/src/engine/testkit/observe.ts`; the `as unknown as { cardSourceOf }` reach is gone,
  and no assertion touches `engine.continuous`, `engine.ledger` or `engine.primitives`.

## Findings

1. **No defect found in the module.** `EX10-012.ts` is unchanged by this lane
   (`git diff` on it is empty). One suspected defect was investigated and dismissed:
   the printed "can't suspend" is compiled as `restriction: "suspend"`, which the
   interpreter normalizes to `beSuspended` (effect suspension), while the attack gate in
   `engine/combat/legality.ts` reads `"suspend"`. Comprehensive §4-23 says the chosen card
   simply can't suspend, which must include the self-suspension of an attack declaration.
   `ContinuousLedger.hasRestriction` (`engine/effects/continuous.ts`, the
   `equivalentRestrictions` set) already treats `suspend` and `beSuspended` as the same
   prohibition at the read boundary, so the attack is refused. Adding
   `blocksCombatSuspend: true` changed nothing (verified by running the suite with and
   without it), so it was reverted; the flag on BT26-019, BT26-031 and EX11-017 looks
   redundant for the same reason. The behaviour is now pinned by a public-intent test.
2. **Cross-clause interaction the old test hid.** The turn-end delete makes the card leave
   the battle area, so its own `[On Deletion]` clause runs: with no blue face-up security
   card the deleted copy is placed face up at the bottom of security rather than landing in
   the trash. The old test asserted only "no longer on the field"; the new one asserts the
   exact security position and face.
3. **Condition scope note (not a defect).** `youHaveNone` with no `zone` counts battle-area
   permanents only (`interpreter/scaling.ts`), so a Digimon in the breeding area does not
   close the gate. No ruling in the local KB settles the breeding case for this card, so it
   is recorded as an open ambiguity rather than tested.

No test is retained red; there is no `it.fails` or `it.skip` in the file.
