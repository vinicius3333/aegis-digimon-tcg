## What changed

The module `EX10-070.ts` is unchanged — the compiled IR is faithful. The whole change is the
test file, which was rebuilt.

The previous suite proved clause 3 and clause 4 with injected timing and hand-set state:

- `advance(s.engine).verb.trash([...])` for the link-card trash — a direct verb call, not a
  game event, so it earned no behavioural credit under the brief;
- `advance(s.engine).fireForInstance(EffectTiming.SecuritySkill, …)` for [Security] — never a
  real security check;
- `s.perm("godGrade").placedByEffect = true` on a hand-laid Board Spec — the Option was never
  played, so neither the [Main] clause nor the §16-17-3 "not the turn it entered play" gate
  was exercised;
- the colour-requirement waiver (clause 1) had no test at all — only an IR shape assertion.

The rebuilt suite drives every clause through public intents and the production turn loop:

- `playCard` for the Option, with the colour requirement accepted or refused as
  `color-requirement-unmet`;
- BT25-073 Dragomon played from hand as the public link-card trasher — its [On Play] cost is
  "By trashing 1 of your Digimon's link cards", a genuine effect trash (the same vehicle the
  accepted EX10-062 lane uses);
- `s.engine.startTurnLoop()` plus `waitForMainPhase` / `endMainPhaseIfOpen` to carry the played
  Option from its arrival turn to its controller's next turn, so §16-17-3 and the later
  activation are both proved on a card that reached the field by its own [Main] clause;
- a real `attack` on the player for [Security];
- `activateEffect` on BT25-070 Logamon for the effect-driven half of Q5184.

No `advance.fire*`, no `verb.*`, no `placedByEffect` hand-set, and no engine internals remain
in the file.

### Fixture notes

`traits = forms ∪ attributes ∪ types` (`engine/cards/cardData.ts` `staticTraitsOf`). On EX10
Appmon cards [Appmon] lives in `forms`, not `types` — a `types`-only reading of the catalog
wrongly suggests no Digimon in the game carries the trait.

- EX10-029 Warpmon — Black Lv.4, [Appmon]; the link host.
- BT24-053 Protecmon — Black Lv.3, [Appmon], "[Link] [Appmon] trait: Cost 1", link effect
  ＜Blocker＞ only; the link card and the link material.
- EX11-027 Maquinamon — carries ＜Link＞ but no [Appmon] trait; used as the host/link pair in
  the negatives. This matters: with an [Appmon] link card, the card Dragomon trashes lands in
  the trash and becomes legal material on its own, so the negative would prove nothing.
- BT21-041 Calendamon — [Tool] trait with a printed ＜Link＞; the material for BT25-070's
  effect link in the Q5184 case.

## Behaviour confirmed

- The colour waiver is real, not cosmetic: with a Red [Appmon] Digimon (BT23-007) or a Red
  [Appmon] Tamer (BT21-084) on the field the Black Option plays; with a Red non-[Appmon]
  Digimon the same intent is refused `color-requirement-unmet` with hand, deck and memory
  untouched.
- [Main] draws exactly the top card of deck (BT1-013, deck left holding BT1-014), pays 2
  memory (5 -> 3) and leaves the Option as a battle-area permanent, not in the trash.
- §16-17-3 holds: a genuine link-card trash on the turn the Option arrived raises no ＜Delay＞
  activation, the Option stays on the field and the [Appmon] material stays in the trash.
- On a later turn the same trigger activates: the Option is trashed as the cost, the [Appmon]
  card leaves the trash and becomes the triggering Digimon's only link card, the trashed link
  card stays trashed, and BT24-053's printed [Link] cost 1 is not charged (memory moves only
  by Dragomon's own play cost 7).
- Declining leaves the Option on the field and links nothing.
- `sourceFilter.controller: "mine"` holds: the opponent trashing their own Digimon's link card
  does not arm the watcher or spend the ＜Delay＞.
- The Link is `from: ["trash"]` and [Appmon]-gated: an [Appmon] copy in hand, or a trash holding
  only a non-[Appmon] ＜Link＞ card and a card with no ＜Link＞ at all, leaves the ＜Delay＞ unspent.
- Q5184 both ways: a `linkCard` intent onto a base-limit-1 host, and BT25-070's [Main] effect
  link onto an already-linked host, each replace the link card through the CR §17-1-3-2-5 rule
  sweep and raise no ＜Delay＞ prompt — even though the replaced [Appmon] card is then sitting in
  the trash as legal material.
- [Security] on a real security check places the Option in the battle area (it survives the
  §17-1-3-2-2 Option sweep because the effect placed it), takes no memory, and runs no [Main]
  draw — the defender's deck and hand are untouched.

## Observations for the coordinator (no change made)

1. **Two optional prompts per activation.** Accepting the ＜Delay＞ raises a second optional
   prompt from the Link action's own `optional: true`. Both are printed ("＜Delay＞" is an
   activation choice, the bullet says "You may link"), so this is arguably faithful, but it is
   worth a consistent house rule across the ＜Delay＞ family: the accepted EX10-062 lane's
   scripted-answer helper assumes one prompt per clause. The test documents the order.
2. **Recipient wording.** The printed text is "1 of those Digimon" (plural source), and the IR
   binds `sourceRef: "triggerSubject"` — one recipient per firing. `whenLinkTrashed` fires once
   per trashed link instance (`primitives.ts` `trash`), so one firing always has exactly one
   subject and the singular binding is correct. No engine gap.
