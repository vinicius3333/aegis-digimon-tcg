## Printed clauses

1. **[Start of Your Turn]** — "If you have 2 or less memory, set it to 3."
2. **[All Turns]** — "When any of your Digimon with [Myotismon] in their names are played, by
   deleting this Tamer, 1 of those Digimon gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn. Then, gain 1 memory."
3. **[Security]** — "Play this card without paying the cost."

## Priority finding: the trash-residency report does NOT reproduce

The EX10-048 lane reported this Tamer's `[All Turns]` watcher firing while the Tamer sat in the
trash (trigger key `inst-3::subtrigger/6/...`). Driven through public intents it does not
reproduce, and no module or engine change was needed:

- **Deleted mid-game, then a second Myotismon is played** ("is inert once it is in the trash"):
  the first play trashes the Tamer and gains 1 memory (10 -> 5); the second BT2-075 play from
  the same hand grants no ＜Rush＞ and gains no memory (10 -> 4).
- **Seeded in the trash from the start** ("is inert from the trash at the start of the game
  too"): playing BT2-075 grants no ＜Rush＞, gains no memory, and `s.decisions` is exactly `[]` —
  no optional prompt, no target prompt, no ordering prompt is raised for the trash-resident
  Tamer.
- **Direct reproduction attempt of the EX10-048 scenario** (scratch probe, removed afterwards):
  seat 0 with EX10-065 in the trash, BT2-067 on the board and EX10-048 in hand, played with
  `autoOrderTriggers: false`. The only decisions raised were EX10-048's own ("Reduce the play
  cost." and its [On Play] `chooseTargets`). **No `orderTriggers` decision appeared at all**, so
  the trash-resident Tamer never entered the trigger batch.

Conclusion for the coordinator: the observation in `EX10-048.md` "Other findings" item 2 is not
a live defect on the public path. Either it came from injected timing, or the Oikawa instance in
that fixture had been played out of the trash before the key was seen. Nothing to fix in
`EX10-065.ts`, and no retained red is warranted. If the coordinator wants the ordering-batch
claim rechecked, the exact probe fixture is written out above.

## Defects and gaps

- **Module**: none. `EX10-065.ts` is unchanged — the compiled IR already matches all three
  printed clauses, including the substring name test, the trigger-subject target, the
  all-or-nothing "by" cost and the `forTheTurn` duration.
- **Test file**: fully rewritten. The committed version proved the [Start of Your Turn] clause
  with `advance.fireForPermanent`, both [All Turns] branches with `advance.fireSubTrigger` and
  the [Security] clause with `advance.fireForInstance`, so none of them exercised a real play,
  a real turn or a real security check. It also asserted nothing about zone residency, the
  opponent's Myotismon, the name-substring boundary or the duration.
- **Catalog discrepancy (do not edit)**: `EX10-065.effectText` holds a NON-BREAKING SPACE
  (U+00A0) at offset 53 of the second line, after `[Myotismon]` in "…with [Myotismon] in their
  names…". The official list has a plain space. It does not affect matching (the token is
  bracket-delimited), so the catalog test normalizes whitespace and says so. The same artefact
  was reported by the EX10-048 lane on that card — it looks systemic in this set's Purple text.
- **Untested edge, deliberate**: memory exactly 3 at the start of your turn. The gate is "2 or
  less", so 3 is outside it and setting 3 to 3 has no observable difference; the falsifiable
  boundary pair 2 (fires) / 4 (does not) is tested instead.
- **No evolution-stack proof**: this is a Tamer. It has no digivolution stack, no inherited text
  and no evolution route. The peer work is the mixed Myotismon pool instead: an inert
  "Myotismon", a "VenomMyotismon" (substring), a non-Myotismon Digimon, an opponent-controlled
  Myotismon and a second board-resident Myotismon as a decoy.
