## Remaining gaps

1. **Controller's own ＜De-Digivolve＞ (structural only).** No printed card in the catalog
   De-Digivolves one of ITS OWN controller's Digimon (checked across the whole catalog),
   so the `byOpponentEffectsOnly` scope has no public route. The test installs the
   protection publicly, then registers a temporary neutral `DeDigivolve` effect on the
   card under audit and fires it with `advance.fireForPermanent`, restoring the original
   record in a `finally`. Injected timing: no behavioural credit claimed.
2. **[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] on the would-leave replacement.** Cannot fire twice
   through public intents: the Digimon leaves the battle area once and does not come
   back, so no legal same-turn second trigger exists. Documented, not tested — the same
   reasoning the coordinator accepted for `whenMovedFromBreeding` gates.
3. **DigiXros duplicate material.** CR 7-2-2-5 allows only one of each specified card;
   placing two SkullKnightmon is not asserted. Low risk, untested.
