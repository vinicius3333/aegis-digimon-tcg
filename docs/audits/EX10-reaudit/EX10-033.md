# EX10-033 Pyramidimon — re-audit

Black Lv.6 Mega, Virus, [Mineral] [LIBERATOR]. Play cost 11, DP 11000.
Digivolve: Black Lv.5 for 3 memory. No inherited text, no security text.

## Printed clauses

1. `＜Fragment (3)＞` — keyword only.
2. `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn]` You may place up to 3 [Mineral] or
   [Rock] trait cards from your trash as this Digimon's **bottom** digivolution cards.
3. `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking]` By trashing up to 3 [Mineral] or [Rock] trait cards
   from **any** of your Digimon's digivolution cards, to 1 of your opponent's Digimon, reduce
   the play cost by 2 until their turn ends **for each card trashed**.

Clause 3 carries no `[Once Per Turn]`, so it may fire on the digivolve and again on the attack.

## Catalog discrepancy

`cards.json` stores `[Rock] trait` in both clauses: the space between `[Rock]` and
`trait` is U+00A0 (non-breaking), not U+0020. Two occurrences, offsets 109 and 258 of
`effectText`. Nothing else in the record deviates from the official list. Not edited — the
catalog assertion now spells the escape so the stored bytes are visible instead of hidden.
Worth a coordinator sweep: any other card whose text was scraped the same way will fail an
exact-text assertion for the same invisible reason.

## Defects found and fixed in this lane

The previous (cancelled) lane left three broken assertions in the test file; all three were
wrong, not the card:

1. **Catalog text assertion** compared against plain spaces and failed on the two U+00A0
   characters. Fixed with explicit ` ` escapes plus a comment.
2. **Once-per-turn test** called `declineBlock` after both attacks. The opponent controls no
   Digimon in that fixture, so no block window stays open and the intent returned
   `{ok:false, reason:"wrong-phase"}`. Removed; the attacks now settle straight on the
   security count.
3. **Q5094 was retained red claiming the engine raises no `orderTriggers` decision.** That is
   false — the engine does raise it, and the `it.fails` failed because it passed. Replaced
   with a real behavioural ordering proof: because clause 2 feeds clause 3 (cards it places
   become legal trash material), the chosen order changes the outcome. Placement first gives
   clause 3 four eligible cards and a -6 reduction on a cost-6 Digimon (down to 0).

## Remaining gaps

- Q5097's "minimum value is 0" is read off `advance(engine).ledgers.modifiers.playCostFor`,
  because no card effect can distinguish a stored `-1` from `0`. The floor itself is covered
  by `engine/effects/modifiers.test.ts`.
- The `[Once Per Turn]` gate is proved through the real turn loop (digivolve spends it, the
  same turn's attack cannot place, the next own turn's attack places again), so no structural
  exception is claimed here.
- Which 3 of 4 eligible cards clause 3 trashes is steered by `preferInstanceIds`; the engine
  offers no ordering guarantee and none is asserted.
- Clause 3 is mandatory, so no "decline the reduction" case exists to test; the only
  optional-decline case is clause 2, and it is covered.
