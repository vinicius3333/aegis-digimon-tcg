## What the previous audit missed

The committed test proved every clause through **injected timing** only:
`advance().fireForPermanent(OnStartTurn, …)`, `advance().fireSubTrigger("whenOneOfYoursDigivolves", …)`
and `advance().fireForInstance(SecuritySkill, …)`. None of it drove a turn, a digivolution
or a security check. Under the re-audit rubric that scores 0 for Behavior. It also used
EX10-065 Yukio Oikawa as the bank host (a Tamer that carries its own `[Start of Your Turn]`
memory clause) and asserted `stack[0]` without a second stack card, so `position: "bottom"`
was never actually distinguished from "directly beneath the top card".

The rewritten test replaces all of it with public intents and `startTurnLoop()`, and adds:
the optional-decline branch, the `[Your Turn]` / "your Digimon" negative, the three
cost-unpayable fixtures, the hand/trash inertness negative, the `forTheTurn` expiry through
the loop, and the EX10-023 cross-card gate.

## Catalog discrepancies

`effectText` contains a **non-breaking space (U+00A0)** after each `＜Save＞` token instead of
a normal space. The text is otherwise identical to the official wording. The test asserts the
catalog string verbatim, so the quirk is now pinned rather than silently tolerated. No edit
was made to the catalog.

## Remaining gaps

- **＜Alliance＞ is proved as an active grant, not exercised in a battle.** The test reads the
  production continuous ledger through `observe().hasKeyword` and proves the grant appears and
  expires with the turn, but does not attack with the recipient to show the DP transfer and
  ＜Security A. +1＞ of §16-24. That is ＜Alliance＞'s own machinery, not this card's clause.
- **`[Start of Your Turn]` is not proved silent on the opponent's turn.** `state.memory` is
  signed from the turn player's view and the pass bonus already puts the incoming player at 3,
  so no public line reaches an opponent turn that starts at 2 or less memory with this Tamer
  in play. The trigger keyword is asserted in the IR contract test instead.
- **Engine observation, not a defect of this card:** `textHasKeyword`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts`) matches a keyword icon
  anywhere in `effectText`/`inheritedEffectText`, including inside parenthetical reminder
  notes, which CR §4-22-5 excludes from "text". No fixture in this lane hits it (reminder notes
  spell the keyword out rather than repeating the icon), but it is a cross-cutting risk for any
  "with ＜XX＞ in its text" filter. Coordinator's call.
