Purple, Lv.5 Ultimate, Virus, [Undead]. Play cost 7, DP 7000, digivolve Purple Lv.4 cost 3.

## Printed clauses

1. **Play-cost replacement** — "When this card would be played, by deleting 1 of your Digimon
   with [Myotismon] in its text, reduce the play cost by 4."
2. **[On Play] [On Deletion]** — "Until your opponent's turn ends, 1 of your purple Digimon
   gains ＜Blocker＞ and ＜Retaliation＞" (one Digimon gains both).

   without paying the cost."

## Retained red

`it.fails("Q5131: the cost deletion's [On Deletion] and this card's [On Play] are ordered by the
turn player")`.

- **Seam**: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts`, `runReplacement`,
  the `mode === "reduceCost"` subscription's `activate` callback — it calls `payCost` for the
  `kind: "deleteOwn"` cost inside the `wouldBePlayed` replacement.
- **Expected**: playing EX10-048 and deleting EX10-047 Arukenimon (which prints its own
  [On Deletion]) raises an `orderTriggers` decision for seat 0 with two keys, one per source.
- **Actual**: no `orderTriggers` decision is raised at all. The cost deletion happens outside
  the play's trigger batch, so the deleted Digimon's [On Deletion] never joins the batch the
  decision is built from. Arukenimon's [On Deletion] did not appear as a trigger key and did not
  resolve (its purple Tamer stayed in the trash).
- **Proof the batching itself works**: with EX10-065 Yukio Oikawa seeded in the trash, the same
  play does produce a two-key `orderTriggers` decision — Myotismon's `OnPlay` plus Oikawa's
  `[All Turns]` subtrigger. Only the cost deletion is missing from the batch.
- Not fixable in a card module: it is the shared replacement-cost path.

## Other findings for the coordinator

1. **Catalog discrepancy (do not edit)**: `EX10-048.effectText` contains a NON-BREAKING SPACE
   (U+00A0) at offset 78, after "[Myotismon]" in "…with [Myotismon] in its text…". The official
   list has a plain space. It does not affect matching (the token is bracket-delimited), but the
   catalog test has to normalize it. `inheritedEffectText` is clean.
2. **Engine seam, unrelated card**: EX10-065 Yukio Oikawa's `[All Turns]` watcher ("When any of
   your Digimon with [Myotismon] in their names are played…") fires while the Tamer is in the
   **trash**. Observed as trigger key
   `inst-3::subtrigger/6/When any of your Digimon with [Myotismon] in their names are played…`
   for an instance seeded in seat 0's trash. A Tamer in the trash has no effects. Belongs to
   whoever owns EX10-065 / the subtrigger installation seam.
3. **Harmless IR noise**: the `Replacement` carries `abortOnDecline: true`. For `mode:
   "reduceCost"` the engine never reads it (declining `activate` simply skips the reduction and
   the play continues at full cost, which is correct). Left in place so the persisted
   `effects.json` record does not drift for a no-op.
4. **Q5130 breadth**: `definitionMatches`'s "text" union covers name, traits, `effectText`,
   `inheritedEffectText`, `securityEffectText`, `linkEffect`, `linkRequirement`, `dualEffect` and
   `optionEffect`. The ruling also names digivolution / DNA / DigiXros / burst / App Fusion /
   Link / Assembly **requirements** as text. Requirements are structured fields
   (`evoCosts`, DigiXros specs), not text fields, so a card that names [Myotismon] only in a
   digivolution requirement would not match today. No EX10 fixture exposes it; recorded as a
   general engine gap rather than a card defect.
