# EX10-046 Devimon — re-audit

Purple Lv.4 Champion, Virus, [Fallen Angel]. Play cost 5, DP 5000,
digivolve from a Purple Lv.3 for 2. Rarity C, max 4 in deck. No Security text.

## Printed clauses

| # | Clause |
|---|---|
| C1 | `[Start of Your Main Phase]` `[When Digivolving]` If your opponent has 10 or fewer cards in their trash, trash the top 2 cards of both players' decks. |
| C2 | Then, if they have 10 or more cards in their trash, you may return 1 card with the [Fallen Angel] or [Undead] trait from your trash to the hand. |
| C3 | Inherited: `[When Attacking]` `[Once Per Turn]` Trash the top card of both players' decks. |

C1 and C2 are two independently gated actions sharing one trigger pair, not a chain
where C2 depends on C1 firing. Both rulings below turn on exactly that.

| C3 | `effects[2]` `TrashTopDeck` both/1, `isInherited`, `frequency: "OncePerTurn"` | real-stack test (Lv.3 -> Devimon -> Lv.5, then attack) and the once-per-turn test |

Notes on the IR shape:

- `match: "trait"` is exact, which is right — the text uses `[Fallen Angel]` / `[Undead]`
  brackets, not "in any of its traits".
- The two triggers are compiled as two separate effects with identical bodies. That is the
  correct reading of the printed `[Start of Your Main Phase] [When Digivolving]` pair: two
  timings, one body, no shared once-per-turn gate (none is printed).
- `controller: "both"` on the mill matches "both players' decks"; the condition seat is
  `opponent`, matching "your opponent"/"they".

## Behavioural coverage

All ten cases drive production windows. No `advance.fire*` injected timing is used
anywhere in this file; the previous version of the test proved C1/C2 and the once-per-turn
reset with `fireForPermanent`, which earns no behavioural credit under this brief.

- Start-of-main timing: `s.engine.startTurnLoop()` + `waitForMainPhase(0)`; the trigger
  fires on its own.
- `[When Digivolving]`: public `digivolve` intent from a Lv.3 Purple base, with stack
  identity and the 2-memory cost asserted, plus a rejected illegal-source digivolve off a
  Red Lv.3 that leaves the board and the opponent's trash untouched.
- Inherited clause: public `attack` intents. Same-turn refusal is proved by unsuspending
  the same host with a public `playCard` of BT1-095 and attacking a second time — a real
  second attack window, not an injected fire. Reset is proved across the real turn loop
  (own turn -> opponent turn -> own turn).
- Deck lengths, not trash lengths, are the mill's endpoints in the attack tests, because a
  security check moves the checked card to the trash and would mask the count.

Fixtures are inert: BT1-009/BT1-013/BT1-014 (no text at all), BT6-076 Feresmon (inert,
[Fallen Angel]), BT2-075 Myotismon (inert, [Undead]), ST6-02 DemiDevimon (inert Purple
Lv.3), BT4-085 Phantomon (inert Purple Lv.5, 10000 DP). No Digi-Egg in deck or security,
no numeric `security: <n>` form. BT1-095 Brave Shield is the one non-inert fixture; its
only side effect is a ＜Blocker＞ grant on the attacker, which nothing here asserts.

## Defects

None in the module. `EX10-046.ts` is unchanged by this lane; the compiled IR already
matches all three clauses and both rulings. The defect was in the evidence, not the
implementation: the previous test proved C1/C2 and the once-per-turn reset only through
injected timing, used the noisy EX10-053 Regulusmon as the inherited-clause host, and had
no threshold boundary, no optional-decline branch, no `[When Digivolving]` route and no
evolution-stack proof.

## Catalog discrepancy

`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` -> `EX10-046.effectText` reads
"… tash the top 2 cards of both players' decks …". "tash" should be "trash". Cosmetic —
the IR is compiled correctly — but it is a text mismatch against the official list.
Not edited (catalog is coordinator-owned).

## Remaining gaps

- The inherited clause's once-per-turn refusal needs an unsuspend to open a second attack
  window, so BT1-095 Brave Shield is in the fixture. That is a public play, but it does mean
  the same-turn case is not reachable with an all-inert board.
- No proof of the clause when a deck is empty (no cards to mill). The text has no such
  branch and no ruling covers it, so nothing is asserted.
- The `[Start of Your Main Phase]` and `[When Digivolving]` copies are proved separately;
  there is no case where both fire in one turn (Devimon would have to be on the board at
  start of main and then be digivolved into again, which the printed card cannot do).
