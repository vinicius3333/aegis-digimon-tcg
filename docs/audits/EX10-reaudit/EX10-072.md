The asymmetry between C4 and C7 is real and is asserted structurally as well: C4's ＜Delay＞
resolves on the OPPONENT's turn, so its delete waits for the controller's own next turn end
(`DelayedDeletePlayed` default `endOfOwnerTurn`); C7's [Security] effect resolves on the
opponent's turn and deletes at the end of THAT turn, so it is an unguarded `endOfTurn`
watcher anchored to the played permanent.

## What this re-audit changed

The module (`EX10-072.ts`) needed no change: every clause behaves correctly under public
intents. The defect was in the evidence, not the implementation.

`EX10-072.test.ts` was rewritten. The previous file proved C3–C7 only through injected
timing — `advance(...).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnEndTurn, …)`,
`fireForInstance(EffectTiming.SecuritySkill, …)` and `fireArmedSubTriggers("endOfTurn", …)`
— which the brief scores 0 in the Behavior column. It also hand-poked
`s.perm("spiral").placedByEffect = true` to survive the §17-1-3-2-2 rule sweep, a workaround
for a state the public [Main] play sets on its own.

New proofs, all through public intents and the production turn loop:

- ＜Delay＞ over four real turns: seat 1 plays EX10-072 with `playCard` (which sets
  `placedByEffect`, so it survives the rule sweep into the next turn — asserted), the
  ＜Delay＞ fires at the end of seat 0's turn, and the played Digimon is deleted at the end
  of seat 1's own next turn. The delete is confirmed to be a deletion, not a disappearance,
  by EX10-057's own [On Deletion] landing it face up at the bottom of security.
- Face-down control: a second [Dark Masters] card sits face-down in the same security stack
  and is never a candidate.
- ＜Delay＞ declined (`autoDeclineOptional`): nothing is played, the security card stays.
- [Security] through a real attack on the player, not an injected `SecuritySkill` fire:
  three variants (play from hand, play from trash, non-matching card refused) plus the
  no-candidate case, and the turn-end delete asserted with a bystander control.
- Q6241 peer/stack case: EX10-072 established in the battle area is placed under BT15-102
  Apocalymon as one of its play-cost [Dark Masters] cards.
- Q5745 simultaneity: BT15-079 Piedmon's `[End of Opponent's Turn] Delete this Digimon`
  gives the same turn end a second processing alongside the [Security] delete; both resolve.

No defect required a module edit and no test is retained red.

## Remaining gaps

- **Q5745 is covered only in outcome, not in choice.** The ruling's substance is that the
  turn player *may choose* the resolve order of the simultaneous turn-end processings. The
  test proves both processings complete at that single turn end; it does not assert an
  `orderTriggers` decision is offered to the turn player, because in this pairing the two
  processings belong to the non-turn player (seat 0) while the turn player is seat 1, and
  the outcome is order-independent (two deletions, no shared target). Proving the *choice*
  needs a pair of same-controller turn-end processings whose outcomes differ by order; no
  such pair exists among EX10-072's own clauses. Reported, not worked around.
- The ＜Delay＞'s `DelayedDeletePlayed` falls back to `ctx.source.permanent()` when nothing
  was played (`removal.ts` `case "DelayedDeletePlayed"`). Here the Option was already
  trashed as the ＜Delay＞ activation cost, so that lookup is undefined and nothing is armed;
  the declined-branch test confirms no stray delete. Noted as a latent sharp edge in the
  shared action, not a defect of this card.
