## Rules basis

- **Q5395** — the part after "then" is processed even when the first "if" is not met.
  C3 must run when the opponent has more than 10 cards in their trash and no mill happened.
- **Q5132** — "instead" replaces the standard processing. C6 and C5 are mutually
  exclusive, not additive.
- **Comprehensive §15-6-2** — "Different processes in the same effect don't have to meet
  the processing conditions for other processes." A processing condition is read when its
  own process is performed. So C4's threshold is read *after* C2's mill has already put 3
  more cards into the opponent's trash. That is why 8 cards in trash still reaches the
  raised maximum (8 + 3 = 11).
- §15-6-3 is not engaged: C2 is not the effect's only process, so the effect still
  activates when the opponent's trash is above 10.

## What the previous audit missed

The committed test file scored the card 10/10 on evidence that was entirely structural:

- three of its four behavioural tests fired the timing directly with
  `advance(...).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.WhenDigivolving | OnUseAttack, ...)`. Per the
  brief that earns no behavioural credit. Nothing proved the card actually digivolves, is
  actually deleted, or actually attacks.
- ＜Blocker＞ (C1) had no test at all — only an IR shape assertion.
- [On Deletion] (C2/C3/C4 on that timing) had no test at all — only the shape assertion.
- The [Once Per Turn] gate on the inherited effect (C5) had no test at all.
- No level boundary was proved in either direction: nothing showed a Lv.4 or Lv.6 surviving,
  so a wrong `levelComparison` value would have passed.
- The fixtures used `EX10-030` and `EX10-053` as opponent boards. Both carry their own
  effect text (EX10-053 prints ＜Rush＞, ＜Blocker＞, an [On Play] and an inherited
  [Once Per Turn] clause), which the brief's fixture rule excludes.
- `EX10-030` was used as the deletion target of a "level 5 or lower" clause while the test
  called it `level5`, but it is a Black/Yellow card — no colour or level boundary was
  actually being asserted.

No module defect was found. The IR is faithful to the printed text and to the three rules
sources above, so `EX10-049.ts` is unchanged and `effects.json` does not drift.

## Remaining gaps

- C4's "add 2 to this effect's level maximum" is read here as a level-5 ceiling applied at
  the moment the delete is processed, i.e. after C2's mill. That follows §15-6-2 and there
  is no ruling on this card contradicting it, but no ruling confirms it directly either.
  Under the alternative reading (both thresholds snapshotted at effect resolution start),
  the "re-reads the trash after the mill" test would be the one to flip. Flagged as an
  interpretation, not a defect.
- The [Once Per Turn] same-turn refusal needs `advance.verb.unsuspend` because no public
  intent unsuspends an attacker mid-turn. This is a seam affordance gap, not a card gap; it
  would be closed by a fixture card that unsuspends, or by a testkit intent for it.
