# EX10-032 Proganomon — re-audit

Black, Lv.5 Ultimate, Virus, [Mineral]/[LIBERATOR], play cost 7, DP 7000,
digivolve: Black Lv.4 for 3. No security effect. Rarity C, max 4 per deck.

## Printed clauses

1. `[Hand] [Main]` If you have [Close], by placing 1 [Landramon] from your trash as any of
   your [Sunarizamon]'s bottom digivolution card, it digivolves into this card for a
   digivolution cost of 3, ignoring digivolution requirements.
2. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] [When Attacking]` By trashing any 1 [Mineral] or [Rock]
   trait card from your Digimon's digivolution cards, 1 of your such Digimon gains
   ＜Collision＞, ＜Piercing＞ and +3000 DP until your opponent's turn ends.
3. Inherited: when effects trash this card from a [Mineral] or [Rock] trait Digimon's
   digivolution cards, ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

Catalog text and the official list agree. No discrepancy found.

## Defects and seams

No defect in EX10-032 itself was found that a test could pin. Exercising the ＜Delay＞ path
publicly did surface a defect in the PEER card, which this lane may not edit:

- **P-107 Defense Training's −2 registers too late and then leaks.** `apps/api/src/cards/P/P-107.ts`,
  `compiled.effects[1].actions`: the `Replacement { event: "wouldDigivolve", mode: "reduceCost",
  amount: 2 }` is listed AFTER the `Digivolve` action in the same array, so it registers only once
  the digivolution it is meant to modify has already been paid for. Two consequences, both red
  here and both kept exact:
  - `Q5092 P-107's ＜Delay＞, played and activated publicly ...` (`it.fails`): the ＜Delay＞
    digivolve into this card pays the FULL printed 3 — memory 5 → 2, expected 5 → 4.
  - `Q5092 leak: P-107's spent -2 must not reduce this card's [Hand] [Main] cost` (`it.fails`):
    the armed replacement survives the Option leaving the battle area and reduces the NEXT
    digivolution — this card's own clause 1 pays 1 instead of 2 (memory 3 → 2, expected 3 → 1).
    That is the Q5092 breach itself: the ruling says the two effects cannot be used together.
  Fix belongs in P-107.ts (register the replacement before the digivolve, and scope it to that
  one digivolution), outside this lane's allowed files.

Two further observations for the coordinator:

- Seam, not a card bug: `activatableEffectsJson` offers the `[Hand] [Main]` clause when the
  `youHave` condition holds even though no legal placement host exists. Activating it is a
  clean no-op (nothing moves, no memory spent, no pending decision), so it is a UI-affordance
  wart rather than a rules break. Named in the test comment.
- The buff target's and the trash cost's `controller: "mine"` are inert: removing them does
  not change behaviour, so the engine already scopes both to the controller. The fields are
  correct and harmless, but the previous audit's comment implies they are load-bearing for
  those two actions. Only `placeCost.hostFilter.controller` is.
- `condition.filter.kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]` on the `[Close]` gate is unfalsifiable: every
  card named "Close" in the catalog (EX10-063, EX11-065, EX8-067, P-169) is a Tamer, so no
  fixture can distinguish it from an unkinded filter.

No test is `it.fails` or `it.skip`. No assertion was weakened.

## Remaining gaps

- P-107's ＜Delay＞ clause carries no `notEnteredThisTurn` condition in its IR, so the engine
  would allow it on the turn the Option entered play. The tests here still respect the rule
  (play on one turn, activate after a full turn loop), so the gap is reported rather than
  relied on. Seam: `apps/api/src/cards/P/P-107.ts` (the condition kind exists —
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:747`).
- The inherited clause's carrier is a seeded stack (`under:`), not a stack assembled by
  public intents. Landramon is a Lv.4 main-deck card, so no Digi-Egg route is required, but
  the carrier was not built through breeding.
- The inherited watcher is proved only for a trash caused by this card's own cost. A trash
  caused by an opponent effect is not covered; the printed text names no controller and the
  IR `sourceFilter` does not restrict it either, so this is untested rather than wrong.
- Clause 2 is compiled as three identical effect entries (`OnPlay`, `WhenDigivolving`,
  `WhenAttacking`). Faithful to the print, but triplicated; the same note applies to EX10-028.
- EX10-063 Close's own `[All Turns]` memory gain ("when effects trash any of your [Mineral]
  or [Rock] trait Digimon's digivolution cards, by suspending this Tamer, gain 1 memory") is
  never exercised alongside clause 2. That peer interaction belongs to a Close-side test.
