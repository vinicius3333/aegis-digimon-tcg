## Card summary

Purple, Digimon, Lv.6 Mega, [Wizard]/[Dark Masters], Virus, play cost 11, 11000 DP,
`evoCosts: []` (no printed digivolution requirement), R, 4 per deck. No inherited effect.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Hand] [Main]` If you don't have any Digimon other than Digimon with [Dark Masters] in
   their texts, you may play this card with the play cost reduced by 5. At turn end, delete
   the Digimon this effect played.
2. `[On Play]` `[When Attacking]` Delete 1 of your opponent's unsuspended Digimon.
3. `[All Turns]` This Digimon can only digivolve into [Apocalymon].
4. `[On Deletion]` If you have no purple face-up security cards, place this Digimon face up as
   the bottom security card.
5. `[Security]` If this card was face-up, you may play 1 level 5 or lower card with
   [Dark Masters] in its text from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

Catalog check: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` matches the official text for every
field asserted in the test. No discrepancy found. The empty `evoCosts` is correct — the card
prints no digivolution requirement, exactly like its sibling EX10-035 Machinedramon — and it
means **there is no legal route that digivolves INTO Piedmon**; it can only be played.

## Peer and stack evidence

- **Sibling comparison.** EX10-035 Machinedramon prints the same five clause shapes in Black.
  The two modules were compared node by node: identical `Main`/`AllTurns`/`OnDeletion`/
  `Security` IR apart from the colour token in the `[On Deletion]` condition, and the
  card-specific middle clause (`Delete` here, `DeDigivolve` there). The colour token is proved
  in both directions — a purple face-up card blocks the placement, a red one does not.
- **Evolution stack.** Piedmon digivolves into BT15-102 Apocalymon through the public
  `digivolve` intent and the resulting permanent's `stack` is asserted to be exactly
  `["EX10-057"]`; the delayed delete then removes that whole permanent at turn end, which is
  the Q5155 identity claim ("the Digimon this effect played" survives a digivolve).
- **No route into Piedmon.** `evoCosts` is empty, so no legal digivolution or DNA route reaches
  this card; the only entries are `playCard` and its own `[Hand] [Main]` play, both exercised.
- **Cross-card.** EX3-029 Airdramon (an unrelated Yellow peer) supplies the only public
  security-shuffle route in the pool and is used to prove Q5153 against a face-up EX10-057.

## What the previous audit missed

The committed `EX10-057.test.ts` claimed 10/10 but:

- proved `[All Turns]` only by reading `advance(engine).ledgers.continuous.digivolveIntoAllowed`
  — a ledger reach, not a `digivolve` intent, and no stack identity;
- proved `[On Play]` only through `advance.fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnPlay, …)` — injected
  timing, which the brief scores 0 in the Behavior column;
- proved `[On Deletion]` only through `advance.verb.deletePermanent` — again injected, and it
  never asserted the BOTTOM position (`toTop: false`) or that the other security cards stay
  face down;
- had **no test at all** for `[When Attacking]`, for the turn-end `DelayedDeletePlayed`
  (Q5739/Q5155/Q5740), for the face-down `[Security]` negative (Q5156), for the level and
  text bounds on the `[Security]` free play, for the trash source, or for Q5153;
- asserted `s.state.memory` as `-6` from a hand-rolled `memory = 0` start rather than showing
  the reduction against the printed cost.

The module IR itself was already hand-corrected and is **correct as committed** — I changed
nothing in `EX10-057.ts`.

### Revert confirmation

One extra run with four IR levers broken at once
(`reduceCostBy: 5 → 0`, both `unsuspended: true` deleted, `Apocalymon → Millenniummon`,
`colors: ["Purple"] → ["Red"]`) turned **9 of the 19 tests red**
(`logs/EX10-057-revert-confirm.log`), each for its documented reason. The module was restored
byte-for-byte from a scratch copy and `git status` confirms `EX10-057.ts` is unmodified.

Caveat found by that run: the two positive `Delete` tests stayed green with `unsuspended: true`
removed, because the harness auto-selector happened to pick the unsuspended candidate anyway.
The lever that actually binds the filter is
`deletes nothing when every opposing Digimon is suspended`, which does go red. Noted rather
than papered over.

## Remaining gaps

- The `[On Play]`/`[When Attacking]` delete is not proved against a board where the *opponent*
  has an eligible ＜Blocker＞ or a would-leave replacement; the interaction is generic engine
  behaviour, not card-specific, and would need another card's clause to drive.
- Q5153 is proved on a face-up EX10-057 that was **seeded** face up in security rather than
  placed there by its own `[On Deletion]` first. Chaining a battle loss and then a shuffle in
  one fixture adds no ruling coverage, so the shorter arrangement was used deliberately.
- No engine seam is missing and no test is retained red.
