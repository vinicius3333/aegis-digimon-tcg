# EX10-056 Bagramon — re-audit

Purple, Lv.6 Mega, [Demon Lord]/[Bagra Army], 13000 DP, play cost 13, EvoCost Purple Lv.5 / 5.

## Printed clauses

1. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place 1 of your opponent's Digimon as any of their other
   Digimon's bottom digivolution card or under any of their Tamers.`
2. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers digivolve or effects
   place cards under them, by trashing any 2 of this Digimon's digivolution cards, trash your
   opponent's top security card.`
3. `[DigiXros -2] 2 Digimon cards w/[Bagra Army] trait`

Catalog matches the official text. No discrepancy found.

## Defects found and fixed

| # | Defect | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The placement moved the victim's WHOLE stack under the host. Q5145 says the placed Digimon leaves the battle area and its own digivolution cards are trashed at the same time. | `shedOwnCards: true` on both `PlaceUnder` actions. |
| 2 | The digivolve watcher's `sourceFilter.kind` listed only `Digimon`. The text reads "Digimon **or Tamers** digivolve", and the engine's `tamerDigivolvedGate` (`actions/subTrigger.ts`) withholds a watcher that does not name Tamer. | `kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]`. |
| 3 | The placement watcher accepted ANY digivolution-card addition. The text restricts it to "**effects** place cards under them". | `byEffect: true` on the `onAddDigivolutionCards` `sourceFilter`. |

The prior audit's "two-material DigiXros limit" correction is confirmed correct: `maxMaterials: 2`
holds, and the per-material reduction is proved to be 2 (13 → 11 with one material, 13 → 9 with
two), matching comprehensive rules §7-2-2-1 ("reduced by the amount shown … for each card placed").

## Remaining gaps and seams for the coordinator

- **Engine seam (offered but impossible effect).** `canAttemptPlaceUnder`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts`, the
  `action.targetIsPermanent === true` early return) answers `true` unconditionally, so the "you may"
  prompt is published even when the opponent controls no Digimon at all. Expected: no prompt when no
  legal source/destination pair exists. Actual: a `dec-1 optional` decision the test must decline.
  Worked around in the fixtures with `autoDeclineOptional`; not a card-level bug.
- **Tamer digivolve branch (defect 2) is structural only.** No public route reaches an opponent
  Tamer digivolving with the cards in this set, so the fix is asserted on the compiled IR, not on
  board state.
- **Q5146/Q5147** need inherited-effect assertions that belong to the engine and to BT17-087.
- `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` will drift from the three IR changes above; the
  coordinator owns the sync.
