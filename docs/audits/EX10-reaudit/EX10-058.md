## Card summary

Catalog (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`): Purple, Digimon, Lv.6, play cost 11,
DP 11000, EvoCost Purple/Lv.5/cost 3, form Mega, attribute Virus, traits `["Demon Lord",
"Bagra Army"]`, rarity R, max 4 per deck. No inherited text, no Security text, not an ACE, no
overflow. The catalog row matches the official text; no discrepancy found.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` — Until your opponent's turn ends, give 1 of their Digimon or
   Tamers "[End of Your Turn] Delete 1 of your Digimon."
2. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` — When any of your opponent's Digimon are played or deleted, by
   trashing any 2 of this Digimon's digivolution cards, you may play 1 level 4 or lower purple
   Digimon card from your trash without paying the cost.
3. `[DigiXros -2] 2 Digimon cards w/[Bagra Army] trait`.

Rules anchors: comprehensive §7-2-1 (materials come from hand and/or battle area),
§7-2-2-1 (the play cost is reduced by the printed amount **for each** card placed),
§7-2-2-4 (any number of the specified cards, never zero), §7-2-2-7 (a battle-area material
contributes only its top card; its own digivolution cards are shed).

## DigiXros evidence (the coordinator's focus)

- **Material sources.** Hand is proved twice (one- and two-material plays). Battle area is proved
  by a new test: the material permanent leaves the battle area, only its top card lands in
  Lilithmon's stack, and the card beneath it goes to the trash (§7-2-2-7). Trash is proved to be
  **refused** — this card's IR declares no trash allowance and no expander Tamer is in play, so a
  hand + trash declaration returns `{ok:false, reason:"invalid-material"}` with memory, board and
  trash unchanged.
- **Reduced cost per material.** One material: 11 − 2 = 9 (memory 11 → 2). Two materials:
  11 − 4 = 7 (memory 11 → 4). The two runs together prove the discount is per placed card
  (§7-2-2-1), not a flat reduction. This is the gap the previous audit left: it only ran the
  two-material case, which a flat "−4" implementation would also pass.
- **Material cap.** Three `[Bagra Army]` materials are refused with an explicit
  `reason:"invalid-material"` (was `.ok === false`), memory and hand unchanged. This is
  `maxMaterials: 2` doing the work; without it `materialsSatisfyRecipe`'s single-slot branch
  would accept any number.
- **Non-matching material.** EX10-040 DemiDevimon is purple but carries `[Evil]`, not
  `[Bagra Army]`. The declaration is refused with `reason:"invalid-material"` (was a weak
  `.not.toEqual({ok:true})`), and the refusal is now asserted to be inert — memory 11, empty
  battle area, hand still 4.

## Retained gaps

1. **Seam 21 — `pending-activation-lapses-when-source-leaves` (Q5160).** The two committed
   Q5160 tests produce the ruling's outcome in both directions: the EX10-044 [Damemon] that is
   trashed as the cost and then replayed from the trash does **not** draw, while a second
   [Damemon] left in the trash does. But the engine has no general gate that cancels a pending
   triggered effect whose source left its area (REVIEW-NOTES.md item 21). EX10-044's inherited
   clause is a `Static` `SubTrigger` on `onDigivolutionCardsDiscardedBatch`, armed only while the
   card sits under a `[Bagra Army]` Digimon, so the right answer here falls out of continuous
   re-derivation plus in-window play ordering — not out of the rule Q5160 states. The tests stay
   green because the observable endpoints are correct; the engine-wide gate is still missing and
   remains the coordinator's item.
2. **The shared once-per-turn key across the two event forms is structural.** The reset and the
   same-turn refusal are now proved through the real turn loop with public `playCard` intents.
   What is still injected is the specific claim that a *played* event and a *deleted* event drain
   the **same** budget (`fireSubTrigger("whenPlayed", …)` after a public deletion). Arranging both
   forms publicly in one turn needs an opponent deletion and an opponent play in the same window,
   which no single legal line here produces.
3. **Latent engine seam, not a defect today (same finding as EX10-055).**
   `validateDigiXros` computes
   `perMaterialReduction = requirement.count === "∞" ? (requirement.costReduction ?? 1) : requirement.count`.
   For a numeric `count`, `costReduction` is **ignored** and `count` doubles as the per-material
   reduction. EX10-058 has `count === costReduction === 2`, so the field is dead but harmless. I
   left it in place: removing it is a no-op behaviourally and would drift `effects.json` for
   nothing. A future `[DigiXros -1] 2 cards …` recipe would silently mischarge.
4. **Q5168's other half is out of scope.** The ruling is written from the perspective of the card
   that *gains* EX10-058's `[All Turns]`; that grant lives in another lane's module. What is
   provable here — that EX10-058's own `[All Turns]` triggers off a deletion its own effect
   caused — is proved.
