`[Machine]`/`[Dark Masters]`, play cost 11, 11000 DP, `evoCosts: []` (no digivolution
requirement — it can only be played), `maxCountInDeck: 4`, no inherited text.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Hand] [Main]` If you don't have any Digimon other than Digimon with `[Dark Masters]` in
   their texts, you may play this card with the play cost reduced by 5. At turn end, delete
   the Digimon this effect played.
2. `[On Play]` `[When Attacking]` ＜De-Digivolve 2＞ 2 of your opponent's Digimon.
3. `[All Turns]` This Digimon can only digivolve into `[Apocalymon]`.
4. `[On Deletion]` If you have no black face-up security cards, place this Digimon face up as
   the bottom security card.
5. `[Security]` If this card was face-up, you may play 1 level 5 or lower card with
   `[Dark Masters]` in its text from your hand or trash without paying the cost.

No catalog discrepancy found: the catalog text matches the official print, and the module
mirrors it clause for clause.

## What the previous state missed

The committed test passed (11 tests) but proved four clauses through injected timing or
private engine state, which earns no behavioural credit under this run's rules:

- `[On Play]` De-Digivolve: `advance(s.engine).fireForPermanent(EffectTiming.OnPlay, …)`.
- `[When Attacking]` De-Digivolve: **not tested at all**.
- Turn-end self-delete: a private `fireTiming(EffectTiming.OnEndTurn)` cast plus a hand-set
  `state.turnSeat`, not the turn loop.
- `[All Turns]` digivolve restriction: a hand-built `ContinuousEffectLedger` and a direct
  `validateDigivolve` call with a hand-written `digivolveIntoAllowed` dep — the card wiring
  was never exercised.
- `[On Deletion]`: fired through `advance.verb.deletePermanent`, and it asserted only
  `faceUp`, never the **bottom** position and never the negative branch (a black face-up
  security card already present).
- Only 3 of the 11 listed Q&A ids were named.

The rewritten suite drives every clause from a public intent (`playCard`, `attack`,
`digivolve`, `activateEffect`) or the real turn loop (`startTurnLoop` +
`advance.waitForMainPhase`). No `advance.fire*` / `fireTiming` remains in the file.

IR verification notes (read against the engine, not assumed):

- `duration: "permanent"` is a real `EffectDurationRef`
  (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/durations.ts`) mapping to `EffectDuration.Permanent`.
- `match: "any"` and `match: "text"` are the same full union (name ∪ traits ∪ every text
  field) in `matchNameOrTrait`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:296`). That is exactly the
  Q5104 semantic, so both the exclude gate and the `[Security]` filter are faithful.
- `RestrictDigivolveInto` is consulted by the production digivolve intent:
  `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolve.ts:540` -> `continuous.digivolveIntoAllowed`. The
  test therefore proves the card wiring, not a hand-installed ledger entry.
- `DeDigivolve` is dispatched with `byEffectSeat: ctx.source.ownerSeat`
  (`interpreter/actions/digivolution.ts:48`), so the EX11-070 stacked-trash-lock and the
  EX10-029 `cantBeDeDigivolved` grant apply correctly. No defect here.
- The level floor is the engine default `stopAtLevel ?? 3` in `peelStackTops`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:1868`); the test asserts it on a Lv.4 top.
