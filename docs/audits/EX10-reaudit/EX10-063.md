official text; no discrepancy found.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Start of Your Main Phase]` By returning this Tamer to the bottom of the deck, you may
   play 1 [Close] from your hand without paying the cost.

## What changed

### Test rewrite (the substantive finding)

The committed test proved every clause through **injected timing**: `advance.fireForPermanent`
for the start-of-main window, `advance.fireSubTrigger("whenDigivolutionTrashed", …)` twice, and
`advance.fireForInstance(EffectTiming.SecuritySkill, …)` for the Security effect. Under the
rubric that is structural evidence only, so the card had no behavioural proof at all.

The file is now 15 tests (13 after round 1, 2 added in round 2), none of which use `advance.fire*`:

- the start-of-main window comes from `s.engine.startTurnLoop()` + `advance.waitForMainPhase(0)`;
- the digivolution-card trash comes from `playCard` on EX10-028 Landramon, whose `[On Play]`
  cost is a genuine effect-trash of a [Mineral] card from one of my Digimon's stacks;
- the Security effect comes from a real `attack` on the player that checks security.

New coverage the committed test did not have:

- Q5743 at all (the newly played Close must not chain its own start-of-main window);
- the "you don't have a Digimon" gate, positive and negative;
- the breeding-area reading of that gate (CR 3-4-5-8);
- the host-trait negative (a [Mineral] card trashed from a non-[Mineral]/[Rock] host does not
  fire the watcher);
- the Tamer being inert in the hand and in the trash (requested by the coordinator);
- the unpayable-cost case (an already suspended Tamer gains nothing);
- `[All Turns]` proved by the watcher still being armed after the turn changes, observed
  through the real turn loop.

### Module

One change, in `apps/api/src/cards/EX10/EX10-063.ts`: the `youHaveNone` filter now names
`zone: "battleArea"` explicitly, with a comment citing CR 3-4-5-8.

Honest scope: this is **documentation strength, not a behaviour fix**. `countMatching`
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:87-107`) only adds the breeding permanent
when `filter.zone` is an array containing `"breeding"`, so the previous zone-less filter already
counted the battle area only. The `a Digimon in BREEDING…` test passes both with and without
the edit. The edit states the intent instead of relying on a default, which the worker brief
asks for. It does drift `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`; the coordinator's sync
covers that.

## Round 2: the opponent-turn firing, proved

### Does ＜De-Digivolve＞ count as "effects trash … digivolution cards"? No.

Checked in the engine, not assumed. `deDigivolve` is `peelStackTops`
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:1849-1929`). It moves the permanent's current
**top card** to the trash and promotes the card beneath it, then emits
`whenDigimonTopTrashed` (line 2828 region, inside the same function). It never calls
`trashDigivolutionCards`.

The two emitters of this Tamer's event are both in the same file:

- `trashDigivolutionCards` — fires `onDigivolutionCardsDiscardedBatch` (line 2769) and then,
  per card, `onDigivolutionCardDiscarded` and `whenDigivolutionTrashed` (line 2796);
- `trashDigivolutionCardsAtomic` — same batch/per-card pair (lines 2882 / 2901).

So an opponent's ＜De-Digivolve＞ (EX8-049 Golemon, EX7-046 Jazarichmon) does **not** arm
EX10-063. That matches the printed text: ＜De-Digivolve＞ trashes the top card, which is the
Digimon itself, not one of its digivolution cards.

### The route that does work

`BT15-019` Crabmon — blue Lv.3, play cost 3 — prints `[On Play] Trash the bottom digivolution
card of 1 of your opponent's Digimon`, compiled as `TrashDigivolution` with
`target.filter.controller: "opponent"`, `amount: 1`, `fromTop: false`. Played by **seat 1 on
seat 1's turn** through the public `playCard` intent, it routes into `trashDigivolutionCards`
and therefore fires `whenDigivolutionTrashed` with my `[Mineral]` host as
`subjectPermanentId`.

`[All Turns] on the OPPONENT's turn: their BT15-019 trashes my [Mineral] Digimon's
digivolution card` drives exactly that, through `startTurnLoop()` (no `advance.fire*`):

| Endpoint | Value |
| --- | --- |
| turn seat | 1 (asserted before and after) |
| my host's stack | `[topFuel]` — the bottom card left |
| my trash | contains `bottomFuel` |
| my Tamer | `isSuspended === true` |
| gauge (`state.memory`, turn-relative) | 6 -> 3 (seat 1 pays for Crabmon) -> **2** (seat 0's Tamer gains 1) |
| opponent hand | one card lighter; Crabmon's own "then" draw did not run |
| pending decision | none |

The gauge assertion is the cross-seat proof. `state.memory` is turn-relative
(`MemoryGauge.value`, `apps/api/src/engine/MemoryGauge.ts:53-56`), so on seat 1's turn a
positive value is seat 1's memory. The `GainMemory` action calls
`gainMemoryForSeat(ctx.source.ownerSeat, …)`
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/resources.ts:44-65`) — the **controller's**
seat, not the turn player's — so the gauge moves 3 -> 2, i.e. one memory to seat 0 while seat
1 is the turn player. A turn-player implementation would have moved it to 4.

This also closes the round-1 controller gap: the trashed cards belong to seat 0's Digimon and
seat 0's Tamer is the one that fired, on a turn seat 0 does not own.

### Peer comparison with EX10-032 Proganomon

`peer EX10-032: this Tamer is the [Close] that opens Proganomon's [Hand] [Main] route` runs the
same board twice. With this Tamer in play, Proganomon exposes exactly one activatable effect,
the `activateEffect` intent places Landramon from the trash as the bottom digivolution card of
BT21-055 Sunarizamon and Sunarizamon digivolves into Proganomon (stack order asserted:
`[landramon, suna]`); this Tamer is still on the board afterwards. Remove the Tamer and
`activatableEffectsJson` is empty — the `youHave [Close]` gate is closed. This is the
cross-card proof that the Tamer's printed **name** is load-bearing for its own archetype.

## Two fixture facts worth recording

- The first turn player does **not** draw, so a turn-loop test that asserts deck identity must
  compare against the untouched deck, not `deck.slice(1)`.
- A permanent in the breeding area parks the turn loop in the Breeding phase until the turn
  player acts; `advance.waitForMainPhase(0)` then throws. The public way through is to settle
  on `Phase.Breeding` and send `endPhase` for that seat before waiting for Main.
