## Contract and evidence

The catalog defines TyrantKabuterimon as a green level-6 Insectoid/TS Digimon,
play cost 11, 11,000 DP. It evolves from a level-5 Insectoid/TS Digimon for
3, or by Assembly for 6 less using four Larva/Insectoid/Titan cards with
different levels. On Play and When Digivolving it may immediately battle one
opposing Digimon. In each of those windows, and at the start of your main
