# BT26-006 — Monimon — 8/10

## Contract and evidence

The catalog defines Monimon as a purple level-2 Digi-Egg with `CRT` and
`Bagra Army` traits. Its inherited text is `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn]
By trashing any 2 digivolution cards from your [Bagra Army] trait Digimon, you
may play or use 1 [Bagra Army] trait card from your hand with the cost reduced
by 2.`

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT26-006` returns Q6959, Q6960, and Q6961. Q6959
requires both cost cards to be trashed atomically; Q6960 says the attack does
not succeed if the attacker becomes DigiXros material; Q6961 says a pending
effect on a trashed source cannot activate after that source leaves the trash.

`BT26-006.ts` maps the text to one inherited `WhenAttacking` effect with
`OncePerTurn` frequency and a one-choice modal. The play branch selects one
own-hand Bagra Army Digimon or Tamer, and the use branch selects one own-hand
Bagra Army Option; both reduce the cost by 2 and share an atomic cost requiring
exactly two cards from own Bagra Army Digimon stacks. Play permits DigiXros.
Registration remains exclusively `registerIrCard("BT26-006", compiled)`.

## Behavioral proof

`BT26-006.test.ts` proves:

- play of a Bagra Army Tamer and Digimon with the printed reduction;
- use of the two-card cross-host cost and once-per-turn suppression;
- Q6959 one-source and protected-source atomic rejection;
- exclusion of sources under a non-Bagra Army host;
- optional refusal with unchanged sources, hand, and memory;
- Q6960 attack termination when the attacker becomes DigiXros material; and
- Q6961 loss of a pending trashed-source effect after DigiXros movement.

The cases use real stacked Digimon and mixed eligible/ineligible board state,
and assert source instance identities, zones, memory, attack/security state,
and pending-effect outcomes.

Fixture correction: the Q6960 and Q6961 attack scenarios use inert main-deck
BT1-009 in security rather than the invalid Digi-Egg BT1-001. Test intent and
behavioral results are unchanged.
