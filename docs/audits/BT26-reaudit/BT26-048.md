# BT26-048 — BloomLordmon — 8/8

## Contract and evidence

The catalog defines BloomLordmon as a green level-6 DM Digimon, play cost 11,
11,000 DP. It evolves from a level-5 DM Digimon for 3. It has Alliance and
Vortex. When Digivolving and When Attacking, it may trash the bottom
face-down card under one of your Digimon to play a level-4-or-lower Digimon
with the Ver.4 trait and 6,000 DP or less from hand without paying its cost.
All Turns, when a face-down digivolution card is trashed from under one of
your Digimon by an effect, one opposing Digimon gets -6,000 DP until the end
of the turn; a simultaneous batch activates this once.

covered by a simultaneous two-card trash test proving one debuff activation.
Q7051 is covered by a public attack: the Digimon played by the When Attacking
effect is available for Alliance in that same attack. The focused suite also
covers the first face-down card above a face-up bottom stack boundary.

`BT26-048.ts` maps alternate evolution, Alliance/Vortex, the face-down trash
cost and Ver.4 target boundary in both windows, and the effect-originated
batch discard reaction. It contains no suppression and registers exclusively
with `registerIrCard("BT26-048", compiled)`.
