## Printed contract

- Red/purple level 4 Digimon, play cost 4, 4000 DP, with alternate level-3 `[TS]` evolution for cost 2.
- On Play / When Digivolving: draw one, then trash one card.
- At the end of your turn, if you have a blue or yellow Digimon, you may return this card to the bottom of its owner's deck to play a red or blue `[Iliad]` Digimon from your trash for 4 less memory.
- Inherited Retaliation.

- Public turn-loop tests now prove both positive and decline End-of-Turn paths. The positive path uses a real red `[Iliad]` target whose printed cost is below the −4 reduction floor; the separate BT26-060 case proves the unaffordable high-cost negative.
- The public trigger is reached through `startTurnLoop` and phase transitions; no direct `turnSeat`, timing injection, numeric security, or Digi-Egg fixture is used.

## PlayWithoutCost mechanism boundary

The real public turn pass sets the outgoing player's gauge to `-3` before `OnEndTurn`. Consequently, BT26-060 (cost 16, reduced to 12) is correctly unaffordable in that public window and must not open an optional decision or pay the self-return cost. The green positive uses BT25-008 (red `[Iliad]`, cost 3), so the same −4 reduction floors at zero: Wizardmon reaches deck bottom and BT25-008 reaches the battle area. The decline path exposes the optional decision and leaves both cards unmoved.

The exact mechanism analysis is recorded in [`BT26-067-PLAY-WITHOUT-COST-MECHANISM.md`](./BT26-067-PLAY-WITHOUT-COST-MECHANISM.md).
