## Mechanisms

### Successful deletion receipt

ST17-04 uses typed lastDeletedMatchesFilter. Removal snapshots top-card physical identity/controller before deletion and retains only selected permanents actually absent after deletion. Prevented/Armor Purge survivors and empty attempts cannot unlock revival. Interpreter result bindings propagate this receipt between sibling actions. interpreter.test.ts covers own/opponent snapshots, prevention and stale receipt clearing. Current filter support is controller plus card-definition predicates; arbitrary board-state predicates and nested receipt scopes are not claimed.

### Latent continuous restrictions under immunity

Restrict target resolution preserves immune selections; continuous state stores the restriction and qualified immunity suppresses its application until immunity expires. ST17-08 Q831 publicly proves installation during immunity, immunity expiry before opponent Active, suspension retained in Active, and restriction expiry at opponent end. Existing immunity and continuous-effect mechanism suites are included in regression.

### Shared unchanged mechanisms

Conformance/combat suites cover Alliance consent/multiplicity, Reboot lifecycle, Blocker/Armor Purge, ACE Counter/Overflow, Delay boundaries and exact evolution requirements. Henry processing/ordering is covered by activation-processing-costs.test.ts; public Rush/expiry is in ST17-10.test.ts and engine/rush-lifecycle.md.
