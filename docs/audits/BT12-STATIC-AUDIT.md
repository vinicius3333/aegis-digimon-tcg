## Result

All 112 catalog cards have direct TypeScript modules, focused behavioral
tests, full compiled IR coverage, no residual clauses, and exactly one
matching `registerIrCard` registration. No BT12 module uses `registerCard`
or a handwritten timing override.

The range reports in `internal-docs/audits/BT12/` are historical discovery
notes from the pre-repair pass and may describe limitations that this branch
resolved. They are not final evidence. The executable proof for every card ID
is the combination of:

- `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/<CARD-ID>.ts`;
- `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/<CARD-ID>.test.ts`;
- the per-card registration and coverage case generated by
  `BT12-catalog-sync.test.ts`; and
- the matching generated record in
  `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`.

## Notable repaired seams

- Reveal-and-add distinct-color validation and atomic remainder movement.
- Mixed-source exact-name placement with deterministic ordering.
- Return-to-deck stack ordering before the host card.
- Optional SelectBind reducers with no payable candidates.
- Effect-driven digivolution cost finalization, including one-use placement
  costs and reservation of the evolving card.
- Typed condition and duration support required by direct BT12 IR.
- Removal of the obsolete `_midHandwritten.ts` and
  `_lateHandwritten.ts` compatibility modules.

The generated effects catalog is no longer maintained manually for this
workflow. Run `pnpm effects:sync:set -- --set BT12` to update BT12 records,
or `pnpm effects:check:set -- --set BT12 --base origin/main` to verify scope
and drift without writing.
