# BT17-032 Kyubimon re-audit (lane 41)

## Card summary

Digimon, Lv4, Yellow, playCost 5, DP 5000, type Mysterious Beast, evoCost Yellow Lv3 = 2.

Printed clauses:

1. `[When Digivolving]` If you don't have `[Rika Nonaka]`, you may play 1 `[Rika Nonaka]`
   from your hand without paying the cost.
2. Inherited `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn]`: When you use an Option card with a cost of 2 or
   more, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gains `＜Security Attack -1＞` until the end of their
   turn.

Both clauses are behaviourally proven. No injected timing is used (the prior test relied on
`fireSubTrigger`/`fireForPermanent`, which earn no behavioural credit; all rewritten cases
drive real `applyIntent` digivolve/playCard flows).

## Defects fixed locally

Replaced three injected-timing cases (`fireSubTrigger`, `fireForPermanent`) with real
`applyIntent` flows, and the two shape-only `toMatchObject` cases with one full-IR `toEqual`

## Remaining gaps

  Running to the opponent's turn end and back is heavier than this lane's test budget allows.

  is asserted only as IR; expiry is not run to (same turn-cycle cost).
- Q2783, Q5455, Q5456 are reasoned from engine source, not built as fixtures (Security-use,
  BeforePayCost reduction, and free-use Options respectively).
