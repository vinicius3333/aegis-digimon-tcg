## Q&A coverage

- **Q2875** (an effect with `[On Play] [Main]` still activates at Main): covered behaviourally.
  Opponent plays BT4-093 Thomas H. Norstein while Menoa + BT17-074 Eosmon are out; its `[On Play]`
  ＜Draw 1＞ never fires (the named deck card stays in the deck, hand size unchanged) while the mask
  is scoped to `onPlay` in the IR. `observe(...).timingEffectDisabled` only exposes
  `onPlay`/`whenDigivolving`/`whenAttacking`, so the Main window is asserted through the IR
  `timings: ["onPlay"]` list, not an observable.
- **Q2876** ("would leave" also covers return to hand/deck, security, breeding and being placed
  under a card): now covered behaviourally. `Q2876: prevents an opponent bounce by deleting
  another Eosmon` has seat 1 play BT15-090 Fox Fire against two BT17-074; the bounce is replaced,
  seat 0's hand stays empty, one Eosmon stays on the field and the other is the deletion cost.
  The previous session's claim that Fox Fire "does nothing in this harness" was a fixture bug, not
  an engine gap: seat 1's play was set up with a memory value that left it unaffordable
  (`maxAffordable` is turn-player-relative). With `state.memory = 10` on seat 1's turn the Option
  resolves and the return happens. No engine change was involved.
