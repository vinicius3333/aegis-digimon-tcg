## Defects fixed

1. **Set-wide `[Name]` substring defect.** Five printed exact bracket references compiled
   as `match: "name"` (substring): the `[Guilmon]` host (both `target.filter` and
   `cost.underFilter`), `[Gallantmon]` in `into`, `[Growlmon]` and `[WarGrowlmon]` in the
   additional costs, and `[Takato Matsuki]` in the cost target. All moved to `nameExact`.
   This was live, not cosmetic: `Growlmon` as a substring matches `WarGrowlmon`,
   `BlackGrowlmon` and `Growlmon (X Antibody)`, so the printed 2-card trash cost could be
   paid with a single WarGrowlmon plus a BlackGrowlmon; `Guilmon` as a substring accepted
   `Guilmon (X Antibody)` as a host. The `[Guilmon]/[Growlmon]/[Gallantmon] in its name`
   condition on clause 1 is correctly left as substring.
2. **"this Tamer" was any Takato.** The cost target matched by name only, so a second copy
   of BT17-080 on the board could be placed instead of the trigger's own source. Added
   `isSelfRef: true`.
3. **`youHave` counted the breeding area.** Added `zone: "battleArea"` (Comprehensive Rules
   §3-4-5-8).

**IR changed: yes.** `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will be red until the coordinator runs
the set sync. `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was not touched.

## Retained red (engine seam)

`it.fails("Q2853: places the cost cards even when the digivolve itself is declined")`.

- Ruling: placing this Tamer, 1 `[Growlmon]` and 1 `[WarGrowlmon]` and then declining the
  digivolve is legal, because the "may" sits on the digivolve, not on the placement.
- IR/engine: the clause is one optional `Digivolve` action carrying the placement as its
  `cost`. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts`
  (`runActionInner`, the optional prompt ahead of `payableActionCost`) asks a single
  yes/no covering cost **and** effect, so a decline skips both. There is no IR spelling
  for "pay, then optionally do" — `DigivolveViaPlacement`
  (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/actions/digivolve.ts:106`) is a different shape (the
  effect source itself digivolves onto the host) and does not fit.
- Expected: BT17-080, BT17-010 and BT17-013 sit in the `[Guilmon]`'s stack, the Digimon is
  still BT17-008, and BT17-016 is still in hand. Actual: nothing moves.
- Not an engine edit (out of lane scope). The existing green test
  "may decline the natural end-of-turn evolution without moving its required cards" pins
  the current behaviour so the seam's closure is visible.

## Engine lane E2 resolution (session 3) — retained red CLOSED

the leading optional prompt for that flag and raises the payload prompt AFTER the
additional-cost loop, so the whole printed "By placing this Tamer and 1 [Growlmon] and 1
[WarGrowlmon]" cost is paid before the digivolve is offered (Q2853). `it.fails` flipped
to `it`.
