## Defects fixed locally (both IR changes)

1. Inherited redirect carried `unsuspended: true` in the `RedirectAttack` filter. The
   printed text sets no suspension restriction, §11-2-7 imposes none on a switched
   target, and Q2842 confirms an unsuspended Eosmon is a legal target. The filter wrongly
   excluded suspended Eosmon. Removed it; the filter now matches any of your `[Eosmon]`.
   Guarded by "redirects ... onto a suspended Eosmon".
2. Digivolve route used `names: ["Eosmon"]` (substring). The printed line
   `[Digivolve]Lv.4 [Eosmon]: Cost 3` has no "in name", so per the coordinator route
   decision it must be `namesExact`. Changed to `namesExact: ["Eosmon"]`.

Both change the compiled IR, so `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will go red until the
coordinator runs the set sync. Not touched here (effects.json is out of lane).
