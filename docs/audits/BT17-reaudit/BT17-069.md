# BT17-069 Fenriloogamon — re-audit (lane 53)

## Card summary and printed clauses

Digimon, Lv6, Purple/Red, DP 11000, play cost 11. Restricted to 1 copy since
2025-03-28 (only BT17 banlist entry).

Catalog `effectText` / `inheritedEffectText` match the lane sheet exactly. No
`[Security]` clause is present, so none was omitted from the sheet.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve] Lv.5 w/[SoC] trait: Cost 3` — trait-based alternate route.
2. `[When Digivolving]` — if `[Eiji Nagasumi]` is in the digivolution cards, may
   play 1 `[Fenriloogamon]`/`[Kazuchimon]` from trash without paying the cost;
   at the next end of the opponent's turn, return that Digimon to the hand.
3. `[All Turns][Once Per Turn]` — when one of your Digimon or Tamers with the
   `[SoC]` trait or `[Pulsemon]` in its text is played, delete 1 opposing
   Digimon with 10000 DP or less.
4. Inherited `[Your Turn]` — while this has `[Fenriloogamon]` in its name, the
   turn-end condition becomes the opponent having 3 or more memory.

## Clause -> test -> IR mapping

- Clause 1 -> `refuses the SoC digivolve route from a non-SoC level 5 source`
  (added) and the SoC-route digivolve inside `plays the matching Fenriloogamon
  from trash...` -> IR `digivolutionRequirement[0]` `{ level: 5, traits:
  ["SoC"], cost: 3, isAlternate: true }`. Route is trait-based, so `traits` is
  correct; the coordinator's `namesExact` rule applies only to name routes and
  does not apply here.
- Clause 2 -> `plays the matching Fenriloogamon from trash and returns it at the
  next opponent turn end` -> IR `WhenDigivolving` actions: `PlayWithoutCost`
  (from `["trash"]`, `bindResultAs: "playedFenriloogamon"`, condition
  `selfDigivolutionStackHasTrait` on `[Eiji Nagasumi]`) then `DelayedEffect`
  (`Return` of `boundRef` to hand, `trigger: nextEndOfOpponentTurn`).
- Clause 3 -> `deletes a 10000 DP opposing Digimon when an SoC Digimon is
  played`, `deletes a 10000 DP opposing Digimon on the Pulsemon-in-text branch
  with no SoC source` (added), `accepts Pulsemon text and enforces the 10000
  DP boundary once per turn`, and `deletes once per turn: refuses a second
  same-turn play, resets on the next own turn` (added) -> IR `AllTurns`
  `SubTrigger` (`whenPlayed`, `sourceFilter` `mine` Digimon/Tamer `nameOrTrait`
  SoC-trait or Pulsemon-text) with `Delete` `opponent` `dp lte 10000`,
  `frequency: OncePerTurn`.
- Clause 4 -> `keeps the active turn through opponent memory 1 or 2, then ends
  at 3` -> IR inherited `YourTurn` `SetTurnEndMemory` `minimum: 3` under
  `selfHasNameContaining ["Fenriloogamon"]`.

Structural shape tests (first three `it`s) assert the IR objects directly; they
carry no behavioural credit on their own.

## Q&A coverage (Q2830-Q2834)

- Q2830 (played Digimon returned at opponent turn end; only top card returned,
  digivolution cards trashed): behaviourally covered — BT14-081 is played from
  trash and returns to hand at the next opponent turn end. Partial on the
  sub-nuance: the played card has no digivolution cards in the fixture, so the
  "trash the under-cards on return" branch is exercised only by the generic
  return path, not with a stacked returnee.
- Q2831 (turn continues at opponent memory 1-2, ends at 3+): behaviourally
  covered by the inherited-clause test (continues at -1, ends at -4).
- Q2832 (effect applies only on your turn): structurally covered — the clause is
  `trigger: "YourTurn"`, so it never installs on the opponent's turn. Not
  separately behaviourally tested (asserting a non-event).
- Q2833 (an `[End of Your Turn]` effect that lowers opponent memory back to 1-2
  lets your turn continue): NOT covered. This is a turn-end re-evaluation timing
  interaction in the engine, not a clause this card can assert in isolation.
- Q2834 (`<Blitz>` still needs opponent memory 1+ to attack): NOT covered —
  depends on the `<Blitz>` keyword engine, outside this card's effect surface.

## Changes made

- `BT17-069.test.ts`: added three behavioural tests.
  - Illegal-source negative for the SoC route: a non-SoC Lv.5 base (BT1-020,
    Earth Dragon) with memory 10 (so the failure is the trait check, not cost)
    is refused with `{ ok: false, reason: "invalid-evolution" }`.
  - Pulsemon-in-text isolation: playing BT16-039 (Pulsemon, no SoC trait,
    `[Pulsemon]` in its text) alone deletes a 10000-DP opponent Digimon. The
    pre-existing OPT test only played BT16-039 after the SoC source had already
    consumed the once-per-turn use, so the Pulsemon-text branch was never proven
    to fire on its own.
  - Once-per-turn reset through the real turn loop: seat 0 plays an SoC Digimon
    (deletes one of three targets, two remain), plays a second SoC Digimon the
    same turn (refused, still two remain), then after a real opponent turn and
    the seat-0 owner-turn-start boundary plays a third SoC Digimon (fires again,
    one remains). Uses `runOneTurn` + `waitForMainPhase(0)` per own turn and an
    emulated `passTurn` (flip `turnSeat`, re-frame the gauge) between turns, the
    same pattern as BT17-048. Intermediate target counts asserted at each step.
- `BT17-069.ts`: unchanged. IR was already correct; no IR change, so
  `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` stays green.
