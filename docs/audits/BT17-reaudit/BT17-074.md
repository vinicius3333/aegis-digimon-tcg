# BT17-074 Eosmon — re-audit

## Card

White Lv4 Digimon, DP 4000, play cost 4, trait Unknown. No printed `evoCosts`,
so the `[Digivolve][Morphomon]: Cost 2` line is the only route onto it.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve][Morphomon]: Cost 2` — alternate route.
2. `[When Digivolving]` If it's your turn, you may play 1 white Tamer card with a
   play cost of 4 or less **or** 1 level 5 or lower [Eosmon] from your hand for a
   cost of 2. If you do, your opponent may play 1 Tamer card from their hand
   without paying the cost.
3. Inherited: `[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn]` When an opponent's Digimon
   attacks, you may switch the attack target to 1 of your [Eosmon].

Catalog text matches the assignment exactly; no discrepancy.

## Q&A covered

- **Q2840** (the cost of 2 is paid even when the played card is a white Tamer):
  covered by "plays a white cost-4-or-less Tamer for 2 memory on your turn" —
  memory 5 -> 1 (2 digivolution cost + 2 effect cost).
- **Q2841** (the inherited effect may switch the target to an *unsuspended*
  [Eosmon]): covered by "redirects an opponent attack only to an unsuspended
  Eosmon" and "redirects only one opponent attack per turn".

## Gaps

- "If it's your turn" has no negative test. A `digivolve` intent is already
  rejected outside your own Main phase, so the `isYourTurn` guard is only
  reachable through an effect-driven digivolve on the opponent's turn; no BT17
  card in this lane provides that route. Structure is asserted instead.
- Cross-lane observation (not my files): in "plays a white cost-4-or-less Tamer…"
  and in the decline test, memory drops by the full digivolution cost of 2.
  `BT17-044` Morphomon prints "[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into
  [Eosmon], reduce the digivolution cost by 1", which would make it 1. That
  reduction does not apply. Belongs to the BT17-044 lane.
