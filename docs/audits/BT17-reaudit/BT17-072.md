# BT17-072 Ornismon — re-audit (lane 4)

## Card summary

Catalog: Digimon, Lv6, Purple, play cost 13, DP 13000, Mega, Virus, type Ancient Bird.
Digivolution requirements: Purple Lv5 cost 5, Yellow Lv5 cost 5. No inherited effect.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's unsuspended Digimon.` — one
   mandatory deletion on two triggers.
2. `[All Turns] While you have another level 6 Digimon, this Digimon gets +2000 DP and
   gains ＜Security Attack +1＞.` — two conditional continuous grants on one condition.

The `it.each` digivolve test proves source-stack identity (same permanent id, Ornismon on
top, exactly the original source instance beneath), the digivolve bonus draw, memory
5 -> 0, and the opponent's deleted Digimon reaching trash. The Security Attack test drives
a real player attack and asserts that two security cards leave (three seeded, one left),
rather than reading the grant only.

The "drops both when it leaves" step removes the other level 6 from the battle area
and calls `s.engine.recomputeContinuousEffects()`, then re-asserts DP 13000 and no
＜Security Attack＞ — proving the aura is conditional, not one-shot.

## Peer/stack comparative case (session 3)

New test: `counts only the top card's level: a level 6 in another Digimon's digivolution cards
is not another level 6`. One fixture holds Ornismon beside a near-miss peer — a BT2-075 (Lv5)
permanent seeded with `under: ["BT17-071"]`, so a level 6 card is physically present on the
board but only inside the peer's digivolution cards. The aura stays off (13000 DP, no
＜Security Attack＞). The same fixture then gains a real BT17-071 permanent through
`putOnBoard`, and both grants switch on (15000 DP, keyword amount 1). This proves the
`youHave levels: [6]` filter reads top cards only, with the matching and non-matching cases
side by side.

## Defects found and fixed locally

The pre-existing suite had no evolution proof, no illegal source, no empty-target case for
`[On Play]`, no opposing-level-6 negative, no proof that the aura drops when the condition
stops holding, and only a boolean `hasKeyword` read for ＜Security Attack +1＞ — which does
not prove the amount is 1 or that it reaches combat. All of that is now covered, and the
partial IR `toMatchObject`/`objectContaining` assertions were replaced with a catalog match
plus full deep equality.

One fixture bug was found while writing the tests: `BT17-071` (Murmukusmon) is level 6, so
it is not a legal digivolution source for this card. The Purple route now uses `BT2-075`
(Myotismon, Lv5 Purple) and the Yellow route `BT1-057` (Sirenmon, Lv5 Yellow).

No module (`BT17-072.ts`) change was needed. The IR was already correct.
