## Card summary and printed clauses

Aldamon, Digimon, Lv5, Red/Purple, 8000 DP, form Hybrid, type Wizard.

- C1 [Hand][Main]: By placing 1 [Agunimon] and 1 [BurningGreymon] from trash under 1 of
  your [Takuya Kanbara]s, digivolve it into this card as if that card is a level 4 red
  Digimon for a digivolution cost of 3.

- C3 inherited [Your Turn]: While it has the [Hybrid]/[Ten Warriors] trait, it doesn't
  activate [Security] effects on option cards it checks.
