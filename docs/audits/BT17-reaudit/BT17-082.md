# BT17-082 Minami Uehara — re-audit

## Card summary

Blue Tamer, Lv-, play cost 3.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play]` You may play 1 [Labramon]/[Seasarmon] from your hand or from one of
   your Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost.
2. `[Your Turn]` When one of your Digimon is played from digivolution cards, by
   suspending this Tamer, 1 of your blue Digimon gains ＜Rush＞ for the turn.
3. `[Security]` Play this card without paying the cost.

No inherited effect.

## Q&A covered

- **Q2860** — the `[Your Turn]` effect triggers when your Digimon is played by an
  effect that plays cards from digivolution cards (＜Partition＞ in the ruling).
  Covered behaviourally: clause 1 is exactly such an effect, and the stack tests
  ("naturally plays Labramon from a stack…", "grants Rush to a blue Digimon…")
  assert the `[Your Turn]` clause fires off it — Minami suspends and a blue
  Digimon gains ＜Rush＞. The negative counterpart ("does not trigger when a
  matching Digimon is played from hand") pins the "from digivolution cards"
  restriction the ruling relies on.
