## Peer/stack proof (added)

`carries the inherited memory gain through the real Digi-Egg route: hatch -> digivolve -> battle area`
walks the whole Digi-Egg lifecycle with public intents only, under the production turn loop:
`hatchEgg` -> `digivolve` the Yellow Lv.3 BT1-045 onto the egg inside the breeding area (Lv.2
Yellow, cost 0, egg asserted as the stack) -> `moveFromBreeding` on the next own turn (carrier top
card BT1-045, stack exactly the hatched egg instance).

The comparative case runs on the same turn with both permanents on the board: a Tamer placed under
the near-miss peer (BT1-013, no BT17-003 beneath) leaves memory at 1, and the same placement under
the routed carrier takes it to 2, with the carrier's stack asserted as `["BT1-085", "BT17-003"]`.
