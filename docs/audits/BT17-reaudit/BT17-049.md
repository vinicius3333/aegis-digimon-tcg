## Q&A ids covered

- **Q2801** (can the inherited effect delete a level 3 [Beast] Digimon and then
  play that same card from the trash?) — covered by "deletes and then replays
  the same suspended level-3 Beast after attacking": the cost target and the
  played card are the same instance id, and it ends on the battle area under a
  new permanent id.
- **Q2802** (the replayed card's `[On Deletion]` must not activate) — covered by
  the new "does not activate the replayed Beast's [On Deletion] Save, per Q2802".
  The cost Digimon is `BT12-060` ChuuChuumon, a Lv3 [Beast] whose `[On Deletion]`
  is `＜Save＞` (compiled as `PlaceUnder` under a Tamer). The board carries a
  Tamer (`BT1-085`) so a Save would have somewhere to land, and the run uses
  `autoAcceptOptional`, so a firing Save would be accepted. The test asserts the
  card is on the battle area, is not in the Tamer's stack, and is not in the
  trash — i.e. the Save never activated, exactly as Q2802 requires.

  the End of Attack trigger, the deletion cost and the trash play are all real.
