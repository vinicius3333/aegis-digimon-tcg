## Defects fixed / retained red

**IR defect fixed (coordinator correction).** The printed Main clause reads
"play 1 [Marcus Damon]/[Rhythm]" and the Security clause "play 1 card with
[Marcus Damon]/[Rhythm]" — neither carries "in its name", so per the set-wide
rule both filters must be exact-name. `effects[0].actions[0]` and
`effects[3].actions[0]` were `match: "name"` (substring) and are now
`match: "nameExact"`. The `[ShineGreymon] in its name` Delay filter keeps
`match: "name"` (substring), as printed.

The fix is proven discriminating: flipping the two refs back to `"name"` turns
both new near-name negatives red (2 failed | 12 passed), and restoring
`"nameExact"` returns 14/14.

### Correction to the reported collision set

The coordinator named AD1-021 "Marcus Damon & Agumon" and ST24-13 "Marcus Damon
& Thomas H. Norstein" as the substring collisions. Only **AD1-021** is a
collision. **ST24-13 is a legitimate [Marcus Damon]**: it is listed in
`packages/shared/src/cards/effectiveNames.ts`
(`STATIC_NAME_ALIASES_BY_CARD_ID["ST24-13"] = ["Marcus Damon", "Thomas H.
Norstein"]`), and `effectiveStaticNames` feeds the `names` array that
`nameExact` compares against
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts`, ~line 330 and
line 355). So ST24-13 answers to the exact-name gate by the combined-name Tamer
rule and must stay playable. AD1-021 carries no alias entry and is correctly
refused.

The tests reflect this: the two negatives use AD1-021, and a third new test
asserts ST24-13 is still played for free (memory 3 -> 0). No engine change was
made and none is needed here.

No retained red on this card.

Note on method: an earlier draft of the two negatives armed the Delay with
`s.engine.grantKeyword?.(...)`, which does not exist on the engine; the optional
call silently no-opped and the negatives were vacuous. Both were rewritten to
arm the Delay through the real opponent-effect Tamer deletion and now assert
`expect(delay).toBeDefined()` before activating.

## Remaining gaps

- The `[All Turns]` arming clause is only exercised through opponent effects.
  Self-inflicted Tamer deletion (own effect) is not covered; the `sourceFilter`
  has no `byOpponentEffectsOnly`, so the printed text ("when one of your Tamers
  is deleted") is correctly unrestricted, but that is argued from the IR rather
  than proven behaviourally.
- Playing [Rhythm] from the trash (as opposed to hand) is not separately tested;
  the trash route is proven with [Marcus Damon].
