## Q&A covered

- **Q2838** — the `[Trash]` effect triggers while this card sits in the trash and your
  Dorugoramon would be deleted. Proven by `digivolves from trash to prevent a natural
  Dorugoramon deletion`: BT17-073 in trash, BT16-064 Dorugoramon under attack, replacement
  digivolves it out of the trash and cancels the deletion.
- **Q2839** — prevention of a DP-becoming-0 deletion. The prevention path is proven (same
  test); the extra nuance (the DP reduction carries over and re-deletes if DexDorugoramon is
  still at 0 DP after digivolving) is **not** separately tested — see Remaining gaps.

## Defects fixed locally (IR changed: YES)

Three name references were substring (`match:"name"` / `names`) but the printed text brackets
each name with no "in name", so all are exact:

1. `digivolutionRequirement` `names` -> `namesExact` (coordinator rule: `[Digivolve][X]: Cost N`
   with no "in name" is exact). Under substring `names`, a base named "DexDorugoramon" (contains
   "Dorugoramon") would wrongly pass the cost-2 route; the near-name negative test is the
   fails-when-reverted guard.
2. Replacement target `nameOrTrait match:"name"` -> `"nameExact"` — "one of your `[Dorugoramon]`"
   must not also protect DexDorugoramon.
3. `selfHasInDigivolutionCards nameOrTrait match:"name"` -> `"nameExact"` — "`[Dorugoramon]` is in
   this Digimon's digivolution cards".

These are IR changes, so **`BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will be red until the coordinator runs the
set sync**. I did not touch `effects.json`.

## Remaining gaps

- **OPT next-own-turn reset** not behaviourally re-proven. Same-turn refusal is proven through
  two real battles; the reset is the engine-generic per-turn tracker (`tracker.count(key,
  "subtrigger")`) and the structural `frequency:"OncePerTurn"`. Crossing a real turn boundary to
  re-fire was skipped to respect the one-test-run discipline.
- **Q2839 DP-carryover re-deletion** untested. The trash replacement is exercised against a
  battle deletion, not a DP-to-0 deletion; modelling the carried-over DP reduction re-checking
  after the digivolve was out of scope for this pass.

No engine seams were hit; no `it.fails` retained.
