# BT17-094 Ancient Guardian Deity — re-audit

Option, Lv-, Red/Blue, play cost 2, type [Ten Warriors].

## Printed clauses

1. Static: "While you have a Tamer or a Digimon with the [Hybrid] trait, you may
   ignore this card's color requirements."
2. `[Main]` "You may return 1 Digimon card with the [Hybrid] trait or [Ten Warriors]
   trait from your trash to the hand."
3. `[Main]` "Then, you may play 1 Digimon card with the [Ten Warriors] trait or 1
   Tamer card with inherited effects from your hand with the play cost reduced by 4."
4. `[Security]` "You may play 1 Tamer card with an inherited effect from your hand or
   trash without paying the cost. Then, add this card to the hand."

Clause 4 is in the catalog (`securityEffectText`) but was omitted from the lane
assignment. It is audited here.
