# BT17-067 DexDoruGreymon — re-audit (lane 51)

Digimon, Lv5, Purple/Black, DP 8000, play cost 8. Undead / X Antibody / SoC.

## Printed clauses

1. `[Digivolve][DoruGreymon]: Cost 1` — named digivolution route, reduced cost 1.
2. `[Trash] [All Turns]` — when one of your [DoruGreymon] would be deleted, digivolve
   it into this card from the trash without paying the cost, preventing that deletion.
3. `[When Digivolving]` — trash 1 hand card, then ＜Draw 1＞; if [DoruGreymon] is in
   this Digimon's digivolution cards **or** this digivolved from the trash, delete 1
   opponent Digimon with play cost ≤ 6 **instead of the draw** (Q2824).
4. Inherited `[End of Attack] [Once Per Turn]` — you may choose 1 of your Digimon;
   delete that chosen Digimon and 1 opponent Digimon with level ≤ the chosen one's.

## IR change made (report to coordinator)

Route `digivolutionRequirement` changed from `names: ["DoruGreymon"]` (substring) to
`namesExact: ["DoruGreymon"]`, per the coordinator route decision: printed
`[Digivolve][X]: Cost N` with no "in name" is exact. Under the old substring form a
source named **DexDoruGreymon** (BT9-078) satisfied the [DoruGreymon] route — wrong.
A near-name negative now guards the exact form.

Consequence: `effects.json` still holds `{"names":["DoruGreymon"],cost:1,isAlternate:true}`,
so `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will be red until the coordinator re-runs the set sync.
`effects.json` was **not** edited.

## Q&A coverage (Q2821–Q2825)

- **Q2821** (Trash effect triggers while in trash when your DoruGreymon would be
  deleted): covered — "naturally replaces an effect deletion from the trash" and
  "allows declining the trash replacement".
- **Q2822** (prevent a DP-to-0 deletion by digivolving, but the DP-reduction carries
  over and re-deletes if DP is still 0): **not behaviourally tested**. Documented
  untestable through the Board Spec / intent surface: it needs a persistent
  DP-reducing effect whose carry-over re-triggers the rule DP check on the digivolved
  DexDoruGreymon. The prevent side of the replacement (`leaveCause: "any"`) is proven;
  the carry-over re-deletion is a rules-layer nuance beyond the fixture surface.
- **Q2823** (cannot activate [When Digivolving] after such a DP-0 digivolution because
  the Digimon is deleted first): **not tested**, same DP-reduction-carryover
  dependency as Q2822.
- **Q2824** (the "delete instead" replaces "Then, ＜Draw 1＞", not the hand trash):
  covered — structural test (Trash unconditional; Draw gated `not(anyOf)`; Delete gated
  `anyOf`) plus two behavioural cases: delete-instead (trash happened, target deleted,
  no draw) and draw branch (trash + draw happened, opponent survives).
- **Q2825** (choosing your own Digimon that can't be deleted by effects still deletes
  the opponent's): covered — the protected host is spared, the opponent Digimon dies.

## Defects / notes

- Route substring bug fixed by the `namesExact` change above (near-name negative added).
- The once-per-turn behavioural test proves the **same-turn refusal** (a second attack
  in the same turn does not delete a second eligible opponent) via a protected host and
  `drainMicrotasks`. The **next-own-turn reset** is not exercised behaviourally (a full
  turn cycle with combat was avoided under the shared-machine test budget); it rests on
  `frequency: OncePerTurn` (structural) and the engine's per-turn OPT ledger reset.
- The `beDeleted` grant is injected with `advance().verb.restrict(..., EffectDuration.Permanent)`
  (structural plumbing) so the protected-choice endpoint is deterministic; the attack,
  End-of-Attack fire, and deletions are all real.

## Catalog discrepancies

  ("a level") in the catalog string. Reported, not edited (official text wins; the IR
  models the intended `level` comparison via `relativeTo`). The catalog assertion pins
  the exact bytes.
- No U+00A0 present in this card's strings (no "in name" / "trait" phrase printed).
