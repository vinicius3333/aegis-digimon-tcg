# BT17-101 Fenriloogamon: Takemikazuchi — re-audit

## Card summary (catalog `cards.json`)

Purple/Yellow Lv.7 Digimon, DP 16000, play cost 16, digivolve from purple Lv.6 / yellow Lv.6 for 6. Types: Dark Animal, X Antibody, SoC. Attribute Virus.

1. `[Trash] [Your Turn]` When one of your level 6 Digimon with [Pulsemon] in its text is played, 2 of your Digimon may DNA digivolve into this card.
2. `[When Digivolving]` 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets −16000 DP for the turn. If DNA digivolving, you may set your opponent's memory to 3. Then, if this Digimon has a Tamer in its digivolution cards, gain 1 memory and ＜Recovery +1 (Deck)＞.
3. `[When Attacking]` By trashing the top card of your security stack, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.

Catalog matches the official text (the printed "set your the opponent's memory" typo is in the catalog too; harmless).
