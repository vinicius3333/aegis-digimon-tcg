# BT17-025 Cerberusmon: Werewolf Mode — re-audit

## Card summary

Digimon, Lv5, Blue/Purple, play 7, DP 7000, types Wizard / Dark Animal.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve][Cerberusmon]: Cost 1` — alternate route off an exactly-named Cerberusmon base.
2. `[When Digivolving]` — you may play 1 level 3 blue or purple Digimon card from your
   trash or from one of your Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost.
3. Same clause, delayed half — at the next end of your opponent's turn, return it to the hand.
4. `[Rule] Trait: Has the [Dark Animal] type.`
5. Inherited `[All Turns][Once Per Turn]` — when an effect plays one of your Digimon,
   return 1 of your opponent's level 3 Digimon to the hand.

## Q&A covered

- **Q2770** (played Digimon later digivolved → only the top card returns, digivolution
  cards are trashed): covered behaviourally.
- **Q2771** (played Digimon already in the trash → nothing returns): covered behaviourally.

## Remaining gaps

- The inherited `[Once Per Turn]` gate is asserted structurally only. A behavioural
  same-turn refusal needs two effect-driven plays in one turn from a single controller,
  which no fixture in reach produces without a second bespoke card; the reset half was
  therefore not attempted either.
- The `from: ["digivolutionCards"]` source is asserted in the IR but only the `trash`
  source is exercised behaviourally.
- `digivolutionRequirement` uses substring `names: ["Cerberusmon"]`, not `namesExact`.
  The printed `[Cerberusmon]` is an exact name reference, so this over-matches
  "Cerberusmon: Werewolf Mode" itself. Left as-is because the accepted exemplar
  BT17-007 uses the same `names` form for `[Koromon]` — this looks set-wide (and likely
  repo-wide) and belongs to a coordinator decision, not a single lane.
