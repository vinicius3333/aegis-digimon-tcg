## Peer/stack proof (added)

`carries the inherited deletion through the real Digi-Egg route: hatch -> digivolve -> battle area`
walks the whole Digi-Egg lifecycle with public intents only, under the production turn loop:

1. `hatchEgg` in the real Breeding window moves BT17-001 off the egg deck (egg deck empty, breeding occupied).
2. `digivolve` puts the Red Lv.3 BT17-007 onto it inside the breeding area (Lv.2 Red, cost 0); the egg instance is asserted as the whole stack and the battle area is still empty.
3. On the next own turn `moveFromBreeding` carries the stack into the battle area; the carrier's top card is BT17-007 and its stack is exactly the hatched egg instance.
4. Only then does the inherited `[When Attacking]` clause fire from under the real host: memory 2 -> 1 and the opposing 3000 DP peer (BT1-009) is trashed, while the 4000 DP peer (BT1-014) beside it survives — the DP boundary asserted with two peers on the board at once.

The turn loop is closed with a `surrender` intent and awaited, and no decision is left pending.
