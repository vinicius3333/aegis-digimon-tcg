## Q&A covered

- **Q2837** (may delete the cost Digimon and then decline to play [Ornismon]).
  Partially covered. The declined branch is tested behaviourally
  ("Q2837: declining ... deletes nothing"): the engine models the clause as one
  opt-in prompt, so declining costs nothing. The other legal line the ruling
  permits — pay the `by deleting 1` cost and *then* decline the play, in order to
  fire clause 3 — is not reachable: the interpreter asks the optional prompt
  before paying the action cost. Seam:
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/play.ts` (PlayWithoutCost
  optional/cost ordering). No red test is retained because "declined" is itself a
  legal outcome; the gap is the missing second choice, not a wrong result.
