# BT17-038 Sakuyamon re-audit (lane 43)

## Card summary

BT17-038 Sakuyamon (Digimon, Lv6, Yellow, 11000 DP, Shaman/Data/Mega). Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve][Sakuyamon: Maid Mode]: Cost 1` — alternate named digivolution route (no
   "in name").
2. `＜Barrier＞` — static keyword.
3. `[When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn. Then, you
   may use 1 Option card with [Plug-In] in its name ... or 1 yellow Option card with a cost
   of 5 or less from your hand without paying the cost.`
4. `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When you use an Option card with a cost of 2 or more, this
   Digimon can't be returned to hand or deck by your opponent's effects until the end of
   their turn.`

No `[Security]` clause on this card (catalog carries none).

## Catalog discrepancy

`effectText` prints the Plug-In branch as "...with [Plug-In] in its name **in its name** or
1 yellow Option...". The phrase "in its name" is duplicated — a catalog typo versus the
official text (single "in its name"). Reported only; catalog not edited. The IR filter is
correct (single `nameOrTrait` name-match token), so behaviour is unaffected.

## Q&A coverage (Q2788, Q2789, Q2790, Q5457, Q5458)

- **Q5458** (unpaid effect-driven use of a cost-2+ Option still triggers the watcher):
  covered behaviourally — "reduces DP, uses a free cost-2 yellow Option, and locks itself
  against opponent return". The `[When Digivolving]` free-use of BT1-102 (Yellow, cost 2)
  fires the `[Your Turn]` watcher and applies the `beReturned` restriction.
- **Q5457** (payment-only cost reduction keeps original use cost → triggers): covered by the
  paid cost-2 use test (BT1-102 used at its printed cost of 2, watcher fires). The engine
  reads `usedOptionCost` = use cost after card-level changes, before payment-only reductions
  (`conditions.ts` `triggerOptionCostAtLeast`, `primitives.ts` `useOptionFromHand`
  `projectedHandUseCost`), so a payment-only discount does not change the gate.
- **Q2790** (card-level cost reduction to ≤1 → does not trigger) and **Q2788** (the watcher
  activates after the used Option's `[Main]` resolves): covered by engine mechanism, not by a
  dedicated fixture. The fire path resolves the Option's `[Main]`, trashes it, then fires
  `whenOptionUsed` with the card-level use cost; a card-level reduction below 2 would set
  `usedOptionCost` < 2 and skip. Building a card-level-reduction fixture needs an unrelated
  reducer card (e.g. BT2-099/BT8-097) — out of this lane's allowed files; documented rather
  than tested. The cost-1 negative test ("does not lock ... less than 2") exercises the same
  `< 2` gate directly.
- **Q2789** (Option activated by `[Security]`/`＜Delay＞` does not trigger): covered by engine
  design — `fireOptionUsed`/`whenOptionUsed` fire only on a genuine "use" (playCard or the
  use-option-without-cost verb), never on a `[Security]`/`＜Delay＞` activation (BT19-040 design
  note). No dedicated fixture (needs a security/delay Option path from another card).

## Remaining gaps

- **Once-per-turn reset** is not independently observable: the restriction is a binary self
  `beReturned` lock, so a second same-turn cost-2 use and a next-own-turn reset produce no
  distinct visible state. The `OncePerTurn` frequency mechanic is proven on an observable
  debuff by the near-matching sibling BT17-032 (same printed clause shape).
- **`byOpponentEffectsOnly` endpoint** (an actual opponent return effect being prevented while
  an own return still works) is asserted via the `isRestricted(beReturned)` proxy, not by
  driving an opponent return card (would need a non-allowed card in the fixture).
- Q2788/Q2789/Q2790 covered by mechanism, not dedicated fixtures (see Q&A section).
