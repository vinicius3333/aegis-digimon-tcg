## Evidence notes

- The bottom-digivolution-card placement is asserted by ORDER, not just membership:
  `stack` is `[placedCard, digivolveSource]`, so the placed card really is the bottom one.
- The digivolve bonus draw consumes the top card BEFORE the reveal. The fixture therefore
  seeds four deck cards and asserts the drawn one reached hand; an earlier three-card
  fixture made the reveal look broken. Worth recording for other lanes.
- Once per turn is proven through two REAL attacks in the same turn
  (`advance(...).verb.unsuspend` between them is the plumbing the brief allows), and the
  reset through a real opponent turn (`advance(s.engine).runTurn(1)`) followed by a real
  own turn driven with `runOneTurn` + `waitForMainPhase(0)` + a third real attack. Memory
  and both decks are refilled before the own turn so the turn loop does not deck-out or
  auto-pass.
- Peer/stack proof: the On Play test seeds a second black permanent (`otherHost`) and
  asserts nothing was placed under it; the End of Attack test seeds a second Machine host
  (BT17-057 with its own BT17-056 underneath) and asserts that stack is untouched. Both
  are comparative same-fixture cases, and the digivolve test is a real evolution-stack case.
- Fixtures use inert main-deck Digimon (BT1-009/012/013/014), the explicit `security: [...]`
  list form, no Digi-Egg anywhere, and keep a spare card in hand.
