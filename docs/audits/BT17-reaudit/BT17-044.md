# BT17-044 Morphomon — re-audit (lane 13)

## Card summary

Digimon, Lv3, Green, Vaccine, Insectoid. Play cost 3, DP 1000, evolve Green Lv2 for 0.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Your Turn] When this Digimon would digivolve into [Eosmon], reduce the digivolution
   cost by 1.`
   - C1a target: a digivolution whose destination is named exactly Eosmon.
   - C1b: reduce the cost by 1.
2. Inherited: `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn] When another [Eosmon] is played, this Digimon
   may digivolve into [Eosmon] in your hand with the digivolution cost Reduced by 3.`
   - C2a trigger: another (not this) Eosmon is played, on your turn.
   - C2b: optional digivolve of the host into an Eosmon in hand.
   - C2c: cost reduced by 3.
   - C2d: once per turn.

## Clause-1 reduction: verified working, not a defect (follow-up round 3)

Lane 20 (BT17-074) reported that BT17-044's clause 1 -- "reduce the digivolution cost by
1 when digivolving into [Eosmon]" -- appeared not to apply: memory dropped the full 2 in
its BT17-074 play tests.

Investigated with a comparative test that runs the same Morphomon -> BT17-074 digivolve
twice: once from `BT6-047` (a plain Morphomon whose only effect is `[On Deletion]`, the
control) and once from `BT17-044`. The control pays the full 2; BT17-044 pays 1. The
reduction fires exactly as printed.

Verdict: **not a BT17-044 IR defect and not an engine seam.** Clause 1 is `isSelfRef` --
it reduces only when BT17-044 is the Digimon that digivolves (its own top-card, battle-area
main effect). Lane 20's setup did not have BT17-044 as the active digivolving Digimon
(BT17-044 buried as a digivolution card, a plain-Morphomon source, or a digivolve from
breeding where main effects are inactive), so the self-ref reduction correctly did not
apply there. BT17-044.ts is unchanged.

## Defect found and fixed

All three printed `[Eosmon]` references compiled as `match: "name"` (substring). The
printed bracket form is an exact-name reference. Fixed to `match: "nameExact"` in
`apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-044.ts` (the `into` filter of the cost replacement, the
inherited `sourceFilter`, and the inherited `Digivolve.into` filter).

Honest note on impact: today the catalog holds no card whose `nameEn` contains "Eosmon"
as a proper substring (the seven Eosmon are all named exactly "Eosmon"), so this fix is
correctness-preserving rather than behaviour-changing at the current card pool. It is
still the right compilation and it guards against future prints.

would have taken is legal, so only the frequency gate can be stopping it. No injected

## Follow-up round: gaps closed

- `[Your Turn]` and the controller gate: seat 1 takes a real turn and plays its own
  `BT17-074 Eosmon`. Seat 0's Morphomon-carrying host does not digivolve, the
  `BT17-075` stays in hand, and nothing is left pending. This is the only constructible
  opponent-turn Eosmon play; the clause-1 cost reduction has no opponent-turn analogue
  because a player cannot digivolve on the opponent's turn without a Counter/Blast
  vehicle, none of which routes into `[Eosmon]`.
- Declined branch: with `autoDeclineOptional`, playing a second Eosmon leaves the host
  at `BT17-074`, the stack at `["BT17-044"]`, the `BT17-075` in hand, and memory down
  only by the 4 the play itself cost.

## Remaining gaps

- `excludeSelf: true` is not observable at this card pool. The inherited effect only
  exists while BT17-044 is a digivolution card, so its host permanent can never be the
  "played [Eosmon]" that would exercise the exclusion. It rests on the IR assertion.
- `nameExact` likewise cannot be proven behaviourally: no catalog card has "Eosmon" as
  a proper substring of its name, so substring and exact matching are indistinguishable
  today. It rests on the IR assertion.

Both are limits of the card pool, not untested behaviour that a fixture could reach.
