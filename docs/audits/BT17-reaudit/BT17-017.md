## Defects and gaps

- No defects found. Every printed clause now has a behavioural test asserted after
  the flow settles (zones, memory, hand, security, `pendingDecision === undefined`).
- Remaining gap: no test covers a DigiXros where only one of the two named
  materials is on the board (a negative on the recipe). The engine rejects the
  intent for a missing material generically, so this is low value; noted, not added.
- Fixtures use only inert main-deck Digimon (BT1-009, BT1-015) plus the printed
  BT17 cards; no Digi-Egg in security or deck and no numeric `security: <n>` form.
- No injected timing (`advance(...).fire` / `fireSubTrigger` / `fireTiming`) is used.
