## Defect found and fixed

**C3 could place the Option under a token (violates Q2896).** The `underFilter` matched
any permanent named "Diaboromon" under my control, and `TOKEN-Diaboromon` has `nameEn`
"Diaboromon". With no other Diaboromon on the field the engine placed the clock under the
token it had just played. Fix, in the card module only: add `excludeToken: true` to the
`PlaceUnder.underFilter` (the same idiom `placeUnder.ts` documents as "the destination
predicate (mine, Tamer, non-Token)"). The new Q2896 test fails against the pristine module
and passes after the change.

**IR changed: yes.** `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will be red until the coordinator runs the
set sync. `effects.json` was not touched.

## Session 3: exact-name defect and the comparative case

**C3/C5 matched [Diaboromon] as a substring.** Both `nameOrTrait` refs (the `PlaceUnder`
`underFilter` and the `Prevent` `deleteOwn` cost) used `match: "name"`. The printed text has no
"in name" wording, so `[Diaboromon]` is an exact name and BT24-065 "Diaboromon (X Antibody)" — a
different card — was wrongly a legal destination and a legal prevention cost. Fixed to
`match: "nameExact"` in the card module. **IR changed: yes** (in addition to the `excludeToken`
fix above); the set sync is still coordinator-owned.

New comparative test: `picks the real clock-free [Diaboromon] over the token and over a
Diaboromon (X Antibody) peer`. One fixture holds the near-miss BT24-065, a real clock-free
BT17-059, and the token this same clause plays. The clock lands under the real Diaboromon only:
the X Antibody peer's stack stays empty (exact name) and the token's stays empty (Q2896). The
test is a witness for the fix — it fails against `match: "name"` and passes after it.

## Remaining gaps

- Q2898's non-deletion leave routes (return to hand/deck, placed in security, moved to
  breeding, placed under another card) are not covered. Attempted with an opponent bounce
  Option (BT6-098 and BT15-090): `playCard` returned `{ ok: true }` for seat 1 but the
  Option's [Main] body never resolved on a hand-laid board — no decision was raised and no
  Digimon moved, with or without `s.ready()`. That is a harness/opponent-card issue rather
  than a BT17-100 defect, so no red test is retained here; the deletion route plus the
  cost-negative are the behavioural evidence for C5.
- C4's "are placed in your battle area" is exercised with `placedByEffect: true` fixtures
  and by C6's live placement, but there is no test that an Option placed by a *normal* play
  fails to count (a normal play of an Option never leaves it on the field, so the case is
  unreachable through public intents).
