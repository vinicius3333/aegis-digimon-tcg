## Card summary (printed clauses)

1. **[When Digivolving]** By placing 1 [Doomsday Clock] from your hand or trash as
   this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, you may play 2 [Diaboromon] Tokens
   without paying their costs (Lv.6 / White / 3000 DP Digimon tokens).
2. **[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn]** When one of your opponent's Digimon
   attacks, you may switch the attack target to 1 of your Digimon with
   [Diaboromon] in its name.

No inherited effect. Stats: Black, Lv6, play 12, 12000 DP, Unidentified,
evoCost Black Lv5 memory 4.

Name matching: the redirect uses `match:"name"` (substring) — correct for the
"...in its name" form. The cost's `[Doomsday Clock]` also compiles to
`match:"name"`; this follows the accepted house convention for bracketed exact
names (see BT23-070 "Belphemon: Sleep Mode", BT23-073 "Mother Eater"). Only
BT17-100 bears the name "Doomsday Clock", so exact vs substring is unobservable
here. No IR change made.

## Q&A coverage (Q2813, Q2814, Q2815, Q5646)

- **Q2813** (two parts):
  - "may decline the whole effect after placing" is split by the engine into a
    single opt-in. The **decline path** (decline everything -> nothing placed,
    no tokens, digivolution still resolves) is covered green.
  - The other half — **place the clock yet decline the tokens** — is **not
    reachable**. Retained as `it.fails` naming the seam (see Retained red).
- **Q2814** (2 tokens = single play timing, "when played" triggers once): handled
  at the engine level. `primitives.ts` `playToken` plays each token under the
  shared resolving-effect `windowToken`, and its own comment cites Q2814 /
  BT2-053 for the `oncePerTiming` dedupe. Not separately testable for BT17-059:
  the Diaboromon tokens carry no counting trigger, and the cited counters live on
  other cards' inherited text. Documented as engine-covered.
- **Q2815** (may redirect to an **unsuspended** named Diaboromon): covered. The
  redirect test asserts the chosen Diaboromon is unsuspended before the switch and
  that the attack lands on it.
- **Q5646** (Diaboromon played mid-attack via BT17-100 cannot then redirect the
  attack already declared): structurally satisfied and untestable via the harness.
  `SubTrigger whenOpponentAttacks` fires at attack **declaration**; a Digimon that
  enters after declaration is not subscribed for that in-flight attack, so no
  retroactive redirect is possible. No production Intent reaches "play BT17-059
  from BT17-100 during a resolving attack", so no behavioural test is added.

## Retained red (engine seam)

`it.fails` — "can place Doomsday Clock while declining the token play (Q2813)".
- File/function: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts`,
  the optional gate at `ctx.ask.optional(ctx, describeAction(action))` (~line 791)
  ahead of `payCost`.
- Expected (Q2813): player may pay the "by placing" cost and still decline to play
  the tokens.
- Actual: one optional prompt covers **both** cost and effect — accept places the
  clock and plays both tokens; decline places nothing. The clock-placed-without-
  tokens outcome is unreachable. Assertion left red, seam named, no engine edit.
