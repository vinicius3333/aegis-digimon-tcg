# BT17-084 Davis Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji re-audit

Tamer, Lv-, Blue/Green, play cost 5. Re-audited independently; the 2026-09-02
10/10 claim was not treated as evidence.

## Printed clauses (catalog `cards.json`)

`effectText` carries three clauses; `securityEffectText` carries a fourth that
the lane sheet omitted. All four are compiled and behaviourally proven.

1. `[Start of Your Turn]` If you have 2 memory or less, set your memory to 3.
2. `[All Turns]` When one of your level 5+ `[Free]` Digimon would be deleted in
   battle, by suspending this Tamer, you may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon
   card from that Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost.
3. `[End of Your Turn]` 1 of your `[Free]` Digimon may attack an opponent's
   Digimon.
4. `[Security]` Play this card without paying the cost.

| Clause | IR | Test |

| Start (set memory 3) | `StartOfYourTurn` -> `SetMemory{value:3, condition memoryAtMost 2}` | "sets memory to 3 ... only from 2 or less" (2->3 fires; 4 does not) |
| All Turns replacement | `AllTurns` -> `Replacement{event wouldBeDeleted, leaveCause byBattle, sourceFilter L>=5 + Free trait}` -> `CostGatedBlock{suspend self}` -> `PlayWithoutCost{from digivolutionCards, level<=4, hostFilter triggerSubject}` | "naturally suspends itself and plays a card from the battled Digimon's stack" |
| End attack | `EndOfYourTurn` -> `Attack{attacker Free trait, target opponent Digimon, optional}` | "naturally attacks an opponent's Digimon at end of turn" |
| Security | `Security` -> `PlayWithoutCost{isSelf, payCost:false}` | "naturally plays itself from security without paying its cost" |

## Q&A coverage (KB-INDEX: Q2863, Q2864, Q2865, Q2866)

- **Q2863** (can activate with no eligible stack card): "can pay the
  battle-deletion replacement cost even with no eligible stack card" — tamer
  suspends, attacker still trashed, nothing played.
- **Q2864** (played card's inherited `<Retaliation>` cannot delete the battled
  opponent): NEW "does not fire the played card's inherited Retaliation against
  the battled opponent" — BT1-083 (Free L6) over BT2-074 Devimon (`<Retaliation>`
  inherited) is deleted attacking suspended BT5-086; Devimon is played, BT1-083
  trashed, and BT5-086 survives. Engine handles it correctly.
- **Q2865** (suspended Free Digimon cannot attack): "does not attack with a
  suspended or non-Free Digimon".
- **Q2866** (two copies -> one attack): NEW "performs only one attack when two
  copies trigger with two Free Digimon" — 2 Tamers + 2 unsuspended Free Digimon
  vs 2 suspended opponents; exactly one Free suspends and exactly one opponent is
  deleted. Note: the assertion proves the observable ruling outcome (one attack);
  it does not, on its own, distinguish "both triggered, one attacked" from "only
  one triggered". Engine result matches the ruling.

## Trait / filter verification

`[Free]` is an attribute-trait (`cardData.staticTraitsOf` = forms ∪ attributes ∪
types). Confirmed the fixtures are honest, not passing by a disabled filter:
BT8-038 Magnamon and BT1-083 GranKuwagamon carry attribute `Free`; the
negative NON_FREE fixture BT1-032 Frigimon is `Vaccine`. Level and Free-trait
source gates are genuinely enforced.
