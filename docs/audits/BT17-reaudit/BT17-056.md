## Set-wide defect found and fixed

`[Parasitemon]` and `[GroundLocomon]` are printed **exact** bracketed name references but
were compiled as `match: "name"` (substring). Both changed to `match: "nameExact"` in
`apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-056.ts`. `[Machine]` is "the [Machine] trait", so
`match: "trait"` (exact) is already correct and was left alone.

`[GroundLocomon]` `nameExact` is exercised behaviourally (the free digivolve still resolves).
`[Parasitemon]` `nameExact` is only asserted structurally: the behavioural fixture's eligible
card matches through the `orFilters` (black, level <= 5) branch, and no Parasitemon printing
is needed to prove the OR.

## Fixture defects fixed

- The old test seeded `security: ["BT1-001"]` — a Digi-Egg in security, one of the known
  set-wide fixture smells. Replaced with inert main-deck Digimon (`BT1-009` .. `BT1-011`).
- No numeric `security: <n>` form and no injected timing (`fire`, `fireSubTrigger`,
  `fireTiming`) is used anywhere in this file. The target switch is produced naturally by
  BT17-059 Diaboromon's own `[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn]` switch effect during a real
  `attack` intent. `advance(...).verb.unsuspend` is used only as plumbing between two real
  attacks, and `advance(...).runTurn(0)` drives the real turn loop for the reset.

## Behavioural endpoints asserted

- Reveal: exact instance id inside `Permanent.stack` (i.e. beneath the top card), both
  remainder instance ids in trash, deck emptied, host `topCard.cardId`, hand size,
  defender's security length, attacker instance in trash, `pendingDecision === undefined`.
- Once per turn: two switches in one turn (two separate Diaboromon, each with its own
  once-per-turn switch) leave the deck at 3 cards and the second eligible card out of the
  stack; after a real seat-0 turn a third switch reveals again and empties the deck.
- Collision: granted on a `[Machine]` host (BT17-058 GroundLocomon), withheld on an
  `[Unidentified]` host (BT17-059 Diaboromon) carrying the same inherited source.

## Remaining gaps

- The `[Parasitemon]` branch of the OR is not exercised behaviourally (see above).
- "Bottom digivolution card" is asserted as membership in `Permanent.stack`, not as position
  index 0; `to: "placeUnder"` is the IR's bottom placement by definition.
- The reset half of the once-per-turn test re-enters the attack window by restoring the
  captured `state.phase` after `runTurn(0)`, because `applyIntent` refuses with
  `wrong-phase` otherwise. That is hand-laid plumbing around a real turn, not a real
  untap/draw sequence into an attack.
