# BT17-055 Infermon — re-audit (lane 16)

## Card summary

Digimon, Lv5, Black, play cost 7, 7000 DP, Ultimate / Unknown / Unidentified.
Evo cost: Black Lv4 for 3 memory.

Printed clauses:

1. `[When Digivolving] ＜De-Digivolve1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon (Trash the top
   card. You can't trash past level 3 cards).`
2. `Then, 1 of your opponent's Digimon with a play cost 8 or less can't attack players
   until the end of their turn.`
3. Inherited: `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When you play another Digimon with
   [Diaboromon]<NBSP>in its name, ＜De-Digivolve1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon.`

The catalog `inheritedEffectText` carries U+00A0 before "in its name"; the test asserts the
exact bytes.

## Q&A ids covered

- **Q2809** — the "can't attack players" target digivolves into a play-cost-9-or-more
  Digimon on its own turn and still cannot attack players. Covered behaviourally in
  `de-digivolves one opponent and keeps the cost-8 restriction after the target grows past
  cost 8 (KB Q2809)`: the target digivolves into BT4-035 (play cost 12) and
  `observe(...).isRestricted(..., "attackPlayers")` stays true.

## Behavioural endpoints asserted

- De-Digivolve: exact instance id moved to the opponent's trash, resulting `topCard.cardId`,
  `stack` length, host top card, `pendingDecision === undefined`.
- Restriction: `isRestricted` on both the chosen target and its cost-12 peer, plus real
  `attack` intents (`{ok:false, reason:"illegal-target"}` for the player, `{ok:true}` for a
  suspended Digimon) and the defender's security count.
- Level-3 floor: opponent trash empty, top card unchanged, battle area intact, while the
  second clause still resolves on the same Digimon.
- Once per turn: second Diaboromon played in the same turn leaves the target's top card
  instance id and the opponent's trash length unchanged; after a real
  `advance(s.engine).runTurn(1)` a third Diaboromon de-digivolves again down to the level-3
  floor card.

## Remaining gaps

- The De-Digivolve target choice is engine-arbitrary when several opponent Digimon match;
  the peer test avoids the ambiguity by making the second Digimon's play cost stay at 12
  either way, rather than by pinning the choice.
- Restriction expiry at the literal end of the opponent's turn is not asserted directly;
  only its persistence into that turn (Q2809) is. `duration: "untilOpponentTurnEnd"` is a
  real duration ref, so it does not silently expire.
