kinds ["Tamer"].

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play]` `[Start of Your Main Phase]` 1 of your green Digimon gets +2000 DP

## Defects fixed / gaps

- No IR change. `effects.json` untouched; `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` is unaffected.
- No retained reds. The known `canPayCost` "up to N" seam does not apply: this
  card's cost suspends exactly one specific Tamer (itself), not "up to N".
- Added this run: `assertNoLoudGap` on every behavioural test (it was missing from
  all of them), the duration-expiry test, the already-suspended-Willis negative,
  and the memory-delta proof of the `-2` reduction. The prior test asserted only
  that the digivolution happened, which does not distinguish a reduced cost from a
  full one.
- Remaining gap: the `[Your Turn]` restriction has no negative case. Playing one of
  your own Digimon during the opponent's turn needs an opponent effect that plays a
  card from your hand, which no fixture in reach provides; the restriction is
  carried by the `YourTurn` trigger and is asserted structurally only.
- Fixtures use no Digi-Eggs in security or deck and no numeric `security: <n>` form.
- Catalog discrepancy (report only, not edited): `effectText` contains the doubled
  word "1 of your **your** Digimon with [Terriermon]/[Lopmon] in its name". The
  official list reads "1 of your Digimon". It is a typo in
  `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` with no behavioural consequence — no
  IR reads that span — but the coordinator may want it corrected at the catalog level.
