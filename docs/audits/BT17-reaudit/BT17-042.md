# BT17-042 Argomon — re-audit (lane 12)

## Card summary

Digimon, Lv3, Green/Purple, DP 2000, play cost 3. Catalog `evoCosts`:
Green Lv2 for 1, Purple Lv2 for 1.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve]Lv.2 [Argomon]: Cost 0` — alternate route from a Lv2 card named
   exactly `Argomon`.
2. `[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 [Argomon] and 1
   [Rhythm] among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.`
3. Inherited: `[On Deletion] Gain 1 memory.`

## Q&A ids covered

- **Q2795** — "if only one of [Argomon]/[Rhythm] is revealed, can I add just
  that card?" Yes. Covered by "adds the only applicable card when just one
  bracket is revealed (Q2795)": a board where the three revealed cards contain
  an Argomon and no Rhythm; the Argomon lands in hand and the other two go to
  the bottom in offered order.
- **Q2796** — "if both are revealed, may I add only one?" No; you must add as
  many as possible. Covered by "adds the revealed Argomon and Rhythm and
  bottoms the remainder (Q2796)": the run sets no `autoAcceptOptional`, so a
  mandatory add is the only way both cards can move, and the test asserts both
  in hand, hand size 3, deck size 2 and `pendingDecision` undefined.

## Defect fixed

Three printed `[Name]` brackets compiled as substring gates:
`[Argomon]` and `[Rhythm]` in the On Play add as `match: "name"` (now
`match: "nameExact"`), and `[Argomon]` in the digivolution requirement as
`names` (now `namesExact`). This is the known set-wide `[Name]` defect.

Honest note on impact: the current catalog holds no card whose `nameEn`
strictly contains "Argomon" or "Rhythm" as a substring, so the two forms are
behaviourally indistinguishable today and no test can separate them. The fix
is correctness-of-intent, not an observed misbehaviour. **IR changed —
`BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will be red until the coordinator runs the set
sync.**
