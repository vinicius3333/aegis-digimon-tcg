# BT17-037 RizeGreymon — re-audit

Digimon, Lv5, Yellow, Vaccine, Cyborg, 7000 DP, play 7, digivolve Yellow Lv4 cost 3.

## Printed clauses

1. `[Your Turn]` While you have a suspended Tamer, this Digimon gets +3000 DP and gains ＜Piercing＞.
2. `[When Digivolving]` `[When Attacking]` By suspending 1 of your yellow Tamers, 1 of your
   opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP for the turn.
3. Inherited `[All Turns]` `[Once Per Turn]` When one of your yellow or red Tamers is deleted,
   place 1 `[Marcus Damon]` from your trash on top of your security stack.

Catalog text matches the official list. No discrepancy.

## Q&A covered

  Test: "places the deleted Marcus Damon itself on top of security" — the Tamer permanent is

## Behavioural endpoints asserted

- Aura: `currentDP` 10000 with the Tamer suspended, 7000 unsuspended, 10000 re-suspended,
  7000 on the opponent's turn; `observe(...).hasPierce` toggles with it; the opposing Lv5
  keeps 6000 DP and no Pierce (peer case, self-only aura).
- When Attacking: only the yellow Tamer suspends (a red-only Tamer, BT1-085, is left
  unsuspended — comparative cost-filter case); the attacked Digimon drops 20000 → 17000.
- Inherited: security length 1 → 2 with the exact `[Marcus Damon]` on top, a second Tamer
  deletion in the same turn leaves it at 2, and after `advance(...).runTurn(1)` a third
  deletion places the next Marcus Damon (BT13-095), taking security to 3. AD1-021
  "Marcus Damon & Agumon" stays in the trash throughout.

## Defects fixed locally

1. **Set-wide name defect.** `BT17-037.ts` compiled `[Marcus Damon]` as
   `match: "name"` (substring), which would also match AD1-021 "Marcus Damon & Agumon" and
   ST24-13 "Marcus Damon & Thomas H. Norstein". Changed to `match: "nameExact"`. **IR changed —
   `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` needs the coordinator's set sync.**
2. **Digi-Egg in security.** Two fixtures seeded `security: ["BT1-001"]` (a Digi-Egg).
   Replaced with the inert main-deck `BT1-009`.

## Gaps

- Piercing is proven as an active grant (`observe(...).hasPierce`), not through excess
  security damage in a real battle; the grant is the engine seam the keyword reads.
- The inherited once-per-turn reset uses hand-laid `turnSeat` around a real
  `advance(...).runTurn(1)`; `runTurn` does not itself hand the turn over.
