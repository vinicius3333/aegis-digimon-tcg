# BT17-039 ShineGreymon — re-audit

Digimon, Lv6, Yellow, Vaccine, Light Dragon, 11000 DP, play 11, digivolve Yellow Lv5 cost 3.
No inherited effect.

## Printed clauses

1. `[When Digivolving]` You may play 1 `[Marcus Damon]` from your hand without paying the cost.
2. `[All Turns]` `[Once Per Turn]` When this Digimon would leave the battle area by an
   opponent's effect, by returning 1 of your yellow Tamers to the hand, prevent it from leaving.

Catalog text matches the official list. No discrepancy.

## Q&A covered

- **Q2791** — "would leave the battle area" covers trashing, return to hand/deck, placement in
  security, move to breeding and placement under another card.
  Covered for two of those modes: deletion to trash ("returns a yellow Tamer to prevent an
  opponent-effect deletion", "prevents only once per turn and resets on the next turn") and a
  natural return-to-hand from an opponent Option ("returns a yellow Tamer to prevent a natural
  opponent Option return", BT3-104). The remaining modes (security, breeding, placed under
  another card) share the same `wouldLeavePlay` replacement seam and no BT17-legal fixture adds
  behavioural information beyond the two proven paths; listed as a gap rather than claimed.

## Behavioural endpoints asserted

- Clause 1: memory 3 → 0 (the digivolve cost only, so the Tamer was free), the Marcus Damon
  instance is in the battle area, and AD1-021 "Marcus Damon & Agumon" stays in hand
  (battle area holds exactly 2 permanents — the exact-name comparative case).
- Clause 2: the ShineGreymon permanent id is still in the battle area after an opponent-effect
  deletion and after a natural BT3-104 return; the paid yellow Tamer's instance id is in hand.
- Once per turn: the second opponent-effect deletion in the same turn removes ShineGreymon
  (BT17-039 in trash) and the second Tamer stays on board — the cost is not paid twice.
- Reset: after `advance(...).runTurn(1)` a further opponent-effect deletion is prevented again
  and the second Tamer is now in hand (no yellow Tamer left on board).

   "Marcus Damon & Thomas H. Norstein". Changed to `match: "nameExact"`. **IR changed —
   `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` needs the coordinator's set sync.**

## Gaps

- Q2791's security / breeding / placed-under leave modes are not separately driven (see above).
- Two tests use `advance(...).verb.deletePermanent(..., "byEffect")` while the opponent holds
  the turn rather than a card that deletes; the natural Option case covers the same seam.
- The once-per-turn reset uses hand-laid `turnSeat` around a real `advance(...).runTurn(1)`;
  `runTurn` does not itself hand the turn over.
