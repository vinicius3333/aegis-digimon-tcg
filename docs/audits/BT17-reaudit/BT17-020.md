# BT17-020 Strabimon — re-audit

## Card

- Digimon, Lv3, Blue, play cost 3, DP 1000, [Beastkin], Hybrid/Variable.
- evoCosts: Blue Lv2 cost 0, Yellow Lv2 cost 0.
- Printed clauses:
  1. `[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 card with the [Hybrid]/[Ten Warriors] trait and 1 Tamer card with an inherited effect among them to the hand. Return the rest to the bottom of the deck.`
  2. Inherited: `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may play 1 Tamer card with an inherited effect from your hand with the play cost reduced by 2.`

The catalog text uses a non-breaking space after `[Ten Warriors]`; the test asserts the exact bytes.

## Q&A covered

| Id | Ruling | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q2751 | If only one of the two card types is revealed, you may still add that one | "adds the single applicable card when only one is revealed (Q2751)" |
| Q2752 | If both types are revealed you must add as many as possible — you cannot add only one | "adds a Hybrid and an eligible Tamer from the top three" and "returns the unpicked revealed cards to the bottom of the deck (Q2752)" |
| Q2753 | "Tamer card with inherited effects" means "Inherited Effect" in the lower text; a Tamer whose lower text is only a [Security] effect does not qualify | "does not treat a Tamer whose lower text is only [Security] as an inherited-effect target" (BT20-089) and the existing BT1-085 case (no lower text at all) |

Q2752's "must add as many as possible" is verified positively (both eligible cards land in hand and only the third goes to the deck bottom). The engine's automated selection has no public intent for declining a mandatory add, so the "you cannot add only one" refusal is not directly observable; the positive form is the strongest available evidence.
