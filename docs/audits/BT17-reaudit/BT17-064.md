## Retained red

`it.fails("Q2816: ...")`. Seam: `attackTargetMatchesFilter` in
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:58`. It resolves the declared defender via
`ctx.game.permanentById` and re-runs `candidatePermanents` against LIVE state at the moment the
sub-trigger fires. The trigger payload carries no declaration-time snapshot of the defender's
digivolution cards.

- Setup: BT17-064 (DP 5000) over BT16-016 [Patamon]; defender BT17-025 (DP 9000) with one
  digivolution card. BT16-016's inherited `[When Attacking]` trashes the defender's only source in
  the same window.
- Expected (Q2816): the clause does not trigger; the defender keeps its slot and BT17-064 loses the
  battle.
- Actual: the defender is deleted before damage (`BT17-025` in trash, BT17-064 alive).

Not fixable from card IR: a `condition` on the `SubTrigger` action itself is evaluated while the
watcher is installed, when the payload has no defender at all. Engine change required; not made,
per the brief.

## Engine lane E2 resolution (session 3) — retained red CLOSED

The seam was real and is fixed. `combat/controller.ts` now captures
`defenderAtDeclaration` (the defender's digivolution-card count at attack declaration, the
sibling of the existing `attackerDPAtDeclaration`), and `attackTargetMatchesFilter` answers
the digivolution-stack half of its filter from that snapshot instead of from live state, so
a same-window inherited trash cannot retroactively arm the delete (Q2816).
