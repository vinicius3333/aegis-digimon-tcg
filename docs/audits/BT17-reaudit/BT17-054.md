# BT17-054 Trailmon — re-audit (lane 15)

## Card summary

Digimon, Lv4, Black, Data, [Machine], 4000 DP, play cost 5, digivolve from
Black Lv3 for 2.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Add 1 Tamer card or 1 Digimon
   card with the [Machine] trait among them to the hand. trash the rest.`
   Sub-clauses: (a) reveal exactly 3 from the top of your deck, (b) add 1 Tamer,
   (c) or 1 Digimon with the exact [Machine] trait, (d) at most 1 added,
   (e) the rest go to trash.
2. Inherited: `[Your Turn] While this Digimon has the [Machine] trait, it gains
   ＜Collision＞ (During this Digimon's attack, all of your opponent's Digimon
   gain ＜Blocker＞, and must block if possible).`
   Sub-clauses: (a) your-turn only, (b) [Machine] condition, (c) grants
   ＜Collision＞ to the host, (d) opponent's Digimon gain ＜Blocker＞, (e) the
   block is mandatory.

## Defects fixed locally

- Fixture smell: the Collision test used the numeric `security: 1` form the brief
  bans. Replaced with an explicit inert main-deck card,
  `security: [{ card: "BT1-009" }]`.

No IR change. `BT17-054.ts` is correct as compiled: both `[Machine]` references
are exact-trait (`match: "trait"`), which is right for "with the [Machine] trait"
and "has the [Machine] trait". The set-wide `nameExact` defect does not apply —
this card has no printed `[Name]` reference.

## Test gaps closed

- The Tamer half of the "Tamer or Machine Digimon" disjunction was never
  exercised; only the `orFilters` branch was. Added a case that reveals BT1-085
  (Tai Kamiya) plus two inert Digimon and asserts hand `== [tamer]`, trash in
  order, deck empty, no pending decision.
- Added the no-match case: three inert non-Machine Digimon, nothing added, all
  three trashed.
- The Collision test proved eligibility but not the "must block if possible" half.
  It now asserts `mustBlock: true` and that `declineBlock` is rejected while an
  eligible blocker exists.

## Remaining gaps

- No case proves the reveal is exactly the top 3 in deck order against a deeper
  deck; fixtures use 3-card decks, so "top 3" and "whole deck" are
  indistinguishable here.
- No case where two matching cards are revealed and the controller picks one
  (`autoSelectCards` takes the first match).
- ＜Collision＞ is asserted only on your turn; there is no opponent's-turn
  negative proving the `YourTurn` gate.

## Catalog discrepancies

- `effectText` reads `... among them to the hand. trash the rest.` — lowercase
  "trash" after a sentence boundary. This matches the official English text as
  printed (a known Bandai typo), so it is recorded, not edited.
