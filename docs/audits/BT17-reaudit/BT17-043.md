## Defect found and fixed

The printed reference `[Terriermon]/[Lopmon]` is an exact-name reference, but the IR
compiled it as `match: "name"` (substring). That is behaviourally wrong, not merely
cosmetic: the catalog contains `Terriermon (X Antibody)` (BT16-038) and
`Terriermon Assistant` (BT5-046, EX4-033), all of which a substring match accepts.

Fixed in `apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-043.ts` to `match: "nameExact"`.

Proof the change is behavioural, not cosmetic: with `match: "name"` restored, the
new test "does not trigger on a played [Terriermon Assistant]" fails (the opponent's
Digimon suspends); with `nameExact` it passes. Verified by a temporary local revert.

`EX4-033 Terriermon Assistant` self-treats its name as `[Terriermon]`, so the negative
test deliberately uses `BT5-046`, which does not.

## Follow-up round: gaps closed

All three gaps named in the first round are now covered.

- `[Your Turn]` negative: seat 1 takes a real turn and attacks seat 0's suspended
  `BT17-046 Gargomon`. Its `[On Deletion]` plays a `[Terriermon]` out of seat 0's trash —
  seat 0's own effect-play, exactly the qualifying event, but on the opponent's turn.
  The Terriermon reaches the battle area and seat 1's bystander stays unsuspended (the
  only suspended seat-1 permanent is the attacker, suspended by attacking).
- `byEffect` origin: a `[Terriermon]` played from hand with the ordinary `playCard`
  intent produces no suspend and no decision, while the identical board played by
  BT17-049's effect does.
- Declined branch: the play prompt is accepted and only the suspend prompt is declined,
  by answering `optional` decisions by hand (`declineOnlyTheSuspend`). `autoDeclineOptional`
  cannot express this — it would also decline the play the suspend depends on. The
  Terriermon is on the board, no opponent Digimon is suspended, nothing is left pending.
