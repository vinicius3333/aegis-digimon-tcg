Name-reference semantics (`matchNameOrTrait`,
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:297`):
`match: "name"` is substring, `match: "nameExact"` is exact. Clause 2
("with [Greymon]/[Garurumon] in its name") and clause 3 ("with [Tai Kamiya]/
[Matt Ishida] in its name") are substring references and correctly use `"name"`.

## Defect fixed locally

Clause 1's `[Agumon]/[Gabumon]` is a bracketed exact-name reference but the IR
used `match: "name"` (substring). That let the free play take ToyAgumon,
SnowAgumon, BushiAgumon, ClearAgumon, Agumon Expert, Fake Agumon Expert,
Agumon - Bond of Bravery, Agumon (X Antibody), Marcus Damon & Agumon and the
Gabumon equivalents — 30 catalog cards that the printed text does not name.
Changed to `match: "nameExact"`.

Regression proof: with the token reverted to `"name"`, the new test
`refuses a free play when only an inexact Agumon name sits in the trash` fails
(ToyAgumon BT2-055 is played for free) along with the structural test; with
`"nameExact"` both pass.

## Remaining gaps

- The Delay's own activation gate (a ＜Delay＞ permanent cannot be used on the
  turn it arrives) is exercised only indirectly: the intrinsic-Delay tests
  advance `turnCount`/`turnSeat` by hand rather than running a full turn loop,
  so a same-turn refusal for this card is not asserted. The card has no "once
  per turn" clause, so no once-per-turn reset test is required.
- Clause 1 is not tested from hand (only from trash); both zones are in the same
  `from` list and the hand branch is covered by the shared `PlayWithoutCost`
  handler, so this is a coverage thinness, not a suspected defect.
- Clause 3's substring semantics ("in its name") have no dedicated negative.
- No retained red tests; no engine seam blocked this card.
