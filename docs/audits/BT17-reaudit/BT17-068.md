## Printed clauses

1. Static: "While this card is revealed from your deck, this card is also treated
   as level 6."
2. "When this card would be played from the hand, by returning 1 [Apocalymon]
   from your trash to the bottom of the deck, reduce the play cost by 3."

   Digimon with the [Dark Masters] trait from your hand or trash without paying
   the cost."

   with [Dark Masters] in its text from your trash as this Digimon's bottom
   digivolution card, this Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn."

matches the official list exactly (verified against KB). No discrepancy.

  unit (`level 6`, `treatedAsLevels [5,6]`).

  BT3-051 [On Play] reveal test — both copies land in hand.

  gate and the real non-effect negative together enforce the ruling.
- Q2829 (inherited targets a level 5-or-lower card with [Dark Masters] in its
  text): covered by the inherited-attack test with BT15-072 (`match: text`).
