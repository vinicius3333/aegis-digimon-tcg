## Defects found and fixed locally

1. The previous suite proved the placement only with `stack.some(...)`, which cannot
   distinguish bottom from top. It now asserts the whole stack in order
   (`[material]` on play, `[material, base]` when digivolving).
2. Three clause parts had no behavioural coverage at all: the "1 of your Digimon"
   target choice, the level-3-blue cost filter (no negative), and the "for the turn"
   duration. All three now have tests.
3. The decline branch and the illegal digivolution source were untested. Added.
4. The old IR assertions were `toMatchObject` fragments; they are now a single
   `toEqual` over `compiled.effects` plus a catalog contract assertion, so a silent
   IR drift (a changed filter, a wrong `position`) fails the suite.

## Remaining gaps

- The "for the turn" expiry test rides the turn boundary the digivolution cost itself
  causes (memory reaches 0), rather than an explicit `endPhase`. That is the real turn
  loop and a real turn end, so the duration is genuinely proven, but the test cannot
  also assert an in-turn End-of-Main state.
- No test covers a second Seasarmon placing under the first mid-chain; the printed
  text does not distinguish that case and no ruling calls for it.
