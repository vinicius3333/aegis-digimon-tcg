## Card summary (catalog `cards.json`)

Blue/Green Option, play cost 2.

1. `[Main]` 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a level 5 or higher Digimon card with the [Free] trait in your hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 4. Then, place this card in the battle area.
2. `[All Turns]` When one of your Digimon with the [Free] trait would be deleted other than by one of your effects, ＜Delay＞ ・By digivolving that Digimon into a Digimon card with [Imperialdramon] in its name in your hand without paying the cost, prevent that deletion.
3. `[Security]` You may play 1 Tamer card with [Davis Motomiya]/[Ken Ichijoji] in its name from your hand or trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area.

Catalog matches the official text. [Free] is the card's *attribute* field in `cards.json`; the engine's `match: "trait"` filter covers attributes, which the behavioural tests confirm (BT3-014 / BT3-017 / BT12-028 / BT12-030 are all `attributes: ["Free"]`).

## Retained red (session 3)

`it.fails("Q2889: Partition from a BT16-025 in the digivolution cards still plays its cards")`.

- Seam: **inherited ＜Partition＞ specifier is read from the top card only.**
- File/function: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts`, the `partitionCandidates` block
  (~line 3715) inside the deletion primitive.
- Mechanism: the gate is `continuous.hasKeyword(permanentId, "Partition")` (which a BT16-025 in
  the digivolution cards does grant) followed by `partitionSpecOf(perm.topCard.cardId)`, which
  parses the specifier out of the **top card's** printed text. With BT12-030 on top the specifier
  is `undefined` and the reaction is skipped.
- Expected (Q2889): the specified blue Lv.4 and green Lv.4 cards are played from the digivolution
  cards. Actual: BT12-030 is trashed with its whole stack and nothing is played.
- Not a BT17-097 defect; the module is unchanged.
