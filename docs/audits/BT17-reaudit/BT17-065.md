## Q&A coverage

- **Q2817** (when the [Trash] effect triggers): covered behaviourally — the
  trash-digivolve replacement fires while DexDorugamon sits in trash and a
  Dorugamon would be deleted ("digivolves from trash..." test).
- **Q2820** (delete replaces the Draw, not the hand trash): covered by IR shape
  (Trash unconditional; Draw `not(anyOf)`; Delete `anyOf`) and behaviourally —
  the [Dorugamon]-route case deletes and still trashes a hand card; the normal-route
  case draws and still trashes a hand card.
- **Q2818 / Q2819** (DP-reduced-to-0 deletion prevented by the digivolve, but the
  DP debuff carries over so deletion recurs, and [When Digivolving] then does not
  activate): **documented untestable**. The testkit deletes by `RemovalCause`
  (`verb.deletePermanent(..., "byEffect")`), not through a lingering DP-to-0
  modifier that re-evaluates the deletion after the replacement digivolves the
  base. Reproducing the recurrence and the [When Digivolving] suppression needs a
  persistent DP-reduction debuff the harness does not expose, so no honest
  behavioural test exists. Not a single named engine seam; left as a gap.
