## IR change (report to coordinator)

Both printed routes are the `[Digivolve][X]: Cost N` form (no "in name"), so per the route-name
coordinator decision they must gate on exact name. Changed `names` -> `namesExact` for both entries
in `BT17-022.ts`.

- Impact: `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` still stores `names` for BT17-022, so
  `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` goes red until the coordinator runs the set sync. effects.json was
  not touched (out of scope).
- No near-name substring victim exists in the catalog for either token, so the change is defensive,
  not behaviour-changing on current data: every name containing `KendoGarurumon`/`Koji Minamoto`
  either equals it or (BT18-088 "Takuya Kanbara & Koji Minamoto") is treated-as `[Koji Minamoto]`
  by its own `[Rule]`, a genuine match — not a false positive. The near-name negative the decision
  asks for is therefore vacuous here; documented rather than faked.

## Q&A coverage (12)

Covered behaviourally:
- **Q2754** (Tamer treated as a Digimon base): the yellow-Tamer digivolve succeeds and the Tamer
  becomes a digivolution card. The "triggers when-a-Digimon-digivolves effects" nuance is engine
  trigger-classification, not separately witnessed here.
- **Q2755** (`[Koji Minamoto]` route + as-a-Digimon): exact route, cost 2.
- **Q2756** (bonus draw on Tamer digivolve): seeded deck card moves to hand on the digivolve.
- **Q2758** (Tamer becomes a digivolution card, trashed on leave): pre-delete stack holds the base;
  trashed-on-leave is shown indirectly — AncientGarurumon's `[On Deletion]` pulls a Tamer/[Hybrid]
  back "from your trash", which is only possible because the stack was trashed first.
- **Q2759** (delayed delete at end of turn): AncientGarurumon is deleted at the owner's end of turn.
  The carry-over through a *further* digivolve/de-digivolve is not separately exercised.

Not covered — documented reason (engine-generic or cross-card, outside a focused single-card test):
- **Q2757**: summoning sickness of a Digimon that digivolved from a Tamer played the same turn —
  generic attack-legality rule; needs the Tamer played mid-turn.
- **Q2760**: `<De-Digivolve>` back to a Tamer cancels the delete — needs a De-Digivolve source.
- **Q2761**: BT3-109 `[Back for Revenge!]` replay interaction — cross-card.
- **Q2762**: slide performed during the end-of-turn window skips the already-passed delete timing —
  timing-window edge; relates to the delayed-delete scheduling seam.
- **Q4659**: cannot decline the 1st effect once the digivolution is declared — engine declaration
  semantics, no separate observable endpoint.
- **Q6564 / Q6565**: a Digimon does not gain a digivolution-card Tamer's security effects but does
  gain its inherited effects — needs a Tamer whose lower text carries each; the inherited-draw test
  uses a Digimon inheritance source, not a Tamer, so it is analogous but not a direct witness.
