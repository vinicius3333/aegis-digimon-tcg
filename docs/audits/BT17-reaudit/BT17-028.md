# BT17-028 AncientGarurumon — re-audit (lane 39)

## Card summary (printed clauses)

- **C1 [On Play] [When Digivolving]** Return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with the lowest level to the hand.
- **C2 [Your Turn] [Once Per Turn]** When an effect adds cards to your or your opponent's hand, your opponent adds the top card of their security stack to the hand.
- **C3 [On Deletion]** You may return 1 Tamer card and 1 [Hybrid] trait Digimon card from your trash to the hand. Then, you may play 1 Tamer card from your hand without paying the cost.
- **C4 [DigiXros -3]** [Lobomon] x [KendoGarurumon].

Catalog `dp` 12000, `playCost` 12, `evoCosts` Blue Lv5 (4) / Yellow Lv5 (4). No inherited effect.

## Defect found and fixed

`C4 [DigiXros -3]` was **not implemented**: the module carried `coverage: "full", residual: []`
but no `digiXrosRequirement`, so `digiXrosRequirementFor("BT17-028")`
(`packages/shared/src/effects/data.ts`) returned `undefined` and the card could never be
DigiXros-played. Fixed by adding the recipe, mirroring the same-set sibling BT17-017
(`[DigiXros -3] [Agunimon] x [BurningGreymon]`):

```ts
digiXrosRequirement: [
  { materials: [{ names: ["Lobomon"] }, { names: ["KendoGarurumon"] }], count: 3 },
],
```

`count` is the per-material cost reduction (`DigiXrosRequirement` doc,
`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/requirements/xrosLink.ts`), so `-3` -> `count: 3`. This makes
`coverage: "full"` honest. **IR changed: yes** — `BT17-catalog-sync.test.ts` will be red until the
coordinator runs the set sync (effects.json currently has no `digiXrosRequirement` for this card).
I did not touch effects.json.

## Retained red (engine seam)

`it.fails` "moves the opponent's top security to hand when an effect adds to your own hand".

- **Seam:** the [Your Turn] effect wires two SubTrigger buses under one shared `oncePerTurnKey`
  ("BT17-028/hand-add"): `whenEffectAddsToOpponentHand` (works) and `whenEffectAddsToHand` (dead).
  BT17-028 is the only card in the catalog wiring both directions under one key.
- **File / function:** `apps/api/src/engine/effects/subtriggers.ts` (SubTriggerRegistry
  subscribe/fire); fire seam at `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:680`
  (`fx.draw` -> `fireSubTrigger("whenEffectAddsToHand")`).
- **Repro:** with BT17-028 in play on your turn (freshly played or established), an effect Draw
  into your own hand (BT1-029 `kind:"Draw"`) fires `whenEffectAddsToHand`; the same seam fires
  BT17-083's single-bus `whenEffectAddsToHand` correctly.
- **Expected vs actual:** opponent security should drop by 1; actual: unchanged (predicate never
  holds). The opponent-hand half of the same clause fires correctly, so only the "your hand"
  direction is lost. Fix belongs in the engine (out of lane scope); assertion not weakened.
