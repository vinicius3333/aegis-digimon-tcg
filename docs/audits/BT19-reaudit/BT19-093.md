## Card

Yellow Option, play cost 3, `[Device]` trait, C, max 4 in deck.

Printed clauses (catalog `effectText` / `securityEffectText`):

1. **Colour waiver** — "While you don't have [Queen Device], you may ignore this card's color
   requirements."
2. **On-trash** — "When an effect trashes this card in your battle area, until the end of your
   opponent's turn, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP and that Digimon's
   [When Digivolving] effects don't activate."
3. **[Main]** — "1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -3000 DP and that Digimon's [When Digivolving]
   effects don't activate until the end of your opponent's turn. Then, place this card in the
   battle area."
4. **[Security]** — "2 of your opponent's Digimon gain ＜Security A. -2＞ for the turn. Then, add
   this card to the hand."

No ＜Delay＞ is printed on this card.

## Defects fixed locally

1. `effects[0].condition.filter.nameOrTrait[0].match` was `"name"` (substring) for the bracketed
   exact reference `[Queen Device]`. Changed to `"nameExact"`. No catalog card has "Queen Device"
   as a proper substring of a longer name, so no test discriminates the two forms directly; the
   `[King Device]` near-miss test proves the gate is name-keyed rather than `[Device]`-trait-keyed.
2. The pre-existing test file was one behavioural test plus one IR snapshot. It is replaced with
   12 tests: catalog, IR, four colour-requirement cases, three [Main] cases (including the two
   real opponent-turn digivolve lock proofs), two on-trash cases, and the [Security] case.
