## Clause -> test -> IR

IR: `effects[0]` (`OnPlay`) and `effects[1]` (`OnDeletion`), structurally
identical: one `Draw` action, `controller: "mine"`, `amount: 1`,
`optional: true`, with

```
cost: {
  kind: "place",
  target: { filter: { zone: "hand", controller: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"],
                      nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Blue Flare"], match: "trait" }] },
            count: 1, from: ["hand"] },
  underFilter: { controller: "mine", kind: ["Tamer"] },
}
```

`match: "trait"` is the exact-trait mode the printed `[Blue Flare] trait`
wording requires (not the `traitContains` substring mode); the IR test asserts
it for both timings.

- `pays the cost and draws when played from hand for its play cost` —
  public `playCard` intent. Endpoints: the Tamer's stack holds exactly the
  BT19-020 instance, hand holds exactly the drawn card, deck empty, Gaossmon
  on the board, memory `10 -> 7` (play cost 3), no pending decision.
- `pays the cost and draws when deleted losing a public battle` — the
  `[On Deletion]` half is reached with no injected timing: Gaossmon (1000 DP)
  attacks a suspended 3000 DP body through the public `attack` intent and
  loses. Endpoints: Gaossmon in the trash, only the Tamer left on the board,
  Tamer stack = the placed BT19-020 instance, hand = the drawn card, deck
  empty.
- `may decline the placement cost, drawing nothing and moving no card` —
  `autoDeclineOptional`: Tamer stack empty, hand and deck untouched.
- `cannot pay with a hand Digimon that lacks the [Blue Flare] trait` — the
  near-miss peer BT1-027 Armadillomon (Blue Lv.3 Digimon, Mollusk, no Blue
  Flare) is the only hand card; with `autoAcceptOptional` on, nothing is
  placed and nothing is drawn.
- `cannot pay without a Tamer of your own, even when the opponent has one` —
  proves the `controller: "mine"` leg of `underFilter`: the opponent's
  BT19-081 Kiriha Aonuma is not a legal host, so the cost is unpayable.
- `places under a Tamer and never under a Digimon, whichever Tamer is
  chosen` — a Tamer (ST1-12) and a Digimon (BT1-009) are both on the board;
  the card goes under the Tamer and the Digimon's stack stays empty, proving
  the `kind: ["Tamer"]` leg.
- `does nothing from inside a digivolution stack when the host is deleted` —
  the realistic evolution-stack case. Gaossmon sits under an inert Blue Lv.4
  host (BT1-037 Gorillamon); the host loses a public battle and the whole
  stack is trashed. Gaossmon prints no inherited effect, so its
  `[On Deletion]` must stay silent: the Tamer stack stays empty and hand and
  deck are untouched, with both cards confirmed in the trash.

## Defects fixed locally

1. The previous `[On Deletion]` proof was
   `advance.verb.deletePermanent([gaossmon])` driven by a parametrised
   `it.each` over the two timings, with the `[On Play]` half reached by
   `advance.fireForPermanent` — injected timing. Replaced by a real lost
   battle through the public `attack` intent.
2. No near-miss for the `[Blue Flare]` trait existed: the old suite put a
   non-matching card in hand alongside a matching one, so the matching one
   was always picked and a filter that ignored the trait entirely would have
   passed. Now the near-miss is the ONLY hand card.
3. The `underFilter` legs (`controller: "mine"`, `kind: ["Tamer"]`) were
   never tested. Added the opponent-Tamer and Digimon-decoy cases.
4. The old assertions used `expect.arrayContaining` on the hand, which cannot
   see an extra or missing card. Replaced by exact instance-id lists plus
   deck-length endpoints.
5. Catalog fields were never asserted. Added, which surfaced the discrepancy
   below.

No IR change: `BT19-016.ts` differs from HEAD only by exporting `compiled`.
`effects.json` stays in sync.

## Remaining gaps

- `abortOnDecline: true` sits on the sole action of each effect, where it is
  dead (the same finding lane 4 recorded on BT19-008). Left as-is
  deliberately: removing it would change the persisted IR and force a
  `BT19-catalog-sync` resync for no behavioural difference. Flagging it for
  a future IR-cleanup pass rather than churning it here.
- The `count: 1` cap is not separately proved with two matching Blue Flare
  cards in hand; `autoSelectCards` takes the maximum allowed, so a broken cap
  would show up as two placed cards, but no test pins that explicitly.

## Catalog discrepancies

`effectText` contains a NO-BREAK SPACE (U+00A0) between `[Blue Flare]` and
`trait`: `"...placing 1 [Blue Flare] trait Digimon card..."`. The
wording is otherwise correct and the IR does not parse this text at runtime,
so nothing behavioural depends on it, but any future text-matching or
diffing against official text will trip on it. Reported, not edited (the
catalog is out of this lane's scope).
