## Clause -> test -> IR

Clause 1 -> `effects[0]`: `trigger: "WhenAttacking"`, `isInherited: true`,
`frequency: "OncePerTurn"`, one `Draw` action (`controller: "mine"`, `amount: 1`,
`optional: true`, `abortOnDecline: true`) whose `cost` is
`kind: "place"` with target filter `controller: "mine", zone: "hand", kind: ["Digimon"]`
and `nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Xros Heart","Blue Flare"], match: "trait" }]`
(exact trait match — correct for "with the [X] trait"), `count: 1`, and
`underFilter: { controller: "mine", kind: ["Tamer"] }`.

Tests in `apps/api/src/cards/BT19/BT19-001.test.ts`:

- `places only a qualifying Digimon from hand under a Tamer and draws` — seeded
  stack: places the [Blue Flare] BT19-016, leaves the off-trait BT19-067 in hand,
  draws the deck top (asserted by instance id), and refuses a second placement/draw on
  a second same-turn attack.
- `may decline the placement cost and does not draw` — `autoDeclineOptional`:
  Tamer stack stays empty, hand unchanged (`abortOnDecline` proved).
- `carries the inherited draw through the real Digi-Egg route and is once per turn`
  — full lifecycle, all public intents:
  - `hatchEgg` in the production Breeding window puts BT19-001 in breeding, egg
    deck empties.
  - `digivolve` intent puts the Red Lv3 BT3-009 (Red Lv2, cost 0) onto the egg in
    breeding; `breeding.stack` is exactly the egg instance and the battle area still
    holds only the seeded Tamer.
  - `moveFromBreeding` on the next own turn carries the stack into the battle area;
    the carrier's `stack` is the same egg instance id.
  - The carrier attacks: exactly the [Xros Heart] BT10-007 lands under the Tamer and
    exactly one card comes off the deck top (asserted by instance id).
    `preferInstanceIds` biases the cost selection toward the off-trait BT19-067, so a
    filter accepting it would fail here — this is the near-miss peer.
  - Same turn, after `verb.unsuspend`, a second attack changes neither the Tamer
    stack nor the hand (Once Per Turn refusal).
  - Turn 5 through the real turn loop: the clause fires again from the same stack,
    the second [Xros Heart] copy is placed and one more card is drawn; the off-trait
    peer is still in hand. `pendingDecision` is undefined at the end.
- `does nothing when there is no Tamer to place the card under` — no Tamer in play:
  the qualifying card stays in hand and the deck is untouched.

No injected timing (`fire`, `fireTiming`, `fireSubTrigger`) anywhere in the file;
`verb.unsuspend` is used only to re-arm the attacker inside a turn, not to fire a
timing. Fixtures carry no Digi-Egg in security or deck (the two seeded-stack tests previously
seeded the Digi-Egg BT1-001 in the main deck; they now use the inert BT1-009/BT1-013)
and no numeric `security: <n>` form.
