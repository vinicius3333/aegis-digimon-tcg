# BT19-021 Xiquemon — re-audit

## Card

Blue Lv.4 Champion, Vaccine, [Avian]/[Aquatic], 5000 DP, play cost 5.
Digivolves from a Blue Lv.3 for 2.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] Return 1 of your opponent's level 3 Digimon to the hand.`
2. `[When Digivolving] Return 1 of your opponent's level 3 Digimon to the hand.`
3. `[Rule] Trait: Has the [Aquatic] type.`
4. Inherited: `＜Jamming＞.`

## Clause -> test -> IR

| Clause | Test | IR |

| Inherited ＜Jamming＞ | `keeps its host alive against a Security Digimon that beats it, while a plain host dies`, `does not grant ＜Jamming＞ to a Digimon that is not its host` | `effects[3]` `Static`, `isInherited:true`, `keywords:[Jamming]` |
| Digivolution cost | the Blue Lv.3 route test and `refuses an illegal Green Lv.3 source` | catalog `evoCosts` |

The two printed timings share one line of text but are two independent windows, and the IR
keeps them as two effects. The digivolve test proves [When Digivolving] fires and [On Play]
does not double up (exactly one card leaves the opposing battle area).

## Behavioural notes

- ＜Jamming＞ is proved by its rules consequence (comprehensive 16-9-1: not deleted as a
  result of a battle with an opponent's Security Digimon), not by a keyword flag: two
  3000 DP BT19-023 Huankunmon hosts attack the player into a 5000 DP inert Security Digimon
  through the real turn loop; the one with BT19-021 in its digivolution cards survives, the
  bare one is trashed.
- The [Rule] trait is proved by a real gate: EX12-031 MarineBullmon prints
  `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[Aquatic]/[Shambala] trait: Cost 3` on top of its normal Blue Lv.4
  cost of 4. Digivolving from BT19-021 costs 3; from the near-miss BT19-020 Greymon
  (Blue Lv.4, [Dinosaur]/[Blue Flare]) it costs 4. `useAlternateCost: true` is passed in
  BOTH runs — the engine accepts the flag either way and silently falls back to the normal
  route, so only the memory delta proves the gate.

## Remaining gaps

- The catalog already lists `Aquatic` among the card's printed `types`, so the [Rule] clause's
  all-zones reach (Q759 / Q3077) is carried by the card data rather than by the `GrantStatic`
  the IR compiles. The `GrantStatic` is therefore redundant on the battle area and cannot be
  distinguished from the printed type by any test; it is left in place because removing it
  would make the IR stop describing a printed clause. No test can currently isolate a
  battle-area-only regression of that grant.

## Catalog discrepancies

None affecting behaviour. `effectText` runs the two timings together on one line with a
double space before `[Rule]`, matching the official printing's layout.
