## Clause -> test -> IR

Clause 1 -> `effects[0]`: `trigger: "OpponentsTurn"`, `isInherited: true`, no
`frequency` (the card prints no Once Per Turn), holding a `SubTrigger` action on
`event: "whenOpponentAttacks"`. Its `cost` is `kind: "return"`, `to: "deckBottom"`,
`isSelf: true`, filter `isSelfRef: true` plus
`nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Aqua","Sea Animal"], match: "traitContains" }]`
(substring — correct for "in one of its traits"; it also makes the catalog's
[Aquatic] type satisfy the printed [Aqua]), and `storeAs: "returnedDigimonLevel"`.
The body returns 1 `controller: "opponent"` Digimon with
`levelLte: "returnedDigimonLevel"` to `hand`. `optional: true` + `abortOnDecline: true`.

Tests in `apps/api/src/cards/BT19/BT19-002.test.ts`:

- `returns its Aqua host to deck bottom and bounces only up to the returned level` —
  seeded stack; host to deck, digivolution cards to trash, Lv4 peer returned to hand
  while the Lv5 peer stays on the board.
- `may decline the return cost and leaves both boards unchanged` — `autoDeclineOptional`.
- `resolves MarineBullmon's Decode interruption before the level-5 bounce from Q3058`
  — BT19-024 host with BT19-002 underneath: ＜Decode (Blue Lv.4)＞ interrupts, BT19-019
  is left on the board, BT19-024 goes to the deck and the opponent's Lv5 is returned.
- `carries the inherited bounce through the real Digi-Egg route and only up to the
  returned level` — full lifecycle, all public intents:
  - `hatchEgg` -> BT19-002 in breeding, egg deck empty.
  - `digivolve` the Blue Lv3 [Sea Animal] BT6-020 onto it in breeding (Blue Lv2,
    cost 0); `breeding.stack` is exactly the egg instance.
  - Turn 2, while the stack is STILL IN BREEDING, the opponent attacks: nothing
    fires — opponent hand and battle area unchanged, breeding top card unchanged.
  - Turn 3 `moveFromBreeding` carries the stack into the battle area (carrier stack
    is the egg instance id).
  - Turn 4, the opponent attacks for real: the host instance is at the BOTTOM of my
    deck (asserted by instance id), the egg instance is in my trash, my battle area
    is empty, and exactly the opponent's Lv3 peer is added to their hand while the
    Lv4 peer keeps its permanent id on the board. `preferInstanceIds` biases the
    bounce toward the Lv4 peer, so a missing level bound would fail here.
- `cannot pay the cost when its host lacks the [Aqua]/[Sea Animal] traits` —
  near-miss peer BT19-016 (Blue Lv3, [Reptile]/[Blue Flare]): no return, no bounce,
  no pending decision.

No injected timing anywhere in the file.

## Remaining gaps

- "as high or lower a LEVEL" is proved at Lv3 vs Lv4 and (seeded) Lv4 vs Lv5; an
  equal-level boundary case at the same level as the returned Digimon is covered only
  by the seeded Lv4/Lv4 arrangement in the first test, not through the lifecycle route.
- The clause has no printed Once Per Turn, so no OPT test applies; multiple opponent
  attacks in one turn are not exercised (the cost consumes the source after the first).

## Catalog discrepancies

- The printed text names the traits `[Aqua]/[Sea Animal]` while the catalog's `types`
  for the Blue BT19 hosts read `Aquatic`. `traitContains` matches both readings, so
  behaviour is unaffected. Reported, not edited.
