## Defects fixed

**`BT19-050.ts:29` and `:57` — removed `suspended: false` from both Suspend target filters.**
The printed clause says "Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon or Tamers" with no freshness
qualifier, and an already-suspended permanent is a legal choice (comprehensive 15-15-5-1/-3;
KB Q845, Q1219). The prior audit's filter narrowed the candidate set and would have forced the
effect onto a fresh peer. Coordinator decision recorded in `REVIEW-NOTES.md`; lanes 17 and 20
made the same reversal on BT19-038, 044 and 046. Every other filter axis
(`controller: "opponent"`, `kind: ["Digimon","Tamer"]`) is unchanged, and the `Restrict`
actions never carried the axis.

**The IR changed, so `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` is stale for this card and
`BT19-catalog-sync.test.ts` will fail until the coordinator resyncs.**

The test file was rebuilt. The previous suite proved ＜Blast Digivolve＞ only by asserting the
IR literal (never opening a counter window), drove both timings through
`advance.fireForPermanent`, faked the opponent's turn by writing `s.state.turnSeat`, never
touched ACE Overflow behaviourally, and never showed that the unsuspend lock survives an
actual unsuspend phase — the only thing the clause is for.

## Remaining gaps

- The two actions choose their targets independently, so with several eligible opponent
  permanents the engine may suspend one and lock another. The printed text allows exactly
  that, so the fixtures pin a single eligible permanent per case rather than asserting an
  ordering the card does not print.
