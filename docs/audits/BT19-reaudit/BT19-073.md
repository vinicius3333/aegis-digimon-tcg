# BT19-073 LordKnightmon (X Antibody) — re-audit (lane 30)

## Card

Purple/Black Lv6 Mega, Virus, Holy Warrior / X Antibody / Royal Knight. Play cost 12, DP 12000,
evolution costs: Black Lv5 for 4, Purple Lv5 for 4. No inherited text.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve][LordKnightmon]: Cost 1`
2. `＜Collision＞.`
3. `＜Piercing＞`
4. `[When Digivolving] For each of your Digimon, ＜De-Digivolve1＞ 1 of your opponent's Digimon.`
5. `Then, until the end of their turn, 1 of their Digimon can't digivolve.`
6. `[All Turns] While [LordKnightmon]/[X Antibody] is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, all
   of your Digimon with [Knightmon] in its text gain ＜Alliance＞ and get +3000 DP.`

## Rulings

| Q&A | Question | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q3134 | With 2 of my Digimon, how many opponent Digimon can ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ affect? (A: 1 — you choose 1 opponent Digimon, then give IT ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ for each of your Digimon) | "Q3134: de-digivolves ONE opponent Digimon once per your Digimon, then locks a DIFFERENT one" |

Supporting rules: Q4568 (a scaled ＜De-Digivolve 1＞ is the peel repeated, not ＜De-Digivolve N＞ —
the engine implements it as a repetition, `interpreter/actions/digivolution.ts:24-48`), and
comprehensive §16-30 for ＜Collision＞ (all opponent Digimon gain ＜Blocker＞ and the opponent must
block when able).

The prior audit's "independent selection" for the digivolve lock is CORRECT against the print:
"Then, ... 1 of their Digimon can't digivolve" is a fresh choice, not the ＜De-Digivolve＞ target.
The Q3134 test proves it by scripting the two decisions onto two different opponent Digimon and
asserting that only the second one is restricted.

## Defects fixed in the module

1. `digivolutionRequirement` used the SUBSTRING gate `names: ["LordKnightmon"]` for the printed
   bracketed `[Digivolve][LordKnightmon]` route. Substring matching lets this card's own print
   ("LordKnightmon (X Antibody)") serve as its own source. Changed to
   `namesExact: ["LordKnightmon"]` (the trap lanes 16 and 19 recorded).
   Proof: "refuses its own print as a source" and "charges the normal Lv5 cost of 4 when the
   base is not exactly [LordKnightmon]" (a `useAlternateCost: true` that silently falls back
   costs 4, not 1).
2. The `[All Turns]` condition matched `[LordKnightmon]` with `match: "name"` (substring).
   Changed to `match: "nameExact"`. This one is not behaviourally separable on the current
   card pool — the only card whose name contains but does not equal "LordKnightmon" is
   BT19-073 itself, which satisfies the same condition through its `X Antibody` trait — so it
   is a correctness fix backed by the IR assertion only.
3. Q3134 violation. `DeDigivolve` carried `bindAs: "deDigivolveTarget"`, which nothing reads on
   a non-`SelectBind` target, and the engine re-resolves the target on every scaled repetition.
   The observed behaviour was one target choice PER repetition, so two of my Digimon let the
   effect peel two DIFFERENT opponent Digimon — the exact case Q3134 answers with "1 Digimon".
   Fixed inside the card: a leading `SelectBind` binds the single opponent Digimon, and
   `DeDigivolve` resolves through `fromSelectionRef: "deDigivolveTarget"`, so each repetition
   peels the same Digimon.

## Remaining gaps

- The `nameExact` fix in the `[All Turns]` condition has no separating fixture on the current
  card pool (see defect 2). It is asserted structurally only.
- The ＜Alliance＞ grant is proved as an active keyword, not by a second attacker joining an
  attack; the printed clause grants the keyword, and the keyword's own combat behaviour is
  covered by the engine's ＜Alliance＞ suites.
- The `[All Turns]` grants use `duration: "permanent"` under a live condition, so they are
  re-derived by the continuous pass; the negative-control test proves the gate, but a
  mid-game loss of the gating digivolution card is not exercised (no public intent in reach of
  this fixture removes one).
