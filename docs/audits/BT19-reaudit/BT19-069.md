## Defects fixed locally

1. **The printed `[Digivolve][Gazimon]/[Gizamon]` route used the substring gate.**
   `DigivolutionRequirement.names` is documented as the SUBSTRING match
   (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/requirements/digivolve.ts:14-24`); a bracketed
   printed name is exact. With `names`, BT9-070 "Gazimon (X Antibody)" (a real
   Purple Lv3 card) would have digivolved for 2 instead of 3. Fix:
   `namesExact: ["Gazimon", "Gizamon"]`. Proof it mattered: reverting to `names`
   turns the near-miss test and the IR test red (2 failed | 10 passed).

`abortOnDecline: true` sits on the sole action of each effect and is therefore
dead, per the lane-4/lane-16 finding. Left in place rather than churning the IR;
the IR assertion deliberately does not pin it.
