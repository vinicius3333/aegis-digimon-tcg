## Card summary

Catalog: Yellow Digimon, Lv5, Ultimate, Data, type `Wizard`, play cost 5, DP 8000,
evoCost Yellow Lv4 at 3 memory, `isAce: true`, `overflowMemory: 3`.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Hand] [Counter] ＜Blast Digivolve＞`
2. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] If it's your turn, you may use 1 single-color Option
   card with a cost of 5 or less from your hand without paying the cost.`
3. `If it's your opponent's turn, 1 of their Digimon gains ＜Security A. -1＞ and can't
   activate [When Digivolving] effects for the turn.`
4. Inherited `[When Attacking] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -4000 DP for the turn.`
5. ACE Overflow ＜3＞ (catalog `overflowMemory`).

## Clause -> test -> IR

- Clause 1 -> `effects[0]` `trigger: "Counter"`, `isFromHand: true`,
  `keywords: [{ keyword: "BlastDigivolve" }]`. Proved twice through the real
  `respondCounter` intent inside an opponent attack's counter window (never a bare
  `digivolve`): the memory gauge is unchanged (cost fully waived) while the digivolution
  bonus draw still lands and the stack holds the base instance.
- Clause 2 -> `UseOptionWithoutCost { payCost: false, optional: true, filter: { kind:
  ["Option"], colorCount: 1, playCostLte: 5 }, condition: isYourTurn }` on both `OnPlay`
  and `WhenDigivolving`.
  - Public `playCard`: memory 10 -> 5 (Taomon's own cost only), BT1-102 in the trash and
    its own text resolved (＜Draw 1＞ per 2 security, the seeded top-deck card is in hand).
  - Near misses left in hand: BT12-104 (Yellow/Red, two colours) and BT1-107 (Yellow,
    cost 6).
  - Public `digivolve` onto BT1-051: memory 5 -> 2 (the printed evoCost only).
  - `autoDeclineOptional`: the Option stays in hand and the trash stays empty.
  - Turn exclusivity: on our own turn the opponent's Digimon has no ＜Security Attack＞
    modifier and no `whenDigivolving` restriction.
- Clause 3 -> `GainKeyword { keyword: SecurityAttack, amount: -1 }` plus
  `DisableTimingEffect { timings: ["whenDigivolving"], target.sameTarget: true }`, both
  gated by `isOpponentsTurn` and expiring `untilOpponentTurnEnd`.
  - Reached the only legal way: ＜Blast Digivolve＞ during the opponent's attack, so the
    `[When Digivolving]` timing genuinely resolves on their turn.
  - `sameTarget` proved by consequence, not by a flag: the unchosen attacker checks one
    security card normally, while the chosen Digimon later attacks and checks NONE
    (1 - 1 = 0) and cannot activate its `[When Digivolving]` effect.
- Clause 4 -> `WhenAttacking` / `isInherited` `ModifyDP { amount: -4000, duration:
  "forTheTurn" }`. A realistic stack (BT1-051 host with BT19-037 under it) attacks
  through a public `attack` intent inside the turn loop: exactly one of two opponent
  Digimon drops 9000 -> 5000, the other is untouched, and the modifier is gone when read
  inside the opponent's own open Main phase. Negative control: the same host with an
  unrelated card (BT1-045) underneath leaves both opponent Digimon at 9000.
- Clause 5 (ACE Overflow ＜3＞) -> catalog `isAce` / `overflowMemory`, proved by
  behaviour: Taomon attacks a suspended 20000-DP wall, dies in battle, and memory drops
  5 -> 2 as it leaves the battle area.

## Defects found

None in the IR. The prior audit's test file was rebuilt: it proved the timings with
`advance.fireForPermanent` (injected timing, no behavioural credit), wrote
`s.state.turnSeat = 1` directly (injected state — see the brief's lane-7 trap), and
asserted the two opponent-turn clauses through `observe().keywordAmount` /
`observe().timingEffectDisabled` flags rather than their rules consequence. The
＜Blast Digivolve＞ clause and ACE Overflow were only asserted as data. All of that is
now driven through `startTurnLoop`, `respondCounter`, `playCard`, `digivolve` and
`attack`.

## Remaining gaps

- Q5538 (another effect activating a suppressed `[When Digivolving]`) is untested; see
  the Q&A table for why.
- `duration: "untilOpponentTurnEnd"` is asserted for both opponent-turn clauses, but
  only within the granting turn. The printed wording is "for the turn" and the clause
  can only ever resolve on the opponent's turn, so the two are equivalent here; a
  cross-turn expiry test would need the locked Digimon to survive into our turn and is
  not a printed-clause obligation.
- No retained red tests; no engine seam gaps found.

## Catalog discrepancies

The printed `effectText` does not spell out ACE / Overflow ＜3＞; the catalog carries it
structurally as `isAce` + `overflowMemory: 3`, which is the project's convention for
every ACE card (BT19-011 is the same). Not a defect.
