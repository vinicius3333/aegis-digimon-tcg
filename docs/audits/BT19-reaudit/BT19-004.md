## Clause -> test -> IR

Clause 1 -> `compiled.effects[0]`: `trigger: "YourTurn"`, `isInherited: true`, one
`Aura` action whose `target.filter` is `{ isSelfRef: true }` with `isSelf: true`,
`effect: { kind: "modifyDP", amount: 2000 }`, and
`while: { kind: "youHave", filter: { controllerDefault: "mine", excludeSelf: true,
kind: ["Digimon"], colors: ["Green"] } }`.

Each IR field is pinned by a behavioural endpoint:

- `amount: 2000` and `isSelf` — "gives its host +2000 DP on your turn while you have
  another green Digimon": host BT1-064 Goblimon (printed 3000 DP) reads 5000 while the
  green peer BT1-065 Mushroomon stays at its printed 4000 and the opponent's blue
  BT1-028 Elecmon at 3000. Deleting the peer drops the host back to 3000 in the same
  tick, so the Aura is continuous, not a one-shot buff.
- `excludeSelf: true` — "does not count the host itself...": the host is itself a green
  Digimon and is alone on your side; DP stays 3000.
- `kind: ["Digimon"]` — same test: BT1-088 Izzy Izumi is a **green Tamer** beside the
  host and does not satisfy the count. (Izzy's only printed effect is `[Main]`, so it
  is inert unless activated.)
- `controllerDefault: "mine"` — same test: the opponent's green BT1-065 does not count.
- Battle-area scope — same test: green Digimon in your hand, your trash and your
  breeding area do not count. The IR omits `zone: "battleArea"`; `countMatching`
  (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts`) defaults an absent `zone` to a
  per-permanent battle-area scan and only counts breeding when the filter names it, so
  the omission is harmless here. The same test then adds a real second green Digimon to
  the battle area and the DP jumps to 5000, proving the count is live rather than
  simply always false.
- `trigger: "YourTurn"` — "applies only on your turn, proved across a real turn
  boundary": through `engine.startTurnLoop()`, 5000 in your main phase, 3000 in the
  opponent's main phase, 5000 again on your next turn. No `state.turnSeat` poking.

## Peer / stack proof

"carries the DP bonus through the real Digi-Egg route: hatch -> digivolve in breeding ->
battle area" exercises the full lifecycle through public intents only:

1. `hatchEgg` in the production Breeding window moves BT19-004 from the egg deck to the
   breeding area (egg deck emptied, breeding top card asserted by card id).
2. `digivolve` puts the Green Lv3 BT1-064 onto the egg **inside the breeding area**
   (Green Lv2, cost 0). The egg's instance id is asserted as the sole entry of
   `breeding.stack`, and BT1-064 has left the hand.
3. Two `endPhase` hops later, `moveFromBreeding` carries the stack into the battle area
   on the next own turn; the carrier's top card and stack identity are asserted again.
4. Only then does the inherited Aura matter. Beside the blue near-miss peer BT1-028 the
   carrier reads its printed 3000; adding the green BT1-065 raises it to 5000 while the
   peer itself stays at 4000 and the blue peer at 3000.

Near-miss peers used: BT1-028 Elecmon (Blue Lv3 — right level and kind, wrong colour),
BT1-088 Izzy Izumi (green, yours, but a Tamer), the opponent's BT1-065 (green Digimon,
wrong controller).

## Defects

None in the module. The IR was correct on the single printed clause.

The **pre-existing test** was weak and is replaced:

- It asserted `s.perm("host").currentDP === 3000` for a BT19-044 host whose printed DP
  is 1000, i.e. it read the +2000 bonus as if it were the printed value, then set
  `state.turnSeat = 1` by hand and asserted 1000. Correct numbers, but the turn switch
  never went through the turn loop.
- It never tested `excludeSelf` (the host alone), never tested the Digimon-vs-Tamer
  gate, never tested the controller gate, never tested zones outside the battle area,
  and never touched the Digi-Egg lifecycle: BT19-004 appeared only as a seeded `under:`
  entry.
- Its host BT19-044 Terriermon carries its own `[Start of Your Main Phase]` and
  `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn]` effects, so a DP reading from that fixture is not
  attributable to BT19-004 alone.

No red tests retained. No engine gap found.

## Remaining gaps
