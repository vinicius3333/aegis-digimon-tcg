Champion, Data, Super Major + Xros Heart, play cost 5, DP 5000, evoCosts Yellow Lv3
and Red Lv3 at 3 memory. Rarity R, max 4 per deck.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve]Lv.3 w/[Xros Heart] trait: Cost 2` — alternate route.
2. `This card is also treated as [Starmons] for a DigiXros.`
3. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When any of your [Xros Heart] trait Digimon are
   played, give 1 of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ and it gets -3000 DP

4. `[On Deletion] You may place 1 Digimon card with the [Xros Heart]/[Blue Flare]
   trait from your hand or trash under any of your Tamers.`
5. Inherited: `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has the [Xros Heart]
   trait, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn.`

## Clause -> test -> IR

### Clause 1 — alternate `[Digivolve]` route

IR: `digivolutionRequirement: [{ level: 3, traits: ["Xros Heart"], cost: 2,
isAlternate: true }]`.

- `takes the Cost 2 alternate route only from a Lv3 with the [Xros Heart] trait` —
  public `digivolve` with `useAlternateCost: true` from BT10-029 Starmons (Yellow
  Lv3, Xros Heart) pays 2 (memory 6 -> 4); the same intent from BT1-050 Liollmon
  (Yellow Lv3, no Xros Heart) silently falls back to the printed route and pays 3
  (6 -> 3). The memory delta is the only discriminator, as the brief requires.
  Source-stack identity and the evolution bonus draw are asserted on both.
- `refuses an illegal evolution source (a Lv4 and a blue Lv3)` — BT9-035 (Lv4) and
  BT1-030 (Blue Lv3) both return `{ ok: false, reason: "invalid-evolution" }`.

### Clause 2 — treated as `[Starmons]` for a DigiXros only

IR: `trigger: "Static"`, `GrantStatic` with `grant: "name"`,
`tokens: ["Starmons"]`, `digiXrosOnly: true`, self target. The alias is read for a
HAND instance through `digiXrosOnlyNameAliasesFor`
(`engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts:46`) via `GameEngine.digiXrosDeps`.

- `counts as [Starmons] for a DigiXros, while a real [Starmon] does not (Q3089)` —
  a real `playCard` with a `digiXros` plan on BT10-013 Shoutmon X5. BT19-035 +
  BT10-008 Shoutmon is accepted (memory 10 -> 4: -2 per placed material) and both
  land in the stack by instance id. The near-miss peer BT9-035 "Starmon" (name is
  not "Starmons", trait is [Mutant]) is refused `invalid-material`.
- `cannot be saved by ＜Material Save＞ from its DigiXros host's stack (Q3089)` —
  a real battle deletes the X5; `digiXrosMatches` (`engine/combat/keywords.ts:247`)
  reads only `nameEn`, so only BT10-008 lands under the Tamer and BT19-035 goes to
  the trash. That is the exact board Q3089 rules on.

### Clause 3 — `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` on an `[Xros Heart]` play

IR: `trigger: "AllTurns"` (not `Static` — the brief's trap), `frequency:
"OncePerTurn"` with a `sharedUseKey`, a `whenPlayed` SubTrigger gated by
`sourceFilter` `nameOrTrait [{ tokens: ["Xros Heart"], match: "trait" }]`, then
`GainKeyword SecurityAttack -1` and `ModifyDP -3000` on the SAME target
(`sameTarget: true`), both `duration: "untilOpponentTurnEnd"`.

- `triggers on its own play, hits one opponent, and is once per turn (Q3090)` —
  playing BT19-035 itself arms and fires the watcher: the pinned opponent goes
  6000 -> 3000 and to ＜Security A. -1＞, the bystander is untouched at 5000/0. A
  second [Xros Heart] play (BT19-033) in the same turn changes nothing.
- `ignores a played Digimon without the exact [Xros Heart] trait` — BT9-035 Starmon
  played with the watcher out leaves the opponent at 5000/0.
- `keeps the debuff through the whole opponent turn and drops it afterwards` — real
  `startTurnLoop()`; the state is read INSIDE the opponent's open Main phase
  (still -3000 / -1) and again inside the next own Main (back to 6000 / 0).

### Clause 4 — `[On Deletion]` place under a Tamer

IR: `trigger: "OnDeletion"`, `PlaceUnder`, `optional: true`, target from
`["hand", "trash"]` with `nameOrTrait [{ tokens: ["Xros Heart", "Blue Flare"],
match: "trait" }]`, `underFilter` = your Tamers.

- `On Deletion places the chosen [Xros Heart] card from hand under a Tamer, not the
  near miss` — deletion is a REAL battle (5000 into a suspended 7000 defender). The
  candidate is pinned by instance id, because ShootingStarmon itself is already in
  the trash when the effect resolves and also carries the [Xros Heart] trait
  (CR 4-14-1/2, the brief's trash trap). The [Mutant] near miss stays in hand.
- `On Deletion can take the card from the trash instead` — the trash half of the
  filter, taking the [Blue Flare] BT19-025 over the near miss.

### Clause 5 — inherited `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn]`

IR: `trigger: "WhenAttacking"`, `isInherited: true`, `frequency: "OncePerTurn"`,
`ModifyDP -2000` `forTheTurn`, `condition: selfHasTrait [Xros Heart]`.

- `inherited attack reduction needs an [Xros Heart] host and fires on a real attack`
  — a real `attack` intent from BT19-038 JaegerDorulumon (Xros Heart) with BT19-035
  in its digivolution cards drops the opponent 6000 -> 4000; the same stack under
  BT1-057 Sirenmon (no Xros Heart) leaves it at 6000.

## Remaining gaps

- The `[On Deletion]` clause is proved for the hand and trash halves and for a
  single Tamer. A board with two Tamers (the "under ANY of your Tamers" choice) is
  not covered.
- The inherited `[Once Per Turn]` is proved by a single attack; a same-turn second
  attack by the same Digimon is illegal anyway, so the limiter cannot be crossed
  without an extra keyword (＜Rush＞ / ＜Reboot＞) fixture.
