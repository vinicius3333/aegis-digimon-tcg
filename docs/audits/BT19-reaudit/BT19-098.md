Purple Option, use cost 4, `[Device]` trait, U, max 4 per deck.

Printed clauses:

1. **Colour waiver.** "While you don't have [King Device], you may ignore this card's color requirements."
2. **On-trash.** "When an effect trashes this card in your battle area, place 1 Option card with the [Device] trait with a use cost of 3 or less from your trash into the battle area."

## Defects fixed locally

- **`match: "name"` on a bracketed `[King Device]` reference** (`BT19-098.ts`, the colour-waiver
  condition). `[Name]` in brackets is the exact-name form; `match: "name"` is the substring form
  and would let any card whose name merely contains "King Device" suppress the waiver. Changed to
  `nameExact`, matching the sibling card BT19-093 Queen Device, which carries the same printed
  sentence and already uses `nameExact` with an explanatory comment. **This changes the IR, so
  `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` is stale for BT19-098 and `BT19-catalog-sync.test.ts`
  will fail for this card until the coordinator resyncs.**
- The prior typed revalidation's other shapes were verified and left alone: `whenTrashedByEffect`
  (not generic deletion) for "when an effect trashes this card"; `AllTurns` (not `Static`) for the
  watcher block; `playCostLte: 3` rather than the `{ op, value }` object form, which
  `interpreter/matching/definition.ts` reads only for permanent targets and would silently drop on
  a trash-zone matcher; and no cost ceiling on the [Security] clause, which prints none.

The whole previous test file was replaced. It built a fake `EffectContext` with hand-rolled
`Primitives` and asserted on recorded verb names, which proves the module calls a stub, not that
the card works. Every clause is now proved on the real engine.

## Remaining gaps

- `nameExact` versus `name` is not discriminated **behaviourally**, because "King Device" is the
  only card name in the catalog containing that string. The near-miss test discriminates
  name-scoped from trait-scoped (BT19-093 Queen Device shares the `[Device]` trait and does not
  block the waiver); exactness itself rests on the printed-bracket rule.
- No test drives the on-trash clause from an effect on the **opponent's** turn: no printed card in
  the corpus trashes an opponent's battle-area Option through the effect-trash bus at a cost the
  opponent can pay. The turn-loop test instead shows the watcher installed and its product
  surviving into the opponent's open Main phase.

## Catalog discrepancies

- `effectText` and `securityEffectText` store a **non-breaking space** (U+00A0) after each
  occurrence of "[Device] trait" (`effectText` offset 173, `securityEffectText` offset 56). Cosmetic
  encoding quirk only; the wording matches the official card. The test normalises it rather than
  editing the catalog.
