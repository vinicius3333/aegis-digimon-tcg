## Card summary

Catalog (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`): White **Option**, play cost 3,
trait `[D-Reaper]`, rarity C, max 4 per deck. No level, no DP.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Security] [Opponent's Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, if all of your Digimon
   and Tamers have the [D-Reaper] trait, for each of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]'s
   digivolution cards, the attacking Digimon get -1000 DP for the turn.`
   A resident watcher that works while the card sits **face up in the security stack**
   (comprehensive 15-14-5-1).
2. `[Main] If you have no face-up security cards, by trashing your top security card, place
   this card face up as your top security card.`
3. `[Security] You may play 1 [D-Reaper] trait card with a play cost equal to or lower than
   the number of digivolution cards of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]'s from your hand without
   paying the cost.` The one-shot security-check skill.

### Catalog discrepancies (reported, not edited)

Both text fields store U+00A0 NO-BREAK SPACE where the printed card has a plain space:

- `effectText` index 118 — after `... have the [D-Reaper] trait,`.
- `securityEffectText` index 36 — after `1 [D-Reaper]`, before `trait card`.

The catalog test asserts both the presence of the NBSP and the normalized text, so a future
catalog clean-up shows up as a deliberate change rather than a silent drift.

  `securityStatic` builder (`builders.ts:507-530`), whose base guard is "the source is in the
  security stack" — the CR 15-14-5-1 gate. Body: one `SubTrigger` on
  `whenOpponentAttacks` with `fireCondition.kind: "allYoursMatchFilter"`
  (`kind: ["Digimon","Tamer"]`, exact `match: "trait"` on `D-Reaper`), whose action is a
  `ModifyDP` of `-1000` `forTheTurn` on `sourceRef: "triggerSubject"`, scaled
  `per: 1, unit: "digivolutionCardsOfFiltered"` over `nameExact: "Mother D-Reaper"`.

    with -2000 the attacker is deleted, without it the wall is. The exact modifier is read

  - Scaling: 5 digivolution cards -> `min DP 5000`; 0 cards -> `10000` and the wall dies.

## Retained red — engine seam, not a card defect

Two tests are `it.fails`:

- `plays a [D-Reaper] card whose cost fits the digivolution-card count, for free`
- `is declinable: the printed 'You may play' leaves every card in hand`

Seam: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:647-720`. The optional
`PlayWithoutCost` preflight builds `staticPreflightTarget` from `action.target` after
materializing `levelComparison.scaling`, `applyPlayDpCeilingModifier` and
`applyPlayCostCeiling` — but **not** `playCostLteScaling`. The clause is therefore judged at a
ceiling of `playCostLte: 0`, `candidateLooseInstances` returns nothing, and line 720 drops the
whole action (`return false`) before any decision is raised.

The resolver itself is correct: `actions/play.ts:443-455` does add
`playCostScalingDelta(scaling, scaleFactor(ctx, scaling))` to `playCostLte`.

Expected vs actual (6 digivolution cards under Mother D-Reaper; hand holds EX2-051 cost 6,
EX2-054 cost 11, BT1-012 cost 3 non-D-Reaper):

- expected: EX2-051 played free onto the board, both near-misses left in hand, memory unmoved.
- actual: no decision at all; all three cards stay in hand.

Diagnosis evidence (local experiments, all reverted): hard-coding `playCostLte: 6` with the
scaling still present makes the clause play exactly EX2-051 and leave both near-misses alone;
`playCostLte: 5` breaks it again; replacing the scaling with a plain `unit: "cards"` scaling
does not help either — so the base value alone decides, which is the preflight, not the
resolver.

The IR was left as printed. The only shape the preflight does understand is
`playCostCeiling`, whose `digivolutionCards` unit **sums** every matching permanent's stack;
the printed text says "1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]'s", which is the max of one, so switching
would bless a wrong answer on a two-Mother board. Per the brief, the red is retained instead.

The negative control `plays nothing when every [D-Reaper] card in hand costs more than the
digivolution-card count` currently passes for the wrong reason and is annotated as such; it
becomes a real discriminator once the preflight is fixed.

## Other observations (out of scope, not fixed)

- `GameEngine.printedColorRequirementMet` (GameEngine.ts:5934-5967) only accepts a Digi-Egg as
  an Option's colour source while it is **in breeding** (`perm.inBreeding === true`). A
  hatched Digi-Egg moved to the battle area is a Digimon (KB Q2684), so a battle-area
  EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper does not satisfy this White Option's colour requirement. The test
  works around it with a White Digimon (EX2-051); flagging for the engine lane.
