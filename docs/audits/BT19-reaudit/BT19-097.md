## Remaining gaps

- Q6244's *reveal / search* half is not proved directly: no card in this lane's
  reach reveals BT19-097 from the deck without also trashing it, and driving a
  foreign reveal card would test the reveal seam rather than this card. The
  clause's scope is instead pinned from two sides — the watcher only answers for
  its own card (`sourceFilter.isSelfRef`, proved with a different milled card),
  and a copy in the HAND is never placed. The engine fires
  `whenTrashedFromDeck` only from the mill path
  (`primitives.ts:2965`, "once per MILLED card"), which is the mechanism Q6244
  describes.
- The ＜Delay＞ entry-turn guard (§16-17-3: cannot activate the turn the Option
  entered play) is covered by `capabilities.test.ts` for this exact shape and is
  not re-proved here; the Delay tests seat the Option as an established
  permanent.
