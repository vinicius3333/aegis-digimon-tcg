## The prior audit's Decode scoping — verified, not assumed

The task asked whether restricting ＜Decode＞ playback to the source Digimon's own
stack is right. It is, on two independent grounds:

- Printed text: "from **its** digivolution cards".
- KB `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` §16-36-1 (via `node tools/kb/query.mjs rules
  "Decode"`): "you may play 1 specified Digimon card from **that Digimon's**
  digivolution cards without paying the cost". §16-36-2 makes it an immediate-type
  effect on "would leave the battle area other than by a battle"; §16-36-3 makes it
  optional.

The IR encodes the scope as `target.filter.isSelfRef: true` on a
`from: ["digivolutionCards"]` pool. In a hosted zone that key means "host permanent
is the source", not "this instance"
(`interpreter/targeting/loose.ts:230` and `:389-393`). The engine additionally
force-scopes any `playedByDecode` play that names no host
(`interpreter/actions/play.ts:102-110`, `applyDecodeHostScope`), so the encoding is
belt-and-braces rather than wrong. The exemplar EX12-016 spells the same scope as
`hostFilter: { isSelfRef: true }`; both reach the same predicate, so I did not churn
the IR to match style. The scoping is now proved behaviourally against a second own
stack holding a legal Blue Lv.5 (see the Q3083 test below).

## Peer / stack evidence

- Evolution stack is realistic: BT2-027 Zudomon already sits on BT1-033 Dolphmon +
  BT1-014 Kokatorimon, and Ryugumon lands on top of it. The resulting stack order
  (`["BT1-014", "BT2-027"]` after the payload leaves) is asserted, not just membership.
- `[When Digivolving]` near-misses: BT1-014 Kokatorimon (Red Lv.4 — right level,
  wrong colour) stays buried, and a SECOND own permanent (BT1-042 LoaderLeomon with
  BT1-037 Gorillamon beneath) is untouched even though `preferInstanceIds` is biased
  towards its Gorillamon. Host scoping is therefore proved against an actively
  preferred alternative, not by absence.
- ＜Decode＞ near-misses: BT1-037 Gorillamon (Blue Lv.4 — right colour, wrong level)
  goes to the trash with the host, and a second own stack's BT1-042 LoaderLeomon
  (Blue Lv.5, perfectly legal but on the wrong host) is refused while being the
  preferred candidate.
- Bounce near-miss: with a Lv.4 paid as the cost, the opponent's Lv.6 Plesiomon is
  out of reach even though it is the preferred pick; only the Lv.3 Gomamon moves.
- Keyword/trait leak: BT2-027 Zudomon standing beside Ryugumon has neither
  [Aquatic] nor ＜Decode＞.

## Catalog discrepancies (reported, not edited)

`cards.json` gives BT19-027 `types: ["Mollusk", "LIBERATOR", "Aquatic"]`. Q3082 says
the `[Rule]` line "always treats it **as if it also has** the [Aquatic] type trait",
which implies [Aquatic] is NOT part of the printed type line — the catalog appears to
have folded the Rule into `types`. The effective behaviour is identical (printed or
granted, the trait is present), so nothing is broken; but it makes the `Rule` /
`GrantStatic` node redundant and unobservable on its own. Same pattern on BT19-028
and on BT19-019 (lane 8 reported it there too).
