## Defects

None found in `BT19-061.ts`. The module was re-derived from the printed text and every node
holds up: the trait tests are exact (`match: "trait"`), the DigiXros alias is the correct
`grant: "name"` + `digiXrosOnly: true` form (not the dead `grant: "nameForDigiXros"`), the
inherited clause uses `YourTurn` rather than `Static`, and the alternate route's cost and
gate match the printing. The IR is UNCHANGED, so no `effects.json` resync is needed for this card.

The pre-existing test was replaced: it asserted the IR shape plus three shallow behaviours and
left the digivolve routes, both reveal triggers' endpoints, the On Deletion placement, the
DigiXros alias and Q3118/Q3119 entirely unproved.

  "maximise" half has no larger count to exercise here.
