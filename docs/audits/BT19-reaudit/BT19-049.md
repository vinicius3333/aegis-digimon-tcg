## Defects fixed

**`BT19-049.ts:51` — removed `suspended: false` from the Suspend target filter.** The printed
clause says "Suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon" with no freshness qualifier, and an
already-suspended Digimon is a legal choice (comprehensive 15-15-5-1/-3; KB Q845, Q1219): the
choice is legal and the suspension simply does nothing. The prior audit's filter narrowed the
candidate set and would have forced the effect onto a fresh peer. Coordinator decision recorded
in `REVIEW-NOTES.md`; lanes 17 and 20 made the same reversal on BT19-038, 044 and 046. Every
other filter axis (`controller: "opponent"`, `kind: ["Digimon"]`) is unchanged.

**The IR changed, so `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` is stale for this card and
`BT19-catalog-sync.test.ts` will fail until the coordinator resyncs.**

The test file was rebuilt: the previous suite proved the optional decline and the whole
inherited clause through injected timing (`advance.fireForPermanent`), and it proved the
once-per-turn limit by firing the same timing twice rather than through real attacks and a
real next turn. It also used a Digimon with printed text (BT19-044) as the digivolution base,
which mixes another card's effects into the endpoints.

## Remaining gaps

- The same-turn refusal needs the host to attack twice; no public intent unsuspends an
  attacker, so `advance.verb.unsuspend` (a production primitive, not injected timing) is used
  between the two real `attack` intents. The trigger itself is never injected.
