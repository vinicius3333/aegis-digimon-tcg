## Defects fixed locally

**`match: "name"` used for bracketed exact `[Name]` references** (the trap logged
by lanes 3 and 5). Both the host filter `[Impmon]` and the target `[Beelzemon]`
used the substring form. `interpreter/matching/definition.ts:361` implements
`match: "name"` as `names.some((name) => name.includes(token))`, so the printed
clause would have accepted:

- **Blimpmon** (BT20-049) as "1 of your [Impmon]" — "Blimpmon" contains "impmon";
- **Impmon (X Antibody)** (BT12-073) as the host;
- **Beelzemon (X Antibody)** (BT12-085) and **Beelzemon: Blast Mode** (BT19-074)
  as the digivolution target.

Fixed to `match: "nameExact"` on both refs, with a header comment naming the
consequence. All four near misses are now behavioural tests: the clause is not
offered and the intent is rejected for the two hosts, and the near-miss target
never becomes the new top card and stays in hand.

`abortOnDecline: true` sits on the sole action of the [Main] effect and is dead
(lanes 4/16). Per the brief it was left in place rather than churned out.
