The prior audit narrowed the placement cost to `from: ["hand"]` with `filter.zone: "hand"`.

behaviourally two ways:

  Device in the trash, nothing is placed and nothing is drawn.

  `zone: "hand"` fails that test (plus the IR shape test). Module restored.

## `deleteOwn` versus `kind: "trash"` — checked, kept

The sibling idiom for "by trashing N of your Option cards in the battle area" is
`cost.kind: "trash"` with `filter.zone: "battleArea"` (EX8-052's inherited clause), which is
what this card's wording suggests. That path ends in
`ctx.fx.trash(topInstanceIds(...))` (`interpreter/costs.ts:1230-1233`) and never reaches
`deletePermanent`, so it does **not** fire `EffectTiming.WhenTrashedFromBattleArea` — the timing
the Device cycle's printed "When this card is trashed in your battle area, …" clauses hang on
(`BT19-095.ts` `trigger: "whenTrashedFromBattleArea"`). The only firing site is inside
`deletePermanent` when `cause === "byEffect"` (`engine/effects/primitives.ts:3852-3858`).

BT19-086's `deleteOwn` is therefore the shape that makes the printed cross-card interaction
work, and the test proves it: after the cost, BT19-095 Knight Device's grant (+4000 DP and
＜Piercing＞) has landed on the controller's Digimon, pinned by permanent identity. The IR was
left unchanged. If the engine ever routes battle-area Option trashing through `fx.trash`, the
`kind: "trash"` idiom becomes the right one and EX8-052 is the card to re-check first — flagged
for the coordinator as a cross-card consistency question, not a defect in this card.
