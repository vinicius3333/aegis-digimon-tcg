## Card summary

Catalog: White Digimon, **no level** (`level` absent), Unknown form / attribute / type,
play cost 3, DP 1000, `evoCosts: []`.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Security] You may play 1 Digimon card with 2000 DP or less from your hand without
   paying the cost.`
2. `[Main] By suspending this Digimon, 1 of your Digimon may digivolve into a Digimon card
   in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 2.`
3. `[All Turns] This Digimon can't attack or block.`
4. `[On Deletion] Place this card on top of your security stack.`

## Rules read

- White (`comprehensive 2-4-2`): an ordinary colour, no special play rule.
- **No level** (`comprehensive 2-9-2`): "Cards that have no level displayed, or with 'Lv.－'
  shown, are treated as having no level." Consequences proved below: the battle-area rule
  sweep for level-2-or-lower Digimon never reaches it, and no ordinary digivolution route
  targets it (nothing is "one level higher" than no level) — it only ever reaches the board
  through a play (its own play cost, or the `[Security]` clause of another card).
- `CR 13-1-8-4`: "A card revealed from a security check is placed in the trash unless it
  belongs to an area."
- `CR 14-2-3`: "Security Digimon aren't deleted even when they lose a battle." This decides
  the interaction below.

## Clause -> test -> IR

- **Clause 1** -> `effects[Security].actions[0]` `PlayWithoutCost`, `from: ["hand"]`,
  `payCost: false`, `optional: true`, filter `controller: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"],
  dp: { op: "lte", value: 2000 }`.
  - The `dp` OBJECT form is read for loose hand cards against the PRINTED DP
    (`interpreter/matching/definition.ts:207`), so this is **not** an instance of the lane-6
    dropped-`playCost`-object trap. Verified by source and by behaviour.
  - `plays a 2000-DP-or-less Digimon free from the security check, then battles the attacker`:
    real opponent turn through `startTurnLoop` + `waitForMainPhase(1)` + a public `attack`
    intent. `BT1-012` Biyomon (2000 DP) is played; `BT1-009` Monodramon (3000 DP) is never a
    candidate and stays in hand. "Without paying the cost" is read at the exact instant of the
    play via the `onEvent` hook (`memoryAtPlay === memoryBeforeCheck`), not after the turn
    hand-off. The 1000 DP attacker then ties the 1000 DP Security Digimon and is deleted.
- **Clause 2** -> `effects[Main].actions[0]` `Digivolve`, `from: ["hand"]`, `payCost: true`,
  `reduceCost: 2`, `cost: { kind: "suspend", target.isSelf }`.
  - `suspends itself to digivolve another Digimon from hand for 2 less, keeping the base
    stack`: `activateEffect` through the public intent. Calumon ends suspended (the cost was
    really paid); the base `BT1-014` Kokatorimon (Lv.4, already carrying `BT1-009` as a
    digivolution card) becomes `BT1-024` MetalTyrannomon, whose printed Lv.4 route costs 3 —
    reduced by 2 it costs 1, so memory 3 -> 2. Stack asserted by instance id bottom-first
    (`[older, base]`), `currentDP` 10000, one bonus draw, no pending decision, no loud gap.
  - `abortOnDecline: true` sits on the sole action and is dead (lanes 4/8 finding); left
    untouched rather than churning the IR.
- **Clause 3** -> `effects[AllTurns].actions[0]` `Restrict`, `restriction: "attackOrBlock"`,
  `duration: "permanent"`. Trigger is `AllTurns`, not `Static` — the printed marker is
  `[All Turns]`.
  - `attackOrBlock` is expanded by the interpreter into the two enforced restrictions
    `attack` and `block` (`interpreter/actions/restrictions.ts:108`); `observe().isRestricted`
    is asserted on both, and on an unrestricted peer for contrast.
  - `refuses a real attack declaration ...`: the public `attack` intent is rejected, security
    untouched, Calumon unsuspended; the same board's `BT1-009` then attacks successfully.
  - `refuses a real block declaration in the opponent's open block window`: a real ＜Blocker＞
    peer (`EX6-012`) is seeded so the window genuinely opens; `declareBlock` with Calumon is
    refused, then the same window accepts the peer and the block redirects the attack away
    from security.
- **Clause 4** -> `effects[OnDeletion].actions[0]` `SecurityManipulation`,
  `op: "placeAsSecurity"`, `controller: "mine"`, `toTop: true`, **no `source`**.
  - `returns to the top of its owner's security stack when deleted in a real opponent-turn
    battle`: Calumon is suspended the only way the card allows (paying its own `[Main]` cost)
    on its controller's turn, stays suspended into the opponent's real turn, and is attacked
    by a 5000 DP Digimon. Security becomes `[calumon, previousTop, ...]` by instance id, and
    the card is never in the trash.

## Defects found and fixed locally

**`[On Deletion]` placed nothing when Calumon was deleted from the battle area.** The action
carried `source: { filter: { isSelfRef: true }, count: 1, isSelf: true }`. That branch of
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/security.ts` resolves PERMANENTS
(`resolvePermanentTargets` at :375, then `topInstanceIds`; `if (ids.length === 0) return`),
and the permanent is already gone by the time an `[On Deletion]` effect resolves, so the card
went to the trash instead. The self form is `source` OMITTED (`security.ts:363-370`:
`ctx.source.permanent()?.topCard?.instanceId ?? ctx.source.instanceId`), which survives the
deletion — the idiom BT2-040 Ophanimon uses for the identical printed sentence. Fixed.

## Engine behaviour confirmed correct (initial test expectation was wrong)

A Security Digimon that loses its battle does **not** trigger `[On Deletion]`. `CR 14-2-3`
says Security Digimon aren't deleted even when they lose, and `CR 13-1-8-4` sends the checked
card to the trash instead; `battleSecurityDigimon`
(`apps/api/src/engine/security/securityCheck.ts:366-405`) implements exactly that and
documents both rules. The Q6243 test now asserts this: after the battle the security stack is
the two untouched cards and Calumon is in the trash — it does **not** come back on top. No red
is retained; the engine is right.
