## Defects fixed locally (IR changed — resync needed)

1. The `Then, ＜Save＞` effect carried no `keywords: [{ keyword: "Save" }]`, so the
   registration normalizer
   (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/normalize.ts`,
   `withSavePlacementDefaults`) never defaulted its `PlaceUnder` actions to
   `position: "bottom"`. Both placements landed directly beneath the Tamer instead of at the
   stack bottom (comprehensive 4-3-2). Confirmed red before the fix: removing the `keywords`
   block again fails `compiles every printed clause` and the three-card stack-order
   assertion.

`BT19-022.ts` also now exports `compiled` so the test can pin the IR. The pre-existing
hand-fix (spurious `colors: ["Blue"]` removed, `underFilter` added) was re-checked and is
correct.

## Remaining gaps

- The [On Deletion] window is opened with `advance.verb.deletePermanent` (the production
  deletion primitive) rather than by losing a battle. ＜Blocker＞ is proved through real
  `attack` / `declareBlock` intents, so the card's combat path is covered separately.
- "under ANY of your Tamers" is proved with one Tamer, and with a second Tamer only through
  BT19-020's sibling test; there is no test here that a player may choose between two Tamers
  for the trash placement.

## Catalog discrepancies

`effectText` reads "1 Digimon card with the [Blue Flare] from your trash"; the printed card
reads "with the [Blue Flare] trait". The catalog string drops the word "trait". Reported, not
edited. The IR follows the official wording with `match: "trait"` (an exact trait test), and
the peer test pins that a [Blue Flare] TAMER (BT19-081 Kiriha Aonuma) is not a candidate.
