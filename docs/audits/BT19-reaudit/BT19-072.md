# BT19-072 LordKnightmon — re-audit (lane 30)

## Card

Purple/Black Lv6 Mega, Virus, Holy Warrior / Royal Knight. Play cost 11, DP 11000, evolution
costs: Black Lv5 for 3, Purple Lv5 for 3. No inherited text.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may play 1 level 4 or lower Digimon card from your trash
   without paying the cost.`
2. `[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may switch the
   attack target to 1 of your Digimon with the [Royal Knight] trait.`

## Rulings

| Q&A | Question | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q3133 | Can the switch target a Digimon that isn't affected by effects? (A: yes) | "Q3133: switches onto a Royal Knight that isn't affected by effects" |

Q3133 mechanism: the redirect targets the source's OWN side, and
`isPermanentUnaffectable` (`interpreter/matching/permanent.ts:858-867`) returns false whenever
`source.ownerSeat === permanent.controllerSeat`, so an own-side "isn't affected by effects"
grant cannot remove the Digimon from the pool. The test seeds that grant through the
production `restrict` primitive and shows the switch still lands.

Evidence notes:

- The redirect is proved through the real turn loop: my wall is suspended by attacking the
  player on my own turn (a seeded `suspended: true` would stand up at my unsuspend phase), then
  the opponent attacks it on their real turn. First attack: the 9000 attacker is switched into

  wall (5000) is deleted by the 8000 attacker.

  board throughout and is asserted absent from the recorded `chooseTargets` candidate ids.
