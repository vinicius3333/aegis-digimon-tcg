## Card summary

Catalog: White Digimon, **no level**, form `D-Reaper`, attribute `-`,
type `[Espionage Agent]`, play cost 5, DP 2000, `evoCosts: []`.

Printed clauses:

1. `[On Play] For each digivolution card of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]s, 1 of your
   opponent's Digimon gets -1000 for the turn.`
2. `[Main] Place this Digimon as the bottom digivolution card of 1 of your [Mother D-Reaper]
   without [ADR-01 Jeri] in its digivolution cards.`
3. Inherited: `[Opponent's Turn] When any of your opponent's Digimon attack, you may play 1
   [ADR-01 Jeri] from this Digimon's digivolution cards without paying the cost. If you
   played, you may change the attack target to the Digimon played by this effect.`

## Rules read

- White (`comprehensive 2-4-2`): an ordinary colour, no special play rule.
- **No level** (`comprehensive 2-9-2`). This is what makes the prior audit's host claim work:
  `EX2-007 Mother D-Reaper` is a **Digi-Egg card** with a printed DP (15000) and no level. It
  is not a level-2-or-lower Digimon, so the battle-area rule sweep never removes it. Verified
  behaviourally, not assumed — a Digi-Egg without DP/level seeded on the battle area is swept
  before the first intent (lane 28 finding), and this one is not.

## Clause -> test -> IR

- **Clause 1** -> `effects[OnPlay].actions[0]` `ModifyDP`, `amount: -1000`,
  `duration: "forTheTurn"`, `target` `controller: "opponent", kind: ["Digimon"], count: 1`,
  `scaling: { per: 1, unit: "digivolutionCardsOfFiltered", filter: { controller: "mine",
  nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Mother D-Reaper"], match: "nameExact" }] } }`.
  - `subtracts 1000 per digivolution card of its own controller's [Mother D-Reaper]`:
    public `playCard`. Controller's Mother carries 2 cards -> the 6000 DP victim ends at
    exactly 4000. Two near misses are on the board at once: a same-controller `BT1-014` with a
    THREE-card stack that is not named `[Mother D-Reaper]`, and the **opponent's** own
    `EX2-007` with a FOUR-card stack ("1 of **your** [Mother D-Reaper]s"). Neither shifts the
    result.
  - `changes nothing when the controller's [Mother D-Reaper] has no digivolution cards`:
    the victim stays at 6000 and memory 10 -> 5.
- **Clause 2** -> `effects[Main].actions[0]` `PlaceUnder`, `targetIsPermanent: true`,
  `position: "bottom"`, `target.isSelf`, `underFilter` `controller: "mine"`,
  `nameOrTrait` `[Mother D-Reaper]` `nameExact`, `excludeCardsNamed: ["ADR-01 Jeri"]`.
  - `places itself at the stack bottom of the Mother that has no [ADR-01 Jeri] under it`:
    public `activateEffect`. Two Mothers are on the board; the blocked one carries `EX2-049`,

    open Mother's stack becomes `[jeri, searcher]` — bottom-first, so Jeri really is the
    bottom digivolution card. Jeri's original permanent is gone from the battle area and
    nothing hit the trash.
  - `stays on the battle area when every [Mother D-Reaper] already has an [ADR-01 Jeri] under
    it`: no legal host, so Jeri stays a permanent and the blocked Mother's stack is unchanged.
- **Clause 3 (inherited)** -> `effects[OpponentsTurn]`, `isInherited: true`, one `SubTrigger`
  on `whenOpponentAttacks` with `sourceFilter` `controller: "opponent", kind: ["Digimon"]`,
  then `PlayWithoutCost` (`fromOwnDigivolutionStack: true`, `payCost: false`,
  `optional: true`, `bindResultAs: "playedJeri"`, target `[ADR-01 Jeri]` `nameExact`) and
  `RedirectAttack` (`optional: true`, `target.filter.boundRef: "playedJeri"`).
  - `installs the watcher under its [Mother D-Reaper] host and redirects a real opponent

  - `does not fire for its own controller's attack on its own turn`: both gates
    (`[Opponent's Turn]` and "your opponent's Digimon") fail; the Mother's stack still holds
    the buried instance and no `cardPlayed` event names BT19-078.

## Defects found and fixed locally

**Bracketed exact names used the substring gate.** `[Mother D-Reaper]` (in the `[Main]`
`underFilter` and in the `[On Play]` scaling filter) and `[ADR-01 Jeri]` (in the inherited
play target) all carried `match: "name"`, which is the substring mode. Changed to
`nameExact`, per the lane-3 / BT19-020 finding. No live near-miss exists today (nothing is
named `*Mother D-Reaper*` or `*ADR-01 Jeri*` without being exactly that), so this is
correctness hardening; it is asserted structurally.

## Test defects fixed (both named in the assignment)

1. **Injected timing removed.** The old test used
   `advance(s.engine).fireSubTrigger("whenOpponentAttacks", ...)` and set
   `s.state.turnSeat = 1` directly in three places. Both are injected state and earn no
   behavioural credit. Every opponent-turn case now runs through
   `startTurnLoop()` + `waitForMainPhase(1)` + a public `attack` intent, closed with
   `surrender` and `await loop`.
2. **Digi-Egg seeded in security removed.** The old redirect test seeded
   `security: ["BT1-001"]` — a Digi-Egg. Security and deck are now inert main-deck Digimon
   only (`BT1-009` / `BT1-013` / `BT1-014`).

## Peer / stack proof

- Exact-name near misses on both sides of the `[On Play]` scaling: a same-controller
  non-Mother permanent with a deeper stack, and an opponent-controlled Mother with a deeper
  stack still.
- `excludeCardsNamed` peer: `EX2-049`, a different card number with the same printed name,
  correctly blocks its host while the clean Mother accepts the placement.
- Realistic stack throughout: the inherited clause is exercised from INSIDE a real host's
  digivolution cards, and an unexpected but genuine cross-card interaction is asserted — the
  host `EX2-007`'s own "reduce its play cost by 1 for each of this Digimon's digivolution
  cards" fires on BT19-078's `[D-Reaper]` form, so the 5-cost play really costs 3 (memory
  10 -> 7).
