## Clause -> test -> IR

Clause 1 -> `effects[0]`: `trigger: "EndOfYourTurn"`, `isInherited: true`,
`frequency: "OncePerTurn"`, one mandatory `Return` action to `hand` with filter
`zone: "trash", controller: "mine", kind: ["Option"]` and
`nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Plug-In"], match: "name" }]` — `name` is the SUBSTRING
mode, which is what "with [Plug-In] in its name" requires (`nameExact` would be wrong).

Tests in `apps/api/src/cards/BT19/BT19-003.test.ts`:

- `returns exactly one Plug-In Option from trash at end of turn and leaves the
  non-Plug-In peer` — seeded stack driven by `advance(...).runTurn(0)`: one P-095
  returns, the second P-095 and the non-Plug-In Option BT1-102 stay in trash, no
  pending decision.
- `carries the inherited return through the real Digi-Egg route and resets once per
  turn on the next own turn` — full lifecycle, all public intents, **no injected
  timing** (this replaces the previous `advance(...).fire(EffectTiming.OnEndTurn, …)`
  proof, which earned no behavioural credit):
  - `hatchEgg` -> BT19-003 in breeding, egg deck empty.
  - `digivolve` the Yellow Lv3 BT1-045 onto it in breeding (Yellow Lv2, cost 0);
    `breeding.stack` is exactly the egg instance and the battle area is empty.
  - Turn 1 ends while the stack is still in breeding: trash length and hand are
    unchanged, so the clause did not fire from breeding.
  - Turn 3 `moveFromBreeding` carries the stack into the battle area (carrier stack
    is the egg instance id); at that turn's NATURAL end exactly one P-095 arrives in
    hand (asserted as a card that was not in hand before the turn ended), and the
    non-Plug-In BT1-102 instance is still in the trash — the near-miss peer.
  - Turn 5, through the real turn loop: the Once Per Turn guard has reset and the
    second P-095 returns, leaving only BT1-102 in trash. `pendingDecision` undefined.

The "once per turn" half is proved on both sides through real turn ends: exactly one
copy on turn 3 with two eligible copies in trash, and one more on the next own turn.
