## Clause -> test -> IR

- **Clause 1 ＜Material Save 1＞** -> `effects[0]` `trigger: "Static"`,
  `keywords: [{ keyword: "MaterialSave", amount: 1 }]`.
  Proved by `observe().keywordAmount(perm, "MaterialSave") === 1` plus the real
  battle deletion that relocates BT19-058 under the Tamer while the non-material
  BT1-014 digivolution card goes to the trash (comprehensive 16-21-1: only cards
  named in the top card's own DigiXros requirement are eligible).
- **Clause 2 ＜De-Digivolve1＞** -> `effects[1]/[2]` action 0, `kind: "DeDigivolve"`,
  `amount: 1`, `filter { controller: "opponent", kind: ["Digimon"] }`.
  Proved on the [When Digivolving] timing (normal Black Lv.4 route) and on the
  [On Play] timing (both the 2-material and the 1-material DigiXros): the top card
  is trashed by instance id and the card beneath becomes the top.
- **Clause 3 conditional deletion** -> `effects[1]/[2]` action 1, `kind: "Delete"`,
  `optional: true`, `condition { kind: "digiXrosCount", minimum: 2 }`,
  `filter { controller: "any", kind: ["Digimon","Tamer"], playCostLte: 3 }`.
  Three-way proof: fires on a 2-material DigiXros and takes the controller's own
  cost-2 Digimon (Q3124) while leaving the cost-5 BT19-020 peer alone; does NOT
  fire on a 1-material DigiXros (7-2-2-4 allows any non-zero number of materials);
  does NOT fire on a normal digivolve even though a matching cost-3 target is on
  the board.
- **Clause 4 [On Deletion] from under Tamers** -> `effects[3]`,
  `PlayWithoutCost`, `from: ["underMyTamers"]`, `payCost: false`, `optional: true`,
  `levelComparison { op: "lte", value: 4 }`,
  `nameOrTrait [{ tokens: ["Knightmon"], match: "text" }]`.
  Proved by a real battle loss against a seeded suspended 20000 DP wall. Both
  near-misses under the same Tamer stay put: BT19-059 DeadlyAxemon (Lv.4, no
  "Knightmon" anywhere in its card text) and BT18-069 Knightmon ("Knightmon" in
  text, but Lv.5). The declined branch has its own test.
- **Clause 5 [DigiXros -2]** -> `digiXrosRequirementFor("BT19-063")`
  `[{ materials: [{ names: ["SkullKnightmon"] }, { names: ["DeadlyAxemon"] }], count: 2 }]`
  (hand-authored in `packages/shared/src/effects/data.ts:1877`; the module's own
  `digiXrosRequirement` header is identical).
  `count` is the PER-MATERIAL reduction (7-2-2-1), so 8 - 2x2 = 4 with two
  materials and 8 - 2 = 6 with one; both memory deltas are asserted. Near-miss
  materials are rejected as `invalid-material`: BT1-014 (no name match) and
  BT19-063 itself (contains "Knightmon" but is not the printed [SkullKnightmon]).
- **Clause 6 inherited [On Deletion] from trash** -> `effects[4]`,
  `isInherited: true`, `from: ["trash"]`.
  Proved under a REAL host: BT1-014 carries BT19-063 as its single digivolution
  card and loses a real battle. The trash holds the eligible BT19-058 plus the two
  near-misses (BT19-059 Lv.4-no-Knightmon, BT18-069 Knightmon-but-Lv.5); only
  BT19-058 leaves the trash, and the deleted host and its digivolution card join
  it there.
