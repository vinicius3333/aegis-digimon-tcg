## Defect fixed locally

The `[All Turns]` clause was modelled as a **bare `AllTurns` action** — `optional: true`,
`DigiXrosMaterialZoneExpansion` with a suspend cost — with no `Replacement` wrapper and no
`sourceFilter`. Consequence, observed: on any board with BT19-079 the clause offers its
"by suspending this Tamer" cost at every `[All Turns]` window, and accepting it suspends the
Tamer with no DigiXros in sight. In the harness this showed up as the Tamer already suspended
before the first play intent in **six** separate fixtures (including one that plays a DigiXros
card entirely from hand and never touches the Tamer).

Fix: adopt the shape its two printed siblings already use — BT19-087 Nene Amano and EX10-064,
both "when a … Digimon with DigiXros requirements would be played, by suspending this Tamer …":
`Replacement { event: "wouldBePlayed", mode: "instead", optional: true }` with a `sourceFilter`
carrying `kind: ["Digimon"]`, the `[Xros Heart]` trait token (`match: "trait"`, exact — the
printed wording is "with the … trait") and `hasDigiXrosRequirement: true`. `zones` moved from
`digivolutionCards` to `underTamers` to match the printed "under your Tamers" and both peers
(`interpreter/actions/play.ts:649-662` accepts either token for the under-Tamer quota, so this
is a fidelity change, not a behaviour change), and `duration` from `untilOpponentTurnEnd` to
the peers' `forTheTurn`.

The registry entry in `packages/shared/src/cards/zoneExpanders.ts` was **not** touched (it is
outside this lane's allowed edits and was already correct: `[Xros Heart]` gate, `underTamerMax`
100, unrestricted host scope).
