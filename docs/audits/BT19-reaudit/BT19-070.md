## Defects fixed locally

1. **The "by deleting 1 of your Digimon" condition was mandatory.** Both the
   `OnPlay` and `WhenDigivolving` first actions carried `abortOnDecline: true` with
   no `optional: true`. The decline prompt is only raised for an optional cost
   (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:854-861`), so the
   sacrifice was forced. Comprehensive 15-7-4 says the controller chooses, and
   Q3131 makes the stakes concrete: a lone Kimeramon had no choice but to delete
   itself on entry. Fix: `optional: true` on both. Proof it mattered: without it,
   `declining the 'by deleting' condition …` and the IR test fail
   (2 failed | 12 passed).
2. **`[Machinedramon]` used the substring name gate.** `match: "name"` is the
   substring matcher; a bracketed printed name is exact. Fix: `match: "nameExact"`.
   This one is structural only — see the gap below.

## Remaining gaps

- The `nameExact` fix for `[Machinedramon]` cannot be shown behaviourally: every
  card in the catalog whose name contains "Machinedramon" is named exactly
  "Machinedramon" (11 printings), so no near-miss peer exists to separate the two
  matchers. The change is made on the printed-text rule, and the IR test pins it.
- The module's own `digiXrosRequirement` field is dead for this card:
  `digiXrosRequirementFor` prefers `DIGIXROS_REQUIREMENT_OVERRIDES`. The two are
  equivalent today, but a future edit to the module field alone would have no
  effect. Shared data is out of lane scope, so this is reported, not reconciled.
- The three deletions are proved to be one per named level, but not that the
  opponent chooses nothing and the controller picks each target independently
  beyond what `preferInstanceIds` pins.
