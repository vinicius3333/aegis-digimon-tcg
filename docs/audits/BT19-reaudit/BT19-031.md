Rookie, Data, types `Major` + `Xros Heart`, play cost 4, DP 1000, evoCosts
Yellow Lv2 / Red Lv2 at 1 memory, rarity C, max 4 per deck.

Printed clauses:

1. `[Digivolve]Lv.2 w/[Xros Heart] trait: Cost 0` — alternate route by trait.
2. `＜Decoy ([Xros Heart] trait)＞` — when another of your [Xros Heart] Digimon
   would be deleted by an opponent's effect, you may delete this Digimon to
   prevent 1 of those deletions.
3. `[On Deletion] You may play 1 [ShootingStarmon] from under your Tamers without
   paying the cost. Then, place 1 [Starmons] and 1 [Pickmons] from your trash as
   that Digimon's bottom digivolution cards.`
4. Inherited: `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has the
   [Xros Heart] trait, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -2000 DP for the turn.`

## Clause → test → IR

- **Clause 1** → `compiled.digivolutionRequirement`
  `[{ level: 2, traits: ["Xros Heart"], cost: 0, isAlternate: true }]`, also
  asserted through `digivolutionRequirementsFor("BT19-031")`.
  - Alternate route, real Digi-Egg lifecycle: `hatchEgg` takes BT19-001 Pickmons
    (Red Lv2, [Xros Heart]) off the egg deck in the production Breeding window,
    then `digivolve` with `useAlternateCost: true` in the open Main phase.
    Endpoints: breeding top card BT19-031, stack `[eggInstanceId]`, DP 1000,
    **memory unchanged** (cost 0), hand emptied, egg deck empty.
  - Trait near-miss: BT1-001 Yokomon (Red Lv2, **no** [Xros Heart]) with the same
    `useAlternateCost: true` request falls back to the printed Red route and
    costs 1 (memory 5 → 4), plus the digivolution bonus draw. Only the memory
    delta discriminates the two routes (worker-brief trap, lane 9/021).
  - Illegal source: BT1-003 Upamon (Blue Lv2) is refused even with
    `useAlternateCost: true`; breeding top card, stack, memory and hand unchanged.
- **Clause 2** → `effects[0]` `trigger: "Static"`,
  `keywords: [{ keyword: "Decoy", raw: "＜Decoy ([Xros Heart] trait)＞" }]`.
  The parenthetical scope is derived from the printed text by
  `decoySpecFromText` (`apps/api/src/engine/combat/keywords.ts:154`) and matched
  against the endangered card's types by `decoySpecMatches`, so no IR
  `traitFilter` is needed — but the behaviour is proved, not assumed:
  - Positive: an opponent-seat effect resolution (`enterEffectResolution(1, …)`)
    deleting BT19-033 Dorulumon ([Xros Heart]) is prevented; the Starmons dies
    instead and lands in the trash by instance id.
  - Trait near-miss: the same board with BT19-030 Renamon (Yellow, Beastkin, no
    [Xros Heart]) as the endangered peer — Renamon dies, the Starmons survives.
  - Negatives: the controller's **own** effect resolution (§16-18-1) and a
    `byBattle` deletion never activate it.
- **Clause 3** → `effects[1]` `OnDeletion`:
  `PlayWithoutCost` (`optional: true`, `abortOnDecline: true`, `payCost: false`,
  `from: ["digivolutionCardsUnderTamers"]`, `bindResultAs`), then two
  `PlaceUnder` actions with `position: "bottom"` and
  `underFilter.boundRef: "playedShootingStarmon"`.
  - Full positive with three near-misses on one board: BT10-034 Dorulumon under
    the same Tamer is not played, the opponent's Tamer (with its own BT19-035) is
    untouched, and a BT19-035 ShootingStarmon in the **trash** does not answer the
    exact `[Starmons]`/`[Pickmons]` gates. Endpoints by instance id: the played
    permanent is the Tamer's ShootingStarmon, its stack is
    `[pickmons, deletedStarmons]` (bottom-most first — the second placement lands
    under the first), and the trash holds only the near-miss.
  - Q3088 partial case as tabled above.
  - A distinct trash `[Starmons]` (BT10-029) is placed just as well as the
    deleted copy, biased with `preferInstanceIds`; the placed card's `nameEn` is
    asserted to be exactly `Starmons`.
  - Decline: with `autoDeclineOptional`, `abortOnDecline: true` on the
    non-final first action stops the two placements — Tamer stack intact, the
    Pickmons stays in the trash, no permanent played.
  - Opponent-only source: the sole ShootingStarmon under the **opponent's** Tamer
    is not reachable ("your Tamers"), so nothing happens.
- **Clause 4** → `effects[2]` `WhenAttacking`, `isInherited: true`,
  `frequency: "OncePerTurn"`, `ModifyDP -2000` `duration: "forTheTurn"` on
  `controller: "opponent"`, gated by `condition.kind: "selfHasTrait"`
  (`[Xros Heart]`).
  - Realistic evolution stack built by a public intent: BT19-031 digivolves into
    BT19-033 Dorulumon (Yellow Lv4 route, memory 5 → 2, bonus draw asserted, stack
    identity by instance id). The host then attacks the player inside the
    production turn loop and exactly one of the two opposing Digimon drops by
    2000; the other is untouched.
  - Once per turn, same turn: a second `[When Attacking]` window on the same host
    adds nothing. **Structural only** — no public intent gives a suspended
    attacker a second attack — so this leg claims no behavioural credit.
  - Reset: the real turn boundary (seat 1's whole turn driven through the loop)
    both expires "for the turn" (both opposing DP back to base) and rearms the
    counter; a second real attack on seat 0's next turn reduces one Digimon again.
  - Trait near-miss host: BT19-031 under BT19-037 Taomon (Yellow Lv5 Wizard, no
    [Xros Heart]) attacking for real reduces nothing.

## Defects found and fixed locally

1. **Fixture defect (assigned).** The previous test seeded `security: ["BT1-001"]`
   — a Digi-Egg in security, forbidden by the brief. The whole file was rewritten;
   security now uses inert main-deck Digimon (BT1-010/BT1-011/BT1-013/BT1-014) and
   Digi-Eggs appear only in `eggDeck` and (as [Pickmons] trash fodder) in `trash`.
2. **IR defect.** Five bracketed exact-name gates used the substring
   `match: "name"`: `[ShootingStarmon]` on the `PlayWithoutCost` target,
   `[Starmons]` and `[Pickmons]` on the two `PlaceUnder` targets, and the two
   `underFilter` name refs. All changed to `nameExact` (worker-brief trap, lanes
   3/007 and 3/009). No live catalog card is a strict substring-not-exact match
   for these tokens today, so this is a correctness fix rather than a behaviour
   change; BT19-035's "also treated as [Starmons]" line is DigiXros-only and is
   already excluded by `parsedStaticNameAliases`.
3. `compiled` is now exported so the test can assert the IR, matching the BT19
   house style (BT19-021 and the BT17 exemplars).

Checked and deliberately **not** changed: `trigger: "Static"` on the ＜Decoy＞
effect (a keyword grant, not a printed `[All Turns]` clause) and
`abortOnDecline: true` (live here — it guards two later actions, so it is not the
dead last-action flag from traps 008/016).
