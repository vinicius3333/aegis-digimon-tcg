# EX10 audit: EX10-051 through EX10-074

Audit date: 2026-09-05

The audit was performed one card at a time. For every card, the exact entry
in `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, the direct card module, its
colocated test, and `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>` were read. The
review traced timing, optional choices, target filters, numeric limits,
payment, zone transitions, visibility, stack/inherited behavior, and
once-per-turn identity where applicable. The local KB query completed for all
24 IDs.

The final EX10-064 focused suite passes `11/11` with `--maxWorkers=2`, including
the effect-play replacement path, independent optional choices, and a compiled
mutation check. The DigiXros material-cap correction for EX10-055, EX10-056,
and EX10-058 first produced the expected `3` red third-material cases and then
`28` green focused cases (`/tmp/ex10-digixros-cap-red.log` and
`/tmp/ex10-digixros-cap-green.log`). The current candidate gates cover 76 collection files /
604 tests, 49 affected mechanism and peer files / 1,140 tests, and a green all-package typecheck,
recorded in `/tmp/ex10-collection-final-candidate.log`,
`/tmp/ex10-mechanisms-final-candidate.log`, and `/tmp/ex10-typecheck-final-candidate.log`.
Independent review accepted the covered behavior. The collection report records final
integrated-tree validation and delivery through PR #4714.

## Shared seam

EX10-059 exposed a real visibility defect. The ordinary `PlaceUnder` runner
passed all opposing-hand instance IDs as explicit visible candidates to
`pickLoose`, which serialized card identities in the decision payload. The
smallest fix adds an optional typed `blind` flag to `PlaceUnderAction` and
omits explicit visible candidates when it is set. EX10-059 opts into that flag
for both On Play and When Digivolving. Normal public searches retain their
existing visibility behavior.

The blind test reproduces the client-facing payload and asserts that the
deciding seat is the effect owner (seat 0), candidate instance IDs remain
selectable, `visibleInstanceIds` contain only those opaque IDs, and
`visibleCards` is empty. The public host decision occurs before the hidden-hand
decision; it exposes only public host information and does not reveal the hand
choice.

EX10-058 Q5160 required a narrow preflight seam. `PlayWithoutCost` now permits
an optional trash play to proceed when its payable digivolution-card trash cost
can itself create a matching post-payment trash candidate. The resolver then
rebuilds the trash pool after payment. Production effect-window deferral keeps
the discarded source's inherited watcher from activating after the source has
already been played out of trash; cards that remain in trash retain their own
normal discard-window eligibility.

The DigiXros preparation seam resolves matching `wouldBePlayed` `instead`
replacements at the continuous timing window, before effect-play material
selection. Static zone-expander fallback remains available for sources without
a handled compiled replacement. Each temporary expansion carries the pending
play instance ID, so nested or adjacent plays cannot consume another play's
grant; cleanup removes only the completed play's temporary entries. EX10-064's
two-copy test accepts one replacement and declines the other, proving optional
activation and per-copy quota behavior.

The EX10-055/056/058 DigiXros cap correction enforces the printed maximum of
two materials for their two-card recipes. A temporary third-material mutation
was red in `3` focused cases, and the corrected suites were green in `28`
cases, recorded in `/tmp/ex10-digixros-cap-red.log` and
`/tmp/ex10-digixros-cap-green.log`.
