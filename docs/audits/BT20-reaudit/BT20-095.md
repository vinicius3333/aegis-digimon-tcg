# BT20-095 — Fellowship of Hope's Keepers

## Contract and source reconciliation

The catalog record at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` identifies BT20-095 as a black Option with play cost 3 and the X Antibody and Chronicle traits. Its printed contract is:

- `[Main]` reveal the top 3 cards, add 1 Chronicle-trait card among them to hand, return the rest to the top or bottom of the deck, then place this Option in the battle area.
- `[All Turns]` when any of your Chronicle-trait Digimon are deleted, activate Delay.
- Delay: by moving your level-3-or-higher Chronicle-trait Digimon from the breeding area to the battle area, it may digivolve into a Chronicle-trait Digimon card in hand or trash without paying the cost.
- `[Security]` you may play 1 Chronicle-trait card with play cost 5 or less from hand or trash without paying the cost, then place this card in the battle area.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-095` returns the 2025-04-18 erratum: the breeding-area cost action no longer requires the moved Digimon itself to have the Chronicle trait; only the destination Digimon card must be Chronicle. No additional Q&A, restriction, or card-specific ruling is returned.

The catalog itself proves that a non-Digimon Chronicle card exists: BT20-095 is an Option with the Chronicle trait. Therefore, the Security `PlayWithoutCost` target must constrain `kind: ["Digimon"]`; “play” cannot select a Chronicle Option. All Chronicle references are trait references (`match: "trait"`), not card-name references, and no exact-name conversion is applicable.

## Defect corrected

The prior Security filter around `BT20-095.ts:86-93` matched Chronicle by trait and play cost but omitted card kind. Since BT20-095 itself is a Chronicle Option, the filter was broader than the printed “play” operation and lacked an observable non-Digimon boundary. The corrected IR adds `kind: ["Digimon"]`. The test adds both a structural assertion for the filter and a natural Security scenario with a BT20-095 Option in hand, asserting that the Option remains in hand while the revealed Security card alone enters the battle area.

The existing Delay movement filter remains intentionally broader after the erratum: it accepts a level-3-or-higher non-Chronicle Digimon from breeding and requires Chronicle only on the Digivolution destination. The existing `match: "trait"` mode is correct for every Chronicle clause because the card text explicitly says “Chronicle trait.”
