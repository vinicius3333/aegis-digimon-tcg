# BT20-099 — Singularity of Chaos

## Contract and source reconciliation

The committed BT20 catalog identifies BT20-099 as the white ACCEL Option
Singularity of Chaos (play cost 2, maximum four copies). Its printed contract is:

- While you have a Digimon with `[Chaosmon]` in its name or the `[ACCEL]` trait,
  ignore this card's color requirements.
- `[Main]` You may play one ACCEL-trait Digimon from hand with its play cost
  reduced by 4. Then place this card as the bottom digivolution card of any of
  your Digimon.
- `[Security]` Gain 1 memory and add this card to hand.
- `[End of Opponent's Turn]` If this Digimon has `[Chaosmon]` in its name, trash
  your opponent's top security card and this Digimon gets -30000 DP for the turn.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-099` returns Q4605. It confirms that the
inherited effect can reduce a Chaosmon: Valdur Arm to 0 DP and that this is a
rules deletion which still triggers Partition. No additional BT20-099 ruling
changes the catalog boundaries.

## Implementation trace

`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-099.ts` is compiled IR with one
`registerIrCard("BT20-099", compiled)` registration, `coverage: "full"`, and an
empty residual. The IR represents the color waiver as `name` matching for
Chaosmon and exact `trait` matching for ACCEL. The Main play is own hand,
Digimon, ACCEL trait, optional, cost-paying with reduction 4; the following
`PlaceUnder` is mandatory, own-Digimon scoped, and bottom-positioned. Security
is `GainMemory(+1)` followed by `AddToHandSelf`. The inherited end-of-opponent's-
turn effect trashes the opponent's top security card and applies `ModifyDP -30000`
with `forTheTurn`, both gated by `selfHasNameContaining Chaosmon`.

## Colocated behavioral proof

`BT20-099.test.ts` covers the complete catalog fields and exact printed clauses,
including the security-resident text and the Q&A-relevant Chaosmon name matcher.
Public flows prove:

- an ACCEL Digimon is played from hand for the reduced cost and this Option is
  placed underneath it;
- `[Chaosmon] in its name` accepts BT20-037 `[Chaosmon: Valdur Arm]`, while a
  nonmatching board does not waive White requirements;
- the inherited clause fires on a real seat-1 Main-to-end transition, trashes
  exactly the opponent's top security, applies -30000 during the public
  `OnEndTurn` resolution window, and is marked inherited rather than Security;
- the Security check gains memory and returns the revealed Option to its owner's
  hand; and
- declining the optional ACCEL play still completes the mandatory placement,
  while non-ACCEL cards remain in hand.

The tests contain no injected `EffectTiming`, `fireGlobal`, `fireFor`, or direct
turn-seat mutation. Deck and security fixtures use legal main-deck cards only.
