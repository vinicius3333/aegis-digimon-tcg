## Defects and corrections

The alternate evolution requirement was encoded as `names: ["Seadramon"]`, which uses substring semantics and could admit Seadramon (X Antibody) or other near names. It is corrected to `namesExact: ["Seadramon"]`. The structural test now asserts the exact route and adds near-name negatives through the shared matcher.

Two tests relied on injected `advance.fire` timing: one fired `WhenDigivolving` directly, and another fired the inherited `OnUseAttack` timing twice. Both were removed because existing public tests already cover natural On Play and When Digivolving flows, natural opponent-turn restriction/expiry, and real public attacks with same-turn refusal and next-turn reset. The remaining behavioral proof therefore uses public intents and resolved state only.

No card-specific KB Q&A or errata were returned. The natural tests cover level-3 return boundaries, exact stack qualification, Tamer suspension scope and duration, legal evolution cost/stack identity, and the inclusive inherited hand threshold.
