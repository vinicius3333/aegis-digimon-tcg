# BT20-059 — Gankoomon (X Antibody)

## Evidence

- Catalog: `BT20-059` is a black level-6 Mega/Data Digimon, play cost 13,
  13000 DP, with black and red level-5 evolution routes costing 5. Its
  traits are Holy Warrior, X Antibody, and Royal Knight.
- Printed clauses: alternate `[Gankoomon]: Cost 2`; When Digivolving,
  De-Digivolve 2 one opposing Digimon, then conditionally make all own
  Digimon unaffected by opponent Digimon effects until the end of the
  opponent's turn; Opponent's Turn, own Digimon with Sistermon/Huckmon in
  their names or Royal Knight trait gain Reboot and Blocker; inherited
  Opponent's Turn while this Digimon is Jesmon GX, all own Digimon gain
  Reboot and Blocker.
- Knowledge base: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-059` returns Q4392–Q4397,
  covering the meaning of immunity, choosing/giving effects to immune cards,
  effects already affecting a card, and timing of effects granted to an
  immune Digimon.
