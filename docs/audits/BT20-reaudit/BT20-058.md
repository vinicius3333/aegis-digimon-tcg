play cost 11 or lower and either the Cyborg trait or the Machine trait.

## Exact matching and stack evidence

The trait filter is an exact trait matcher, not a substring or kind match. The
positive source cases use BT9-042 Raijinmon (Cyborg) and BT9-029 Suijinmon
(Machine). BT20-058 itself is Machine but has play cost 12, so it is a useful
upper-bound negative. BT20-017 Jesmon is a legal Digimon fixture that is neither
Cyborg nor Machine and is used as a nonmatching DigiXros name. The DigiXros
requirement has three distinct named slots; the shared matcher permits any two
distinct slots, reducing each by two, which is why all three two-card pairs are
tested. No near-name variant is present in the committed catalog.

The stack cases use ordinary main-deck Digimon and prove source identity after
natural public DigiXros play and after a public Gaia Force departure. Accepted
replacement keeps only the selected eligible source in the battle area; refusal
and the cost-12 negative move the departing stack to trash without replay.
Fixtures contain no Digi-Egg in a deck or Security zone.
