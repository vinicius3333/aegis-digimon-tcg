- Alternate `[Digivolve] [Raptordramon]/Lv.4 w/[Chronicle] trait: Cost 3`.
- `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` you may play 1 `[Dorumon]/[Ryudamon]` from hand to your empty breeding area without paying the cost. Then, if during an attack until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of your Digimon isn't affected by your opponent's Digimon's effects and gets +5000 DP.

The attack-time +5000 and opponent-Digimon immunity last until the opponent's turn ends; the inherited redirect is optional and once per opponent turn.

## Defects and corrections

The named `[Raptordramon]` alternate route was encoded as substring `names`; it is corrected to `namesExact`, with negatives for name extensions. The existing Dorumon/Ryudamon and Kota matchers were already exact. The former Q4721 proof directly injected `EffectTiming.WhenDigivolving` during an attack; it was removed because the colocated natural attack flow already causes BT20-012's public When Attacking digivolution into Grademon and proves the same condition without bypassing lifecycle behavior.

The natural attack-time test now uses a legal BT1-010 security card instead of Digi-Egg BT20-001. Existing tests cover optional refusal, empty-breeding requirement, target selection, duration, public play/evolution, inherited redirection and reset, legal stacks, and no-target boundaries. No direct timing fire or injected effect resolution remains.
