# BT20-085 — Shoto Kazama

## Contract and source reconciliation

The catalog record identifies BT20-085 as the green Tamer Shoto Kazama (play cost 3,
LIBERATOR trait, maximum four copies). Its printed clauses are:

- `[Start of Your Main Phase]` By returning this Tamer to the bottom of the deck, you may play one `[Shoto Kazama]` from hand without paying its cost.
- Then, if you don't have a Digimon, you may play one level 3 Digimon card with `[Avian]/[Bird]` in any of its traits from trash without paying its cost.
- `[End of Your Turn]` By suspending this Tamer, suspend one of your opponent's Digimon and, until the end of their turn, one of your Digimon with the `[Vortex Warriors]` trait gets +2000 DP.
- `[Security]` Play this card without paying its cost.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-085` returns Q5553 and Q5554. Q5553 confirms
that the part after “Then” cannot be processed unless the Tamer was returned to
the bottom of the deck. Q5554 confirms that a Shoto played by this start-of-main
effect does not activate another start-of-main effect in the same timing window.
Q4409–Q4411 are indexed for BT20-083's breeding/security rulings, not BT20-085,
so they are not applicable to this card.

## Defects and fidelity corrections

The hand-authored IR used substring `match: "name"` for the bracketed
`[Shoto Kazama]` reference. It now uses `nameExact`; the test includes a direct
near-name negative (`Shoto Kazama Jr.`) and checks the catalog and printed text.
The injected `advance.fireGlobal(EffectTiming.StartOfYourMainPhase)` proofs were
replaced by `startTurnLoop()` and `waitForMainPhase(0)`. The end-of-turn test now
uses the public `runOneTurn()` result and the emitted `effectResolved` record
(`timing: "OnEndTurn"`) to
prove that the suspension effect completed, then continues from the observed
seat-1 Main phase through a natural turn loop. It no longer mutates `turnSeat` or
`memory`, and observes duration expiry. The opposing Digimon is not incorrectly
asserted to remain suspended after its natural unsuspend phase.
All deck/security fixtures use main-deck
Digimon and contain no Digi-Eggs.
