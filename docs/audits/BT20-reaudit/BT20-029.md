It is corrected to `namesExact: ["Bibimon"]`, and the structural test now asserts exact route shape plus near-name negatives (`Bibimon X`, `Bibimonmon`). The complete-text Pulsemon matcher remains `match: "text"`, as required by Q4322; changing it to an exact-name matcher would incorrectly reject Pulsemon references in effects, inherited text, or evolution requirements.

The colocated suite retains natural public attack/battle flows proving deletion, once-per-turn refusal, next-own-turn reset, and Q4324 simultaneous-deletion refusal. No injected timing calls remain.

The breeding fixture uses a Digimon in the raising area to exercise Q4323's inactive source boundary; egg evolution routes use legal Digi-Egg cards only in the breeding area. No Digi-Egg is placed in a deck or security stack. Fixtures use legal Digimon cards and neutral Option fillers where required.
