# BT20-100 — The Last Guardian

## Contract and rules evidence

The committed catalog (`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`) identifies BT20-100 as **The Last Guardian**, a white Royal Knight Option with play cost 4, no evolution route, and maximum count 4. Its printed contract is:

- `[Main]` reveal the top 3 cards; add exactly one `[Cool Boy]` and exactly one card with the `[Royal Knight]/[X Antibody]` trait among them; return the rest to the bottom of the deck; then place this card in the battle area.
- `[All Turns]` when any own Digimon with `[Omnimon]` in its name would leave the battle area, `<Delay>`; one of those Digimon doesn't leave.
- `[Security]` you may play one `[Omekamon]/[Cool Boy]` from hand or trash without paying its cost; then place this card in the battle area.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-100` returns Q4905 (2025-07-04). Q4905 covers the simultaneous leave-trigger timing: BT20-100's Delay remains activatable alongside another applicable prevention effect, and the player may order the triggers. The public peer proof imports real BT22-052 Leopardmon, whose All Turns would-leave watcher gains 2 memory while BT20-100 prevents the Omnimon leave, demonstrating that the independent Leopardmon trigger still resolves. There is no evolution, inherited, once-per-turn, or duration clause on this Option.

## Defects, gaps, and rubric

- The prior Delay tests seeded BT20-100 directly in the battle area and mutated `placedByEffect` and `turnCount`. Those timing mutations were removed. The replacement tests play BT20-100 through the public Main intent, advance through the public turn loop, and only then invoke the public deletion verb. Opponent Main is closed publicly rather than surrendered, so the game remains live for the next own Main phase.
- Public Main fixtures now include the legal white BT20-092 Battle NPC color source. This is required for the white Option's color requirement and does not open an unrelated effect because each fixture already has a Digimon.
- No source-module correction was required: the existing IR already has the correct independent Main add groups, `deckBottom`, mandatory placement tails, exact Security union, and Delay replacement semantics.
- Evolution-stack proof is not applicable: BT20-100 is an Option with `evoCosts: []`; the Delay source/target boundary is proven against both direct BT10-086 Omnimon (X Antibody) and a non-Omnimon Digimon.
- The real BT22-052 Leopardmon peer supplies the independent simultaneous leave watcher for the Q4905 proof: its gain-2-memory event remains observable after BT20-100 prevents the Omnimon leave.
