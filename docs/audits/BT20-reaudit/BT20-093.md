## Defect and fidelity findings

The implementation already had the correct ordered optional Main play and mandatory self-placement, cost reduction, Delay replacement boundary, two-material DNA action, and Security self-placement. The audit strengthened the test from partial structural checks to full catalog/IR assertions, exact catalog candidate-cost assertions plus observed total-payment checks for both reduced plays, and both sides of every union boundary. The Security `match: "name"` matcher is intentional: the printed clause says `[Dracomon] in its name`, which includes names such as Dracomon (X Antibody), while it must not treat a Coredramon card as eligible merely because Dracomon appears in its text.

The previous Delay tests drove separate `runOneTurn` calls and directly rewrote `turnSeat`; they now use one public `startTurnLoop`, `advance(...).waitForMainPhase`, public play/attack/return intents, and public surrender. The sole memory assignment in the accepted/declined return flow is a payment fixture after the opponent's real Main phase opens; it does not fire or replace card timing. The battle boundary uses a real public attack and confirms no DNA replacement on a battle departure.

Fixtures use legal main-deck Digimon, Options, and Security cards; no Digi-Egg appears in a deck or Security. All Delay effects are settled before final zone assertions.
