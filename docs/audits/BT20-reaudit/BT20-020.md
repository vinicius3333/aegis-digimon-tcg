# BT20-020 — Imperialdramon: Fighter Mode

## Contract and source reconciliation

The catalog record at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:32177-32212` identifies BT20-020 as a red/purple level-6 Virus Ancient Dragonkin Digimon with play cost 13, 13000 DP, red or purple level-5 evolution cost 5, and an alternate `[Digivolve] [Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]: Cost 2` route. Its printed clauses are:

- `Raid` and `Piercing`.
- `[When Digivolving]` Your opponent can't play Digimon or Tamers by effects until the end of their turn. Then, if `[Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]` is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, trash your opponent's top security card.
- `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` When your opponent's security stack is removed from, delete 1 of their Digimon with as much or less DP as this Digimon.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-020` returns Q4309, Q4665, Q4666, Q4667, Q4668, and Q6245:

- Q4309 establishes the priority of Security effects and security-removal watchers during a security check.
- Q4665-Q4668 define the player-scoped effect-play restriction, including that opponent Digimon/Tamers cannot be played by effects while the restriction is active, while an effect controlled by the restricting player may still play an opponent's card.
- Q6245 confirms the effect-play restriction also covers an opponent effect attempting to play a Digimon or Tamer into breeding.

Q4763 is not a BT20-020 ruling. The local KB assigns Q4763 to EX9-020 CresGarurumon, concerning that card's DNA replacement and whether the resulting Digimon leaves the battle area. It is not applicable to Imperialdramon: Fighter Mode, which has no Blast DNA, Alliance, or Blocker text. Those requested keywords likewise do not appear in BT20-020's catalog record and are not introduced here.

The references to `[Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]` are bracketed card-name references, so they require exact printed-name matching. The catalog contains many cards with the same exact name across sets and also distinct Fighter/Paladin names; substring matching would incorrectly admit a hypothetical `Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode (X Antibody)` variant. The corrected module uses `nameExact` in both the alternate evolution requirement and the digivolution-stack condition.

## Defects and corrections

The direct module used substring `match: "name"` for both the stack gate and the alternate evolution requirement. Because the printed text uses a bracketed `[Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode]` card reference, this was too broad. The module now uses `match: "nameExact"` and `namesExact`, and the structural test includes positive exact and negative near-name matcher cases.

The existing boundary test used `advance.fireSubTrigger("whenSecurityRemoved", ...)`, which is injected timing and not behavioral proof. It was removed because the focused suite already contains natural public security checks, one-per-turn refusal, and a real opponent-turn reset. The natural attack test explicitly declines the mandatory Raid redirection so the security check and removal watcher resolve in the intended public path.

No printed Blast DNA, Alliance, Blocker, or inherited restriction clause exists on BT20-020. The relevant restriction is resident and lasts until the opponent's turn ends. No unrelated Q4763 behavior is implemented or claimed.

All existing deck and security fixtures use legal cards; no Digi-Egg fixture or security-win shortcut was found in this card test.
