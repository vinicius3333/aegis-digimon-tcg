# BT20-052 — Oblivimon

## Evidence

- Catalog: `BT20-052` is Oblivimon, a black/blue level 5 Digimon, play cost 7,
  7000 DP, Ultimate/Virus, with Cyborg/LIBERATOR traits. Its ordinary routes
  are black or blue level 4 for memory 4. Its printed alternate route is
  level 4 with [Cyborg] or [Machine] trait for memory 3.
- Catalog text: Security — at the end of the opponent's turn, play this card
  without paying the cost; When Digivolving, flip the opponent's top face-down
  security card face-up; Your Turn — when your Digimon checks a face-up
  security card, you may place this Digimon's top card face-up at the bottom
  of the security stack. Inherited Your Turn — this Digimon's attack target
  can't be switched.
- Knowledge base: Q4375 (flip the second card when only the top card is already
  face-up), Q4376-Q4379 (face-up security visibility and shuffle/check rules),
  Q4380 (Security effects take precedence on a check), Q4381 (the underlying
  card's End of Attack still resolves after Oblivimon is moved), Q4719 (a
  successful attack can still lose at zero security before the delayed play),
  and Q4720 (moving Oblivimon's top card can expose a Tamer and remove the
  attacking Digimon).

## Implementation mapping

| Printed behavior | IR | Observable proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security End of Opponent's Turn play, free | Security `EndOfOpponentsTurn` → `PlayWithoutCost`, `payCost:false`, self-target | Natural opponent-turn lifecycle moves face-up security Oblivimon into the owner's battle area and removes it from security. |
| When Digivolving flip top face-down opposing security | `WhenDigivolving` → `SecurityManipulation flipFaceUp`, `controller:"opponent"` | Public alternate evolution over BT20-050 pays exactly 3 and flips only the next face-down card after an already face-up top card. |
| Your Turn face-up check may move this Digimon's top card | `YourTurn` → live `whenCheckedFaceUpSecurity` SubTrigger; optional self `addBottom`, `faceUp:true`, `detachPermanentTop:true` | Public attack into face-up security moves only Oblivimon face-up to the owner's security bottom and promotes BT20-050; explicit optional refusal and the face-down negative both leave the card in battle. |
| Inherited attack target cannot switch on controller's turn | Inherited `YourTurn` self `Restrict attackTargetChange`, permanent | A BT20-053 host with Oblivimon underneath is restricted on its turn, a standalone Oblivimon is not, and the restriction clears on the opponent's turn; a public Blocker fixture confirms no redirect window. |

The module registers only through `registerIrCard("BT20-052", compiled)` and has
no `@ts-nocheck` or legacy registration. The live security-check event is used;
the former dead `whenChecksSecurity`/manual timing workaround is not present.
The test suite's old `advance(...).fireForInstance(EffectTiming.OnEndTurn, ...)`
probe was removed. The natural security test now uses legal main-deck card
`BT1-010` instead of Digi-Egg `BT20-001`.

The route assertions exercise exact level/trait matching for both printed
alternatives: BT20-050 (Cyborg) and BT20-049 (Machine) match, while level-3
BT20-010 does not. The public evolution assertion verifies the 3-memory cost,
stack transition, and resulting security visibility.
