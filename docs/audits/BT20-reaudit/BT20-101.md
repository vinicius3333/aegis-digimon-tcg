# BT20-101 — Zephagamon

## Contract and rules evidence

The committed catalog identifies BT20-101 as the green level-6 Mega Zephagamon:
play cost 8, 13,000 DP, normal evolution from a green level-5 for 5, and
alternate evolution from a level-6 Vortex Warriors Digimon with play cost 10 or
higher for 1. It is an Ace with Overflow -4 and has Magic Knight, Vortex
Warriors, LIBERATOR, and Bird Dragon traits.

Its printed clauses are:

- `[Digivolve]` play cost 10 or higher Lv.6 with the `[Vortex Warriors]` trait:
  cost 1;
- `[Hand] [Counter]` `<Blast Digivolve>`;
- `<Piercing>`, `<Vortex>`, and `<Blocker>`;
- `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` when any Digimon suspends, this Digimon may
  unsuspend;
- `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` you may suspend 1 Digimon, then for every 2
  suspended Digimon you may return 1 of your opponent's suspended Digimon to the
  bottom of the deck.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-101` returns Q4415 and Q4416. Q4415 confirms
that either player's Digimon suspension triggers the All Turns effect. Q4416
confirms that the On Play/When Digivolving suspension may target either player's
Digimon.

## Implementation trace

`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-101.ts` is compiled IR with exactly one
`registerIrCard("BT20-101", compiled)` registration, `coverage: "full"`, and an
empty residual. The hand-fixed source filter intentionally uses
`controllerDefault: "any"` for the `whenSuspended` watcher and its scaling
filter; this is required by Q4415 and the unqualified “every 2 suspended
Digimon” wording. The suspend target likewise uses any controller, while the
return target is specifically an opponent's suspended Digimon and moves to
`deckBottom`. The watcher is `OncePerTurn`, its unsuspend is optional, and the
two entry triggers carry the same ordered suspend-then-scale return actions.
The alternate evolution requirement is represented separately with level 6,
Vortex Warriors, base play cost minimum 10, and cost 1.

## Defects and corrections

filters are consistent with Q4415/Q4416 and the printed unqualified scaling
clause. The test was strengthened with the complete catalog contract, NBSP-safe
printed-text evidence, alternate-route helper boundaries, exact returned
instance/zone assertions, a live When Digivolving path, and natural public
turn-flow coverage for both-player suspension and once-per-turn reset. Existing
direct `turnSeat` and `advance(...).verb.suspend` setup was removed from the
behavioral proofs.
