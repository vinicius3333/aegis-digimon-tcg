# BT20-060 Alphamon: Ouryuken audit

## Sources and printed contract

- Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` (`BT20-060`).
- Local rulings: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-060`.
- Implementation: `apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-060.ts`.
- Behavioral proof: `apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-060.test.ts`.

The catalog identifies a level 7, 16000 DP Alphamon: Ouryuken ACE with play cost
9, black/yellow/red colors, Mega/Vaccine form and attribute, and NO DATA/X
Antibody/Royal Knight/Chronicle traits. It has three level 6 evolution routes,
each costing 6 memory, is an ACE with Overflow -5, and prints:

1. `[Hand] [Counter] <Blast DNA Digivolve ([Alphamon] + [Ouryumon])>`.
2. `[On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -15000 DP until the end of their turn. Then, if DNA digivolving, trash your opponent's top security card and <Recovery +1 (Deck)>`.
3. `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn] When security stacks are removed from, gain 3 memory.`

The KB returns Q4398, Q4399, and Q4726. Q4398 says a Digimon reaching 0 DP is
not deleted until the entire effect finishes. Q4399 gives the general ordering
of Security effects versus other simultaneous triggers. Q4726 confirms that a
turn-duration Rush grant from another card survives BT20-060's +3 memory event
when the turn does not immediately end.

## Public-flow and ruling evidence

The former direct `fireSubTrigger("whenSecurityRemoved", ...)` proof was
replaced with a public attack by a BT20-057 stack carrying ST1-07 underneath;
ST1-07's inherited Security Attack +1 naturally removes two inert BT1-090
security Options. This proves the once-per-turn deduplication at the
observable game-state boundary without a timing seam. Because the attacker is
seat 1 and the card is owned by seat 0, the owner's +3 is observed as -3 on
the global memory gauge.
The Overflow test likewise no longer calls a privileged delete verb: an
opponent’s public attack deletes the ACE in battle.

Q4398 is covered by the public Blast DNA counter scenario. It records event
ordering: security trash and Recovery complete before the 0-DP target is moved
to trash. Q4399 is a general simultaneous-trigger ordering ruling; BT20-060 has
no `[Security]` effect, so its ordering comparison is not a card-specific
clause and is documented rather than misrepresented as independently tested.
Q4726 concerns a Rush grant from another card’s End of Your Turn effect; this
card grants no Rush and has no End of Your Turn effect, so that cross-card
duration interaction is outside this lane’s executable contract.

All stacks and fixtures use ordinary main-deck Digimon/cards; no Digi-Egg is
placed in deck or Security. The public counter proof uses a legal Alphamon base
and Ouryumon material, while normal play/evolution tests exercise a legal
level-6 route and its exact memory cost; the catalog metadata assertion records
all three printed color routes.
