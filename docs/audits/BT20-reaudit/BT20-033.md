# BT20-033 — LoaderLeomon

## Contract and source reconciliation

The catalog record at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:32551-32578` identifies BT20-033 as a yellow/black level-5 Vaccine Machine/ACCEL Ultimate Digimon with play cost 6, 6000 DP, and ordinary yellow or black level-4 evolution cost 4. Its printed clauses are:

- Alternate `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[ACCEL] trait: Cost 3`.
- `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` until the end of your opponent's turn, 1 of their Digimon can't activate `[When Digivolving]` effects and gets -3000 DP.
- Inherited `[Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn]` when one of your opponent's Digimon attacks, you may change the attack target to this Digimon.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-033` returns five current Q&A entries:

- Q4326: “When Digivolving effects don't activate” prevents both triggering and effect-driven activation.
- Q4327: the restriction does not prevent the affected card's `[When Attacking]` half from activating.
- Q4328: another effect also cannot activate the affected card's `[When Digivolving]` effect.
- Q4329: the “by” condition of a restricted `[When Digivolving]` effect cannot be processed independently.
- Q4330: a combined `[When Digivolving] [When Attacking] [Once Per Turn]` effect does not consume its once-per-turn use when blocked at `[When Digivolving]` timing.

There is no Security effect, named-card condition, trait target condition, or optional entry effect. The only optional clause is the inherited attack redirection, which is modeled as an optional action. The DP and restriction duration is the opponent's turn end, not a permanent or own-turn duration.
