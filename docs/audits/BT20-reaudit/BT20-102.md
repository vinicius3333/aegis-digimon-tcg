# BT20-102 — Omnimon (X Antibody)

## Contract and rules evidence

The committed catalog identifies BT20-102 as a level 7, 16-cost, 16000-DP blue/white/red Digimon. It is Mega/Vaccine and has Holy Warrior, X Antibody, Royal Knight, and LIBERATOR traits. Its ordinary evolution routes are blue or red level 6 for cost 6; its alternate route is `[Digivolve] [Omnimon]: Cost 2`. The printed effects are:

- `<Raid>`, `<Piercing>`, and `<Blocker>`.
- `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` if an `[Omnimon]` or `[X Antibody]` card is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, choose one Digimon belonging to both players and delete all other Digimon; then return one opposing Digimon to the bottom of its owner's deck.
- `[End of Your Turn] [Once Per Turn]` one own Digimon may gain `<Rush>` for the turn and attack without suspending.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-102` returns Q4417, Q4418, Q4419, Q4725, Q4726, Q5907, and Q6018:

- Q4417: after choosing the Rush Digimon, its attack cannot be declined when possible.
- Q4418: a suspended Digimon may be chosen for the unsuspending attack.
- Q4419: simultaneous copies cannot create a second attack while the first attack is in progress.
- Q4725: the `Then` return still resolves when the first conditional delete process did not apply.
- Q4726: Rush lasts through a security-triggered +3-memory event that prevents the turn from ending.
- Q5907: when another effect plays BT20-102 under its former source, the newly played card's stack satisfies its own `[Omnimon]/[X Antibody]` condition.
- Q6018: an activated entry effect continues through its `Then` process even if an immediate effect removes its source.

## Defects and rubric

- Removed all direct `advance.fireForPermanent(...)` timing injections from the prior On Play tests.
- Removed direct `turnSeat` mutation and replaced both End-of-Your-Turn proofs with `startTurnLoop()`, `waitForMainPhase()`, and public phase closure. Attack assertions now use saved `attackDeclared` and `securityRevealed` events keyed to the permanent ID, because the turn loop clears the transient `hasAttackedThisTurn` observation by the time final state is read. Q4419 slices attack events from a baseline captured immediately before End-of-Your-Turn, avoiding unrelated setup events. Q4418 captures the suspended state through the observable event hook at declaration, before the next Active phase can unsuspend it. The declined path ends by surrendering only after its assertions; the positive paths close the opponent phase publicly before final cleanup.
- The End-of-Your-Turn Attack now sets `drainTimingWindowDuringAttack: true`. This is the card-local IR seam required by Q4419: simultaneous copies are activated before the first attack's Counter timing, and the second copy's Attack action is rejected while the first attack is in flight. BT20-102 has no Security Attack +1; the regression therefore asserts one new declaration and one check, not two checks interpreted as one attack.
