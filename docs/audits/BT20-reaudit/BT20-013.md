## Contract and source reconciliation

The catalog record at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:31988-32008` identifies BT20-013 as a red level-4 Data Dinosaur Digimon with play cost 5, 5000 DP, and a red level-3 evolution route costing 2. Its printed clauses are:

- `[Main] [Once Per Turn]` You may play 1 Digimon with `[Sistermon]`/`[Gankoomon]` in its name from your hand with the play cost reduced by 2.
- Inherited `[Your Turn]` All of your Digimon get +1000 DP.

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT20-013` returns Q4293, Q4294, Q4295, and Q4296:

- Q4293 (2026-03-13) says two copies cannot combine simultaneous card-playing activations into one play or a total reduction of 4.
- Q4294 (2025-01-24) confirms that BaoHuckmon's reduction stacks with BT20-057 Gankoomon's own reduction, for a total reduction of 6.
- Q4295 (2025-01-24) says the effect can activate and play through ST12-03 Solarmon, but the play-cost reduction does not apply.
- Q4296 (2025-01-24) says the effect can activate through BT9-047 Pomumon's effect-play lock, but cannot use it to play the Digimon.

The printed phrase “with [Sistermon]/[Gankoomon] in its name” is a substring name reference, not an exact-name reference. The catalog includes variants such as Sistermon Ciel (Awakened) and Gankoomon (X Antibody), so `match: "name"` is correct. The target is separately constrained to `kind: ["Digimon"]`; this prevents the Chronicle Option BT23-099 The Sistermon Sisters Training Gym from being selected merely because its name contains Sistermon.

## Defects and corrections

The executable IR was already faithful: no exact-name conversion is appropriate, and the inline `reduceCostBy: 2` correctly scopes BaoHuckmon's reduction to the selected play while respecting the two global restrictions covered by Q4295/Q4296. The colocated proof was expanded to cover both restriction rulings and the non-Digimon name-match boundary.

The once-per-turn reset fixture previously used BT20-001 through BT20-006 as deck cards. Those are Digi-Eggs and are not legal main-deck cards. They now use legal inert BT1-090 Option cards while preserving the real opponent-turn lifecycle. The inherited proof uses a legal red Lv5 SaviorHuckmon over a red Lv4 BaoHuckmon, matching the catalog's evolution color/level route.

Q4293's ruling is recorded and the existing same-turn once-per-turn test proves that one BaoHuckmon cannot be activated twice. The engine's public activation flow does not expose a simultaneous multi-effect declaration distinct from this once-per-turn identity, so no stronger Q4293 claim is made here.
