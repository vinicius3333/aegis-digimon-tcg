## Exact matcher and fixture audit

The printed `[Yuuki]` reference is an exact card-name reference, so
`match: "nameExact"` is correct. The near-match negative uses LM-018 Gyuukimon,
whose name contains the `Yuuki` substring but is not Yuuki. The three slash-
separated trait references are represented as an exact trait union, not a name
substring or loose kind filter. BT20-069 Punkmon proves the Dark Dragon branch;
BT20-047 Solarmon, BT20-048 Dorumon, and BT20-049 Blimpmon provide legal
nonmatching fillers. There are no once-per-turn, optional, Security, deletion,
duration, or effect-trigger reset clauses on this card.

The Yaamon evolution fixture was corrected from an illegal battle-area Digi-Egg
to a legal breeding-area Yaamon. No Digi-Egg is placed in a deck or Security;
all reveal cards and stack fixtures are ordinary main-deck cards. The public
evolution assertion verifies both the zero payment and source-card stack.
