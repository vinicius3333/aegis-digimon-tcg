# BT20-056 — Alphamon

## Evidence

- Catalog: `BT20-056` is Alphamon, a black/yellow level 6 Digimon, play cost
  12, 11000 DP, Mega/Vaccine, with Holy Warrior/X Antibody/Royal Knight/
  Chronicle traits. Its ordinary black and yellow level-5 evolution routes
  each cost 3.
- Complete catalog text: Barrier; On Play/When Digivolving, Recovery +1
  (Deck), then if during an attack, one of your breeding Digimon may
  digivolve into a level-6-or-lower Chronicle Digimon card from hand or trash
  without paying the cost; All Turns once per turn, when security stacks are
  removed, one opposing Digimon gets -8000 DP for the turn.
- Inherited text: All Turns once per turn, when this Digimon would leave the
  battle area other than by your effects, if this Digimon is [Alphamon:
  Ouryuken], by trashing your top security card, it doesn't leave.
- Knowledge base: Q4389 (breeding-area digivolution does not activate the
  digivolved card's When Digivolving effect), Q4390 (Security effects take
  precedence over other simultaneous security/removal triggers), and Q4724
  (an effect-driven evolution into Alphamon during an opposing attack still
  satisfies the during-attack condition).

The direct module registers exclusively through `registerIrCard("BT20-056",
compiled)`, has full coverage and an empty residual, and contains no legacy
registration or `@ts-nocheck`. `selfHasName` is the engine's exact effective
name condition, so the inherited Ouryuken gate does not use substring matching.
The shared entry action intentionally has no `isDnaDigivolving` flag: Q4389's
breeding-area Digivolve primitive suppresses the newly evolved card's entry
trigger through the public engine path.

  `fireForPermanent`, `fireSubTrigger`, or effect-resolution context mutation
  remains.

  preserving the -8000 once-per-turn proof; a real `startTurnLoop()` transition
  proves that the once-per-turn tracker resets on a later opponent turn.

  asserting the subsequent Recovery card. No production IR change was needed.
