## Result

All 112 catalog cards have direct TypeScript modules, focused behavioral
tests, full compiled IR coverage, no residual clauses, and exactly one
matching `registerIrCard` registration. No BT11 module uses `registerCard`,
`@ts-nocheck`, or a handwritten timing override.

The detailed ledger in `docs/audits/BT11-AUDIT.md` and the range reports in
`internal-docs/audits/BT11/` are historical discovery evidence. Some notes
describe limitations that this branch resolved and are not the final delivery
status. Reproducible evidence for every card ID is the combination of:

- `apps/api/src/cards/BT11/<CARD-ID>.ts`;
- `apps/api/src/cards/BT11/<CARD-ID>.test.ts`;
- the per-card registration and coverage case generated by
  `BT11-catalog-sync.test.ts`; and
- the matching generated record in
  `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`.

## Notable repaired seams

- Canonical trigger and event identifiers for Phoenixmon and Lilithmon.
- Exact-name, containing-trait, controller, stack, and target scopes across
  the Sukamon, Vemmon, X Antibody, Sea Animal, Tamer, and attack effects.
- Mervamon's Q2111 all-or-none selection when two or more eligible cards are
  available, including hostile partial-selection rejection.
- KingSukamon's executable original name, color, and base-DP rewrite with a
  typed shared IR contract.
- Alternate digivolution and placement requirements that were previously
  encoded too broadly.
- Removal of the obsolete `_earlyMidHandwritten.ts` compatibility module.

The generated effects catalog is not maintained manually for this workflow.
Run `pnpm effects:sync:set -- --set BT11` to update BT11 records, or
`pnpm effects:check:set -- --set BT11 --base origin/main` to verify scope and
drift without writing.
