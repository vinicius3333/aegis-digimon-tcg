# BT22-039 Ouranosmon

Catalog fields identify a level 6 yellow/white Appmon God with App Fusion [Entermon] & [Fakemon] cost 0, Alliance, and Link +1. The IR implements shared once-per-turn When Digivolving/When Attacking optional play of one level 4 or lower [Appmon] from hand, plus the All Turns once-per-turn reaction to any of your Digimon being played: optionally link one [Appmon] source from Ouranosmon's own evolution stack to one of your Digimon without cost.

Tests prove App Fusion pairing, attack-time free Appmon play, actual source movement from Ouranosmon's stack into a link, and the negative that an eligible Appmon in another Digimon's stack is not linkable. The previously incorrect assertion was corrected to match observable link semantics: linking removes the card from the evolution stack. Focused suite: 3 tests passed.

Applicable local KB rulings: Q4892 (non-Link cards cannot be linked) and Q4893 (the play trigger also fires when Ouranosmon itself is played). No card-local ambiguity or unsupported clause remains.
