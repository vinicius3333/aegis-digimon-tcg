## Reusable patch and mechanism regression

`docs/audits/BT22-reaudit/patches/rotation-preflight.patch` proposes a non-mutating `projectedRotationBase` helper in `canAttemptDigivolve`. It detects only a `placeOwnTopAtStackBottom` nested in a compound cost, projects the complete promoted top card and bottom-first stack, and retains ordinary, alternate, name, trait, and base-granted requirement checks. It also requires awaiting the asynchronous placement primitive and carries the exact paid host through the post-cost resolver; the no-DP DigiEgg exception is scoped to that host for that resolution.
