# BT22-026 MetalGarurumon

Catalog fields identify a level 6 blue Data Digimon with alternate level 5 [Garurumon] in name or [CS] trait evolution for cost 3. The IR registers only with `registerIrCard`. The hand Main effect is conditional on having [Nokia Shiramine], evolves one [Gabumon] for exactly 6 while ignoring requirements, and the When Digivolving modal either free-evolves one [Agumon] into [WarGreymon] from hand ignoring requirements or returns one opponent's lowest-level Digimon to hand. The inherited When Attacking effect checks [Omnimon] in the host name and unsuspends once per turn.

Behavioral proof in `BT22-026.test.ts` covers the Nokia hand evolution and cost, the free [Agumon] to [WarGreymon] branch, the exact lowest-level hand return branch, and inherited behavior on a realistic BT22-026 under an Omnimon stack with the once-per-turn boundary. The focused suite passed 5 tests.

No card-local ambiguity or unsupported clause remains. Collection delivery gates remain owned by the lead audit.
