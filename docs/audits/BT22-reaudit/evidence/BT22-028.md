# BT22-028 Ariemon

Catalog fields identify a level 7 blue Data Aquatic/LIBERATOR Digimon with alternate [Yao Qinglan], [MarineBullmon] evolution for cost 6. Decode is executable for a level 6 or lower [Aqua] or [Sea Animal] card from its own stack on a non-battle leave. The When Digivolving play effect is optional as a whole, but Q5213 requires one each of levels 3, 4, and 5 whenever available; the IR therefore has three mandatory per-level plays and preserves the Aqua-or-Sea-Animal boundary. The When Digivolving and When Attacking costed effects share one once-per-turn identity, place another Digimon under Ariemon, bottom-deck one opponent Digimon, and unsuspend Ariemon. This preserves the Once Per Turn shown on the official English and Japanese card images despite the English HTML omission.

Behavioral proof in `BT22-028.test.ts` uses a realistic level 3/4/5/6 evolution stack to prove Q5213's one-each requirement and level-6 retention, proves bottom-deck order, placement cost, unsuspend, shared Once Per Turn across the two timings, and proves optional refusal leaves zones and suspension unchanged. Decode is also proven from the own stack on non-battle deletion. The focused suite passed 5 tests.

Q4878 and Q5213 are the applicable local KB rulings. No card-local ambiguity or unsupported clause remains. Collection delivery gates remain owned by the lead audit.
