# BT24-005 Kyokyomon

Independent reaudit evidence for BT24-005.

## Contract and trace

The committed catalog entry identifies Kyokyomon as a black level-2 Digi-Egg with
Lesser, X Antibody, DigiPolice, and SEEKERS traits. Its inherited text is `[Your
Turn] [Once Per Turn] When Tamer cards are placed in this Digimon's digivolution
cards, reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Return the revealed cards to the top or
bottom of the deck.`

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT24-005` returned Q5577: the revealed cards are shown
to the opponent, and their destination and order are confirmed before returning
them. The direct module uses one inherited Your Turn OncePerTurn
`onAddDigivolutionCards` SubTrigger, self-host binding, an added-card kind filter for
Tamer, and `RevealAdd` with revealCount 3, no hand additions, and top-or-bottom
restacking. It registers only through `registerIrCard("BT24-005", compiled)`, with
full coverage and no residuals.

## Behavioral proof

- `matches the catalog identity` checks immutable identity, color, form, level, and traits.
- `reveals exactly three cards and lets the player return them to the top or bottom` checks the full IR clause mapping.
- `reveals and returns all three cards without adding any to hand` checks exact count, deck conservation, face-down final state, and no hand addition.
- `does not reveal when a non-Tamer is added or a different host receives the Tamer` checks card-kind and self-host boundaries.

The current public intent surface has no player-issued action for placing a loose
Tamer directly into a Digimon's digivolution cards. The positive event is therefore
exercised through the testkit's observable placement seam. This is an explicit
engine/testkit gap, reported to Astra; it is not treated as natural timing proof.

## Peer/stack proof

and a different host. The source binding and card identity remain observable in the
evolution stack.
