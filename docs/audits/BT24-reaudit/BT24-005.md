The committed catalog entry identifies Kyokyomon as a black level-2 Digi-Egg with
Lesser, X Antibody, DigiPolice, and SEEKERS traits. Its inherited text is `[Your
Turn] [Once Per Turn] When Tamer cards are placed in this Digimon's digivolution
cards, reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Return the revealed cards to the top or

`node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT24-005` returned Q5577: the revealed cards are shown
to the opponent, and their destination and order are confirmed before returning
them. The direct module uses one inherited Your Turn OncePerTurn
`onAddDigivolutionCards` SubTrigger, self-host binding, an added-card kind filter for
Tamer, and `RevealAdd` with revealCount 3, no hand additions, and top-or-bottom
restacking. It registers only through `registerIrCard("BT24-005", compiled)`, with
full coverage and no residuals.

## Behavioral proof

Ten focused tests pass. The legal Ryudamon host carries Kyokyomon and the actual Tamer source. Both top and bottom decisions expose all three identities. A public `respondDecision` returns them in a nontrivial third/first/second order, preserving exact deck identity, the unrevealed fourth card, and no hand additions. Broadcast `cardRevealed` events contain the same three cards, proving the opponent-visible reveal channel required by Q5577. Repeating the placement event in the same turn does not reveal again.

Negative tests cover a named Tamer still in hand, a non-Tamer placement, another host and the opponent turn. Public breeding evolution builds Kyokyomon into Ryudamon and checks source identity. A public Digimon play triggers the existing Witch of the System Mind Link watcher and places that Tamer under the Kyokyomon host.

The earlier disappearing-card report was a fixture error: Digi-Eggs had been put in the main deck, and the engine correctly returned them to the egg deck. The corrected negative fixtures now use inert main-deck Digimon (BT1-009..012), while the only Digi-Egg is the breeding-area Kyokyomon. Main-deck Digimon exercise both reorder destinations. No engine fix was required.
