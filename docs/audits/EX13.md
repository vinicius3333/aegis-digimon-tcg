# EX13 audit

## Status

EX13 (EXTRA BOOSTER CHIVALROUS XIII) is an announced, not-yet-released product
(official release date 2026-10-02). The catalog carries the 60 cards revealed so
far by the community database as of `88241f0fc`; 18 numbers in the set remain
unrevealed and are not yet in `cards.json`. Card modules are being authored one at
a time against the catalog and the rules knowledge base, with behavioral proof per
card, following `.agents/skills/verify-card-implementation`. Until every revealed
card is verified and the set is fully released, EX13 is gated to the beta
battle-mode flag rather than exposed in the default legal pool.
