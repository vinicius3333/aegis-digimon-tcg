## Method, runtime seams, and gate status

For each card, the immutable catalog record was reread for identity, colors, kind, level, play cost, DP, evolution requirements, form, attribute, traits, effect text, inherited text, Security text, rarity, and copy limit. Each card was queried in the local KB, then its direct TypeScript module, colocated test, registration path, shared runtime seams, realistic peers, and legal evolution/stack shapes were inspected. No audited module has a second registration: each direct module has exactly one `registerIrCard("BT14-NNN", compiled)` call and no `registerCard` call.

The shared runtime seams inspected included:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` for compiled normalization, trigger routing, and the exclusive IR registration path.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts` for timing homes, turn-owner guards, and the `Main`/Security routing.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts` for opponent-security direction, host/subject filters, attack-event subjects, self-suspend costs, and own-stack anchoring.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts` and `actions/board.ts` for action-level costs, `MovePermanent`, and placement dispatch.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` for partial reveal selection and bottom-decking.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/security.ts` and security-check fire sites for natural Security activation and returned Security cards.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/mindLink.ts` and `actions/statics.ts` for legal Mind Link targets and host-only inherited Aura application.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/permanent.ts` and `matching/trigger.ts` for level, color, controller, host, and attack-subject predicates.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts`, `GameEngine.ts`, and combat controller paths for natural suspension, attack, deletion, security removal, and breeding-to-battle transitions.

The five scoring components are worth two points each: catalog/rules fidelity, executable IR trace, behavior-driving proof, peer/evolution-stack proof, and executed gates. Structural assertions, direct timing/event injection, and manual event injection do not receive full behavioral credit when a natural production origin exists. No tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser check, collection gate, mechanism gate, or `git diff --check` was run, as required; executed gates are therefore `0/2` and every score is provisional.
