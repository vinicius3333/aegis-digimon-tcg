## Method, runtime seams, and gate status

For every card, the immutable catalog record was reread for color, kind, level, play cost, DP, every evolution requirement, form, attribute, trait, effect text, inherited text, Security text, rarity, maximum copies, and ACE/Overflow metadata where applicable. Each card was queried in the local rules KB, then its direct compiled module, colocated test, shared runtime, neighboring peers, realistic evolution/stack shapes, and registration path were inspected. Every direct module below has `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and exactly one executable registration of the form `registerIrCard("BT14-NNN", compiled)`; no audited module has a duplicate `registerCard` registration.

The shared runtime paths inspected were:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:44-120` for compiled module normalization, trigger routing, and registration.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts:10-43` for registered compiled cards and universal Rule-name aliases.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:422-434, 788-805, 898-906` for self-scoped versus filtered `whenEffectSuspends`, attack event subjects, and leave-cause gates.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts:186-255` for prevention replacements and `otherThanYourEffect` cause matching.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts:29-47, 145-180` for Save-style placement and stack relocation.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:108-188, 808-809` for reveal/add/play, partial candidate counts, and deck-bottom routing.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts:96-121` and `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/combat.ts:1-55` for When Attacking, turn-end, and forced-attack routing.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:3325-3350` and `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts:1333-1350` for effect-suspension and battle-deletion event origins, including inherited leave-play watchers.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:899-916` was inspected and deliberately left unchanged. The temporary global `universalNameAliasesFor` addition was reverted: none of BT14-051 through BT14-060 needs `selfHasNameContaining` to consult a universal Rule-name alias. BT14-052's inherited check is proved with a legal stack ending in printed-name `BT4-061 BanchoLeomon`, while BT14-060's executable clauses use trait filters and leave-play replacement rather than that condition. Consequently, there is no audited behavior that should fail without this global change, and introducing a synthetic alias-only test would misrepresent the scope.

The five scoring components are each worth two points: catalog/rules fidelity, executable IR trace, behavior-driving proof, peer/stack proof, and executed gates. Structural assertions and manual timing/event injection were not counted as full behavior where a natural origin was available. No tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser check, collection gate, mechanism gate, or `git diff --check` was run, as required by the dispatch. Therefore executed gates are fixed at `0/2` and every maximum is provisional at `8/10`; colocated tests cited below are source evidence present in the worktree, not executed evidence.
