## BT14-101 — WarGreymon

### Catalog and rules

The immutable entry identifies a red level 6 Digimon, play cost 12, 12000 DP, Vaccine/Dragonkin,
with the ordinary red level 5 evolution cost 4. Its printed clauses are: conditional hand evolution
of one Agumon for 4 while a Tamer named Tai Kamiya and an opposing Digimon at 10000 DP or more are
present; When Digivolving, gain Raid for the turn and optionally attack; and When Attacking, if a
Tamer is present, gain Security Attack +1 and Piercing for the turn.

The separate local KB query was `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT14-101`. Q2484 (2024-03-28) confirms
that the conditional effect may digivolve an Agumon in the battle area into BT14-101 in hand when the
Tai Kamiya and opposing 10000-DP conditions are satisfied.

Rules trace: comprehensive rules §15-7 (the optional processing condition and its dependent action),
§15-16 (When Digivolving/When Attacking timing), §16-23 (Raid selects the highest-DP unsuspended
opposing Digimon, with controller choice on ties), §16-4 (Security Attack +1), §16-7 (Piercing),
and §11-1 (attack target removal/attack lifecycle). The local KB text also records the same timing,
Raid, security, and Piercing semantics.

## BT14-102 — Angemon

### Catalog and rules

The immutable entry identifies a yellow level 4 Digimon, play cost 6, 6000 DP, Vaccine/Angel, with
the alternate Patamon evolution cost 2. Its clauses are: When Attacking, by deleting itself, activate
one of two effects (place an opponent's Virus Digimon at the bottom of security, or give one opposing
Digimon -5000 DP for the turn); On Deletion, place this card at the bottom of its owner's security,
then, if a Tamer is present, optionally hatch; and inherited On Deletion, optionally place one yellow
Vaccine card from hand at the bottom of its owner's security.

The separate local KB query was `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT14-102`. Q2485 confirms self-deletion
is legal even with no opposing Digimon. Q2486 confirms hatching is unavailable when the breeding area
is occupied. Q2487 confirms self-placement in security still occurs when hatching is unavailable.
Q2488 confirms the inherited hand placement is optional when the eligible card is in a private hand.

Rules trace: comprehensive rules §15-7 (by-cost optional processing and modal follow-up), §15-8
(triggered/deletion timing), §15-16 (When Attacking/On Deletion), §3-4-7 (single breeding slot),
§3-1-3-5 (bottom-security placement), and the local KB's Q2485–Q2488 interpretations.

### Direct IR and runtime trace

`apps/api/src/cards/BT14/BT14-102.ts:7-56` carries the When Attacking modal, its `deleteOwn` cost,
the Virus bottom-security branch, the -5000 branch, and optional abort semantics. `:59-85` carries
self bottom-security placement followed by the Tamer-conditioned optional Hatch. `:87-108` carries
the inherited, optional, hand-sourced yellow Vaccine placement. The module ends with the sole
registration at `:121`.

The generic action runner pays the `deleteOwn` action cost before resolving the modal and aborts the
remaining clause if payment fails (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:454-531`).
Modal resolution selects exactly one available branch (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/modal.ts:13-87`).
`SecurityManipulation.placeAsSecurity` resolves both field targets and loose hand targets, and uses
`toTop: false` for bottom placement (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/security.ts:288-407`).
The Hatch action delegates to the single-slot hatch primitive (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/board.ts:146-153`),
while the optional Hatch preflight suppresses a no-op when breeding is occupied or the egg deck is empty
(`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:314-320`).

Deletion fires the on-deletion bus before movement and then opens the post-movement On Deletion window
with the actual deleted instance set and stack-card subset (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:3013-3043`,
`:3167-3229`). The On Deletion builder admits the deleted source and distinguishes inherited stack
cards from the deleted top card (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/builders.ts:171-197`). This is the seam
that makes the inherited BT14-102 source resolve after a natural security-battle deletion.
