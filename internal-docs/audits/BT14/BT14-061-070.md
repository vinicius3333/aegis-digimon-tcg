## Method, score model, and global findings

For each card I reread the complete immutable catalog record: identity, colors, kinds, level, play cost, DP, every evolution requirement, form, attribute, traits, printed effect text, inherited effect text, Security text where present, rarity, and maximum count. I queried the local KB separately per card, inspected the direct TypeScript module and colocated test, traced each shared interpreter/runtime seam used by the card, and compared realistic peers and evolution/stack shapes. The colocated tests below are source proof present in the worktree; they were not executed, as required.

The five scoring components are each worth two points:

1. Catalog and rules fidelity.
2. IR trace.
3. Observable behavior-driving proof.
4. Peer and evolution-stack proof.
5. Executed delivery gates.

Component 5 is fixed at `0/2` for every card because this pass explicitly prohibits tests, typecheck, lint, formatters, browser checks, collection/mechanism gates, and `git diff --check`. Thus the maximum is provisional `8/10`; structural assertions and direct/manual timing or event injection were not counted as full behavioral proof where a natural public origin was available. BT14-064 is additionally `7/10 provisional` because the natural fixture proves its On Play and inherited watcher paths but does not naturally exercise the separate When Digivolving path.

The shared seams inspected were:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` and the card registry for compiled module normalization, trigger routing, inherited-effect collection, and exclusive registration.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts` for optional activation, mandatory-after-activation costs, trigger dispatch, and frequency handling.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/costs.ts` and `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts` for return/trash costs, deck movement, deletion, De-Digivolve, memory, and battle leave processing.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` for reveal ownership, RevealAdd disposition, free play, play-cost budgets, revealed-card scaling, and top/bottom restoration.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts` for DeleteBudget and deletion target/cost accumulation.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolution.ts` for repeated De-Digivolve dispatch.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/continuous.ts`, `actions/subTrigger.ts`, and `effects/subtriggers.ts` for turn-scoped grants, inherited watchers, `OncePerTurn`, opponent-effect restrictions, and deletion subjects.
- `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts`, `actions/attack.ts`, and the testkit `harness.ts`/`advance.ts`/`observe.ts` for natural play, evolution, battle deletion, block windows, and real turn-end windows.

All ten direct modules have `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and exactly one executable registration of the form `registerIrCard("BT14-NNN", compiled)`. No audited module introduces or preserves a duplicate `registerCard` registration.

## BT14-061 — Vegiemon — 8/10 provisional

### Implementation and runtime

`apps/api/src/cards/BT14/BT14-061.ts:8-61` has the same `GainMemory` action for both triggers, amount 1, an opponent-trash Digimon return cost, `optional: true`, and `abortOnDecline: true`; the cost itself is not marked optional. `costs.ts` resolves the return candidate and `payCost` moves the selected card to the deck top before the gain; `runAction.ts` first asks whether to activate the optional By clause and then treats the selected return as mandatory. This preserves the rules distinction requested for a By cost: activation is optional, but the cost is not optional after activation. The module ends with the sole `registerIrCard("BT14-061", compiled)`.

### Behavioral, peer, and stack evidence

`apps/api/src/cards/BT14/BT14-061.test.ts:20-32` drives a natural On Play from hand, returning an opponent Digimon from trash to deck top and gaining memory. Lines 34-56 drive a legal black level-3 `BT14-055` into Vegiemon and prove the natural When Digivolving path with the same opponent-trash return. Lines 58-85 cover the no-legal-cost case and declining the activation prompt, proving that the card does not gain memory when the mandatory return cannot be paid and that optional activation was retained.

The BT14-055 -> BT14-061 evolution is a realistic adjacent black stack and is also used as the lower stack in the BT14-065 De-Digivolve proof. The return-to-deck-top primitive and KB Q2432 were compared with other black By-return memory effects.

### Corrections, limitations, and score

No production correction was necessary.
