# BT14-071–BT14-080 Implementation Audit

## Scope and method

This audit covers exactly BT14-071 through BT14-080, in ascending order, on branch
`audit-bt14-071-080-luna`, starting at baseline commit `9acedd447d320a89ad569b3f23b57971c73dc067`.
The immutable catalog was re-read directly from blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` for every card. Each card was queried against the
local rules KB, its direct TypeScript module and colocated test were inspected, and the relevant
interpreter/runtime seams plus realistic peers and evolution stacks were compared.

The audit did not modify `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`, `BT14-STATIC-AUDIT.md`,
`internal-docs/audits/BT14.md`, or unrelated files. Every direct module registers only through
`registerIrCard("BT14-xxx", compiled)`; no target module has a duplicate `registerCard` call.

Scores use the campaign's five components worth two points each: Catalog and rules, IR trace,
Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed gates. Per instruction,
tests and all gates were not run, so the executed-gates component is `0/2` for every card and the
maximum provisional score is `8/10`. A behavior score below `2/2` records a material clause that
is still only structural or manually stimulated despite a feasible natural production origin.

## Shared runtime evidence

The following seams were inspected and are the runtime basis for the card-level conclusions:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:2045-2181` moves hand cards and emits both
  `whenHandTrashed` (once per action/seat) and `whenTrashedFromHand` (per card), carrying
  `byEffectSeat` when the trash originates in an effect.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/conditions.ts:746` evaluates `triggerByYourEffect`; the
  BT14-073 correction uses this production payload rather than treating every hand trash as
  an effect owned by the watcher.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:2445-2456` emits the opponent-deck discard
  event used by BT14-077 and BT14-080. `TrashTopDeck` dispatches through this seam for
  `mine`, `opponent`, and `both` controllers.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/play.ts:792-800` stores the permanent IDs
  produced by `bindResultAs`; `matching/permanent.ts:195-198` makes `filter.boundRef` resolve
  only that result set. BT14-076 now uses this to apply Rush only to the Agumon it played.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:157`,
  `actions/removal.ts:322-326` and `actions/removal.ts:611-612` provide the floor-based trash
  scaling and deletion-level ceiling used by BT14-075, BT14-078, and BT14-080.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:137-142` derives one
  once-per-turn ledger key for a `sharedUseKey`; BT14-080 uses the same key for its
  When Digivolving and first When Attacking mill clauses.
- `apps/api/src/engine/testkit/advance.ts:22-55` drives production Main-phase and complete-turn
  transitions. BT14-071 no longer injects `OnStartMainPhase`; BT14-078 uses the complete turn
  loop for End of Your Turn.

## BT14-071 — Loogamon

### Immutable catalog record

The blob records a Purple Digimon, level 3, play cost 3, 1000 DP, with Purple level-2
digivolution cost 0 and types `Dark Animal`, `X Antibody`, and `SoC`:

`[Start of Your Main Phase] By placing 1 [Eiji Nagasumi] from your hand or trash as this
Digimon's bottom digivolution card, gain 1 memory.`

Its inherited text is:

`[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When you play a card with the [Dark Animal] or [SoC] trait, gain 1 memory.`

### KB, implementation, peers, and proof

The local KB query for BT14-071 returned no entries. The direct module is full-coverage IR with
`StartOfYourMainPhase`, a placement cost restricted to Eiji from hand/trash at the bottom of
the stack, and an inherited once-per-turn `whenPlayed` watcher filtered to Dark Animal/SoC.
The registration is exclusively `registerIrCard("BT14-071", compiled)`.

The realistic stack comparison is BT14-071 → BT14-072 or BT14-074, then BT14-079 → BT14-080;
the inherited watcher matches the adjacent Fangmon/Loogarmon/Soloogarmon line. The colocated
test now drives `engine.runOneTurn()` to the real Main phase, observes Eiji placed and memory
changing from 3 to 4, then closes the phase. A second test puts BT14-071 under a host and plays
Fangmon publicly, observing the Dark Animal return/discard and inherited memory change from 10
to 7.

### Corrections and score

No implementation correction was required. The test correction removes direct timing injection
and adds the natural inherited watcher proof.

**Score: 8/10 provisional** — Catalog and rules `2/2`; IR trace `2/2`; Behavioral proof `2/2`;
Peer and stack proof `2/2`; Executed gates `0/2`. Limitation: no tests or gates were executed.

digivolution observes the hand cost, lowest own/opponent deletion, natural On Deletion Agumon
play, and Rush on Agumon only. This covers the implementation binding seam rather than merely

### Corrections and score
