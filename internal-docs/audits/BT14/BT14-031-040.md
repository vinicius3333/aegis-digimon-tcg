## Audit method and score model

For each card I read the complete catalog identity and printed clauses from the immutable blob, queried the local KB with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>`, read applicable rules and glossary entries under `data/kb/rules/`, inspected the direct module and colocated test, traced every used shared runtime seam, and compared nearby peers and realistic evolution stacks. The direct modules are all imported by `apps/api/src/cards/BT14/index.ts` and each audited card has exactly one executable registration in the form `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no audited card has a second `registerCard` registration.

The five components are each worth two points: (1) catalog and rules, (2) direct IR and shared-runtime trace, (3) observable behavioral proof, (4) peer and evolution-stack proof, and (5) executed delivery gates. Component 5 is fixed at `0/2` because the requested pass does not execute tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser checks, mechanism or collection gates, or `git diff --check`; therefore every result below is provisional and capped at `8/10`. Structural assertions and manually injected timing are not treated as complete behavior when a natural production origin is available.

The relevant shared traces were read in `apps/api/src/engine/TurnStateMachine.ts`, `apps/api/src/engine/testkit/advance.ts`, `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts`, `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/board.ts`, `actions/security.ts`, `actions/digivolve.ts`, `actions/subTrigger.ts`, `actions/play.ts`, `actions/removal.ts`, `effects/interpreter/conditions.ts`, `effects/interpreter/security/securityCheck.ts`, and `effects/interpreter/primitives.ts`. The rules pass included security timing and removal, battle deletion, search/reveal/shuffle, inherited effects, Once Per Turn identity, Barrier, Armor Purge, Blast Digivolve, Overflow, and Recovery.
