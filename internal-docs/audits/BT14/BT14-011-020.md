## Scope and evidence method

This is a fresh static re-audit. The catalog record for every card was read directly from the
immutable blob with `git cat-file blob efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`, and the local
KB was queried in ascending order with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT14-NNN`. Historical
`internal-docs/audits/BT14.md`, `BT14-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, and the prior ledger were used only to
locate context; their earlier scores were not reused.

The review covered each direct module, its registered IR, the shared interpreter and primitives,
the colocated behavior tests, the registration path, and realistic peers/evolution stacks. The
card modules all have `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and register exactly once through
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. No audited module retains a duplicate `registerCard` call.

The score is five components worth two points each: catalog/rules, IR trace, behavioral proof,
peer/stack proof, and executed gates. Per the dispatch constraint, tests, typecheck, lint,
formatters, browser/UI checks, collection/mechanism gates, and `git diff --check` were not run;
the executed-gates component is therefore `0/2` for every card. These are provisional static
scores with a maximum of `8/10`, not collection completion evidence.

## BT14-017 — Dinorexmon

### Catalog and rules trace

The immutable record is a red Level 6 Mega/Data/Dinosaur/X Antibody Digimon, play cost 11,
11000 DP, with red Level 5 evolution cost 3. Its `[When Digivolving]` grants `＜Blitz＞`; during
all turns, while the opponent has one or more memory, the opponent cannot play Digimon with
6000 DP or less and this Digimon gets +4000 DP. KB Q2379 confirms the opponent-relative positive
memory perspective; Q2380 includes effect-played Digimon; Q2381 includes 6000-DP-or-less Digimon
tokens; Q2382 uses original printed DP even if an On Play effect would raise it; Q2383 checks the
restriction at declaration before payment crosses the gauge; Q2384 disallows paying without a
legal play declaration.

### IR and runtime trace

`BT14-017.ts` separates the clauses correctly: `WhenDigivolving` grants Blitz for the turn;
`AllTurns` supplies a self +4000 aura and an opponent `RestrictPlay` for Digimon with printed DP
at most 6000, both conditioned on opponent-relative `memoryAtLeast(1)`. The RestrictPlay filter
now explicitly carries `allowTokens: true` for Q2381. Registration is exclusively
`registerIrCard("BT14-017", compiled)`.

### Correction and shared seam

The audit found that generic `RestrictPlay` treated every token as exempt, while BT14-017/Q2381
explicitly includes Digimon tokens. The smallest reusable correction adds an opt-in `allowTokens`
field to the shared play match, preserves the default exemption for Q3834, passes the IR filter
through the RestrictPlay interpreter, and makes the token primitive consult the active prohibition
using the resolving effect's source seat. Thus a source player's effect can still play into the
restricted area under the normal source-seat rule, while a restricted player's own Goldramon
effect cannot create the matching tokens. A focused continuous-ledger test covers the opt-in and
the BT14 card test covers the natural effect path.

### Behavioral, peer, and stack proof

`BT14-017.test.ts` covers the natural hand-play prohibition, the +4000 DP live condition, natural
evolution across the memory gauge, the turn-scoped Blitz attack, the effect-played 6000-DP
boundary, and the positive-to-zero memory lift. The Q2380/Q2381 case was upgraded so natural
Goldramon hand play drives its own On Play token effect; the low-DP effect play remains in hand
and no Amon/Umon token enters. Shared continuous DP/restriction, memory perspective, token play,
and Blitz peers cover the mechanism and realistic Dinorexmon evolution stack.
