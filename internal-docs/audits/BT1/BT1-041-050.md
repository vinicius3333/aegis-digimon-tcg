## BT1-041 — Zudomon

**Catalog and clauses.** `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` defines a blue level-5 Ultimate/Vaccine/Sea Beast, play cost 7, 6000 DP, evolving from blue level 4 for 3. Its On Play clause is “Trigger Draw 2”; its inherited When Attacking clause grants 1 memory if the opponent has a Digimon with no digivolution cards in play.

**KB and implementation.** `data/kb/rulings/` query Q898 establishes that the inherited clause grants exactly one memory regardless of how many eligible opponent Digimon exist; Q899 excludes source-less Digimon in the breeding area. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-041.ts` maps On Play to `Draw` amount 2 and inherited When Attacking to `GainMemory` amount 1 with an opponent battle-area Digimon filter and `digivolutionCards:"none"`. The condition is count-minimum-one, so it is existence-based rather than per-target or multiplicity-based.

**Primitives, proof, and stacks.** The trigger/condition path is `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts` plus `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts`. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-041.test.ts` covers Draw 2, exact-one memory with two source-less opponent battle-area Digimon, and no memory for a source-less opponent breeding-area Digimon. The test uses BT1-042 as an evolution/source-stack peer under BT1-041, and `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-041-050.audit.test.ts` checks the compiled clause shape. Sea Beast peers checked include BT1-030/BT1-034 and later BT14-020/BT14-023/BT14-026 implementations.

**Status.** No fidelity gap found. Provisional score: **8/10**; execution gates remain outstanding.

## BT1-043 — SaberLeomon

**Catalog and clauses.** `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` defines a blue level-6 Mega/Data/Ancient Animal with play cost 11, 10000 DP, and blue level-5 evolution for 3. Its When Digivolving clause trashes four digivolution cards under one opponent's Digimon. The committed English catalog text does not explicitly say “from the bottom.”

**KB and implementation.** No BT1-043-specific ruling was found in `data/kb/rulings/`; the local KB therefore does not resolve the omitted source-position qualifier. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-043.ts` compiles the clause as `TrashDigivolution` on one opponent Digimon, amount 4, `fromTop:false`, and `hasAny` source targeting. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts` confirms that this means the bottom four source cards, with fewer than four available handled as a mandatory as-much-as-possible action.

**Proof, peers, and stacks.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-043.test.ts` covers a target with five sources and a target with two sources; the added boundary assertion proves that the remaining card is the top source and the four removed cards are the bottom sources. The audit test verifies the single-target amount-four/from-bottom shape. Bottom-source peers checked include BT10-027 and BT15-019, with source-stack construction from existing digivolution fixtures; the card also uses the ordinary BT1-039-to-SaberLeomon evolution path.

**Ambiguity.** The direct IR and local source-stack primitive are internally consistent with the bottom-four interpretation, but the committed catalog omits the position and the local KB has no card-specific answer. This remains a documentation/source-fidelity ambiguity until the catalog or KB records the qualifier, so the score is intentionally reduced.

**Status.** Implementation is provisionally faithful under the bottom-four interpretation, with a catalog ambiguity. Provisional score: **7/10**; execution gates and catalog/KB clarification remain outstanding.

## BT1-044 — MetalGarurumon

**Catalog and clauses.** `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` defines a blue level-6 Mega/Data/Cyborg with play cost 12, 11000 DP, and blue level-5 evolution for 3. Its When Attacking clause plays one level-4-or-lower digivolution card under this card as another Digimon without paying its memory cost.

**KB and implementation.** `data/kb/rulings/` Q900 says a Digi-Egg source cannot be played; Q901 says the played source enters unsuspended; Q902 says it does not inherit the original source's current effects; Q903 and Q904 establish that the new Digimon's On Play fires at the appropriate timing; Q905 says the newly played Digimon cannot attack that turn; Q906 makes the effect mandatory while an eligible source exists. The prior `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-044.ts` target searched generic digivolution cards across both players. It was corrected to require `zone:"digivolutionCards"`, `controller:"mine"`, a Digimon of level at most 4, and `hostFilter:{isSelfRef:true}` while retaining `PlayWithoutCost` and one-card selection.

**Primitives, proof, and stacks.** `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/play.ts` and `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/loose.ts` now receive the explicit self-host filter; `playInstances` supplies the unsuspended/no-same-turn-attack lifecycle and On Play dispatch described by Q901-Q905. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-044.test.ts` covers the eligible source under the attacking MetalGarurumon, its On Play effect, preservation of the Digi-Egg, rejection of a level-5 source and Digi-Egg, and the added regression with eligible cards under other own and opponent stacks. BT1-044's legal evolution and source stack constitute the realistic play/evolution fixture. BT3-030 was checked as a contrasting “one of your Digimon” multi-stack effect, while BT7-028 and other source-play cards were checked for source-stack lifecycle patterns.

**Status.** Fidelity gap corrected: selection is now restricted to one eligible level-4-or-lower Digimon in the attacking card's own stack. Provisional score: **8/10**; execution gates remain outstanding.
