## Scope and score policy

This range was audited in ascending card-ID order. Each card was checked against the exact committed catalog record, its local rules/QA query, direct TypeScript module, generated compiled-IR aggregate, interpreter/shared primitives, colocated behavioral tests, comparable peers and traits, and a legal evolution-stack scenario where the card is a Digimon.

All scores are provisional. No focused card tests, mechanism tests, typecheck, lint, formatting check, or `git diff --check` was run, per the dispatch instruction. The 2-point execution-gate component is therefore 0/2 and caps every status at 8/10; cards with a material generated-aggregate reconciliation ambiguity are recorded below at 7/10 rather than silently treating the discrepancy as resolved.

The requested correction was completed first: `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-015.test.ts` no longer places level-4 BT1-015 beneath illegal level-4 BT1-016. It now uses legal BT1-021 MetalGreymon over BT1-015 Greymon, evolves that stack into BT1-025 WarGreymon, and asserts catalog DP 9000→13000 and memory 6→3; the prior-range report was updated in focused commit `18bd5e052`.

All ten audited modules use `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and none contains a second explicit `registerCard` call. The `registerCard` invocation internal to `registerIrCard` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` is the expected registry seam, not duplicate card-module registration.
