## Method and shared evidence

Each card was audited in ascending ID order against the committed catalog,
rules knowledge base, direct card module, compiled effect snapshot, shared
interpreter/combat primitives, colocated test, peer cards, traits, and a
realistic evolution or effect stack where the card's text requires one.

- Catalog source: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:1336–1580`,
  covering BT1-031 through BT1-040 with colors, kind, level, play cost, DP,
  evolution requirements, form, attribute, type, and printed effect text.
- KB source: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>` and `data/kb/qa.json`.
  The range has Q889–Q897 and related memory-interaction questions for
  BT1-033, BT1-034, BT1-035, BT1-039, and BT1-040; BT1-031, BT1-032, BT1-036,
  BT1-037, and BT1-038 have no card-specific KB entry.
- Implementation sources: `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-031.ts` through
  `BT1-040.ts`, `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:2640–2736`, the
  colocated `*.test.ts` files, and BT1 aggregate audit suites.
- Shared runtime sources: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts`,
  `scaling.ts`, `matching/permanent.ts`, `actions/restrictions.ts`,
  `actions/board.ts`, `costs.ts`, `actions/resources.ts`,
  `interpreter/effect.ts`, `effects/stack.ts`, combat `legality.ts`, and
  `effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:323–341`.
- Rules sources: `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:546–547,575–577` for
  breeding-area isolation; `2129+` for effect categories and timing,
  `2184+` for end-of-turn effects, `2253+` for activation types, and
  `2329+` for optional processing and targets. `data/kb/rules/glossary.md`
  covers Blocker at `240–245`, Jamming at `280–283`, and Twice Per Turn at
  `156–163`.

All ten direct modules register executable behavior exclusively through
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; none contains a second `registerCard`
call. All ten compiled records declare `coverage: "full"` and
`residual: []`. Those metadata claims remain provisional until the deferred
execution gates run.

The generated `effects.json` snapshot is not uniformly current with the direct
IR. In particular, its BT1-033 record uses an older generated Aura shape,
BT1-034 uses generic `beBlocked`, and BT1-039 puts `optional` on the action
rather than the effect. The direct modules are the executable IR authority and
match the printed clauses and the shared runtime seams, so no generated JSON
edit was made; this representation drift is recorded as an ambiguity for
follow-up rather than treated as a direct-module defect.
