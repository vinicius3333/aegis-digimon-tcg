## Scope, evidence, and score model

The committed catalog contract was read in full for every card at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, including color, kind, level, play cost, DP, evolution requirements, form, attribute, type, effect text, inherited text, rarity, limits, and the absence of Security text where applicable. Each card was queried with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>` and applicable local rules/rulings were traced through `data/kb/qa.json`, `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`, `data/kb/rules/glossary.md`, and the shared interpreter seams. Each direct module and colocated test was read, along with trait/effect peers and realistic evolution/source-stack fixtures.

All ten cards are registered exactly once by `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` from `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/index.ts`; no `registerCard` registration was added or retained for this range. Their direct IR records already report `coverage: "full"` and `residual: []`; no direct module change was needed after tracing the implementations. The proof changes are colocated tests and one range-level IR contract test.

Scoring uses the canonical model: catalog+rules 2/2, IR 2/2, behavior 2/2, peer+stack 2/2, and unrun gates 0/2. Therefore every card is capped at provisional 8/10; no card is claimed as 10/10. The catalog's OCR typo in BT1-078 is explicitly documented below, but Q930-Q933 and the existing engine vocabulary resolve its intended semantic behavior, so it is not treated as an unresolved rules ambiguity.

## Shared primitive and rule findings

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts` maps `WhenAttacking`, `WhenDigivolving`, `YourTurn`, `Static`, and inherited effects to their timing windows; `turnOwnerGuard` and `withSubTriggerTurnScope` preserve owner-turn semantics for continuous watchers.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/resources.ts` resolves `GainMemory`, including negative amounts and the `at:"endOfTurn"` deferred path; `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts` installs the anchorless one-shot delayed memory watcher so it survives source deletion. `data/kb/qa.json` Q925-Q926 and Q1080/Q1087/Q1097 establish the relevant immediate/delayed memory behavior.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts` counts matching permanents for `ModifyDP` scaling, using `controller`, `kind`, and `suspended` filters; `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` §4-27-5 confirms a single trigger does not multiply a fixed gain.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` implements `RevealAdd` and its top-of-deck reveal, bounded selection, add-to-hand, bottom-of-deck ordering, optional digivolution, and short-deck behavior. `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` §§3-1-3, 4-27 and `data/kb/qa.json` Q924/Q930-Q933 cover these source-position and timing rules.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts` maps `whenDeletesInBattle`, scopes `isSelfRef` watchers to the host, carries `[Your Turn]`, and applies source filters. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts` enforces `triggerSourceNotDeletedAtSameTiming`; `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts` emits the battle-delete event only for a surviving winner, including a blocker that redirected an attack.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts`, `matching/permanent.ts`, `matching/trigger.ts`, and `apps/api/src/engine/testkit/observe.ts` provide effective keyword, controller, kind, and target-boundary semantics, including a Blocker granted by another effect.
- Standard play/evolution/source transitions follow `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolve.ts`, `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolveState.ts`, and the rules KB §§4-3, 4-7, 4-8, and 8-1.

## BT1-072 — Woodmon

**Catalog contract.** `cards.json` defines a green level-4 Champion/Virus/Vegetation, play cost 5, 6000 DP, evolving from green level 3 for 2. Its clauses are `Blocker` and `[When Attacking] Lose 2 memory.`

**KB and IR.** `data/kb/qa.json` Q923 confirms that Woodmon can attack with fewer than 2 memory and the attack continues before the loss moves the gauge to the opponent's side. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-072.ts` encodes the keyword in a Static record and the attack clause as `GainMemory` amount -2; no optional or once-per-turn flag is present, matching the text.

**Proof and stacks.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-072.test.ts` proves Blocker redirection, the -2 memory amount, the Q923 low-memory attack boundary, effective absence of Blocker in a source stack, and a legal green level-3-to-Woodmon evolution that activates Blocker only after Woodmon becomes the top card. Static keyword resolution uses `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/keywords.ts` and combat redirection in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts`. Vegetation peers BT1-065/BT1-067/BT1-074/BT1-078 and later Vegetation cards were checked.

**Status.** No fidelity gap found. Provisional score: **8/10** (2/2 catalog+rules, 2/2 IR, 2/2 behavior, 2/2 peer+stack, 0/2 gates).

## BT1-075 — Digitamamon

**Catalog contract.** `cards.json` defines a green level-5 Ultimate/Data/Perfect, play cost 6, 7000 DP, evolving from green level 4 for 3. Its When Attacking clause gains 3 memory, then at end of turn loses 3 memory.

**KB and IR.** `data/kb/qa.json` Q925 says the delayed loss still occurs if Digitamamon is deleted after the attack effect activates; Q926 says announcing a pass after the gain moves the gauge to the opponent and the delayed loss moves it three more. Q1080/Q1087/Q1097/Q1415 cover the general memory-gain lock interaction: a blocked effect gain does not suppress a specified loss. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-075.ts` emits immediate `GainMemory` +3 followed by `GainMemory` -3 with `at:"endOfTurn"`. `resources.ts` and the anchorless delayed-memory primitive preserve that second action after source deletion.

**Proof, peers, and stacks.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-075.test.ts` proves the immediate gain, delayed loss after deletion, the pass-plus-loss boundary, and the added legal green level-4-to-Digitamamon stack with source retention, DP, and cost. Perfect peers BT11-053 and EX9-063 were checked, along with BT1-090 Gravity Crush and BT1-040/BT1-058 for the same immediate/delayed-memory vocabulary.

**Status.** No fidelity gap found. Provisional score: **8/10** (2/2 catalog+rules, 2/2 IR, 2/2 behavior, 2/2 peer+stack, 0/2 gates).

## BT1-077 — Okuwamon

**Catalog contract.** `cards.json` defines a green level-5 Ultimate/Virus/Insectoid, play cost 7, 6000 DP, evolving from green level 4 for 2. Its inherited Your Turn clause gains 1 memory when this Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon in battle and survives.

Q929 says it does activate when the opponent's Digimon is deleted during a block, even when it was not the original attack target. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-077.ts` maps the clause to inherited YourTurn `SubTrigger` event `whenDeletesInBattle`, self/own-Digimon source scope, and a one-memory gain gated by `triggerSourceNotDeletedAtSameTiming`.

**Proof, peers, and stacks.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-077.test.ts` proves a surviving direct battle, a tied battle where both are deleted, Q928 security exclusion, Q929 Blocker redirection, the opponent-turn negative, and the top-card negative. The blocked test imports BT1-072 and resolves the actual Blocker decision before asserting memory. `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts` publishes `whenDeletesInBattle` only for the surviving battle winner; `matching/trigger.ts` binds `isSelfRef` to the host. Battle-deletion peers BT10-045, BT5-062, BT20-044, BT18-005, and BT1-112 were checked. The realistic source stack is BT1-077 under BT1-081; `BT1-079.test.ts` and the range tests also exercise legal green evolution transitions.

**Status.** No fidelity gap found. Provisional score: **8/10** (2/2 catalog+rules, 2/2 IR, 2/2 behavior, 2/2 peer+stack, 0/2 gates).

## BT1-078 — Jagamon

**Catalog contract.** `cards.json` defines a green level-5 Ultimate/Vaccine/Vegetation, play cost 7, 7000 DP, evolving from green level 4 for 3. Its When Attacking text reveals 3 cards, may digivolve this card into one revealed level-6 green Digimon without paying its memory cost, then places the remaining revealed cards at the deck bottom in any order. The catalog text contains an OCR typo (`digivoLv.e`), but the intended digivolution clause is unambiguous from the effect vocabulary and KB.

**KB, IR, and source position.** `data/kb/qa.json` Q930 confirms the player may reveal and decline the digivolution, returning all revealed cards to the bottom in any order. Q931 places the digivolution bonus draw at the stack-placement point and draws from unrevealed deck cards; Q932 says the remaining revealed cards are bottomed before the new card's When Digivolving window; Q933 allows activation with 2 or fewer deck cards and reveals as many as possible. `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-078.ts` uses `RevealAdd` revealCount 3, an optional free `digivolveOption` into a green level-6 Digimon on the self host, and `deckBottomAnyOrder`. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` implements the exact order: reveal, optional selection, evolution bonus draw, bottom the remainder, then the evolved card's When Digivolving effects.

**Proof, peers, and stacks.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT1/BT1-078.test.ts` proves free level-6 evolution, optional refusal, Q932 ordering before the new card's When Digivolving window, Q933 short-deck reveal, and the added legal green level-4-to-Jagamon stack transition. The range audit test verifies green/level-6/self/optional/no-cost shape. Reveal/digivolve peers BT1-074, BT1-067, BT1-087, BT19-056, BT10-056, and later optional reveal-evolution cards were checked.

**Status.** The OCR spelling is documented but semantically resolved; no fidelity gap found. Provisional score: **8/10** (2/2 catalog+rules, 2/2 IR, 2/2 behavior, 2/2 peer+stack, 0/2 gates).
