## Shared seams, snapshots, peers, and stacks

The shared interpreter trace covered:

- `nameExact` versus substring `name` matching in `cardPredicates.ts` and `matching/definition.ts`. Bracketed card references such as `[Ravemon]`, `[ProtoGizmon]`, `[Akihiro Kurata]`, and `[Belphemon: Rage Mode]` use literal matching; prose explicitly saying “with `[Lucemon]` in its name” or “cards with `[Gizmon]` in their names” remains substring matching.
- Cost `return` handling in `costs.ts`, including `to: "deckBottom"` and `orderReturnedCards: true`, plus the order-card decision seam. BT13-083's direct IR now puts both fields on the `CostGatedBlock` cost object.
- `CostGatedBlock`'s one-payment wrapper and nested optional action, which allow BT13-083 to pay the two-card return and independently decline the XT play. The same optional-cost pattern is used by BT13-088: the Rage Mode placement cost is optional, while `abortOnDecline` on the parent Restrict prevents immunity/restriction from applying when it is declined.
- Natural `playCard`, `OnPlay`, `OnDeletion`, `WhenAttacking`, `OnEndTurn`, `whenTrashedFromHand`, and `whenOpponentAttacks` event dispatch; the shared optional and card-selection responders; nested play-cost reduction; temporary/static restriction; delayed end-of-opponent-turn subscriptions; and reveal/add/trash movement.

## Per-card audit

### BT13-081 — Porcupamon — provisional 8/10

The direct module is full/no-residual and has one exclusive registration. Its On Play and On Deletion effects each delete one opponent Lv.3 Digimon. The inherited End of Opponent's Turn effect is Once Per Turn and draws one, then trashes one own hand card.

`BT13-081.test.ts` drives natural On Play deletion, natural deletion of the source, and an inherited stack event at the opponent's end of turn. The live source confirms both deletion origins and draw-then-trash. The generated snapshot is equivalent for this card. No card-specific KB ambiguity remains.

### BT13-082 — Peckmon — provisional 8/10

The direct module gives Blocker and an inherited On Deletion hand-trash action selected by the opponent, gated by the structured `triggerRemovalCause != byBattle` condition. The direct condition preserves Q2327's Retaliation/outside-battle boundary.

`BT13-082.test.ts` drives a real outside-battle host deletion and a real `byBattle` deletion, proving the opponent hand card moves only in the former case. The snapshot's raw condition is drift evidence only; it was not edited. The shared deletion-cause predicate and Blocker keyword ledger provide the traced peer/mechanism support.

### BT13-083 — Gizmon: AT — provisional 8/10

The direct module's replacement source is the exact `[Gizmon: AT]` card, and the XT play target is the exact `[Gizmon: XT]` card. Its On Play draw/trash and permanent no-digivolution restriction remain explicit. The On Deletion action is an optional `CostGatedBlock`: its cost returns two own `[Gizmon]` name-substring cards from trash to `deckBottom` with `orderReturnedCards: true`, and its nested optional action then plays exact XT free. The substring is correct here because the catalog says cards with `[Gizmon]` in their names.

`BT13-083.test.ts` drives the real On Play draw/trash and natural On Deletion paths. It proves Q2330 cost-only return with no XT in trash, wrapper decline leaves all cards in place, nested XT-play decline still pays and returns the two cards, and a manually reversed bottom-deck order using distinct BT13-083 and BT13-080 instances. The behavioral component is now 2/2. Q2328-Q2330 and peers BT13-028/BT13-033 support the no-digivolution, cost-only, and ordered-return interpretation.

### BT13-084 — Astamon — provisional 8/10

Both On Play and When Digivolving actions are optional: delete one other own Purple Digimon, then free-digivolve this Digimon into a hand card with `[Belphemon]` in its name. The inherited Opponent's Turn Once Per Turn watcher listens for a card trashed from hand and may play one own Lv.4-or-lower Purple Digimon from trash free. The `[Belphemon]` and Purple filters are intentionally broad because the catalog uses “in its name” and the level/color restrictions.

`BT13-084.test.ts` drives accepted optional On Play processing through a real `playCard` intent with the printed 7-memory payment, declined optional On Play processing, and a natural hand-trash event that activates the inherited rescue. The live source proves deletion/payment ordering, no-cost evolution, optional decline, and Once Per Turn inherited play. There is no card-specific KB entry and no direct module correction was needed.

### BT13-085 — Crowmon — provisional 8/10

The direct When Attacking action is optional, requires an own Tamer, and digivolves the self into the exact `[Ravemon]` from trash for its printed evolution cost. The inherited outside-battle deletion rescue remains an optional free play of an own Lv.4-or-lower Purple Digimon from trash. The exact Ravemon correction is necessary because the catalog brackets a card name rather than saying “in its name.”

`BT13-085.test.ts` drives the inherited rescue through natural host deletion and drives the attacking effect with a real attack intent, a Tamer, and a live Ravemon in trash; the attack's printed evolution cost is paid by the live engine. The snapshot still has substring Ravemon matching and was not edited. Q2331 and the BT13-092/BT26 Ravemon peers support the outside-battle source boundary.

### BT13-086 — Gizmon: XT — provisional 7/10

The direct replacement source now identifies exact `[Gizmon: XT]`; its level-4 deletion cost reduces play cost by 6 and remains optional/abortable. The On Play `[Akihiro Kurata]` target is exact and mandatory, matching the catalog's “Play 1” wording rather than a “may” clause. The All Turns no-digivolution restriction is permanent. The snapshot's RawUnparsed Akihiro path and substring identities were read-only drift evidence.

### BT13-087 — Dynasmon — provisional 8/10

The On Play and When Digivolving reveal effects each reveal four, add up to two cards from the union of Lucemon-name substring or Royal Knight trait, and trash the rest. The Your Turn watcher listens for another own qualifying Digimon and deletes all opponent Lv.4-or-lower Digimon. No exact matching is substituted for the catalog's explicit “in its name” text.

`BT13-087.test.ts` drives the Your Turn deletion through a real Royal Knight play and drives the On Play four-card reveal through a real `playCard` intent with one Lucemon, one Royal Knight, and two non-matches, proving both qualifying cards reach hand and both remainder cards reach trash. Q2332-Q2333 and the Royal Knight peers support the union and up-to-two semantics. Structural When Digivolving parity is not treated as a separate executed gate.

### BT13-088 — Belphemon: Sleep Mode — provisional 8/10

Both On Play and When Digivolving now model the CR 15-7 processing cost as optional at the cost level, with `abortOnDecline` on the parent Restrict. Accepting places exact `[Belphemon: Rage Mode]` from trash as this card's top stack card and then applies the until-opponent-turn-end attack restriction and opponent-effect immunity; declining leaves the card unchanged and applies neither consequence. The alternate requirement is `namesExact: ["Belphemon: Rage Mode"]`, preventing a name extension from satisfying the bracketed source, and the Opponent's Turn Once Per Turn attack watcher remains an optional two-hand-card cost ending the attack.

`BT13-088.test.ts` drives real `playCard` acceptance and decline, proving placement plus both restrictions/immunity only after payment. It also drives a real opponent attack through the card-owned Once Per Turn watcher, proving the first attack ends after two hand cards are paid and a second same-turn attack is not intercepted. The snapshot's substring alternate and flattened optional metadata remain untouched; no separate When Digivolving event is claimed beyond direct parity.

### BT13-089 — Ravemon — provisional 8/10

The End of Your Turn action is optional and requires deleting this self only when its stack has Bird or Avian. It arms the shared `nextEndOfOpponentTurn` delayed effect, which may play an exact own `[Ravemon]` from trash free. The On Deletion action may play an exact `[Falcomon]` or `[Keenan Crier]` from hand or trash free. Exact matching is required for each bracketed card identity.

`BT13-089.test.ts` drives the complete delayed path naturally: an eligible stacked Ravemon is deleted at its own end of turn and a Ravemon from trash is played at the next opponent end. A separate natural deletion source proves exact Falcomon play from hand. Q2334-Q2335 and BT17-069 support delayed timing and stack/Burst boundaries. The snapshot omits the delayed action and was not edited.

The same source also drives Q2335's Burst boundary naturally: BT13-092 Ravemon: Burst Mode is placed on top by a real alternate digivolution, then the source's end-of-turn top-card trash does not arm BT13-089 while Burst Mode remains on top; the opponent's subsequent end of turn leaves BT13-089 in place. This proves the delayed watcher is not falsely armed by a non-Bird/Avian top card.

### BT13-090 — LordKnightmon — provisional 8/10

The On Play and When Digivolving returns are optional and target the printed union of Lucemon name substring or Royal Knight trait from own trash to hand. Its Opponent's Turn Once Per Turn watcher listens for a real opponent Digimon attack and gains one memory per own Royal Knight Digimon.

`BT13-090.test.ts` drives accepted and declined natural On Play return and a natural opponent attack with the source LordKnightmon plus BT13-075 and BT13-087 in battle area, proving the gain is 3 (the source counts). A breeding-only BT13-087 contributes zero, so the same attack gains only 1; a second same-turn opponent attack leaves memory unchanged, proving the card-owned Once Per Turn boundary. The direct filter remains intentionally trait/name broad where the catalog says "with `[Lucemon]` in its name or the `[Royal Knight]` trait," while its explicit `zone: "battleArea"` excludes breeding. Mixed-family peers and the shared attack/scaling/Once Per Turn seams were reviewed.
