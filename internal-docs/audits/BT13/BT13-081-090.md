### BT13-082 — Peckmon — provisional 8/10

### BT13-083 — Gizmon: AT — provisional 7/10

The direct module's replacement source is the exact `[Gizmon: AT]` card, and the XT play target is the exact `[Gizmon: XT]` card. Its On Play draw/trash and permanent no-digivolution restriction remain explicit. The On Deletion cost now explicitly returns two own `[Gizmon]` name-substring cards from trash to `deckBottom` with `orderReturnedCards: true`, then optionally plays exact XT free.
