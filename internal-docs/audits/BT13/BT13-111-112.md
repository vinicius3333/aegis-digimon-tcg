## Catalog and local KB evidence

The immutable catalog records, in ascending order, establish these printed facts:

- **BT13-111 Gallantmon** is a red level-6 Digimon, play cost 13, DP 13,000, with a red level-5 evolution cost of 5 and the Holy Warrior/Royal Knight traits. When this card would be played from the hand, if its controller has no Digimon, its play cost is reduced by 2 for every five total cards in both players' trashes. It has Rush, and its On Play, When Digivolving, and When Attacking effects each delete one opponent Digimon at 6,000 DP or less; if that effect deleted no opponent Digimon, it deletes one opponent Digimon at 13,000 DP or more.
- **BT13-112 Omnimon** is a white level-7 Digimon, play cost 14, DP 14,000, with red level-6 evolution cost 4 or blue level-6 evolution cost 4 and the Holy Warrior/Royal Knight traits. On Play and When Digivolving, its controller may delete one opponent Digimon, or play one of each different-name Royal Knight among the digivolution cards of the controller's Digimon in the breeding area without paying costs. When cards are played by that effect, the breeding Digimon is trashed and all the controller's Digimon gain Rush for the turn.

The local KB results were:

- **Q2364**: “for every 5 cards in both players' trash” is a combined count; 12 own-trash plus 8 opponent-trash cards gives four groups and an 8-memory reduction.
- **Q2365**: a +1,000 DP max-deletion modifier raises the 6,000 threshold to 7,000 but does not change the independent 13,000 fallback threshold.
- **Q2366**: BT13-112's breeding-stack branch plays one card for each distinct Royal Knight name represented by the stack; the source Omnimon itself is not a stack card and is not part of that set.
- **Q2367**: “You must play all of the Digimon that you can play or play no Digimon.” A Royal Knight prohibited by RestrictPlay is not one that can be played, so it is excluded before selection; every remaining playable distinct-name Royal Knight is played.
- **Q2368**: after cards are played from the breeding stack, the breeding Digimon and remaining stack cards are trashed.
- **Q2369**: Overflow is processed when stack cards move to the trash, so the host/remaining-stack trash path must use ordinary trash movement rather than silently removing cards.

The rules review also covered §15-1-7's unspecified-area default (battle area, not breeding), §3-4-7's breeding-area isolation, §15-1-2's ordered effect processing, exact versus substring name matching, standard evolution costs from the catalog card definition, and the effect-play restriction and source-zone seams.

## Shared seams, snapshots, peers, and stacks

The shared interpreter trace covered:

- `youHaveNone` and `countMatching`, where an unspecified area can include breeding in the shared count path; BT13-111 therefore now carries an explicit `zone: "battleArea"` for the printed no-Digimon gate required by §15-1-7.
- `collectWouldBePlayedSelfReducers` in `registration/reducers.ts`, whose verified-card allowlist is required for automatic pay-time self-reducer behavior. BT13-111 is now registered there with its combined-trash/no-battle-area proof.
- The modal resolver and `PlayWithoutCost` loose-card targeting path: BT13-112 uses `from: ["digivolutionCards"]`, a breeding `hostFilter`, Royal Knight trait matching, `distinctNames: true`, and `count: "all"`.
- The filtered `PlayWithoutCost` path: RestrictPlay removes cards that the resolving player cannot legally play before selection, and `playInstances` then moves the remaining playable cards. For BT13-112, this is important because Q2367 says to play all Digimon that can be played; a prohibited Royal Knight is not in that playable set and remains under the breeding host until the subsequent cleanup action.
- `playInstances`'s stack-origin bookkeeping and `fireEnteredByEffect` behavior. Each effect-played Digimon is placed in the battle area, its own On Play is eligible, then the one batch's `whenPlayed` event is published. The BT13-112 effect itself does not rely on a nested On Play side effect, but this is the shared path that makes the play origin and resulting stack movement observable.
- The standard card-definition `evoCosts` path. BT13-112's red/blue level-6 cost-4 routes are present in the immutable card definition; they are not alternate requirements and therefore are not duplicated in `CompiledCard.digivolutionRequirement`.

Read-only snapshot drift was recorded without editing `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`:

Its static Rush placement and deletion thresholds are otherwise recognizable.

## Per-card audit

### BT13-111 — Gallantmon — provisional 8/10

The direct module at `apps/api/src/cards/BT13/BT13-111.ts` is full/no-residual and has one exclusive registration. It gives the source card permanent Rush. Its BeforePayCost replacement is self-scoped, reduces by 2, and scales by every five cards in both players' trashes. The gate is now explicit `youHaveNone` over own battle-area Digimon, preserving the printed “you have no Digimon” default while excluding a breeding-only Digimon. On Play, When Digivolving, and When Attacking each carry the ordered 6,000-or-less deletion followed by the structured `ifThisEffectDidNotDelete` 13,000-or-more fallback.

The source case `BT13-111.test.ts` now drives the cost path with 12 own-trash and 8 opponent-trash cards, a breeding-only own Digimon, and five memory. The 20 combined trash cards grant an 8-memory reduction, making the 13-cost hand play affordable and distinguishing the reduction from an unreduced play. A companion case places an own battle-area Digimon and 20 trash cards with 12 memory; the self-reducer is correctly unavailable and the play consumes 13 memory, proving the explicit zone boundary. The same test source also drives natural On Play low-target deletion and high-target fallback, legal red level-5 digivolution, and an attack intent.

The shared allowlist correction is executable rather than merely structural: without the `BT13-111` entry in `VERIFIED_SELF_REDUCER_CARDS`, the nested replacement would not be collected into the real pay-time reducer path. The direct source and its natural cost cases now establish both the eligibility condition and the combined-trash calculation. Q2364 and Q2365 support the count and independent threshold interpretation; BT13-045/BT9-112 support the verified self-reducer seam.

The generated snapshot remains stale in the ways listed above and was not used as executable authority. No further card-level ambiguity remains in this static review. Execution is intentionally unrun, so the executed-gates component is `0/2`.

### BT13-112 — Omnimon — provisional 8/10

The direct module at `apps/api/src/cards/BT13/BT13-112.ts` is full/no-residual and has one exclusive registration. Both On Play and When Digivolving are the same optional modal: option 0 deletes one opponent Digimon; option 1 gathers one of each different-name Royal Knight from the controller's breeding-area digivolution cards, plays every selected card free, trashes the breeding host when at least one card was played, and grants all own Digimon Rush for the turn. The `hostFilter: { zone: "breeding" }` is paired with `from: ["digivolutionCards"]`, so stack cards under battle-area permanents are not admitted. The standard red/blue level-6 cost-4 evolution routes are sourced from the card-definition `evoCosts` as printed, rather than incorrectly added as alternate IR requirements.

The direct `PlayWithoutCost` action carries `distinctNames: true` plus `count: "all"` for Q2366/Q2367. The interpreter first filters cards that the resolving player is prohibited from playing, then selects one card for each remaining distinct name. Thus a blocked Royal Knight is not treated as playable for Q2367, while every distinct Royal Knight that can be played is included; duplicate copies of one name collapse to one selected card.

`BT13-112.test.ts` drives the printed behavior through natural On Play and When Digivolving intents. The deletion branch removes an opposing Digimon; the breeding branch uses a real breeding host with Magnamon, Gallantmon, and a duplicate Magnamon stack, and proves one Magnamon plus one Gallantmon are played, the host is trashed, and both played cards and Omnimon have Rush. The optional modal decline case plays Omnimon but leaves the opposing Digimon intact.

The Q2367 boundary uses the production continuous restriction ledger plus a natural Omnimon On Play branch: a 10,000-DP ceiling blocks the 7,000-DP Magnamon while allowing the 13,000-DP Gallantmon. Gallantmon is played because it is the distinct Royal Knight that can be played; the blocked Magnamon remains under the breeding host until the next ordered cleanup action, which trashes the host and all remaining stack cards and grants Rush because at least one Digimon was played. The source asserts the production `cardPlayed` event precedes the breeding-to-trash cleanup event, rather than manually injecting a trigger or relying on an object-shape assertion.

The snapshot's generic deletion/watcher structure is drift evidence only. BT13-110 and BT13-007 provide realistic breeding-stack and Royal Knight peers; Q2366-Q2369 provide the distinct-name, batch, cleanup, and Overflow interpretations. Because no tests or gates were executed, the execution component remains `0/2` despite the natural source cases.
