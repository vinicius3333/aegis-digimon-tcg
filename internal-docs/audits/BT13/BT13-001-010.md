## Shared seam findings

The following shared behavior was traced once and then applied card by card:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:273-335` and `packages/shared/src/effects/ir/filters/cardPredicates.ts:96-105` distinguish `match: "name"` substring matching from `match: "nameExact"` normalized literal equality. Comprehensive Rules §2-3-1-2 requires bracket-only references such as `[Marcus Damon]`, `[BaoHuckmon]`, `[Garudamon]`, and `[Kristy Damon]` to be exact; §2-3-1-3 gives the broader substring semantics for wording such as `with [Sistermon] in its name`.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts:22-100` makes the condition-counting default include battle-area and breeding permanents, while an explicit `zone: "battleArea"` excludes breeding and `zone: "breeding"` selects breeding. `conditions.ts:230-243` routes `opponentHas` through this seam. This is why BT13-004 needed an explicit battle-area boundary, while BT13-002 already had one.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:21-130` enumerates target permanents and defaults target enumeration to battle area. Thus BT13-001's delete target, which has no explicit zone, still resolves through the target seam as battle-area-only; its condition is not the broader `countMatching` default.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/statics.ts` re-evaluates Aura `while` conditions and applies the current DP ledger. `subTrigger.ts` routes suspension, security removal, play, option-play, and deletion events, including event source filters and once-per-turn state. `builders.ts` supplies timing/base guards and the breeding-area trigger boundary.
- `actions/digivolve.ts` and `actions/runAction.ts` enforce destination, hand source, optional processing, cost, alternate requirements, and `ignoreRequirements`. `actions/removal.ts` applies Delete targets and DP ceilings. `actions/resources.ts` handles Draw/GainMemory and the relevant modifiers. `actions/placeUnder.ts` and `actions/reveal.ts` were traced for BT13-007's breeding/egg-deck operations.
- Comprehensive Rules §3-4-5-3 was used for breeding-area affected-card boundaries; §4-14 for drawing; §4-15 and §15-16-4 for deletion/On Deletion; §8-1-2-2 for ignoring digivolution requirements; §15-3 for inherited effects; §15-4-3 and §15-15-7 for simultaneous triggers/activation order; §15-5-2 for one trigger-condition occurrence; §15-8-4 for optional processing; §15-12-1 for treated-as-Digimon information; and §15-16 for timing windows.
