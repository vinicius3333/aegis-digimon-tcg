# BT13-101–BT13-110 static implementation audit

## Scope and authority

This audit covers BT13-101 through BT13-110 in exact ascending order. The printed catalog authority was the immutable catalog blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` (verified as a blob before review), with the repository card catalog at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` used for card definitions and the local ruling knowledge base at `data/kb/qa.json` queried for each card. The generated snapshot `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was inspected only as read-only drift evidence; it was not edited.

For every card, the direct module under `apps/api/src/cards/BT13` was traced into the shared IR interpreter, including matching, targeting, costs, timing, conditions, duration, and registration seams. Colocated tests, relevant peers, and realistic card stacks were inspected; source-level behavior cases were added where a load-bearing clause needed a stronger boundary. Every module uses the exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` path. No `registerCard` path was added or retained.

Per-card scoring has five two-point components: catalog/rules, IR trace, behavioral proof, peer/stack proof, and executed gates. Executed gates are fixed at 0/2 because this audit intentionally ran no tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI, collection/mechanism, or `git diff --check` command. Consequently, 8/10 is the maximum honest score in this report; source cases are evidence in the repository but were not executed under the task restriction.

## Shared interpreter seams

- Definition matching treats `colors` as an any-color predicate and `colorsAll` as an all-color predicate; `multicolor` only requires at least two colors. BT13-101 therefore needed `colorsAll: ["Yellow", "Black"]` to implement the printed two-color requirement.
- `whenPlayed` `byEffect: true` is evaluated by the trigger matcher against effect-entry provenance. This distinguishes BT13-102’s effect-play trigger from an ordinary main-phase play/DigiXros action, while still accepting either player’s effect-played Digimon.
- `opponentDeclinedTrash` reads the current effect’s `lastOpponentDeclined` receipt. The opponent-directed optional hand-trash path now records a zero-card selection as a decline, allowing BT13-102’s following gain-memory/draw branch to resolve.
- Dynamic target filters use a prior `SelectBind` selection and `relativeTo: { attr, op, selectionRef }`. The former BT13-107 `dpOfChosen` object was not consumed by permanent matching; the corrected IR binds the chosen own Digimon and compares opposing suspended Digimon against that bound DP.
- Stack-card return costs resolve through loose digivolution-card candidates. The shared cost seam now honors `topCardOnly` for both payability and payment, filtering each host’s candidates to `host.stack.at(-1)` before returning the selected card.
- `GrantAuraToOpponents` routes the two BT13-108 quoted effects through the registered granted-effect library. The resulting synthetic effects remain anchored to the granted permanent, use the normal timing/subtrigger bus, and preserve the Option-only opponent-effect boundary.

## Card-by-card findings

### BT13-101 — Miki Kurosaki & Megumi Shirakawa

**Catalog/rules.** The immutable catalog prints an On Play option to play one Digimon with `[PawnChessmon] in its name` from hand without paying, and an All Turns trigger when the player plays a two-color black/yellow Digimon: suspend this Tamer to Draw 1 and gain 1 memory. Security plays this Tamer without paying. Local KB Q2348 (line 5876 of `data/kb/qa.json`) confirms both color orders satisfy the two-color condition; Q2349 (line 5883) confirms the trigger still fires when the played Digimon is immediately deleted by a prior DP-zero effect.

**IR and snapshot.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT13/BT13-101.ts:8-95` has the optional PawnChessmon play, an All Turns `whenPlayed` SubTrigger, a suspend cost that aborts the Draw on decline, and the conditional memory tail. The implementation correction changes the watcher’s color predicate to `colorsAll: ["Yellow", "Black"]`; the previous `colors` shape could admit a multicolor Digimon with only one of the required colors. The snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:23642-23707` retains the old any-color `colors` shape and marks the draw/memory portions optional, so it is drift evidence rather than the executable authority.

**Behavior and peers.** `BT13-101.test.ts:53-106` retains the PawnChessmon play case and adds a live ordinary play of black/yellow BT13-035 that draws and suspends the Tamer, plus a red/yellow BT10-009 Shoutmon X4 negative case that receives no reward. This is a genuine color boundary rather than a structural assertion. The effect uses the shared `SubTrigger` and `ifThisEffectActed` seams used by comparable two-color Tamer implementations.

**Score: 8/10.** Catalog/rules 2/2; IR trace 2/2; behavioral proof 2/2; peer/stack proof 2/2; executed gates 0/2.

### BT13-102 — Keenan Crier

**Catalog/rules.** The catalog prints an On Play choice for the opponent to trash one Tamer or Option from hand; if they do not, the activating player gains 1 memory and Draws 1. During the opponent’s turn, when an effect plays a Digimon, the Tamer may be suspended to gain 1 memory. Security plays it without paying. Q2350 (`data/kb/qa.json:5892`) assigns the reward to the player who activated the card; Q2351 (`:5899`) says the opponent-turn trigger fires for either player’s effect-played Digimon; Q2352 (`:5906`) excludes an ordinary DigiXros play but includes DigiXros performed inside an effect-played Digimon event.

**IR and snapshot.** `BT13-102.ts:8-97` has the opponent chooser, optional/up-to hand trash, `opponentDeclinedTrash` reward conditions, and an Opponent’s Turn `whenPlayed` watcher with `byEffect: true` and a suspend cost. The snapshot at `effects.json:23708-23762` drifts: it uses `upTo: false`, raw conditions, and a watcher without the executable `byEffect` predicate. The source module remains the executable authority.

**Correction and behavior.** The shared hand-removal seam in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts:456-463` now records `lastOpponentDeclined` when an opponent-directed optional hand selection resolves with zero cards (or no cards move). This is the smallest shared correction for the printed “may trash” decision and does not alter mandatory hand-trash actions. `BT13-102.test.ts:49-82` proves the zero-card/no-hand decline reward and naturally effect-plays BT13-035 through the BT13-101 On Play path during the opponent’s turn, proving the `byEffect` watcher suspends Keenan.

**Score: 8/10.** Catalog/rules 2/2; IR trace 2/2; behavioral proof 2/2; peer/stack proof 2/2; executed gates 0/2.

### BT13-107 — Vulcan Crusher

**Behavior and peers.** `BT13-107.test.ts:9-93` updates structural assertions to the bound dynamic filter and exact name, retains the Security proof, and adds a natural Main case with an own DP ceiling, one qualifying suspended opposing Digimon, one nonqualifying high-DP opposing Digimon, and an exact Leopardmon: Leopard Mode card at the top of an own evolution stack. The source case proves the low target returns, the high target remains, the host unsuspends, and the exact stack card moves to hand. **Score: 8/10.** Catalog/rules 2/2; IR trace 2/2; behavioral proof 2/2; peer/stack proof 2/2; executed gates 0/2.

1. BT13-101 direct IR now requires both Yellow and Black with `colorsAll`, eliminating false positives for unrelated two-color Digimon.

5. BT13-109’s bracketed `[Belphemon: Sleep Mode]` evolution target now uses exact-name matching.
6. BT13-110’s bracketed `[King Drasil_7D6]` breeding host target now uses exact-name matching.

The source-proof additions cover BT13-101, BT13-102, BT13-103, BT13-104, BT13-107, and BT13-108; exact-name structural assertions were updated for BT13-109 and BT13-110. No duplicate registration route exists in any audited module.
