## Authority, method, and score policy

The printed source for this audit was the immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`, read with `git cat-file blob`.
Cards were processed in the order BT13-071, BT13-072, BT13-073, BT13-074,
BT13-075, BT13-076, BT13-077, BT13-078, BT13-079, and BT13-080. For every
card, the local knowledge base was queried with
`node tools/kb/query.mjs card <cardId>`, followed by inspection of the direct
TypeScript module, its compiled IR, the generated effects snapshot as
read-only drift evidence, shared interpreter/mechanism seams, colocated
behavioral source, relevant peers, and realistic evolution/zone stacks.

The direct card module is executable through the exclusive
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration path. All ten modules in this
range use that path and no card-local duplicate `registerCard` registration is
present. `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and ledger
`BT13-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were read-only and were not edited.

Each card is scored across five two-point components: catalog/rules fidelity,
IR trace fidelity, behavioral proof, peer/stack proof, and executed gates.
Executed gates are fixed at `0/2` because this task explicitly prohibited
focused tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI checks, mechanism and
collection gates, and `git diff --check`. A structural assertion is not treated
as runtime proof; behavioral `2/2` is awarded only where source-level tests
exercise the relevant behavior-driving clauses. These are static audit scores,
not a claim of collection completion or 10/10 results.

## Shared rules and interpreter trace

- Comprehensive Rules §2-2 and §4-3-1 (`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`)
  distinguish Digi-Egg and Digimon card categories while treating Digi-Eggs
  and Digimon cards placed on the field as Digimon. This is material to
  BT13-080's level-2 breeding-area deletion cost: the direct target explicitly
  accepts both `Digimon` and `DigiEgg` kinds.
- Comprehensive Rules §3-4-7 defines the breeding area as separate from the
  battle area and constrains effects that can act across that boundary. The
  BT13-080 reduction target explicitly uses `zone: "breeding"`.
- Comprehensive Rules §15-5 through §15-8 cover trigger timing, effect
  resolution, and once-per-turn identity. These rules support the inherited
  end-of-turn and security/deletion watchers in BT13-071, BT13-072, BT13-078,
  and BT13-079, as well as the once-per-turn effects in BT13-075 and BT13-076.
- Comprehensive Rules §15-12 and §15-15-5 cover changes to DP and restrictions
  on affected Digimon. The direct `Restrict`/`GrantStatic` representations
  preserve BT13-072's conditional DP immunity and BT13-077's opponent-Digimon
  effect immunity.
- Comprehensive Rules §15-7-1 through §15-7-4 make a “By placing ...”
  processing condition optional and provide the decline/unperformed-cost
  consequence. BT13-075 therefore uses an optional placement cost with
  `abortOnDecline: true`, so declining the cost skips both restrictions.
- The shared restriction seam in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/restrictions.ts:49-60`
  re-evaluates `whileMatchesTargetFilter` against the live permanent. This is
  required for BT13-075's cost-9-to-cost-13 evolution boundary.
- Comprehensive Rules §16-11 defines Reboot, supporting BT13-074's dynamic
  Reboot aura. The keyword is represented in the direct IR rather than by a
  stale snapshot-only unsuspend action.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:323-341`
  normalizes compiled IR, registers the compiled record, and installs the
  executable runtime. Every module here ends with `registerIrCard` and does not
  register the same card through a legacy path.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:273-339`
  provides name and trait matching used by the direct filters. In this range,
  `name` is correct for printed “in name” clauses such as Chessmon, Mamemon,
  Etemon/Sukamon, and Gizmon; trait matching is used for X Antibody and Royal
  Knight.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/combat.ts:9-55` executes
  the direct `Attack` action used by BT13-077. Its neighboring
  `RedirectAttack` path (`:81-101`) only redirects an attack already in flight
  and is a no-op when no attack exists, which is why BT13-077 was corrected to
  a forced `Attack` action.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` executes
  reveal/add/play and rest-to-trash sequencing used by BT13-072 and BT13-074.
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolve.ts:439-457`
  carries reduction fields into digivolution resolution; the BT13-080 play
  reduction uses the corresponding replacement mechanism.
- The shared keyword, aura, restriction, replacement, security, deletion,
  and place-under actions were traced through their interpreter seams for each
  direct module. No shared engine change was required by this range.
