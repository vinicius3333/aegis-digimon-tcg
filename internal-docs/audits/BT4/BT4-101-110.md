## Shared interpreter, peer, and stack evidence

- `runEffect` preserves action order and abort behavior (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts:597-618`), while `runAction` dispatches Return, TrashDigivolution, SecurityManipulation, GrantAura, RevealAdd, board actions, and keyword actions to their shared implementations (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:451-515,613-733`).
- GrantAuraToOpponents installs the exact library-granted effect through the statics path (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/statics.ts:83-209`; BT4-101's exact text mapping is `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/grantedEffects.ts:163-175`). The attack-target and no-source condition are therefore evaluated by the shared granted-effect mechanism rather than a card-local callback.
- `returnToHand` collects a whole permanent and trashes ordinary attachments as rule teardown (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:3523-3613`), while `runTrashDigivolution` fires `whenDigivolutionTrashed`. BT6-002 Q1399 proves the former is required here: the printed trash sentence after a return is explanatory and must not fire a source-trash-by-effect watcher.
- RevealAdd records the exact number of cards it added to hand and places the remainder at deck bottom (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:108-374`); this is the shared seam used by BT4-107's named count. The target matcher supports text-based name/trait matching and the `allowTokens` exception (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/permanent.ts:635-729`; `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:272-341`).
- Suspend and Unsuspend are ordered board actions (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/board.ts:50-111`). `sameTarget` uses the previous target binding (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:373-375`), `lastTargetDpAtLeast` evaluates the boosted target's threshold (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:70-77`), and dynamic play-cost ceilings use base plus per-matching-card scaling (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/permanent.ts:583-604`).
- `addSecurity` is the shared place-as-security seam (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:4015-4193`). It now diverts a Digi-Egg to the bottom of its owner's Digi-Egg deck and drops a token instead of inserting either into Security, while only real added security cards fire the security-added subtrigger. This is the minimal shared correction required by BT4-105 Q1270-Q1272 and comprehensive rules §3-1-3-9.

BT6-002 Q1399 and its focused return-teardown negative are the controlling peer evidence for BT4-102/103; `addSecurity` supplies the equivalent canonical whole-stack movement for BT4-105. Other inspected peers cover target scaling, GrantAura, and RevealAdd. Legal stack fixtures remain in the assigned focused files, and the aggregate proof imports all ten direct modules and guards the corrected canonical movement shapes.
