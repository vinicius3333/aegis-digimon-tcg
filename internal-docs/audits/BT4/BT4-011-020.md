## Audit policy and canonical score

This is a static audit. I inspected the committed catalog, the card-specific local KB results, the rules KB, direct TypeScript modules, the generated effects snapshot, shared interpreter primitives, focused tests, and legal source stacks. I corrected only proven defects in the assigned range, did not edit `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`, and did not run tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI validation, or `git diff --check`.

Each card is scored against five canonical components worth two points each:

1. Catalog and rules fidelity — 2/2 when the printed identity and rules clauses are accounted for.
2. Direct IR completeness and registration — 2/2 when the direct module maps the printed behavior and uses only `registerIrCard`.
3. Shared-engine semantic trace — 2/2 when the real primitives support the mapped clauses, including timing, targeting, costs, zones, and duration.
4. Focused, boundary, peer, and legal-stack tests — 2/2 when static test evidence covers the meaningful positive and negative contracts.
5. Executed validation gates — 0/2 because execution was explicitly prohibited for this pass.

All ten cards therefore receive a provisional **8/10**, never 10/10. The range remains provisional until the deferred gates are run; this report does not claim collection completion.

### Direct IR, generated snapshot, and interpreter

All ten direct modules use one executable registration path, `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`, with no duplicate `registerCard`: `apps/api/src/cards/BT4/BT4-011.ts:3-40`, `BT4-012.ts:3-52`, `BT4-013.ts:3-57`, `BT4-014.ts:3-5`, `BT4-015.ts:3-27`, `BT4-016.ts:3-66`, `BT4-017.ts:3-98`, `BT4-018.ts:3-32`, `BT4-019.ts:3-52`, and `BT4-020.ts:3-47`.

The generated snapshot is `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:104441-104684`. Its BT4-012 and BT4-019 records still contain the malformed generated shape: a standalone `Trash` action targeting two of the player's Digimon, followed by an uncosted `Delete`. The direct modules now carry the Digi-Burst source-trash cost on the actual delete action, so those two snapshot records are stale and intentionally untouched. The snapshot also carries gateless `{ cost: 0, isAlternate: true, baseIsTamer: true }` requirements for BT4-011 and BT4-013. This is a known stale shadow, not a direct legality gap: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts:7-20` makes the directly registered record authoritative, while `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:44-82` registers the normalized module and feeds the Tamer-onto side registry.

The shared engine trace is as follows:

- `compiledCards.ts:14-15` resolves the registered direct record before the generated aggregate, and `registration/module.ts:323-340` stores and registers that record.
- `registration/keywords.ts:272-295` detects a Static `Digivolve` action with a Tamer `onto` filter and records its `asLevel`.
- `apps/api/src/engine/cards/tamerOntoDigivolve.ts:1-22` stores that level, and `apps/api/src/engine/cards/cardData.ts:520-560` derives the alternate requirement from the direct `asLevel`, the Tamer base, shared color, and the evolving card's real level-3 cost. This preserves BT4-011's 2-memory and BT4-013's 3-memory costs despite the stale snapshot's zero.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:420-450` gates target-dependent actions before optional processing and payment; `:451-500` pays an action's cost transactionally. A delete with no legal target is not allowed to pay the Digi-Burst cost first.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/costs.ts:35-80` and `:480-550` validate and pay self digivolution-card trash costs. `actions/removal.ts` handles ordinary field `Trash` actions separately, which is why the old BT4-012/019 standalone Digimon-trash actions were a proven defect: they could remove the host card rather than only its sources.
- `actions/statics.ts:45-85` applies DP modifications through the continuous ledger; `actions/grantStatic.ts` handles RizeGreymon's additive yellow identity; and `actions/subTrigger.ts:45-110` plus `:1220-1255` register and gate `whenSuspended` events. `actions/board.ts:250-285` handles `GainKeyword`, and `continuous.ts` owns keyword amounts and durations.
- `actions/play.ts:697-760` resolves `PlayWithoutCost` from hand, honors `payCost: false`, and leaves BT4-017's separate Digi-Burst action cost to the generic action-cost path. This direct shape is valid and is not changed.
