## Direct registration and shared primitive trace

Every assigned module contains a single executable registration through `registerIrCard`: `apps/api/src/cards/BT4/BT4-001.ts:2-31`, `BT4-002.ts:2-37`, `BT4-003.ts:2-42`, `BT4-004.ts:2-40`, `BT4-005.ts:2-47`, `BT4-006.ts:2-46`, `BT4-007.ts:2-6`, `BT4-008.ts:2-36`, `BT4-009.ts:2-50`, and `BT4-010.ts:1-10`. A static registration search found no duplicate `registerCard` path in these modules.

The shared seams support the direct IR without requiring a correction:

- `registerIrCard` normalizes the direct record, stores it as the registered compiled record, builds the trigger module, carries `OncePerTurn` into `maxPerTurn`, and applies turn guards through `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:274-305` and `323-341`.
- `[Your Turn]` is derived from the trigger owner through `turnOwnerGuard` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts:460-483`. Continuous keyword readers are ordered after providers through `readsSelfKeyword` and the priority calculation in `effect.ts:405-428` and `registration/module.ts:274-293`.
- `selfHasKeyword`, `zoneCount`, `selfHasTrait`, and `selfLevelIs` read the live permanent/top card and live zones in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:185-190`, `283-292`, and `438-486`.
- Aura DP/keyword effects use the continuous-effect path; this is the appropriate primitive for BT4-004 through BT4-006 rather than a one-shot trigger.
- `TrashDigivolution` honors `amount`, level-filtered targets, and bottom selection (`fromTop: false`) in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts:524-544` and `641-701`; the stack is explicitly read bottom-first at `placeUnder.ts:681-686`.
- Digi-Burst source removal emits the batch event with all trashed instance IDs in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:2189-2229`. The self-source gate for `onDigiBurstCardDiscarded` matches those IDs in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:715-727`, preserving BT4-008's source-specific reaction.
- RevealAdd independently tracks selected cards, applies both add slots, and returns the remainder to the deck in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:108-126`, `202-240`, and `353-365`. This supports BT4-009's two distinct card categories and bottom-rest behavior.
- Retaliation is evaluated on the side that actually died alone in battle by `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts:1198-1227`; the peer proof in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/retaliationOverflow.test.ts` covers holder death, survivor non-triggering, ties, and the ACE interaction.

Catalog evidence identifies a red level 2 Digi-Egg with inherited `[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon is level 7, gain 1 memory` (`cards.json:52529-52544`). The direct IR in `apps/api/src/cards/BT4/BT4-001.ts:8-31` is an inherited `WhenAttacking` effect with `GainMemory: 1`, `selfLevelIs: 7`, and `frequency: OncePerTurn`; it is full coverage with no residual.

The registration path derives the once-per-turn bound and the attack trigger, while `selfLevelIs` reads the live host's current top card. This matches the inherited-effect and attack-declaration rules. The focused test now uses a legal red stack `BT4-001` (level 2) → `BT4-008` (level 3) → `BT4-010` (level 4) → `BT4-014` (level 5) → `BT4-018` (level 6) → `BT3-019` (level 7), a legal level-6 negative, and a repeated-attack once-per-turn assertion in `apps/api/src/cards/BT4/BT4-001.test.ts`.

No direct behavior correction was required.
