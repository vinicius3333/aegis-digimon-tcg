## Audit controls and method

Each card was reviewed against every applicable field in the immutable catalog,
one local rules-KB query, its direct TypeScript module and colocated source
assertions, the read-only generated effects snapshot, the relevant interpreter
and runtime seams, nearby implementations, and realistic evolution/trait/link
stacks. The card query was run exactly once per card, with no repetition:

- BT26-011: Q6965.
- BT26-012: Q6966-Q6968.
- BT26-013: no card-specific entries.
- BT26-014: Q6969.
- BT26-015: Q6970-Q6975.
- BT26-016: Q6976-Q6981.
- BT26-017: Q6982.
- BT26-018: no card-specific entries.
- BT26-019: no card-specific entries.
- BT26-020: no card-specific entries.

All ten direct modules register exactly once through
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and do not contain `registerCard`. The BT26
index imports all ten modules in ascending order at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT26/index.ts:12-21`. No implementation or shared/runtime
change was required in this pass. The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`
was inspected read-only; all ten BT26 entries are absent because these cards
are hand-authored direct modules. It was not edited.

The execution gates requested by the protocol were deliberately not run: no
tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI check,
focused/mechanism/collection gate, or `git diff --check`. Consequently the
Executed delivery gates component is `0/2` for every card and no result below
is a collection-completion claim.
