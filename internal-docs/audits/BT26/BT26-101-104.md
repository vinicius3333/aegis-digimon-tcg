## Card findings

### BT26-101 — Cross Arts

The immutable record describes a white, cost-4 Option with ADAMAS/TS types. Its
Use Req is a TS-trait card. The Main effect conditionally grants all own TS
Digimon Blocker and +3000 DP until the opponent's turn ends when a Dan Yuki or
Kanan Yuki Tamer is present. It then chooses either deletion of one opposing
Digimon whose DP is no greater than one own TS Digimon's DP, or unsuspending one
own TS Digimon. The printed Security text is a separate optional play of one
play-cost-4-or-lower TS card from hand or trash without paying its cost.

The direct IR at `BT26-101.ts:5-92` traces these parts as follows:

* `WaiveColorRequirement` is gated by an own TS card.
* The two conditional Main grants target all own TS Digimon and last through
  the opponent's turn end.
* The first modal branch binds an own TS Digimon and uses its live `dp` value
  as the opposing deletion threshold; the second unsuspends one own TS Digimon.
* The Security branch targets one own Digimon or Tamer with play cost at most 4
  and TS trait from hand or trash, with no cost payment.

The source assertions in `BT26-101.test.ts:9-40` cover catalog identity, full
coverage, the waiver, Security play, and the Main modal. Public `playCard`
source proofs cover the waiver at lines 43-67, named-Tamer grant/deletion at
129-157, the no-Tamer unsuspend path at 159-179, and the Q7182 no-Tamer modal
case beginning at 181. The Security source proofs at lines 69-127 cover an
eligible hand card, an eligible Tamer from trash, and rejection of non-TS or
over-cost cards. They were inspected but not executed.

Q7182 and the modal runtime seams confirm that the named Tamer is not
incorrectly imposed on the “Then” choice. The live DP binding, modal branch,
and duration idioms match the inspected peers. This supports 2/2 in each of
Catalog/rules, IR trace, Behavioral proof, and Peer and stack proof; gates are
0/2, for 8/10.

The structural and direct source proofs in `BT26-103.test.ts:9-49` and

### BT26-104 — Kunlun

The immutable record describes a white, cost-5 Tamer with Shambala/SW/TB/TS
types. It gains one memory at the start of its controller's Main Phase. On
Play, it may trash one Shambala card from hand to draw two. At End of Your
Turn, if the controller has a Tentei Hachibushu Digimon, it may suspend this
Tamer to use one Shambala Option from hand without paying. The printed Security
text plays this card without paying its cost.

The direct IR at `BT26-104.ts:5-58` traces all three ordinary timings and the
Security self-play. The On Play cost is a Shambala hand-card trash and is
optional/abort-on-decline; the End of Your Turn condition is restricted to an
own Tentei Hachibushu Digimon and the borrowed Option is restricted to an own
hand Shambala Option, with the Tamer suspension as cost.

The structural assertions in `BT26-104.test.ts:9-44` cover identity and all
four printed timings. Source proofs at lines 46-90 cover Start of Main Phase
memory and On Play payment/draw, including the no-eligible-card negative case.
The End of Your Turn source proofs at lines 92-198 cover a valid Tentei
Hachibushu case, missing-condition cases, decline, and no eligible Option.
The Security proof at lines 200-218 verifies free self-play. These tests were
inspected but not executed. Because the timing proofs call effect callbacks
directly rather than driving a natural play and turn transition, Behavioral
proof is 1/2.

Q7190 and the End of Turn runtime establish that this effect remains pending
after the relevant preceding event and resolves before Counter timing. The
borrowed-option and self-cost seams match peers. Catalog/rules, IR trace, and
Peer and stack proof are each 2/2; gates are 0/2, for 7/10.
