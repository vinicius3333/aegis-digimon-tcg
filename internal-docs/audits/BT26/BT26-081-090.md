kind, play cost, DP, level/form, attributes, traits, alternate evolution
requirements, Assembly requirements, keywords, inherited text, Security text,
Link requirements/effects, and the exact printed limits. The audit also used
the one required rules knowledge-base query, the direct TypeScript module, its
source assertions, the read-only effects snapshots, relevant interpreter and
runtime seams, nearby peers, and realistic evolution, Link, security, deletion,
and stack shapes.

trace, Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates.

manually injected primitive/subtrigger origins, and structural assertions do
