## Shared rules and runtime evidence

The local KB entries and `data/kb/rules` were used for the following
interpretations: the most-security tie is chosen by the activating player;
effect-triggered zero-DP deletion waits until the complete effect resolves;
simultaneous triggers are ordered by the turn player; "if it's your turn"
gates the continuation; a card may be suspended even when it was not
suspended by the preceding action; reductions stack; a card without a
printed Link requirement cannot be linked; linking immediately opens the
standard battle option; effects that do not affect a Digimon do not prevent a
rules battle; App Fusion uses distinct ordered pairs; battle-win triggers are
published after the battle/deletion window; and a winning trigger still fires
when the losing Digimon's deletion is replaced or prevented. The no-entry
cards use the same comprehensive rules for ordinary timing, optional
processing, traits, stacks, and public card movement.

The runtime seams reviewed include:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/security.ts` and the
  security primitives implement top-security trashing, Recovery, and the
  player choice among tied largest security stacks.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/controlFlow.ts` handles
  the once-paid `CostGatedBlock` and conditional continuation used by the
  recovery, suspension, and placement clauses.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/statics.ts` treats an
  un-targeted `WaiveColorRequirement` as a self waiver; static grant,
  restrictions, and cost modifiers are installed in the continuous ledger.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/play.ts` and
  `borrowed.ts` keep Option use separate from Digimon/Tamer play and apply
  the requested reductions.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/digivolve.ts` enforces
  ordinary and alternate requirements, source zones, and `costDelta`.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts` moves the
  selected hand card face-down to the source stack at the requested bottom
  position and records the action result used by `ifThisEffectActed`.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` reveals the
  exact count, selects one matching card, and returns the rest to deck bottom;
  its OR-filter path handles the green-Tamer alternative.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts` installs
  the `wouldLeavePlay` prevention and its `placeAsSecurity` cost.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts` scopes
  `whenBattleWon` and `whenLinked` to the triggering source/subject; the
  combat controller publishes winner events after the battle and deletion
  replacement window.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/link.ts` enforces the
  source's printed Link requirement, and `actions/assembly.ts` enforces one
  exact Assembly material from trash with the printed reduction.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts` applies
  action conditions, optional decisions, target/cost preflights, and live
  scaling. `scaling.ts` counts face-down digivolution cards for Drimogemon's
  DP modifier and live security for Wide Plasment's use-cost modifier.
