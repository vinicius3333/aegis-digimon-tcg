# BT23 audit ledger: BT23-046 through BT23-042

Each card was audited individually against every catalog field, its exact local KB output, direct implementation, shared interpreter seams and focused public behavior. Standard optional/card/target decisions are the applicable UI path.

## BT23-046 Rosemon — 10/10

Green level 6 Fairy/CS Data Mega, cost 11, 11000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from green level 5 and alternatively for 3 from level-5 CS. Fortitude. On Play/When Digivolving may suspend any Digimon or Tamer as its cost, then prevents one opposing Digimon or Tamer from unsuspending through the opponent's turn end. Its Opponent's Turn OPT may redirect an opposing Digimon's attack to one of the controller's suspended Digimon whose traits include Vegetation, Plant or Fairy, or CS. Q5311 confirms the suspend cost may use either player's permanent; Q5312 confirms the redirect target is an own suspended qualifying Digimon. Both trigger copies incorrectly limited the cost to an own permanent and now use the exact any-controller scope. Public proofs suspend the only eligible opposing cost candidate, observe the unsuspend restriction, and resolve an actual player attack redirected into Rosemon, deleting the smaller attacker in battle. Traits, costs, failures, redirect choice, zones, duration, OPT, Fortitude and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-045 TigerVespamon ACE — 10/10

Green/black level 6 Cyborg/X Antibody/Royal Base/Zaxon/CS/Insectoid Virus Mega ACE, cost 8, 12000 DP, Overflow -4; normally evolves for 4 from green or black level 5 and alternatively for 3 from level-5 Royal Base or CS. Blast Digivolve. On Play/When Digivolving must place one Royal Base/Zaxon Digimon from hand or trash face up at security bottom to return an opposing Digimon with at most its DP to hand. Whenever it suspends, it may flip the top face-up security card face down to unsuspend one own Digimon. Q5307-Q5310 define face-up security visibility, checks, Security effects and shuffle normalization; Q5331 confirms the placement condition is mandatory once the effect activates. Public proofs observe exact hand-to-bottom placement face/order before the DP-bounded bounce and the top face-up flip before unsuspension. Counter origin, mandatory cost, card/target decisions, hand/trash/security zones, bottom/top order, face state and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-044 Lilamon — 10/10

Green level 5 Fairy/CS Data Ultimate, cost 7, 7000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from green level 4 and alternatively for 3 from level-4 CS. Its hand play cost is reduced by 3 while the controller has Yuuko Kamishiro or a CS Digimon. On Play/When Digivolving may suspend either player's Digimon to protect one own Vegetation/Plant/Fairy/CS Digimon from effect returns to hand or deck through the opponent's turn end. Its inherited All Turns OPT trashes the opponent's top security when its carrier deletes an opposing Digimon in battle. Q5305 confirms either controller is legal for the suspend cost; Q5306 confirms the inherited effect cannot activate when the carrier is simultaneously deleted. The inherited clause was absent and is now installed on the live self-anchored `whenBattleWon` event. An actual Digimon battle proves opposing deletion, carrier survival, top-security trash and zone identity. Cost reduction, trait OR, decisions, restriction categories/duration, battle timing, simultaneity ruling, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-043 CannonBeemon — 10/10

Green/black level 5 Cyborg/X Antibody/Royal Base/CS/Insectoid Virus Ultimate, cost 8, 8000 DP; normally evolves for 4 from green or black level 4 and alternatively for 3 from level-4 Royal Base or CS. From security during the opponent's turn it grants Blocker to all own Royal Base Digimon. Its own All Turns OPT can flip the top face-up security card down when it would leave other than by an own effect so it remains. Its inherited All Turns OPT performs the same payment to preserve one own Royal Base Digimon that would leave other than by an own effect. Q5304 confirms that after CannonBeemon prevents its deletion in a simultaneous All Delete event, QueenBeemon may still move all affected Royal Base Digimon into security. The entire inherited clause was absent; it now carries both the broad Royal Base scope and the interpreter's explicit single protected target. Public proof has an opponent-turn effect deletion preserved while the exact top security card flips face down. Security applicability, Blocker scope, replacement cause/controller, payment failure, face/order, simultaneous-event ruling, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-042 Waspmon — 10/10

Green/black level 4 Cyborg/X Antibody/Royal Base/CS/Insectoid Virus Champion, cost 4, 4000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from green or black level 3 and alternatively for 2 from level-3 Royal Base or CS. From security during all turns it grants +1000 DP to all own Royal Base Digimon. When Digivolving, with at most one Tamer, it may play a Tamer containing Royal Base in its text from hand free. Its inherited All Turns effect gives its carrier +1000 DP. Q5303 defines “in its text” across names, traits, effects, inherited text, rules and all evolution/fusion/link requirement fields. The inherited DP clause was absent and is now executable. Public proofs play Fei from hand through effect-text matching without payment and observe the inherited continuous DP bonus on a real carrier. Count boundary, text matcher, choice, hand/battle zones, payment, continuous security/inherited applicability and evolution requirements map to tested seams.
