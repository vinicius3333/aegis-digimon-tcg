## Scoring rubric

Each audit has five independently scored components, each worth two points:

1. **Contract and rules (2/2):** exact committed catalog clauses are traced to
   the local KB and applicable local rules/rulings.
2. **IR trace (0–2/2):** clauses are represented by the direct module's
   registered compiled IR, with registration exclusively through
   `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and no `registerCard` call. A handwritten
   timing fallback or an empty registered IR record lowers this component when
   the executable behavior cannot be attributed wholly to the compiled record.
3. **Behavioral proof (2/2):** colocated tests and source prove applicable
   positive, negative, optional, cost, duration, frequency, zone, ownership,
   and target boundaries. These are reviewed proofs, not executed proofs.
4. **Peer/stack proof (2/2):** shared interpreter seams, neighboring cards, and
   realistic play/evolution or inherited-stack cases were inspected.
5. **Delivery gates (0/2):** deliberately unavailable because all test and
   validation gates were prohibited in this dispatch.
