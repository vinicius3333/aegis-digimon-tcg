## Scoring rubric

Each card has five independently scored components, each worth two points:

- **Contract and rules:** exact catalog clauses reconciled with the local KB,
  comprehensive rules, manual, and rulings.
- **IR trace:** every clause attributable to the direct module's compiled IR,
  with registration exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and
  no direct `registerCard` call. Empty IR records or handwritten replacement
  behavior do not receive IR credit.
- **Behavioral proof:** colocated source covers the applicable positive,
  negative, optional, cost, duration, frequency, zone, ownership, and target
  boundaries. Reviewed tests are not treated as executed tests.
- **Peer/stack proof:** relevant shared seams, neighboring implementations,
  and realistic evolution/DigiXros/inherited-stack cases were inspected.
- **Delivery gates:** focused, mechanism, collection, typecheck, style, and
  diff-validation commands passed. This is 0/2 by explicit instruction.

## Detailed card traces

### BT12-111 — DarknessBagramon

**Catalog and rules contract.** The exact catalog record identifies
DarknessBagramon as a purple/black level-7 Mega/Virus Digimon with Composite
and Bagra Army traits, printed play cost 16, 15000 DP, and purple level-6
evolution cost 6 or black level-5 evolution cost 9. Its printed DigiXros
clause is the committed string `DigiXros -3: [DarkKnightmon] xD7 [Bagramon]`;
the `xD7` token is not normalized by the blob and is the sole contract
ambiguity. Its On Play and When Digivolving clauses each delete one opponent
Digimon, then optionally place up to five Bagra Army Digimon cards from the
owner's trash under this Digimon. During the opponent's turn, if an opponent
Digimon digivolves or attacks, the owner may trash five cards from this
Digimon's digivolution cards to return all Tamers to their owners' hands. The
record has no Security or inherited effect. Card KB returned no entries;
applicable local timing, DigiXros, stack, and Tamer rules were read. Ambiguity
count is 1 for the malformed DigiXros token.

**Direct implementation and snapshot.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/BT12-111.ts:1-8`
calls `lateBt12Module("BT12-111")`, registers an empty `{ effects: [],
coverage: "full", residual: [] }` value once through `registerIrCard`, and
overrides `effectsForTiming` with the handwritten module. There is no direct
`registerCard`, but none of the executable clauses is supplied by the
registered compiled record, so the IR trace is 0/2. The fallback in
`apps/api/src/cards/BT12/_lateHandwritten.ts:1053-1171` implements the delete
and trash-to-self actions, subscribes to opponent attacks, and uses a board
entry watcher for the opponent action that returns Tamers after trashing five
stack cards.

The read-only snapshot at
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:18865-18930` reports full coverage
and empty residuals, but it is not faithful enough to replace the fallback:
its PlaceUnder target searches any zone for Bagra Army cards rather than the

OpponentsTurn condition is raw prose rather than an attack/digivolution event
trigger, and its DigiXros requirement contains only a three-count DarkKnightmon

**Behavioral and peer/stack proof.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/BT12-111.test.ts:9-51`

### BT12-112 — Shoutmon X7: Superior Mode

**Catalog and rules contract.** The exact catalog record identifies Shoutmon
X7: Superior Mode as a white/red level-7 Mega/Data Digimon with Composite,
Xros Heart, and Blue Flare traits, printed play cost 15, DP 17000, rarity UR,
and deck limit 4. It has no ordinary evolution costs. Its DigiXros requirement
allows an unlimited number of Digimon cards with Xros Heart or Blue Flare,
with different card numbers. When this card would be played from hand, the
owner may place one own Shoutmon as a digivolution card under this Digimon to
reduce its play cost by 1, then place cards in the owner's trash as DigiXros
digivolution cards. On Play returns all digivolution cards under one opponent
Digimon to their owner's deck in any order and returns that Digimon to deck
bottom. During the owner's turn, opponent Option Security effects do not
activate. The exact catalog has no Security or inherited clause. Q2848 is an
explicit ambiguity because it refers to an “if this effect returned a level 7
white card” condition absent from this committed effect text; no behavior was
invented for it. Ambiguity count is 1.

**Direct implementation and snapshot.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/BT12-112.ts:1-28`
clones `getCompiledCard("BT12-112")`, replaces the generated
`Restrict`/`activateSecurity` node when present with a
`DisableSecurityEffect` targeting self, `sourceKind: "option"`,
`scope: "seat"`, and `duration: "forTheTurn"`, then registers that clone
exactly once through `registerIrCard`; it does not import or override
`lateBt12Module`. The shared IR action type now carries the optional seat
scope (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/actions/security.ts:30-38`). The
read-only snapshot at
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:18932-19003` contains the complete
On Play return with `returnDigivolutionCardsFirst`, the generated Your Turn
all-opponent Option Security restriction, the `wouldBePlayed` cost reducer with
SelectBind/TrashDigivolution/PlaceUnder actions, the allowance to use trashed
cards as DigiXros materials, full coverage, empty residuals, and the infinite
Xros Heart/Blue Flare different-card-number recipe. The snapshot remains
unchanged as read-only evidence: its `activateSecurity` Restrict is not
executable for flipped Security cards because that target resolver looks for
battle-area permanents. The direct module's normalization is intentional
runtime IR drift from the generated snapshot; Q2848 is tracked separately as
a KB/catalog ambiguity.

The generated cost body is executable through the shared reducer path: it
selects a battle-area Shoutmon, trashes its stack before deferred placement,
and moves the selected top card under the pending BT12-112 permanent. This
ordering directly addresses Q2249–Q2253 and the X Antibody rule in Q2251.
The `registerIrCard` collector also exposes the DigiXros recipe and trash
material allowance, while the normal replacement timing ensures the optional
reduction is offered before the 15-memory payment.

**Behavioral and peer/stack proof.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT12/BT12-112.test.ts:27-47`

The replacement reducer seams in `registration/reducers.ts`, pay-time flow in
