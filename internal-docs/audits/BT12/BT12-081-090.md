Each score has five components worth two points:

- **Contract and rules:** all catalog fields, printed clauses, limits, and
  applicable KB/rules were reconciled.
- **IR trace:** every clause is represented by the direct compiled record and
  its sole `registerIrCard` registration. A card whose direct module replaces
  the compiled timing surface with a handwritten `effectsForTiming` override
  receives 1/2 until that runtime limitation is removed.
- **Behavioral proof:** the colocated source covers the card-specific positive,
  boundary, negative, optional, zone, timing, inherited, Security, and
  once-per-turn cases that apply.
- **Peer and stack proof:** shared interpreter seams, neighboring effect
  vocabulary, relevant trait/name boundaries, and realistic stacks were read
  and exercised where applicable.
- **Delivery gates:** tests and static gates were intentionally deferred, so
  this component is 0/2 for every card.
