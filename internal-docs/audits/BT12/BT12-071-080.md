## Scoring rubric

Each reviewed component is worth 2 points:

- **Contract and rules:** the exact catalog fields and each printed clause are
  reconciled with the local KB and applicable local rules/rulings.
- **IR trace:** each clause maps to concrete compiled IR with full coverage,
  empty residuals, and exactly one exclusive
  `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration; no assigned module uses
  `registerCard`.
- **Behavioral proof:** colocated source covers the applicable positive,
  boundary, negative, optional, cost, duration, frequency, zone, ownership,
  and target cases.
- **Peer/stack proof:** relevant shared seams, neighboring implementations,
  and realistic evolution or inherited stacks were inspected.
- **Delivery gates:** focused, mechanism, collection, typecheck, style, and
  diff-validation commands passed; this is 0/2 by explicit instruction.
