## Applicable rules and shared seams

The following local rule material was used across the card traces:

- Comprehensive §4-3-2/§4-3-3 (`comprehensive.md:657-663`) makes cards under
  Digimon digivolution cards and grants their inherited effects; §4-4-2
  (`:672-678`) places new cards under a Tamer at the bottom by default;
  §4-7-8 (`:746-749`) trashes stacked cards when a host leaves the field.
- §4-23 (`:917-944`) distinguishes printed text from gained effects and
  distinguishes the `<Save>` icon from `<Material Save>`; this is the source
  rule for every Save-text evolution, reveal, placement, or inherited gate in
  this range.
- §6-2 (`:1063-1074`) covers the unsuspend phase. §16-11
  (`:2897-2910`) makes Reboot persistent, mandatory, opponent-phase
  unsuspending, and once-only even for multiple instances.
- §7-2 (`:1202-1318`) covers DigiXros declaration, per-material play-cost
  reduction, source placement, and exact stack handling.
- §8-1 (`:1392-1450`) covers the reveal/pay/stack/draw digivolution sequence.
  The tamer-onto paths use the engine's structural `baseIsTamer` and
  `baseColors` requirement projection, checked by `cardData.ts:339-344` and
  `:538-556`.
- §11-2-7 (`:1728-1734`) permits attack-target switching after declaration;
  §15-16-11/13 (`:2746-2771`) defines start-of-opponent-turn and
  start-of-your-main-phase timing.
- §16-12 (`:2911-2930`) defines De-Digivolve as mandatory top-card trashing,
  one card at a time, without going below level 3. §16-20
  (`:3014-3019`) makes Save optional.

The shared seams checked in addition to each card's own module and test source
were `GameEngine.ts:1350-1424` for the own and opponent Reboot unsuspend
