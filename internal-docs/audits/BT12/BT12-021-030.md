## Scoring rubric

Each independently reviewed area is worth 2 points:

- **Contract and rules:** every catalog field and printed clause is reconciled
  with the local card KB and applicable local rules material.
- **IR trace:** every clause maps to executable compiled IR, with one exclusive
  `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration, `coverage: "full"`, no
  residual nodes, and no unparsed behavior.
- **Behavioral proof:** the colocated suite covers the meaningful positive,
  boundary, negative, optional, frequency, cost, duration, zone, and Security
  cases applicable to the card.
- **Peer and stack proof:** relevant neighboring implementations, shared
  targeting/timing primitives, trait filters, ownership, and realistic
  evolution-stack transitions were inspected and represented by comparative
  evidence where applicable.
- **Delivery gates:** focused, mechanism, collection, typecheck, style, and
  diff-validation commands have passed. This is 0/2 for this report because
  execution was explicitly deferred.

### BT12-021 — Veemon

**Catalog and rules contract.** The committed catalog record describes a blue
level-3 Rookie Digimon, play cost 3, 1000 DP, with blue or green level-2
evolution cost 0, Free trait, and Mini Dragon type. Its On Play clause reveals
the top three cards, adds one card with Imperialdramon in its name or Free trait
and one card with Davis Motomiya in its name, and places the remainder on the
bottom of the deck in any order. Its inherited End of Your Turn clause permits
DNA digivolving this Digimon and one other Digimon into a Digimon card in hand
by paying that card's DNA cost. Local card KB Q2148–Q2150 confirms partial hits,
that both available branches must be added when both exist, and that a Davis
Motomiya & Ken Ichijoji card qualifies by name. The local rules search was
checked against comprehensive §15-15-3 and §8-2-2-1-7 and the manual's
“Returning Revealed Cards to the Deck” and DNA Digivolve sections.

**Implementation and snapshot.** `BT12-021.ts` registers the committed compiled
record directly. The read-only snapshot contains `RevealAdd(revealCount: 3)`
with two mandatory hand slots and `rest: "deckBottom"`, plus inherited optional
`DnaDigivolve` at `EndOfYourTurn` with `payCost: true`; the DNA action's self
material shape is resolved by the shared DNA interpreter as this Digimon plus
one other Digimon. `runRevealAdd` preserves revealed identities, enforces the
union/slot counts, returns unchosen cards to the deck bottom, and supports
controller ordering. `runDnaDigivolve` resolves the self and partner, checks
the result and material legality, pays the DNA cost, merges stacks, draws the
digivolution bonus, and enters unsuspended as required by the DNA rules.

**Behavioral, peer, and stack proof.** `BT12-021.test.ts` covers both mandatory

**Correction history and drift.** `b16ed1665` moved the module from the legacy
