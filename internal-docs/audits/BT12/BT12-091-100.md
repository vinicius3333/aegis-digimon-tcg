# BT12-091–BT12-100 Card-by-Card Audit

Date: 2026-08-28

This report covers BT12-091 through BT12-100 in strict ascending order. Each
catalog contract was read from the exact committed catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; no mutable catalog copy was used
as the contract authority. Before inspection, this reused worker branch fetched
`origin` and merged `origin/audit-bt-card-by-card` with a regular, non-rebasing
merge; the synchronization merge is `24ca5b3c3`.

The local card KB was queried one card at a time with
`node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>`. Direct TypeScript modules, colocated
test sources, the generated effects snapshot, shared interpreter and primitive
seams, adjacent peers, and realistic play/evolution/attack stacks were all
reviewed. `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and `BT12-STATIC-AUDIT.md`
were read-only evidence and were not edited.

This audit is deliberately provisional. Execution remains **0/2**: focused
card tests, mechanism tests, collection tests, typecheck, lint, formatting,
browser/UI checks, and `git diff --check` were not run because this dispatch
explicitly prohibited those gates. No card, range, or collection is claimed
complete or 10/10. The maximum provisional card score is therefore 8/10.

Each score has five components worth two points:

- **Contract and rules (2/2):** the immutable catalog fields and every printed
  clause were reconciled with the local KB and applicable local rules.
- **IR trace (2/2):** every executable clause is represented by direct compiled
  IR with full coverage and no residuals, and the module contains one exclusive
  `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration. A card still using
  `lateBt12Module` or assigning an `effectsForTiming` runtime override is an
  unresolved handwritten limitation and is scored **1/2** here, regardless of
  its behavioral source tests.
- **Behavioral proof (2/2):** the colocated source contains the relevant
  positive, boundary, negative, optional, cost, duration, ownership, zone,
  target, and stack assertions. These are source-level traces only; they were
  not executed in this dispatch.
- **Peer and stack proof (2/2):** relevant shared interpreter/primitive seams,
  neighboring implementations, and realistic play/evolution or attack stacks
  were inspected.
- **Delivery gates (0/2):** execution was explicitly deferred and no gate pass
  is claimed.

## KB and rules trace

The card-KB results were:

- BT12-091, BT12-093, BT12-095, BT12-096, and BT12-097 returned no
  card-specific entries.
- BT12-092 returned Q2227–Q2230 and Q5978–Q5980. These clarify that a Tamer
  treated as a 3000 DP Digimon remains both a Tamer and a Digimon, keeps its
  inherited effects, cannot attack when played that turn, and has later
  same-kind DP treatment overwritten by a newer effect while unrelated gained
  effects accumulate. They also clarify both Digimon/Tamer effect identity and
  immunity interactions.
- BT12-094 returned the 2023-04-14 erratum changing the Start of Your Main
  Phase reward from +2000 DP to gain 1 memory; the direct source and snapshot
  were checked against the corrected contract.
- BT12-098 returned Q2231–Q2232: one eligible category is still added when the
  other category is absent, and when both categories are available the effect
  must add as many qualifying cards as possible rather than voluntarily adding
  only one.
- BT12-099 returned Q2233–Q2234: the effect-driven attack does not bypass a
  suspended or same-turn-played attack restriction, and the attack activates
  the attacker's When Attacking effects.
- BT12-100 returned Q2235–Q2236: the same attack legality restriction applies,
  and the effect-driven attack activates When Attacking effects.

The common rules evidence was read from `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`:

- `:560-578` covers breeding-area isolation from ordinary battle-area trigger
  conditions; this matters when a Save digivolution watcher identifies its
  own-Digimon source.
- `:630-678` and `:717-749` cover Digimon/Tamer identity, cards stacked under
  Tamers, bottom stack orientation, and stack removal.
- `:917-944` distinguishes a printed term in a card's text from a gained
  keyword and distinguishes `<Save>` from other Save-like mechanics.
- `:1422-1434` covers digivolution processing and memory movement when a cost
  is reduced or cannot be paid.
- `:1600-1665` covers Option Main timing and security resolution.
- `:1895-1945` covers effect and inherited-effect processing.
- `:2230-2241` covers trigger-time references.
- `:2282-2294` covers immediate “would” timing, which is why a digivolution
  cost replacement must be installed before the digivolution rather than from
  a watcher that fires after it.
- `:3000-3035` covers Save's optional placement behavior.

The shared implementation seams reviewed were:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts:336-455`.
  The reduce-cost path installs a pre-event replacement and consumes its
  `cost` plus `additionalCosts`; it does not execute a replacement's ordinary
  action body as a post-event payload. This explains the drift in the old
  nested Save-Tamer records and why a direct nested Replacement with a suspend
  cost plus a place additional cost is required.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:143-180,994-1153`.
  This installs event watchers and runs their bodies only at their event site;
  it is not an equivalent substitute for a `wouldDigivolve` cost replacement.
- `apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts:1690-1703,3406-3413`. The ordinary
  `whenOneOfYoursDigivolves` event is dispatched after the digivolution, while
  the cost calculation consults the earlier `wouldDigivolve` seam.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/costs.ts:1492-1695` and
  `packages/shared/src/effects/ir/predicates/costs.ts:10-130`. These implement
  compound costs, loose cards under Tamers, the `underTamers` source zone,
  bottom `digivolutionStack` placement, and the `target`/`isTriggerSource`
  destination host.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/combat.ts:9-55` and
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:4828-4892`. The force-attack path
  runs the normal attack lifecycle and checks attack legality, including
  suspended and same-turn restrictions, rather than bypassing those rules.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts` and its loose
  targeting helpers. These own Watchmaker's two category adds and bottom-deck
  remainder handling.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:180-276` and
  `:323-338`. These map static/turn IR to runtime timing and make a direct
  registered compiled record the executable authority.

The closest peer for the corrected Save reducer is
`apps/api/src/cards/BT21/BT21-088.ts:45-110`, whose direct IR uses an outer
`wouldDigivolve` filter and an inner reduce-cost Replacement with a suspend
`cost` and a bottom-stack `additionalCosts` place action. Its source proof at
`BT21-088.test.ts:141-150` demonstrates that an under-Tamer card becomes the
true bottom card of the evolving host. `BT12-097` and its colocated reducer
tests are the closest behavioral peer for optional decline behavior. The
BT12-092/095/097/099/100 handwritten module cases were retained as explicit
limitations rather than being scored as IR-complete.
