## Scoring rubric

Each independently reviewed area is worth 2 points:

- **Contract and rules:** every catalog field and printed clause is reconciled
  with local KB entries, errata, and applicable comprehensive-rule material.
- **IR trace:** every clause maps to executable compiled IR, with one exclusive
  `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration, `coverage: "full"`, and no
  residual behavior.
- **Behavioral proof:** the colocated source covers applicable positive,
  boundary, negative, optional, frequency, cost, duration, zone, Security, and
  stack cases. These are static test-source traces in this dispatch, not an
  executed-test claim.
- **Peer and stack proof:** shared timing, targeting, cost, keyword, reveal,
  evolution, and placement seams were inspected with relevant peers and
  realistic board or evolution-stack fixtures.
- **Delivery gates:** focused, mechanism, collection, typecheck, style, and
  diff-validation commands have passed. This is 0/2 because execution was
  explicitly deferred.
