### EX12-051 Lamortmon — 8/10 (no change)

Clauses: alternate `[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[Angoramon] in text or w/[NSp] trait: Cost 3`;
`<Reboot>`; `<Blocker>`; `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` suspend one opposing Digimon **or Tamer**,
then `<De-Digivolve 1>` one of their **Digimon**; inherited `[All Turns][Once Per Turn]` battle-win
security trash.

IR trace: the two alternates are separate `digivolutionRequirement` entries (`traits`/`texts` are
ORed by `cardData.ts:456`, and `texts` implements the Q6829 "in its text" scope at
`cardData.ts:491`). The suspend and de-digivolve are two independent targets, which is what the
printed "Then," sequencing requires — the de-digivolve is Digimon-only while the suspend also
accepts Tamers, and the module keeps the two `kind` lists distinct. The inherited effect is a
`SubTrigger` on `whenBattleWon`; the engine fires that event from both
`combat/controller.ts:1136` (with `subjectPermanentId` = winner) and
`security/securityCheck.ts:373` for a Security Digimon battle, which is exactly Q6831. The
`sourceFilter.isSelfRef` gate is enforced positively in
`interpreter/matching/trigger.ts:72-75` against `ctx.source.permanent()` — for an inherited
effect that resolves to the host permanent, not the stack card — and the `nameOrTrait`
disjunction is then applied by `permanentMatchesFilter`, so the "with [Angoramon] in its text or
the [NSp] trait" gate really is checked and is not swallowed by `isSelfRef`.

Defects corrected: none. Tests added: none. Existing suite already covers Q6830/Q6831/Q6834,
once-per-turn identity, host scoping, and all four evolution routes.

Mutation check: change `kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]` to Digimon-only and
`expect(s.perm("tamer").isSuspended).toBe(true)` fails; drop `isSelfRef` and
"only for its own host" fails on the `other` permanent; drop `frequency: "OncePerTurn"` and the
same test's second fire drops security to 1.

Open ambiguity: none.

### EX12-052 Diarbbitmon — 8/10 (defect corrected)

Clauses: two alternates at Lv.5; `<Piercing>`; `<Vortex>`; `[When Digivolving]` opponent-Digimon
effect immunity on one of your Digimon until their turn ends;
`[When Digivolving][When Attacking][Counter][Once Per Turn]` +3000 DP then a direct battle;
Option side `<Use Req. ([NSp] trait)>` + `[Main]` unsuspend/suspend-2/lock-2.

IR trace: `<Use Req.>` is correctly a `WaiveColorRequirement` static gated on a `youHave`
condition — CR §16-42-1 defines `<Use Req.>` as a colour-requirement waiver, and this is the
corpus idiom (`BT25-104.ts:128`, `EX12-072.ts`). The immunity uses
`Restrict{restriction:"beAffected", fromSourceKind:["Digimon"], byOpponentEffectsOnly:true}`,
which matches Q6837/Q6838 (still choosable, just unaffected) and the Option-source carve-out the
existing test exercises. The battle clause binds the buffed Digimon with `Target.bindAs` and
replays it through `BattleAction.attacker.fromSelectionRef`, so the Digimon that gained the DP is
the one that battles — the Q6836 identity requirement.

**Defect corrected — the buff/battle sequence was optional.**

Before:

```ts
{ kind: "ModifyDP", target: { …, bindAs: "buffedDigimon" }, amount: 3000,
  duration: "untilOpponentTurnEnd", optional: true },
```

After: the `optional: true` is removed. Nothing in the printed clause is a "may" except the
"may battle", and Q6836 states outright that you cannot take the DP and decline the battle.
`optional: true` let a controller decline; because the following `Battle` reads
`fromSelectionRef: "buffedDigimon"` and no binding would exist, declining silently skipped the
whole clause on the two **mandatory** timings (`[When Digivolving]`, `[When Attacking]`). The
`[Counter]` timing keeps its correct optionality one level up: counter abilities are opt-in
player activations enumerated by `GameEngine.counterEligibleSources` (`GameEngine.ts:5679`), not
by an action-level `optional`.

Tests added:
`"Q6836 resolves the DP boost and battle with no optional prompt on the mandatory timing"` runs
`[When Digivolving]` with `autoAcceptOptional: false` and asserts no `optional` decision opens, DP
reaches 15000, and the opponent is deleted — this is the assertion that fails when the fix is
reverted. The shape test now asserts that neither the effect nor any of its actions carries
`optional` on all three shared timings.

Mutation check: restore `optional: true` → the new test's `settle` never observes the opponent
leaving; change `fromSelectionRef` → the buffed Digimon is no longer the battler and the DP/battle
assertions decouple; change `duration` → the 15000 DP assertion in the immunity test fails; drop
`sharedUseKey` → "shares the once-per-turn budget" sees a second 3000 boost.

Open ambiguity: the `<Use Req.>` `youHave` filter is `{zone: "battleArea", controllerDefault:
"mine", nameOrTrait: [NSp]}` with no `kind` gate, while peer `BT25-104` restricts to
`kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]`. There is no [NSp] Option in the catalog, so the two readings cannot
be told apart today; I left the existing shape rather than narrow it on speculation.

### EX12-060 Chaosdramon — 8/10 (defect corrected)

Clauses: `[DNA Digivolve] Red/Black Lv.6 + Purple/Yellow Lv.6: Cost 0`;
`[Assembly -8] 6 Lv.6-or-lower [Machine]/[Cyborg]/[ME] cards w/different names`; `<Piercing>`;
`<Security A. +1>`; `<Fragment (2)>`; `<Engage>`;
`[On Play][When Digivolving][When Attacking][Once Per Turn]` `<De-Digivolve 2>` **all** of your
opponent's Digimon, then by placing 2 level-5-or-lower [Machine]/[Cyborg]/[ME] **cards** from hand
or trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution cards, delete 2 of your opponent's Digimon with a
play cost no higher than this Digimon's digivolution-card count.

IR trace of the hard parts:

- The dynamic delete ceiling is `playCostLte: 0` plus
  `playCostLteScaling: {per: 1, filter: {}, unit: "digivolutionCards"}`.
  `interpreter/scaling.ts:266` resolves `digivolutionCards` from `ctx.source.permanent().stack`
  and applies the (empty, therefore match-all) filter, so **Digi-Eggs in the stack are counted** —
  the cross-batch undercount pattern does *not* apply here, precisely because the filter is `{}`
  rather than `{kind: ["Digimon"]}`.
- The ceiling is computed **after** the placement cost: `runAction.ts:242-248` marks a Delete with
  `target.filter.playCostLteScaling` as `dynamicallyScaledDeleteTarget` and defers target
  resolution past cost payment, so the two just-placed cards count toward the cap. That is the
  correct rules order.
- `<Engage>` is declared both as a keyword and as an explicit `EndOfYourTurn` optional self-attack.
  That is deliberate and not a duplicate: `declaresUnimplementedEngageKeyword`
  (`registration/keywords.ts:59`) suppresses the synthesized effect precisely when an explicit
  `EndOfYourTurn` `Attack` is already present.
- No `Decode` replacement and no `hostFilter` appears anywhere in this batch.

**Defect corrected — the placement cost excluded Digi-Eggs.**

Before (all three timings):

```ts
cost: { kind: "place", target: { filter: {
  controller: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"],
  levelComparison: { op: "lte", value: 5 },

}, count: 2, from: ["hand", "trash"] }, … }
```

After: `kind: ["Digimon"]` is removed. The printed cost takes level-5-or-lower matching **cards**,
and a Digi-Egg such as EX12-003 Kapurimon (Lv.2, [Lesser]/[ME]) qualifies. The sibling clause on

printed wording. Options and Tamers are still excluded by `levelComparison`

Tests added: `"accepts a level 2 [ME] Digi-Egg as one of the two placed materials"` — with only
one Digimon material in hand and a Digi-Egg in trash, the payment succeeds and both cards end up

The shape assertion now pins the full place-cost filter so the gate cannot silently return.

Mutation check: restore `kind: ["Digimon"]` → the new Digi-Egg test fails; change the DeDigivolve
`count` from `"all"` to 1 → the multi-target de-digivolve assertion fails; change `per` or `unit`

## 6. Seam requests

The audit, neither blocking:

1. **`DigiXrosMaterial` cannot express a card kind.** EX12-056's printed material is "1 Lv.5 or
   lower **Digimon** card", but `packages/shared/src/effects/ir/requirements/xrosLink.ts` offers no
   `kind` field. Proposed minimal diff:

   ```ts
   export interface DigiXrosMaterial {
   ```
