### EX12-014 Canoweissmon

**Clauses.** Red/Yellow, Lv.5 Ultimate, Vaccine, 7000 DP, play cost 7, types Sky Dragon / VB.
`[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[Gammamon] in text or w/[VB] trait: Cost 3`;
`＜Decode (Lv.4 or lower w/[Gammamon] in text or w/[VB] trait)＞`;
`[On Play] [When Digivolving] You may place 1 level 5 or lower Digimon card with [Gammamon] in
its text or the [VB] trait from your hand or trash as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card.
Then, 1 of your Digimon may attack.`; inherited ＜Decode (same restriction)＞.

**Decode semantics verified against the interpreter.** CR §16-36-1: "When the Digimon with this
effect would leave the battle area **other than by a battle**, you may play 1 specified Digimon
card from **that Digimon's** digivolution cards without paying the cost"; §16-36-2 makes it an
immediate-type effect; §16-36-3 makes the processing optional. Mapping:
- `event: "wouldLeavePlay"` + `leaveCause: "otherThanBattle"` — `replacement.ts:533-535` maps
  `otherThanBattle` to `cause !== "byBattle"`, and `leavePrevention.ts:68-70` sources
  `wouldLeavePlay` subscriptions for every leave including bounces.
- No `mode`, no `cost`, no nested `Prevent` ⇒ `mode` defaults to `"instead"`
  (`replacement.ts:155-163`). The `"instead"` branch is documented at `replacement.ts:513-515`
  as "a substitute side effect the leave-prevention consult runs **alongside** (not instead of)
  the event; it never itself blocks the removal" — which is exactly Decode: the Digimon still
  leaves. This is the correct replacement semantics for the keyword, and the existing test at
  `EX12-014.test.ts:161-174` asserts the host is gone **and** the decoded card is in play.
- `optional: true` on the inner `PlayWithoutCost` supplies §16-36-3.
- Source liveness: `leavePrevention.ts:87-96` builds the reaction context from
  `host.permanentById(repl.sourcePermanentId)` and skips the subscription when that permanent is
  already gone, so `ctx.source.permanent()` is still live during the consult. A host-scoped
  filter is therefore safe.

**Defect corrected (over-broad Decode source zone).** `from: ["digivolutionCards"]` with only
`controller: "mine"` gathers loose cards from **every stack the controller owns**
(`interpreter/targeting/loose.ts:67`, `:264-266`), not just the leaving Digimon's. CR §16-36-1
says "that Digimon's digivolution cards". The corpus already has the correct encoding for
"this Digimon's digivolution cards": `hostFilter: { isSelfRef: true }`, consumed at
`loose.ts:274-290` (`hostMatches`, `hostFilter.isSelfRef` ⇒
`ctx.source.permanent()?.permanentId === cand.hostPermanentId`) and used by BT24-038, BT24-077,
BT24-079, BT25-070 and BT25-072.

- **Before** (both the printed and the inherited Decode replacement):
  `filter: { controller: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"], levelComparison: { op: "lte", value: 4 }, nameOrTrait: [...] }`
- **After:**
  `filter: { controller: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"], hostFilter: { isSelfRef: true }, levelComparison: { op: "lte", value: 4 }, nameOrTrait: [...] }`

The inherited copy resolves the same way: when EX12-014 sits in another Digimon's stack,
`ctx.source.permanent()` is that host, so the host-scoped filter still names the correct stack.

**Non-defect confirmed.** The optional `PlaceUnder` deliberately carries **no**
`abortOnDecline`, so declining or having no eligible card does not cancel the following
"Then, 1 of your Digimon may attack". CR §15-6-2 states that separate processes in the same
effect do not have to meet the other processes' conditions, and the printed text has no
"if you did" clause (contrast EX12-018, which prints "If this effect placed"). `effect.ts:772`
is the only site that reads `abortOnDecline` on a leading action, so the persisted record's
`abortOnDecline: true` would have suppressed the attack. Already proved by
`EX12-014.test.ts:73` ("still offers the following attack when no eligible material is
available"). Attack mandatoriness: `interpreter/actions/combat.ts:46-53` calls
`ctx.fx.forceAttack` unconditionally, with the printed "may" supplied by `optional: true`
prompting earlier in `runAction`.

**Test added.** `EX12-014.test.ts` — "plays only from its own digivolution cards, never from a
neighbor's stack": an EX12-014 with an **empty** stack next to an EX12-015 whose stack holds an
EX12-013 (Lv.4, VB — a card that satisfies the Decode filter). Deleting the EX12-014 `byEffect`
must leave the EX12-013 under the neighbour and must not put it into play. This test fails
before the fix (the neighbour's stack card was a legal Decode candidate) and passes after it.

**Type errors resolved.** None in this card.

**Mutation check.** `leaveCause: "otherThanBattle"` → `:176` (byBattle does not Decode).
`hostFilter: { isSelfRef: true }` → the new neighbour-stack test. Level ceiling 4 and the
`nameOrTrait` union → the printed/inherited Decode pair at `:141-172` plus EX12-017's Q6742
analogue. `position: "bottom"` and `from: ["hand","trash"]` → `:9`/`:40`. Attack independence
from the placement → `:73`. Digivolution requirement level/text/trait/cost → `:190`/`:222`.

**Open ambiguity.** None.

### EX12-015 Gokuumon

**Clauses.** Red/Yellow, Lv.5 Ultimate, Virus, 7000 DP, play cost 7, types Beastkin / Shambala / SW.
`[Digivolve] Lv.4 w/[Shambala] trait: Cost 3`;
`[DigiXros -2] 1 Lv.5 or lower Digimon card w/[Gokuumon] in text or w/[SW] trait`; ＜Raid＞;
`[On Play] [When Digivolving] 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -4000 DP until their turn ends.
Then, 1 of your other Digimon with the [SW] trait may gain ＜Alliance＞ and attack.`;
inherited `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn] You may delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with
6000 DP or less`.

**IR trace.** `ModifyDP -4000 / untilOpponentTurnEnd` (`duration.ts:22` ⇒
`EffectDuration.UntilOpponentTurnEnd`), then `GainKeyword Alliance` on
`{ controller: "mine", excludeSelf: true, kind: ["Digimon"], nameOrTrait: [SW trait] }` with
`optional: true` and `duration: "forTheTurn"`, then a **non-optional** `Attack` with
`sameTarget: true` and `condition: { kind: "ifThisEffectActed" }`.

Q6737 ("A Digimon that gains ＜Alliance＞ from this effect attacks if possible") is what the
last two fields encode: the *choice of recipient* is optional, the *attack* is not.
`board.ts:305-307` writes `ctx.lastEffectActed = ids.length > 0` with a comment that names
EX12-015 as the reason; `conditions.ts:751-758` reads it; `permanents.ts:383-385` resolves
`sameTarget` from `ctx.lastResolvedPermanentIds`, which `resolvePermanentTargets` wrote for the
`GainKeyword` target. `combat.ts:52-53` then force-attacks that permanent. `mandatory: true` on
the action is declarative only, per its own doc comment at
`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/actions/combat.ts:26-30` ("`forceAttack` already enforces it") —
it is self-documentation, not a dead field, and was kept.

`duration: "forTheTurn"` for the Alliance grant is the tightest correct choice: the printed text
gives no duration, `forThisAttack` would expire against an attack that has not been declared yet,
and the persisted record's `"permanent"` would leave Alliance on the recipient for the rest of
the game.

**Defects corrected.** None in the module — the drift is entirely on the persisted side (see
section 3).

**Tests.** No change needed. Already covered: both windows, the optional-refusal path with
**no** forced attack, the inherited 6000-DP ceiling and its 7000-DP boundary refusal, the
DigiXros recipe including a Lv.6 material rejection (Q6738), a two-material rejection, a
level-5 non-SW material accepted through Gokuumon text (Q6736), the KB-mandated
Alliance-to-attack linkage in IR, and both evolution routes.

**Mutation check.** `-4000` and `untilOpponentTurnEnd` → `:9`/`:41`. `excludeSelf` and the SW
trait gate → `:9` (the grant lands on the other SW Digimon, not on Gokuumon). The forced attack
and its `ifThisEffectActed` gate → `:74` ("may decline granting Alliance, in which case no

**Open ambiguity.** Q6739 (a Digimon reduced to 0 DP is deleted at the next rule check, not
immediately) is an engine-level rule-check ordering property, not a card field. It is not
asserted here and is out of scope for this card's module.

## 6. Seam requests

### SR-1 — `SubTrigger` with `on:` must preserve an unaffectable recipient (blocks EX12-016 / Q6740)

**Problem.** Q6740 for EX12-016: "Can I use this card's [On Play] [When Digivolving] effect to
give '[Start of Your Main Phase] This Digimon attacks' to a Digimon that isn't affected by
effects? — **You can give the effect**, but if the Digimon isn't affected by effects at the time
it would be triggered, it will not trigger."

The fire-time half is implemented: `interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:613-625`
(`grantedEffectAffectableGate`) re-evaluates unaffectability when the watcher fires and cites
Q6740 by name. The **grant-time** half is not. `subTrigger.ts:171` resolves the recipient with

```ts
const targetIds = await resolvePermanentTargets(ctx, action.on);
```

`resolvePermanentTargets` strips unaffectable ids through `finalize` unless
`preserveUnaffectableSelection` is passed (`targeting/permanents.ts:428-430`). With an
already-unaffectable opponent board, `targetIds` is empty, `grantTo` is `undefined`, and
`subTrigger.ts:173` returns without granting anything — the opposite of the ruling. The sibling
`runGainTriggeredEffect` already gets this right: `subTrigger.ts:1214` passes
`{ preserveUnaffectableSelection: true }`.

**Proposed minimal diff** (`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts`):

```diff
-    const targetIds = await resolvePermanentTargets(ctx, action.on);
+    // Q6740: a granted trigger may be GIVEN to a permanent that is currently unaffected by
+    // effects; `grantedEffectAffectableGate` below suppresses it at fire time instead.
+    // Mirrors runGainTriggeredEffect, which already preserves the selection.
+    const targetIds = await resolvePermanentTargets(ctx, action.on, {
+      preserveUnaffectableSelection: true,
+    });
```

**Test that proves the need** (add to `apps/api/src/cards/EX12/EX12-016.test.ts`; fails today
because the grant never lands):

```ts
it("gives the delayed attack even to an already-unaffectable Digimon (Q6740)", async () => {
  const s = setupEngine(
    {
      0: { hand: [{ card: "EX12-016", as: "source" }], security: ["BT1-090"] },
      1: {
        battleArea: [
          { card: "BT1-011", as: "deletion", dp: 6000 },
          { card: "BT1-009", as: "victim" },
        ],
      },
    },
    { autoAcceptOptional: true, autoSelectCards: true },
  );
  s.state.memory = 7;

  // Immunity exists BEFORE the grant, unlike the existing Q6740 case which adds it after.
  advance(s.engine).ledgers.continuous.addRestriction(
    s.perm("victim").permanentId,
    "beAffected",
    EffectDuration.Permanent,
    { fromSourceKind: ["Digimon"] },
  );

  expect(s.engine.applyIntent(0, { type: "playCard", instanceId: s.inst("source").instanceId })).toEqual({ ok: true });
  await settle(() => s.state.players[1]!.battleArea.length === 1);

  // The grant landed: lifting the immunity lets the granted trigger fire on the recipient's turn.
  advance(s.engine).ledgers.continuous.clearRestrictions(s.perm("victim").permanentId);
  s.state.turnSeat = 1;
  await advance(s.engine).fireGlobal(EffectTiming.OnStartMainPhase);

  expect(s.events.some((event) => event.kind === "attackDeclared")).toBe(true);
});
```

(The `clearRestrictions` helper name should be checked against the continuous ledger's actual
API before the test is committed; the load-bearing part is that the immunity precedes the grant.)

**Scope note.** This change also affects every other card that uses `SubTrigger` with `on:`, so
it belongs to the engine owner, not this batch.

### SR-2 — Decode's over-broad source zone is a set-wide pattern, not only this batch

The `hostFilter: { isSelfRef: true }` fix applied here to EX12-014, EX12-016 and EX12-017 is
needed by every other EX12 card that encodes ＜Decode＞ the same way. Grep for `playedByDecode`

### SR-3 — `unit: "digivolutionCards"` scaling with `kind: ["Digimon"]` undercounts Digi-Eggs

The generated shape `scaling: { per: 1, filter: { controllerDefault: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"] },
unit: "digivolutionCards" }` silently skips a Digi-Egg at the bottom of the source's own stack,
because `matching/definition.ts:93-99` enforces `kind` and Digi-Eggs carry catalog kind
`"DigiEgg"`. This was corrected for EX12-018 here. **BT25-103** carries the identical shape at
two sites (`BT25-103.ts:98-104` and `:131-137`) and is outside this batch; it should be checked
by whoever owns that set. Two safe encodings exist: an empty filter (used here), or
`filter: { isSelfRef: true }` as in BT22-060 — `definitionMatches` ignores both fields, so they
are equivalent, and the `digivolutionCards` **unit** already restricts the count to the source's
own stack.
