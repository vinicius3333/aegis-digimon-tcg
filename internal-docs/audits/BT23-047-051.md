# BT23 audit ledger: BT23-051 through BT23-047

Each card was audited individually against every catalog field, its exact local KB output, direct implementation, shared interpreter seams and focused public behavior. Standard optional/card/target decisions are the applicable UI path.

## BT23-051 Golemon — 10/10

Black/red level 4 Mineral/Hudie/CS Virus Champion, cost 5, 5000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from black or red level 3 and alternatively for 2 from level-3 CS. Alliance and Blocker. Its All Turns OPT deletes one opposing 4000-DP-or-lower Digimon whenever it suspends. During its controller's turn it cannot attack opposing Digimon, but may still attack the player. No KB entry. The implementation incorrectly used the blanket `attack` restriction and now uses the engine's precise `cantAttackDigimon` consumer. Public proof observes one boundary target deleted, a second eligible target survive the spent OPT, a 5000-DP control survive, a Digimon attack rejected and a player attack accepted. Suspension identity, target/DP choice, OPT, attack category, keywords and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-050 Ankylomon — 10/10

Black/yellow level 4 Ankylosaur/Hudie/CS Free Champion, cost 5, 5000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from yellow or blue level 3 and alternatively for 2 from Armadillomon or level-3 CS. Printed and inherited Blocker. On Play/When Digivolving gives one opposing Digimon -2000 DP through the opponent's turn end, then during its controller's turn two own Digimon may DNA evolve into Shakkoumon in hand. Q5317 confirms a Digimon reaching 0 DP is not deleted until the whole effect finishes; Q5318 rejects a DNA evolution when the played material cannot evolve; Q5319 confirms the DNA result is unsuspended and can be used by a second pending Alliance. Public proof observes a 0-DP opponent leave only after a legal Ankylomon plus yellow level-4 material becomes an unsuspended Shakkoumon with both materials retained in order. DP duration, state-based deletion timing, turn gate, optional DNA decision, material zones/order, payment, inherited keyword and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-049 Monodramon — 10/10

Black level 3 Mini Dragon/CS Vaccine Rookie, cost 3, 1000 DP; evolves for 0 from black level 2 or level-2 CS. At the start of its controller's main phase it must trash one Dragonkin/Cyborg/Device/CS hand card to draw 1 and gain 1 memory. Its inherited All Turns effect grants +1000 DP. No KB entry. Public mixed-hand proof observes exactly one matching cost enter trash, the nonmatching Tamer remain, one deck card enter hand and one memory gained. A separate unpayable case proves both payloads abort together without drawing or gaining memory. Exact trait OR, hand/trash zones, cost failure, clause ordering, inherited DP and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-048 Gotsumon — 10/10

Black/red level 3 Rock/Hudie/CS Data Rookie, cost 3, 1000 DP; normally evolves for 1 from black or red level 2 and alternatively for 0 from level-2 CS. On Play reveals 3, adds one Hudie card and one CS Tamer/Option, then bottoms the rest. Its inherited When Attacking OPT may play a play-cost-5-or-lower Hudie Digimon from hand free; that exact Digimon cannot evolve and is deleted at the end of the opponent's turn. Q5316 permits a later pending Alliance to suspend the newly played Digimon. Q5567 confirms the delayed deletion and that the evolution lock remains if another effect prevents deletion. Q5568 makes that deletion simultaneous with other opponent-turn-end triggers. The shared delayed-delete seam incorrectly hardcoded owner turn end and now accepts the explicit opponent-turn boundary. Public proofs observe both distinct reveal selections and bottom order, the play-cost boundary, binding/lock, survival through owner turn end and deletion at opponent turn end. Reveal visibility/order, optionality, zones, OPT, delayed identity/duration and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-047 Examon — 10/10

Green/red/blue level 7 Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/CS Data Mega, cost 15, 15000 DP, evolving for 5 from green, red or blue level 6. Piercing, Security Attack +1 and Partition (green level 5 and blue level 5). On Play/When Digivolving suspends five opposing Digimon/Tamers, prevents all opposing Digimon from unsuspending in their next unsuspend phase, then may attack. Its Your Turn OPT reacts whenever the opponent's security is removed from, trashes one effect-placed opposing battle-area Option, then deletes one opposing suspended Digimon/Tamer. Q5313 establishes Security-effect priority over pending check/removal triggers; Q5314 defines the eligible Option; Q5315 confirms the pending Examon effect may trash an Option that the checked Security effect just placed. The watcher incorrectly used a self reference that the security-removal bus interpreted as the owner's stack; it now explicitly watches the opponent's stack, regardless of the removing attacker/effect. Public proofs observe exactly five mixed permanents suspend, every opposing Digimon receive the unsuspend lock, a different attacker remove opposing security, the Option and suspended target leave in order, and the second event remain blocked by OPT. Keywords, Partition parameters, choices, count boundaries, duration, attack decision, event direction/order and OPT map to tested seams.
