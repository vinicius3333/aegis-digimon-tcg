## Card-by-card evidence

### BT20-091 — Cool Boy — provisional 6/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. White cost-4 LIBERATOR Tamer. During the owner's turn, a played or digivolving own Royal Knight Digimon allows this Tamer to suspend for Draw 1 and +1 memory. During the opponent's turn, Once Per Turn, an own Royal Knight Digimon that would leave may be replaced by playing Omekamon from hand without paying the cost. Security plays this Tamer without paying the cost. No card-specific KB entry was returned; the immutable catalog, timing, trait, suspension cost, optional play, and Once Per Turn clauses were used.
- IR trace: 2/2. `apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-091.ts` has separate played/digivolving watchers, an own-turn gate, Royal Knight subject filter, suspend-self cost, Draw 1, and +1 memory. Its opponent-turn replacement is Once Per Turn, own Royal Knight scoped, optional, and plays exact-name Omekamon from hand for free; Security is a free self-play. No semantic correction was needed before the exact-name follow-up.
- Behavioral proof: 0/2. `BT20-091.test.ts` includes structural checks and manually invokes `fireSubTrigger`/`fireTiming`; those calls do not prove the natural play, digivolution, or would-leave origins under this audit standard. No tests were executed.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. The direct module preserves the two distinct timing windows and separates the Royal Knight trigger from the Omekamon replacement target. The replacement stays in the battle-area leave seam, while the Security clause remains a separate face-up-security effect; no unrelated stack source is used.

### BT20-092 — Battle NPC — provisional 6/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. White cost-4 LIBERATOR Tamer. At the start of the owner's turn, memory at 2 or less is set to 3. On Play, a level-3 Digimon from hand is placed under this Tamer to draw 1. At the start of the owner's Main Phase, if the owner has no Digimon, a play-cost-3-or-less Digimon under this Tamer may be played for free and then this Tamer is deleted. Security plays this Tamer for free. No card-specific KB entry was returned; the inclusive memory and level boundaries and mandatory delete tail come from the catalog/rules model.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT20-092.ts` uses the start-of-turn `memoryAtMost2` gate, On Play hand-to-bottom-stack placement of an exact level-3 Digimon before Draw 1, the no-own-Digimon gate, under-Tamer free play at level/play-cost boundaries, and mandatory self-delete. Security is an independent free self-play.
- Behavioral proof: 0/2. `BT20-092.test.ts` only inspects the compiled IR; no natural turn, On Play, or Main Phase execution was run.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. The stack placement is scoped to level 3 and the Tamer itself, while the later play is sourced from that Tamer's under-cards and independently uses the play-cost ceiling. No card-wide Digimon filter can leak into the Tamer stack clause.
