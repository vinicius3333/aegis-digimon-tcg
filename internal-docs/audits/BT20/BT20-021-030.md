### BT20-021 Jesmon GX — 7/10 provisional

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** The immutable record is a red/black level-7 Holy Warrior/X Antibody/Royal Knight Digimon, play cost 9, 16000 DP, with red and black level-6 evolution routes. The direct scaling filter now matches every Royal Knight trait card and no longer incorrectly restricts the counted stack cards to `kind: ["Digimon"]`. - **Behavioral proof — 1/2.** `BT20-021.test.ts` observes the exact 16000-DP boundary, bottom-stack placement, shared entry/attack use, and top-security removal with four Royal Knight Digimon sources. The positive entry and attack timings are injected with `advance(...).fire`, and the new Option-trait case is source-guarded but not executed in this static dispatch; natural attack/play proof remains follow-up work.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** The test uses a legal BT20-010 source stack and BT20-017 Royal Knight material, distinguishes a 16000-DP target from 17000 DP, and proves top-security targeting. - **Snapshot drift/correction.** The read-only snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:59713` still has a generic opponent security target (no `position: "top"`) and incorrectly contains `kind: ["Digimon"]` in the scaling filter.
