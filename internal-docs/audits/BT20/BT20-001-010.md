# BT20-001–BT20-010 Static Audit

Status: provisional card-by-card preparation audit only. This report covers exactly
BT20-001 through BT20-010, in strict ascending card-ID order, from immutable catalog
blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` and the current local rules
knowledge base. It is not an integration or collection-completion report; BT19 static
coverage must be recorded before this range can be integrated.

## Scoring boundary

Each card is scored against exactly five fixed two-point components:

1. Catalog/rules — immutable catalog text and local KB/rules interpretation.
2. IR trace — direct module shape and the executable shared-interpreter path.
3. Behavioral proof — behavior-driving source proof, with natural public intents preferred.
4. Peer and stack proof — relevant peer implementations and legal evolution-stack evidence.
5. Executed delivery gates — intentionally fixed at `0/2` for this dispatch.

The delivery-gate column is fixed at `0/2`, so totals are provisional and capped at
`8/10` even where the first four components are fully evidenced. Tests, typecheck,
lint, formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and
`git diff --check` were explicitly not run. The report records source-level evidence
without claiming that any unexecuted assertion passed.

The immutable catalog blob was inspected directly. Generated
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was inspected read-only and was not treated
as permission to edit generated output; `BT20-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was also inspected
read-only. Both protected files remain unchanged. All ten direct modules register
executable behavior exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no second
`registerCard` registration is present.

## Per-card findings

### BT20-001 — DemiVeemon — provisional 7/10

- Catalog/rules: red level-2 Digi-Egg, Baby Dragon; inherited `[Your Turn]` gives
  this Digimon +2000 DP with 4 or more digivolution cards. The card query returned
  no card-specific KB entry; the general inherited, turn-scope, and stack-count rules
  apply.
- IR trace: `BT20-001.ts` has a `YourTurn` inherited `ModifyDP` targeting self for
  `2000`, with `selfDigivolutionCountAtLeast(4)`, permanent duration, `coverage:
  "full"`, and no residual. The condition is interpreted through the continuous
  effect seam, where `Permanent.stack.length` counts cards under the top card.
- Behavioral proof: `BT20-001.test.ts` observes +2000 at the four-card boundary,
  no bonus below it, and no bonus after changing turn ownership. This is a natural
  continuous-state observation and does not inject the card timing event.
- Peer and stack proof: the source test uses exactly four and three under-cards, but
  its cross-card stacks are synthetic and do not satisfy every catalog evolution
  requirement. The IR is compatible with legal red stacks (for example a red egg,
  red level-3, red level-4/5, and a red host), but that legal sequence is not the
  current behavior fixture, so this component remains `1/2`.
- Corrections and limitations: the prior direct correction added the inclusive
  four-card condition and focused evidence (`369568b8e`); no new executable defect
  was found and no generated file was edited. Delivery gates remain deferred.

### BT20-002 — Bebydomon — provisional 7/10

- Catalog/rules: blue level-2 Digi-Egg, Baby Dragon; inherited `[When Attacking]
  [Once Per Turn]` draws 1 when this Digimon has `[Dracomon]` or `[Examon]` in its
  text. The card query returned no card-specific entry; the shared full-text
  predicate, attack timing, and once-per-turn rules apply.
- IR trace: `BT20-002.ts` maps the inherited effect to `WhenAttacking`, draws one
  for the controller, and uses `selfTopHasText` with text matches for Dracomon or
  Examon. `frequency: "OncePerTurn"`, full coverage, and an empty residual are
  explicit. The interpreter maps this trigger to the production attack window and
  evaluates the live top card text.
- Behavioral proof: `BT20-002.test.ts` reaches the positive case through a public
  attack intent, observes one draw, uses the production timing seam only to exercise
  the otherwise unavailable same-turn repeat, and reaches a negative attack through
  a public intent. The natural positive and negative paths support `2/2`; the seam
  is supplemental to the natural first attack.
- Peer and stack proof: BT20-007 under Bebydomon is a legal alternate evolution
  witness for the positive case. The negative Ryudamon-under-blue-egg fixture is not
  a legal stack for the catalog's red/black evolution requirements, so the source
  does not yet provide a fully legal positive/negative pair; `1/2` is retained.
- Corrections and limitations: the direct module already matches the generated
  trigger after read-only comparison and remains exclusively IR-registered. No new
  runtime correction was required; the once-per-turn supplemental seam and negative
  fixture limitation remain documented.

### BT20-003 — Bibimon — provisional 6/10

- Catalog/rules: yellow level-2 Digi-Egg with Lesser, Abadin Electronics, and SEEKERS

  field Tamers with `[Pulsemon]` in its text or the `[SoC]/[SEEKERS]` trait at the
  bottom of this Digimon's stack, only when that stack has no Tamer. Q4283 defines
  the printed “in its text” union.
- IR trace: `BT20-003.ts` uses an inherited once-per-turn `EndOfYourTurn` optional
  `PlaceUnder`. The target is a controlled Tamer matching the text/trait union;
  `targetIsPermanent` binds the destination to self; `position: "bottom"` and
  `digivolutionStackKindExclude: ["Tamer"]` enforce the bottom placement and
  no-Tamer gate. The shared permanent matcher and relocation seam support the trace.
- Behavioral proof: `BT20-003.test.ts` uses the production `fire` seam directly for
  the end-of-turn window and proves both eligible placement and the blocked case.
  Because end-of-turn is naturally reachable through `runTurn`, and the current
  source does not use that path, this component is `1/2`.
- Peer and stack proof: BT20-089 is a relevant SoC/SEEKERS Tamer peer, but both
  current host fixtures are synthetic and include invalid cross-color/evolution
  combinations; the blocked fixture intentionally includes a Tamer under the host.
  The IR gate is correct, but legal stack proof is incomplete (`1/2`).
- Corrections and limitations: prior commit `51538576c` corrected the generated
  loose-card/host behavior, added self binding, bottom placement, and the Tamer
  exclusion. No new production correction was found; natural-turn evidence remains
  the follow-up limitation.

### BT20-005 — Kapurimon — provisional 7/10

- Catalog/rules: black level-2 Lesser/LIBERATOR Digi-Egg; inherited `[Your Turn]`
  gives this Digimon Jamming for the turn when it checks a security card that was
  already face up. Q4284 establishes security-effect ordering and distinguishes the
  pre-existing face-up check from a normal reveal.
- IR trace: `BT20-005.ts` maps the inherited effect to a your-turn
  `whenCheckedFaceUpSecurity` sub-trigger and grants Jamming to self for the turn.
  The production security-check seam snapshots face-up state before reveal and
  emits the event only for a pre-existing face-up card.
- Behavioral proof: `BT20-005.test.ts` uses public attack intents against a face-up
  and a face-down security card, observing Jamming only in the former path. No
  direct timing injection is used; this is natural security-check coverage.
- Peer and stack proof: the two security states and identical security-card peer are
  strong boundary evidence, but both behavior fixtures place a red BT20-011 under
  a black Kapurimon egg, which is not a legal evolution stack. Legal black examples
  such as Kapurimon -> Solarmon -> Blimpmon were inspected but are not in the source
  fixture, so `1/2` is retained.
- Corrections and limitations: prior commit `8b7ebf966` corrected the generated
  direct keyword grant to the face-up security-check trigger. No new production
  correction was found; legal-stack fixture coverage remains limited.

### BT20-006 — DemiMeramon — provisional 7/10

  Q4285, Q4286, and Q5905 cover pending deletion-effect ordering and the rule that

- IR trace: `BT20-006.ts` uses an inherited optional `OnDeletion` return to the

  trait. The deletion engine preserves the captured inherited source while the
  return action uses the normal trash-to-hand movement seam.
- Behavioral proof: `BT20-006.test.ts` reaches deletion through a public attack

  stack was inspected but is not the current test fixture, so `1/2` is retained.
- Corrections and limitations: no new direct/runtime defect was found; the existing
  IR already matches the printed optional Ghost recovery. The KB pending-order
  cases are relevant review constraints but are not separately behavior-driven here.

### BT20-007 — Dracomon — provisional 7/10

- Catalog/rules: red level-3 Dragon, play cost 3 and 1000 DP; normal red level-2
  evolution cost 0 plus alternate Bebydomon cost 0. Its start-of-main effect says
  “by trashing” one hand card with Dracomon or Examon in its text, draw 1 and gain 1
  memory; inherited `[Your Turn]` gives the host +2000 DP. Q4290 confirms the
  matching-text trash payment and Q4291 defines the full text union.
- IR trace: `BT20-007.ts` represents the hand trash as an optional cost with
  `abortOnDecline`, followed by Draw 1 and mandatory GainMemory 1. This preserves
  the printed dependency: declining the payment aborts the whole processing
  condition. The inherited self modifier, alternate requirement, full coverage,
  and empty residual are present.
- Behavioral proof: the source accepts and declines the start-of-main action and
  checks zones/memory, but reaches the main timing window through direct `fire`.
  The inherited DP path is observed through continuous recomputation; because the
  key start-main proof is not a natural `runTurn`, this is `1/2`.
- Peer and stack proof: the hand pool includes Dracomon/Examon-text candidates and
  the inherited host uses a legal red BT20-007 -> BT20-012 stack. The alternate
  Bebydomon requirement is represented in the module and catalog trace.
- Corrections and limitations: prior commits `ce0bf8711` and `dc39a1e8d` corrected
  the mandatory post-decline memory gain and restored the optional-abort payment
  semantics. No new executable correction was found; natural start-main evidence
  remains deferred.

### BT20-010 — Ryudamon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog/rules: red/black level-3 Vaccine Beast/X Antibody/Chronicle Digimon, play
