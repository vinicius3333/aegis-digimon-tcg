# BT20-041 through BT20-050 audit

## Scope and authority

This report covers exactly BT20-041 through BT20-050 in strict ascending card-ID order. The catalog authority is the immutable catalog blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. Each card was compared with the catalog record, the local rules knowledge base, its direct TypeScript module, read-only generated IR, shared interpreter/runtime seams, peer implementations, legal evolution stacks, and the behavior-driving test source.

All ten direct modules have full compiled coverage with empty residual coverage and register executable behavior exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. No audited card has a second `registerCard` registration. The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot, `BT20-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, and unrelated card files were not modified.

This is a static/source-proof audit. `Executed delivery gates` is fixed at `0/2` for every card because this dispatch explicitly prohibited all tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check`. Structural assertions and manually injected timing/events are supplemental only and do not earn behavioral `2/2`. This report makes no collection-completion claim and does not mark the Orca worktree completed.

## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point dimensions:

1. Catalog/rules — catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. IR trace — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive registration, with generated IR recorded as read-only comparison evidence.
3. Behavioral proof — observable state changes from behavior-driving tests; structural assertions and injected timing alone do not earn `2/2`.
4. Peer and stack proof — useful peer/runtime comparison and legal stack/target coverage.
5. Executed delivery gates — fixed at `0/2` by the task boundary.

The range subtotal is `74/100`, with an average provisional score of `7.4/10`. Scores are capped provisionally at `8/10` by the fixed gate boundary.
