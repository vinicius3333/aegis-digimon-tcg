## Scope and scoring policy

This audit covers exactly BT20-011 through BT20-020 in ascending order. The
authoritative catalog text was read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. The local card KB was queried
exactly once for each card, and local rules, errata, banlist, the generated
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot (read-only), each direct
TypeScript module, shared interpreter seams, peer implementations, legal
evolution/stack routes, and behavior-driving test source were inspected.

The protected `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and
`BT20-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were not edited. This range was prepared during the
permitted BT20 overlap and integrated only after BT19 static coverage was
recorded.

Every scored component is out of two points: Catalog/rules, IR trace,
Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates. The
delivery-gate component is fixed at 0/2 by task instruction, so every result is
provisional and capped at 8/10. Structural-only assertions do not receive
behavioral 2/2; manually injected timing or subtrigger calls are supplemental
when a feasible natural originating event exists.

All ten direct modules use exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`
registration. No second `registerCard` registration was added or retained in
this range.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT20-011 — ExVeemon

- **Catalog/rules:** Red level 4 Free/Mythical Dragon, play cost 4, 4000 DP,
  with red or purple level-3 evolution for 2. On Play and When Digivolving
  delete one opposing Digimon at 3000 DP or less, then on the controller's
  turn optionally DNA digivolve two of that player's Digimon into a hand
  Imperialdramon-name or Free Digimon while paying its cost; the inherited
  clause gives the host +2000 DP on its controller's turn. KB Q6017 confirms
  that the `then` portion continues if the source leaves during the first
  deletion. No range-specific erratum or restriction applies.
- **IR trace:** `BT20-011.ts` shares a sequential Delete-then-DNA body across
  both entry timings, uses the exact 3000 ceiling, own two-material selection,
  hand destination, Free/name union, `payCost: true`, and the inherited turn
  DP grant. The body uses the real deletion and DNA primitives and registers
  only through `registerIrCard`.
- **Behavioral proof:** The test source contains a natural play path that
  deletes the 3000-DP boundary while preserving a 4000-DP peer, then performs
  the paid DNA evolution; it also covers inherited turn scope and source
  continuity. This is behavioral source evidence, not a structural assertion.
- **Peer and stack proof:** The route uses ExVeemon beside a legal purple
  material and merges into Free Paildramon while paying its printed result
  cost; the stack inherits +2000 only during its controller's turn.

## Snapshot drift, corrections, and limitations

The generated effects snapshot was inspected read-only and was not regenerated
or edited. It remains materially different from the direct executable modules:

- BT20-011 is substantially aligned with the direct body.
- BT20-012 omits the direct `payCost: true` and `useAlternateCost: true` flags.
- BT20-013 represents the reduction as a sibling replacement instead of the
  inline reduction consumed by the play action.
- BT20-014 omits the inherited Royal Knight condition.
- BT20-015 places the rookie in the battle area and models a future attack
  watcher instead of empty-breeding placement and immediate `duringAttack`
  evaluation.
- BT20-016 does not preserve the direct same-target binding between the
  Piercing and +4000 actions.
- BT20-017 has stale token text/metadata and places the attack outside the
  `whenPlayed` watcher body.
- BT20-018 models a future attack watcher rather than immediate breeding
  evolution and lacks the direct target-breeding semantics.
- BT20-019 leaves immunity raw/incomplete and omits the unsuspended-attack
  capability from the direct auras.
- BT20-020 uses broad restriction/security-removal shapes instead of the direct
  effect-play and opponent-security primitives.

Two real semantic defects were corrected in affected-card order:

- `36a33e645` — BT20-018: explicitly watch security removal from either stack,
  and lock that direction in the source assertion. The shared
  `securityRemovalGate` defaults an unspecified watcher to the source owner's
  stack, which is not the printed BT20-018 behavior.
- `dc63d9aa3` — BT20-020: explicitly watch opponent security removal, and lock
  that direction in the source assertion. Without the filter, the same shared
  default incorrectly watches the source owner's stack.

No other range card required a direct runtime correction in this pass. The
available source tests provide natural public-intent behavior for most cards;
BT20-014, BT20-018, and BT20-020 retain partial behavioral scores because
feasible End-of-Turn, security-removal, or opponent-effect origins are reached
through explicit timing/subtrigger seams in the source. All test source and
runtime changes above are unexecuted under the task restrictions.
