> Historical lane report. Provisional scores, unexecuted-gate statements, and catalog-drift notes below are superseded by `docs/audits/BT20-AUDIT.md` and the final card ledger at `apps/api/src/cards/BT20/AUDIT.md`.

## Scope and scoring policy

This audit covers exactly BT20-071 through BT20-080 in ascending order. The
authoritative catalog text was read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. For each card, the repository
rules knowledge base, comprehensive rules, errata/banlist data, protected
generated effects snapshot (read-only), direct TypeScript module, interpreter
seams, peer cards, legal evolution/stack routes, and source tests were
inspected.

Every scored component is out of two points: Catalog/rules, IR trace,
Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates. The
delivery-gate component is fixed at 0/2 by task instruction, so every result is
provisional and capped at 8/10. Structural-only assertions do not receive
behavioral 2/2; manually injected timing or event calls are supplemental when
a feasible natural originating event exists.

All ten direct modules use exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`
registration. No second `registerCard` registration was added or retained in
this range. The protected `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and
`BT20-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were not edited.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT20-071 — Soloogarmon

- **Catalog/rules:** Purple/Red level 5 Dark Animal/X-Antibody/SoC/SEEKERS,
  play cost 7, 7000 DP, with red or yellow level-4 evolution for 4. The
  alternate routes are Loogarmon by name and level 4 with SEEKERS, each for 3.
  On Play and When Digivolving, the optional `By trashing 1 card in your hand`
  condition gives one allied Digimon Raid and +3000 DP for the turn. All Turns
  placement of a Tamer card under this Digimon deletes one opposing Digimon at
  6000 DP or less; the inherited Your Turn clause suppresses Option Security
  effects for a SoC/SEEKERS host. The comprehensive rules §15-7 treatment of
  `By X, Y` makes the processing condition optional and aborts its tail when
  declined. The card-specific KB query had no entry.
- **IR trace:** `BT20-071.ts` now represents the `By` condition as an optional
  hand Trash with `abortOnDecline`, followed by the bound DP and Raid actions.
  The Tamer stack watcher filters both the source permanent and the added
  card kind, and the inherited suppression is trait- and turn-scoped. The
  module has `coverage: "full"`, an empty residual, and exclusive IR
  registration. This was corrected in `fdf25ed1d`.
- **Behavioral proof:** `BT20-071.test.ts` naturally digivolves onto a
  SEEKERS level-4 base, observes the hand card being trashed and the Raid/DP
  grant, and separately declines the optional condition to prove that no tail
  effect resolves. The Tamer-under-host watcher and its 6000-DP deletion are
  reached only through direct `advance(...).verb.placeUnder`; because no natural
  card-origin placement is present, that central clause is structural/seam
  evidence and this component is 1/2. The inherited Option suppression is
  checked on and off turn.
- **Peer and stack proof:** The source tests exercise BT20-070 Loogarmon and
  BT20-032 Bulkmon as the two alternate routes and compare BT20-080 and a
  non-SoC/SEEKERS host for inherited behavior. The direct card history includes
  the prior Tamer-stack correction `9b5156029` and audit `63c423fac`.

## Snapshot drift, corrections, and limitations

The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot was inspected
read-only and was not regenerated or edited. It is materially stale versus the
direct executable modules in this range:

- BT20-071's snapshot expresses hand trash as a nested cost on separate
  optional actions instead of the direct optional processing condition that
  aborts the tail.
- BT20-074's snapshot reduces the replacement DNA material shape and does not
  carry the direct hand/deck return replacement semantics.
- BT20-075's snapshot omits the direct Raid and Piercing grants after the
  bound +4000 recipient.
- BT20-076's snapshot retains a raw DNA condition instead of the typed
  `isDnaDigivolving` condition used by the executable module.
- BT20-077's snapshot uses a fixed one-card trash and a stale sibling cost
  modifier rather than the tracked until-four hand count and scaled DP ceiling.
- BT20-078 retains a `RawUnparsed` residual for the effect-driven watcher;
  BT20-080 retains the old Tamer trigger/action shape. BT20-079 is aligned for
  the audited effect bodies.

The direct corrections and source-proof commits, in card order, are:

  abort the Raid/DP tail on decline.

  DNA replacement creates a new result permanent.
- `38c801cfe` — BT20-079: add natural play and losing-battle On Deletion proof.
