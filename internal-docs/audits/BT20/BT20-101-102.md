## BT20-101 — Zephagamon

### Catalog and rules evidence

The immutable catalog record identifies Zephagamon as a green level-6 Digimon with play
cost 8, 13,000 DP, green level-5 cost 5, Mega/Data, and the traits Magic Knight, Vortex
Warriors, LIBERATOR, and Bird Dragon. It is UR, limited to four copies, ACE with overflow
memory 4. Its printed clauses are Blast Digivolve from hand, Piercing, Vortex, Blocker;
an `[All Turns] [Once Per Turn]` watcher for “any Digimon” suspending that may unsuspend
this card; and On Play/When Digivolving effects that may suspend one Digimon, then return
one opponent suspended Digimon for every two suspended Digimon.

The alternate route is play-cost 10 or higher, level 6, `[Vortex Warriors]`, for cost 1.
The local rules KB confirms both scope points that are easy to miscompile:

- Q4415: the All Turns watcher triggers when either player’s Digimon suspends.
- Q4416: the On Play/When Digivolving suspend may choose either player’s Digimon.

### IR and runtime trace

`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-101.ts:19-179` is full-coverage IR with an empty residual and
registers only through `registerIrCard("BT20-101", compiled)`.

- The hand Counter effect carries `BlastDigivolve`; Piercing, Vortex, and Blocker are
  separate static keyword effects.
- The All Turns effect is a once-per-turn `whenSuspended` SubTrigger. Its source filter is
  `{ controllerDefault: "any", kind: ["Digimon"] }`, and its optional nested Unsuspend
  is self-targeted.
- Both entry timings suspend an optional Digimon from either controller, then perform an
  optional opponent-only return to deck bottom. The return target requires `suspended: true`
  and its scaling counts every suspended Digimon across both controllers, two per returned
  card.
- The alternate requirement is represented as level 6, trait Vortex Warriors,
  `basePlayCostMin: 10`, cost 1, alternate. `cardData.ts:445-488` applies the exact level,
  trait, and minimum play-cost gates.

The target generated record at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:64639` matches
the direct module’s relevant clauses, including the minimum base play cost. The runtime
registration seam is documented in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter.ts:1-7,32-56`.

### Behavioral, peer, and stack evidence

`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-101.test.ts:5-46` provides structural coverage for the
alternate requirement, any-controller suspension watcher, self-unsuspend, and both
scaling filters. `apps/api/src/engine/combat/vortexSummoningSickness.test.ts:31-55`
provides a natural runtime Vortex attack scenario using BT20-101, but it proves the
keyword/combat seam rather than this card’s All Turns or entry effects. No natural
BT20-101 scenario was executed in this audit, and structural/manual evidence cannot be
promoted to 2/2 Behavioral proof where a natural origin is feasible.

`apps/api/src/cards/EX11/EX11-035.ts:45-112` is a useful Vortex Warriors peer: its
same-family suspended-count scaling also explicitly uses `controllerDefault: "any"`.
`apps/api/src/engine/cards/cardData.test.ts:160-168` supplies the legal stack source
comparison: EX11-035 (play cost 11) qualifies for BT20-101’s cost-1 route, while the
play-cost-8 BT20-101 record does not. There is no executed natural BT20-101 stack/effect
scenario in this audit.

### Corrections and limitations

No new BT20-101 correction was required. The existing prior correction `ac79578ff`
already added the minimum base play-cost gate and its source assertion; the existing
hand-fixed IR already pins both “any Digimon” filters instead of allowing the compiler’s
default “mine” scope to regress.

The direct module has no duplicate legacy `registerCard` registration. The generated IR,
effects JSON, and static audit artifacts remained untouched as required.

## BT20-102 — Omnimon (X Antibody)

### Catalog and rules evidence

The immutable catalog record identifies Omnimon (X Antibody) as a blue/white/red level-7
Digimon with play cost 16, 16,000 DP, blue and red level-6 cost-6 evolution entries,
Mega/Vaccine, and Holy Warrior, X Antibody, Royal Knight, and LIBERATOR traits. It is UR,
limited to four copies. The printed effects are Raid, Piercing, Blocker; On Play/When
Digivolving conditional mass deletion that chooses one Digimon belonging to either player,
deletes all other Digimon, then returns one opponent Digimon to deck bottom; and a once-per-
turn End of Your Turn effect that may give one of your Digimon Rush for the turn and have
that same Digimon attack without suspending.

The alternate evolution route is `[Omnimon]: Cost 2`. The local rules KB confirms:

- Q4417: accepting Rush obligates the same Digimon’s attack without suspending when possible.
- Q4418: the attack is legal even if that Digimon is already suspended.
- Q4419: simultaneous copies cannot declare a second attack during the existing attack.
- Q4725: the post-Then return still resolves when the conditional first process fails.
- Q4726: Rush remains for the turn if an effect prevents the turn from ending immediately.
- Q5907: a card played from an On Deletion effect and placed under BT20-102 satisfies the
  Omnimon/X Antibody stack condition.
- Q6018: removal of the source during the first process does not prevent the post-Then process.

### IR and runtime trace

`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-102.ts:8-184` is full-coverage IR with an empty residual and
registers only through `registerIrCard("BT20-102", compiled)`.

- Raid, Piercing, and Blocker are static keyword effects.
- Both On Play and When Digivolving have a Digimon `count: "all"` Delete with a nested
  `except` selection of one Digimon from either player, followed by an unconditional
  opponent Digimon return to deck bottom.
- The stack condition is now represented in both timings as the OR union
  `{ tokens: ["Omnimon"], match: "nameExact" }` or
  `{ tokens: ["X Antibody"], match: "trait" }`.
- End of Your Turn is once per turn: optional mine-only Rush for the turn with
  `abortOnDecline`, then a same-target Attack with `withoutSuspending: true` conditioned
  on the first action acting.
- The alternate requirement is `namesExact: ["Omnimon"]`, cost 2, alternate.

The condition correction in commit `812aea458` fixes a confirmed semantic defect. Before
the correction, one matcher entry used `tokens: ["Omnimon", "X Antibody"]` with
`match: "name"`, so a stack card carrying only the X Antibody trait could not satisfy the
printed slash condition. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:327-345`
defines name and trait matching separately, and `:163-168` defines multiple `nameOrTrait`
entries as a union; the corrected two-entry shape therefore implements the printed OR.
The additive exact-name follow-up then tightened the standalone `[Omnimon]` arm from
substring `match: "name"` to `match: "nameExact"` in both timings and changed the
alternate requirement from `names` to `namesExact`, preventing suffixed names from
satisfying text that names `[Omnimon]` exactly.

The generated record at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:64717` was inspected but
not edited. It still contains the pre-correction combined name matcher and the historical
generated End of Your Turn record without the direct module’s Attack action. The direct
module is the executable registration path for this card; regeneration of the read-only
snapshot is a follow-up limitation outside this dispatch.

The `except` runtime seam is present in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:323-344`
and is consumed by Delete in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts:51-139`.
That path resolves the selected survivor first, removes it from the mass-delete result, and
then continues to the unconditional Return action, matching Q4725/Q6018’s post-Then rules.

### Behavioral, peer, and stack evidence

`apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-102.test.ts:29-47` structurally covers Rush, mandatory same-
target attack, and unsuspending attack. Its natural scenario at `:72-109` digivolves from
BT5-086 (an Omnimon source) and checks that the chosen survivor remains while other own and
opponent Digimon are deleted. The added structural predicate assertion at `:50-70` checks
both entry timings. These sources provide partial behavior evidence, but no natural scenario
was executed here for an X Antibody trait-only stack, the false-condition post-Then return,
or the End of Your Turn attack sequence; consequently Behavioral proof is not 2/2.

`apps/api/src/cards/EX11/EX11-046.ts:12-30,83-101` and its natural scenario in
`apps/api/src/cards/EX11/EX11-046.test.ts:61-92` are a close `Target.except` mass-delete
peer. `apps/api/src/cards/BT16/BT16-079.ts:96-105` is the matching condition-shape peer,
using separate name and trait entries for `[Cherubimon] or [X Antibody]`.
`apps/api/src/cards/EX11/EX11-053.test.ts:43-60` supplies the Q5907 legal-stack source:
an X Antibody Omekamon is placed under a played BT20-102. The target test’s BT5-086 route
and these peers corroborate the legal stack/effect design, but there is no executed natural
X-only condition scenario in this audit.

### Corrections and limitations

Corrected both entry-timing conditions in `apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-102.ts` and added
the corresponding source assertion in `apps/api/src/cards/BT20/BT20-102.test.ts`.
The additive follow-up tightens the Omnimon arm and alternate route to exact-name fields
and extends the source assertion to both representations. The corrections are card-local;
they do not change the shared matcher or any other card. The module has no duplicate
legacy `registerCard` registration.

The generated effects snapshot remains intentionally stale for this card’s corrected
condition and direct End of Your Turn Attack because the task marked it read-only. The
existing natural target test does not exercise the newly corrected X-only branch or the
post-Then false-condition branch. All such behavior remains a documented follow-up for
the prohibited execution/regeneration workflow.
