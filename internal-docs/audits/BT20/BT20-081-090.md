## Card-by-card findings

### BT20-081 — Fenriloogamon: Takemikazuchi — provisional 6/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. The immutable record is a purple/yellow level-7 Virus
  Digimon with Mega, Dark Animal, X Antibody, SoC, and SEEKERS traits, play cost 9,
  16000 DP, and purple or yellow level-6 evolution for 6. It is an ACE with
  Overflow 5 and `[Hand][Counter] <Blast DNA Digivolve ([Fenriloogamon] +
  [Kazuchimon])>`. On Play/When Digivolving it gives two opposing Digimon -10000
  DP for the turn, then deletes one opposing Digimon at 10000 DP or less when a
  Tamer is in its stack. When Attacking, trashing the top security card activates
  one of its When Digivolving effects. KB Q4405 defines the text predicate, Q4406
  requires distinct targets for the two-Digimon modifier, and Q4407 defers
  zero-DP deletion until effect processing completes.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT20-081.ts` contains the hand Counter marker and exact Blast DNA
  materials, repeats the two-target -10000 modifier and stack-Tamer conditional
  delete at both entry timings, and makes top-security trash an optional cost for
  `ReactivateEffect(WhenDigivolving)`. Coverage is full with an empty residual and
  registration is exclusive. The generated snapshot still has a raw stack-Tamer
  condition and does not preserve the direct top-security source boundary; it was
  not edited.
- Behavioral proof: 0/2. `BT20-081.test.ts` only inspects compiled IR shape. It does
  not drive a natural play, evolution, attack, security removal, or Blast DNA
  origin, so the structural assertions do not receive behavioral points.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. The Blast DNA materials are exact Fenriloogamon and
  Kazuchimon names, the delete condition reads this Digimon's own stack rather
  than global battle state, and the DP modifier/deletion target scopes are
  independent. Legal Fenriloogamon/Kazuchimon source combinations and the
  Tamer-under-host boundary were checked against the surrounding BT20 stack.

### BT20-083 — Omekamon — provisional 6/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. The immutable record is a white level-4 Data Puppet/X
  Antibody/LIBERATOR Digimon, play cost 5 and 5000 DP, with no normal evolution
  cost. It has Blocker; at one or fewer security cards it may free-evolve into
  hand Omnimon (X Antibody) while ignoring requirements; on deletion it may place
  itself at the bottom of an own King Drasil_7D6 stack in breeding. Its inherited
  breeding/opponent-turn effect triggers when the owner's security is removed,
  suspends this Digimon, and plays one Omekamon from this Digimon's stack for free.
  KB Q4409 confines the inherited effect to breeding, Q4410 gives security effects
  timing priority, and Q4411 confirms the same Omekamon can be played from its
  source stack after the host is suspended.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT20-083.ts` has Blocker without an unprinted name grant, gates
  the free Omnimon evolution with `securityAtMost: 1`, self-binds deletion
  placement to an own breeding King Drasil_7D6, and marks the inherited watcher as
  breeding-only/opponent-turn. The inherited play now uses
  `fromOwnDigivolutionStack: true`, which binds candidates to the source
  Omekamon's own stack; the generic `from: ["digivolutionCards"]` path would scan
  every stack. The correction is committed below; coverage is full and residual is
  empty.
- Behavioral proof: 0/2. `BT20-083.test.ts` checks Blocker, security gating,
  deletion placement fields, and the source primitive only. It does not drive
  natural deletion, security removal, breeding, or the same-card stack play.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. The exact King Drasil_7D6 receiver and Omnimon (X
  Antibody) hand target were checked against BT13-007/BT23-072 and BT10-086/
  BT20-102 peers. The inherited clause's source must be this Omekamon's own
  stack, not another permanent or opponent stack, and the direct correction
  preserves that distinction.
