## Card-by-card evidence

### BT20-031 — Liamon — provisional 7/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. Yellow/black level-4 Champion with alternate level-3 `[ACCEL]` evolution for cost 2; both On Play and When Digivolving apply -3000 DP to one opposing Digimon for the turn; inherited Barrier. No card-specific KB entry was returned; the catalog keyword, timing, target, duration, and alternate-evolution contract were used.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT20-031.ts` has one opponent-Digimon `ModifyDP` action at each entry timing, `duration: "forTheTurn"`, an inherited Barrier keyword, and the exact alternate ACCEL requirement. No semantic correction was needed.
- Behavioral proof: 1/2. `BT20-031.test.ts` naturally plays and alternate-digivolves Liamon and observes the -3000 DP result. The inherited Barrier case is an `observe.hasKeyword` assertion rather than a natural prevention/deletion exchange, so it does not receive full behavioral credit under this audit standard.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. The source fixtures use BT20-030 as the evolution base, BT20-010 as the opposing target, and BT20-032 under a host to distinguish inherited Barrier from a top-card Liamon.

### BT20-032 — Bulkmon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. Yellow/green level-4 Champion with alternate `[Pulsemon]` or level-3 `[SEEKERS]` cost 2; On Play/When Digivolving may take the top security card at 3 or more security, then Recovery +1 from deck at 2 or fewer; inherited Once Per Turn battle deletion gains 1 memory. KB Q4325 confirms the inherited effect cannot activate when the opponent's Digimon and the host are deleted at the same timing.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT20-032.ts` repeats the ordered optional `SecurityManipulation(toHand, toTop)` and mandatory conditional `SecurityManipulation(addTop, deck)` for both entry timings, with inclusive security thresholds; the inherited watcher is `whenDeletesInBattle` plus Once Per Turn GainMemory. No semantic correction was needed.
- Behavioral proof: 2/2. `BT20-032.test.ts` naturally plays at exactly three security, verifies the top card enters hand and the next card recovers after the count becomes two, and naturally attacks into an opposing Digimon to prove the inherited memory gain while the host survives.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. The test uses ordered security/deck identities and BT20-034 under Bulkmon to exercise inherited battle deletion, while BT20-010 supplies the opposing Digimon target.

### BT20-033 — LoaderLeomon — provisional 7/10

KB Q4326-Q4330 confirm suppression of triggered and externally activated When Digivolving effects, preservation of When Attacking effects, rejection of processing only a `by` condition, and no Once Per Turn consumption when suppression prevents activation.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT20-033.ts` uses `Restrict(cannotActivateWhenDigivolving)` followed by `ModifyDP(-3000)` for both entry timings, and the latter target now has `sameTarget: true`, preserving the printed singular “one of their Digimon.” The inherited watcher redirects an opposing attack to this Digimon, and the alternate ACCEL route is exact. Correction committed in `209ee634a9d75419ff6759c04b496f5cfda58dc4`.
- Behavioral proof: 1/2. The source test naturally plays LoaderLeomon and observes the DP reduction/restriction, and naturally attacks an opponent's player through an inherited host to prove redirection. The When Digivolving suppression boundaries and the new same-target regression assertion remain unexecuted, so this is partial proof.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. BT20-030 is the direct target fixture, while BT20-036 under LoaderLeomon is the inherited host and BT20-010 is the opposing attacker. The target-binding correction follows the same `sameTarget` pattern used by BT19-093 and BT19-037.
