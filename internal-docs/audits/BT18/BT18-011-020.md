## Per-card findings and proof

### BT18-011 — Agunimon — 7/10 provisional

The immutable catalog identifies a Red/Purple level-4 Hybrid, 5000 DP, play
cost 5, with Red/Purple level-3 evolution and alternate `[Takuya Kanbara]: 2`
and `[BurningGreymon]: 0` routes. The direct IR returns one of the controller's
trash cards matching the `[Hybrid]/[Ten Warriors]` trait or a Tamer with
inherited effects during When Digivolving, and gives its host +2000 DP during
its controller's turn as an inherited effect. The alternate requirements and
permanent self-bound DP modifier match the catalog.

Natural public evolution from BurningGreymon now drives the return and decline
proof; the test also checks the inherited-Tamer/plain-Tamer split, both
alternate costs, and the owner-turn DP boundary. The former direct
When-Digivolving timing calls were removed. Peer/stack proof is 1/2 because the
source test directly exercises Hybrid and inherited-Tamer candidates but does
not separately target an exact Ten Warriors candidate; this is a proof gap,
not an identified runtime mismatch.

### BT18-012 — Grumblemon — 7/10 provisional

The catalog identifies a Red level-4 Hybrid, 5000 DP, play cost 5, with
`[Gigasmon]: 0`. The IR has mandatory one-opponent-Digimon deletion at 3000 DP
or less on play and When Digivolving, plus the same inherited When Attacking
effect with `OncePerTurn`; the Gigasmon alternate route is present.

Natural play, Gigasmon evolution, exact 3000/4000 deletion boundaries, and an
inherited attack now drive the focused source proof. The invalid second manual
event-bus fire was removed. Behavioral proof is 1/2 because a second attack by
the same host in the same turn is not naturally exercised after the host
suspends; the structural `OncePerTurn` trace remains present, but no manual
timing injection is counted as a substitute.

### BT18-013 — Deltamon — 8/10 provisional

The catalog identifies a Red/Purple level-4 Composite, 5000 DP, play cost 5,
with `[Gazimon]/[Gizamon]: 2`, Raid, an On Play/When Digivolving hand-trash
cost, and return of one Composite/Wicked God card from trash. Retaliation is
correctly represented as inherited. The local KB has no card-specific entry.

Natural play and both legal evolution sources prove the paid return and cost;
optional decline preserves both cards. An added mixed-trait case selects a
real Wicked God card while leaving a nonmatching card in trash, and the
Retaliation proof uses a real battle. No unresolved non-gate seam was found.

### BT18-014 — Gigasmon — 7/10 provisional

The catalog identifies a Red level-4 Hybrid, 6000 DP, play cost 6, with
`[Grumblemon]: 1`. The IR grants Rush to one own Digimon on play and When
Digivolving, and carries inherited When Attacking deletion of one opposing

Natural play and Grumblemon evolution prove Rush, and a real attack proves the

### BT18-015 — Kimeramon — 8/10 provisional

The catalog identifies a Red/Purple level-5 Composite, 8000 DP, play cost 8,
with level-4 Composite evolution at 3. Both When Digivolving and When Attacking
delete one own Digimon as cost before deleting one opponent's lowest-DP
Digimon. On Deletion may DNA digivolve a Machinedramon in play and a Kimeramon
in trash into Millenniummon in hand; Security Attack +1 is inherited.

Q2922–Q2924 and Q3014 were applied to the DNA requirement and source zones.
Natural evolution and attack flows prove both deletion clauses, exact lowest-DP
selection, cost decline, and the inherited Security Attack. A natural losing
battle now moves Kimeramon to trash and drives the DNA recovery with a live
Machinedramon; the old direct deletion seam was removed. No unresolved non-gate
seam was found.

### BT18-018 — EmperorGreymon — 8/10 provisional

The catalog identifies a Red/Green/Blue level-6 Hybrid, 13000 DP, play cost 13,

### BT18-019 — Millenniummon — 8/10 provisional

The catalog identifies a Red/Black level-7 Composite, 14000 DP, play cost 14,

one opposing Digimon and, when DNA digivolving, may return one level-bearing

Q2928–Q2931, Q5779–Q5780, and Q6013 were applied. The direct module retains the

drive both the complete and incomplete On Deletion costs, replacing the direct
delete seam. No unresolved non-gate seam was found.
