## BT18-101 — Lucemon: Satan Mode

### Catalog and rules

The immutable catalog specifies Purple level 7, play cost 16, 16,000 DP,
digivolution from `Lucemon: Chaos Mode` for 6, and the following behavior:

* When Digivolving, play one `Lucemon: Larva` from trash into an empty
  breeding area without paying its cost, then delete one opposing Digimon or
  Tamer.
* At End of All Turns, once per turn, trash the opponent's top security card;
  if that effect did not trash a card, delete one opposing Digimon and one
  opposing Tamer.

The local QA entry `Q3053` in `data/kb/qa.json:12970-12977` confirms that the
security trash is mandatory when the opponent has a top security card; the
player cannot choose the fallback instead. The comprehensive rules at
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2542-2549` define the relevant Breeding,
Trash, and Security effect locations, and
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2524-2527` covers once-per-turn use counting
even when processing cannot execute.

### Implementation correction

The original generated trace represented the Larva play as a generic delete
cost string and did not provide the executable source-zone and empty-breeding
constraints. `apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-101.ts:12-48` now explicitly uses
`PlayWithoutCost` from `trash`, requires an empty breeding area, and makes the
play mandatory. The delete follows only when the play actually acted, so an
occupied breeding area does not incorrectly delete an opposing card.

The End-of-All-Turns implementation at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-101.ts:50-98` explicitly trashes from Security,
binds the result, and runs the Digimon-plus-Tamer fallback only when no
security card was trashed. It remains once per turn and uses the card's
mandatory timing semantics.

### Behavior-driving proof

`apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-101.test.ts:46-104` drives a natural legal
digivolution from the registered `BT18-082` Chaos Mode, verifies Larva enters
the breeding area and the opposing target is deleted, and verifies the
occupied-breeding negative case. Lines `126-160` drive a natural turn through
the engine's end-of-turn timing, proving both the security-trash path and the
no-security Digimon/Tamer fallback. These are source-level behavioral tests;
they were not executed because the task explicitly prohibited test and gate
execution.

## BT18-102 — Susanoomon

### Catalog and rules

The immutable catalog specifies White level 7, play cost 9, 16,000 DP, all
seven level-6 color digivolution costs of 6, Mega/Hybrid forms, Vaccine,
Shaman, `isAce`, and overflow memory 5. It also specifies:

* Digivolve from `Takuya Kanbara` or `Koji Minamoto` with 10 Hybrid trait cards
  under it for 6.
* Hand Counter Blast Digivolve.
* When Digivolving/When Attacking, delete one opposing Digimon at or below a
  DP ceiling that starts at 10,000 and rises by 2,000 for each color in this
  Digimon's digivolution cards.
* When Attacking, place up to five of this Digimon's Tamer digivolution cards
  as bottom security cards, then trash the opponent's top security card for
  each card placed.
* Rule Trait: Hybrid.

Local QA entries `Q3054` and `Q3057` in `data/kb/qa.json:12979-13006` confirm
that the player may choose the order of multiple Tamer security cards.
`Q3055` confirms that more than 10 Hybrid cards still satisfies the alternate
digivolution requirement, while `Q3056` confirms that requirement cannot be
used for Blast Digivolve. `Q6665-Q6670` at
`data/kb/qa.json:13009-13048` establish the Tamer-origin digivolution and
stack semantics. The comprehensive rules at
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:717-764` define stacked and digivolution
cards, and lines `962-967` establish that a “with/have X cards” requirement
is fulfilled at X or more.

### Implementation correction

The generated trace had three behaviorally material gaps: its DP scaling was
not sourced from this Digimon's digivolution stack, its security source was
not restricted to Tamers, and its custom security-trash operation did not
provide a supported scaling path. `apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-102.ts:29-76`
now attaches `dpCeilingScaling` directly to each Delete action, with distinct
colors counted from the source Digimon's `digivolutionCards` stack. Attaching
the scaling to Delete is necessary because a later cost-modifier action cannot
retroactively change a deletion already resolved by the runtime.

The second When Attacking effect at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-102.ts:79-107` uses the supported
`SecurityManipulation` primitive with a Tamer-only digivolution-card source,
bottom placement, an up-to-five limit, and an actual placed-card count for
the subsequent opponent security trash. The shared runtime correction at
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/security.ts:565-586` records
the number of selected cards that actually arrived in security and exposes it
to the follow-up action, including when the source is a digivolution stack.

The alternate digivolution metadata at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-102.ts:127-138` requires at least 10 Hybrid
trait cards and marks the requirement incompatible with Blast Digivolve. The
module retains the Hand Counter Blast Digivolve marker and the Rule Hybrid
trait, and registers only through `registerIrCard`.

### Behavior-driving proof

`apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-102.test.ts:97-124` performs a natural alternate
digivolution from a Tamer carrying exactly 10 Hybrid cards plus Tamer cards,
then verifies the delete ceiling, resulting stack, Hybrid trait, and memory.
Lines `126-166` perform a natural attack with a six-color legal stack and
verify the scaled deletion, exactly five Tamer placements, bottom-security
count change, corresponding opponent security trash, and source suspension.
Lines `168-205` exercise the natural attack-to-player counter window and
respond with a standard level-6 Digimon to prove Blast Digivolve. Lines
`207-238` exercise the same natural counter opportunity with the Tamer/10
Hybrid state and verify Susanoomon is not eligible through the incompatible
alternate requirement. These are source-level behavioral tests; they were not
executed because the task explicitly prohibited test and gate execution.
