## Scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable local KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — complete compiled-IR trigger/action trace and exclusive registration.
3. **Behavioral proof** — behavior-driving source proof through feasible natural origins; structural-only assertions are not counted as behavior.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — comparison with shared/peer mechanics and legal card stacks.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at 0/2 by the task boundary.
