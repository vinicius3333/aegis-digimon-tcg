## Scoring boundary

Each card is scored against the five fixed two-point components:

1. Catalog/rules — catalog text and local KB interpretation.
2. IR trace — direct module shape and executable shared-interpreter path.
3. Behavioral proof — behavior-driving source proof and natural test coverage.
4. Peer and stack proof — useful peer implementations and legal evolution stacks.
5. Executed delivery gates — intentionally fixed at `0/2` for this dispatch.

Tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and
`git diff --check` were not run. Therefore every total below is provisional and capped at
`8/10` by the deferred-gate boundary; a `2/2` in each of the first four components means
that no additional card-local source-proof gap remained after the listed correction. No
collection-completion claim is made.

The ten direct modules retain exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration;
no duplicate `registerCard` registration was added or preserved. Neither
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` nor the root protected ledger
`BT18-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was modified.

## Per-card findings

### BT18-001 DemiMeramon

- Catalog/rules: the immutable entry is a red level-2 Digi-Egg with inherited
  `[When Attacking][Once Per Turn]`, a red-Tamer condition, and deletion of one opposing
  Digimon at 3000 DP or less. The local KB query returned no BT18-001 entry.
- IR trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-001.ts` maps the inherited clause to
  `WhenAttacking`, `youHave` a red Tamer, opponent Digimon `dp <= 3000`, `Delete`, and
  `OncePerTurn`; coverage is full and residuals are empty.
- Behavioral proof: `BT18-001.test.ts` now uses a legal red chain ending in Jesmon, a red
  Tamer, and a natural attack followed by Jesmon's natural unsuspend and a second natural
  attack. The second attack does not repeat the deletion; the no-red-Tamer case remains
  negative. No event-bus injection remains.
- Peer/stack proof: the Jesmon/Sistermon red stack exercises inherited source placement,
  the shared `WhenAttacking` binding, and the same once-per-turn semantics used by other
  inherited attack reactions.
- Corrections and seams: replaced the former invalid blue host and manual second trigger
  with the legal red stack and natural re-attack proof. No unresolved non-gate seam remains;
  delivery gates are deferred at 0/2.
