# BT18-021–BT18-030 Static Audit

> Historical range report: superseded by `docs/audits/BT18-AUDIT.md`.

Date: 2026-08-28

This report covers exactly BT18-021 through BT18-030, inspected one card at a
time in strict ascending ID order under the `verify-card-implementation`
protocol. The immutable catalog authority was blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; the mutable working-tree catalog
was not used as authority. Every card was compared with its catalog record,
the current local KB, direct TypeScript module, generated IR snapshot,
shared interpreter/runtime seams, useful peers, legal stacks, and
behavior-driving source proof.

`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and `BT18-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were
preserved and not edited. All ten direct modules use exclusive
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration; no duplicate `registerCard`
registration was added or retained.

## Deferred execution boundary

This is a provisional static audit. The dispatch explicitly prohibited all
tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser checks, focused/mechanism/collection
gates, and `git diff --check`; none of those commands was run. Therefore the
Executed delivery gates component is fixed at **0/2** for every card, every
result is provisional, and no card is claimed as 10/10. Natural public-intent
behavior scenarios present in the colocated source were counted as behavioral
proof; structural assertions and direct timing/event injection were not counted
as behavioral credit. This revision removes the residual direct timing
scenarios from BT18-022, BT18-024, and BT18-026 while preserving the natural
replacements.

The five fixed two-point components are:

1. **Catalog/rules:** immutable catalog and applicable local KB rulings.
2. **IR trace:** direct executable timing, targets, costs, zones, duration,
   optionality, and registration.
3. **Behavioral proof:** natural originating event and observable state
   assertions, including relevant boundaries and negative cases.
4. **Peer and stack proof:** shared runtime semantics, useful peers, and legal
   evolution-stack behavior.
5. **Executed delivery gates:** intentionally deferred here, 0/2.

## Immutable catalog and KB trace

- **BT18-021 — Penguinmon:** Blue/Red level 3, play cost 3, 1000 DP,
  Vaccine/Avian; Blue or Red level 2 evolution for 1. Its Your Turn,
  once-per-turn replacement reduces by 1 when this Digimon or an own Tamer
  would digivolve into a multicolor blue or red Digimon. Its inherited effect
  is Jamming. Q2932 confirms that the replacement does not apply from the
  breeding area.
- **BT18-022 — Kumamon:** Blue/Red level 4, play cost 5, 5000 DP,
  Hybrid/Variable/Beastkin/Ice-Snow; Blue or Red level 3 evolution for 3,
  Tommy Himi for 2, and Korikakumon for 0. When Digivolving it trashes the
  bottom two cards of one opposing stack. When Attacking once per turn it may
  digivolve one own Digimon or Tamer into a blue/red Hybrid in hand for 1
  less. It grants Ice-Snow and its inherited replacement may play an inherited-
  effect Tamer from its own stack when leaving other than by its effect.
  Q2933–Q2935 and Q6590–Q6592 cover Tamer evolution treatment, bonus draws,
  attack timing, Tamer stack identity, and inherited effects.
- **BT18-023 — Lanamon:** Blue level 4, play cost 5, 5000 DP,
  Hybrid/Variable/Fairy/Aquatic; Blue level 3 evolution for 2 and Calmaramon
  for 0. On Play and When Digivolving it reveals three and adds an Aqua/Sea
  Animal Digimon to hand or places it under a blue Digimon, bottoming the
  rest. It grants Aquatic and inherits an optional once-per-turn level-3
  return on attack. Q2936–Q2939 and Q6593–Q6595 cover the same Tamer and
  inherited-effect boundaries and the as-many-as-possible reveal selection.

## Card-by-card implementation evidence

### BT18-021 — Penguinmon — 8/10 provisional

The direct module uses a Your Turn once-per-turn `wouldDigivolve`
replacement. Its source filter is own self or own Tamer, and its destination
filter requires an own multicolor Digimon with Blue and Red colors; the
inherited Jamming projection is static. The module is full, residual-free,
and exclusively registered. The generated snapshot has a broader stale
Digimon/Tamer source filter and was read-only evidence.

The colocated source naturally evolves Penguinmon into multicolor BT18-022,
checks the monocolor BT18-023 rejection added in `6e648b09`, evolves two
Tamers to expose the once-per-turn boundary, and checks that breeding-area
Penguinmon does not reduce the cost. BT18-022 and BT1-030 provide legal
multicolor and inherited-stack peers. Shared replacement cost reduction,
breeding-area subject guards, and once-per-turn replacement identity were
inspected. No executable seam remains.

### BT18-022 — Kumamon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR maps bottom-two `TrashDigivolution`, an optional once-per-turn
When Attacking hand `Digivolve` with `reduceCost: 1` and `payCost: true`, the
Ice-Snow Rule trait, and an inherited `wouldLeavePlay` replacement with
`leaveCause: otherThanYourEffect`, own-stack-only inherited Tamer play, and
free payment. It carries Tommy Himi and Korikakumon alternate requirements
and is full, residual-free, and exclusively registered. The generated
snapshot omitted `payCost: true` and `fromOwnDigivolutionStack: true`; the
direct executable module is the audited authority and the protected snapshot
was not edited.

Natural source proof now covers Tommy Himi evolution and bottom-stack trash,
Kumamon's attack evolution into a hand Hybrid, and opponent battle deletion
of a legal host that naturally plays the inherited Tamer from that host's
stack. The redundant direct When Digivolving and manual leave timing fixtures
were removed; the natural scenarios carry the behavioral evidence.
BT18-021, BT18-025, and BT18-089 provide the reduction, Hybrid, and Tamer
stack peers. Shared bottom-stack indexing, own-stack filtering, leave-cause
classification, and once-per-turn attack evolution were inspected.

### BT18-023 — Lanamon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR duplicates the RevealAdd behavior for On Play and When
Digivolving, with three-card reveal, Aqua/Sea Animal Digimon filtering,
hand/place-under alternatives, and deck-bottom remainder. It grants
Aquatic, carries the Calmaramon cost-0 alternate route, and has the inherited
once-per-turn level-3 return. It is full, residual-free, and exclusively
registered; the generated snapshot matches the direct shape.

Natural source proof covers hand addition on play, the place-under alternative
under a legal blue host added in `080d3a7f`, the natural Calmaramon evolution
reveal, the alternate source stack, and the inherited attack return. BT18-022
and BT18-024 supply adjacent Hybrid/reveal stack peers. Shared RevealAdd
option selection, blue-host placement, and deck-bottom remainder semantics
were inspected. No executable seam remains.

### BT18-024 — Calmaramon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR has typed stack-count and binding-empty conditions for both On
Play and When Digivolving branches: return an opposing level-4-or-lower
Digimon when a level-3 card is present, otherwise optionally place a blue
level-3 Digimon from hand under the source. It also carries the inherited
once-per-turn attack return and Lanamon cost-1 alternate route. Coverage is
full, residuals are empty, and registration is exclusive. The generated
snapshot retains raw conditions rather than the direct typed condition forms;
the snapshot was not edited.

Natural proof covers the no-stack On Play placement through a public play
intent, the When Digivolving placement branch, the return branch on a legal
Lanamon stack already carrying a level-3 card, the inherited attack return,
and the printed alternate cost. The impossible pre-stacked On Play timing
fixture was removed; the reachable When Digivolving stack branch is covered
naturally in `a2fc34f5`. BT18-023 and BT18-029 provide reveal/stack and
leave-replacement peers. Shared binding conditions, stack-level filters, and
placement position were inspected; no executable seam remains.

### BT18-025 — Korikakumon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR carries main and inherited Jamming, separate On Play and When
Digivolving Restrict actions targeting one opposing stackless Digimon,
until-opponent-turn-end duration, the Ice-Snow Rule trait, and Tommy/Kumamon
alternate costs. It is full, residual-free, and exclusively registered. The
generated snapshot adds a stale `whileMatchesTargetFilter` field to both
restrictions; the direct selection-time behavior and protected snapshot were
left unchanged.

Natural source proof covers On Play restriction, the When Digivolving
restriction added in `67dfc610`, both named alternate costs, rejection of a
stacked target, and both Jamming projections. BT18-022 and BT18-026 provide
the legal Hybrid/Ice-Snow stack peers. Shared Restrict target selection and
duration handling were inspected. No executable seam remains.

### BT18-026 — DaiPenmon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR maps the Hand/Main action to an own blue/red Tamer, requires one
Kumamon and one Korikakumon from trash as separate placement costs, places
them under the selected Tamer, then performs a hand Digivolve for exactly 3
while ignoring requirements. It separately carries Ice Clad, the
When Digivolving no-evolution-card deletion, inherited +2000 DP, Ice-Snow,
and the level-4 Hybrid alternate route. Coverage is full, residual-free, and
registration is exclusive. The generated snapshot matches the direct
placement, cost, and trigger structure.

The hand Main source uses natural `activateEffect` for both the successful
placement/evolution and the missing-Korikakumon refusal; direct When
Digivolving evidence was removed as redundant. The source also naturally
evolves from a Hybrid to delete an opposing stackless Digimon. The legal trash
stack, alternate evolution, and inherited DP proof are preserved. BT18-022,
BT18-025, and BT18-089 provide the Kumamon/Korikakumon, Hybrid, and Tamer
peers. Shared hand activation, atomic multi-card placement, requirement
bypass, and deletion targeting were inspected. No executable seam remains.

### BT18-027 — Mermaimon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR has an optional When Attacking `PlayWithoutCost` from
digivolution cards. Its filter is an own blue level-3 Digimon OR an own Tamer
of any color, and payment is explicitly false. It is full, residual-free, and
exclusively registered. The generated snapshot combines kind/color/level
fields in a stale shape that does not express the direct any-color Tamer
alternative; the protected snapshot was not edited.

Natural source proof plays both legal source alternatives from a real attack,
asserts memory remains unchanged after the free play in `151421ed`, rejects a
non-blue level-3 near match, checks optional decline, and evolves onto a legal
BT18-024 stack. BT18-023 and BT18-028 provide source-stack play peers. Shared
free-play source ownership, target OR filtering, and optional attack
resolution were inspected. No executable seam remains.

### BT18-028 — AncientMegatheriummon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR maps both On Play and When Digivolving to bottom-two trash of
all opposing stacks followed by a live Restrict over opposing stackless
Digimon. Its inherited all-turns leave replacement plays one qualifying
level-4-or-lower Digimon from its own stack for free, and its Rule trait is
Ice-Snow. The Kumamon/Korikakumon DigiXros requirement is exact. Coverage is
full, residual-free, and registration is exclusive. The generated snapshot
omits the direct `whileMatchesTargetFilter` detail; it was retained read-only.

Natural source proof covers On Play bottom trash and the Q2952 live restriction
boundary, When Digivolving bottom trash/restriction added in `eb5819b`, natural
leave replacement play from the own stack, DigiXros with distinct materials,
and duplicate-material rejection. BT18-022, BT18-025, and BT18-029 provide
bottom-stack, restriction, and leave-replacement peers. Shared bottom-stack
indexing, live Restrict filtering, own-stack play, and DigiXros slot identity
were inspected. No executable seam remains.

### BT18-029 — AncientMermaimon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR maps On Play and When Digivolving return plus level-ceiling
scaling from other own Digimon. Its all-turns leave replacement is a modal
hand return or own-stack free play, with the blue level-4-or-lower filter and
`fromOwnDigivolutionStack: true`; its Lanamon/Calmaramon DigiXros requirement

Natural source proof covers On Play scaling, both modal leave choices through

### BT18-030 — Candlemon — 8/10 provisional

The direct IR maps On Play RevealAdd to three cards, one yellow Data and one
Witchelny hand add, with the remainder bottomed. Its inherited all-turns
once-per-turn replacement is limited to opponent-effect leave events on an
own yellow Data/Witchelny Digimon and pays by trashing the top security card;

registration is exclusive. The generated snapshot matches the direct shape.

Natural source proof covers both reveal filters and deck-bottom remainder,
natural opponent BT18-008 deletion protection added in `be5fbd8`, once-per-turn
consumption, and the controller-effect negative. BT18-023 supplies a reveal
peer and shared leave-prevention/security-cost semantics were inspected. Q2953
