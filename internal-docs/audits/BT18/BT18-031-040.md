## Scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — catalog fidelity and applicable local KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — complete compiled-IR mapping, trigger/action/requirement trace, and exclusive registration.
3. **Behavioral proof** — source proof through feasible natural originating events; injected timing/event-bus calls are supplemental only.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — useful peer/runtime comparison and legal stack or targeting coverage.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at 0/2 by the task boundary above.

## Card findings and proof

### BT18-031 Neemon

- Catalog text is represented: reveal the top three, add one Hybrid/Ten Warriors card and one yellow Tamer with inherited effects, bottom the remainder, then once-per-turn memory on the controller's inherited-effect Tamer play.
- The current KB ruling is Q2954: the reveal must add as many qualifying cards as possible, subject to the one-card cap per category. The compiled IR carries the two independent category selections and natural bottom-deck resolution.
- Source proof covers positive and negative category matching, duplicate Hybrid candidates proving only one card is added per category, own-versus-opponent controller scope, and once-per-turn inherited-Tamer memory. The duplicate-candidate proof was added in `be1e4b007`.
- Peer/stack review compared the reveal/category selectors with BT18 Hybrid/Ten Warriors search effects and legal yellow inherited-effect Tamer fixtures, including both positive inherited-effect Tamers and a plain yellow Tamer negative control. No unresolved card-specific seam remains.

### BT18-032 Luxmon

- Catalog text is represented: `[Your Turn] [Once Per Turn]` watches another Angel/Archangel/Three Great Angels Digimon being played, and the inherited attack clause reduces one opposing Digimon by 2000 for the turn.
- The IR uses a your-turn watcher, excludes the source itself, matches the three catalog traits, and carries the inherited once-per-turn opponent-Digimon modifier.
- Source proof covers matching and nonmatching plays, the self-exclusion, first-trigger-only behavior, opponent-turn non-trigger behavior, and the inherited attack target/duration. The opponent-turn natural-play proof was added in `f547f1643`.
- Legal peer stacks include Angel/Archangel/Three Great Angels Digimon played into the battle area and a level-3 Luxmon host; the shared watcher and once-per-turn tracker paths match neighboring compiled cards. No unresolved card-specific seam remains.

### BT18-033 Patamon

- Catalog text is represented: from hand at Main, if breeding is empty, return one Three Great Angels Digimon card from trash to the bottom of the deck and play Patamon in breeding without paying its cost.
- The IR is full coverage, hand-only Main, requires an empty breeding area, requires the exact Three Great Angels return cost, performs the bottom-deck cost before `PlayWithoutCost`, and sets the breeding destination.
- Source proof covers the natural hand activation, exact trash-card cost, empty breeding placement, occupied-breeding rejection, missing-cost rejection, and optional decline branch. The compiled object was exported for direct IR assertions in `a1c1f3fa8`.
- Legal stack proof uses Patamon as a loose hand card, a Three Great Angels card in trash, and both empty and occupied breeding states; the peer comparison follows the same hand-activation and `PlayWithoutCost` interpreter path. No unresolved card-specific seam remains.

### BT18-034 Lucemon

- Catalog text is represented: legal Cupimon evolution, Start of Main/On Play hand trash, opponent may trash top security, Recovery +1 when the opponent did not trash, and once-per-turn End of Turn Chaos Mode evolution from trash by placing a level-6 Digimon on top of security.
- The IR preserves the equivalent Start-of-Main and On-Play clauses as two separately typed effects, with the mandatory hand cost, opponent optional security action, `lastEffectDidNotAct` recovery condition, and the alternate Chaos Mode route with the required level-6 security cost.
- KB rulings Q2956 and Q4999 are preserved: “if this effect didn't trash” means the opponent declined or could not trash, and BT7-111 is excluded from the Chaos Mode route. Source proof now reaches On Play through actual play, Start of Main and End of Turn through natural turn progression, and retains the positive/negative recovery and BT7-111 exclusion checks. The natural timing proof was added in `b2f08c198`.
- Legal peer/stack proof uses Cupimon as the named level-3 route, a level-6 security card for Chaos Mode, and the explicit BT7-111 negative case. No unresolved card-specific seam remains.
