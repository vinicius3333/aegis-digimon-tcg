## Scope and evidence

This audit covers exactly BT18-061 through BT18-070 in ascending card-ID order. The catalog source was verified against immutable blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; the local rules KB, direct TypeScript modules, generated IR (read-only), shared interpreter, neighboring cards, legal evolution/DigiXros stacks, and behavior-driving test source were inspected.

BT17 static coverage is recorded, and this range is now reviewed and chronologically integrated after BT18-051–060. The report still makes no collection-completion claim.

Every audited module registers executable behavior exactly once through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and has no `registerCard` registration. All ten direct modules report `coverage: "full"` and `residual: []`.

The requested score categories are fixed as follows: Catalog/rules, IR trace, Behavioral proof, and Peer and stack proof are each scored out of 2; Executed delivery gates is scored out of 2 and remains `0/2` because tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check` were explicitly prohibited. Consequently every score below is a provisional `8/10`, not a 10/10 delivery claim.

### BT18-061 Trailmon

- Catalog/rules: Reveal exactly three; independently accept a Tamer or black level-4-or-lower card as bottom material; return the rest top or bottom; at opponent-turn end play one stack Tamer for free once per turn; inherited Machine-only Collision on your turn.
- Direct IR: `RevealAdd` uses an `orFilters` union for Tamer versus black level cap; the end-turn action is stack-only and once-per-turn; inherited Collision is an `Aura` guarded by `selfHasTrait Machine`.
- Behavior source: existing tests cover both union branches, refusal, stack ownership, once-per-turn behavior, and inherited Collision; an added test drives the opponent turn through `advance.runTurn(1)`.
- KB: no BT18-061-specific entry. Stack and inherited behavior were compared with neighboring BT18 cards using the same interpreter primitives.

### BT18-062 Gladimon

- Catalog/rules: On Play and When Digivolving trash one hand card with Knightmon in its text to protect one of your Digimon from opponent-effect deletion through the opponent turn; inherited +1000 DP all turns.
- Direct IR: both trigger clauses use `Restrict` with `byOpponentEffectsOnly`, duration `untilOpponentTurnEnd`, optional payment, and the inherited `ModifyDP` is host-scoped.
- Behavior source: existing tests cover payment, refusal, unavailable payment, both trigger types, protected deletion, and host-only DP; an added natural completed opponent-turn test proves expiration.
- KB: no BT18-062-specific entry. Neighboring restriction/duration cards and legal black stacks were checked.

### BT18-063 Beetlemon

- Catalog/rules: J.P. alternate evolution cost 2 and MetalKabuterimon cost 0; opponent-effect deletion protection on evolution; attack trigger evolves one of your Digimon or Tamers from hand into a black/yellow Hybrid for one less; inherited leave replacement plays an inherited-effect Tamer from its own stack.
- Direct IR: alternate requirements, `Restrict`, attack `Digivolve` with `reduceCost: 1`, and the `wouldLeavePlay` replacement all match the printed clauses.
- Behavior source: existing tests cover both alternate routes, protection, Tamer evolution and draw timing, inherited stack ownership, and own-effect versus opponent-effect leave; the attack proof now uses a natural attack intent instead of injecting `OnUseAttack`.
- KB: Q2993–Q2995 and Q6633–Q6636 cover Tamer evolution, draw timing, attack eligibility, stack identity, and inherited effects.

### BT18-064 Mercurymon

- Direct IR: both restrictions share the correct opponent-only return restriction and duration; the alternate route and inherited host-scoped modifier are present.
- Behavior source: existing tests cover play, evolution, hand/deck return distinction, alternate evolution, and inherited DP; an added natural opponent-turn test proves the return restriction expires.
- KB: Q2996–Q2998 and Q6637–Q6639 cover the corresponding return/evolution and inherited-effect rulings.

### BT18-065 Snatchmon

- Catalog/rules: no-non-Vemmon condition unlocks trash DigiXros material; up to two Vemmon from trash become bottom materials on evolution; at four or more sources, optional end-of-your-turn evolution from hand; DigiXros four Vemmon; inherited Vemmon-bottom-deck return unsuspends and grants Blocker once per turn.
- Direct IR: hand-fixed IR retains the trash condition, DigiXros requirement, bottom placement, four-source threshold, hand-only destination, and event-driven inherited watcher.
- Behavior source: existing tests cover DigiXros legality, no-non-Vemmon gate, trash placement/refusal/opponent exclusion, threshold and hand-only evolution, and inherited once-per-turn behavior; all end-turn cases now use `advance.runTurn(0)`.
- KB: Q6014 covers the no-other-Digimon condition; Q6931 covers DigiXros material placement and delayed triggers.

### BT18-068 Wisemon

- Catalog/rules: Blocker; on play/evolution reveal five from either player's deck and return the revealed cards to the chosen deck's top or bottom.
- Direct IR: corrected both `RevealAdd` actions to `controller: "any"` with `deckTopOrBottom`, preserving the required destination choice. The generated bare `Reveal` form was read-only evidence and did not provide the return choice.
- Behavior source: existing test covers own-deck top return and Blocker; added source proof drives the opponent-deck choice and top return through real decisions.
- KB: no BT18-068-specific entry. RevealAdd controller/destination semantics and neighboring reveal cards were checked.

### BT18-069 Knightmon

- Catalog/rules: Blocker; at opponent-turn end optionally choose an opponent Digimon and force it to attack its player once per turn; inherited all-turns +2000 DP for your Digimon with Knightmon in their text.
- Direct IR: structured `Attack` carries a mandatory forced attack with `attackPlayer: true`; inherited `ModifyDP` uses a live text predicate and all-turns duration.
- Behavior source: added natural opponent-turn proof that the selected Digimon attacks; added host-only inherited DP proof. A shared matcher correction makes bracketed `[Knightmon]` references in inherited text resolve wherever they occur, not only at an inherited-text line header.
- KB: Q3005–Q3008 cover target choice, already-attacking cases, and the meaning of “must attack.” BT13-077 is the direct forced-attack peer.

### BT18-070 RhinoKabuterimon

- Catalog/rules: black/yellow level-4 Hybrid alternate evolution cost 3; hand Main effect requires one Beetlemon and one MetalKabuterimon from trash under a black/yellow Tamer, then digivolves that Tamer for cost 3 ignoring requirements; Collision; attack-target-switch unsuspension once per turn; inherited once-per-turn -4000 DP to one opposing Digimon when attacking.
- Direct IR: hand-fixed `DigivolveViaPlacement` retains the Tamer host filter, trash source, bottom placement, cost 3, and ignored requirements. Added `requiredNamesExact: ["Beetlemon", "MetalKabuterimon"]` prevents two same-name materials from satisfying the cost.
- Behavior source: existing positive Main-effect proof now asserts the exact material trace and Collision; added negative proof for two Beetlemon cards and inherited -4000 DP behavior from a natural attack.
- KB: Q3009–Q3013 and Q6646–Q6648 cover hand Main timing, both-required materials, Tamer evolution, and inherited effects.
