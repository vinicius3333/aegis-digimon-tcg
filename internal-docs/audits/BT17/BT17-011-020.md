# BT17-011 through BT17-020 audit

## Scope and provenance

This report covers exactly BT17-011 through BT17-020, audited one card at a time in strict ascending ID order. The catalog source was the immutable catalog blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; rules decisions were checked against the current local knowledge base. BT16 static coverage and chronological integration are recorded; BT16 remains open only for deferred gates and recorded limitations. This is a range audit only, and this report makes no BT17 integration or collection-completion claim.

The audit inspected each catalog record, local KB entries, direct TypeScript module, compiled IR, shared interpreter/runtime path, relevant peer cards and legal stacks, and behavior-driving source proof. Existing structural tests were retained where useful, but behavioral credit below is based on natural originating events and natural stacks added or already present in the focused tests; no manual event-bus/timing injection was used as a substitute for a playable event.

## Card findings and evidence

### BT17-011 Agunimon — 8/10 provisional

- Catalog/rules: Red/Purple level 4, alternate evolution from Takuya Kanbara for 2 or BurningGreymon for 1, hand evolution onto a red Tamer as though it were a level 3 red Digimon, AncientGreymon follow-up for 3 when its stated condition is met, end-of-turn self-deletion, and inherited +2000 DP during the controller's turn. Relevant KB entries were Q2723-Q2731, Q4657, and Q6554-Q6555, including Tamer-as-Digimon stack behavior and the rule that a follow-up AncientGreymon evolution at end of turn does not retroactively receive the delayed self-deletion.
- IR trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-011.ts` is a full compiled-IR module with empty residual coverage and exclusive `registerIrCard("BT17-011", compiled)` registration.
- Behavioral proof: `apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-011.test.ts` naturally evolves from BT12-088 Takuya onto Agunimon and naturally chains BurningGreymon into Agunimon and then AncientGreymon, resolving the end-of-turn deletion through a real turn window.
- Peer and stack proof: the Tamer-onto evolution uses a legal red Tamer source and the AncientGreymon follow-up uses an actual BurningGreymon source, exercising the same source-stack and delayed-effect paths used by the neighboring Hybrid cards.
- Correction/unresolved seams: no implementation correction was required; focused natural proof was added. No card-specific unresolved seam remains.
- Atomic commit: `4cfa303c4` (`Prove BT17-011 evolution behavior`).
