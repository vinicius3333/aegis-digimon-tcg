## Immutable catalog and KB trace

- **BT17-031 — Renamon:** Yellow level 3, play cost 3, 1000 DP. On Play
  reveals three, adds one Kyubimon/Taomon/Sakuyamon-name Digimon and one Rika
  Nonaka or Option cost 2 or more, and bottoms the rest. Its inherited Your
  Turn once-per-turn clause gives one opposing Digimon Security Attack -1
  after using an Option with cost 2 or more. Q2778–Q2781 and Q5453 confirm
  post-Main timing, reject Security/Delay activation, distinguish reduced use
  cost from reduced payment, and allow free use while preserving original use
  cost.
- **BT17-032 — Kyubimon:** Yellow level 4, play cost 5, 5000 DP. When
  Digivolving, it may play Rika Nonaka from hand without paying when no Rika is
  present; its inherited clause is the same Option-use trigger as Renamon.
  Q2782–Q2783 and Q5454–Q5456 confirm the same timing, activation, cost, and
  free-use boundaries.
- **BT17-033 — GeoGreymon:** Yellow level 4, play cost 5, 5000 DP. When
  attacking, it may suspend one own yellow Tamer for +3000 DP for the turn;
  inherited Your Turn gives all opposing security Digimon -3000 DP. No
  card-specific KB entry was returned.
- **BT17-034 — Bulkmon:** Yellow/Green level 4, play cost 5, 5000 DP, with
  Pulsemon alternate evolution. Its When Digivolving clause uses the mutually
  exclusive-looking but exact-three-inclusive security branches: at least 3
  security gives one opposing Digimon -3000, while 3 or fewer suspends one.
  Its inherited text gives +1000 while its text contains Pulsemon, and its All
  Turns once-per-turn clause recovers when a card is trashed from security with
  Leon Alexander in the stack. Q2784 confirms that exactly 3 activates both
  When Digivolving branches.
- **BT17-035 — Taomon:** Yellow level 5, play cost 7, 7000 DP, Barrier. When
  Digivolving it may use from hand one Plug-In-name or yellow Option with
  payment reduced by 2; its inherited once-per-turn When Attacking clause adds
  the Sakuyamon-name condition. Q2785 confirms that the effect does not waive
  Option color requirements.
- **BT17-036 — Boutmon:** Yellow/Green level 5, play cost 7, 7000 DP, with
  Pulsemon alternate evolution. It has an opponent-effect leave replacement
  that trashes the top security card, an All Turns effect-only security-trash
  watcher that may free-digivolve into a hand Digimon whose text contains
  Pulsemon when Leon is in the stack, and inherited End of Attack security
  trash to unsuspend when the text condition holds. Q2786 defines “would leave
  the battle area” as trash, hand/deck, security, breeding, or under-card
  movement.
- **BT17-037 — RizeGreymon:** Yellow level 5, play cost 7, 7000 DP. During its
  turn, a suspended own Tamer gives +3000 DP and Piercing; its When Digivolving
  and When Attacking clauses suspend a yellow Tamer to give one opposing
  Digimon -3000 for the turn. Its inherited once-per-turn trigger places one
  Marcus Damon from trash face-down on top of security after a red or yellow
  Tamer is deleted. Q2787 confirms that the deleted Marcus card itself may be
  placed.
- **BT17-038 — Sakuyamon:** Yellow level 6, play cost 11, 11000 DP, with
  Sakuyamon: Maid Mode alternate evolution and Barrier. When Digivolving it
  gives one opposing Digimon -6000, then may use a Plug-In-name Option or a
  yellow Option costing 5 or less from hand without paying. During its turn,
  once per turn, use of an Option costing 2 or more prevents opponent-effect
  return to hand or deck through the opponent's turn. Q2788–Q2790 and
  Q5457–Q5458 confirm post-Main timing, no trigger from Security/Delay, and
  original-use-cost semantics including free use.
- **BT17-039 — ShineGreymon:** Yellow level 6, play cost 11, 11000 DP. When
  Digivolving it may play Marcus Damon from hand without paying. Its All Turns
  once-per-turn replacement prevents opponent-effect leave by returning one
  own yellow Tamer. Q2791 enumerates trash, hand/deck, security, breeding, and
  under-card movement as leave events.
- **BT17-040 — Kazuchimon:** Yellow/Green level 6, play cost 12, 12000 DP,
  with Pulsemon alternate evolution. When Digivolving it suspends one opposing
  Digimon and, with Leon in the stack, gives all opposing Digimon Security
  Attack -1 through the opponent's turn. At End of Your Turn, at security 3 or
  more it gives one opposing Digimon -6000; at 3 or fewer it recovers one;
  then one own Digimon may attack an opposing Digimon. Its inherited
  Fenriloogamon-name security-removal watcher gives one opposing Digimon -8000.
  Q2792 confirms that newly played opposing Digimon receive the Leon aura;
  Q2793 confirms both branches at exactly 3; Q2794 confirms that the post-Then
  attack remains available after Recovery changes the security count.
