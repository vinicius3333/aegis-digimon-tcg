`registerIrCard("BT17-NNN", compiled)` registration and no duplicate

## Per-card findings and proof

### BT17-041 — ShineGreymon: Burst Mode

Catalog facts are Yellow level 7, play cost 8, 15000 DP, with the Yellow level
6 route and alternate `[ShineGreymon]: Cost 4`. The direct IR carries the
Blast Digivolve marker, On Play/When Digivolving free Tamer play followed by
per-Tamer -5000 DP, and When Attacking Security Attack scaling from up to two
yellow Tamers suspended by the cost. Natural source proof covers the play
sequence and attack suspension/scaling. No unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-042 — Argomon

Catalog facts are Green/Purple level 3, play cost 3, 2000 DP, with the
Green/Purple level 2 route and alternate `[Argomon]: Cost 0`. The IR reveals
three, independently adds an Argomon and a Rhythm when available, bottoms the
rest, and carries inherited On Deletion memory gain. Natural play proof uses
three known deck cards and observes both additions plus the bottomed card;
Q2795/Q2796 cover the independent and “as many as possible” selections. No
unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-043 — Terriermon

Catalog facts are Green level 3, play cost 3, 1000 DP, with Green level 2
evolution at 0. The Your Turn once-per-turn watcher is scoped to an effect-
played own Terriermon/Lopmon or green Tamer, and the inherited aura gives the
host +1000 DP while suspended. Natural proof uses BT17-049's actual
When Digivolving free Terriermon play to trigger the watcher, and a real host
attack to activate the aura; manual `fireSubTrigger` injection was removed.
Q2797 confirms effect-play timing. No unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-044 — Morphomon

Catalog facts are Green level 3, play cost 3, 1000 DP, with Green level 2
evolution at 0. The IR reduces a legal Eosmon digivolution by 1 during the
owner's turn and carries the inherited once-per-turn watcher that may evolve
into a hand Eosmon for 3 less when another Eosmon is played. Natural proof
plays one Eosmon and observes the second Eosmon evolve onto Morphomon through
the real watcher. Q2798 was applied to the requirement-preserving evolution.
No unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-045 — Argomon

Catalog facts are Green/Purple level 4, play cost 5, 5000 DP, with the
Green/Purple level 3 route and alternate `[Argomon]: Cost 2`. The IR correctly
checks that no Rhythm is present before free-playing one from hand on
digivolution and carries inherited On Deletion memory gain. Existing natural
BT17-042-to-BT17-045 evolution proof observes free Rhythm play and natural
inherited deletion memory. No unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-046 — Gargomon

Catalog facts are Green level 4, play cost 6, 6000 DP, with Green level 3
evolution at 2. The IR free-plays one own Terriermon from trash on deletion
and carries the inherited +1000 suspended aura. Natural proof deletes
Gargomon in battle and observes the Terriermon recovery, then attacks with a
host carrying BT17-046 to observe the inherited DP increase. No unresolved
seam or correction.

### BT17-047 — Parrotmon

Catalog facts are Green level 5, play cost 7, 7000 DP, with Green level 4
evolution at 3. The IR plays itself from security when its owner has no
Digimon, suspends an opposing Digimon on play/digivolution, and carries the
once-per-turn inherited battle-delete unsuspension. Natural proof covers
security play and On Play suspension; the inherited proof now uses a real
attack that deletes the opposing Digimon instead of manual event injection.
No unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-048 — Argomon

Catalog facts are Green/Purple level 5, play cost 8, 7000 DP, with
Green/Purple level 4 evolution at 5 and alternate level 4 `[Argomon]: Cost 4`.
The audit found that the prior Static `CostModifier` used an unsupported
`amount: { kind: "perSuspendedTamer", max: 5 }` shape and therefore could not
execute. It also omitted the alternate evolution requirement in the direct
module.

The correction replaces that inert shape with a nested `wouldDigivolve`
reducer carrying a payable up-to-five-Tamer suspension cost and adds the
alternate requirement. Registration collects this verified hand-resident
self-reducer, skips its marker after the card enters play, and the reducer
counts eligible unsuspended Tamers before paying and awards one reduction per
actually suspended Tamer. Natural proof covers two-Tamer reduced evolution,
post-deletion four-Argomon counting for free level 6 play, and inherited
Rhythm-paid host unsuspension. Q2799/Q2800/Q2808 were applied. No unresolved
non-gate seam remains.

### BT17-049 — Antylamon

Catalog facts are Green/Purple level 5, play cost 8, 8000 DP, with
Green/Purple level 4 evolution at 4 and alternate `[Turuiemon]/[Wendigomon]:
Cost 3`. The IR carries Alliance, free-play of one own level 3 Green/Yellow
Digimon from trash on digivolution, and inherited once-per-turn deletion of
another suspended own Digimon to free-play a level 3 Beast from trash. Natural
proof covers the named alternate evolution and free Terriermon play, plus a
real attack that deletes and replays the suspended Beast. Q2801/Q2802 were
applied. No unresolved seam or correction.

### BT17-050 — Parasitemon

Catalog facts are Green level 6, play cost 9, 4000 DP, with Green level 5
evolution at 3. The IR exposes the `[Hand][Main]` four-memory modal: place
this card under an own level 5+ Digimon, suspend an opposing Digimon, and
attack an opposing Digimon with the bound host; the alternate modal is an
empty choice. It also carries the End of Attack placement and inherited
opponent-effect deletion replacement plus Your Turn +3000 DP.

Natural proof activates the hand effect through the public `activateEffect`
intent, observes the four-memory payment, placement, host attack, and
inherited +3000 DP. Q2803/Q2804/Q2805 were applied. No unresolved seam or
correction.
