# BT17-071 through BT17-080 audit

## Scope and provenance

This report covers exactly BT17-071 through BT17-080, audited one card at a time in strict ascending ID order. The catalog source was the immutable catalog blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; rules decisions were checked against the current local knowledge base. This is a range audit only, and this report makes no collection-completion claim and does not mark the Orca worktree completed.

For every card, the audit inspected the catalog/rules record, local KB, direct TypeScript module, compiled IR, shared interpreter/runtime path, useful peer cards and legal stacks, and behavior-driving source proof. Structural assertions were retained where useful, but behavioral credit is based on natural originating events and natural legal stacks; manual event-bus or timing injection is not used as a substitute in the final BT17-071 through BT17-080 proofs.

## Card findings and evidence

### BT17-071 Murmukusmon — 8/10 provisional

- Catalog/rules: Purple level 6, play cost 12, 12000 DP, evolution from Purple/Yellow level 5 for 4, and Virus/Mega/Demon Lord traits. Its evolution trigger recognizes a stack containing both [Darcmon] and [HippoGryphonmon], then may delete another own Digimon to play BT17-072 Ornismon from trash without paying; its all-turns trigger once per turn deletes an opposing Digimon whose level is no greater than the deleted own Digimon. KB Q2837 confirms that the player may decline the optional trash play after paying the deletion cost.
- IR trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-071.ts` is full compiled IR with empty residual coverage and exclusive `registerIrCard("BT17-071", compiled)` registration.
- Behavioral proof: `apps/api/src/cards/BT17/BT17-071.test.ts` naturally evolves a BT17-063 Darcmon host carrying BT17-066 HippoGryphonmon, naturally rejects a Darcmon-only stack, and naturally exercises battle deletion, the level bound, and the once-per-turn limit.
- Peer and stack proof: the positive case uses the legal named two-card stack, while the negative case isolates the missing HippoGryphonmon requirement. The deletion proof uses a natural battle-originating deletion and the shared last-deleted-level path.
- Correction/unresolved seams: the original `nameOrTrait` token matcher was an OR and could accept either stack card; it now uses an `allOf` containing exact Darcmon and HippoGryphonmon stack clauses. No card-specific unresolved seam remains.
- Atomic commit: `4e0bb4f1a9beb140513c6b466daf2255f633bee5` (`Fix BT17-071 stack-name gate`).

## Corrections, registrations, protected files, and history disclosure

Two audited card implementations required scoped corrections:

1. BT17-071 now requires both named stack cards through an `allOf` matcher instead of accepting either name through an OR-shaped token filter.
2. BT17-074 now restricts inherited attack redirection to `unsuspended: true` Eosmon targets and exports its compiled value for direct source proof.

BT17-075 and BT17-076 already contained the baseline scaling corrections needed to count the intended Tamer population; those direct modules were inspected and preserved, with this dispatch adding natural behavioral proof. The remaining cards were proof-only changes in this dispatch. All ten direct modules use exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration; no duplicate `registerCard` was introduced or preserved. `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`, `BT17-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, and collection ledgers were not modified.

History was kept exactly as it existed after correction; no further amend, rebase, reset, or force push was used. For transparency, after the original BT17-080 commit `7b52a86320e75d01d680fb0ed5e8ca27c9fbd27d`, an accidental `git commit --amend` bundled a BT17-079 assertion change into the BT17-080 commit and created cross-card commit `951efba0b4ca8c276ea512e2c5747150708b67aa`. The mistake was then undone by the additive revert `ffe8efb8e9094accc22a3383cc6dc8c84f9e73dc`; BT17-079 was restored in additive commit `94efe312477dac568670371153e8fc153e188947`, and BT17-080 was restored in the separate additive commit `261b934a36552d26aa92152975dbdb8da8e8598a`. The final history therefore intentionally retains the transparent amend/revert sequence rather than rewriting it.
