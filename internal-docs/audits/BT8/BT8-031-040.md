## Result

This is a static, card-by-card audit of BT8-031 through BT8-040 in exact ascending order. The committed catalog, exactly one local KB query per card, applicable rules and rulings, errata and restriction data, direct TypeScript modules, generated effects snapshot, shared interpreter primitives, focused tests, same-mechanism peers, trait/name/color boundaries, and legal evolution stacks were inspected.

All ten direct modules use the required exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration and no direct module contains a second `registerCard` call. BT8-036 now explicitly marks its replacement source as `isSelfRef`, preventing the resident static replacement from reducing unrelated cards while preserving its verified self-reducer path. The existing direct BT8-040 optional hand-trash correction is present and was verified against the printed clause. Six focused test files were strengthened, including legal level-by-level evolution stacks, BT8-036 inherited-effect and unrelated-card boundaries, the BT8-038 non-Armor-Form trait boundary, and the BT8-040 Option-card color boundary. The direct modules BT8-031 through BT8-040 now carry no `@ts-nocheck` suppression.

The audit intentionally does not claim executable-gate completion. Focused tests, mechanism tests, collection tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI checks, and `git diff --check` were prohibited and remain deferred, so every card is scored at the provisional ceiling of 8/10.
