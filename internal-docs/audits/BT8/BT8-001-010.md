## Result

This is a static, card-by-card audit of BT8-001 through BT8-010 in exact ascending order. The committed catalog, exactly one local KB query per card, applicable rules, rulings, errata and restriction data, direct TypeScript modules, generated effects snapshot, interpreter primitives, focused tests, same-mechanism peers, trait/name/color boundaries, and legal evolution stacks were inspected.

All ten direct modules already use the required exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration and no direct module contains a duplicate `registerCard` call. Two known direct-vs-snapshot gaps were already corrected in the checked-in direct authority (BT8-005 placement provenance and BT8-006 own-deck milling); this audit added no new direct behavior correction. Eight focused test files were strengthened to replace impossible egg-to-level-4/5/6 fixtures with catalog-legal stacks, and BT8-004 gained an unsuspended-other-Digimon boundary case.

The audit intentionally does not claim executable-gate completion. Tests, mechanism tests, collection tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI checks, and `git diff --check` were prohibited and remain deferred, so every card is scored at the provisional ceiling of 8/10.

## Shared rules and interpreter evidence

- `data/kb/rules/manual.md:203-206` and `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:546-579` enforce the breeding-area boundary: effects that do not explicitly specify breeding cannot activate there, affect it, choose it, or reference it. `glossary.md:54-58` describes the relevant timing windows; `glossary.md:106` states that an inherited effect is activated by the Digimon that gains it.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:322-330` defines inherited text, `:660-663` defines cards under a Digimon as digivolution cards whose inherited effects are gained by the host, and `:367-370` defines multicolor cards as having all printed colors and not being treatable as lacking one.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2540-2608` and `data/kb/rules/manual.md:1492-1493` cover reveal processing and owner-selected return order. Revealed cards use their printed definitions for this search; a later treatment effect cannot activate while the card is only revealed, which is the Q1699 boundary.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:274-301` carries turn scope, frequency, inherited state, and conditions onto executable effects. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts:384-403` carries `[Your Turn]`/`[Opponent's Turn]` and Once Per Turn onto SubTrigger watchers; `:468-483` enforces the owner’s turn.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/builders.ts:86-110` applies the breeding observer guard, and `:151-168` makes a `WhenAttacking` effect self-scoped unless `attackScope: "ally"` is explicitly present. This is load-bearing for BT8-001, BT8-008, and BT8-010.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:150-157` implements `allYoursMatchFilter` over the owner’s battle-area permanents; `:242-263` implements opponent absence, security thresholds, and live condition evaluation. The Digimon kind filter excludes Tamers from BT8-004’s all-Digimon condition.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:231-246` resolves `onDiscardLibrary` against the milled deck owner, with `controller: "mine"` selecting the watcher’s own deck. Lines `:302-308` define effect-provenance gating for placement events; lines `:997-1036` preserve source identity and Once Per Turn scope.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:1607-1654` emits `onAddDigivolutionCards` with the receiving permanent as subject and effect provenance when applicable. Lines `:2408-2418` emit one `onDiscardLibrary` event per mill operation, while the separate `whenTrashedFromDeck` event is per card.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:108-121`, `:277-350`, and `:511-551` implement reveal counts, printed-definition selection, hand movement, and bottom/top remainder ordering. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:68-123` implements kind/color/multicolor predicates; `:272-328` distinguishes substring name matching from exact-name matching. No BT8 card in this range needs a name or trait selector; BT8-008’s red Tamer, BT8-009’s red multicolor, and BT8-010’s yellow Digimon predicates are the relevant color/kind boundaries.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/reducers.ts:63-89` explicitly allowlists BT8-010’s verified self reducer. Lines `:230-301` extract its nested conditional `wouldBePlayed` reduction, and `apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts:3504-3573` evaluates the reduction once in the pay-time window, proving the Q1700 single-reduction boundary.

None justified changing the direct BT8 modules.
