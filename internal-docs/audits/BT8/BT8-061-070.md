## Result

This is a static, card-by-card audit of BT8-061 through BT8-070 in exact ascending order. The committed catalog, exactly one local KB query per card, applicable rules and rulings, errata and restriction data, direct TypeScript module, generated effects snapshot, shared interpreter primitives, focused tests, same-mechanism peers, name/trait/color boundaries, and legal evolution stacks were inspected.

Three direct modules required proven corrections: BT8-065 now matches Mamemon by name only; BT8-066 now requires an effect-driven source-placement event; and BT8-069 now requires an effect-driven placement under one of your own Digimon. This is four direct IR field-level corrections across three cards. Six focused test files were strengthened with static shape assertions, legal evolution stacks, corrected color/trait/name boundaries, and an optional-refusal case.

All ten direct modules are suppression-free and typed against the shared compiled-card IR; this typing cleanup was semantically neutral and introduced no new behavior.

All ten direct modules have full coverage and empty residuals, each uses exactly one exclusive `registerIrCard("BT8-xxx", compiled)` registration, and none has a direct `registerCard` registration. The generated effects snapshot was inspected read-only and not edited. Tests, mechanism tests, collection tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI checks, and `git diff --check` were prohibited, so every card remains at the provisional 8/10 ceiling rather than executable-gate completion.

## Shared rules, interpreter, and peer evidence

- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:322-330` defines inherited-effect text; `:657-663` says stacked cards are digivolution cards and their inherited effects are gained by the host. `:366-371` says a multicolor card is treated as having all printed colors. These rules govern BT8-063, BT8-064, BT8-067, and BT8-070.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:540-582` enforces the breeding-area boundary: effects do not trigger, activate, choose, or reference breeding-area cards unless they explicitly say so. `data/kb/rules/glossary.md:52-60` defines When Digivolving and When Attacking timing, `:104-106` defines inherited activation, and `:148-155` defines Once Per Turn.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2559-2608` governs reveal processing and chooser-controlled order; `data/kb/rules/manual.md:1492-1494` likewise requires the activating player to choose the order for returned revealed cards. This is load-bearing for BT8-065 and BT8-068.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:274-301` carries trigger, inherited state, frequency, and condition into executable effects; `effect.ts:380-403` carries turn scope and Once Per Turn to SubTriggers; `effect.ts:468-483` enforces Your Turn and Opponent's Turn ownership.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:68-128` handles kind, printed color, multicolor, level, and cost predicates. `:272-337` separates name substring, exact name, exact trait, trait substring, and text matching. This proves the name-only BT8-065 correction, the Mamemon name boundaries on BT8-068, and the Alphamon name condition on BT8-069.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts:567-597` reads traits and colors from the host's digivolution stack; `:720-736` reads action receipts and named counts; `:899-915` implements the inherited Alphamon name-substring condition.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:248-308` gives `onAddDigivolutionCards` its dedicated effect-provenance gate; `:778-802` applies `triggerFilter` to the receiving permanent; and `:929-983` combines all watcher gates. This is why BT8-066 and BT8-069 need `byEffect: true`, and why BT8-069 needs a receiving-Digimon `triggerFilter`.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:1622-1654` places loose cards under a permanent and emits `onAddDigivolutionCards`, including `byEffectSeat` when the placement came from an effect. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:188-210`, `:242-299`, and `:359-410` implement revealed-card filtering, bounded count selection, play disposition, and remainder trashing. `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts:213-296` implements one combined `DeleteBudget` by printed play-cost total.

Representative peers were inspected: BT11-063 and BT15-035 for Rule-scoped name aliases; BT7-005 and LM-017 (`sourceFilter: { isSelfRef: true, byEffect: true }`) for effect-driven source placement; BT25-005 (`sourceFilter` plus `triggerFilter`) for “under one of your Digimon”; BT11-044 for reveal-and-play/trash scaling; BT8-063/064 for inherited conditional Blocker; and the dedicated `GrantCanAttackUnsuspended` and `DeleteBudget` interpreter paths for BT8-067/070.
