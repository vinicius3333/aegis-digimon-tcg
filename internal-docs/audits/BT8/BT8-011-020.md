## Scope and method

This audit covers BT8-011 through BT8-020 in exact ascending order. The committed catalog at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:63152-63425` was treated as the card-text and metadata source, each card received exactly one local query with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <card ID>`, and the direct TypeScript module under `apps/api/src/cards/BT8` was treated as executable authority.

The generated snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:116936-117148` was inspected read-only. Shared interpreter evidence included registration and normalization in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts`, inherited-effect collection in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/collect.ts:163`, effect dispatch/static keyword handling in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts`, deletion accounting in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts`, unsuspended attack permission in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/combat.ts`, attack legality and prohibition precedence in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/legality.ts`, Armor Purge replacement in `apps/api/src/engine/combat/controller.ts:1229-1250`, and DNA validation/selection in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/dna.ts`.
