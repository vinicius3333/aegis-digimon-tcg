## Shared evidence

The shared interpreter and primitives provide the load-bearing behavior used by
this range:

- Definition matching handles exact levels, colors, names, traits, text,
  attributes, card kinds, and exact color counts. The `colorCount: 2` gate is
  material for BT16-031 because `multicolor: true` also admits three-color
  cards.
- Normal and alternate evolution matching enforces the printed evolution
  requirements unless an effect explicitly says to ignore them. The BT16-040
  trash effect has no ignore-requirements flag, which is the Q2634 behavior.
- Effect-driven `Digivolve` resolves a controller-owned target, enumerates the
  requested source zone, filters by level/trait/kind, checks the target's
  normal or alternate evolution requirement, applies `reduceCost`, and pays the
  remaining cost.
- `RevealAdd` reveals the requested top cards, resolves each mandatory add slot
  against the remaining revealed cards, prevents one instance from satisfying
  two slots, and returns unclaimed cards to the requested deck position in the
  selected order.
- Inherited effects are collected from the live evolution stack. Aura
  predicates re-evaluate the current top card, suspension state, names, and
  text; sub-trigger frequency identities reset on actual turn transitions.
- Combat uses ordinary attack intents, then resolves inherited attack effects,
  battle outcomes, security checks, Reboot, and end-of-attack cleanup.

The local KB returned Q2629 for BT16-033, Q2630 for BT16-034, Q2631 for
BT16-036, Q2632–Q2633 for BT16-039, and Q2634/Q4254 for BT16-040. It returned
no card-specific entries for BT16-031, BT16-032, BT16-035, BT16-037, or
BT16-038.

## Per-card findings

### BT16-031 — Gatomon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog contract: Yellow/Purple level 4, play cost 4, 4000 DP, with the
  Salamon alternate evolution for 2; Barrier; On Play/When Digivolving returns
  one level-6-or-lower red or purple two-color Digimon card from trash to hand
  by trashing one hand card; inherited Your Turn opposing Security Digimon
  -3000 DP.
- Direct IR and registration were otherwise complete, but both recovery target
  filters used `multicolor: true` without requiring exactly two colors. This
  admitted an invalid three-color card. The production correction adds
  `colorCount: 2` to both recovery filters; the module remains exclusively
  registered with `registerIrCard`.
- Source-level proof now covers natural play and natural Salamon evolution,
  the hand-trash payment, exact two-color red/purple recovery, rejection of a
  three-color or single-color card, the level-7 boundary, and the inherited
  Security DP modifier.
- Shared matching, trash-to-hand movement, conditional hand cost, and live
  security-DP duration were traced through the interpreter. No unresolved
  catalog or rules ambiguity remains.
