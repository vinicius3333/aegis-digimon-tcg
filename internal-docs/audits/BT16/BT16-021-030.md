## Ordered findings and proof changes

### BT16-021 — Togemogumon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a blue/green level-4 ArmorForm Mammal with the Wormmon
alternate evolution, Blocker, Armor Purge, and an All Turns once-per-turn
trigger: when an opposing Digimon becomes suspended, trash its top evolution
card and then prevent an opposing no-source Digimon from attacking or blocking
for the turn. The card KB query returned no card-specific entry.

The original action used generic `Trash`, which could remove a loose card. The
production IR now uses `TrashDigivolution`, targets an opposing Digimon with
`digivolutionCards: "hasAny"`, and removes exactly one card. Shared top-stack
trashing, nested `Then`, and the restriction ledger provide the source-stack
behavior; BT16-019 and BT16-022 were useful inherited-trash peers. The test
uses a natural opposing attack to create the suspension event and asserts both
source-card trash and attack/block restriction. No other production gap was
found.

Commit: `7fce2b6d7` (`Correct BT16-021 source trashing`).

### BT16-024 — MagnaAngemon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a blue/yellow level-5 Archangel Ultimate with the
Angemon route. On its turn, On Play and When Digivolving search security for
Angel or Three Great Angels, may digivolve into a found card with a two-cost
reduction, shuffle security, and after a successful evolution may place an
Angel-family card from hand at security's bottom. Its inherited Opponent's
Turn effect grants Blocker to Angel, Archangel, and Three Great Angels.

KB Q2619 covers declining the evolution while still shuffling the full search;
Q2620 covers the post-evolution hand placement; Q2621 covers child timing after
the remaining search/shuffle work; and Q3747 covers simultaneous trigger
ordering. The original shared security Search interpreter prompted for a
subset even when the card required a full search. The production correction
adds `to: "revealed"` to the Search IR, adds it to both MagnaAngemon searches,
and makes this form select all matching security cards while preserving the
full set for `amongPreviousSearch`; ordinary subset Search behavior is
unchanged. The existing nested timing queue already defers child effects.
Peers included BT14-033, BT14-093, BT18-037, EX5-027, and P-122. Tests cover
full-reveal shape, natural inherited Blocker, and natural security search,
reduced-cost evolution, and remainder handling.

Commit: `c8fcb07b9` (`Correct BT16-024 security search`).

### BT16-028 — Imperialdramon: Dragon Mode — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a blue/green level-6 Ancient Dragon Mega with Paildramon
or Dinobeemon alternate evolution. Its evolution restricts one opposing
Digimon or Tamer from unsuspending for the turn, then suspends one opposing
Digimon or Tamer to unsuspend one own Digimon. All Turns watches an opponent's
Digimon played or digivolved by an effect and may Blast Digivolve into Fighter
Mode when its controller has a Tamer.

KB Q2623 covers simultaneous opponent-effect and watcher triggers; Q2624
requires an effect-play/effect-digivolution origin rather than an ordinary
effect that merely ignores requirements. The two cross-permanent watchers
incorrectly used `triggerEnteredByEffect`, which compares the event with the
watcher's seat and misses an opponent's effect entry. The production fix uses
`triggerPlayedOrDigivolvedByEffect` for both watchers, the shared predicate
designed for effect plays and effect digivolutions. Blast registry, nested
digivolution, Tamer gate, and trigger ordering were traced. Tests use natural
Dragon Mode evolution and natural opponent effect-play and effect-digivolution
origins before asserting the source's natural Blast Digivolve. No other gap was
found.

Commit: `c1df3709e` (`Correct BT16-028 effect provenance`).
