## Ordered findings and proof changes

### BT16-071 — MadLeomon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a purple/green level-4 Undead/Bagra Army Digimon with
Liollmon or Elecmon alternate evolution for two. Its When Attacking effect may
digivolve itself into a card with Leomon in its name from hand or trash, while
the inherited End of Attack effect may delete itself to play a level-4-or-lower
Digimon from trash without paying its cost. KB Q2658 confirms that this
effect-driven evolution does not ignore the destination's normal requirements.

The direct IR carries the Leomon name filter, both source zones, the alternate
evolution metadata, and the inherited self-delete cost with a level cap. The
shared `runDigivolve`, target legality, End of Attack dispatch, and
`PlayWithoutCost`/`deleteOwn` runtime paths were traced; the catalog and runtime
requirement rule remains intact. The test proof was strengthened from a direct
timing injection to a natural attack intent, then observes the inherited host's
deletion and trash play. Useful peers were BT16-071's Leomon evolution family,
BT16-074's inherited End of Attack security cost, and stack-aware inherited
effects around the BT16 purple line. No production correction was warranted.

Commit: `d9b4a9426` (`test: prove BT16-071 through a natural attack`).

### BT16-072 — Arukenimon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a purple level-5 Dark Animal with Blocker. On Play it
reveals five, trashes two purple cards, and bottoms the rest; on deletion it may
play a Myotismon-text Tamer from trash without the same name as any of its
Tamers. KB Q2659 confirms that two simultaneous copies can play only one copy of
the same-name Tamer: the first resolution changes the battle-area name set seen
by the second.

remainder, and deletion reaction with `notSameNameAs: ["battleArea"]`. Shared
reveal selection, definition color matching, deletion source matching, and
Tamer uniqueness were traced. Existing natural On Play proof was retained and a
production deletion-batch proof now asserts that two simultaneous reactions
cannot place two same-name Tamers. Useful peers were BT16-073 for the paired
Myotismon reaction and BT16-089 for the legal Myotismon-text Tamer stack. No
production correction was warranted.

### BT16-075 — Cerberusmon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a purple level-5 Dark Animal. On Play and When
Digivolving it may return one Digimon with Dark Animal or Shaman from trash to
hand. Its inherited Your Turn once-per-turn effect says that when an effect
plays one of your Digimon, one of your Digimon gains Rush for the turn. The
card-specific KB query returned no entry.

`playedByEffect` marker emitted by `PlayWithoutCost`/`playInstances`, so manual
plays do not incorrectly grant Rush. Existing natural On Play proof remains,
and the structural proof now records the provenance gate. Useful peers were
BT15-081, BT17-043, and EX5 effect-play watchers, plus BT16-068/BT16-076 for
the Dark Animal and SoC stack family.

Commit: `311199eb5` (`fix: gate BT16-075 Rush trigger to effect plays`).

### BT16-078 — Pharaohmon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a purple level-6 Wizard. On Play and When Digivolving it
deletes one level-4-or-lower Digimon without restricting controller. Its Your
Turn once-per-turn effect says that when an effect deletes another Digimon, it
may play a level-5-or-lower Undead or Dark Animal from trash without paying.
KB Q2665 confirms that the deletion target may be the owner's own low-level
Digimon; declining is not a legal substitute when an eligible target exists.

The direct IR already had the unrestricted level cap, the other-Digimon source
filter, and the free Undead/Dark Animal play. The source filter was missing the
runtime deletion-cause gate, so battle/rule deletions could incorrectly fire
the reaction. The correction adds `deleteCause: "byEffect"`; the shared
`onDeletionOf` matcher compares this with the deletion payload's
`removalCause`, while `excludeSelf` remains enforced. The existing natural
own-Digimon effect deletion test proves the reaction and remains compatible
with the correction. Useful peers were LM-016 and BT16-076 for effect-deletion
source gates and trash evolution/play paths.

Commit: `b83d10df2` (`fix: gate BT16-078 reaction to effect deletions`).

### BT16-080 — Shroudmon — provisional 8/10

The catalog identifies a purple/yellow level-6 Wizard with a level-5
Pulsemon-in-text alternate evolution for five. At When Digivolving and End of
Attack, once per turn across both timings, three or more security gives one

traces covered End of Attack attacker/defender identity, security conditions,

opponent AncientSphinxmon effect deletion for leave prevention, and the
existing natural battle-deletion recovery proof. Useful peers were BT16-023,
BT16-034, BT16-044, BT16-059, BT18-084, and BT19-043 for Pulsemon security
branches, effect-leave replacements, and repeated recovery. No production
