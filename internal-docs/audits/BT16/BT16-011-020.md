## Scope, evidence, and score

This is a static implementation audit of exactly ten cards. For each card, I
read the immutable catalog record, ran the local card-specific KB query, checked
errata/restriction data and applicable rules, inspected the direct TypeScript
module and its colocated source-level tests, traced the compiled IR through the
shared interpreter/runtime primitives, and compared useful mechanism and
evolution/source-stack peers. The direct card module is the executable
authority; the generated aggregate snapshot was read as secondary evidence and
was not edited.

The five components are catalog/rules, IR trace, behavioral proof, peer/stack
proof, and executed gates, each worth two points. The first four are static
`2/2` findings for every card. Executed gates are fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch
instructions: no tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser check, focused or
mechanism/collection gate, or `git diff --check` was to be run. Every result is
therefore provisional `8/10`; this report does not claim 10/10 or collection
completion.

## Catalog, KB, and runtime findings

The contiguous catalog records in the immutable blob identify the following
printed contracts:

- **BT16-011 Garudamon (X Antibody)** — red level 5, play cost 8, 8,000 DP;
  alternate `[Garudamon]: Cost 0`; optional red recovery on play/evolution,
  conditional deletion at or below this Digimon's DP when Garudamon or X
  Antibody is in the stack, a once-per-turn red-card-return Rush trigger, and
  inherited security-top trash on deletion. The local KB includes the current
  one-copy restriction (effective 2025-09-01) and Q2615 about a source
  condition disappearing before activation.
- **BT16-012 Silphymon** — red/yellow level 5, play cost 8, 8,000 DP; DNA red
  level 4 plus yellow level 4 for cost 0 with Partition; DNA evolution gives
  one opposing Digimon -7,000 DP through the opponent's turn; its evolution
  and attack effects delete one opposing Digimon at 4,000 DP or less; the
  inherited Partition clause returns the specified materials on a qualifying
  departure. The card query returned no card-specific KB entry.
- **BT16-013 Valkyrimon** — red/yellow level 6, play cost 7, 12,000 DP;
  `[Silphymon]: Cost 3`, Blast Digivolve, -5,000 DP to all opposing Digimon
  on play/evolution, and an all-turns once-per-turn security-removal trigger:
  delete an opposing Digimon at 8,000 DP or less, otherwise gain Security
  Attack +1 through the end of the turn. It is an ACE card with overflow 4.
  Q2606-Q2611 cover security-removal ownership, next-turn activation, the
  once-per-turn limit, Security Attack timing, deletion of an attacker before
  a later check, and a source leaving before trigger activation.
- **BT16-014 Goldramon (X Antibody)** — red/yellow level 6, play cost 13,
  13,000 DP; alternate `[Goldramon]: Cost 2`; Raid; its evolution and attack
  effects may use one God Flame or Four Great Dragons Option from hand without
  cost; all turns it gains effects of Goldramon cards in its stack. Q2612
  requires a newly gained Goldramon source's When Digivolving effect to
  activate, and Q2613 covers a source that returns an Option from trash.
- **BT16-015 Phoenixmon (X Antibody)** — red level 6, play cost 13, 13,000
  DP; alternate `[Phoenixmon]: Cost 2`; When Digivolving gains Blitz and its
  turn projection can attach End of Attack to all own On Deletion effects
  while Phoenixmon or X Antibody is in the stack. On deletion it may play an
  eligible red 11,000-DP-or-lower Avian/Bird/Beast/Animal/Sovereign Digimon
  from hand (excluding Sea Animal), then deletes an opposing Digimon at or
  below the played card's DP. Q2614 and Q2615 define the inherited-effect
  projection and live source-condition behavior.
- **BT16-016 Patamon** — blue/yellow level 3, play cost 3, 2,000 DP;
  alternate `[Tokomon]: Cost 0`; at the start of the turn or on play, if it
  is the player's turn, it may reduce the evolution cost of a level 4
  Angel/Free card in hand by 1; inherited When Attacking trashes the top
  opposing evolution card. Q2816 says the inherited source must evaluate the
  opposing stack as it exists at the trigger timing.
- **BT16-017 Veemon** — blue/red level 3, play cost 3, 2,000 DP; alternate
  `[DemiVeemon]: Cost 0`; once per turn on the player's turn, another own
  Digimon being played or evolved gives 1 memory when that resulting Digimon
  is Free or green; inherited Your Turn gives +2,000 DP. Q2616 says the
  condition references the Digimon after it evolves.
- **BT16-018 ExVeemon** — blue/red level 4, play cost 4, 4,000 DP; alternate
  `[Veemon]: Cost 2`; on play/evolution it selects one own Digimon that can't
  be deleted in battle through the opponent's turn; inherited Your Turn gives
  +2,000 DP. Q2617 confirms this protection applies to a battle against a
  Security Digimon.
- **BT16-019 Angemon** — blue/yellow level 4, play cost 4, 4,000 DP;
  alternate `[Patamon]: Cost 2`; Blocker; on play/evolution unsuspends one own
  level 4-or-lower Digimon; inherited When Attacking trashes the top opposing
  evolution card. The local KB query returned no card-specific entry.
- **BT16-020 GaoGamon** — blue level 4, play cost 5, 5,000 DP; alternate
  level-3 Night Claw/Light Fang for cost 2; on evolution both players draw 1,
  then it gains 1 memory if the opponent has at least eight cards in hand or

  deletion in battles against Security Digimon. Q2775 confirms both draws at

## Ordered per-card findings and proof changes

### BT16-011 — Garudamon (X Antibody) — provisional 8/10

The direct full IR already represented the optional red return, conditional
DP-bounded deletion, once-per-turn red return-to-Rush sub-trigger, and
inherited security trash. Two conditions incorrectly encoded `Garudamon` and
`X Antibody` as name tokens in one name matcher; the condition now has a
`Garudamon` exact-name entry and an `X Antibody` trait entry in both the play/
evolution action and its natural test assertion. The conditional deletion is
now mandatory after the optional return, as printed. Source tests cover natural
play, natural red evolution carrying the X Antibody source, the optional
decline path, the exact DP boundary, and a natural red Tamer return that
activates Rush. Peer evidence is BT13-014's inherited DP deletion and
BT15-013's red recovery/sub-trigger shape.

Commits: `594a36a67` (`Correct BT16-011 trigger semantics`),
`937070722` (`Clarify BT16-011 natural-play proof`).
