## Sources, registration, and runtime trace

`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` at the immutable catalog blob
above. The generated entries in `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`
were inspected read-only and were not modified. `BT16-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was
also preserved unchanged.

`registerIrCard("BT16-NNN", compiled)` registration and no duplicate

binding, restrictions, cost payment, evolution, attack, security, and turn

and observable state; no new manual event-bus or timing injection was used as
a substitute for a natural originating event.

DigiPolice, security-threshold, inherited-aura, redirect, and battle-deletion

## Per-card evidence

### BT16-041 — Stingmon

The catalog identifies a Green/Purple level 4, play cost 4, 4000 DP,
Free/Insectoid Digimon. Its normal evolution is from a Blue or Red level 3
for 3, with alternate `[Wormmon]` evolution for 2. The direct IR maps
Retaliation, On Play and When Digivolving suspension of one opposing
Digimon, and the inherited When Attacking/Once Per Turn suspension effect.

The colocated proof asserts the alternate evolution requirement, naturally
evolves from BT16-040 using the alternate cost, and naturally attacks with an
inherited BT16-041 source. Assertions cover the memory payment, source-card
transition, opponent target, and inherited suspension. No KB ambiguity or
production correction remains.

### BT16-042 — BladeKuwagamon

The catalog identifies a Green level 4, play cost 4, 4000 DP,
Virus/Machine/Insectoid Digimon with Green level 3 evolution for 2. The Rule
trait grants Insectoid. On Play and When Digivolving grant one own Digimon
+3000 DP through the opponent's turn, while the inherited All Turns effect
grants +1000 DP while the host is suspended.

The direct IR has the Rule trait, both +3000 triggers, and the suspended
inherited aura. Source proof naturally plays the card with an ally and
opponent to check controller/target scope, naturally evolves from BT16-041
to check the digivolution trigger and memory cost, and checks the inherited
bonus on a suspended stack. No KB ambiguity or production correction remains.

### BT16-043 — Runnermon

The catalog identifies a Green level 4, play cost 4, 4000 DP, Virus/Beast
Digimon with Green level 3 evolution for 2 and alternate `[Pulsemon]`
evolution for 2. On Play and When Digivolving, 3 or more security cards
suspend one opposing Digimon; 3 or fewer security cards gain 1 memory. The
inherited All Turns effect grants +1000 DP while the top card has `[Pulsemon]`
in its text.

Q2635 was applied to the exact-three boundary. The proof covers both natural
play branches, natural alternate evolution from BT16-039 at exactly three
security cards, and inherited positive/negative host cases. The direct IR
uses distinct security-at-least and security-at-most conditions and the
Pulsemon-text live aura. No production correction remains.

### BT16-044 — Pistmon

The catalog identifies a Green level 5, play cost 6, 6000 DP,
Virus/Vegetation Digimon with Green or Yellow level 4 evolution for 3 and
alternate level 4 with `[Pulsemon]` in its text for 3. On Play and When
Digivolving, 3 or more security cards suspend one opposing Digimon and keep
that card from unsuspending during the next opponent unsuspend phase; 3 or
fewer security cards gain 2 memory. The inherited End of Attack/Once Per Turn
effect checks Pulsemon text, trashes the top security card, and unsuspends
this Digimon.

Q2636 was applied to the exact-three boundary. The direct module binds the
suspended target so the restriction follows the same target, and the source
proof naturally digivolves into Pistmon and naturally attacks with BT16-047
under it, accepting the inherited cost and checking the resulting security
zone and unsuspension. No additional production correction was required in
this card's commit.

### BT16-045 — MetallifeKuwagamon

The catalog identifies a Green level 5, play cost 7, 7000 DP,
Virus/Insectoid Digimon with Green level 4 evolution for 3. On Play and When
Digivolving, it may suspend one Digimon, then one own Digimon gets +3000 DP
through the opponent's turn. Its inherited Opponent's Turn/Once Per Turn
effect may redirect an opponent Digimon attack to one suspended own Insectoid
host.

Q2637 was applied to the inherited redirect. The production gap was that both
the On Play and When Digivolving follow-up `ModifyDP` actions were marked
optional, allowing refusal of the printed mandatory “Then” clause. The
direct module now makes only the suspension optional; the +3000 follow-up is
mandatory when a legal own target exists. Source proof declines the
suspension and still observes the +3000 on play and digivolution, then
naturally redirects an opponent attack into a suspended Insectoid host and
checks battle deletion/security state.

### BT16-046 — GranKuwagamon

The catalog identifies a Green/Purple level 6, play cost 7, 12000 DP,
Free/Insectoid Digimon with Green or Purple level 5 evolution for 4 and
alternate `[Dinobeemon]` or level 5 Insectoid evolution for 3. Its On Play
and When Digivolving effect suspends two opposing Digimon or Tamers, prevents
those cards from unsuspending in the next opponent unsuspend phase, and then
deletes one of their suspended Tamers. During the owner's turn, once per
turn, when this Digimon becomes suspended, one own Digimon gains Security
Attack +1 for the turn.

Q2638 was applied to the Digimon/Tamer target union and delayed unsuspend
restriction. The production gap was a missing
`sourceFilter: { isSelfRef: true }` on the suspension watcher, which could
award Security Attack when an unrelated Digimon was suspended. The watcher
now scopes to GranKuwagamon itself. Proof naturally resolves the
When Digivolving Digimon/Tamer sequence and Tamer deletion, naturally attacks
with a host carrying GranKuwagamon to earn Security Attack, and naturally
plays BT16-041 to confirm an unrelated suspension does not award the bonus.

### BT16-047 — Achillesmon

The catalog identifies a Green level 6, play cost 11, 11000 DP,
Virus/Beastkin Digimon with Green level 5 evolution for 3 and alternate
level 5 with `[Pulsemon]` in its text for 3. When Digivolving, it suspends
one opposing Digimon and then prevents one of their Digimon from
unsuspending until end of turn. All Turns, when this Digimon deletes an
opponent Digimon in battle, 3 or more security cards trash the opponent's
top security card; 3 or fewer gain 2 memory.

Q2639 was applied to the exact-three boundary. The production gap was a
missing self source filter on the battle-deletion watcher, allowing another
own attacker to award Achillesmon's security/memory result. The direct
module now scopes the watcher to Achillesmon. Proof uses natural attacks by
Achillesmon at exactly three and above-three security, checks the
When Digivolving suspension/restriction, and has a negative natural attack by
another own Digimon on the same board.

### BT16-048 — TyrantKabuterimon

The catalog identifies a Green/Purple level 6, play cost 7, 12000 DP,
Free/Insectoid Digimon with Green or Purple level 5 evolution for 4 and an
alternate level 6 Insectoid evolution with play cost 13 or less for 2. Its
When Digivolving effect may play one Insectoid or Larva from hand with an
8-cost reduction. While suspended it is unaffected by opponent Digimon
effects. At the end of the owner's turn, once per turn, it may suspend one
other own Digimon and bottom-deck one opponent Digimon whose DP is no greater
than the suspended Digimon.

Q2640, with related Q4661/Q5001/Q5015 context, was applied to the suspended
immunity and opponent-effect interaction. The direct module already uses the
typed `PlayWithoutCost` payment shape (`payCost: true`, `reduceCostBy: 8`)
and the live suspended immunity. Proof replaces an earlier manual end-turn
timing injection with a natural turn run, checks the DP boundary and
bottom-deck result, naturally resolves the reduced-cost Insectoid play, and
naturally exercises the level-6 Insectoid alternate route. No additional
production correction was required in this card's commit.

### BT16-049 — Armadillomon

The catalog identifies a Black/Yellow level 3, play cost 3, 2000 DP,
Free/Mammal Digimon with Yellow or Blue level 2 evolution for 1 and
alternate `[Upamon]` evolution for 0. During the owner's turn, once per turn,
when one other own Digimon is played or digivolves, if it is Free or Yellow,
gain 1 memory. The inherited All Turns effect grants +1000 DP.

Q2641 was applied to the post-digivolution trigger subject. The direct IR
uses one shared Your Turn/Once Per Turn identity for both play and
digivolution subtriggers, excludes this source, and checks the Free-or-Yellow
condition. Proof naturally plays two qualifying cards in one turn to show
the once-per-turn limit, naturally evolves through the Upamon alternate route
to check the post-digivolution subject, rejects a non-Free/non-Yellow card,
and checks the inherited DP aura on a stack.

### BT16-050 — Commandramon

The catalog identifies a Black level 3, play cost 3, 1000 DP,
Virus/Cyborg/D-Brigade/DigiPolice Digimon with Black level 2 evolution for 0.
Both the main and inherited All Turns effects grant +1000 DP to all other own
Digimon with the D-Brigade or DigiPolice trait.

There are no card-specific KB entries. The direct IR uses one live OR trait
filter, own-controller scope, Digimon kind, all-target count, and source
exclusion for both main and inherited effects. Source proof asserts the
immutable catalog identity, checks D-Brigade and DigiPolice positive matches,
non-matching cards, self exclusion, opponent exclusion, and the inherited
aura on a legal Black level-3-to-level-4 stack. No production correction
remains.
