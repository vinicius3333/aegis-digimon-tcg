## Ordered findings and corrections

### BT16-061 — DoruGreymon — provisional 6/10

Catalog/rules, peer and stack, and the compiled trace for Collision, target-switch
digivolution, SoC Tamer condition, hand source, cost waiver, and
`ignoreRequirements: false` are present. The inherited catalog wording says
“when this Digimon deletes another Digimon,” while the direct IR uses
`WhenBattleDeleteOpponent`, which is the narrower surviving-attacker battle
event. The runtime has no generic deleter identity on effect-driven deletes;
adding one would require a broad provenance/event change across shared delete
primitives, so this remains an explicit IR gap and the card receives `0/2` for
IR trace. The natural test now drives the target switch through Collision and
an actual `declareBlock`, and drives the current battle-only inherited play
through an actual attack that deletes a suspended opposing Digimon.

Correction: source-level behavior proof only; no broad runtime change.
Commit: `9b7fbc6b1` (`Audit BT16-061 natural event coverage`).

### BT16-062 — Zanmetsumon — provisional 8/10

The compiled IR covers both entry timings, DP-relative De-Digivolve, the
play-cost deletion, All Turns Gammamon copy, inherited copy, and alternate
evolution. Existing natural play proof covers the de-digivolve/delete path;
new natural attack proof uses a legal BT10-078 level-4 Gammamon stack source
and demonstrates copied inherited Retaliation in a losing battle. No source
correction was required.
Commit: `94668da67` (`Audit BT16-062 copied effect behavior`).

### BT16-063 — Shakkoumon — provisional 8/10

requirements, opponent-Digimon immunity, and DNA-only bottom-security
placement with `chooseEitherSecurityCount`. The new natural DNA intent uses
legal BT10-061 black and BT10-035 yellow level-4 materials and three own

opponent's security and that the result enters unsuspended. No source

Commit: `982fd7da8` (`Audit BT16-063 natural DNA behavior`).

### BT16-064 — Dorugoramon — provisional 8/10

The direct module matches Collision, the SoC-Tamer evolution deletion, and the
once-per-turn unsuspend effect. The source filter incorrectly had
`controllerDefault: "mine"`, which excluded an opposing Digimon even though
the catalog says “another Digimon.” That field was removed; the source-level
assertion now locks the controller-neutral filter, and a natural attack that
deletes an opposing Digimon proves the host unsuspends.
Correction: broadened `onDeletionOf` to either controller.
Commit: `2d4ff322c` (`Correct BT16-064 deletion scope`).

### BT16-065 — Darkdramon — provisional 8/10

DNA clause. Existing natural play proofs cover the stacked reductions and
reveal/delete behavior; the reduction assertion was strengthened from a
zone-total check to exactly six D-Brigade cards in the deck and no remaining
trash. No source correction was required.
Commit: `1509586ca` (`Strengthen BT16-065 reduction proof`).
