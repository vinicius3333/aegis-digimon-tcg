## Shared runtime trace

- `onDeletionOf` carries the deletion cause in the production payload. The
  dedicated `sourceFilter.deleteCause: "byEffect"` gate distinguishes an
  effect deletion from battle or rule deletion; the generic `requireByEffect`
  gate does not inspect this payload.
- `whenMovedFromBreeding` is a production breeding-area event. `RevealAdd`
  tracks selected cards and bottoms the unselected revealed cards; `Hatch`
  consumes the optional breeding slot through the normal runtime.
- `Return` costs with a Digi-Egg filter route a returned Digi-Egg to the
  owner’s Digi-Egg deck. `lowestLevel` compares printed Digimon levels and
  keeps ties. `CostGatedBlock` pays compound costs before its optional nested
  play, and the breeding play path honors the one-slot destination.
- Play-result bindings are carried by `boundRef` across delayed watchers.
  `DelayedDelete` uses the permanent IDs from the immediately preceding play;
  the shared primitive installs a one-shot opponent-turn-end watcher.
- `MindLink` itself relocates the Tamer under the selected Digimon. A second
  `PlaceUnder` would be redundant and could attempt an unintended relocation.
  `fromOwnDigivolutionStack` scopes inherited stack play to the host permanent,
  matching the printed “from this Digimon’s digivolution cards.”

When Digivolving and When Attacking clauses: delete one of your Digimon or
Tamers to delete an unsuspended opposing Digimon, otherwise delete an opposing
Tamer. Its All Turns once-per-turn watcher trashes the top opposing security
card when an effect deletes another of your Digimon or Tamers. No card-specific

deletion cost, deletes the opposing unsuspended Digimon, and observes the
opposing security trash. Useful peers were the effect-deletion watcher family
and the BT16-072 level-5 purple legal stack.
