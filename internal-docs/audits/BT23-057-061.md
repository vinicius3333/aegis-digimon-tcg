# BT23 audit ledger: BT23-061 through BT23-057

Each card was audited individually against every catalog field, its exact local KB output, direct implementation, shared interpreter seams and focused public behavior. Standard optional/card/target decisions are the applicable UI path.

## BT23-061 Ghostmon — 10/10

Purple level 3 Ghost/LIBERATOR Data Rookie, cost 3, 1000 DP, evolving for 0 from purple level 2. On Play and On Deletion, one own Ghost Digimon gains Blocker until the opponent's turn ends. Its inherited On Deletion gains 1 memory. No KB entry. Public behavior proves a selected Ghost receives Blocker while a non-Ghost does not; controller choice, source timing, duration, inherited timing, memory and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-060 Machinedramon — 10/10

Black level 6 Machine/CS Virus Mega, cost 12, 12000 DP; evolves for 4 from black level 5 or level-5 CS. Security Attack +1 and Reboot. On Play/When Digivolving de-digivolves one opposing Digimon before deleting one opposing 8000-DP-or-lower Digimon. Its When Attacking OPT activates one On Play effect of a face-up Zaxon Digimon in own security as this Digimon's effect. Q5330 confirms the mandatory activation consumes the OPT even when no face-up Zaxon exists; Q5331 confirms a borrowed On Play "by" clause must be paid when possible. Public stacked-target proof observes de-digivolution first and deletion against the resulting live DP, while a larger control survives; security visibility, borrowed source attribution, controller decision, action order and OPT map to tested seams.

## BT23-059 Justimon: Blitz Arm — 10/10

Black level 6 Cyborg/CS Vaccine Mega, cost 11, 11000 DP; evolves for 1 from either named Justimon arm or for 3 from level-5 CS. Blocker. Its shared On Play/When Digivolving/When Attacking OPT must trash any battle-area Option to delete one opposing lowest-play-cost Digimon. Its All Turns OPT reacts to a battle-area Option being trashed, unsuspends this Digimon, then grants immunity from opposing Digimon effects for the turn. Q5323 confirms "Option card in the battle area" means an effect-placed Option permanent; Q5324-Q5329 establish choice and immunity semantics. Public proof uses a rules-valid effect-placed Option and observes the cost enter trash, the lowest-cost opponent leave, and Justimon unsuspend. Mandatory cost, shared OPT, ties/choice, event order, source immunity, duration and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-058 Craniamon — 10/10

Black level 6 Holy Warrior/CS Vaccine Mega, cost 11, 11000 DP; evolves for 3 from black level 5 or level-5 CS. Reboot and Blocker. Its All Turns replacement may suspend this Digimon so one own Digimon or Tamer does not leave from an opposing effect. Its second All Turns OPT deletes all opposing Digimon tied for the lowest play cost whenever this Digimon suspends. No KB entry. Public proof suspends Craniamon through the reactive event and observes every tied minimum leave while the higher-cost control remains; affected-card choice, replacement cost/failure, Digimon-or-Tamer scope, opponent-effect provenance, all-ties targeting, timing and OPT map to tested seams.

## BT23-057 Gankoomon — 10/10

Red/black level 6 Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/CS Vaccine Mega, cost 11, 11000 DP; evolves for 3 from red or black level 5 or level-5 CS. Before play, returning exactly three Huckmon/Sistermon/Jesmon-name cards from trash to the chosen deck end reduces play cost by 5. On Play/When Digivolving may first play a white 6000-DP Hinukamuy Token with Alliance, Reboot and Blocker, then deletes one opposing Digimon at play cost 6 or less, raising the ceiling by 3 for every other own Digimon. Q5322 confirms the return cost is exactly three, never two. The token registry was corrected to the printed level-less white 6000-DP definition and the action now resolves the registered token name and dynamic deletion ceiling directly. Public proof observes the real token identity, stats and three keywords, counts it plus another own Digimon, and deletes a cost-7 opponent. Deck-end choice, exact cost/failure, optional token decision, action order, dynamic count, target choice and evolution requirements map to tested seams.
