# BT23 audit ledger: BT23-056 through BT23-052

Each card was audited individually against every catalog field, its exact local KB output, direct implementation, shared interpreter seams and focused public behavior. Standard optional/card/target decisions are the applicable UI path.

## BT23-056 WereGarurumon — 10/10

Black level 5 Beastkin/CS Virus Ultimate, cost 7, 7000 DP; evolves for 3 from black level 4 or level-4 CS. Blocker. On Play/When Digivolving requires an own CS Tamer, then gives one opposing Digimon a Start of Your Main Phase forced-attack ability until that opponent's turn ends. Its inherited All Turns OPT de-digivolves one opposing Digimon whenever attack targets change. Q5321 confirms the ability may be granted to an unaffected Digimon but will not trigger if it remains unaffected at the future timing. Public proof observes no immediate attack, the chosen opponent attacking at its own main-phase start, and a legal level-6 evolution host applying exactly one inherited de-digivolution across repeated events. Trait gate, target decision, duration, immunity interaction, timing, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-055 Cyberdramon — 10/10

Black level 5 Cyborg/Hudie/CS Vaccine Ultimate, cost 7, 7000 DP; evolves for 3 from black level 4 or level-4 CS. On Play/When Digivolving deletes one opposing play-cost-5-or-lower Digimon. Its printed All Turns OPT prevents its own departure by trashing one own effect-placed battle-area Option; its inherited All Turns OPT protects an own Cyberdramon-, Justimon-name or CS Digimon through the same cost. Q5320 identifies the eligible Option as one placed in the battle area by an effect. Public proof observes the cost-5 boundary while cost 6 survives, then observes a real effect-placed Option enter trash to prevent the first deletion and the second deletion succeed after the OPT is spent. Cost availability/failure, controller confirmation, zones, replacement order, name/trait OR, inherited host and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-054 Magnamon — 10/10

Black/blue level 4 Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/CS Free Armor Form, cost 7, 7000 DP; normally evolves for 4 from black or blue level 3 and alternatively for 3 from Veemon or level-3 CS. Blocker and Armor Purge. On Play/When Digivolving draws 1, then protects one own Royal Knight/CS Digimon from being returned to hands or decks by opposing effects until the opponent's turn ends. No KB entry. Public proof observes the draw, blocks an opponent-controlled return, and permits the controller's own return against the same selected permanent. Trait OR, target choice, opponent-only provenance, both destinations, duration, keywords and every evolution route map to tested seams.

## BT23-053 Strikedramon — 10/10

Black level 4 Dragonkin/CS Vaccine Champion, cost 5, 5000 DP; evolves for 2 from black level 3 or level-3 CS. During its controller's turn, when one of that player's Options is placed in the battle area, it may evolve from hand into Cyberdramon-name or CS Digimon with the cost reduced by 2. Its inherited All Turns effect grants +1000 DP. No KB entry. Public mixed-hand proof observes a real Option placement evolve Strikedramon into Cyberdramon, retains the non-CS control, preserves the stack order and pays 1 memory after reducing Cyberdramon's normal cost 3. The implementation incorrectly omitted normal payment and now sets `payCost: true`. Turn direction, event/controller identity, optionality, name/trait OR, source zone, cost floor/reduction, stack state, inherited DP and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-052 Consulmon — 10/10

Black level 4 Saving Life Sup./Appmon, cost 4, 4000 DP; evolves for 2 from black level 3. Its Security effect waits until its security battle ends, then plays itself free. On Play/When Digivolving restricts one opposing Digimon from attacking players until that opponent's turn ends. Its Link header links to an Appmon for cost 2, contributes +3000 DP, and its When Linking effect grants Reboot and Blocker until the opponent's turn ends. No KB entry. The implementation lacked executable Link metadata/effects and resolved the delayed Security payload immediately. It now carries a linked-effect flag, re-derives a newly linked source before publishing the link event, and binds the deferred security watcher to the exact checked instance. Public attack proof observes the checked card leave security, complete battle, leave trash and enter play; public link intent proves the Appmon requirement, cost 2, +3000 DP, Reboot and Blocker. Security timing, source identity, zones/face state, free play, restriction choice/duration, link legality/cost/order and linked duration map to tested seams.
