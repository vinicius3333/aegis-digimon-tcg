## BT23-084 Erika Mishima — 10/10

Green Hudie/CS Tamer, cost 4. Security self-plays; start-main gains 1 with an own CS Digimon; end-turn atomically suspends self and returns one own Hudie Digimon, then may play one level 3 CS Digimon from hand into an empty breeding area free. Its inherited Your Turn effect grants Alliance only while the host is Hudiemon, Eater Legion, or Eater EDEN. Q5257/Q5319 preserve simultaneous Alliance ordering across the derived DNA evolution, and Q5357 confirms the breeding-area play does not trigger On Play. Public proof observes both costs, hand return, exact breeding destination, and absence from battle; structured name gates, level/trait, empty-area failure, cost atomicity, turn ownership, inherited source, and Security are mapped.

Green Royal Base/CS Tamer, cost 3. Start-main gains 1 with an own Royal Base or CS Digimon. All Turns watches cards placed face up in the controller's security; if an added card has Zaxon or Royal Base, suspending self gains 1 and draws 1 only when the hand has at most 7 cards. Yellow CS Tamer, cost 3; no card-specific KB entry. Start-main gains 1 only if the opponent has a Digimon. Public tests cover qualifying and nonqualifying evolved traits, the returned source cost, Lopmon/level-3-CS result, opponent presence, and the owner-turn boundary.
