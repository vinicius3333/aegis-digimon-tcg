6. Decisions/controllers: player chooses at most one eligible hand card, one own CS Tamer for cost, and exactly one opposing Digimon for DP modification; Q5571's single-target boundary is encoded by count 1.
7. Zones/order/face: play is hand-only; returned Tamer goes to hand; played-card derived triggers wait until Hudiemon finishes, covered by interpreter ordering regressions.
8. Interactions: Q5257/Q5319 and Q5389/Q6708-Q6712 were inspected; Alliance and Tamer-source evolution use shared engine suites, with no card-specific UI state.
9. UI: no presentation-only path; all choices are ordinary public intents, so Orca Browser is not applicable.
10. Gates: colocated assertions, conditional Tamer evolution tests, shared capability regressions, BT23 aggregate tests, typecheck, oxfmt, and diff-check passed.
