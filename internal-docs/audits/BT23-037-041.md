# BT23 audit ledger: BT23-041 through BT23-037

Each card was audited individually against every catalog field, its exact local KB output, direct implementation, shared interpreter seams and focused public behavior. Standard optional/card/target decisions are the applicable UI path.

## BT23-041 Kabuterimon — 10/10

Green/yellow level 4 Insectoid/Hudie/CS Vaccine Champion, cost 5, 5000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from green or yellow level 3 and alternatively for 2 from level-3 CS. Alliance. Its All Turns OPT reacts only when this Digimon suspends and gives one own Digimon Piercing and +3000 DP for the turn. No KB entry. The two payload actions previously opened independent target decisions despite the singular printed subject; the DP action now reuses the keyword selection. Public proof accepts either legal UI choice but requires exactly one Piercing recipient, both benefits on that same permanent and no DP spill to the other candidate. Keyword, event identity, controller/target choice, binding, duration, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-040 Wormmon — 10/10

Green level 3 Larva/Hudie/CS Free Rookie, cost 4, 3000 DP; normally evolves for 1 from green level 2 and alternatively for 0 from level-2 CS. At the start of its controller's main phase it may place an in-play Erika Mishima as this Digimon's bottom digivolution card, then evolve into Hudiemon from hand or trash with the cost reduced by 2. Its inherited All Turns effect gives every own Hudie Digimon +1000 DP. Q5302 expressly excludes Erika from hand or trash and permits only the battle-area permanent. The cost was incorrectly preflighted as a loose hand card, the paid evolution did not select its matching alternate CS cost, and the inherited clause was absent. `targetIsPermanent`, `payCost`, `useAlternateCost` and the shared interpreter handoff now model the exact decisions. Public proof observes Erika leave battle area for stack bottom, Hudiemon become top, and memory fall from 5 to 3; a second proof observes the inherited aura on carrier and ally. Timing, breeding exclusion, zones/order, alternate cost/reduction, optional failure and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-039 Perorimon — 10/10

Green level 3 Gourmet Entertainment Appmon, cost 3, 1000 DP; normally evolves for 0 from green level 2 and alternatively for 0 from level-2 Appmon. On Play reveals 3, adds one Appmon card and one Game/Invincible (App Name) card, then bottoms the rest. It links to an Appmon for 1, contributes +2000 DP, and its optional When Linking suspends one opposing Digimon. No KB entry. The complete Link requirement/effect was absent. The official Invincible (App Name) token is retained alongside the catalog's normalized `Invincible` trait alias. Public proofs observe two distinct reveal categories and remainder order, then a real Link intent paying exactly 1 memory, moving the card to the linked zone, adding exactly 2000 DP and suspending the chosen opponent. Reveal visibility, choices, normalized trait, zones/order, link host/cost/DP, optionality and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-038 FunBeemon — 10/10

Green/black level 3 Insectoid/X Antibody/Royal Base/CS Virus Rookie, cost 3, 1000 DP; normally evolves for 1 from green or black level 2 and alternatively for 0 from level-2 Royal Base or CS. From security during all turns it grants +1000 DP to all own Royal Base Digimon. On Play reveals 3, adds one card containing Royal Base in its text and one CS card, then bottoms the rest. Its inherited All Turns effect gives its carrier +1000 DP. Q5301 defines “in its text” across names, traits, effects, inherited text, rules and every evolution/fusion/link/assembly requirement. The inherited clause was absent and is now executable. Public proofs select distinct Winr and CS candidates, preserve the exact remainder at deck bottom and observe the inherited continuous bonus on a real carrier. Security applicability, text matching, category choices, zones/order, DP duration and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-037 Tentomon — 10/10

Green/yellow level 3 Insectoid/Hudie/CS Vaccine Rookie, cost 3, 1000 DP; normally evolves for 1 from green or yellow level 2 and alternatively for 0 from level-2 CS. During its controller's turn, while in battle area, it reduces by 1 the cost to evolve into a CS Digimon. Its inherited When Attacking OPT may play a play-cost-5-or-lower Hudie Digimon from hand free; the exact result cannot evolve and is deleted at the end of the opponent's turn. Q5299 excludes the breeding area; Q5300 allows a pending Alliance to suspend the newly played Digimon; Q5565 keeps the permanent evolution lock if deletion is prevented; Q5566 makes the delayed deletion simultaneous with other opponent-turn-end processing. The complete inherited clause was absent and is now bound to the play result with the correct opponent-turn delay. Public proof observes the cost boundary, free play, exact-result permanent lock, survival through owner turn end and deletion only at opponent turn end. Zone gate, replacement cost, binding, payment, Alliance ordering, persistent failure state, simultaneous timing, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.
