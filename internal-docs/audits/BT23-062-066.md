## BT23-066 Matadormon — 10/10

Purple level 5 Undead/CS Virus Ultimate, cost 7, 7000 DP; evolves for 3 from purple level 4 or level-4 CS. Scapegoat. On Play/When Digivolving deletes one opposing level-4-or-lower Digimon; only when the evolution came from trash may it then play one play-cost-3-or-lower Undead/CS card from trash free. Its inherited All Turns OPT may delete this Digimon to prevent another own Digimon leaving. No KB entry. Public On Play proof observes level boundary and proves the trash-evolution-only tail remains inactive; conditional provenance, play-cost/trait OR, source exclusion, cost, prevention, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-065 Phantomon — 10/10

Purple level 5 Ghost/LIBERATOR Virus Ultimate, cost 7, 7000 DP, evolves from purple level 4 for 3. ## BT23-063 Sangloupmon — 10/10

Purple level 4 Dark Animal/CS Virus Champion, cost 4, 4000 DP; evolves for 2 from purple level 3 or level-3 CS. When Attacking it may evolve itself using an Undead/CS card in trash. Its inherited When Attacking OPT may evolve one own Digimon using an Undead/Dark Animal card in trash. Source/self, OR traits, trash, controller choice, OPT and timing map to tested seams.
