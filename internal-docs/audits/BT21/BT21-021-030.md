# BT21-021 through BT21-030 card audit

> Historical provisional range report. Superseded by `docs/audits/BT21-AUDIT.md` and `docs/audits/BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md`.

## Scope, authority, and scoring

This static audit covers exactly BT21-021 through BT21-030 in ascending card-ID order. Catalog facts were read from immutable blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` (the working catalog file hashes to that blob); the local rules knowledge base was queried for every card, and the audit inspected the direct TypeScript modules, read-only generated IR, interpreter/runtime seams, peer implementations, legal stacks, and behavior-driving test source.

Every card receives exactly five two-point columns: Catalog/rules, IR trace, Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates. The Executed delivery gates column is fixed at `0/2` by task instruction, so every total is provisional and capped at 8/10. Structural-only assertions and manually injected timing/event or primitive calls are supplemental; they do not receive Behavioral `2/2` where a natural card/engine origin is feasible.

All ten direct modules have full compiled coverage with empty residual coverage and register executable behavior exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. No audited module has a second `registerCard` registration. `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and the root `BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were inspected read-only and were not changed.

## Card findings

### BT21-021 OmniShoutmon — provisional 7/10

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** The immutable record is a red/yellow level-5 Dragonkin/Xros Heart/Hero Digimon with the standalone `[Shoutmon]` and level-4 Xros Heart/Hero alternate evolution routes, DigiXros treatment as Shoutmon, optional End of Attack trait-card play followed by conditional self-deletion, optional On Deletion trait-Digimon placement from hand/trash followed by optional Save, and inherited Xros Heart-gated Rush. KB Q4529 confirms the self-delete requires a successful play; Q4530 confirms Save can place the card under the newly played Tamer; Q4727 confirms DigiXros removal can eliminate the later deletion target; Q4728 confirms the played card cannot attack during the still-open End of Attack processing.
- **IR trace — 2/2.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT21/BT21-021.ts` traces both alternate routes, the DigiXros name treatment, cost-reduced hand play, `ifThisEffectActed` self-delete, hand/trash placement, optional Save, and inherited Rush. The audit tightened the On Deletion source to Digimon cards, excluded token Tamers, and encoded the standalone alternate source as `namesExact: ["Shoutmon"]`. The structured DigiXros `names` slot is already exact in the shared DigiXros consumer; it remains unchanged. The generated snapshot is stale: it omits the Digimon/non-token boundaries, represents Save as `ActivateEffect`, and retains the substring alternate-name field.
- **Behavioral proof — 1/2.** `BT21-021.test.ts` uses the production deletion verb to observe trait-card placement and Save under a Tamer, and a production play path for the reduced-cost clause. End of Attack is manually fired, so the available source proof is supplemental rather than full natural timing coverage.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** The direct BT21-016 and BT21-011 Save implementations establish the non-token Tamer and optional Save conventions. The source uses a legal Tamer and Xros Heart trash card and checks an Xros Heart versus nonmatching inherited host.
- **Correction/limitation.** Atomic correction commit `e310762f412bef63e1506519d8ac5a1f589af475` added the Digimon/non-token boundaries, exact alternate name gate, and matching source assertions. Natural End of Attack event-origin proof remains unexecuted.

### BT21-022 Canoweissmon — provisional 8/10

- **IR trace — 2/2.** Both entry timings in `BT21-022.ts` use the Digimon/Gammamon-text hand filter and bottom self-stack placement before deletion, with `optional` plus `abortOnDecline` preserving the printed “By placing” activation gate. The inherited replacement retains `mode: "prevent"`, `leaveCause: "byOpponentEffect"`, a three-card Digimon stack cost, and Once Per Turn. The generated snapshot agrees on entry-cost optionality but remains stale where it flattens the inherited cost without its self-stack/source boundaries.
- **Behavioral proof — 2/2.** `BT21-022.test.ts` drives a natural On Play through `applyIntent`, observes the qualifying material at the bottom and exact 7000-DP deletion boundary, proves a nonmatching hand card cannot pay, proves a payable cost may be declined without deletion, and drives natural opponent-effect deletion to show exactly-three-card prevention and the second deletion leaving the field.

- **Correction/limitation.** Initial audit commit `25c4f32a5e1b3ab5913296b1348aebea82bfe9c2` incorrectly removed the optional-cost flags. reconciliation commit `985f6cf2d274045670ea569213489cd037208058` restored the correct activation/refusal semantics and the decline assertion, so BT21-022 has no net semantic correction attributed to this range. ### BT21-023 Globemon — provisional 7/10

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** The immutable record is a red/yellow level-5 Global/Hero Digimon with free App Fusion from DoGatchmon and Timemon, Security Attack +1, optional free Link of one level-4-or-lower Digimon from hand or this Digimon's digivolution cards, a Your Turn Once Per Turn linked deletion scaled to Globemon DP, and a Link effect that deletes an opposing Digimon with as much or less DP. The read-only generated entry omits the Link-capability gate, source zones, host pin, and Link effect, so it is only a comparison snapshot.
- **Behavioral proof — 1/2.** Existing source drives natural On Play linking and checks linkless/level boundaries; the linked deletion watcher is manually fired. The added mixed-stack source assertion manually fires On Play and proves the foreign stack remains untouched, but it is supplemental.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** BT26-007 demonstrates the same hand-or-own-stack OR/host-filter seam. BT21-018 is used as a legal Link candidate and BT1-009 as a linkless/level-negative control; the added fixture places matching Link cards under Globemon and a different host.
- **Correction/limitation.** Atomic correction commit `53a9ebcda56a42bc93c0dc06cf08d8c3802b6975` pins stack sources to Globemon and adds the mixed-stack assertion. Natural `whenLinked` producer proof remains unexecuted.
