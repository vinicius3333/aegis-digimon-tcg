## Outcome

The ten cards were audited against the immutable catalog blob
efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a, the local rules knowledge base,
the direct TypeScript card modules, compiled IR, shared alternate-evolution
overrides, focused/mechanism sources, peer implementations, and the BT21

## Evidence and authority

The catalog was read from the committed packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json
blob at the supplied immutable object ID. Rules and rulings were queried with
node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT21-061 through BT21-070 and cross-checked
against data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md. The relevant general rules are:

- data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:918-945: “with [X] in its text” covers
  printed name, traits, effects, inherited effects, and printed requirements;
  gained text and notes do not qualify.
- data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2911-2944: De-Digivolve is resolved as
  separate processes, so a scaling count of four colors produces two
  De-Digivolve 1 processes rather than one De-Digivolve 2.
- data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:3014-3019: Save places the card under one
  of its owner's Tamers and its processing is optional when the effect reaches
  the Save instruction.
- data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:3075-3097: Alliance grants the keyword and
  permits a different own Digimon to attack after the Alliance processing.
- data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2721-2744, 2767-2770, and 583-604:
  Linking, start-of-main, and Security end-of-battle timing.

The executable direct modules are authoritative for runtime behavior. The
BT21 index imports every target module at apps/api/src/cards/BT21/index.ts:62-71.
The target modules register at BT21-061 line 204, BT21-062 line 92, BT21-063
line 99, BT21-064 line 71, BT21-065 line 55, BT21-066 line 161, BT21-067 line
116, BT21-068 line 89, BT21-069 line 134, and BT21-070 line 101. Shared

packages/shared/src/effects/data.ts:656-675 for BT21-063 and BT21-066.

packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json is a stale generated snapshot in this

## Per-card audit

### BT21-061 MetalGreymon

Catalog and rules: The catalog record is Black level 5, 7 play cost, 7,000 DP,
with the normal Black level 4 cost 3 route and alternate level 4 route for a
card with Greymon in its name or ADVENTURE trait at cost 3. Its main text has
On Play/When Digivolving De-Digivolve 1 for every two colors among the
controller's Tamers, followed by a once-per-turn Your Turn trigger: when
another own Digimon is played or digivolves, an ADVENTURE Digimon gains
Alliance and one own Digimon may attack. The inherited effect is Alliance.
KB Q4565-Q4568 confirm Alliance, distinct Alliance recipient/attacker, an
optional attack, and separate De-Digivolve 1 processes for four colors. Q4731
confirms that the later attack remains available when the ADVENTURE condition
is not met.

IR and runtime: BT21-061.ts:7-164 contains both digivolution triggers, the two
nested Your Turn source triggers, the conditional Alliance grant, the optional
attack, and inherited Alliance. scaling.ts:152-215 supplies distinct Tamer
color counting, and digivolution.ts:16-49 resolves each De-Digivolve repeat
independently. The module ends with the sole registerIrCard("BT21-061",
compiled) registration. The compiled object has no residual effects.

Behavioral proof (source only): BT21-061.test.ts:58-151 covers color scaling,
the Q4568 two-process case, Q4565/Q4567 optionality, Q4731 non-ADVENTURE
continuation, and inherited Alliance on a realistic evolution stack. These
assertions use public play, digivolution, and attack intent for the important
branches.

Peer and stack proof: The engine's De-Digivolve implementation and Alliance
rules match the checked-in test expectations; the inherited Alliance assertion
also exercises the card as a source under a higher evolution. No duplicate
legacy registration was found. Result: no confirmed defect.

Score: 2/2 + 2/2 + 2/2 + 2/2 + 0/2 = 8/10 provisional.

### BT21-066 Arresterdramon

Catalog and rules: The record is Purple/Red level 4, 6 play cost, 6,000 DP,
with normal level 3 cost 3 and alternate level 3 routes for Save in text or
Hero trait at cost 2. On Play/When Digivolving it may play a Hunter or Hero
Tamer for free. On Deletion it may place one qualifying Save-text/Hero Digimon
from hand or trash under an own Tamer, then performs Save. DigiXros is one
Digimon card with Save in its text. Q4574 confirms the broad printed-text
matching for Save; the printed “Then, Save” sequencing and peer implementations
model the second placement as a mandatory follow-up when the first optional
placement is declined.

IR and runtime: BT21-066.ts:15-160 contains the free Tamer branch, the optional
first placement from hand/trash, the mandatory second self-placement for Save,
inherited DP, both alternate routes, and the Save DigiXros requirement. It
registers only through registerIrCard("BT21-066", compiled) at line 161, with
no residual effects.

Confirmed correction: Before this audit, the first optional placement had
abortOnDecline: true. That incorrectly stopped the following mandatory Save
when the player declined the first optional placement. The flag was removed;
the second place-under action remains mandatory. This matches the same
sequencing in BT21-016.ts:5-16,43-81 and BT19-026.ts, where an optional
preparatory placement does not abort an independent mandatory Save. The new
natural regression source at BT21-066.test.ts:108-129 deletes an
Arresterdramon with an optional-decline setup and asserts that the card still
reaches the Tamer while the qualifying hand card remains in hand.

Behavioral proof (source only): BT21-066.test.ts:21-131 covers free Hunter/Hero
Tamer play, Save/Hero placement, nonmatching Tamer behavior, the newly added
natural decline regression, inherited DP, and alternate/DigiXros structural
coverage. The correction's critical sequencing is represented by a natural
deletion; the source test was not executed.

Peer and stack proof: BT21-016 and BT19-026 provide direct peer proof for
optional-first-placement followed by mandatory Save. runAction.ts aborts a
sequence only when abortOnDecline is set, which explains both the defect and
the minimal correction. Result: corrected; no known remaining semantic defect.

Score: 2/2 + 2/2 + 2/2 + 2/2 + 0/2 = 8/10 provisional.
