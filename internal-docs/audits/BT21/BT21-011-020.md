# BT21-011–020 Static Card Audit

> Historical provisional range report. Superseded by `docs/audits/BT21-AUDIT.md` and `docs/audits/BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md`.

## Scope and scoring policy

This is the provisional static audit for BT21-011 through BT21-020, in ascending card-ID order. The catalog authority is the immutable blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` (the committed `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` snapshot). Direct TypeScript card modules under `apps/api/src/cards/BT21` are the executable source authority; `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` is a read-only generated-IR snapshot and was not edited.

Each card is scored in exactly five two-point columns: Catalog/rules, IR trace, Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates. The delivery-gate column is fixed at 0/2 because this audit intentionally does not execute tests or gates; therefore every result is provisional and the maximum possible result is 8/10. Structural assertions and manually injected events/timing are recorded as supplemental evidence only and do not earn Behavioral 2/2 when a natural card/engine origin is feasible.

All ten modules use only `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no duplicate `registerCard` registration is present. The report, source corrections, and existing catalog/effects snapshots are kept separate: neither `BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md` nor `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was changed.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT21-011 — Shoutmon

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** The immutable catalog requires the Xros Heart/Hero would-digivolve reduction, Save on deletion, and inherited Rush only while this Digimon has Xros Heart. The direct source preserves these clauses; KB query Q4521 confirms that the turn reducer does not trigger in the breeding area.
- **IR trace — 2/2.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT21/BT21-011.ts` compiles the self-source reducer, deletion placement under a Tamer, and conditional inherited Rush through the registered IR path. The interpreter’s would-digivolve reducer collection, `isSelfRef` matching, and timing registration provide the relevant runtime seams; no residual direct-card gap remains.
- **Behavioral proof — 2/2.** The behavior source contains natural public digivolution cases for matching and nonmatching Xros Heart/Hero targets, turn/breeding boundaries, natural deletion/Save placement, and the inherited Rush condition. A supplemental `EffectTiming.None` firing is present, but the score relies on the natural public card/engine paths rather than that injection; checks were not run.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** BT21-016 and BT21-013 supply legal Xros Heart/Hero and Hero targets, while BT21-083 supplies the Tamer placement context. The source assertions cover the corrected trait condition and deletion destination.

### BT21-012 — Flamemon

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** The catalog requires suspending Flamemon to play a red Tamer with inherited effects from hand for free, then placing Flamemon under that played Tamer, plus inherited +2000 DP. The direct source matches the public Main-cost and inherited-effect contract.
- **IR trace — 2/2.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT21/BT21-012.ts` uses `PlayWithoutCost`, an `ifThisEffectActed` guard, and `lastPlayed: true` to bind the subsequent `PlaceUnder` to the Tamer played by this effect. The context predicate and effect-result seams support that binding; the direct module is the executable authority over the stale generated snapshot.
- **Behavioral proof — 2/2.** The behavior source activates the public Main effect, verifies free play/suspension/placement, rejects non-red or non-inherited-effect Tamers, handles decline without suspension, and checks inherited DP on and off turn. These are natural public activation paths; checks were not run.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** BT21-082 is a legal red Tamer with inherited effects; BT1-085 and BT17-083 provide the negative eligibility boundaries. BT21-013 provides a legal host for the inherited DP comparison.
