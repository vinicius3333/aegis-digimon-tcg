## Scope and authority

This report covers exactly BT21-041 through BT21-050 in strict ascending card-ID order. The immutable catalog authority is blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. Each card was compared with its catalog record, the local rules knowledge base at `data/kb/qa.json`, its direct TypeScript module, the read-only generated snapshot, shared interpreter/runtime seams, peer implementations, legal stacks, and source-test evidence.

All ten direct modules have `coverage: "full"`, empty residual coverage, and executable registration exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no audited module has a second `registerCard` registration. The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot, `BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, collection ledgers, and unrelated card files were not modified. The direct modules are the executable authority where the generated snapshot is stale or lossy.

This is a static/source-proof audit. `Executed delivery gates` is fixed at `0/2` for every card because this dispatch explicitly prohibited tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check`. Structural assertions and manually injected timing/events are supplemental only and do not earn behavioral `2/2` when a natural origin exists. The range subtotal is `76/100`, an average provisional score of `7.6/10`; no score exceeds the provisional `8/10` ceiling and this report makes no collection-completion claim.

## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point dimensions:

1. Catalog/rules — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. IR trace — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive registration, with generated IR recorded only as comparison evidence.
3. Behavioral proof — observable state changes from source tests, prioritizing natural public origins.
4. Peer and stack proof — useful peer comparison and legal evolution/link/inherited stack coverage.
5. Executed delivery gates — fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch boundary.
