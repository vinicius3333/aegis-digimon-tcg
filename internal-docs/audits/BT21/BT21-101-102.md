## BT21-101 Gaiamon

### Catalog/rules — 2/2

The immutable catalog defines Gaiamon as a White/Red Lv.6 Digimon with 13 play
cost, 13,000 DP, `God`/`Appmon` forms, and `Creation` type. Its text declares
`[App Fusion] [Globemon] & [Charismon]: Cost 0`, `＜Blocker＞`, `＜Link +1＞`, two
optional Link windows, and a Your Turn Once Per Turn watcher for when one of your
Digimon gets linked. The card-specific KB entry Q4591 (2025-04-04) answers that
the Link windows cannot link a card without `＜Link＞`; the corrected IR now
expresses that requirement explicitly.

The local comprehensive-rules KB also confirms App Fusion in §8-4, Link in
§10-1, and `＜Link +＞` in §16-40. The latter is represented by the static keyword
record with amount `1`.

### IR trace — 2/2

The executable module at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT21/BT21-101.ts:29-132` now carries the following faithful
trace for both `WhenDigivolving` and `WhenAttacking`:

- one optional Link action, with `payCost: false`;
- own Digimon material with the `Appmon` trait and `hasLinkRequirement: true`;
- material from `hand` or `digivolutionCards`;
- `target.source: "thisDigimon"`, which narrows the stack branch to the source
  Gaiamon's own digivolution cards while leaving the hand branch available;
- one recipient among the controller's Digimon.

The Your Turn watcher at lines 95–132 is Once Per Turn and uses
`sourceFilter: { controller: "mine", kind: ["Digimon"] }`. Its action pays by
unsuspending this Gaiamon, then trashes the opponent's top security card. The
App Fusion requirement and `coverage: "full"`/empty residual are preserved.

The direct module registers exactly once through
`registerIrCard("BT21-101", compiled)` at line 145. There is no second
`registerCard` registration. The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`
entry was inspected read-only: it is stale and omits the Appmon/Link-capability
filter, source-stack scope, source `from` zones, and own-Digimon watcher filter;
it was not edited. `generated-digivolve-overrides.json` has no BT21-101 entry.

### Behavioral proof — 1/2

Before correction, the Link target's broad loose-card scan could offer a matching
Appmon card from another own permanent's digivolution stack because the target
did not identify this Gaiamon's host. The runtime `candidateLooseInstances` seam
explicitly applies `target.source: "thisDigimon"` to hosted cards. The target also
now states `hasLinkRequirement: true`, matching the runtime `linkEligible` guard
and KB Q4591 rather than relying only on the later server-side eligibility pass.

Before correction, the unfiltered `whenLinked` bus could arm Gaiamon when an
opponent's Digimon was the event subject. The generic SubTrigger path resolves
`sourceFilter` through `subjectMatchesFilter`, so the own-controller/kind filter
now excludes that event while accepting a natural own-Digimon Link.

The source assertions in `BT21-101.test.ts:16-59` cover the exact IR shape. A
natural public `linkCard` intent regression case at lines 61–92 links catalog
card BT21-009 to Gaiamon and checks the security result. The later opponent
subject is manually injected after the once-per-turn budget was already spent,
so it does not independently prove the corrected opponent boundary. The two
corrected When Digivolving/When Attacking Link windows also remain structural
rather than natural public-origin paths. The existing once-per-turn behavior
case remains at lines 101–119. These are authored source proofs; they were not
executed under the assigned no-tests instruction.

### Peer and stack proof — 2/2

The catalog supplies the legal App Fusion pair directly: `BT21-023` Globemon is
Red/Yellow, Lv.5, `Appmon`, and has `[Link] [Appmon] trait: Cost 3`; `BT21-073`
Charismon is Purple/Black, Lv.5, `Appmon`, and has the same Link requirement.
That exactly matches Gaiamon's `Globemon & Charismon` App Fusion header. The
BT21-023 executable peer uses explicit Link-capability and own-stack scoping for
its analogous Link window, while BT21-005 and BT24-087 demonstrate self/own
Digimon `whenLinked` watcher filtering. BT21-009 Gatchmon is an Appmon/Hero
Digimon with a structured Link requirement and is the natural positive fixture
used by the source proof.

## BT21-102 Tai Kamiya

### Catalog/rules — 2/2

The immutable catalog defines Tai Kamiya as a White, play-cost-4 Tamer with
`ADVENTURE` and `Hero` traits. Its text is: set memory to 3 at the start of the
turn when memory is 2 or less; when one of your Digimon attacks, suspend this
Tamer to Draw 1; and once per turn in Main, optionally play a hand card with the
`ADVENTURE` or `Hero` trait and play cost 2 or less, adding one to that maximum
for each of your Tamers' colors, then return this Tamer to the bottom of the
deck. Its Security text plays itself without paying its cost.

No card-specific KB entry exists for BT21-102. The local rules KB's color rules
recognize multicolor cards as carrying each printed color, which is the required
basis for distinct-Tamer-color scaling.

### IR trace — 2/2

The executable module at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT21/BT21-102.ts:8-111` retains exact Start of Your Turn,
own-Digimon attack watcher, Main Once Per Turn, and Security traces. The Main
play action at lines 55–80 now carries:

```text
playCostLte: 2
playCostLteScaling: {
  per: 1,
  filter: { controller: "mine", kind: ["Tamer"] },
  unit: "colors"
}
```

This is the live interpreter seam: `runPlayAction` resolves the scaling before
candidate selection and `scaleFactor` counts distinct effective colors among
matching Tamers. The subsequent mandatory Return action remains separate, so a
declined optional play does not suppress the printed return-to-deck instruction.

Before correction, the module placed a targetless `CostModifier` with
`costType: "playcost"` before the play action. `runResourceAction` returns without
installing a modifier for a targetless play-cost action; that representation was
therefore inert. The direct module now registers exactly once through
`registerIrCard("BT21-102", compiled)` at line 117, with no duplicate
`registerCard` registration.

The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` entry was inspected
read-only. It has the same targetless no-op `CostModifier` drift (and places it
after the play action), so it is not an executable authority for this corrected
card module. `generated-digivolve-overrides.json` has no BT21-102 entry; neither
shared file was edited.

### Behavioral proof — 1/2

The corrected Main action uses the exact production selection path: the dynamic
`playCostLte` is computed before loose hand candidates are filtered. With Tai's
white color, the effective ceiling is 3, so catalog Gatchmon (`BT21-009`, a
play-cost-3 `Hero`/`Appmon` Digimon) is a positive natural candidate. The
source test at `BT21-102.test.ts:102-125` activates the public Main effect and
checks that Gatchmon is played and Tai reaches the deck bottom. The same source
file covers the memory boundary at lines 68–77, own-Digimon attack draw/suspend
at lines 79–100, and Security self-play at lines 127–134.

Those source proofs were not executed under the assigned no-tests instruction.
The corrected IR assertion is at `BT21-102.test.ts:25-48`, including the
`playCostLteScaling` filter and the shifted Return action. No manual cost ledger
or primitive injection is used for the load-bearing Main play proof, but the
Start of Turn, attack-watcher, and Security cases enter their timings directly;
therefore the card does not receive full behavioral credit.

### Peer and stack proof — 2/2

`BT21-061` MetalGreymon and the ST20 Tamer implementations provide nearby
same-rules patterns for scaling by the distinct colors of your Tamers. The
catalog's `ST20-12` Sora & Kari is a legal multicolor Red/Yellow Tamer fixture,
which demonstrates why color counting must be distinct-color based rather than
one-card/one-unit based. BT21-009 Gatchmon is a legal positive target because
its `Hero` trait and play cost 3 satisfy the base-2 ceiling plus Tai's one White
color. The own-controller filter excludes opponent Tamers from the scaling pool
and from the play target.

### Correction and limitation

Correction committed atomically as `74ccac2bc` (`Implement BT21-102 color play ceiling`):

- removed the targetless, runtime-inert `CostModifier`;
- encoded the printed dynamic ceiling on the `PlayWithoutCost` target using
  `playCostLteScaling` with distinct own-Tamer colors;
- updated structural assertions while retaining the public Main activation proof.

No catalog, generated snapshot, shared override, static ledger, or rules KB file
was changed. The directly entered timing paths above reduce Behavioral proof,
and delivery-gate execution remains `0/2`.
