# BT21-071–080 Card-by-Card Audit

> Historical provisional range report. Superseded by `docs/audits/BT21-AUDIT.md` and `docs/audits/BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md`.

## Scope and evidence authority

This report audits exactly BT21-071 through BT21-080 in ascending order. The immutable
catalog source was read from blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` and checked
against `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`. The local rules/knowledge base was
queried with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD_ID>` and the applicable comprehensive-rule
entries were inspected.

The audit also inspected every direct TypeScript module and its compiled IR, the read-only
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot, shared interpreter seams, registration,
source assertions, peer cards, legal evolution/link stacks, and mechanism assertions. The
generated snapshot was not treated as authoritative where it diverges from the direct module,
and neither protected data file was edited: `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and
`BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md` are unchanged.

Every score has five independent two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR trace, Behavioral
proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates. The task explicitly prohibited all
execution gates, so Executed delivery gates is fixed at `0/2` for every card and every total is
provisional and capped at `8/10`. Behavioral `2/2` is reserved for source evidence with a
natural public-intent origin; structural assertions and manually injected timing are recorded
as supplemental evidence and do not receive full behavioral credit by themselves.

## Read-only snapshot and registration reconciliation

The direct modules are the executable authority and all ten report `coverage: "full"` with
`residual: []`. The generated snapshot was inspected but not edited. Relevant drift includes:

- BT21-071's snapshot omits the Link Draw/Trash effect and does not express the explicit host
  selected by the placement cost; the direct module carries both.
- BT21-073's snapshot omits the Link requirement/protection and has an unbound Link target;
  the direct module now binds the own stack source, self recipient, and self `whenLinked`
  watcher.
- BT21-074's snapshot models the placement protection as a broad opponent-effect immunity and
  omits the direct restriction/host binding; the direct module uses the return and
  De-Digivolve restrictions and binds the placement host.
- BT21-076's snapshot leaves the attack reduction as a separate replacement and scopes its
  scaling incorrectly; the direct module integrates both-trash reduction into the Digivolve
  action.
- BT21-077's snapshot omits the gained start-of-main attack effect and merges the deletion
  branches too broadly; the direct module separates exact Canoweissmon from the Gammamon-text
  level-4 branch.
- BT21-078's snapshot represents the Tamer-color level increase as a detached CostModifier and
  omits the full conditional/action ordering; the direct module has explicit deletion branches,
  mandatory Alliance, and optional attack.
- BT21-079's snapshot deletes only opposing Digimon, counts only the source controller's
  trash, and leaves the dynamic ceiling as a sibling modifier; the direct module fixes all
  three seams.
- BT21-080's snapshot lacks the trigger filter and puts the suspend cost on individual payload
  actions; the direct module applies one aborting cost to the combined Draw/memory payload.

Every direct module from BT21-071 through BT21-080 imports `registerIrCard` and ends with
exactly one `registerIrCard("BT21-0xx", compiled)` call. No duplicate `registerCard` call is
present. `apps/api/src/cards/BT21/index.ts` imports all ten modules in range.
