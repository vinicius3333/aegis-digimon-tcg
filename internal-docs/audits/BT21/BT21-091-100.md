## Outcome and authority

This audit covers all ten cards in strict ascending card-ID order. The
immutable catalog authority is the committed Git blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` for
`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`. The local rules KB, direct

The direct modules are the executable authority for these registered cards;
the generated snapshot `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was inspected

`86ec3ff83ca6458c357c14cd63bde5061c63f9d6`) and was not edited. The root
`BT21-STATIC-AUDIT.md` and `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were also
left unchanged.

One confirmed semantic defect was corrected. The specialized
`fromSelectedPermanentDigivolutionCards` branch of `runPlaceUnder` passed a
controller-selected bottom-to-top card order directly to the primitive, whose
bottom insertion uses `unshift`; with multiple cards this inverted the chosen
order. BT21-092 now reverses only bottom placements before the primitive call,
and its natural source assertion moves two Digimon cards and checks the exact
resulting stack order. This is the only distinct corrected card in this audit;
the other nine cards were already semantically aligned in their direct IR.

All ten direct modules expose `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and exactly
one `registerIrCard` registration. No audited module has a second
`registerCard` registration. The existing direct-module ports and prior source
assertions are retained; no duplicate legacy registration was added.

## Rules and KB evidence

The card-specific KB query returned no entries for BT21-091, BT21-094, or
BT21-100. The following available rulings were applied:

- BT21-092 Q4606-Q4607: the controller may choose the order of Digimon cards
  placed under a Tamer, and new cards go to the bottom when that Tamer already
  has cards.
- BT21-093 Q4608: Security effects take precedence in a simultaneous security
  check; the direct module keeps its Security deletion as a separate entry.
- BT21-095 Q4609-Q4614: zero security satisfies no face-up-security condition;
  the Main effect can still place this card as face-up security; face-up cards
  remain revealed and trigger their face-up security effects when checked;
  shuffling flips the revealed card down before the shuffle.
- BT21-096 Q4615-Q4620 and Q6022-Q6024: a Tamer treated as a Digimon remains
  both a Tamer and a Digimon; it receives the treated DP/effect behavior,
  rule-checks at 0 DP, can attack through this effect when Rush/can-attack
  permission is granted, and opponent Digimon-effect immunity does not block a
  treated Tamer's Tamer effect. The source grants the printed temporary
  Digimon treatment before the optional attack.
- BT21-097 Q4621 and Q4734: Delay cannot link a card without a Link requirement,
  and “on the field” includes the battle and breeding areas. The card's
  Appmon waiver consequently includes both Digimon and Tamers and the breeding
  area through the normal field matcher.
- BT21-098 Q4622-Q4624: “in its text” covers the printed name, traits, effects,
  inherited effects, Rule text, evolution requirements, DNA/DigiXros/Burst/App
  Fusion/Link/Assembly text; if the opponent has a Digimon one must be chosen;
  a deletion prevention means the effect did not delete. The Security filter
  is restricted to playable Digimon/Tamers while retaining the broad Vemmon
  text matcher.
- BT21-099 Q4625-Q4626: the same broad printed-text definition applies to
  Save, and a newly placed card goes to the bottom of an existing Tamer stack.

The general rules sections used for the remaining interpretations were
`§3-4-7-6/7/8` (breeding-area trigger and information boundaries), `§3-7`
(private/security information), `§4-3` (Digimon and inherited effects),

`§10` (Link), `§11` (attacks), `§13` (Security), `§15-12` (treated
