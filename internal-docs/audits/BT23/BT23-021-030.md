## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive
   registration, with the generated snapshot recorded only as comparison evidence.
3. **Behavioral proof** — observable source-test evidence, prioritizing natural
   card origins over direct timing or primitive injection.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — useful legal peers and evolution/link/inherited stack
   coverage.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch constraints.
