## Scope and scoring policy

This audit covers exactly BT23-011 through BT23-020 in ascending order. The
authoritative catalog text was read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. The local card rules KB was queried
exactly once for each card. For every card, the direct TypeScript module,
compiled IR and runtime seams, exclusive registration, source assertions,
legal peers and evolution/stack routes, BT23's index, and the read-only
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot were inspected.

The protected `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`,
`BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, and `BT23-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were not edited. Every direct
module in this range uses exclusive `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`
registration; no duplicate `registerCard` registration was added or retained.

Every card is scored on exactly five two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR
trace, Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates.
The delivery-gate component is fixed at 0/2 by task instruction, so every
result is provisional and capped at 8/10. Structural assertions and manually
injected timing or primitive calls are supplemental and do not receive full
behavioral credit where a natural originating event is feasible.

  before the specified play), Q5237 (activation is unavailable without an

- BT23-019: no card-specific entry.
- BT23-020: no card-specific entry.

The shared rules and engine seams establish the following audit constraints:
effects default to the battle area unless a card explicitly names another
zone; `countMatching` otherwise considers breeding-area permanents too;
slashes in card text are OR alternatives; `TrashDigivolution` supports a
single pool across multiple Digimon; delayed deletion resolves at the
opponent's turn end; and linking/suspension subtriggers can be scoped to the
event's actual host or suspended permanent.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT23-011 — Birdramon

- **Catalog/rules:** Red level-4 Giant Bird/CS, play cost 4, 4000 DP, with a
  red level-3 route for 2 and an alternate CS level-3 route for 2. On Play and
  When Digivolving delete one opposing Digimon at 4000 DP or less. Its
  inherited On Deletion effect may play a red or CS Tamer from hand for free.
- **IR trace:** `BT23-011.ts` has separate On Play and When Digivolving Delete
  actions with an inclusive 4000-DP ceiling. The inherited PlayWithoutCost
  filter is an OR between red and CS Tamer, from hand, optional, and free.
  Coverage is full with no residual. The module registers only through
  `registerIrCard("BT23-011", compiled)`.
- **Behavioral proof:** The source tests naturally play the card against exact
  4000-DP and 5000-DP boundaries and naturally digivolve through the off-color
  CS route. The inherited deletion effect is exercised through an explicit
  deletion primitive, so it is not full natural-origin coverage: 1/2.
- **Peer and stack proof:** The CS level-3 route is compared against a legal
  CS peer (`BT22-017`), and the source assertions cover red/CS Tamer matching,
  the nonmatching negative, optional refusal, and the source's inherited
  placement context.

### BT23-014 — Gallantmon

- **Catalog/rules:** Red level-6 Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/CS, play cost 11,
  11000 DP, with the alternate CS level-5 cost-3 route. On Play and When
  Digivolving, until the opponent's turn ends, opponent effects cannot play
  Digimon or Tamers from trash. On Play, When Digivolving, and When Attacking,
  delete one opposing Digimon at 8000 DP or less, adding 2000 to that maximum
  for each opposing Digimon and Tamer.
- **IR trace:** The direct module has separate RestrictPlay and Delete effects
  for each printed timing, with `byEffectOnly: true`, trash-only Digimon/Tamer
  restriction, and selectable resolution order. The three dynamic deletion
  filters now explicitly use `zone: "battleArea"`, preventing breeding-area
  Digimon from inflating the DP ceiling. The source test assertion was updated
  to lock this zone requirement. Coverage is full and registration is
  exclusive.
- **Correction:** The prior scaling filter counted matching opponent
  Digimon/Tamers without a zone. The shared matcher includes breeding-area
  permanents by default, while the card says “their Digimon and Tamers” in
  the battle-area play context. The established EX4-012 peer explicitly adds
  `zone: "battleArea"` for the same “Digimon in play” scaling shape, so the
  missing zone was a definite semantic mismatch. Corrected in commit
  `0b141ed34`.
- **Behavioral proof:** Source assertions cover the exact 12000-DP scaled
  boundary, a 13000-DP negative, all three timing declarations, the trash
  floodgate's effect-only/seat/zone behavior, and the alternate evolution.
  The timing and primitive restriction checks are explicit seams rather than
  full natural event flows, so behavioral credit is 1/2.
- **Peer and stack proof:** The alternate CS route is compared with the legal
  level-5 CS peer `BT22-023`; dynamic scaling is checked alongside an
  opponent Tamer, and the restriction includes both Digimon and Tamers while
  allowing your effect to play an opponent's trash card.

### BT23-019 — Gekomon

- **Catalog/rules:** Blue level-4 Amphibian/CS, play cost 4, 4000 DP, with a
  blue level-3 cost-2 route and alternate CS level-3 cost-2 route. On Play and

- **IR trace:** `BT23-019.ts` has both timings as `TrashDigivolution` with an

- **Correction:** The prior action omitted the scope, so it could only select
  two cards from one Digimon. The exact wording is clarified by the BT14-091
  peer as two total cards across any combination of opponent Digimon, and
  EX7-021 uses the same `scope: "acrossDigimon"` runtime action. Corrected in
  commit `2b1c06ea1`.
- **Behavioral proof:** Natural play proves the On Play path and exact two-card
  removal from a stacked opponent Digimon; the source also covers the
  one-card clamp. The newly corrected cross-host behavior and the
  When Digivolving timing are represented by source assertions/fixtures but
  were not executed, so behavioral credit is 1/2.
- **Peer and stack proof:** The CS level-3 route is tested through the legal
  `BT23-017` peer, while the stack fixture and EX7-021/BT14-091 comparisons

## Generated snapshot, index, and runtime checks

`apps/api/src/cards/BT23/index.ts` imports BT23-011 through BT23-020 in order.
All ten direct modules expose executable compiled IR through the exclusive
`registerIrCard` seam, and no direct module has a second legacy registration.
The interpreter seams were traced for Delete DP scaling, RestrictPlay,
same-name matching, Once Per Turn shared keys, linking, delayed deletion,
security placement, bottom-stack placement, and cross-Digimon
`TrashDigivolution` selection.

The read-only generated snapshot is not authoritative over the direct modules
for this audit and was not regenerated or edited. It is stale relative to the
direct implementations in several places: BT23-011's red/CS Tamer OR is
collapsed into an AND; BT23-012 applies the Sea Animal exclusion too broadly;
BT23-013 omits the full Sistermon branch, scoped same-name behavior, and
Huckmon DP gate; BT23-014 omits the explicit battle-area scaling filter;
BT23-016 omits the link effect; BT23-017 omits cost/result/restriction details;
BT23-019 omits the across-Digimon scope; and BT23-020 omits the self source
filter. BT23-015 relies on the runtime's self-source default for its security
placement, while BT23-018's generated shape is aligned for the inspected
clauses. None of these snapshot observations were “fixed” by editing the
protected JSON.
