### BT23-064 Bakemon — provisional 7/10 after reconciliation

- Catalog/rules: Purple level 4 Champion/Virus/Ghost/LIBERATOR, play cost 4,
  4000 DP, with Purple level 3 for 2. On Play and When Digivolving, the card
  says to delete one of your Digimon, then delete one opposing level 4 or lower
  Digimon; its inherited On Deletion gains 1 memory.
- Follow-up reconciliation: the initial mandatory-cost finding was overclaimed.
  CR §15-7-1 and Official Rule Manual `manual-0053` classify every printed
  “By X, Y” as an optional processing condition: the player may decline the
  condition, and `abortOnDecline: true` prevents the dependent deletion from
  resolving. Both entry Delete actions therefore restore `optional: true` while
  retaining the `deleteOwn` cost and abort behavior.
- Runtime/peer proof: `runAction` offers `action.optional` before paying a
  mandatory cost, which is the existing encoding of this optional By condition;
  `cost.optional` is the separate “decline payment while continuing the action”
  path. BT19-070 and BT16-081 remain structural By peers, while the conformance
  and manual authority resolve their optional-processing meaning. Direct On Play
  timing covers accepted and declined processing, while deletion-primitive proof
  covers the inherited effect; because natural entry origins remain feasible,
  Behavioral proof is 1/2. No catalog or registration gap remains.

### BT23-068 GranDracmon — provisional 7/10 after correction

- Catalog/rules: Purple level 6 Mega/Virus/Dark Animal/CS, play cost 13,
  13000 DP, with Purple level 5 for 5 and alternate level-5 Undead/CS for 4.
  At the start of your main phase or on deletion, one of your Digimon may
  digivolve into a level 6-or-lower Undead/Dark Animal card from trash for free;
  its When Digivolving plays a level 4-or-lower purple Digimon from trash; and
  its once-per-turn All Turns clause deletes all opposing lowest-level Digimon

- Initial finding and correction: the direct SubTrigger carried an unsupported
  `fromZone: "trash"` field. `SubTriggerAction` and `runSubTrigger` do not consume
  that field, so the watcher could fire for a hand-origin evolution. The watcher
  now uses the supported `fireCondition: { kind: "digivolvedFromZone", zone:
  "trash" }`, which is populated by the effect-driven digivolve seam and checked
  by the subtrigger runtime.
- Proof/peers: source scenarios cover free trash evolution, resulting When
  Digivolving play, lowest-level deletion, and alternate evolution. A new natural
  hand-origin negative verifies the opposing Digimon survives, but positive deletion
  begins from the deletion primitive and the entry play is directly timed. Behavioral
  proof is therefore 1/2. Q5336 confirms that evolving into this card from trash also
  triggers the watcher. The read-only snapshot omitted the provenance gate.
- Correction commit: `aac5d5787`.
