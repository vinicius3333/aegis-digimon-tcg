## Scope and evidence policy

This report covers BT23-071 through BT23-080 in ascending order. Catalog facts were read from the immutable `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; the working tree catalog and effects snapshot were not treated as authority. The local rules knowledge base was queried exactly once per card with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <card-id>`.

The audit traced each direct TypeScript module, compiled IR, interpreter/runtime consumer, `registerIrCard` registration, BT23 index import, focused source assertions, legal peers, representative stacks, and the read-only `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot. All ten modules register executable behavior exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no duplicate `registerCard` registration was introduced or retained.

Scores use the required five columns, each worth two points. `Executed delivery gates` is intentionally `0/2` for every card because the task prohibited tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check`. The behavioral column records source-level focused assertions and natural-origin cases where present; those assertions were not executed under the prohibition, so every total is provisional and no card is claimed as 10/10.

Aggregate score: BT23-071–BT23-080 — 70/100 points, or 7/10 per card. Behavioral proof is 1/2 throughout because each card still relies on direct timing or primitive injection for at least one feasible natural origin. The withheld execution-gate column accounts for another 20 points across the range.
