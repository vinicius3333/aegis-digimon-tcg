`apps/api/src/cards/BT23/index.ts:32-41`. Every audited module registers executable

where the generated snapshot is stale or lossy, and the snapshot was not edited.

## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive
   registration, with generated IR recorded only as comparison evidence.
3. **Behavioral proof** — observable source-test evidence, prioritizing public or
   natural card origins over direct timing and primitive injection.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — legal peer comparisons and realistic evolution/link/
   inherited stack evidence where applicable.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch boundary.
