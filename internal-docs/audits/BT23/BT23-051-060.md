## Scope and authority

This audit covers BT23-051 through BT23-060 in ascending card-ID order. The immutable catalog authority was read from Git blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; the current `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` hashes to that blob. The direct TypeScript modules under `apps/api/src/cards/BT23`, their focused source assertions, compiled IR registration path, interpreter/runtime consumers, legal peers and representative stacks, the BT23 index, and the read-only snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were inspected.

The local rules knowledge base was queried exactly once per card with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <cardId>`. BT23-051, BT23-052, BT23-053, BT23-054, and BT23-058 returned no card-specific entries. The relevant entries were Q5320 for BT23-055 (the trash cost is an Option placed in the battle area by an effect), Q5321 for BT23-056 (an unaffected Digimon may receive the delayed attack effect but cannot trigger it while unaffected), Q5322 for BT23-057 (all three named trash cards are required for the reduction), Q5323–Q5329 for BT23-059 (effect-placed Option scope, immunity/choice/timing behavior), and Q5330–Q5331 for BT23-060 (once-per-turn activation is consumed even without a qualifying security card; a borrowed On Play effect resolves its mandatory “by” condition).

The snapshot and `BT23-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were treated as read-only; neither was edited. Every audited module is imported by the BT23 index and registers executable behavior exactly once through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`, with no duplicate `registerCard` registration.

Tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check` were prohibited by the dispatch and were not run. The Executed delivery gates component is therefore `0/2` for every card, and all scores below are provisional with a maximum of `8/10`.

## Scoring model

Each card receives exactly five independent two-point components:

1. Catalog/rules evidence
2. IR trace and runtime seam
3. Behavioral proof in source
4. Peer and legal-stack proof
5. Executed delivery gates

Structural assertions and manually injected timing/events/primitives are recorded as useful source evidence but do not receive full Behavioral credit where a natural card-origin path is feasible. Source tests were inspected but not executed in this dispatch.

## Runtime and registration findings

The direct card modules all route through the compiled interpreter registry. The inspected runtime seams cover the range's future events (`whenSuspended`, `whenSecurityBattleEnded`, `whenOptionPlayed`, `startOfYourMainPhase`, `whenAttackTargetSwitched`, `whenOptionInBattleAreaTrashed`), static restrictions and keywords, leave-play replacements, stack operations, token creation, play-cost scaling, and borrowed security effects.

The Option-placement primitive marks an Option permanent as effect-placed and emits `whenOptionPlayed`; the trash primitive emits `whenOptionInBattleAreaTrashed` only after an Option permanent actually moves to trash. The permanent matcher treats a battle-area Option as satisfying `placedInBattleAreaByEffect` under the engine's invariant that Options reach the battle area through an effect-placement path. The new BT23-059 filter makes the Q5323 restriction explicit in the executable IR while retaining the printed no-controller restriction.

The security battle consumer fires `whenSecurityBattleEnded` after the attacker has battled the revealed security card. The start-of-main-phase bus, attack-target-switch bus, once-per-turn ledger, leave-play replacement consumer, exact-count return cost, `playCostCeiling` scaling, Hinukamuy token registry, and `ActivateForeignEffect` security consumer were each traced to their production call sites. The reducer registry explicitly includes BT23-057 for its self-play reduction.

The generated snapshot is stale or weaker than several direct modules. In particular, it omits BT23-052's linked behavior, retains an older BT23-056 token-effect shape instead of the delayed trigger, represents BT23-057's ceiling as separate legacy actions, leaves BT23-058 partially raw, and retains the older BT23-059 generic Option cost/All Turns shape. It was consulted for comparison only and was not changed or allowed to override the direct modules.
