## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive
   registration, with generated IR recorded only as comparison evidence.
3. **Behavioral proof** — observable source-test evidence, prioritizing public or
   natural card origins over direct timing and event injection.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — legal peer comparisons and realistic evolution,
   link, inherited, or Option-stack evidence where applicable.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch boundary.

## Runtime trace and CR 15-7-1 recheck

The assigned catalog records contain exactly one printed lowercase “by” clause:
BT23-097's `[Trash] [Your Turn]` clause, “by returning this card to the bottom
of the deck, activate this card's [Main] effects.” The direct IR at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-097.ts:27-42` represents it as an `ActivateMain`
action with a return-to-deck-bottom cost, `optional: true`, and
`abortOnDecline: true`; the enclosing trigger is `isFromTrash: true`. This
matches CR 15-7-1: `runAction`'s action-level optional decision models declining
the processing condition, the cost is paid only after acceptance, and
`abortOnDecline` stops the remaining effect sequence. The added assertion at
`BT23-097.test.ts:49-70` verifies that declining leaves the card in trash and
does not delete the opponent's target.

No other assigned catalog effect contains a “By X, Y” processing condition.
The other `optional: true` actions are ordinary “may” payloads or Delay
payloads: BT23-093 Link, BT23-098 play/digivolve, BT23-099 Sistermon play, and
BT23-100 Tamer/Security play. Their optionality and tail placement were
rechecked against the printed ordering; no additional correction was definite.

The relevant runtime consumers were traced as follows:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts` handles
  action-level optional decisions and returns the abort signal used by ordered
  effect resolution.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` installs
  exclusive IR modules, routes Option Main/Security clauses, and applies
  intrinsic Delay turn-entry and trash-cost gates to continuous SubTriggers.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts` resolves
  event subjects, turn scope, Delay payment, and nested actions.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:70-90`
  supports nested `and` conjunctions; `nameOrTrait` itself remains an OR union
  within one filter.
- `runDigivolve` uses loose candidate matching and legal evolution checks, so
  an unsupported IR property is not a harmless annotation: it broadens the
  candidate pool unless expressed through a supported predicate.
- The shared `runLink`, restriction, De-Digivolve, Return, and
  `PlaceInBattleAreaSelf` consumers cover the corresponding Option payloads.

The generated snapshot was inspected read-only. It remains mostly equivalent
for this range, with historical lossy representations such as generic
`field` static conditions and simplified restrictions. Its BT23-098 record still
contains the unsupported `allNameOrTraits` shape; that file was intentionally
not edited, and the direct module now supplies the supported runtime predicate.
