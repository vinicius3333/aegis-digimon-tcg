### Follow-up optional-condition reconciliation

The first audit's removal of `optional: true` from BT23-045 and BT23-046 was overclaimed. The
local conformance authority in `manual-03-effect-rules-and-keywords.test.ts` states that “By
doing” conditions are player-MAY: the player may perform the named cost, and declining it skips
the effect's later processing. Chapter 15-02 §15-7-1 likewise identifies “by X, Y” as an
optional processing condition; §15-7-2 says that when it is not executed, the processing after
it cannot be executed.

The captured Q5331 question asks whether, when a face-up BT23-045 is used to activate that
card's [On Play] effect from BT23-060's [When Attacking] effect, the player can choose not to
process the “by” condition. Its answer is a context-specific override: the borrowed activation
must process the condition; an eligible [Royal Base]/[Zaxon] card in trash must be used before
hand becomes the fallback. This does not change ordinary BT23-045 resolution, so its three By
actions correctly retain `optional: true` and `abortOnDecline: true`. The forced borrowed path
belongs to BT23-060 and is tracked in the BT23-051–060 audit.

The captured Q5311 question asks whether BT23-046's [On Play]/[When Digivolving] cost may
suspend either the player's or opponent's Digimon/Tamer. Its exact answer is: “Yes, either can
be suspended.” It does not make activation or payment mandatory. Runtime keeps the distinction
explicit: `action.optional` asks whether to execute the whole By clause; once accepted,
`cost.optional` being unset makes payment mandatory, and `abortOnDecline: true` skips the
remaining payload if the condition is declined or cannot be paid. BT23-044 and comparable
printed By-cost clauses in BT23-072 and BT23-087 follow the same optional-processing model.
