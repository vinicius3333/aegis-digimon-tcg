## Registration and runtime findings

All ten direct modules are compiled IR modules and register their executable behavior
through exactly one `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` call. No target module retains a
second `registerCard` registration. The BT23 index imports all ten cards in its normal
ascending index, and runtime tracing confirmed that trigger timing, source scope,
inherited scope, link scope, once-per-turn keys, security-instance matching, stack
movement, and option placement are interpreted by the compiled runtime.

The read-only `effects.json` snapshot contains historical forms that are less precise
than the direct modules for several cards: raw trait predicates for BT23-001/002, a
broad Option attack source for BT23-003, unbound independent grants for BT23-004, an
immediate security play for BT23-007, a split restack/reduced-play representation for
BT23-008, a missing linked end-turn attack for BT23-009, and an immediate rather than
end-of-battle security play for BT23-010. The direct modules contain the semantically
required forms; the snapshot was deliberately left untouched.

The initial range pass found no definite semantic mismatch. A later CR §15-7-1
cross-sweep found one card-logic gap: BT23-008 combined an optional “By” processing
condition with an independently optional play, but its cost lacked the separate
`optional: true` decision. Three proof tests were also strengthened to
use natural public game paths for the important behavior: BT23-003 now plays a real
CS Option, BT23-004 deletes its source through a natural opponent attack, and BT23-009
reaches its linked attack through natural turn progression. The pre-existing semantic
correction history includes `aa88c50f7`, `4e32eb471`, `2f6d9ff7c`, `38a9b6023`,
`526b698f2`, `7477c7189`, and `31bbbbecd`; the prior audit commits for the remaining
cards were also reviewed as historical context.

## Card findings

### BT23-001 Flickmon

- Catalog/rules: The immutable catalog identifies a Blue level-2 Digi-Egg with the
  Appmon, Game, and Flick traits and an inherited once-per-turn When Attacking Draw 1
  effect conditional on the Appmon trait.
- IR trace: The direct module uses `WhenAttacking`, `selfHasTrait(Appmon)`, Draw 1,
  `isInherited`, and `OncePerTurn`, with `coverage: "full"` and no residual clauses.
  Runtime tracing confirms the condition is evaluated on the inherited source carrier,
  not as an unrestricted raw predicate.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover a legal Appmon carrier, refusal outside
  the Appmon trait, once-per-turn repetition, and independent tracking for two Flickmon
  sources. The dynamic assertions inspect draw counts and source state.
- Peer and stack proof: The fixtures place Flickmon under valid Digimon carriers and
  compare trait-matching and nonmatching peers, while the independent-source case
  checks source identity rather than collapsing all copies into one turn key.
- No new semantic correction was required in this dispatch.

### BT23-002 Yokomon

- Catalog/rules: The immutable catalog identifies a Green level-2 Digi-Egg with Bulb
  and CS traits and an inherited once-per-turn When Attacking Draw 1 effect conditional
  on the CS trait.
- IR trace: The direct module uses `WhenAttacking`, `selfHasTrait(CS)`, Draw 1,
  `isInherited`, and `OncePerTurn`, with full coverage and no residual clauses.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover a legal CS carrier, a non-CS carrier, the
  once-per-turn limit, and independent use by separate Yokomon sources; the checks
  observe actual hand/draw changes.
- Peer and stack proof: The test fixtures use legal inherited hosts and contrasting
  trait peers, and validate that separate stacked sources retain separate once-per-turn
  identity.
- No new semantic correction was required in this dispatch.

### BT23-003 Motimon

- Catalog/rules: The immutable catalog identifies a Black level-2 Digi-Egg with Lesser
  and CS traits whose inherited effect is a once-per-turn Your Turn reaction to one of
  the controller's CS Option cards being placed in the battle area, allowing this
  Digimon to attack.
- IR trace: The direct module uses a Your Turn inherited `SubTrigger` for
  `whenOptionPlayed`, filters to the controller's Option cards with the CS trait, and
  attacks the self reference optionally. The runtime event is emitted by natural
  Option placement, and the source's once-per-turn key and controller scope are
  preserved; coverage is full with no residual clauses.
- Behavioral proof: The positive test now uses the public `playCard` intent with
  `useAs: "option"` and BT23-100, rather than directly injecting an Option permanent.
  It checks the first natural trigger, unsuspends only as secondary test setup, checks
  the second Option does not retrigger, and retains non-CS, opponent-controller, and
  declined-attack cases.
- Peer and stack proof: BT22-043 provides a legal CS host, BT23-100 is the legal CS
  Option peer whose normal play path emits `whenOptionPlayed`, and Motimon remains an
  inherited stacked source. The BT23 index and the explicit BT23-100 test import ensure
  both modules are registered for the natural scenario.
- No new semantic card correction was required; proof was strengthened in commit
  `b9d0a48d4`.

### BT23-004 DemiMeramon

- Catalog/rules: The immutable catalog identifies a Purple level-2 Digi-Egg with Flame
  and LIBERATOR traits. On deletion, one friendly Ghost Digimon gains Blocker and
  Retaliation until the opponent's turn ends.
- IR trace: The direct module selects exactly one friendly Ghost and binds it, then
  grants both keywords from that same selection reference with
  `untilOpponentTurnEnd`. It is inherited, full coverage, and residual-free. Runtime
  duration tracking and selection references were traced directly.
- Behavioral proof: The positive test now causes the source to be deleted by a natural
  opponent attack and observes both keywords on only the selected Ghost, not the other
  friendly Ghost, a non-Ghost, or the opponent's Ghost. It then runs the opponent turn
  and verifies both grants expire; the no-target case remains covered.
- Peer and stack proof: The source is a valid inherited stack under a Digimon, with
  friendly Ghost and non-Ghost peers plus an opposing Ghost. The attack uses a legal
  opponent Digimon and the source is suspended so normal battle deletion can occur.
- No new semantic card correction was required; proof was strengthened in commit
  `a1d2cc705`.

  `wouldDigivolve` event whose destination is Reptile or Dragonkin and reduces the
  selected cost by 1. The inherited DP effect is Your Turn scoped, full, and

  cost interaction required by Q5215/Q5586.
- Behavioral proof: Existing source assertions cover an allowed trait, a disallowed
  evolution, breeding-area non-triggering (Q5215), the Q5586 effect-driven cost
  override, controller-turn DP, and the distinction between a main source and an
  inherited source.
- Peer and stack proof: The tests use legal Reptile/Dragonkin and nonmatching evolution
  peers, a self-evolution effect-driven cost path, and separate top-card/inherited
  source contexts. Breeding-area isolation is explicitly asserted.
- No new semantic correction was required in this dispatch.

- No new semantic correction was required in this dispatch.

### BT23-008 Greymon

- Catalog/rules: The immutable catalog identifies a Red level-4 Champion CS Dinosaur
  with Raid, the printed once-per-turn Main stack-restack and reduced play effect, a
  +2000 inherited Your Turn effect, and Agumon/CS alternate evolution recipes. Q5216
  through Q5219 govern the stack and rule-check edge cases.
- IR trace: The direct module places the top card as the bottom evolution card, then
  plays a named Gabumon or Nokia from hand at two less cost. The cost and action are
  independently optional, while `abortOnDecline` prevents the payload when the “By”
  condition is refused. Runtime tracing confirms that the effect cannot
  start without an evolution card, exposes and checks the correct card, and applies the
  rule deletion for a 0-DP exposed source. Coverage is full and residual-free.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover the normal reduced play (Q5216), missing
  evolution-card refusal (Q5217), exposed Digi-Egg behavior (Q5218), exposed 0-DP
  deletion (Q5219), optional decline, both alternate evolution boundaries, Raid, and
  the inherited DP turn scope.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures use legal Gabumon/Nokia targets, real evolution-card
  stacks, a Digi-Egg-only stack, a 1000-DP source, and nonmatching evolution peers.
  Stack order and source-card movement are checked after the effect.
- Follow-up correction `a1fb86255` separated the optional restack processing
  condition from the independently optional play.

### BT23-009 Coachmon

- Catalog/rules: The immutable catalog identifies a Red level-4 Sup./Appmon whose
  Your Turn once-per-turn link trigger gives one friendly Digimon +4000 DP for the
  turn, and whose linked End of Your Turn once-per-turn effect may attack a player.
  Link DP is 3000 and the link requirement is Appmon for cost 2.
- IR trace: The direct module scopes the first subtrigger to a linked self source and
  the second effect to a linked self source at EndOfYourTurn; the attack is optional,
  player-targeted, and retains its timing-window behavior. Link cost and link DP are
  represented separately, with full coverage and no residual clauses.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover linking and exact DP contributions,
  friendly-target selection, legal cost, and refusal of an invalid host. The positive
  end-turn test now reaches the attack through `runTurn(0)` and observes a security
  change, while the decline test uses the same natural turn boundary and verifies no
  suspension or security check.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures use an Appmon host and a non-Appmon rejection peer,
  a linked Coachmon source, friendly Digimon selection, and a player with security for
  the optional attack. Link state and source identity are asserted.
- No new semantic card correction was required; proof was strengthened in commit
  `58c7d2a95`.
