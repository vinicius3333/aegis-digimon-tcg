## BT23-101 — Hudiemon

### Catalog and rules

- The catalog identifies a Green/Yellow level-4 Digimon with play cost 7, 7000 DP, Champion
  form, Free attribute, and Insectoid/Hudie/CS traits. It has Green and Yellow level-3
  evolution costs of 5.
- The printed effects are Alliance; an On Play/When Digivolving optional free play of one
  play-cost-5-or-lower CS card from hand followed by a mandatory one-opponent-Digimon
  `-3000 DP` per own Hudie Digimon for the turn; and a once-per-turn When Attacking return of
  one own CS Tamer to activate one On Play effect.
- The alternate evolution paths are level 3 with CS for 4, or exact Erika Mishima for 3 while
  controlling at least four Hudie Tamers.
- KB Q5257/Q5319 confirm simultaneous Alliance and When Attacking timing and that the derived
  On Play effect resolves before the next Alliance decision; Q5389 preserves summoning sickness
  after evolving from a Tamer played that turn. Q5571 confirms that the DP reduction chooses one
  opponent Digimon, Q5572 defers a played card's On Play until the current effect completes, and
  Q5573 defers zero-DP deletion until that processing completes. Q6708–Q6712 cover the special
  Tamer evolution semantics and inherited/security text handling.

### IR and runtime trace

- The direct module is [BT23-101.ts](../../../apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-101.ts:8) and declares
  `coverage: "full"` with `residual: []`; it registers only at line 173 through
  `registerIrCard("BT23-101", compiled)`.
- The Static Alliance marker is consumed by the shared keyword/combat layer. Both entry timings
  use `PlayWithoutCost` from hand, with an inclusive play-cost-5 ceiling, own-controller CS
  trait matching, and an optional choice. The following `ModifyDP` is mandatory, targets exactly
  one opposing Digimon, uses `-3000`, lasts for the turn, and scales by each own Hudie Digimon.
- `countMatching` and `ModifyDP` are wired in the interpreter. Scale resolution occurs immediately
  before the DP action, so a card played by the preceding action is already a permanent when the
  Hudie count is calculated. The default field scan includes battle and breeding permanents,
  matching the shared condition/scaling convention.
- The When Attacking action is a once-per-turn `ReactivateEffect` with `fromTrigger: "OnPlay"`
  and `count: 1`. Its optional cost returns one own CS Tamer to hand and aborts the reactivation
  when declined or unpaid. The meta interpreter resolves the directly registered module's On Play
  effect, so it does not depend on the stale generated snapshot spelling.
- The read-only snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:83031–83138` has the same
  broad shape, but its historical `fromTrigger: "On Play"` at line 83107 is not the runtime
  spelling. It remains unmodified as required; `runtimeCompiledCard` prefers the registered direct
  module.

## BT23-102 — Mastemon

### Catalog and rules

- The catalog identifies a Yellow/Purple level-6 Digimon with play cost 13, 13000 DP, Mega form,
  Vaccine attribute, and Angel/CS traits. It has Yellow and Purple level-5 evolution costs of 5.
- The printed effects are Barrier; Partition (Angewomon & LadyDevimon); a When Digivolving
  optional free play of one level-5-or-lower yellow or purple card from hand or trash followed by
  a conditional trim of both security stacks to three when the stack has two or more same-level
  cards; and an All Turns once-per-turn watcher for security-stack removal that may place one
  Digimon at the bottom of security.
- KB Q5390 gives Security-effect priority over other simultaneous security-removal triggers.
  Q5391 confirms that either the owner's or opponent's Digimon can be chosen for the All Turns
  placement, and Q5392 confirms that placing Mastemon itself does not trigger Partition.

### IR and runtime trace

- The direct module is [BT23-102.ts](../../../apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-102.ts:22) with
  `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and only
  `registerIrCard("BT23-102", compiled)` at line 107. The BT23 index imports it at
  [index.ts](../../../apps/api/src/cards/BT23/index.ts:103).
- Barrier and the exact Partition specifier are static keyword records. The shared keyword layer
  recognizes both markers, and its Partition parser resolves the catalog's bracketed Angewomon
  and LadyDevimon names. The deletion/replay primitive therefore has a live peer implementation
  for the keyword rather than an unparsed residual.
- The When Digivolving play action targets own Digimon, Yellow or Purple, level at most 5, from
  hand or trash, without paying the cost, and is optional. The target explicitly now includes
  `kind: ["Digimon"]` at [BT23-102.ts](../../../apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-102.ts:48). This is
  necessary because level-bearing Digi-Eggs are permanent kinds, but have no play cost and are
  not Digimon cards; the interpreter's `playInstances` accepts Digi-Egg permanents, so color and
  level alone were too broad. The local ruling Q843 says a Digi-Egg cannot be played by an effect
  requiring a play cost, and Q900 states that Digi-Egg cards are not Digimon cards.
- The following `SecurityManipulation` uses `trashTop`, `bothPlayers: true`, `leaveCount: 3`, and
  the structured `selfDigivolutionStackHasSameLevelPair` condition. The condition checks the
  source's complete stack, including its current top card, and the both-player operation computes
  each seat's independent amount to leave exactly three.
- The All Turns effect installs a `whenSecurityRemoved` SubTrigger with once-per-turn frequency.
  Its optional `addBottom` source uses `controller: "any"` and an `isDigimon` filter, allowing a
  Digimon from either player's board as ruled by Q5391. The security-removal gate interprets
  `controller: "any"` as either removed security stack; the add-bottom primitive moves the selected
  physical top card to the activating player's bottom security, which is the behavior asserted by
  the focused source scenario.
- The read-only generated snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:83140–83204`
  mirrors the prior broad play filter but omits the corrected Digimon-kind restriction. It was not
  edited. The direct registered module is the executable authority.

### Behavioral proof and limitation

The focused assertions in [BT23-102.test.ts](../../../apps/api/src/cards/BT23/BT23-102.test.ts:8)
cover catalog/keywords/coverage, the exact free-play kind/level/color/source filter, same-level
condition, both-player trim, either-player placement source, and once-per-turn shape. Observable
tests manually fire When Digivolving with a realistic two-Mastemon stack, free-play a level-5
Angewomon, and trim 5/4 security to 3/3; a negative fixture preserves security and field state
for level-6/non-Yellow-Purple candidates and an absent same-level pair. A separate manually fired
security-removal SubTrigger moves an opposing Digimon to the bottom and proves the second trigger
does not repeat in the same turn. This earns 1/2: the primitives and positive/negative states are
observable, but neither a natural digivolution nor a natural security-removal event was executed
in this dispatch. Manual timing/sub-trigger injection is supplemental only, per the audit rule.

### Peer and stack proof

The direct positive/negative stack fixtures prove that the condition must count the current top
card as well as cards underneath. The shared same-level predicate is covered by its BT22-031 peer
ruling, while the Partition parser and deletion replay are exercised by the shared
`executePartition` mechanism. EX8-028 and EX9-056 provide peer security-placement implementations
for either-player Digimon sources. The BT23-031 Angewomon and BT23-067 LadyDevimon records are
catalog-valid named Partition materials. These sources support 2/2 for peer and stack proof;
natural execution remains a delivery limitation.

### Correction

One genuine semantic correction was made:

1. Added `kind: ["Digimon"]` to the When Digivolving `PlayWithoutCost` filter and added a
   source-level assertion in BT23-102.test.ts. Without this restriction, a Yellow/Purple Digi-Egg
   with a level at or below 5 could enter the candidate pool because `playInstances` treats
   Digi-Eggs as field permanents. The printed “card” is constrained by its level/color and is
   being played as a Digimon card; Q843/Q900 establish the no-play-cost/non-Digimon boundary.
