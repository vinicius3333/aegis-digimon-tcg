## BT15-021 — Gomamon (X Antibody)

### Evidence

- Catalog: Blue level 3 Digimon, play cost 3, 1000 DP, Vaccine, `Sea Beast` /
  `X Antibody`; normal evolution is Blue level 2 for 0. Printed effects are
  `[On Play] [When Digivolving]` reveal top 3, add 1 card with
  `Sea Beast`/`Plesiosaur`/`Beastkin`/`X Antibody`, and bottom the rest; the
  inherited `[When Attacking] [Once Per Turn]` effect restricts one opposing
  Digimon with no more digivolution cards than this Digimon until the end of
  the opponent's turn.
- Local KB query: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT15-021` returned Q2505. Q2505
  confirms the comparison is snapshotted when the inherited effect activates;
  a later increase in the target's digivolution cards does not restore its
  ability to attack during the duration.
- Direct module: `apps/api/src/cards/BT15/BT15-021.ts` contains two exact
  `RevealAdd` actions (`OnPlay`, `WhenDigivolving`) and one inherited,
  once-per-turn `Restrict` action with
  `digivolutionCardsCompareToSource: "lte"`, opponent Digimon targeting, and
  `untilOpponentTurnEnd`. It has exactly one `registerIrCard("BT15-021", ...)`
  and no `registerCard` duplicate.
- Shared seams read: `runRevealAdd` resolves the source owner's top three,
  selects a bounded matching card, returns the remainder to deck bottom, and
  records the hand addition; the canonical permanent matcher compares stack
  lengths to the live source at activation; registration maps inherited
  `OncePerTurn` to the per-effect limit; continuous restrictions feed attack
  legality and are swept at the opponent's turn end.
- Peers/stack: `BT15-020` and `BT15-024` provide nearby blue Gabumon-family
  level routes and inherited attack behavior; `BT10-022` is the Plesiosaur
  trait peer and `BT1-030` is the Sea Beast peer. The source test includes
  `BT1-009` as a nonmatching card, checks equal/fewer/more opposing stack
  counts, and now drives On Play through `playCard` plus When Digivolving
  through a legal Blue level-2 `BT15-002` breeding stack.

### Corrections and proof

The executable IR was already faithful and needed no production change. The
colocated source proof was strengthened to assert the catalog identity and
evolution route, use the public play intent for On Play, and use a public

  card was played by an effect, restricting one opposing Digimon from
  attacking until the end of its owner's turn. The inherited reminder text
  grants Jamming (cannot be deleted in battles against Security Digimon).
- Local KB query: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT15-022` returned no
  knowledge-base entries; no local ruling, erratum, or restriction was
  exposed.
- Direct module: `apps/api/src/cards/BT15/BT15-022.ts` has one `OnPlay`
  `Restrict` with opponent Digimon targeting, `attack` restriction,
  `untilOpponentTurnEnd` duration, and `triggerEnteredByEffect` condition,
  plus one inherited `Static` Jamming keyword effect. It has exactly one
  `registerIrCard("BT15-022", ...)` and no `registerCard` duplicate.

  accepts that marker only for entry windows; `Restrict` installs a continuous
  attack prohibition consumed by attack legality and duration sweeps; inherited
  Static keywords are projected from the evolution stack; Jamming is consumed
  by the Security battle deletion path.
- Peers/stack: `BT15-021` and `BT15-024` are adjacent blue level routes, while
  `BT14-009` provides an effect-play peer. The source proof now uses the public
  effect-play verb for the positive condition and a normal `playCard` intent
  for the negative condition. It also evolves `BT15-002` → BT15-022 →
  BT15-023 through public intents, then proves inherited Jamming in a Security
  battle; the opposing-Digimon battle negative remains covered.

### Corrections and proof

timing injection with a public manual play, and add a legal multi-step blue
