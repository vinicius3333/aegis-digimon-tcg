## Method and gate status

For every card, the immutable catalog record was read directly from the catalog blob, followed by a separate local query with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>`. The direct TypeScript module, colocated test, shared IR/interpreter/runtime primitives, useful peers, and applicable legal evolution/source-stack shapes were inspected before the card was finished. Every audited module registers executable behavior exclusively with `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and has no duplicate `registerCard` registration.

The read-only `BT15-STATIC-AUDIT.md` and `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were not modified. Per the audit brief, no tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser, focused, mechanism, collection, or `git diff --check` gate was run. Structural assertions and manual timing injection are not counted as full behavioral proof where a natural public origin is feasible; the added natural behavior cases are source evidence only because execution gates were prohibited.

The fixed `Executed gates` score is `0/2` for every card. Gate execution is intentionally deferred for this campaign and is not an integration action. All results below are therefore provisional and intentionally capped at `8/10`.

## BT15-091 — Wolf of Friendship — 8/10 provisional

### Catalog, rules, and KB

The immutable record identifies a blue level-4 Option costing 4. Its Main effect places a Gabumon and a WereGarurumon from trash under one of your Gabumon, then may digivolve that Gabumon into MetalGarurumon from hand without paying its cost or meeting requirements. Its Security effect may play a Gabumon from hand or trash, then adds itself to hand. The separate local KB query returned Q2589: choosing not to perform the MetalGarurumon digivolution does not prevent the two trash placements.

### IR, behavior, peers, and correction

`apps/api/src/cards/BT15/BT15-091.ts` now represents the two trash placements as one `CostGatedBlock` compound cost. The first placement binds the selected Gabumon host; the second targets that bound host, and only then does the optional free MetalGarurumon digivolution resolve. The Security branch remains the optional Gabumon play plus self hand return, and the module has exactly one `registerIrCard("BT15-091", compiled)` registration.

The shared compound-cost payment, `bindHostAs`/`boundRef` host resolution, free digivolution, and selection-reference consumers were inspected. BT25-096 and BT26-098 were compared for the same material-first `CostGatedBlock` shape. `BT15-091.test.ts` now drives natural completion from BT15-020 Gabumon with BT15-024 Garurumon and BT15-026 WereGarurumon in trash, and a natural incomplete-material case that leaves the MetalGarurumon in hand.

The production correction fixed the prior sequencing hole: an optional digivolution action had made the first two material payments individually optional and failed to bind the host across them. Correction commit: `0d6ea26f7`. Catalog/rules 2/2; IR trace 2/2; Behavioral proof 2/2; Peer/stack proof 2/2; Executed gates 0/2.
