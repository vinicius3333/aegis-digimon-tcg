# BT15 card-by-card audit: BT15-071 through BT15-080

## Audit frame

This report covers exactly BT15-071 through BT15-080 in strict ascending
card-ID order. The audit started from branch `audit-bt-card-by-card`
at `461467a02`. Each card was independently read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`, followed by a separate local
`node tools/kb/query.mjs card <ID>` query, direct TypeScript module and test
review, shared IR/runtime primitive review, useful peer comparison, and legal
evolution or source-stack review where applicable.

All ten direct modules register executable behavior exclusively through one
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` call; no duplicate `registerCard` calls were
added or retained. `BT15-STATIC-AUDIT.md` and
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were not modified.

## Scoring and execution boundary

Each card receives exactly five two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR trace,
Behavioral proof, Peer/stack proof, and Executed gates. Executed gates is fixed
at `0/2`: focused, mechanism, collection, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser,
and `git diff --check` gates were prohibited and were not run. The behavioral
notes below are source/runtime natural-path evidence; structural assertions are
not presented as executed behavior, and no manual timing injection is used as
a substitute for a feasible natural origin. Therefore every result is the
requested **8/10 provisional**.

## Card findings

### BT15-071 — Loogamon

- Catalog/rules: Purple/Red Level 3 Rookie Virus Dark Animal/X Antibody/SoC,
  play cost 3, 2000 DP; When Attacking, trash one hand card to delete one
  opposing Digimon at 3000 DP or less, then draw one if a Tamer with SoC is in
  this Digimon's digivolution cards; inherited End of Attack once per turn,
  gain one memory when the opponent has one or more memory.
- KB: Q2558 confirms that the draw cannot activate without performing the
  trash-cost action; Q2559 confirms that paying the trash cost still activates
  the draw even when no opposing Digimon is eligible.
- IR/runtime: the Delete action now carries `allowCostWithoutTarget: true`, so
  the shared action preflight can pay the hand-trash cost before finding no
  eligible deletion target. The draw condition is restricted to a `Tamer`
  carrying the SoC trait, and the inherited effect retains its opponent-memory
  gate and once-per-turn identity.
- Behavioral/peer proof: the natural When Attacking path is cost first, then
  conditional draw; the shared stack matcher checks both the Tamer kind and SoC
  trait. BT16-058 is the useful same-wording peer for the Tamer-qualified SoC
  stack shape, while the inherited effect remains an End-of-Attack stack-card
  effect.
- Corrections: `d7443c6be` fixed the no-target processing-cost path;
  `4f7db3ef4` tightened the SoC condition to Tamer cards. Both changes are
  additive commits.

  overflow memory 3.

  choosing another hand card, and the normal Counter window—not a manually

- Corrections: `5bf0903e7` added the executable hand Counter Digivolve and the
  shared hand-Counter discovery/residency seam; additive follow-up `5e3f4851c`

### BT15-078 — WaruSeadramon

- Catalog/rules: Purple Level 5 Ultimate Virus Aquatic, play cost 8, 8000 DP;
  All Turns once per turn, when an effect plays an opponent's Digimon, all of
  that opponent's Digimon gain `[On Deletion] Lose 1 memory` until the end of
  their turn; When Attacking, the opponent plays a Level 4-or-lower Digimon from
  trash suspended without paying, its On Play effects do not activate, and the
  attacker may switch to it; inherited Piercing.
- KB: Q2570 says no redirect is possible when no eligible trash card exists;
  Q2571 excludes a breeding-area effect play from the All Turns trigger; Q2572
  confirms the trigger does fire when the controller's own effect plays an
  opponent's Digimon.
- IR/runtime: the once-per-turn `whenPlayed` watcher requires an opponent
  Digimon in the battle area and `byEffect: true`, then grants the opponent
  deletion-memory loss through the opponent-turn-end duration. The attack action
  plays one opposing Level 4-or-lower trash Digimon suspended with
  `suppressOnPlayEffects`, binds the actual result, and conditionally redirects
  to that binding. Static inherited Piercing is present.
- Behavioral/peer proof: the shared play primitive naturally suppresses the
  played card's On Play effects and publishes the normal effect-play event only
  for a battle-area play, while the attack redirect consumes the bound permanent
  only when a legal card was played. The source filter and duration match the
  same-set aura and redirect peers.
- Corrections: no production correction was justified; audit-only commit

### BT15-079 — Piedmon

- Catalog/rules: Purple Level 6 Mega Virus Wizard/Dark Masters, play cost 11,
  11000 DP; On Play and When Attacking delete one opposing unsuspended Digimon;
  during the controller's turn this Digimon can only digivolve into white
  Digimon; at the end of the opponent's turn delete this Digimon, then optionally
  play a non-Piedmon Dark Masters Digimon from hand for free; inherited battle-
  loss deletion deletes the battled Digimon.
- KB: Q2514, Q2534, Q2552, and Q2573 confirm that a card played during the
  opponent-end timing does not retroactively trigger that already-passed timing;
  Q2574 confirms a two-color Digimon that includes white is a legal white-only
  destination.
- IR/runtime: the On Play/When Attacking unsuspended opponent filters and the
  opponent-end self-delete/free Dark Masters hand play are faithful. The former
  Your Turn clause used an inert legacy `Restrict` shape with top-level `on` and
  `filter` fields, which the restriction runner does not consume; it now uses
  `RestrictDigivolveInto` targeted to this Digimon with `into: { colors:
  ["White"] }`. The shared definition matcher accepts a multi-color destination
  containing white, and the inherited battle-opponent action retains its natural
  battle-loss binding.
- Behavioral/peer proof: BT15-031's equivalent MetalSeadramon white-only clause
  is the direct peer for the corrected restriction shape. The End of Opponents
  Turn trigger is a discrete natural turn window, so a Digimon played after its
  firing point waits for the next opponent-end window as required by the KB.
- Corrections: `82758e70f` replaced the inert restriction encoding and extended
  the card contract assertion.
