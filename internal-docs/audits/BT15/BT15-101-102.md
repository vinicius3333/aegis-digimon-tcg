# BT15 card-by-card audit: BT15-101 through BT15-102

## Audit frame

This report covers exactly BT15-101 and BT15-102 in strict ascending
card-ID order. Each card was independently read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`, followed by a separate local KB
query, direct TypeScript module and source-level test review, compiled IR and
shared runtime primitive review, useful peer comparison, and legal evolution
or source-stack review where applicable.

Both direct card modules register executable behavior exclusively through one
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` call. No duplicate `registerCard`
registration was added or retained. `BT15-STATIC-AUDIT.md` and
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were not modified.

## Scoring and execution boundary

Each card receives five two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR trace,
Behavioral proof, Peer/stack proof, and Executed gates. Executed gates is fixed
at `0/2`, so the maximum is the requested **8/10 provisional**. Focused,
mechanism, collection, typecheck, lint, formatter, and browser gates were
prohibited and were not run. A single `git diff --check` was accidentally
invoked during a read-only review; it produced no output and earns no gate
credit. No further gate was run.

## Card findings

### BT15-101 — MetalGarurumon

- Catalog/rules: Blue Level 6 Mega Data/Cyborg, play cost 12, 12000 DP, with
  the Blue Level 5 cost-4 evolution route; the card's alternate route allows a
  Gabumon to digivolve from hand for 4 when the controller has a Matt Ishida
  Tamer and the opponent has a 10000 DP-or-higher Digimon. When Digivolving,
  three opposing Digimon or Tamers cannot suspend until the opponent's turn
  ends. All Turns once per turn, when this Digimon becomes suspended, it may
  unsuspend. When it would be deleted, it may suspend to prevent that deletion.
- KB/rules: Q2598 confirms the conditional Gabumon special-evolution
  requirement. Comprehensive rules §§8-1-2, 15-8-5, and 15-14-1 cover ignored
  evolution requirements, the immediate would-be-deletion replacement window,
  and once-per-turn timing; §4-24-1 permits the Digimon/Tamer choice in the
  three-target restriction.
- IR/runtime: the direct module was already IR-only but lacked the
  would-be-deleted suspend replacement and the alternate
  `digivolutionRequirement`. Both are now explicit compiled IR. The replacement
  is self-scoped, optional, suspends this Digimon as its cost, and prevents the
  deletion; the existing suspension-triggered unsuspend remains once per turn.
- Behavioral/peer proof: source-level proof now drives a natural
  `deletePermanent(..., "byEffect")` path and checks that the card remains in
  the battle area, is unsuspended by its own triggered effect, and is not in
  trash. A separate source-level negative path suspends it first, then checks
  that deletion succeeds when the replacement cost is unavailable. BT9-044,
  BT15-033, and BT15-091 provide useful replacement, suspension, and
  once-per-turn peers.
- Correction: `09aacd357` added the missing deletion replacement,
  alternate-evolution metadata, and source-level natural-path proof.

### BT15-102 — Apocalymon

- Catalog/rules: White Level 7 Mega Unknown/Unidentified, play cost 15,
  15000 DP, with Blue/Green/Black/Purple Level 6 cost-6 evolution routes.
  Before play, up to three differently named Dark Masters trait cards may be
  placed from the battle area or trash under this card, reducing play cost by
  4 per placed card. At the end of the controller's turn once per turn, it may
  place a Level 6-or-lower trash card underneath, activate that card's On Play
  effect as this Digimon's effect, then trash two opponent deck cards per
  Level 6 digivolution card.
- KB/rules: Q2599 establishes that a battle-area permanent contributes only
  its top card, not its digivolution cards or cards under a Tamer. Q2600 makes
  the following mill clause dependent on successfully placing a qualifying
  trash card. Q6241 confirms that a qualifying Dark Masters Option in the
  battle area may be used. Q5036, Q5063, Q5110, and Q5155 cover player choice
  ordering for related end-turn effects. Comprehensive rules §§4-25,
  15-10-2, 15-15-7, and 15-16-12 cover distinct-name identity, up-to
  selection, activating another effect with this Digimon as its source, and
  the End of Your Turn trigger point.
- IR/runtime: the nested placement cost is now explicitly hosted by self and
  is handled through the shared verified self-reducer path; no card-local
  handwritten registration was introduced. The resolver combines eligible
  trash cards with only battle-area top cards, supports up-to selection and
  distinct names, and sheds the selected battle-area permanent stack into
  trash while moving its top card underneath Apocalymon. The shared pending
  relocation representation accepts that stack-shedding form while preserving
  legacy string relocations. The end-turn activation targets this Digimon's
  digivolution cards, is restricted to the card most recently placed by the
  cost, and can activate a newly placed face-down card's On Play; the mill
  action remains downstream of the placement/activation action.
- Behavioral/peer proof: source-level proof models a natural play from hand
  with two qualifying trash cards and a battle-area Dark Masters top card,
  checks three distinct cards under Apocalymon, confirms the source permanent
  and its under-card are shed correctly, and checks play-cost reduction. A
  natural turn-end path places a Level 6-or-lower trash card, activates its On
  Play effect, and mills according to the resulting Level 6 stack. A negative
  natural turn-end path with no qualifying trash card confirms the dependent
  mill does not occur. EX10-061 is a mixed-source placement peer; BT10-112,
  BT18-066, and EX9-073 provide useful activation, stack-source, and
  end-of-turn peers.
- Corrections: `d7c021d93` fixed the shared nested placement reducer and
  source-zone semantics, clarified self hosting in the direct IR, preserved
  battle-area top-card shedding, and added last-placed stack tracking for the
  On Play activation. The direct module remains exclusively
  `registerIrCard("BT15-102", compiled)`.
