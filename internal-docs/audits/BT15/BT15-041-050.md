# BT15 card-by-card audit: BT15-041 through BT15-050

## Audit frame

This report covers exactly BT15-041 through BT15-050 in strict ascending card-ID
order. Each card was independently checked against immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`, followed by a separate local
`node tools/kb/query.mjs card <ID>` query, direct module and test review, shared
IR/runtime primitive review, useful peer comparison, and legal evolution or
source-stack review where applicable.

All ten direct modules use `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and exactly one
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration. No card module has a duplicate
`registerCard` registration. `BT15-STATIC-AUDIT.md` and
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were not modified.

## Scoring and execution boundary

Each card receives exactly five two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR trace,
Behavioral proof, Peer/stack proof, and Executed gates. Executed gates is fixed
at 0/2 because focused, mechanism, collection, typecheck, lint, formatter,
browser, and `git diff --check` gates were prohibited and were not run. Thus
each result below is a provisional maximum of 8/10; source assertions and
commits are evidence, not claims that the prohibited gates passed.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT15-041 — Babamon

Catalog/rules: Yellow/Green Level 6 Mega Vaccine Ancient, play 10, 10000 DP;
Yellow or Green Level 5 evolution for 3. Its On Play and On Deletion effects
give one opposing Digimon -6000 DP until the end of its controller's turn. At
the end of the opponent's turn it may delete itself to play a Rosemon or Jijimon
from hand for free, then activate that Digimon's When Digivolving effects. The
separate KB query returned no entry.

IR trace and correction: `BT15-041.ts` already had executable On Play/On
Deletion `ModifyDP` and an EndOfOpponentsTurn `CostGatedBlock` that deletes
self, plays the named hand target, and reactivates the played target's When
Digivolving effects. No production correction was needed.

Behavioral and peer/stack proof: The tests retain the -6000 public play proof,
use the production turn machine for the end-of-opponent-turn delete/play/
reactivation path, and prove legal Green Level 5 evolution with source-stack
preservation. Rosemon's existing When Digivolving registration is used as the
peer effect target; a small test-only registry seam remains only to make its
memory change observable.

removal, and prove legal Yellow Level 5 evolution with the Security cost and

Green Level 4 evolution for 3; Ace with overflow 3. The Hand/Counter clause
allows a Digimon to digivolve into this card without paying its cost. On Play

gate, natural Counter Timing eligibility and free Blast Digivolution, and
legal Green Level 4 evolution with the +3000 When Digivolving result and source
stack. BT15-038 and BT15-026 are direct Blast Digivolve counter peers; the

### BT15-050 — Cherrymon

Catalog/rules: Green Level 5 Ultimate Virus Vegetation, play 6, 6000 DP;
Green Level 4 evolution for 3. On Play, reveal four cards, add up to two
Level 6 or higher cards, and bottom-deck the rest. At the end of its
controller's turn, it may delete one own Digimon to play one Dark Masters
Digimon from hand into an empty breeding area without paying. Its inherited
effect is Piercing. The separate KB query returned Q2529 (add as many as
available when fewer than two hits are revealed), Q2530-Q2531 (breeding plays
do not trigger On Play or “when played” effects), Q2532 (summoning sickness
still applies), and Q2533 (play prohibition prevents the play).

IR trace and correction: `BT15-050.ts` uses `RevealAdd` with four-card reveal,
up-to-two Level 6+ hand filter, and deck-bottom remainder; an optional
EndOfYourTurn `PlayWithoutCost` with a `deleteOwn` cost, Dark Masters trait
filter, and `breeding: true`; and inherited Piercing. No production correction
was needed.

Behavioral and peer/stack proof: Tests prove two hits plus bottom-decked misses,
the Q2529 single-hit case, and natural end-turn delete/play into breeding.
That natural path also proves BT15-031's On Play does not fire in breeding and
that moving the played Digimon out retains summoning sickness. A runtime
Piercing observation and legal Green Level 4 evolution prove inherited and
source-stack behavior. BT15-027 and BT15-062 are direct reveal/Dark Masters
breeding peers; the shared breeding preflight and play primitive carry the
Q2530-Q2533 rules shape.
