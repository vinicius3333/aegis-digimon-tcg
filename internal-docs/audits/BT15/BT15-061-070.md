## Card findings and corrections

### BT15-061 — Guardromon

- Catalog/rules: Black level 4 Machine/Cyborg; Blocker; On Play/When Digivolving trashes a Machine/Cyborg from hand and protects one of your Digimon from opponent effects; the no-opponent-Digimon attack restriction; inherited Reboot. KB query: no entries.
- Correction: replaced the inherited static `Unsuspend` action with the `Reboot` keyword and an empty static action list. A continuously recomputed static unsuspend would incorrectly refresh the Digimon outside the opponent unsuspend phase.
- Proof: `BT15-061.test.ts` covers the printed effects and drives inherited Reboot through a legal level 3-to-4-to-5 stack.
- Commit: `744a68a39` (`Correct BT15-061 inherited Reboot behavior`).

### BT15-062 — Gigadramon

- Catalog/rules: Black level 5 Machine/Cyborg; On Play reveals four and adds up to two level 6 or higher cards; end of your turn may delete one of your Digimon to play a Dark Masters card into an empty breeding area without cost; inherited Reboot. KB Q2545–Q2549 confirm “up to two,” breeding-area placement, no On Play window for the breeding play, no attack that turn, and the empty-area/prohibition constraints.
- Correction: replaced the inherited static `Unsuspend` action with the `Reboot` keyword and empty static actions for the same phase-correctness reason as BT15-061.
- Proof: `BT15-062.test.ts` covers the reveal cap and drives the end-of-turn delete-plus-breeding play through public turn progression.
- Commit: `3c2c212ca` (`Correct BT15-062 inherited Reboot behavior`).

### BT15-063 — Hisyaryumon

- Catalog/rules: Black/Green level 5 Beast Dragon/DigiPolice; Blocker; when an effect suspends another Digimon or Tamer, conditional free digivolution into a Beast Dragon/DigiPolice; inherited once-per-turn unsuspend of one of your Beast Dragon/DigiPolice when an effect suspends another Digimon or Tamer. KB query: no entries.
- Correction: both suspension watchers now use `controller: "any"`, `excludeSelf: true`, and `kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"]`. The runtime’s unfiltered `whenEffectSuspends` event is self-scoped; the explicit filter is required to observe another permanent while retaining the printed Digimon-or-Tamer and self-exclusion boundaries.
- Proof: `BT15-063.test.ts` drives a natural BT14-043 effect suspension and verifies the inherited branch acts on an eligible ally rather than the suspended source.
- Commit: `330086372` (`Scope BT15-063 suspension watchers`).

### BT15-064 — Megadramon

- Catalog/rules: Black level 5 Machine/Cyborg/SoC; On Play/When Digivolving reveals three, places one matching card under this Digimon, adds one matching card to hand, and trashes the rest; SoC attack deletion; inherited once-per-turn de-digivolve and trash bounded at level 3. KB Q2550–Q2551 confirm the place-under and same-trait hand behavior.
- Correction: both reveal actions now specify `underFilter: { isSelfRef: true }`. Without the explicit host binding, `to: "placeUnder"` could choose another eligible Machine/Cyborg/SoC host instead of this Megadramon.
- Proof: `BT15-064.test.ts` uses another Machine host and verifies the first qualifying reveal is placed under Megadramon, with the separate hand result preserved.
- Commit: `f03ba10cd` (`Anchor BT15-064 reveal sources`).

### BT15-065 — WaruMonzaemon

- Catalog/rules: Black level 5 Puppet; On Play/When Digivolving trashes a Numemon from hand or this Digimon’s stack to de-digivolve an opposing Digimon without passing level 3; a separate On Play places a Numemon from trash under this Digimon and restricts low-cost opposing attacks; inherited Security Attack +1 while the name condition is met. KB query: no entries.
- Correction: the first cost target now has `source: "thisDigimon"`. The combined `from: ["hand", "digivolutionCards"]` source previously allowed a matching Numemon from any own stack; the card limits the stack source to this Digimon.
- Proof: `BT15-065.test.ts` uses a hand Numemon to pay the first clause, then verifies the second clause places the trash Numemon under the same WaruMonzaemon and applies the attack restriction.
- Commit: `d00ec5866` (`Scope BT15-065 Numemon cost`).

### BT15-066 — Machinedramon

- Catalog/rules: Black level 6 Machine/Dark Masters; On Play/When Attacking de-digivolves and trashes up to two while stopping at level 3; during your turn it can only digivolve into white Digimon; end of opponent’s turn deletes itself and may play a non-Machinedramon Dark Masters from hand; inherited Reboot. - Commit: `5b713084a` (`Correct BT15-066 evolution restriction`).

### BT15-067 — Ouryumon

- Catalog/rules: Black/Green level 6 Beast Dragon/DigiPolice; Blocker; conditional return of a suspended opposing Digimon/Tamer when DigiPolice is in stack; inherited once-per-turn free play of a Beast Dragon/DigiPolice at 5000 DP or less when this Digimon becomes suspended. KB query: no entries.

- Proof: `BT15-067.test.ts` uses BT14-043’s natural suspension cost to suspend Ouryumon and verifies the qualifying Ginryumon is played.
- Commit: `03456599a` (`Scope BT15-067 suspension watcher`).

### BT15-068 — Gizamon

- Catalog/rules: Purple level 3 Sea Animal; On Play grants one opposing Digimon an On Deletion “Lose 1 memory” effect through the end of the opponent’s turn; inherited once-per-turn memory gain when an effect plays an opponent’s Digimon. KB Q2554–Q2555 establish that effect-play into breeding does not activate the inherited effect and that effects controlled by either player do activate it.
- Correction: the inherited `whenPlayed` source filter now requires `controller: "opponent"`, `kind: ["Digimon"]`, `zone: "battleArea"`, and `byEffect: true`. This excludes manual plays and breeding-area effect plays while accepting effect plays controlled by either player relative to Gizamon’s owner, matching the WaruSeadramon peer shape.
- Proof: `BT15-068.test.ts` checks the exact filter, drives WaruSeadramon’s natural opponent-trash play, verifies the inherited memory gain, and retains positive/negative grant-deletion coverage.
- Commit: `c721d01d6` (`Correct BT15-068 effect-play watcher`).

### BT15-069 — Candlemon

- Proof: `BT15-069.test.ts` drives a natural battle deletion at the one-memory boundary and verifies both the draw and memory gain.
- Commit: `8a060ce4d` (`Prove BT15-069 memory boundary`).
