## Card findings

### BT15-031 MetalSeadramon

- Catalog/rules: Blue level 6 Mega, with `[On Play]` and `[When Attacking]` level 5-or-lower opponent bounce; Your Turn white-only evolution restriction; opponent-end deletion followed by optional free Dark Masters play excluding MetalSeadramon; inherited Blocker.
- KB: Q2514, Q2534, Q2552, and Q2573 establish the opponent-end timing and that a card played during that timing does not retroactively trigger its own already-passed end-of-opponent-turn effect.
- IR/runtime: exact `levelComparison lte 5`, white-only `RestrictDigivolveInto`, end-of-opponent-turn delete/optional hand play, and static Blocker all route through shared consumers with no residual.
- Behavioral/peer proof: existing bounce, evolution, deletion/free-play, and real Blocker battle coverage was supplemented with natural public turn progression for the opponent-end trigger; evolution restriction and Dark Masters targeting were compared with shared restriction/play peers.
- Correction: test-only natural-origin proof; no production correction.

### BT15-032 Plesiomon (X Antibody)

- Catalog/rules: Blue level 6 Mega; both `[When Digivolving]` and `[When Attacking]` once-per-turn effects return one opponent Digimon with stack size less than or equal to this Digimon; opponent's equal-or-smaller-stack attack grants 2 memory when Plesiomon or X Antibody is in this stack.
- KB: no entry.
- IR/runtime: both return actions share one once-per-turn key; the opponent-attack watcher uses a live subject filter and `digivolutionCardsCompareToSource: lte`; conditional memory gain checks the source stack names.
- Behavioral/peer proof: tests cover exact-equal and over-sized stacks, shared once-per-turn use, missing source names, and opponent-turn triggering; stack-size comparison and subject-filter behavior were traced through `permanentMatchesFilter` and the attack subtrigger path.
- Correction: none; direct IR is faithful and stronger than the canonical effect shell where it supplies the attacker source filter.

### BT15-033 Tapirmon

- Behavioral/peer proof: real battle coverage proves payment, prevention, decline, empty-security failure, effect deletion non-payment, and inherited hosting; replacement matching was compared with other Barrier/replacement cards.
- Correction: none.

### BT15-034 Salamon

- Catalog/rules: Yellow level 3 Vaccine Mammal; at the start of the owner’s main phase, optional top-security-to-hand at 3+ security, or optional Vaccine yellow Digimon hand placement at 2-or-fewer security; inherited once-per-turn opponent DP -2000 when a security card is removed.
- KB: Q2515 confirms the live count is reevaluated after the first action, allowing the 3-security branch to reduce the stack to 2 and the second branch to activate in the same resolution.
- IR/runtime: sequential `SecurityManipulation` actions use live `securityAtLeast`/`securityAtMost` conditions and top/bottom choice; inherited security-removal watcher is mine-scoped, once per turn, and applies to one opponent Digimon for the turn.
- Behavioral/peer proof: tests cover the Q2515 3-to-2 transition, exact 2 threshold, Vaccine/color filtering, top/bottom choices, and own-versus-opponent removal; live security sequencing and source-seat removal gates were traced.
- Correction: none.

### BT15-037 Gatomon

- Catalog/rules: Yellow level 4 Vaccine Holy Beast; directly effect-trashed security card may play itself free; once per turn, own security removal grants 1 memory; top-level and inherited Barrier replacements.
- KB: Q2518 limits the play trigger to direct security trash rather than reveal/search; Q2519 confirms a card played directly from security is removed at the same time and can trigger the removal effect.
- IR/runtime: `OnDiscardSecurity` free self-play, own-scoped once-per-turn `whenSecurityRemoved` memory gain, and both Barrier clauses are present. The shared `playFromSecurity` primitive previously moved the card without publishing either security-removal bus, so Q2519 was not observable for cards played rather than trashed.
- Behavioral/peer proof: existing direct-trash, own/opponent removal, once-per-turn reset, and real Barrier battle proofs were supplemented with a natural BT15-092 main effect that plays Gatomon from security and checks the same-time memory gain.
- Correction: `playFromSecurity` now publishes `whenEffectRemovesFromSecurity` and `whenSecurityRemoved` after the entering card’s live effects are installed; this is the smallest reusable runtime correction and avoids false `OnDiscardSecurity` activation.

### BT15-039 Bombermon

- Catalog/rules: Yellow level 5 Mutant; On Play/When Digivolving give one opponent Digimon -3000 DP and `[On Deletion] Lose 1 memory` through opponent turn end; both top and inherited effects gain all effects of Gammamon-named stack cards. Q2520 confirms the granted deletion loss still applies when the DP reduction deletes the target; Q2521 confirms gained Gammamon When Digivolving effects can trigger; Q2522 confirms gained Blitz can attack; Q2523 excludes inherited effects of the matched stack cards.
- IR/runtime: targeted `GainTriggeredEffect` plus same-target DP reduction correctly preserve the deletion recipient; stack conferrals are live continuous grants. The previous conferral collector had no way to exclude inherited effects, so the Q2523 rule was not enforceable.
- Behavioral/peer proof: existing same-target deletion/memory proof was supplemented with a natural attack over a Gammamon card carrying an inherited effect, proving that inherited text is not borrowed; the conferral flow was compared with BT16-062’s stack-effect path and inherited-only placement semantics.
- Correction: added an explicit `excludeInherited` conferral flag through the shared Action/context/continuous/collector path and enabled it only for BT15-039’s two printed Gammamon grants. No unrelated card behavior is changed.

Q2524 confirms the effect selects only one opponent Digimon, even though the modifier scales by own Digimon count.
- IR/runtime: live stack-name condition, hand-only free play, exclude-self `whenPlayed` watcher, one-target DP action, and `cards` scaling over own battle-area Digimon are all wired with no residual.
- Behavioral/peer proof: tests prove a single chosen opponent is debuffed by the current own-Digimon count while an unchosen opponent remains unchanged; scaling and one-target resolution were traced through `countMatching`, `scaleFactor`, and `resolvePermanentTargets`.
- Correction: none.
