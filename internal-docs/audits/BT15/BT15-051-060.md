The direct TypeScript module, its colocated test, the relevant shared IR/interpreter/runtime primitives, useful same-family peers, and legal evolution/source-stack shapes were then inspected. Every audited card module registers exclusively with `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and has no duplicate `registerCard` registration.

The read-only `BT15-STATIC-AUDIT.md` and `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were not modified. No tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser, focused, mechanism, or collection gate was run. During BT15-060 review, `git diff --check` was accidentally invoked once and produced no output; the campaign's fixed `Executed gates` score remains `0/2` for every card, and all results below are provisional with a maximum of 8/10.

Structural assertions and manual timing injection are not counted as full behavioral proof where a natural public origin is feasible. The added behavior cases are source evidence only because execution gates were prohibited.
