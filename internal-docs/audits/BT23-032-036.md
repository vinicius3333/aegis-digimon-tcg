# BT23 audit ledger: BT23-036 through BT23-032

Each card was audited individually against every catalog field, its exact local KB output, direct implementation, shared interpreter seams and focused public behavior. Standard optional/card/target decisions are the applicable UI path.

## BT23-036 BanchoLeomon — 10/10

Yellow/red level 6 Beastkin/Boss/CS Vaccine Mega, cost 12, 12000 DP; normally evolves for 4 from yellow or red level 5 and alternatively for 3 from level-5 Leomon-name or CS. Its hand play cost is reduced by 5 when the opponent has a 10000-DP-or-higher Digimon. On Play/When Digivolving, one other own Digimon may evolve free into a level-6-or-lower Leomon-name or CS Digimon from hand. Its End of Your Turn OPT gives one own Digimon Raid, then that same Digimon may attack. Q5297 confirms the Leomon-name OR CS destination; Q5298 permits granting Raid while declining the attack. The reducer IR was not registered at pay time and is now explicitly verified in the conservative self-reducer catalog. Public proofs pay exactly 7 at the 10000-DP boundary and evolve only the other Digimon into a qualifying level-6 CS card without changing memory. Cost gate, self exclusion, destination OR/level, hand/stack zones, free payment, target binding, optional attack, OPT and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-035 Dynasmon — 10/10

Yellow/red level 6 Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/CS Data Mega, cost 12, 12000 DP; normally evolves for 4 from yellow or red level 5 and alternatively for 3 from level-5 Witchelny or CS. Barrier. On Play/When Digivolving trashes the controller's top security to give all opposing Digimon -6000 DP for the turn. Its All Turns OPT reacts when its own security is removed from, gives Security Attack +1 through its controller's turn end, then at three or fewer security performs Recovery +1 from deck top. Q5293 confirms the DP rule check precedes a newly played Digimon's On Play; Q5294 gives Security effects priority; Q5295 permits another activation on the following turn; Q5296 permits the extra check after Barrier preserves Dynasmon in a security battle. The watcher lacked own-stack direction and Recovery incorrectly constrained deck top to Digimon. Public proof first removes opposing security without firing, then removes own security, observes exactly one Security Attack grant and recovers an Option to the exact security top. Payment, global DP duration, event controller, priority, OPT reset semantics, zone/count condition and arbitrary deck-top Recovery map to tested seams.

## BT23-034 Sakuyamon — 10/10

Yellow level 6 Shaman/Zaxon/CS Data Mega, cost 11, 11000 DP; normally evolves for 3 from yellow level 5 and alternatively for 3 from level-5 CS. Its hand play cost is reduced by 5 with a Zaxon Tamer. Shared On Play/When Digivolving/When Attacking OPT gives one opposing Digimon -6000 DP and prevents its When Digivolving effects through the opponent's turn end. On Deletion places this card face up at security bottom. Q5282-Q5286 define the exact timing suppression, preserve mixed When Attacking activation, reject indirect activation and “by” payment, and do not spend an OPT that could not activate. Q5289-Q5292 define face-up security checks/effects and shuffle normalization. The reducer was absent from pay-time registration, and the deletion source incorrectly tried to resolve the already-gone permanent rather than the loose self instance. Public proofs pay exactly 6, bind both riders to one target, and move the deleted instance from trash behind existing security face up. Timing applicability, shared OPT, target binding, duration, cost, deletion zone/order/face and evolution requirements map to tested seams.

## BT23-033 Beautymon — 10/10

Yellow/black level 5 Beauty Life Ultimate Appmon, cost 8, 8000 DP, App Fusion Coordemon plus Consulmon for 0. Barrier. On Play/When Digivolving may link a Link-capable level-4-or-lower Digimon from trash or its own sources free. Its Your Turn OPT reacts when it gets linked: at five or fewer security it recovers one, then gives one opposing Digimon -1000 DP per current security through the opponent's turn end. It links to Appmon for 3, contributes +4000 DP, and When Linking protects the host from opposing hand/deck returns and De-Digivolve through the opponent's turn end. Q5280 excludes cards without Link; Q5281 requires the scaled DP reduction even when Recovery is ineligible. The Link-capability gate, event nesting, Link metadata and linked protection were absent. Public proof with six security links only the capable candidate, performs no Recovery, leaves deck intact and still applies -6000 DP. Barrier, App Fusion, source zones, Link gate/cost/DP, event identity, conditional ordering, scaling after Recovery, protection categories/duration and OPT map to tested seams.

## BT23-032 Shakkoumon — 10/10

Yellow/black level 5 Mutant/Hudie/CS/Angel Free Ultimate, cost 8, 8000 DP, evolving for 4 from yellow or black level 4. When Digivolving gives an opposing Digimon a forced start-of-main attack through the opponent's turn end, then on DNA evolution De-Digivolves one opposing Digimon by 1. Its own and inherited All Turns OPT each react when the carrier would leave other than by an own effect and may play a level-4-or-lower yellow/black OR CS Digimon from its sources free. The inherited copy was absent. Q5277 covers effect immunity at the granted trigger; Q5278/Q5279 define the color OR trait eligibility; Q6250 permits Barrier first and then playing even Angemon, but notes that playing Angemon first loses Barrier. Public proof deletes a carrier by an opponent effect, plays only the eligible source before departure, completes carrier deletion and sends the ineligible source to trash. Granted timing, immunity applicability, DNA condition, De-Digivolve target, leave cause/order, source OR/level, free play, optionality and separate OPT copies map to tested seams.
