## Rules and shared mechanism evidence

The relevant sections in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` are §4-14 and
§16-8 (Draw), §4-15 and §4-16 (deletion/trashing, including hand trash),
§4-22 (color), §4-23 (named cards/traits), §7-2 (DigiXros), §8-1-3 (effect
Digivolution), §15-4-4 and §15-5 (pending and simultaneous triggers),
§15-6–§15-10 (conditions, optional processing, target cardinality, duration),
§16-13 (Retaliation), §16-19 (Armor Purge), and §16-20 (Save).

The shared trace is:

- `effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` stores the direct compiled card
  under its ID; all ten modules use only `registerIrCard`.
- `effects/interpreter/effect.ts` maps turn/inherited/static triggers and
  hand-trash and gained-lifecycle triggers. `actions/statics.ts` evaluates
  BT10-071/078 Aura conditions live.
- `effects/interpreter/conditions.ts` handles zone counts, turn checks, and
  `digivolvedFromZone: trash`. `targeting/permanents.ts` resolves owner,
  opponent, zone, kind, name, and trait filters.
- `targeting/loose.ts` distinguishes all stack cards from the source
  permanent's own stack when `isSelfRef: true` is present. This is the source
  cost seam for BT10-076/077.
- `actions/runAction.ts` pays costs, prompts optional actions, and aborts
  dependent actions after decline; this is why BT10-072's placement-bearing
  Draw is both optional and abort-on-decline.
- `actions/reveal.ts` resolves BT10-073's two add buckets from one reveal and
  bottoms the remainder. `actions/board.ts` implements BT10-077's variable
  hand trash from the triggering effect's added-instance count, with opponent
  choice. `whenEffectAddsToOpponentHand` keeps separate effects separate.
- `actions/digivolve.ts` pays and moves a selected trash card while carrying
  `digivolvedFromZone`; `GainTriggeredEffect` installs BT10-080's
  `onDeletionOf` effect with `untilOpponentTurnEnd` duration.
- `effects/primitives.ts` emits `onDigivolutionCardsDiscardedBatch` with exact
  trashed instance IDs, allowing inherited effects to match their source.
