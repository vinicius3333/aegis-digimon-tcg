Audit date: 2026-08-28

This is a static-only audit of BT10-091 through BT10-100, performed in exact
ascending order. `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` and the local
knowledge base were treated as authoritative for printed data and rulings.
Each direct TypeScript card module, its registered compiled IR, the generated
shared effect snapshot, interpreter primitives, colocated tests, mechanism
peers, and realistic board/deck stacks were inspected.

or UI checks, or `git diff --check` were run. Consequently every card has
executed gates 0/2 and a provisional ceiling of 8/10; this report does not
claim green runtime behavior or collection completion. Generated effects data

## Shared mechanism and interpreter traces

The following shared seams were inspected and connected to the clauses below:

* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts:1-180,280-385,430-620`
  maps Security effects to `SecuritySkill`, maps Main effects to option-use
  windows, maps Delay Main to declaration, and installs static/turn-scoped
  watchers. `turnOwnerGuard` keeps Your Turn and Opponent Turn clauses on
  their correct player's turn; static modifiers are recomputed while their
  source remains live.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:108-215,280-365,
  454-465` implements bounded, disjoint RevealAdd slots, whole-action optional
  choice, and remainder routing to deck bottom. This is the key seam for
  BT10-092, BT10-096, and BT10-097.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts:210-390`
  resolves the explicit hand source and destination `underFilter`, places at
  the requested bottom position, and records `lastEffectActed`. That outcome
  is consumed by BT10-094's conditional Draw 1.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/board.ts:260-350`
  resolves a GainKeyword target count and applies `countModifier`; therefore
  BT10-099's Venusmon condition changes one target into exactly three rather
  than adding an unbounded aura. The keyword duration ledger applies the
  opponent-turn endpoint.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/replacement.ts` and
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/reducers.ts:1-540`
  collect nested `wouldBePlayed` reductions and apply them at payment time.
  The allowlisted self-Option path covers BT10-098's conditional memory
  reduction.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:240-340,
  760-840,900-1035,1185-1240` handles source identity, attack and
  `onAddDigivolutionCards` payload filters, once-per-turn scopes, and owner
  versus turn-seat attribution. This supports BT10-091's attack watcher and
  BT10-093's two trigger/replacement clauses.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts:1-180` resolves
  legal opposing Digimon targets and performs delete/return outcomes; the
  level/DP predicates in BT10-096 and BT10-098 therefore remain ordinary
  battle-area targeting unless the card explicitly names another zone.
* `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/meta.ts:1-74` dispatches
  ActivateMain and related metadata without confusing it with a Security
  skill, while `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts`
  routes Delay, self-place, and security continuations. The Security
  dispatch paths in `effect.ts:51-60,133,295-300,539-540` preserve the checked
  card's identity while it resolves from Security.
* `apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts:3517-3705,3728-3790,4750-4760` contains
  the explicit, hard-gated BT10-093 cross-permanent reducer seam. It watches
  a BT10-093 Tamer, limits the replacement to a level 4+ Bagra Army play,
  offers zero through three purple Digimon from under own Tamers, reduces by
  two per chosen card, records the chosen instance IDs, and places them under
  the played permanent before its On Play. The source identity and
  once-per-turn tracker complete the direct IR's reduction shape without a
  duplicate registration or generated-snapshot edit.

## Per-card evidence

### BT10-091 — Ruli Tsukiyono

* The catalog entry at `cards.json:5443-5458` identifies a green Tamer with
  play cost 4. Its Start of Your Turn clause sets memory to 3 if memory is 2
  or less. During its owner's turn, when one of that player's Digimon attacks,
  if the attacker has Angoramon in its name or is level 5 or higher, the
  controller may suspend Ruli to suspend one opposing Digimon with DP 5000 or
  less. Its Security clause plays Ruli without cost.
* `apps/api/src/cards/BT10/BT10-091.ts:1-103` encodes the memory condition as
  `memoryAtMost2` and `SetMemory 3`. Its attack `SubTrigger` is guarded by
  the own Digimon subject plus the Angoramon-name OR level-minimum predicate;
  the body suspends the source Tamer as its cost, then chooses one opposing
  Digimon with `dpLte: 5000`. It is optional with abort on decline, and the
  Security effect is `PlayWithoutCost` for self. The module is full IR and
  has no residual clause.
* Four colocated tests in `BT10-091.test.ts:1-154` statically prove the
  memory reset, a qualifying level-5 attack and 5000/6000 DP boundary,
  Angoramon name matching (including a level-3 Angoramon) with an already
  suspended Ruli unable to pay, and rejection of an opponent's attack.
  Realistic peers/stacks include Angoramon attackers, high-level green
  Digimon, and a 5000-DP versus 6000-DP opposing board.
* Snapshot `effects.json:7621-7674` retains the same trigger, filters, cost,
  DP ceiling, and free Security play. No correction was required.
