# BT10-021 through BT10-030 audit

Audit date: 2026-08-28

This is a static-only audit of BT10-021 through BT10-030, performed in exact
ascending order. `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` and the local
knowledge base were treated as authoritative for printed data and rulings.
Each direct TypeScript card module, its registered compiled IR, the generated
shared effect snapshot, interpreter primitives, colocated tests, mechanism
peers, and realistic evolution/board stacks were inspected.

Per the dispatch constraints, no tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser or
UI checks, or `git diff --check` were run. Therefore every card has executed
gates 0/2 and a provisional ceiling of 8/10; this report does not claim green
runtime behavior or collection completion.

Q1948 requires BT10-023's six-card hand check to be evaluated after the
digivolution bonus, so a resulting seven-card hand does not draw. Q1949
allows BT10-024 to select an opponent Digimon with no sources, and Q1950 keeps
that selected Digimon restricted if it later gains sources. Q1951 requires
BT10-027 to play both available level buckets when its optional effect is
accepted, while allowing the sole available bucket when only one level is
present. Q1952 confirms that BT10-030's Security Attack -1 remains on a target
that later becomes level 6, until the stated opponent-turn-end duration.

The card queries showed no card-specific errata or banlist entries. The local
rules passages inspected include `data/kb/rules/manual.md:1288-1310` and
`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:545-570`: ordinary effects and targets use the
battle area unless they explicitly name the breeding area, and breeding-area
cards cannot be referenced or affected by ordinary effects.

## Per-card evidence

### BT10-021 — MailBirdramon

* Catalog `cards.json:3668-3691` identifies a Blue level 4 Champion/Data
  Machine and Blue Flare Digimon, play cost 5, DP 5000, with the Blue level 3
  route for 2. Its On Play has mutually exclusive optional branches: return an
  exact MetalGreymon from the controller's trash when Kiriha Aonuma is in play,
  otherwise play Kiriha from hand without memory. On Deletion it has Save under
  one of the controller's Tamers. The inherited once-per-turn attack effect
  requires this Digimon's Blue Flare trait and at least two opposing Digimon in
  play, then prevents one opposing Digimon from attacking and blocking through
  the opponent's turn.
* `BT10-021.ts:10-142` represents both optional On Play branches, exact name
  matching, Save under a mine Tamer, and two same-event restrictions. The
  restrictions use `sameTarget`, `attack` plus `block`, and
  `untilOpponentTurnEnd`; the effect is marked inherited and once per turn.
* The colocated test has eight static/runtime-shaped cases in
  `BT10-021.test.ts`: both Kiriha branches, an exact MetalGreymon/X Antibody
  negative, Blue Flare and two-opponent positive/one-opponent negative, the
  added breeding-area boundary, and Save under a Tamer. The inherited fixture
  uses BT10-019 over BT10-021 and the Save fixture uses BT10-088.
* Correction: the original direct IR omitted `zone: "battleArea"` from both
  Kiriha presence checks and both opponent-count conditions. The shared
  `countMatching` primitive includes breeding-area permanents when no zone is
  specified, so those omissions could make an out-of-play breeding Digimon
  satisfy “in play.” All four conditions now name the battle area explicitly.
  The generated snapshot also had a stale combined `attackOrBlock` action and
  an opponent Blue Flare condition; the direct hand-fixed module remains the
  executable authority and was not replaced by that snapshot.

### BT10-027 — Regalecusmon

* Catalog `cards.json:3829-3851` identifies a Blue level 6 Mega/Virus Aquatic
  Digimon, play cost 11, DP 11000, with Blue level 5 evolution for 3. Its
  When Digivolving effect trashes two bottom source cards from one opposing
  Digimon. Its optional When Attacking effect requires an opposing source-less
  Digimon and then plays one level 3 and one level 4 Digimon from this
  Digimon's source cards without memory.
* `BT10-027.ts:8-40` keeps the bottom-two opposing trash effect and two ordered
  level-specific free-play actions. Both play actions now explicitly use
  `fromOwnDigivolutionStack: true`, controller `mine`, and free cost, under an
  optional source-less-opponent condition.
* Six colocated cases in `BT10-027.test.ts` cover bottom ordering, positive
  level 3 plus level 4 play, the no-source condition, Q1951's sole-level
  branch, and the added neighboring/opposing-stack isolation case. The
  positive stack uses BT9-021, BT9-025, and BT10-023; the isolation fixture
  contains unrelated level 3/4 cards in both a neighboring own stack and an
  opposing stack.
* Correction: the original direct IR used generic `from: ["digivolutionCards"]`
  targeting without the own-stack seam. `candidateLooseInstances` therefore
  could enumerate unrelated hosted cards. `actions/play.ts` already provides
  the reusable source-permanent path, so the smallest fix was to opt both
  actions into it and retain the controller filter. This also preserves Q1951's
  mandatory as-many-as-possible sequential behavior.
* The generated snapshot is stale: it has one level-max-3 action, no level-4
  action, and no own-stack binding. It was not edited because the direct module
  is the executable authority.
