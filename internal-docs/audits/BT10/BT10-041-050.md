another card's Your Turn digivolution effect, not BT10-042's printed clauses,

and Carnivorous Plant; Q1972 permits adding whichever eligible category cards
are present; Q1973 makes both Palmon search buckets mandatory when available.

  catalog evolution routes, and attack evolution. Peers include BT4-105,
  BT5-044, BT9-103, BT12-104, P-037, ST22-08 and BT10-039; the realistic
  stacks exercise Option use, linked/battle-area Option movement and security
  replacement.
