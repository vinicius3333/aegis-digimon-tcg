## Shared mechanism and registration trace

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:323-348`
  normalizes and stores each direct record in both `compiledEffects` and the
  registered runtime map before installing its effect module. This makes the
  direct `registerIrCard` record the runtime authority for structural readers
  as well as effect timing.
- `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digiXros.ts:389-530` resolves trait-only
  DigiXros slots, one-card reductions, distinct recipe slots, and the one
  permitted mismatch supplied by a `DigiXrosSubstitute` keyword. The shared
  `digiXrosRequirementFor` reader is consumed by both play validation and
  target highlighting. `actions/play.ts` performs the printed On Play timing
  before DigiXros declaration, consistent with §7-2-2-2.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/statics.ts` evaluates
  direct Auras live. `matching/definition.ts` evaluates the
  `hasDigiXrosRequirements` trash-return filter; the direct BT10-111 recipe
  is therefore required for its own header and for downstream references.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/duration.ts` maps
  `forTheTurn` to turn-end expiry. `BT10-111` now uses this for the
  DigiXros-substitution grant, while the static alias remains permanent.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts` anchors an
  ordinary PlaceUnder action to the activating source permanent and respects
  `underFilter` plus bottom placement. `borrowed.ts`'s
  `lastPlacedOnly` path limits BT10-112's ActivateEffect to the Royal Knight
  card just placed by this effect, while still allowing the effect to resolve
  as Jesmon GX's effect.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/scaling.ts` counts the activating
  permanent's own `digivolutionCards` and applies the direct Royal Knight
  trait filter. This is the Security Attack +1 scaling seam; the same live
  Aura machinery supplies Piercing and Blocker only while a Royal Knight
  source remains.
- `apps/api/src/engine/actions/digivolve.ts` checks the regular color/level
  EvoCost and the trait-gated requirement independently. The direct BT10-112
  requirement is marked `isAlternate: true`, and its off-color proof uses a
  yellow level-6 Dynasmon with the Royal Knight trait.

## Generated snapshot drift and ambiguities

The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` records at
`effects.json:8665-8748` were compared but intentionally left untouched.
The BT10-111 snapshot omits the card's DigiXros recipe, places Material Save
on On Play instead of the keyword/static record, leaves substitution as an
unparsed raw clause, and adds a Shoutmon name gate to the Xros Heart recipe;
the catalog requires only one Xros Heart-trait Digimon. The BT10-112 snapshot
has the stale loose under-filter, a `suspendedTarget` activation reference,
permanent Blitz, no Security Attack Aura, plural `Royal Knights` scaling, and
`isAlternate: true` while the pre-fix direct record had false. Runtime direct
modules now supersede those stale aggregate records through
`registerIrCard`; editing `effects.json` was outside this audit's authority.

Ambiguity count: **1 maintenance ambiguity**, namely the stale generated
aggregate's disagreement with the direct records. There is no unresolved
printed-rule ambiguity after applying the catalog and KB: the apparent
plural `Royal Knights` in BT10-112 is treated as the singular authoritative
trait identity, and BT10-111's requirement is the catalog's Xros Heart-only
slot. The stale snapshot is preserved for review rather than
silently treated as executable evidence.
