## Card findings

### BT25-071 Orochimon

The catalog defines a Black level-5 Dragon/Titan/TS Ultimate with Black level-4 evolution cost 3 and an alternate TS level-4 evolution cost 3. On Play and When Digivolving, it prevents one opposing Digimon or Tamer from attacking until the opponent’s turn ends. On all turns, once per turn when Orochimon suspends, it reveals the top 3 cards, may play one TS Digimon with play cost 4 or less, and places the rest on the bottom of the deck. The inherited effect repeats the self-suspension reveal/play sequence.

The direct IR keeps separate On Play and When Digivolving restriction effects, a self-bound All Turns `whenSuspended` watcher, the TS/play-cost-4 reveal filter, optional free play, bottom-deck remainder, and an independent inherited copy. Source assertions cover catalog identity, both entry timings, exactly one opposing Digimon/Tamer, natural self-suspension, the reveal boundary, shared once-per-turn behavior, alternate evolution, and a realistic inherited stack. No semantic correction was required.

Peers BT25-062/063/064/065/069/071 establish the TS alternate-evolution and inherited-stack vocabulary; BT25-068 supplies the adjacent self-suspension watcher. No Link or security face is printed.

### BT25-072 Shutmon

The catalog defines a Black/Purple level-5 Tool/Forced Termination Appmon with Jamming, Black and Purple level-4 evolution costs 4, and App Fusion `[Logamon] & [Timemon]: Cost 0`. On Play, When Digivolving, and during the controller’s turn when attacking, it may link one Social, Tool, or Game Digimon from trash or its own stack at Link cost reduced by 2. Its All Turns once-per-turn effect restricts one opposing Digimon or Tamer from digivolving when Shutmon gets linked. The linked face is 4000 DP, requires an Appmon Link at cost 3, and when linking restricts two opposing Digimon or Tamers from unsuspending until the opponent’s turn ends.

The direct IR preserves all three conditional Link timings, the mixed trash/own-stack source, `hasLinkRequirement: true`, self-host and own-Digimon boundaries, cost reduction, App Fusion metadata, and the linked-face restriction. The semantic gap was the host-level All Turns `whenLinked` watcher lacking a source filter; because the event bus is broad, it could react when another host received a link. The correction adds `sourceFilter: { isSelfRef: true }`. Source assertions cover natural On Play, attack, linked-face behavior, alternate evolution/stack boundaries, the Q6368 no-Link rejection, and a natural negative in which a different host receives Shutmon without imposing the digivolve restriction. No duplicate registration was introduced.

Peers BT25-060/070/089 establish self-scoped linked watchers; BT25-069 and BT25-093 establish `hasLinkRequirement` behavior and Appmon link targets. No further semantic gap remains.
