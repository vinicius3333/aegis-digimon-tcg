The direct IR already preserved the costed start-phase Draw plus GainMemory sequence, including `optional: true` and `abortOnDecline: true`, and the self-linked `Static`/`SubTrigger(whenLinked)` restriction targeting exactly one opposing Digimon with `unsuspend`/`untilOpponentTurnEnd`. The surviving gap was the missing compiled `linkRequirement`; this audit added `[{ traits: ["Appmon"], cost: 1 }]`. The colocated source test now asserts that requirement and already covers acceptance/refusal, mandatory Appmon payment, natural linking, one-opposing-Digimon selection, expiry, and no retrigger from an older linked copy. Q&A: none.

Peers BT25-007, BT25-045, BT25-052, and BT25-089 establish the Appmon Link requirement/cost and linked-face conventions; BT25-062/065 provide adjacent TS-stack comparisons. Evolution and nonmatching-base boundaries are asserted from BT21-005 versus BT1-007, and the linked host/relink behavior is covered with BT21-009. No further semantic gap remains.
