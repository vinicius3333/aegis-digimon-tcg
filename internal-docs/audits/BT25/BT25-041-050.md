## Card findings

### BT25-041 Murasamemon

The immutable catalog requires a Yellow level-5 Ultimate Glowing Dawn Digimon with Alliance; its When Digivolving and When Attacking once-per-turn effect, during the controller's turn, may either add the top security card or trash the bottom face-down card under any own Tamer, then may play a Glowing Dawn Digimon/Tamer or use a Glowing Dawn Option with cost reduced by 3. The inherited End of Attack effect may trash that Tamer-stack card to unsuspend this Glowing Dawn Digimon once per turn.

The direct module is hand-authored compiled IR with two independent nested modals: both security payment choices lead to both play/use choices. The outer processing action is `optional: true` with `abortOnDecline: true`; after acceptance, the selected security cost and the selected paid play/use remain mandatory. The inherited effect uses the same bottom-face-down Tamer cost and a self-reference plus Glowing Dawn filter. Existing source assertions inspect both modal branches, and a natural attack assertion covers playing a Glowing Dawn Tamer with the reduction; the inherited host and non-Glowing Dawn boundary are asserted through the stack seam. The alternate level-4 Glowing Dawn evolution requirement and Alliance keyword were also checked. No semantic correction was required in this audit.

### BT25-043 Habakirimon / Habakiri

No semantic correction was required in this audit.
