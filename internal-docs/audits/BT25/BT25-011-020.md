## Card findings

### BT25-011 Aquilamon

The catalog identity is Red/Green level 4, play cost 4, 4000 DP, Giant Bird/Iliad/TS, with alternate evolution from `[Hawkmon]` or a level-3 TS Digimon for 2. The IR maps Raid, the On Play/When Digivolving suspension, the conditional your-turn DNA digivolution into Silphymon from hand, and inherited +2000 DP. Added source assertions cover a real play origin that suspends an opponent and performs the DNA digivolution, plus catalog identity and alternate evolution requirements.

The remaining limitation is Raid generic attack redirection/handling in the shared runtime; no out-of-scope engine change was made.

### BT25-012 Grizzlymon

The IR and existing source assertions cover the exact Beast/Animal/Sovereign or Shaman/TS target union, Raid and +3000 DP for one turn, the inherited all-turns +1000 DP, and the alternate level-3 Red/Green evolution requirement. Natural play and When Digivolving target behavior, target reuse, and stack binding are represented in the colocated assertions. No correction was required.

### BT25-013 Firamon

The IR and source assertions cover the optional hand-trash cost, optional red/blue Iliad recovery, the your-turn blue-gated Flaremon evolution with cost reduction, inherited +2000 DP, and the alternate level-3 Red/Blue evolution requirement. The runtime cost/decline and post-digivolution watcher seams match Q6255-Q6257. No correction was required.

### BT25-014 Meramon

The IR and source assertions cover the once-per-turn Main hand cost, mandatory deletion selection at 4000 DP or less, draw two when the effect did not delete, inherited attack deletion, and the alternate level-3 Red evolution requirement. The runtime allows paying the cost when no target exists and preserves deletion-immune selection for the draw branch, matching Q6258-Q6260. No correction was required.

### BT25-016 GrapLeomon
