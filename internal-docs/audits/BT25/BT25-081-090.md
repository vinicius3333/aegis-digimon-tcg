## Card findings

### BT25-081 Fangmon

The catalog defines a Purple level-4 Champion Data Dark Animal/BEATBREAK Digimon with a Purple level-3 evolution cost of 2. On Play and When Digivolving it suspends one non-Purple Tamer. On all turns, once per turn, when any opposing Tamer suspends, it gains 1 memory. Its inherited effect is Retaliation. No security or Link fields are printed.

The direct IR has separate On Play and When Digivolving suspension effects targeting exactly one Tamer while excluding Purple, plus the all-turns opposing-Tamer `whenSuspended` watcher and a physical-copy once-per-turn budget. Retaliation is retained as inherited keyword state. The source tests cover own non-Purple versus Purple targets, opposing mixed-color boundaries, both entry windows, one trigger per copy per turn, and Retaliation through a realistic stack. Q6386 confirms the non-Purple suspension requirement.

Peers BT25-068/081 and the adjacent Purple/TS stack modules establish self-independent suspension watchers and inherited keyword placement. No semantic correction was required.

### BT25-082 BlackGatomon

The catalog defines a Purple/Black level-4 Champion Virus Dark Animal/Iliad/TS Digimon. It has alternate Purple level-3 and Black level-3 evolution routes, each costing 3 in the catalog's printed alternate requirements. Its printed alternate `[Digivolve]` clause also allows a level-3 card with `[Three Musketeers]` in text or the TS trait for cost 2. On Play and When Digivolving, with one or fewer own Tamers, it may play a Tamer with `[Three Musketeers]` in its text for free. On all turns, while its controller has such a Tamer, it may digivolve into a Three Musketeers Digimon for cost 4 while ignoring requirements. Its inherited once-per-turn When Attacking clause says “By placing” a Three Musketeers card from hand or trash as this Digimon's bottom source, draw 1. There are no security or Link fields.

The direct IR preserves both alternate evolution requirements, the optional free Tamer play at the exact one-Tamer boundary, and the continuous all-turns digivolution permission with self target, cost override 4, ignored requirements, and the required Tamer-text condition. The inherited Draw keeps `optional: true`, `abortOnDecline: true`, and a mandatory accepted bottom-placement cost from hand or trash. Existing source tests use public digivolve intents for the battle-area permission, reject the breeding-area case, cover effect-driven paid evolution and the P-108-style reduction, test the On Play boundary/refusal, and verify that no unprinted inherited draw is added. Q6387-Q6392 cover text matching, breeding restrictions, effect-driven use, simultaneous activation choices, and the allowed opponent-counter timing.

The runtime audit found that this All Turns clause is a continuous base grant consumed by `BASE_GRANTED_DIGIVOLVE`/`matchBaseGrantedDigivolve`, not a player-declared activated ability. The existing interpreter exception intentionally routes only direct `YourTurn` Digivolve/DnaDigivolve/AppFuse actions through the Main-phase declaration/activated seam. No runtime change was made. A candidate global All Turns broadening was rejected because it would fabricate a second activatable ability for BlackGatomon's continuous permission and alter five unrelated Delay/All Turns cards plus one mixed effect: BT21-090, BT21-091, BT21-093, BT21-094, BT25-082, and P-204. The six affected effect objects contain six direct Digivolve actions, with zero direct DnaDigivolve and zero direct AppFuse actions; nested actions under SubTrigger/Replacement do not satisfy that direct-action predicate. No semantic correction was required.

Peers BT25-083/084/085 validate Three Musketeers and TS evolution stacks, while BT25-092 and BT25-093 provide the Tamer-text and Option/trait boundaries used in the tests.

### BT25-083 LadyDevimon

The catalog defines a Purple level-5 Fallen Angel/Titan/TS Virus Digimon with Purple level-4 alternate evolution requirements for the Three Musketeers text and TS trait, each costing 3. On Play and When Digivolving, “By placing” one Three Musketeers card from hand or trash as the bottom source of any own Digimon, it draws 1. Once per turn, on When Digivolving or When Attacking, “By trashing” an Option from any own Digimon's digivolution cards, it may use a Three Musketeers Option from trash with its cost reduced by 3. Its inherited On Deletion effect may play a level-4-or-lower Digimon with Three Musketeers in its text from trash. No security or Link fields are printed.

The direct IR separates the entry draw and the once-per-turn shared `UseOptionWithoutCost` windows, scopes source and host zones to the controller, preserves the bottom placement, and marks both printed “By” costs optional with `abortOnDecline`. The inherited play effect is optional and restricted to the printed level/text boundary. Tests cover placement from hand and trash, bottom ordering, refusal, physical dual Option use from a stack, shared once-per-turn timing, simultaneous triggers, and inherited On Deletion. Q6390 and Q6393-Q6396 confirm DUAL trait references, text matching, effect ordering, and that a used Option is no longer a second trash trigger.

Peers BT25-082/085 provide the adjacent Three Musketeers/TS Option stack, and BT25-093 exercises the dual-card boundary. No semantic correction was required.

### BT25-084 Titamon

The catalog defines a Purple/Red/Green level-6 Shaman/Titan/TS Virus Digimon with alternate Titamon and TS level-5 evolution routes. On Play, When Digivolving, or When Attacking, once per turn, “By trashing” one card from hand, it deletes all opposing Digimon with the highest DP; if it entered the battle area by an effect, it then trashes the opponent's top security card. On all turns, once per turn, when it would leave the battle area, “by trashing” two cards from hand, it does not leave. On all turns, whenever a card is trashed from its controller's hand, it deletes one opposing lowest-DP Digimon. No security or Link fields are printed.

The direct override preserves all maximum-DP ties, the one-card mandatory accepted cost, the entered-by-effect security gate, and the shared once-per-turn budget across On Play, When Digivolving, and When Attacking. The leave replacement requires the indivisible two-card payment. The hand-trash watcher is seat-gated and fires per trash action, not per individual card. Q6397-Q6401 confirm full payment, repeated rule-check ordering, watcher seat ownership, and one-versus-two action boundaries. Existing source tests cover effect-play/evolution versus manual and attack entry, highest-DP ties, leave prevention/refusal, hand-trash triggers, shared once-per-turn behavior, and the three-color/TS alternate evolutions.

Peers BT25-083/085 establish the shared Three Musketeers/Titan effect vocabulary; BT25-073 and BT25-095 provide stack and highest/lowest-DP comparisons. No semantic correction was required.

During its controller's turn, once per turn, when an own Digimon would digivolve into a DATA SQUAD Digimon, “by trashing” the bottom face-down card under any own Tamer, it reduces that evolution's cost by 1. It is now `YourTurn`, while retaining `Replacement`/`wouldDigivolve`, amount 1, own DATA SQUAD destination, own-Tamer payment, and `OncePerTurn`. A source assertion was added to pin the trigger and frequency. Existing tests cover the start-turn memory boundary, opponent-versus-controller hand additions, top-first face-down stacking and privacy, two-copy stacked reductions, and security play. Q6409-Q6414 confirm bottom order, no reorder, ownership/privacy, face-down trash visibility, and top-first multi-card processing.

Peers BT25-088/090 use the same Tamer resource stack and turn-scoped replacement pattern. BT25-086 and BT25-089 provide adjacent TS/Appmon Tamer timing and cost interactions. This is the only surviving semantic card-module correction in the range.
