## Card-by-card findings

### BT5-111 — Omnimon X (Anti-body) — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: White level-7 Digimon, play cost 15, 15000 DP, evolution from Red level 6 for 6 or Blue level 6 for 6, Mega, Vaccine, Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/X Antibody, rarity SEC, maximum 4. Main text: `Your Digimon with [Omnimon] in its name can digivolve into this card in your hand for a memory cost of 3, ignoring this card's digivolution requirements.[When Attacking] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon with DP less than or equal to this Digimon's DP.[Opponent's Turn] When your opponent's Digimon attacks, you may trash 2 of this Digimon's digivolution cards to end the attack.`

The direct module contains the alternate requirement `{ names: ["Omnimon"], cost: 3, isAlternate: true, battleAreaOnly: true }`. `names: ["Omnimon"]` is the required “in its name” substring match, while `battleAreaOnly` enforces Q1385's breeding-area exclusion; Q1386 confirms the legal battle-area path. The When Attacking effect deletes exactly one opposing Digimon whose live DP is less than or equal to this source's DP. The Opponent's Turn watcher listens for `whenOpponentAttacks` once per timing and offers an optional EndAttack action whose exact two-card cost trashes this source's digivolution cards. It is full/residual-free and exclusively registered with `registerIrCard("BT5-111", compiled)`.

The focused tests cover the legal BT5-086 Omnimon-to-BT5-111 shortcut at three memory, Q1385 breeding-area rejection, wrong-name rejection, the newly asserted registered `battleAreaOnly` requirement, the DP 15000/15001 boundary, exact two-source payment before the security check while retaining a third source, declining the optional effect without trashing sources, and failure to pay with only one source. Q1387 supports the exact all-or-nothing two-card cost; Q1388–Q1390 support the EndAttack timing transition, unaffected-attacker behavior, and subsequent End of Attack processing. The alternate Omnimon requirement was compared with BT5-086 and BT10-086, and the self-stack EndAttack cost was compared with other source-cost and EndAttack peers including BT23-069. Q2277 was checked as a distinct effect/source interaction: explicit play-from-digivolution-card effects may see a breeding-area stack, but that does not permit this card's own alternate digivolution shortcut there.

Shared interpreter review traced the alternate requirement through `validateDigivolve` and `matchingAlternateDigivolutionRequirement`, including the `battleAreaOnly` check; source-DP filtering through the live source permanent; `whenOpponentAttacks` subscription and source anchoring; optional action preflight; exact `trash` cost payment; and `EndAttack`'s combat timing transition. The `oncePerTiming` guard suppresses duplicate firing within an attack window, while the source subscription is removed if the source leaves play.

Snapshot drift: the generated record has the correct When Attacking DP comparison but represents the cost as an optional `TrashDigivolution` action with `amount: 2`, `fromTop: true`, followed by a separate mandatory `EndAttack`, and omits `battleAreaOnly` from the alternate requirement. The direct module instead binds the exact two-card cost to the optional EndAttack action and carries the direct battle-area gate; the shared `packages/shared/src/effects/data.ts` override independently corroborates that gate. The snapshot was not edited.

Direct correction: added `battleAreaOnly: true` to the registered BT5-111 alternate requirement. Focused test strengthened: asserted the gate on `runtimeCompiledCard("BT5-111")` so future generated/direct registration regressions cannot silently re-open the breeding-area path.

### BT5-112 — Omnimon Zwart Defeat — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: White level-7 Digimon, play cost 15, 13000 DP, evolution from Black level 6 for 3 or Purple level 6 for 3, Mega, Virus, Holy Warrior, rarity SEC, maximum 4. Main text: `[Security] Play this card without battling and without paying its memory cost.[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Tamers.[On Deletion] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon.`

The direct hand-authored override contains a Security effect marked `isSecurity: true` that targets this card with the self-reference shape and uses `from: ["security"]`, `payCost: false`. The When Digivolving effect deletes exactly one opposing permanent of kind Tamer, and the On Deletion effect deletes exactly one opposing permanent of kind Digimon. The module is full/residual-free and exclusively registered with `registerIrCard("BT5-112", compiled)`.

The focused tests cover complete residual-free runtime coverage; registered Security timing and self-instance dispatch to `playFromSecurity` with no hand fallback; opponent-Tamer-only selection excluding own Tamers, opponent Digimon, and Digimon that merely digivolved from a Tamer; opponent-Digimon-only On Deletion dispatch; unrelated-timing silence; a legal BT5-082-to-BT5-112 evolution stack; real deletion of BT5-112; and a Security attack where BT5-112 is played without battling and the attacker remains alive. Q1395 and Q1396 match the Security primitive and continuation of remaining checks; Q1397 supports the kind-specific Tamer target; Q5026 supports independent On Deletion chaining behavior. The Black/Purple level-6 legal evolution paths were checked against the catalog and compared with same-mechanism Security self-play peers such as BT6-087, BT6-088, and BT5-110.

Shared interpreter review traced Security timing through the Security effect builder, `PlayWithoutCost` self-target dispatch, `playFromSecurity`, and the security-check loop. This path moves the revealed card into the battle area without invoking a battle, after which remaining security cards continue checking. Target resolution enforces opponent controller and exact permanent kind, so a Digimon stacked on a Tamer is not a Tamer target.

Snapshot drift: the generated Security record has the same self-target shape but incorrectly uses `from: ["hand"]`; direct Security execution therefore would not model this card's printed self-play behavior. The generated When Digivolving and On Deletion target records match the direct corrected clauses. The direct module and Security primitive are executable authority; the snapshot was not edited.

Direct correction: none in this range for BT5-112; the existing direct override is already the proven correction for the stale generated Security route and its focused tests guard that behavior.
