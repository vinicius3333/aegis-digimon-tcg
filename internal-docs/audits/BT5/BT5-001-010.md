## Card-by-card findings

### BT5-001 — Koromon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Red Digi-Egg, level 2, play cost `-1` sentinel, 0 DP, no evolution cost, In-Training, Lesser, rarity U, maximum 4. It has no main effect. Its inherited text is: `[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] If this Digimon has [Omnimon] or [Greymon] (other than [DoruGreymon], [BurningGreymon], or [DexDoruGreymon]) in its name, trigger ＜Draw 1＞.`

The module uses an inherited `WhenAttacking` effect with `frequency: "OncePerTurn"`, draws one card for the owner, and uses `selfHasNameContaining` with the three explicit name exclusions. `conditions.ts` performs live-top-card name matching and `actions/resources.ts` draws from the source owner's deck. The focused test covers an eligible Greymon/Omnimon family host, all three exclusions, unrelated-name negatives, a legal BT5-001 → BT5-010 stack, and the once-per-turn repeat attack case. Same-name behavior was cross-checked against BT5-015/BT5-016 family implementations.

### BT5-002 — Tsunomon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Blue Digi-Egg, level 2, play cost `-1`, 0 DP, no evolution cost, In-Training, Lesser, rarity U, maximum 4. Its inherited text is: `[Your Turn] While this Digimon has [Garurumon] or [Omnimon] in its name, it gets +1000 DP.`

The module is an inherited `YourTurn` self aura for +1000 DP gated by `selfHasNameContaining` for Garurumon or Omnimon. The static-effect path rederives the aura only on the source owner's turn, targets the host itself, and removes the bonus when the name gate or turn gate no longer holds. The focused test covers Garurumon, Omnimon, an unrelated baseline, owner-turn versus opponent-turn behavior, and the legal BT5-002 → BT1-029 → BT1-036 → BT1-040 → BT4-114 evolution path. BT5-024 is a same-mechanism peer for an inherited name gate.

### BT5-003 — Pickmon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Yellow Digi-Egg, level 2, play cost `-1`, 0 DP, no evolution cost, In-Training, Minor, rarity U, maximum 4. Its inherited text is: `[When Attacking] If you have 3 or more Digimon in play, 1 of your opponent's Digimon gets -1000 DP for the turn.`

The module uses an inherited `WhenAttacking` effect, counts three or more own `Digimon` in the explicit `battleArea` zone, then applies -1000 DP for the turn to exactly one opposing Digimon. The explicit zone is required by comprehensive rule §15-1-7 for this unspecified battle-area reference; it also prevents a breeding-area card from being counted or affected. `countMatching`, `permanentMatchesFilter`, and `resolvePermanentTargets` provide the matching/count/one-target seams. The focused test covers two versus three own battle-area Digimon, a breeding-area count negative, one opposing target without touching own Digimon, expiry at turn end, and legal BT5-003 → BT1-045 evolution followed by movement to the battle area. Q1282 was reviewed for the inherited-stack context.

### BT5-004 — Yokomon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Green Digi-Egg, level 2, play cost `-1`, 0 DP, no evolution cost, In-Training, Bulb, rarity U, maximum 4. Its inherited text is: `[Your Turn] When this card is trashed due to activating this Digimon's ＜Digi-Burst＞, 1 of your Digimon gets +2000 DP for the turn.` There is no printed once-per-turn clause.

The module correctly uses an inherited `YourTurn` `SubTrigger` for `onDigiBurstCardDiscarded`, with `sourceFilter: { isSelfRef: true }`, and applies +2000 DP for the turn to one own Digimon. The sub-trigger installer preserves the discarded inherited source identity, and the primitive emits a dedicated Digi-Burst batch before ordinary per-card discard events. This distinguishes the qualifying event from another host's Digi-Burst and from ordinary stack trashing. The focused test covers all three distinctions, a legal BT5-004 → BT5-046 Digi-Burst stack, own-target selection, and turn-end expiry. BT5-050 and BT5-046 are same-mechanism peers.

Snapshot drift: the generated record contains only a generic `YourTurn` +2000 `ModifyDP`; it omits the `SubTrigger`, event, and self-source filter. The direct module is authoritative and was not altered to match the stale snapshot.

### BT5-006 — Gigimon — provisional 8/10

### BT5-007 — Agumon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Red Digimon, level 3, play cost 3, 2000 DP, evolution Red level 2 for 0, Rookie, Vaccine, Reptile, rarity C, maximum 4. Its effect is: `[On Play] Reveal 3 cards from the top of your deck. Add 1 Digimon card with [Greymon] in its name (other than [DoruGreymon], [BurningGreymon], or [DexDoruGreymon]) and 1 Digimon card with [Omnimon] in its name among them to your hand. Place the remaining cards at the bottom of your deck in any order.`

The module uses `RevealAdd` with three cards, two independent one-card hand slots, the Greymon name filter plus all three exclusions, the Omnimon name filter, and `deckBottom` for the remainder. `runRevealAdd` keeps already selected instances unavailable for later slots, permits either slot to resolve when the other category is absent, and returns untaken cards to the bottom; Q1284 confirms the one-category behavior. The focused test covers both categories, one category only, all exclusions, and the expected remaining deck. BT1-067 is a same-mechanism RevealAdd peer, and the BT5-007 → BT5-010 legal stack is covered by the BT5-010 tests.

### BT5-008 — Gaossmon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Red Digimon, level 3, play cost 3, 2000 DP, evolution Red level 2 for 0, Rookie, Virus, Reptile, rarity C, maximum 4. Its effect is: `[Your Turn] Your other [Gaossmon] all get +3000 DP.[Opponent's Turn] Your opponent can't reduce digivolution costs.`

The module has two direct effects. The `YourTurn` aura targets all own non-self Gaossmon by name and grants +3000 DP; `count: "all"` is required by “all.” The `OpponentsTurn` effect installs `RestrictCostReduction` for the opponent seat and `digivolve` cost type. The static interpreter resolves turn-relative seats, clears/rederives continuous effects at turn boundaries, and `blocksCostReduction` blocks reductions while leaving fixed-cost effects available. Q1285–Q1287 were reviewed for Digisorption/reduction versus fixed-cost behavior. The focused test covers all other own Gaossmon, unrelated and opposing Digimon, owner/opponent turn gates, and the restriction channel.

Snapshot drift: the generated record collapses the aura to a one-target `ModifyDP` and loses the `Aura`/`count: "all"` shape. The direct module is authoritative and the snapshot was not edited.

Same-mechanism peers BT5-021 and BT5-033 confirm the opponent-turn digivolution-reduction restriction; ST12-03 confirms the shared restriction primitive with a different seat/cost channel.

The direct module is authoritative and the snapshot was not edited.
