## Card-by-card findings

### BT5-031 — MetalGarurumon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Blue Digimon, level 6, play cost 11, 11000 DP, evolution from Blue level 5 for 3, Mega, Data, Cyborg, rarity R, maximum 4. Its effect is: `[When Digivolving] If a Digimon card with [Garurumon] in its name (other than [KendoGarurumon]) is in this Digimon's digivolution cards, return 1 of your opponent's Digimon with an [On Deletion] effect to the bottom of its owner's deck. Trash all of the digivolution cards of that Digimon.` Its inherited text is: `[When Attacking][Once Per Turn] Gain 1 memory.`

The corrected direct module uses `selfDigivolutionStackHasTrait` with `match: "name"` and `excludeNames: ["KendoGarurumon"]`, then canonically returns exactly one opposing Digimon whose definition text matches `[On Deletion]` to deck bottom. The Return primitive trashes attached sources as rule teardown. BT6-002 Q1399 proves that the printed source-trash sentence is explanatory and must not notify inherited source-trash watchers. Its inherited `When Attacking` effect grants one memory with `OncePerTurn`. The focused tests cover the legal evolution stack, target/exclusion boundaries, inherited text, effect-granted `[On Deletion]`, final source destinations, and the corrected BT6-002 watcher no-draw case.

Snapshot drift: the generated record uses a `nameOrTrait` match of `trait` for `[On Deletion]` and adds a generic Trash action after Return. The corrected direct module's text matching and canonical Q1399 Return are executable authority; the snapshot was not edited.

### BT5-032 — Hexeblaumon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Blue Digimon, level 6, play cost 12, 11000 DP, evolution from Blue level 5 for 3, Mega, Data, Magic Knight, rarity SR, maximum 4. Its effect is: `[When Attacking] Trash up to 2 digivolution cards from the bottom of 1 of your opponent's Digimon. Then, if your opponent has a Digimon with no digivolution cards in play, this Digimon gains ＜Jamming＞ (This Digimon can't be deleted in battles against Security Digimon) for the turn.[All Turns] Your opponent's Digimon with no digivolution cards can't attack or block.`

The direct module uses `TrashDigivolution` against one opposing Digimon, from the bottom, amount 2, `upTo: true`; it then grants Jamming to the attacking source for the turn when an opposing no-source Digimon exists. The action ordering means the no-source condition is evaluated after source trashing, as confirmed by Q1310. Separate all-turn auras apply attack and block restrictions to all opposing Digimon with no sources; the static ledger clears those restrictions when Hexeblaumon leaves play. Q1311 and Q1312 confirm that the effect is evaluated at `When Attacking` resolution rather than retroactively during security processing. The focused tests cover bottom-source trashing, Jamming with and without a remaining source, one-target/two-card selection, attack/block restrictions, and removal of those restrictions when the source leaves; `BT4-034` and `BT5-097` are the relevant bottom-trash peers.

Snapshot drift: the generated record uses a generic Trash action with `count: 2` rather than the direct bottom-stack `TrashDigivolution`, and collapses the separate attack/block restrictions into one combined restriction. The direct module is authoritative and the snapshot was not edited.
