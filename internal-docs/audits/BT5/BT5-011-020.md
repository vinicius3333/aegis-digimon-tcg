## Shared interpreter and peer evidence

- `ModifyDP` in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/board.ts` resolves the
  filtered permanent set, applies the declared amount, and honors
  `forTheTurn` duration. `candidatePermanents` and
  `resolvePermanentTargets` enforce controller, kind, self-exclusion, count,
  and DP boundaries.
- Blocker is represented as a continuous keyword and is consumed by the
  combat block timing path; `GainMemory` accepts the negative amount used by
  BT5-012. `BT5-026.ts`/`.test.ts` provides the same Blocker-plus-minus-two
  memory pattern.
- `selfDigivolutionStackHasTrait` in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/conditions.ts` delegates to the
  stack matcher, which checks source cards below the top card. The matching
  layer applies name tokens and exclusions against each source definition.
  This is the basis for BT5-015 and BT5-016's MetalGreymon/Greymon filters.
- Alternate Shoutmon evolution metadata is interpreted by
  `matchingAlternateDigivolutionRequirement` and the normal digivolve legality
  path; the direct BT5-014 record's alternate requirement is names `Shoutmon`,
  cost 4, `isAlternate: true`. The breeding-area exclusion comes from the
  normal effect-activation boundary. BT5-009 is the nearby Shoutmon/Blitz
  peer, and its tests cover reveal and inherited Blitz behavior.
- `GrantCanAttackUnsuspended` is routed through the combat interpreter and
  recorded in the continuous ledger with `forTheTurn`. Combat legality allows
  unsuspended Digimon targets only when the granted condition is active.
  BT4-090 and ST12-08 are same-mechanism peers; their tests establish the
  positive grant and baseline illegal unsuspended-target path.
- BT5-018's `Trash` action binds the selected hand card as
  `trashedRedDigimon`; `AddDPFromTrashedCard` consumes the just-trashed card's
  DP, applies it to self for the turn, and no-ops when the optional cost is
  declined. The adjacent action binding is important for repeated attacks and
  is supported by the removal and board interpreter primitives.
- BT5-019's `PlaceUnder` moves the selected red hand card to the source stack
  at the top. Its `digivolutionCards` scaling counts only matching named
  sources and multiplies the one-target deletion count; the target boundary is
  inclusive at 5,000 DP. BT7-017 provides a same-mechanism source-placement
  and scaling peer.
- BT5-020's `RevealAdd` reveals exactly three, treats the Garurumon and Omnimon
  entries as independent one-card slots, prevents one revealed card from
  satisfying both slots, and bottoms every untaken card. BT5-009 is the
  closest two-category reveal peer, and BT5-024's test exercises a legal
  Gabumon-to-Garurumon evolution stack containing BT5-020.
