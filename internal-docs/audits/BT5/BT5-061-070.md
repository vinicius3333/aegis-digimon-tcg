`coverage: "full"` and an empty residual list. The direct TypeScript modules

a fresh card-by-card audit. No direct behavior correction was proven necessary;
one focused test was strengthened for BT5-062 to prove that its opponent-turn
battle watcher remains available for a second qualifying event.

BT5-069, and BT5-070. Per the audit instruction,
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was not edited; the direct
`registerIrCard` modules are the executable authority. BT5-062's direct module
already omits a one-shot flag, matching the snapshot and the printed repeatable
trigger; the added test makes that cardinality explicit.

The shared `SubTriggerEvent` union now includes the already-supported
`whenSecurityBattleEnded` event required to type BT5-065's Security timing.

## Card-by-card findings

### BT5-061 — Commandramon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Black Digimon, level 3, play cost 4, 2000 DP, evolution from
Black level 2 for 0, Rookie, Virus, Cyborg and D-Brigade, rarity C, maximum 4.
Its effect is: `＜Blocker＞ (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend
this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.)`

The direct module represents Blocker as a Static keyword and contains no extra
action, which is the correct persistent keyword registration. The shared
Blocker/combat seam suspends this source only when the controller chooses the
redirect and changes the attack target to this Digimon. The focused test covers
redirecting an opponent's attack, the source becoming suspended, and the
source being deleted. Same-mechanism Blocker peers and the legal Black level-2
to level-3 evolution path were reviewed; no trait, inherited, Security, or
alternate evolution clause applies.

Snapshot comparison found no material difference from the direct module.

### BT5-062 — Mekanorimon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Black Digimon, level 4, play cost 4, 6000 DP, evolution from
Black level 3 for 3, Champion, Virus, Machine, rarity C, maximum 4. Its effect
is: `＜Blocker＞ (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend this
Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.)[Your Turn] This Digimon
can't attack.[Opponent's Turn] When this Digimon deletes an opponent's Digimon
in battle and survives, unsuspend it.`

The direct module has a Static Blocker keyword, an owner-turn self attack
restriction, and an opponent-turn `whenDeletesInBattle` SubTrigger anchored by
`sourceFilter: { isSelfRef: true }`. The unsuspend action targets the source
Mekanorimon, so the shared combat hook only fires when this Digimon is the
surviving battle winner; it does not react to another own Digimon's deletion.
The subscription intentionally has no `once` flag: the printed effect has no
once-per-turn qualifier and the shared SubTrigger primitive otherwise removes a
subscription after its first event. The focused test covers Blocker and the
owner-turn attack restriction, one actual opponent-turn battle deletion in
which Mekanorimon survives, and two manually published matching battle-delete
events to prove the watcher remains repeatable. The `whenDeletesInBattle`
source-anchor behavior was compared with BT11-059 and other battle-watcher
peers; the ordinary Black level-3 to level-4 evolution path has no additional
restriction.

Snapshot comparison found no material difference from the corrected direct
module.

### BT5-063 — Kurisarimon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Black Digimon, level 4, play cost 5, 4000 DP, evolution from
Black level 3 for 2, Champion, Unknown, Unidentified, rarity C, maximum 4. Its
effect is: `[When Digivolving] If you don't have an [Arata Sanada] card in play,

Digimon gain ＜Rush＞. (This Digimon can attack the turn it comes into play.)`

with an exact `[Arata Sanada]` name filter, an exact-name `youHaveNone`
condition, and optional processing. The inherited owner-turn aura targets all
other own Digimon, excludes the source, and uses the live `isSameName` matcher
to grant Rush permanently while the stack source remains active. This preserves
the distinction between a same-name peer and the inherited source itself.
The focused tests cover the real BT10-058 base to BT5-063 evolution, free play
of BT5-090 Arata Sanada, exact name matching, the optional refusal, and Rush on
other same-name Digimon. They also cover the legal BT5-084 source under
BT5-063 and a subsequent legal evolution into a different-name BT18-102, where
Q1338's loss-of-Rush behavior is asserted. BT5-059, BT5-084, BT5-029, and
BT5-055 were reviewed as alternate-evolution, inherited-source, and same-name
peers.

Snapshot drift: the generated record omits the exact Arata target name and
`isSameName` target condition, and encodes the `youHaveNone` name match in an
older raw shape. The direct module is authoritative and the snapshot was not
edited.

### BT5-065 — Shademon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Black Digimon, level 4, play cost 6, 5000 DP, evolution from
Black level 3 for 2, Champion, Virus, Unidentified, rarity U, maximum 4. Its
effect is: `[Security] At the end of the battle, play this card without paying
its memory cost.＜Blocker＞ (When an opponent's Digimon attacks, you may suspend
this Digimon to force the opponent to attack it instead.)[Your Turn] This
Digimon can't attack.`

The direct module puts Blocker in a Static keyword block, keeps the owner-turn
attack restriction separate, and installs a Security `whenSecurityBattleEnded`
SubTrigger whose nested PlayWithoutCost action plays this security instance
without memory. The security-check primitive resolves the Security effect while
the card is in security, then performs the battle and fires the battle-ended
window; the play therefore occurs after the battle and before the next check.
The focused test uses an actual security attack with BT5-065 in security,
checks the battle outcome and security count, confirms the play event's order
between two checks, verifies memory is not paid, and covers Blocker redirection
and the owner-turn attack restriction. Q1339–Q1341 and EX1-065/security
play peers were reviewed. No alternate or inherited evolution clause applies.

Snapshot drift: the generated record models an immediate Security play and
grants Blocker from inside that Security action. It does not preserve the
printed end-of-battle timing or the direct Static keyword registration. The
direct module is authoritative and the snapshot was not edited.

### BT5-070 — MetalGarurumon — provisional 8/10

Catalog contract: Black Digimon, level 6, play cost 11, 11000 DP, evolution
from Black level 5 for 3, Mega, Virus, Cyborg, rarity SR, maximum 4. Its effect
is: `＜Reboot＞ (Unsuspend this Digimon during your opponent's unsuspend
phase.)[When Digivolving] ＜Digi-Burst 2＞ (Trash 2 of this Digimon's
Digivolution cards to activate the effect below.)・Delete 1 of your opponent's
Digimon with a play cost of 6 or less. If no Digimon was deleted by this
effect, trash the top card of your opponent's security stack.`

The direct module registers Reboot as a Static keyword. Its When Digivolving
sequence uses a Digi-Burst cost that trashes exactly two cards from this
Digimon's own digivolution stack, is optional, and aborts the following actions
when declined. If accepted, it deletes exactly one opposing Digimon with play
cost at most 6, then conditionally trashes one opposing security card only when
the deletion process deleted no Digimon. The shared cost primitive preserves
the host stack and the shared conditional reads the deletion count from this
effect, so a valid but deletion-immune target still leads to security trashing.
The focused tests cover a real evolution into BT5-070, two-source Digi-Burst
trashing, a play-cost-6 deletion, the no-eligible-target branch, a play-cost
greater than 6 negative, optional decline with no downstream processing, a
deletion-immune play-cost-6 target, and Reboot at the opponent's unsuspend
phase. BT5-046 and other Digi-Burst cards were reviewed as same-mechanism peers;
the legal BT10-013 to BT5-070 evolution stack and source transitions were
covered.

Snapshot drift: the generated record uses a generic trash target rather than
the host's own digivolution-card stack, lacks the direct optional Digi-Burst
cost metadata, and represents the no-deletion condition as raw text rather than
the executable `ifThisEffectDidNotDelete` condition. The direct module is
authoritative and the snapshot was not edited.
