## Shared interpreter and peer evidence

- `candidatePermanents` and `resolvePermanentTargets` in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/targeting/permanents.ts:10-124` and
  `:336-440` apply controller, kind, self, count, state, and target-boundary
  filters. This is the targeting foundation for Deramon, BanchoLillymon,
  Rafflesimon, Rosemon, and Argomon.
- `ModifyDP` in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/board.ts:153-205`
  resolves the filtered targets, applies the scaling amount, and records the
  declared duration. The BT5-053 scaling filter excludes self and counts only
  other own suspended battle-area Digimon of kind Digimon. BT5-006 and
  BT5-042 are same-mechanism ModifyDP peers.
- The `Return` path in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/removal.ts:574-705` calls
  `returnToDeck`; `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:3678-3775`
  collects the entire selected permanent, puts only its top card face-down in
  the owner's deck, and trashes attached digivolution cards face-up.
  `collectForReturn` at `primitives.ts:5564-5587` confirms the selected stack
  is the only stack collected. BT5-031 and BT5-097 provide related
  return-to-deck peers. Therefore BT5-055's single direct Return correctly
  implements the printed source trashing without a second Trash action.
- `Restrict` in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/restrictions.ts:49-122`
  normalizes `attackOrBlock` into both attack and block restrictions. Combat
  legality consumes those restrictions at
  `apps/api/src/engine/combat/legality.ts:185-205` and `:345-373`. BT5-102
  and EX4-027 are same-mechanism attack/block restriction peers.
- SubTrigger subscriptions preserve their event, source filter, once-per-turn
  key, source anchor, and duration in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/subtriggers.ts:31-143`; the BT5-056 direct
  `SubTrigger` therefore watches the actual `onDigiBurstCardDiscarded` event,
  filters to an own Digimon host, and uses the shared once-per-turn ledger.
  BT5-004 and BT5-050 are same-event Digi-Burst peers.
- `RevealAdd` is dispatched by `runAction` at
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/runAction.ts:735-750` and
  implemented at `reveal.ts:108-209`. It reveals exactly the requested count,
  tracks taken cards in a set so independent add slots cannot reuse one card,
  and returns remaining cards through the declared rest destination. The
  play branch at `reveal.ts:374-411` uses `playInstances` with
  `payCost: false`; this supports BT5-060's free Monitamon play. BT5-007,
  BT5-009, BT5-020, and BT5-046 are same-mechanism reveal peers.
- Digisorption is intentionally an intrinsic hand-time cost interaction.
  `registerDigisorptionFromEffects` in
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/keywords.ts:334-357`
  records a `wouldDigivolve` reduction with a suspend cost, while
  `registration/module.ts:75-109` registers that reduction and omits the
  marker from the live resulting Digimon. `GameEngine.ts:1495-1560` and
  `:1887-1944` apply the optional suspend and exact cost reduction. This
  matches the comprehensive Digisorption rule and the BT2/BT3 Digisorption
  family; it prevents Argomon's own keyword marker from reducing a later
  evolution after Argomon enters play.
- The security interpreter's only look operation is the security-specific
  `lookAndMayAddToHand` at
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/security.ts:264-282`;
  generic deck top look has no typed state-changing action. This is material
  for BT5-060 and is recorded as the sole static ambiguity below.
