## Method and shared evidence

The audit compared each card in order against the committed catalog, local KB
and rules, direct TypeScript module, generated effects snapshot, shared
interpreter primitives, colocated focused tests, traits and same-mechanism
peers, and a rules-legal evolution/source stack. The primary references are:

- Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:29906–30153` contains
  BT2-031 through BT2-040, including colors, levels, costs, DP, evolution
  requirements, traits, and printed effect/inherited-effect text.
- Card-specific KB: `data/kb/qa.json:14561–14609` contains Q1007/Q1008 for
  BT2-032, Q1009 for BT2-034, Q1011/Q1012 for BT2-039, and Q1013 for BT2-040.
  No card-specific QA entry was found for BT2-031, BT2-033, BT2-035, BT2-036,
  BT2-037, or BT2-038, and no card-specific errata or banlist entry was found
  for this range. The reproducible query form is
  `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>`.
- Generated snapshot: `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:56854–57149`
  contains a full record and empty residual array for every card.
- Shared interpreter: `conditions.ts:173–177,192–198,218–222,270–290,546–551`
  implements live keyword, controller/zone count, and turn/phase conditions;
  `scaling.ts:22–98` and `matching/permanent.ts:147–185` resolve explicit
  battle-area scope and controller seats; `actions/statics.ts:17–81`
  implements DP, Security Attack, and Security-DP auras;
  `actions/security.ts:288–407,429–551` implements face-down placement and
  deck-to-security Recovery; `actions/subTrigger.ts:57–63,143–181,289–296,
  347–371,455–458,997–1036` normalizes unsuspend/deletion watchers and their
  inherited source/frequency behavior; `actions/play.ts:104–258` and
  `primitives.ts:830–840,946–956` implement optional free plays and suppression
  of nested On Play effects; `primitives.ts:3387–3417` fires
  `whenUnsuspended` only after a real transition; `effect.ts:384–403,468–480`
  carries timing and Once Per Turn state; and
  `registration/module.ts:291–341` registers compiled IR.
- Rules: `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:654–663` defines Digimon inherited
  effects; `679–682` defines Security Digimon; `717–755` defines stack
  identity, lower cards, and face-down information; `794–807` defines card
  orientation; `813–833` distinguishes deletion from trashing;
  `1046–1074` defines turn/unsuspend/main phase order; `1900–1907` defines
  default battle-area scope and Security Digimon references;
  `1925–1936` defines inherited-source semantics; `1958–2008` defines
  one-at-a-time and simultaneous pending activation; `2039–2081` defines
  trigger and processing conditions; `2170–2183` anchors deletion triggers to
  the original top card; `2709–2740` defines relevant effect timings; and
  `2830–2868` defines Recovery and Draw.

All ten direct modules register executable behavior exclusively with
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and contain no duplicate `registerCard`
registration. All ten direct records declare `coverage: "full"` and
`residual: []`; those metadata claims still await the deferred gates.

The snapshot has three semantic-shape ambiguities relative to the direct IR:
BT2-032 omits the direct `phaseIs Main` condition from its
`whenUnsuspended` watcher; BT2-038 represents Tamer On Play suppression as an
additional `DisableTimingEffect`, while the direct IR uses the shared
`suppressOnPlayEffects: true` play option; and BT2-040's snapshot spells out a
self source target while the direct IR uses the shared implicit self form.
The direct forms are supported by the inspected interpreter seams and are
recorded as validation ambiguities rather than silently treated as generated
regeneration proof.
