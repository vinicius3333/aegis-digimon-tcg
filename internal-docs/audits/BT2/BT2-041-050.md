## Scoring policy

Each card receives five integer components worth two points each:

1. Catalog and rules — exact printed data and applicable comprehensive-rule sections.
2. IR trace — direct module action mapping, generated snapshot comparison, and interpreter primitive trace.
3. Behavioral proof — focused tests and the added aggregate IR assertions are present; they were not executed.
4. Peer and stack proof — same-mechanism peers, traits, and legal source stacks were inspected.
5. Executed gates — deliberately 0/2 because the task prohibits tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, and git diff --check.

Nine cards below are **8/10 provisional**. BT2-049 is **7/10
provisional** because its exact duration was corrected but the direct IR still
captures only the opponent Digimon present at activation; whether the printed
next-unsuspend restriction must also cover Digimon that enter later remains
unresolved. The range total is 79/100 provisional points. No card is above
8/10 and this report makes no completion claim.

## Static-only evidence policy

I read the committed catalog, card-specific KB results, rules KB, direct TypeScript modules, generated effects snapshot, shared IR/interpreter primitives, focused tests, traits, peer cards, legal evolution stacks, and banlist data where applicable.

No behavioral test, typecheck, lint, formatter, or git diff --check was run. The exact deferred commands are recorded at the end of this report.
