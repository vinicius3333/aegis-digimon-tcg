## Scope and canonical result

This report audits BT2-061 through BT2-070 in strict ascending order against
the committed catalog, local rules and KB, direct TypeScript modules, generated
effects snapshot, shared IR primitives, colocated focused tests, traits and
same-mechanism peers, and legal evolution/source stacks. Every executable card
module in the range registers only through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`;
no duplicate `registerCard` registration was added or retained.

All ten cards have direct compiled IR with `coverage: "full"` and
`residual: []`, and each printed clause maps to an inspected interpreter seam.
The executed-gates component is intentionally `0/2` because execution was
explicitly prohibited, so every card is capped at provisional `8/10`; no card
is claimed complete and the collection is not claimed complete.
