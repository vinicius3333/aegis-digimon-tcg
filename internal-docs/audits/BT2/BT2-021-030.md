catalog, card-specific local KB output, comprehensive rules, direct TypeScript

## Per-card findings

### BT2-021 Veemon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog: Blue Lv.3 Rookie, 2000 DP, play cost 3, Blue Lv.2 evolution cost 0,
  `Free` attribute and `Mini Dragon` type. The inherited text at
  `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:29658-29676` is
  `[Your Turn][Once Per Turn] When this Digimon becomes unsuspended during your
  main phase, trigger Draw 1`.
- KB: Q1001 establishes that targeting an already-active host does not count as
  becoming unsuspended. The catalog's main-phase and once-per-turn boundaries
  are independently retained in the IR.
- Implementation: `apps/api/src/cards/BT2/BT2-021.ts:8-41` uses a
  `YourTurn` inherited `SubTrigger` for `whenUnsuspended`, self source filter,
  `phaseIs Main`, `Draw mine 1`, and `frequency: "OncePerTurn"`; it declares
  `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and only `registerIrCard`.
- Proof: `apps/api/src/cards/BT2/BT2-021.test.ts:8-71` covers a real host
  unsuspend, Q1001's already-active negative, Active-phase and opponent-turn
  negatives, and once-per-turn suppression after a second real unsuspend. The
  host is `BT2-026` with `BT2-021` underneath, a legal Blue Lv.3-to-Lv.4
  evolution/source stack. Blue Rookie peers BT2-022 and BT2-023 were compared;
  neither has an inherited trigger that changes this source behavior.
- Status: timing, source identity, phase, frequency, controller, and Draw
  result are mapped and covered. No direct implementation correction was
  required; deferred execution keeps the canonical score at 8/10.

## Corrections and ambiguities

- Removed seven range-local `@ts-nocheck` directives from the typed BT2-021–030 modules.
- Added direct focused-test imports for the previously side-effect-free vanilla

`apps/api/src/cards/BT2/BT2-021-030.audit.test.ts:1-180`. It imports all ten
modules, proves each runtime record is `coverage: "full"` with no residual, and
asserts every printed trigger, target boundary, cost type, keyword, duration,
inherited clause, and BT2-030 effect count. The aggregate test is intentionally
deferred like the focused tests.

The committed `effects.json` snapshot is stale or incomplete in five places:

- `BT2-021` at `effects.json:56673-56691` omits the direct module's Main-phase
  `fireCondition`.
- `BT2-023` at `:56693-56713` says `costType: "memory"` and omits the direct
  self-target, hand-resident, and battle-area fields; the catalog clearly says
  play cost, and the direct module/tests use play cost.
- `BT2-028` at `:56759-56797` omits the direct inherited Main-phase
  `fireCondition`.
- `BT2-029` at `:56798-56814` uses generic `beBlocked` instead of the dedicated
  source-less-blocker restriction.
- `BT2-030` at `:56815-56852` contains an extra own-Digimon `Trash` action in
  On Play and also uses generic `beBlocked`.

These are recorded as snapshot-authority ambiguities, not copied into modules:
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:48-51` and
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts:7-16` explicitly make
the directly registered module authoritative when the snapshot differs. The
later verification pass should decide whether a separate snapshot refresh is
owned; this worker did not alter generated/shared data.

There are no unresolved card-rules ambiguities beyond the stale snapshot
records above. The no-Q&A cards (BT2-022, BT2-024, BT2-025, BT2-026, and BT2-027)
are supported by their catalog text, general rules, direct modules, and tests.
No browser flow is exposed or needed for these server-authoritative card
effects; the engine testkit is the observable source-stack and combat harness.
