## Method and shared evidence

The audit compared each card in order against the committed catalog, local KB
and rules, direct module, generated effects snapshot, interpreter primitives,
colocated focused tests, peer cards, and a rules-legal evolution/source stack.
The primary references are:

- Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:29216–29407` contains
  BT2-001 through BT2-010, including card kind, color, level, DP, evolution
  requirements, traits, and printed effect/inherited-effect text.
- Card-specific KB: `data/kb/qa.json:14425–14449` contains Q993 for BT2-002,
  Q994 for BT2-004, and Q995 for BT2-006. There is no card-specific QA entry
  for BT2-001, BT2-003, BT2-005, BT2-007, BT2-008, BT2-009, or BT2-010, and no
  card-specific errata or banlist entry was found for this range. The exact
  deferred query form is `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>`.
- Generated snapshot: `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:56323–56507`
  has full coverage and empty residual arrays for all ten records.
- Shared interpreter: `conditions.ts:173–177,270–290,546–551` implements
  live-keyword, zone-count, turn, and phase conditions;
  `matching/permanent.ts:162–185,531–539` handles explicit field zones,
  self-exclusion, and same-name matching; `actions/statics.ts:17–81`
  implements continuous DP and Security-DP auras;
  `actions/removal.ts:419–423,505–521` distinguishes trashing and takes deck
  cards from the top; `actions/subTrigger.ts:57–63,455–458,983–1034`
  normalizes `whenUnsuspended`, binds inherited self watchers to the host, and
  carries watcher frequency; `effect.ts:384–403,468–480` carries turn scope
  and Once Per Turn semantics; and `registration/module.ts:291–341` builds and
  registers the compiled IR.
- Transition and timing seams: `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:3387–3417`
  fires `whenUnsuspended` only for a real suspended-to-unsuspended transition;
  `apps/api/src/engine/GameEngine.ts:1343–1360` applies the unsuspend-phase
  transition, including Reboot handling.
- Rules: `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:321–330` defines the inherited-effect
  field; `654–663` says a field Digimon gains inherited effects;
  `679–682` defines Security Digimon; `717–739` defines stacks and explains
  that the lower cards are part of the top card; `794–807` defines orientation;
  `813–833` distinguishes deletion from trashing; `1046–1074` defines phase
  order and the unsuspend phase; `1690–1745` defines attack timing and valid
  targets; `1818–1879` defines Security Digimon checks and battles;
  `1900–1936` defines default battle-area scope and inherited-source behavior;
  `2039–2075` defines trigger and processing conditions; and
  `2705–2745,2897–2910` define the relevant timings and Reboot.

All ten direct modules register executable behavior exclusively through
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` and contain no duplicate `registerCard`
registration. All ten direct records say `coverage: "full"` and
`residual: []`; those metadata claims still await the deferred gates.

The generated snapshot is not fully semantically equivalent to the direct IR
in several records: BT2-002 omits `phaseIs Main`, BT2-003 retains a broad
`modifyDP`/all-own-Digimon shape instead of the direct Security-DP action,
BT2-004 omits `phaseIs Active`, BT2-006 omits the structured `isSameName`
predicate, and BT2-007 omits `zone: "deck"`. The direct modules contain the
more specific behavior required by the catalog, rules, and interpreter seams;
these snapshot divergences are recorded as a validation ambiguity rather than
silently treated as a passing generated-regeneration proof.
