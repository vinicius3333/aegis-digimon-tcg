## Shared evidence and generated-snapshot comparison

`registerIrCard` in
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` normalizes
the direct record, stores it in the runtime compiled-card registry, and
registers the shared interpreter module. The runtime `compiledEffects` entry
is therefore overwritten by the direct module when that module is imported;
the generated JSON is useful comparison evidence but is not a substitute for
the committed card module.

The following snapshot divergences were recorded without modifying the
snapshot:

- BT11-021, BT11-022, BT11-023, BT11-026, BT11-027, and BT11-028 match the
  direct records for the audited clauses and empty residuals.
- BT11-024's generated filter flattens the blue-level-3 and Aqua/Sea Animal
  alternatives into one conjunction and omits the bottom destination. The
  direct IR retains the required `or` branches and now explicitly sets
  `position: "bottom"`.
- BT11-025's generated first action omits the direct `isYourTurn` half of the
  static condition. The direct IR requires both the controller turn and the
  opponent hand count of at least eight.
- BT11-029's generated inherited action has the stale `effectType: "your "`
  shape and no Rina target. The direct IR targets a controller-owned Rina
  Shinomiya and activates one of her `OnPlay` effects.
- BT11-030's generated record retains the old `Static` alias trigger and its
  `PlaceUnder` actions omit the hand/under-Tamer zone union and bottom
  destination. The direct IR now uses `Rule` for the universal aliases and
  sets both source zones plus `position: "bottom"`.

The shared placement path in
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts` and
`costs.ts` treats an absent position as placement directly below the current
top (`belowTop: true`); `position: "bottom"` is required for the printed
bottom digivolution-card destination. The shared
`universalNameAliasesFor` seam in
`apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts` only exposes
self-targeted name grants from `Rule` effects to every-zone DigiXros material
matching. Those two seams drove the concrete corrections below.
