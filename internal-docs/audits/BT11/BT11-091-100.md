## Shared evidence and generated-snapshot comparison

The registration seam in `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/compiledCards.ts` and `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts` consumes the direct module after `registerIrCard` registration. `irCardModule` routes `StartOfYourTurn` and `StartOfYourMainPhase`, Option `Main`/`Security`, static cost replacements, and reactive `YourTurn`/`OpponentsTurn` entries into their shared timing builders. This lets the card modules use the same server-authoritative target, cost, duration, security, and event paths as peer cards.

The mechanism traces relevant to this slice are:

- `ModifyDP`, `Restrict`, `Delete`, `Return`, `PlayWithoutCost`, `Draw`, `GainMemory`, `TrashDigivolution`, and `Suspend` resolve through shared action dispatch and live target matching. Controller scopes, levels, colors, traits, names, DP bounds, superlatives, stack-card counts, and duration boundaries are not client-provided.
- `SubTrigger` listens to shared evolution, attack, and unsuspend events. Its trigger subject remains available to `sourceRef: "triggerSubject"`, and the once-per-turn wrapper uses the source instance/effect identity.
- Nested `Replacement` actions with `mode: "reduceCost"` are collected by the shared reducer registry for BT11-096, BT11-099, and BT11-100. Their own Option use is reduced only when a matching red, blue, or yellow Tamer is currently in the owner's battle area.
- `ActivateMain` resolves a Security effect through the card's registered direct `Main` record. BT11-097's `ActivateEffect` is normalized by the shared borrowed-effect seam, and `useLenderAsSource` makes the selected red Vaccine Digimon the borrowed-effect source while preserving the Crimson Flare activation context.
- `PlaceUnder`/DigiXros replacement matching distinguishes cards under Tamers from cards under Digimon. `PlayWithoutCost` from `digivolutionCards` resolves against a live blue host stack, and the following BT11-098 return sees the post-play board.
- `TrashDigivolution` removes the top three available stack cards, then BT11-099's `digivolutionCards: "none"` target is evaluated after that movement. A BT11-100 `untilOpponentTurnEnd` modifier remains active across the turn boundary through the shared duration primitive.

The comprehensive rules evidence used for the above includes §4-1 for memory/cost, §4-4-2 and §4-7 for Tamer and digivolution-card stacks, §6-2-1-1 for start-of-turn timing before the main phase, §7-2 for DigiXros material legality, and §11-2-7 for declared player attacks and redirection. The Q&A entries add the following material boundaries:

- Q2117 and Q2124 require BT11-092 and BT11-095 Start of Your Main Phase effects not to resolve after an earlier effect has already moved memory to the opponent and ended the turn.
- Q2118 confirms BT11-092 may still redirect an attack when the original attack was declared against a player, even if Raid later changes the target.
- Q2119–Q2121 establish that BT11-093's Option immunity includes the relevant opponent Option effects, expires at the opponent-turn boundary, and does not permanently erase an Option effect after the restriction expires.
- Q2122–Q2123 require BT11-094 to play the opposite Angewomon/LadyDevimon name and reject the same name.
- Q2125–Q2129 require BT11-095 to use cards under any of the owner's Tamers, reject cards under a Digimon, and listen only to a single Digimon play with a DigiXros requirement.
- Q2130 confirms BT11-098's conditional return still activates when the Seadramon was played earlier by the same Main effect.

The generated snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` agrees with the direct IR for BT11-091 through BT11-096 and BT11-099 through BT11-100, apart from nonsemantic formatting/metadata representation. Two read-only differences matter to this report:

- The snapshot's BT11-098 conditional `Return` retains `optional: true` at line 13273. The direct module already removed that flag in existing correction commit `e29e84acb`, and `BT11-098.test.ts` proves that declining the optional source play does not suppress the mandatory return. `effects.json` was not changed.
