# BT11-071–080 Static Card Audit

## Scope and audit posture

This report covers exactly BT11-071 through BT11-080, in strict ascending
card order. Before the audit, the reused branch fetched `origin/audit-bt-card-by-card`
and merged it with a regular merge (no rebase). The synchronized branch was
based on the upstream BT11 audit history; the catalog contract was read from
blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`.

For each card, I read the committed catalog record, queried the local rules
and Q&A knowledge base, traced the direct TypeScript module and its compiled
IR registration, inspected the generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`
entry as read-only evidence, and reviewed shared interpreter/mechanism seams,
neighboring peers, realistic evolution or deletion stacks, and colocated
behavioral test source. Every module in this range has full coverage and an
empty residual, and every executable registration is exclusively
`registerIrCard("BT11-<ID>", compiled)`; no second `registerCard` registration
was introduced or retained.

The historical `BT11-AUDIT.md` and `BT11-STATIC-AUDIT.md` ledgers were
preserved, and `BT11-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was not updated. The generated effects
snapshot was not edited. Tests, typecheck, lint, format, browser/UI checks,
and `git diff --check` were explicitly forbidden, so execution is 0/2 for
every card and each otherwise complete card remains provisional at 8/10. The
single justified implementation correction is in BT11-072 and is described
below; its new negative proof is source-reviewed but unexecuted.

#### Contract and KB

The catalog defines a black/purple level-5 Ultimate, Virus, Enhancement /
Bagra Army / Twilight Digimon with play cost 10, 8000 DP, and black level-5

is in the resulting sources, it De-Digivolves up to three opposing Digimon by

The snapshot preserves the same behavior. Its PlaceUnder target omits the

### BT11-072 — Machinedramon — 8/10 provisional

#### Contract and KB

The catalog defines a black level-6 Mega, Virus, Machine Digimon with play
cost 11, 11000 DP, and black/red level-5 cost-3 evolution. On Play and When
Digivolving it reveals five cards, adds one Analogman to hand, and
independently adds one Cyborg or Machine card to hand or may place it under
itself; the rest is trashed. On Deletion, by placing one Analogman from play
at the bottom of its owner's deck, it may play one Machinedramon from hand
without paying its cost.

The KB query returned Q2100, Q2101, and Q2214. Q2100 confirms that the
Analogman and Cyborg/Machine choices are independent. Q2101 confirms that
Analogman may still be bottom-decked when no Machinedramon is in hand. Q2214
confirms that a source card can activate an inherited On Deletion effect.
These answers leave no unresolved ambiguity.

#### Direct IR and exact correction

`apps/api/src/cards/BT11/BT11-072.ts` has full coverage, no residual, and one
`registerIrCard` call. The shared RevealAdd action reveals five, adds
Analogman, independently offers the Cyborg/Machine hand-or-self-stack
choice, and trashes the remainder. The deletion action returns one Analogman
to `deckBottom` with optional decline allowed and then plays one Machinedramon
from hand without cost.

The direct source had a concrete continuation gap: the second action had no
condition, so a Machinedramon in hand could be played even when no Analogman
was present and no bottom-deck placement occurred. I corrected the
`PlayWithoutCost` action with
`condition: { kind: "ifThisEffectActed", raw: "if you placed an Analogman" }`.
This preserves Q2101: a successful Analogman placement still resolves even
when the hand has no Machinedramon, while preventing a free play when the
required placement cannot happen. I also added the negative case to
`apps/api/src/cards/BT11/BT11-072.test.ts`, which deletes a source with a
Machinedramon replacement in hand but no Analogman and asserts that the
replacement remains in hand. Neither the new source proof nor any test was
executed under this dispatch.

The read-only generated record predates this correction and also uses the
older `alternative` PlaceUnder representation for the reveal choice instead
of the direct `orDispositions` representation. This is one material snapshot
drift. The snapshot was not edited.

#### Seams, peers, and proof

The audit reviewed RevealAdd's independent add dispositions and cost budget,
the Return `deckBottom` seam, `ifThisEffectActed` action gating, and
PlayWithoutCost from hand. BT11-055 and BT11-072's nearby Machine-family
cards provide peer conventions. Existing test source covers catalog/mapping,
the five-card reveal and trash, independent Machine addition when Analogman
is absent, self-stack placement, successful Analogman payment followed by a
free Machinedramon play, and the Q2101 no-Machinedramon-hand boundary. The
new test covers the converse boundary exposed by the correction. Tests were
read but not executed.

#### Score and correction

Contract 2/2; direct IR 2/2; seams/peers 2/2; proof/stacks 2/2; execution
0/2. Total 8/10 provisional. Historical independent-choice and Q2101
corrections remain intact; this pass adds the direct `ifThisEffectActed`
continuation gate and its focused negative proof. Ambiguity count is zero.
