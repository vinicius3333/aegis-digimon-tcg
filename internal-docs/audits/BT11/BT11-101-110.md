# BT11-101–110 Static Card Audit

## Scope and audit posture

This report covers exactly BT11-101 through BT11-110, audited in strict
ascending card order. The reused branch was synchronized first by fetching
`origin/audit-bt-card-by-card` and performing a regular fast-forward merge
without rebasing; the review therefore includes the upstream BT11 audit
history through `2372a7b01`. Printed contracts were read from catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`.

For every card, I queried the local rules/Q&A knowledge base, read the direct
TypeScript module and its colocated behavioral test source, compared the
compiled IR with the generated
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` record as read-only evidence, and
followed the shared interpreter/primitives, neighboring peers, and realistic
evolution/deletion/stack fixtures far enough to establish the static result.
Every module in this range has `coverage: "full"` and an empty `residual`,
and each executable registration is exclusively
`registerIrCard("BT11-<ID>", compiled)`; no second `registerCard` registration
was added or preserved.

The historical `BT11-AUDIT.md` and `BT11-STATIC-AUDIT.md` ledgers were
preserved, and `BT11-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was not updated by this dispatch. The
generated effects snapshot was inspected only and never edited. Tests,
typecheck, lint, format, browser/UI checks, and `git diff --check` were
explicitly forbidden, so execution is 0/2 for every card and every otherwise
complete result is provisional at 8/10. Three concrete implementation/proof
gaps were corrected: BT11-105 now gates its optional evolution on successful
material placement; BT11-108 encodes the De-Digivolve floor on the keyword
action; and BT11-109 now performs bottom whole-permanent relocation while
shedding the moved source's existing stack (including the smallest shared
seam and colocated proof needed for that flag).

### BT11-105 — Fusionize — 8/10 provisional

#### Contract and KB

The catalog defines Fusionize as a black Option with use cost 1. Its use
cost falls by one with an own Snatchmon. Main first places one Vemmon or
Destromon from trash under one own Digimon as its bottom digivolution card;
after paying that placement cost, it may digivolve one own Digimon into a
Destromon or Galacticmon from trash for its printed digivolution cost.
Security may reveal three, play one revealed Vemmon without paying, and trash
the remainder.

The KB query returned Q2133. It explicitly permits the placement even when no
Destromon or Galacticmon is available in trash, so placement and evolution
must not be collapsed into one optional destination-dependent cost. Ambiguity
count is zero.

#### Direct IR and exact correction

`apps/api/src/cards/BT11/BT11-105.ts` has full coverage, no residual, and one
exclusive `registerIrCard`. Static reduction uses the own Snatchmon gate.
Main places one matching trash card under an own Digimon at the bottom, then
offers the paid trash evolution into the two named destinations.

The pre-existing direct shape correctly separated placement from the optional
evolution, but the second action had no continuation gate. If no Vemmon or
Destromon could be placed, the engine would still attempt an unrelated
Destromon/Galacticmon evolution from trash. I added
`condition: { kind: "ifThisEffectActed", raw: "if you placed a card under a Digimon" }`
to the Digivolve action. Successful Q2133 placement still allows the
subsequent optional evolution, while a missing placement cannot pay its
activation condition. `BT11-105.test.ts` now covers the converse boundary
with a Galacticmon in trash but no eligible placement card and asserts the
host does not evolve.

The generated snapshot still has the older two-action shape without this
condition. This is one material snapshot drift; `effects.json` was not
edited.

#### Seams, peers, and proof

The reviewed mechanisms are the condition-only self-reducer, mandatory
loose-card `PlaceUnder` followed by `ifThisEffectActed`, bottom-stack order,
paid trash evolution, and optional Security RevealAdd. BT13-028 demonstrates
the comparable placement-then-evolution binding; BT18-095 and the Vemmon
peers corroborate trash sourcing and optional placement conventions.

`BT11-105.test.ts` covers the catalog and IR, successful Vemmon placement and
