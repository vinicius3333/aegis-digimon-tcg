### EX10-013 Lucemon — **defect corrected**

**Clauses.** Lv.3 / 10000 / play 10 / Yellow / Rookie / Vaccine / [Angel].
(1) `[Digivolve] [Cupimon]: Cost 5`. (2) `＜Blocker＞`. (3) `{Breeding}[When Digivolving] This
Digimon may move.` (4) `[End of Your Turn] By returning 5 cards with [Lucemon] in their texts from
your trash to the bottom of the deck, this Digimon may digivolve into [Lucemon: Chaos Mode] in the
trash without paying the cost.` Inherited: `＜Blocker＞`.

**IR trace.** (1) `digivolutionRequirement [{names:["Cupimon"], level:2, cost:5, isAlternate:true}]`.
(2)+(inherited) two `Static` keyword effects, one with `isInherited:true`.
(3) `WhenDigivolving` + `isBreeding:true` + `MovePermanent{direction:"toBattle", optional:true}`.
(4) `EndOfYourTurn` + `Digivolve{into: nameOrTrait match:"nameExact" ["Lucemon: Chaos Mode"],
from:["trash"], payCost:false}` with `cost.kind:"return"`, `count:5`,
`filter {controller:"mine", zone:"trash", textContains:"Lucemon"}`, `to:"deckBottom"`.
`payCost:false` waives the memory only — `actions/digivolve.ts` still enforces the target's own
requirements, which is Q5041. No `kind` restriction on the cost filter, so Digi-Egg cards qualify
and `fx` routes them to the egg deck (Q5734). `nameExact` is stricter than the record's `name`.

**Defect (before → after).** The `cost` was **mandatory** and the action carried no
`abortOnDecline`.

- Before: `cost: { kind:"return", …, to:"deckBottom" }`, action `optional: true` only.
- After: `cost: { …, optional: true }`, action `optional: true` **and** `abortOnDecline: true`.

Why it is a real defect, traced through `actions/runAction.ts`:

- The printed clause is a "By …" **processing condition** on a MANDATORY `[End of Your Turn]`
  trigger. CR 15-7-4 makes a processing condition declinable. With `cost.optional` absent,
  `runAction` takes the `else` branch and calls `payCost` unconditionally: every end of your turn,
  with five Lucemon-text cards in trash, the controller was **forced** to shuffle them under the
  deck. There was no way to say no.
- `runAction`'s combined branch — "when both the processing condition and payload are optional, pay
  the former first, then offer the payload choice" — is gated on `actionCost?.optional === true`.
  Without it the second prompt never ran, so **Q5040 (return the 5, then choose NOT to digivolve)
  was unreachable**: the payload `optional:true` was dead.
- `abortOnDecline:true` is what makes refusing the payment stop the clause (`runAction`'s
  `willPay === false` branch) instead of silently proceeding to a free digivolve. The persisted
  record carried this flag; the module had lost it. This is the one hint the record supplied.

Q5039 is unaffected: `payCost` still fails wholesale on a 4-card pool, and the existing test proves it.

**Tests added** (`EX10-013.test.ts`):

1. IR assertion extended with `abortOnDecline: true` and `cost.optional: true`.
2. `"CR 15-7-4 may decline the processing condition outright, keeping all 5 cards in trash"` —
   `autoDeclineOptional`, a full 5-card + Chaos Mode trash; asserts an optional decision was
   offered, the trash instance list is byte-identical afterwards, the egg deck and deck are empty,
   and Lucemon did not digivolve. **This test fails on the pre-fix module**, where the cost was
   mandatory and the five cards left the trash regardless of the refusal.
3. `"Q5040 pays the 5-card condition and may still decline the Chaos Mode digivolve"` — answers the
   two optional decisions differently (accept the payment, refuse the digivolve) by driving
   `respondDecision` from `state.pendingDecision`; asserts exactly two optional prompts were seen,
   all five cost cards reached the egg deck, the trash holds only the untouched Chaos Mode card, and
   the top card is still EX10-013. **This test cannot even be expressed on the pre-fix module** —
   only one optional prompt existed.

**Mutation check.** `count:5` → the Q5039 "4 cards change nothing" assertion.
`textContains:"Lucemon"` → the same test (its three non-matching trash cards would become payable).
`to:"deckBottom"` → the egg-deck/deck placement assertions.
`nameExact` → the Q5041 assertion that BT7-111 stays in trash.
`payCost:false` → the zero-memory digivolve assertion.
`isBreeding:true` → the breeding-move test's `breeding` toBeUndefined assertion.
`isInherited` on the ＜Blocker＞ effect → `observe().hasKeyword(host,"Blocker")` in the stack test.
`cost.optional` → new test 2. `abortOnDecline` → new tests 2 and 3.
`digivolutionRequirement.cost:5` → the breeding test's `memory` 5 → 0 assertion.

### EX10-014 Weatherdramon — **blocked by a shared-type seam; 6/10**

**Clauses.** Lv.4 / 4000 / play 4 / Yellow / [Sup.][Appmon] / Navi / [Weather]; EvoCost Lv.3
Yellow for 2; `linkDp 3000`; `[Link] [Appmon] trait: Cost 2`.
(1) `[Security] At the end of the battle, play this card without paying the cost.`
(2) `[On Play][When Digivolving] Give 2 of your opponent's Digimon ＜Security A. -1＞ until their
turn ends.` (3) link effect: `[When Attacking] By trashing 1 of this Digimon's link cards, 1 of
your opponent's Digimon gets -6000 DP for the turn.`

**IR trace.** (1) `trigger:"Security"`, `isSecurity:true`, `timing:"endOfBattle"`, wrapping a
`SubTrigger{event:"whenSecurityBattleEnded", once:true}` around `PlayWithoutCost{from:["trash"],
payCost:false}`. `CardEffect.timing` is **provenance-only** — no interpreter file reads it — so the
SubTrigger is the only thing that actually defers the play, and it is genuinely wired:
`engine/security/securityCheck.ts:326` fires `whenSecurityBattleEnded` and
`interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:78,429` subscribes it behind a dedicated gate.
`from:["trash"]` is correct because the card has legitimately reached the trash by the time the
event fires (`effects/primitives.ts:5647`). Q6510-class behavior (effect resolves, then it battles,
then it is played) follows.
(2) `GainKeyword{keyword:"SecurityAttack", amount:-1, duration:"untilOpponentTurnEnd"}` × 2 targets.
(3) `WhenAttacking` + `isLinked:true` + `ModifyDP{-6000, forTheTurn}` with
`cost {kind:"trash", target.filter {zone:"linked", isSelfRef:true}, count:1}`, `optional:true`,
`abortOnDecline:true`. Verified against `interpreter/costs.ts:770-830`: for a `zone:"linked"` cost
with `isSelfRef`, the HOST is `ctx.source.permanent()` and **every** one of that host's link cards
becomes a candidate — which is exactly Q5042 (may trash this card itself) **and** Q5043 (may trash a
different link card under ＜Link +X＞). No defect.

**Type error and what it means.** `EX10-014.ts(15,11): Type '"whenSecurityBattleEnded"' is not
assignable to type 'SubTriggerEvent'.` This is **not** a dead clause. The event is fired, gated and
consumed at runtime; it is simply absent from the `SubTriggerEvent` union in
`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/actions/subTrigger.ts`. The union already lists 40+ siblings
including `whenSecurityRemoved` and `whenCheckedFaceUpSecurity`; this one was never added. Five
other cards use the identical shape (`BT3-011`, `BT23-007`, `BT23-010`, `BT23-028`, `BT23-052`),
so this is a set-wide shared-type gap, not an EX10 authoring error.

**Why the module was not "fixed".** Every in-module alternative is a semantic regression:
dropping the SubTrigger (as the persisted record does) resolves the play DURING the security check
instead of after the battle; there is no other event name that means "the security battle finished".
Per the brief's hard rule I made no shared or engine edit — see §6, seam request S-1. A comment
block was added to the module recording the exact gap so no later sweep "fixes" it by deleting the
deferral.

**Tests.** Left unchanged; the existing suite already asserts the SubTrigger IR shape and the
behavioral "battles in security first, then plays itself and resolves On Play" path.

**Mutation check.** `event:"whenSecurityBattleEnded"` → the security-battle test's
"card reaches the battle area after the battle" assertion. `amount:-1` and `count:2` → the
＜Security A.＞ assertions. `zone:"linked"`+`isSelfRef` → the Q5042/Q5043 link-cost assertions.
`abortOnDecline:true` → the declined-link-cost negative (no −6000 DP applied).

### EX10-015 Psychemon — **two defects corrected**

**Clauses.** Lv.3 / 1000 / play 4 / Green+Purple / Rookie / Data / [Reptile]; EvoCost Lv.2 Green
for 1 and Lv.2 Purple for 1; **no printed `[Digivolve]` header**.
(1) `[Start of Your Main Phase] By trashing 1 card with ＜Save＞ in its text from your hand,
＜Draw 1＞ and suspend 1 of your opponent's Digimon.` (2) `[On Deletion] ＜Save＞`.
(3) `[DigiXros -2] 1 Digimon card with ＜Save＞ in text`. Inherited: `＜Piercing＞`.

**IR trace.** (1) `Draw{amount:1, controller:"mine"}` carrying the `trash` cost
(`zone:"hand" ∧ textContains:"Save"`, count 1) with `optional:true` + `abortOnDecline:true`,
followed by a bare `Suspend{controller:"opponent", kind:["Digimon"], count:1}`. Traced through
`runAction`: a declined or unpayable cost returns `abortOnDecline === true`, and `runEffect` stops
the remaining actions — so the suspend cannot happen without the trash. That is Q5045, and it is
the same guarantee the record reached by a different route (`ifThisEffectActed` on the suspend).
(2) `OnDeletion` with `keywords:[{keyword:"Save"}]` **and** an explicit
`PlaceUnder{target:self, underFilter: controller mine ∧ kind:["Tamer"] ∧ excludeToken, optional:true}` —
matching the proven BT12-074 record, which also carries both. The keyword marker alone synthesizes
nothing, so the record's `actions: []` would have made ＜Save＞ inert.
(3) see below. Inherited `＜Piercing＞` as a `Static` `isInherited` keyword effect.

**Defect 1 — DigiXros material carried an unread, non-existent field (before → after).**

- Before: `materials: [{ kind: ["Digimon"], nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Save"], match: "text" }] }]`
- After: `materials: [{ texts: ["Save"] }]`

`DigiXrosMaterial` (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/requirements/xrosLink.ts`) has **no `kind`
field**, and `materialMatchesSlot` (`apps/api/src/engine/actions/digiXros.ts:389`) never reads one —
so the printed "**Digimon** card" half was being carried by a field the matcher cannot see.
It happened not to be exploitable, because that function opens with a Digimon-only guard for any
slot that does not name a specific card; but the encoding was a lie about where the constraint
lives. `texts` is the exact peer encoding for the identical printed recipe on BT12-011, BT12-074,
BT12-075 and BT12-048, and it routes through the same `matchNameOrTrait(..., match:"text")` union
the `nameOrTrait` form used, so matching is unchanged. `count: 2` (the PER-MATERIAL cost reduction,
per the `DigiXrosRequirement` doc comment) is kept and is the peer-standard value; the record's
`count:1 + costReduction:2` is the stale form.

**Defect 2 — `digivolutionRequirement` restated the printed EvoCost rows (before → after).**

- Before: `digivolutionRequirement: [{level:2, colors:["Green"], cost:1}, {level:2, colors:["Purple"], cost:1}]`
- After: field removed.

This card prints **no** bracketed `[Digivolve]` header. Those two entries are simply the
`cards.json` `evoCosts` rows copied into the alternate-path table. That table is not a mirror of
the printed EvoCost: `matchingAlternateDigivolutionRequirement` in
`apps/api/src/engine/cards/cardData.ts` consults **every** entry uniformly as an alternate route and
ignores `isAlternate` entirely (`interpreter.test.ts:4049` states this explicitly), so restating
printed rows can only register additional, unprinted routes that bypass the standard rule's own
checks. Here the level and color gates happen to coincide with the standard route, so no illegal
digivolution was reachable — but the encoding asserted two printed alternate paths that do not
exist, and the missing `isAlternate` was the type error pointing straight at it. Deleting the field
is the truthful encoding; the standard color+level rule already grants both cost-1 routes, which the
existing test proves from both colors and rejects for red.

*Scope note:* `digivolutionRequirementsFor` reads `effects.json` (there is no generated override for
any EX10 card), so this change becomes observable only after the audit regenerates the record.
I deliberately did **not** assert `digivolutionRequirementsFor(CARD_ID)` in the test, because that
lookup still returns the stale record until regeneration.

**Type errors resolved.** All three (`isAlternate` × 2, `kind` on a DigiXros material) — and each
