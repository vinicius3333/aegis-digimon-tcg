## 4. Per-card findings

### EX10-061 Apocalymon

Clauses: `[Digivolve] Lv.6 w/[Dark Masters]: Cost 5`; a `wouldBePlayed` self cost reduction of
4 per placed face-up distinct-named [Dark Masters] security card; `[On Play] [When Digivolving]`
play one of each distinct-named [Dark Masters] card from this Digimon's digivolution cards
without paying, then all your [Dark Masters] Digimon gain ＜Rush＞ for the turn, then delete
those played Digimon at turn end.

IR trace: `Static` → nested `Replacement/wouldBePlayed` with `sourceFilter.isSelfRef` and a
`place` cost carrying `underFilter.isSelfRef` and `target {count: "all", distinctNames: true}`.
`collectWouldBePlayedSelfReducers` matches the nested branch: EX10-061 **is** in
`VERIFIED_SELF_REDUCER_CARDS` (reducers.ts:136), the branch reads `inner.amountPerPlaced` and
maps it to `amountPerPaid`, and `applyWouldBePlayedSelfReducer` then runs the dedicated
distinct-name multi-placement loop (it enforces one card per printed name itself, which is
exactly Q5783/Q5784). The `[On Play]`/`[When Digivolving]` bodies use `PlayWithoutCost` from
`digivolutionCards` with `count: "all"` + `distinctNames` (Q5785/Q5786) and a `DelayedDelete`
that arms on `ctx.lastPlayedPermanentIds` (Q5741/Q5742).

Type error resolved: `amountPerPlaced` on `ReplacementAction`. **The interpreter does read it**
(`reducers.ts:300,304`), so the clause was live, not dead — the gap was a missing declaration in
the shared IR type. No in-module encoding could preserve the semantics: the alternative typed
route (`amountFromPaidCost: true` on a flat `reduceCost`) is rejected by `captureReducer`
because `"place"` is absent from `STRUCTURED_REDUCER_COSTS`. This was raised as a seam request
and the audit landed `ReplacementAction.amountPerPlaced?: number` (commit d7db03755);
no module change was needed. Suite re-run green afterwards.

Mutation check: change `amountPerPlaced` to any other number or drop it and both the contract
assertion at `EX10-061.test.ts:38` and the cost delta in
`"Q5783/Q5784: places one face-up card of each distinct Dark Masters name and reduces cost by 4
each"` fail; drop `distinctNames` from either target and that test or
`"Q5785/Q5786: plays one of each distinct Dark Masters name from its stack"` fails.

Gap closed: added `"Rushes every [Dark Masters] Digimon but deletes only the ones this effect
played"`. It boards Apocalymon with one [Dark Masters] card in its stack, a **pre-existing**
[Dark Masters] Digimon (EX10-035) and a plain Digimon (BT1-009), then asserts the played card
and the resident [Dark Masters] Digimon both gain ＜Rush＞ while the plain Digimon does not, and
that the turn-end `DelayedDelete` removes **only** the played permanent. Mutation executed:
dropping the [Dark Masters] `nameOrTrait` from the ＜Rush＞ target turns it RED (the plain
Digimon gains ＜Rush＞); the same test also goes RED if `DelayedDelete` stops being scoped to the
played permanents. Remaining nuance: `duration: "forTheTurn"` is proven as "granted", not as
"expires at turn end" — the batch's suites have no turn-rollover helper and the sweep belongs to
the shared duration ledger rather than to this card's IR.

Open ambiguity: none.

### EX10-064 Yuu Amano & Nene Amano

Clauses: `[Start of Your Main Phase]` by placing 1 [Bagra Army]/[Twilight] Digimon card from
hand or trash under this Tamer, ＜Draw 1＞; `[All Turns]` when any of your [Bagra Army]/[Twilight]
Digimon cards with DigiXros requirements would be played, by suspending this Tamer, 1 card from
under your Tamers and 1 card in your trash can also be placed for their DigiXros; `[Security]`
play without paying.

IR trace: the place cost has no `underFilter` and no `destination`, so `payCost`'s default
`place` branch resolves the host to `ctx.source.permanent()` — this Tamer — and `placeUnder`
appends at the bottom of the under-cards (Q5174). The `[All Turns]` clause is a
`Replacement/wouldBePlayed` with `hasDigiXrosRequirement` (the singular predicate is the live
one, CAP-H-05) whose payload is `DigiXrosMaterialZoneExpansion`, recorded per seat by
`expandDigiXrosZones` and read back by `actions/play.ts` when an *effect* plays a DigiXros card.

**Defect corrected (type error and semantics).**
Before: `zones: ["tamerCards", "trash"]`.
After: `zones: ["underTamers", "trash"]`.
`"tamerCards"` is not a `ZoneRef` at all. The consumer in `actions/play.ts:596` accepts only
`underTamers` / `underMyTamers` / `underTamer` / `digivolutionCards` for the under-Tamer half
and `trash` for the trash half, so the under-Tamers half of Q5175/Q5176 was silently dead in the
ledger path and only the trash half applied. The type error was therefore a real semantic
defect, exactly as the brief predicted.

Scope of the defect: the `playCard` **intent** path (`apps/api/src/engine/actions/digiXros.ts`)
does not read the ledger — it reads `DIGIXROS_ZONE_EXPANDERS["EX10-064"]` in the shared package,
which already grants `underTamerMax: 1, trashMax: 1`. That is why the existing Q5175/Q5176 and
Q5178 tests passed with the broken token: they drive the intent path and pass
`expanderPermanentIds`. The ledger only matters when a card effect plays the DigiXros Digimon
(`PlayWithoutCost` with `allowDigiXros`).

Tests: the contract assertion now pins `zones: ["underTamers", "trash"]`. A behavioral test for
the ledger-only path is **not** added, and the search behind that decision is:

- `digiXrosExpandedZones` has exactly one consumer, `actions/play.ts:595`, inside the
  `action.allowDigiXros === true` branch — i.e. only an EFFECT-driven play consults the ledger;
  the `playCard` intent path (`engine/actions/digiXros.ts`) reads the shared card-id registry.
- Exactly two modules in the whole corpus set `allowDigiXros: true`: `BT19-027` and `BT26-006`
  (`grep -rn allowDigiXros apps/api/src/cards` → 3 hits, one of them a test).
- BT19-027's ＜Decode＞ play is filtered to blue level-5 Digimon. Of the 17 [Bagra Army]/[Twilight]
  cards that carry a `digiXrosRequirement`, **none** is blue level 5, so BT19-027 can never
  produce a play this Tamer's replacement applies to.
- BT26-006's inherited `[When Attacking]` play IS compatible ([Bagra Army] from hand). But the
  ledger writer and the registry reader are mutually exclusive within one play: writing the
  ledger costs the Tamer's suspension, and the registry path skips suspended Tamers. So the
  proof needs **two plays in one turn** — an intent play to arm the ledger, then a BT26-006
  attack whose inherited effect plays a second [Bagra Army] DigiXros card — on top of BT26-006's
  own "trash 2 digivolution cards from a [Bagra Army] Digimon" cost and its Once Per Turn limit.

That is a seven-condition, three-set fixture; it is recorded as residual proof debt with the
shape a follow-up needs, rather than approximated by an intent-path test that would pass either
way.

Mutation check: change either zone token and the contract test fails; remove the `place` cost's
`from: ["hand","trash"]` or its placement and `"Q5174 places the payment at the Tamer's true
bottom and draws exactly 1"` fails; remove the suspend cost and the `isSuspended` assertion in
`"Q5175/Q5176 DigiXroses with one card under another Tamer and one from trash"` fails; widen the
[Bagra Army]/[Twilight] gate and `"rejects the expander for a DigiXros card outside Bagra
Army/Twilight"` fails.

Open ambiguity: none on the printed text. Q6961 (a card that left the trash before its pending
"when trashed from digivolution cards" effect activates cannot activate it) is an engine-level
pending-effect rule, not encodable on this card.

Before: `delayArmedIntrinsic: true` on the `SubTrigger`.
After: `keywords: [{ keyword: "Delay", raw: "＜Delay＞" }]` on the `[Your Turn]` effect.
`delayArmedIntrinsic` is documented in `interpreter/effect.ts:369` as "a synthesized marker read
by `runSubTrigger`/`runReplacement`, **not part of the compiled IR**". `withIntrinsicDelayGate`

`registration/module.ts:283` applies that transform when the effect carries the ＜Delay＞ keyword
and its trigger maps to `EffectTiming.None` (`YourTurn` does). Hand-writing the marker happened
to work at runtime but bypassed the printed-keyword encoding every peer uses (BT23-093) and
could not typecheck. Behavior after the change is identical — the §16-17-1 trash-this-card cost
and §16-17-3 entered-play guard still fire; the suite proves it.

**Defect 2 corrected (semantic, Q5183 — found during the trace, not on the type-error list).**
Before: `into: { traits: ["Mineral", "LIBERATOR"] }`.
After: `into: { nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Mineral"], match: "trait" }], traits: ["LIBERATOR"] }`.
`filter.traits` is routed through a **single** `matchNameOrTrait` call whose tokens are
`.some()`-matched (`matching/definition.ts:170-171` and `:349`), so the old form accepted a
Mineral-only or LIBERATOR-only card — precisely what Q5183 forbids. `nameOrTrait` and `traits`
are separate conjunctive gates, which `cardPredicates.ts:101` documents as the way to AND a
trait with another predicate. The persisted record has the same OR bug in a different shape
(two `nameOrTrait` entries).
