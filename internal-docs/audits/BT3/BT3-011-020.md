## Per-card findings

### BT3-011 Greymon — provisional 8/10

Catalog/rules evidence confirms that the printed clause is delayed until the end of the Security battle. The original module (`apps/api/src/cards/BT3/BT3-011.ts`) played the card immediately from the Security trigger, which skipped the required battle and contradicted Q1052/Q1053. I corrected it to `timing: "endOfBattle"` plus a one-shot `whenSecurityBattleEnded` SubTrigger that plays the exact source from trash without paying; this follows the BT23 peers and the engine event ordering.

Focused proof in `BT3-011.test.ts:6-54` now checks a losing Security battle, asserts the play occurs after `securityChecked`, and checks the winning Security-battle case. The generated snapshot remains the pre-fix immediate shape at `effects.json:102097-102112`; because the corrected direct module is the exclusive executable registration and is fully traced, this is deferred artifact synchronization rather than a score-reducing ambiguity.

### BT3-012 Aquilamon — provisional 8/10

Direct IR `BT3-012.ts:8-35` is full and registration-only: inherited When Attacking, opponent Digimon, current DP `<= 2000`, count one. The focused test `BT3-012.test.ts:6-47` proves two eligible targets, excludes a 2001-DP card, and resolves exactly one. The host BT3-015 over BT3-012 is a legal red level 4-to-5 stack; deletion/filter behavior matches same-mechanism inherited delete peers. Snapshot and direct IR agree; no static defect was found.

### BT3-013 Duramon — provisional 8/10

### BT3-014 Silphymon — provisional 8/10

The hand-fixed direct module `BT3-019.ts:7-63` correctly declares Security Attack +1 and Reboot, then performs optional PlaceUnder from hand with an exact Durandamon/BryweLudramon name filter, top position, self host, and conditional GainMemory 3 only when placement acted. `BT3-019.test.ts:7-71` proves legal AD1-004 red/black level 6 → BT3-019 red level 7 evolution, placement and memory restoration, both keywords, and optional decline; Q1058–Q1060 and the shared PlaceUnder/ifThisEffectActed mechanisms support the interpretation.
