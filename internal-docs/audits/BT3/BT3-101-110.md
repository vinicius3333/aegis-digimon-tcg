order and removed all remaining range-local `@ts-nocheck` directives. No

## Shared evidence

### Catalog and local KB

The committed catalog entries are contiguous at `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:52311-52466`:

- BT3-101 through BT3-102 are yellow Options, with Bifrost's `-3000 DP`/Security Attack -1 and Code Cracking's opponent security choice/Recovery +1 text (`:52311-52339`).
- BT3-103 through BT3-104 are green Options, with Hidden Potential's next green digivolution reduction and Positron Laser's attack/block restriction plus blue-gated bounce (`:52342-52371`).

- BT3-108 through BT3-110 are purple Options, with Retaliation, the On Deletion replay grant, and level-5-purple-from-trash replay (`:52422-52466`).

The card-specific local KB in `data/kb/qa.json` was queried in range order (`:24640-24792`):

- BT3-102 Q1135-Q1136: the opponent chooses whether to trash their top security; declining gives the user Recovery +1, and an empty opponent security stack gives Recovery +1 automatically.
- BT3-103 Q1137-Q1140: a Digimon suspended by the effect may digivolve; the source must already be green, green-to-non-green qualifies, and effect-activated digivolution also qualifies. Q1285, Q1302, Q1313, and Q4276 cover cost-reduction interaction and stacking.
- BT3-104 Q1141: the blue Digimon must be present when the Option effect is activated; playing blue later does not retroactively enable the bounce.
- BT3-105 Q1142: the Security restriction affects opponent Digimon entering the battle area later in the turn.
- BT3-106 Q1143-Q1144: a Digimon with both Blocker and Reboot receives only one Security Attack +1, and loses that grant if it later loses either qualifying keyword.
- BT3-107 Q1145-Q1146: a cost-5-or-more Digimon can be deleted if De-Digivolve exposes a current cost of 4 or less; a cost-4-or-less level-3/no-stack target can also be deleted.
- BT3-109 Q1147-Q1149 and Q2730/Q2761: sources remain in trash, replay loses prior effects, replay cannot attack that turn, and a later digivolution causes the evolved top card to replay.
- BT3-101 and BT3-108 have no card-specific QA entries in the local KB; their printed text, direct IR, and mechanism peers provide the available static evidence. BT3-110 likewise has no range-local QA entry.

Relevant comprehensive-rule anchors in `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md` are Option timing and immediate activation (`:1616-1662`, `:2253-2305`), current-state references (`:2230-2252`), optional processing and failed optional conditions (`:2082-2125`), target cardinality and “all” (`:2334-2367`), and the keyword rules for Security Attack/Blocker/Recovery/Digisorption/Reboot/De-Digivolve/Retaliation (`:2810-2965`). These rules support resolving each clause at its printed timing, retaining the same target where the text says “that Digimon,” and reevaluating live keyword boundaries where the effect is tied to a filter.

Same-mechanism peers inspected include BT3-056's Digisorption replacement (`apps/api/src/cards/BT3/BT3-056.ts:7-22`), EX2-064's interactive `wouldDigivolve` cost replacement (`apps/api/src/cards/EX2/EX2-064.ts:5-45`), BT3-030's live filter grant (`apps/api/src/cards/BT3/BT3-030.ts:35-50`), BT18-028's live restriction (`apps/api/src/cards/BT18/BT18-028.ts:27-39`), BT3-100's blue/green condition shape, and P-220's legal Digimon with both static Reboot and Blocker (`apps/api/src/cards/P/P-220.ts:4-33`).
