# BT3-021 through BT3-030 Card Implementation Audit

Archival notice: this range report preserves clause-level evidence; final executed results are in `docs/audits/BT3-STATIC-AUDIT.md`.

Audit date: 2026-08-27

This range was audited in strict ascending card-ID order against the committed
catalog, card-specific local KB output, comprehensive rules, direct TypeScript
modules, the committed compiled-effects snapshot, shared interpreter primitives,
colocated behavioral tests, same-mechanism peers, trait boundaries, and legal
evolution/source stacks. Every executable card module in the range uses exactly
one `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration; no duplicate `registerCard`
registration was added or retained.

The requested static-only scope was followed. No focused tests, mechanism tests,
aggregate tests, typecheck, lint, formatting, browser/UI validation, or
`git diff --check` were run. The executed-gates component is therefore 0/2 for
every card, every score below is provisional, and no card is claimed as 10/10 or
as collection-complete.

## Shared evidence and deferred validation

The authoritative catalog records for the ten cards are in
`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json:50356-50606`. The records establish
the printed colors, levels, play/evolution costs, DP, forms, attributes,
types/traits, effect text, and inherited text used below. The generated effects
snapshot is at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json:102314-102493` and
contains full-coverage records for all ten IDs, including empty records for the
two vanilla cards.

Card-specific KB queries were performed in range order:


BT3-021, BT3-022, BT3-023, and BT3-025 through BT3-029 returned no
card-specific entries.
BT3-024 returned Q1061 (a card played by its effect is a normal Digimon in the
battle area), Q1062 (the Security effect may play Airdramon even when it loses
the security battle), and Q1063 (after Airdramon is played, a remaining security
check proceeds). BT3-030 returned Q1064 (its optional effect may be declined),
Q1065 (its own digivolution cards are eligible), and Q1066 (a recipient loses
the granted Jamming immediately upon reaching level 5 or higher). The errata
and rules indexes were checked for overrides; none changed these contracts.

Applicable general rules include `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:322-330`
(inherited effects), `:660-662` (a Digimon gains inherited effects from its
stack), `:679-681` and `:1834-1878` (Security Digimon, security battles, and
end-of-battle timing), `:760-764` (digivolution-card references),
`:1926-1934` (inherited-effect source and "this Digimon" scope), and the
trigger/once-per-turn sections around `:2000-2055`. The Jamming definition is
also explicit in `data/kb/rules/glossary.md:280-283`: a Digimon with Jamming
cannot be deleted in battles against Security Digimon, while the glossary
timing entries at `:52-79` distinguish When Digivolving, When Attacking, end of
battle, and Your Turn.

The shared seams traced for this range were:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/registration/module.ts:323-341`
  normalizes a direct IR module, stores its compiled record, and constructs the
  executable module. Direct card files are therefore the implementation
  authority; every effect-bearing registration in this range is checked for
  the required shape. Vanilla BT3-022 and BT3-028 now have explicit empty
  full-coverage modules and direct focused-test imports; set-index ownership is
  outside this lane.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/placeUnder.ts:524-686`
  implements `TrashDigivolution`; `fromTop: false` selects the bottom stack
  entry and no-source targets resolve to no action.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/permanent.ts:272-278`
  handles level comparisons, `:476-478` handles `hasAny`/`none` source
  matching, and controller matching maps `mine`/`opponent` against the effect
  source seat.
- `apps/api/src/engine/security/securityCheck.ts:161-355` resolves each
  security card one at a time, runs its Security effect while the card remains
  in security, then performs the battle and continues to the next check. Its
  Jamming branch skips deletion only for a battle against a Security Digimon.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:414-421`
  gates a deferred Security-battle event to the exact checked card instance,
  and `actions/play.ts:148-205` resolves the subsequent self
  `PlayWithoutCost` from trash without paying its cost.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:44-84,
  :1029-1050` maps `whenPlayed`, applies `controllerDefault` and `excludeSelf`,
  and namespaces once-per-turn watcher keys to the source instance;
  `apps/api/src/engine/effects/subtriggers.ts:485-552` enforces the per-turn
  ledger. `continuous.ts` reevaluates Leopardmon's live level filter and its
  turn duration.

## Per-card findings

### BT3-021 Veemon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog: Blue Lv.3 Rookie, 2000 DP, play cost 3, Blue Lv.2 evolution cost 0,
  `Free` attribute and `Mini Dragon` type. Its sole printed clause is
  `＜Jamming＞` (cannot be deleted in battles against Security Digimon).
- KB: no card-specific entry. The comprehensive Jamming, Security Digimon,
  battle, and inherited-effect rules supply the full contract.
- Implementation: `apps/api/src/cards/BT3/BT3-021.ts` contains a Static Jamming
  keyword, full coverage, no residual, and only `registerIrCard`.
- Proof: `BT3-021.test.ts` now covers the keyword, survival in a losing battle
  against the stronger Security Digimon BT1-081, deletion in a losing battle
  against an opposing BT1-081, and the negative case where Veemon is a source
  under a legal Blue level-4 host. BT1-016 was used as the same-mechanism
  Jamming peer.
- Status: the static IR, Security-only deletion protection, opposing-Digimon
  boundary, and stack boundary are all mapped. No trait-filtered behavior is
  printed or implied, and no direct behavior correction was required.

### BT3-023 Angemon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog: Blue Lv.4 Champion, 3000 DP, play cost 4, Blue Lv.3 evolution cost 2,
  `Vaccine` attribute and `Angel` type. The inherited When Attacking clause
  trashes the bottom digivolution card of one opponent's Digimon.
- KB: no card-specific entry. Comprehensive §§4-3, 4-8, and 15-3 constrain
  inherited activation to a card in the stack and define the target as an
  opposing Digimon with a digivolution card.
- Implementation: `BT3-023.ts` uses an inherited When Attacking
  `TrashDigivolution`, opponent Digimon filter, `hasAny` source requirement,
  amount 1, and `fromTop: false`; it is full coverage with no residual.
- Proof: `BT3-023.test.ts` uses a legal BT3-026-over-BT3-023 source stack and
  now gives the opposing target both bottom BT1-010 and top BT1-011 sources.
  It verifies only the bottom source is trashed, the top source remains, and a
  source-less opposing Digimon is unchanged. BT2-025 was compared as the
  analogous top-source trash peer; the difference in `fromTop` is intentional.
- Status: inherited scope, opponent controller, source requirement, amount,
  and bottom ordering are all proven statically. No trait-filtered behavior is
  printed or implied, and no direct behavior correction was required.

### BT3-024 Airdramon — provisional 8/10

- Catalog: Blue Lv.4 Champion, 4000 DP, play cost 5, Blue Lv.3 evolution cost 2,
  `Vaccine` attribute and `Mythical Beast` type. Its Security clause plays this
  card without paying its memory cost at the end of the battle.
- KB: Q1061 confirms the result is an ordinary Digimon in the battle area;
  Q1062 requires the effect even when the Security Digimon loses its battle;
  Q1063 requires the next security check to continue after the play.
- Implementation: the original direct module played the card immediately from
  Security, before the required battle. It was corrected to
  `timing: "endOfBattle"` plus a one-shot `whenSecurityBattleEnded` SubTrigger
  that plays the exact source from trash without paying, with full coverage and
  no residual. This follows the same delayed Security-play seam as BT3-011.
- Proof: `BT3-024.test.ts` covers Airdramon winning against weaker BT1-010,
  losing against stronger BT1-081 (Q1062), and being played before the second
  check when BT2-079 has Security Attack +1 with a two-card security stack
  (Q1063). It now also proves the weaker attacker is deleted by the Security
  battle, the stronger attacker survives, and the play event occurs after the
  first battle but before the next security check. Q1061's normal-battle-area
  identity is asserted by the field permanent result. BT3-011, BT2-079, and
  BT2-050 were checked as delayed-play and multi-check/security peers.
- Status: Security timing, self identity, free play, loss/win branches, and
  multi-check ordering are mapped after the direct timing correction. No
  trait-filtered behavior is printed or implied.
