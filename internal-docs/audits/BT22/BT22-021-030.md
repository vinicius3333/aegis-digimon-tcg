## Outcome and authority

This audit covers all ten cards in strict ascending card-ID order. The
immutable catalog authority is the committed Git blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` for
`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`. Each card received exactly one
local rules-KB query. The direct TypeScript module, compiled IR/runtime seams,
registration, source assertions, legal peers and stack fixtures, BT22 index,
and read-only generated snapshot were inspected.

The direct modules are the executable authority for these cards. The generated
snapshot `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was inspected read-only at
Git blob `86ec3ff83ca6458c357c14cd63bde5061c63f9d6`; it was not edited. The
BT22 static audit document and `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were
also left unchanged.

Five distinct semantic corrections were required:

- BT22-021 Shellmon now has an executable Decode replacement for a non-battle
  leave, scoped to its own stack.
- BT22-024 MarineBullmon now binds the chosen Sangomon through the supported
  selection-reference seam, scopes inherited play to its own stack, and has an
  executable Decode replacement.
- BT22-026 MetalGarurumon's modal Agumon and WarGreymon targets now require
  Digimon kind, matching the printed card types.
- BT22-027 Ryugumon now has an executable Decode replacement for a non-battle
  leave, scoped to its own stack.
- BT22-028 Ariemon now has an executable Decode replacement and scopes each
  mandatory level bucket to Ariemon's own digivolution stack.

BT22-027's self-scoped `onAddDigivolutionCards` watcher was already represented
by a `SubTrigger` with `sourceFilter.isSelfRef`; its missing executable Decode
replacement was corrected in this pass. BT22-030's self-linked trigger and
`isLinked` attack clause were already present. No semantic change was needed
for BT22-022, BT22-023, BT22-025, BT22-029, or BT22-030.

All ten direct modules expose `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, and exactly
one `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` call. No audited module has a second
`registerCard` registration. Each corrected-card change is in an atomic commit;
the report is committed separately. A final cross-card comparison found the
BT22-027 Decode gap after the BT22-028 commit, so the BT22-027 atomic correction
follows that commit in history while the report retains ascending card order.

## Rules and KB evidence

The card-specific KB query returned no entries for BT22-021, BT22-023,
BT22-025, BT22-026, BT22-027, BT22-029, or BT22-030. The available rulings
applied to this range were:

- BT22-022 Q4875: an `X in its text` reference includes the card's name,
  traits, effects, inherited effects, Rule text, evolution requirements, DNA,
  DigiXros, burst, App Fusion, and related printed text. The Veedramon Tamer
  search therefore uses the broad text matcher.
- BT22-024 Q4876: a Shellmon placed from trash under Sangomon can be made into
  the required cost-2 source before the Sangomon effect digivolves it into
  MarineBullmon. Q4877: the Sangomon placement effect and P-104's effect cannot
  both be activated simultaneously where the timing rule disallows it.
- BT22-028 Q4878: simultaneous effects may be ordered by their controller. Q5213:
  the effect must play one qualifying level-3, one level-4, and one level-5
  card whenever possible; it is not a choice to play only some buckets.

The general rules seams used for the remaining interpretations were §4-3 and
§4-4 (Digimon identity and stacks), §4-7 (Tamer and digivolution-card
handling), §4-23 (name/trait/text matching), §10 (Link), §11 (attacks),
§16-36/§16-36-1 (Decode), §18 (pending effects), and the printed timing and
once-per-turn rules.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT22-021 — Shellmon — provisional 7/10

- Catalog/rules: 2/2. Blue level-4 Digimon, play cost 5, blue level-3
  evolution cost 2, Mollusk/LIBERATOR/Aquatic traits. It has Decode for a
  level-3 Aqua or Sea Animal card in any trait, places a level-5-or-lower
  qualifying Digimon from hand under one of your Digimon on play and when
  digivolving, and has inherited Jamming. No card-specific KB entry.
- IR trace: 2/2. `BT22-021.ts` retains both hand-placement triggers and inherited
  Jamming, and now adds an `AllTurns` non-battle-leave Replacement that plays
  one level-3 qualifying card from the source's own stack without cost. The
  existing `traitContains` matching correctly treats Aqua and Sea Animal as
  trait alternatives.
- Behavioral proof: 1/2. `BT22-021.test.ts` asserts the Decode replacement,
  hand placement, Jamming, and adds a non-battle leave scenario. The source
  test also proves the hand level ceiling. The Decode leave is invoked with a
  direct delete primitive and was not executed, so it is supplemental rather
  than full natural-origin proof.
- Peer and stack proof: 2/2. BT19-024 and EX12 Decode implementations establish
  the replacement shape; BT22-018 is a legal level-3 Aqua/Sea Animal stack
  peer. The own-stack runtime seam prevents a neighboring stack from being
  selected.
- Correction: `36242b98a` adds the executable Decode replacement and source
  assertions. Registration remains exclusively `registerIrCard`.
