# BT22-061–070 Card Audit

## Scope and scoring policy

This audit covers exactly BT22-061 through BT22-070, in ascending card order.
The authoritative catalog records were read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. The local card-rules knowledge
base was queried exactly once for each card. For every card, this pass inspected
the direct TypeScript module, compiled IR, interpreter/runtime seams,
registration, focused source assertions, legal peers and evolution/stack
routes, and the read-only generated snapshot at
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`.

The protected `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and
`BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were not edited. The audit stayed within the assigned
range. Every audited module registers executable behavior exclusively through
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no duplicate `registerCard` registration
was added or retained.

Every card is scored on exactly five two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR
trace, Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates.
The delivery-gate component is fixed at 0/2 by task instruction, so every score
is provisional and capped at 8/10. Structural assertions and manually
injected timing or primitive calls are supplemental and do not receive full
behavioral credit where a natural originating event is feasible.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT22-061 — Vademon

- **Catalog/rules:** Black level 5 Ultimate Alien/DM/Ver.2, play cost 8 and
  8000 DP. It evolves from Vegiemon for 3 or a level-4 DM Digimon for 4. A
  Ver.2 Digimon evolving into this card receives a cost reduction of 1 per
  face-down source. Fragment (3) is printed. The shared When Digivolving and
  When Attacking Once Per Turn effect De-Digivolves one opposing Digimon, then
  may trash this Digimon's bottom face-down source to return one opposing
  play-cost-4-or-lower Digimon or Tamer. The inherited Opponent's Turn effect
  may redirect one opponent Digimon's attack to this Digimon.
- **IR trace:** `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/BT22-061.ts` contains a real
  `wouldDigivolve` replacement scoped to a controller-owned Ver.2 source and
  Vademon as the destination. Its scaling filter is self-bound and
  face-down-only. Fragment, both shared trigger timings, De-Digivolve, the
  bottom face-down trash cost, the opponent Digimon/Tamer cost ceiling, and the
  inherited self-targeted RedirectAttack are all executable IR. The two main
  timings share `ir-shared-0`, preserving one Once Per Turn across them.
- **Behavioral proof:** The focused source assertions cover the replacement,
  face-down scaling, shared frequency, ordered De-Digivolve/return cost, and
  inherited redirect. The natural public evolution case evolves from BT22-049
  Vegiemon with two face-down sources, uses one memory, De-Digivolves the
  opposing BT22-071 stack, returns its BT1-009 source, and trashes exactly one
  host source. Behavioral credit remains 1/2 because the inherited redirect
  and the alternate When Attacking origin were not naturally executed in this
  prohibited-gate pass.
- **Peer and stack proof:** BT22-038 and BT22-076 provide face-down stack
  reduction peers; BT22-060 provides the same opponent-choice inherited attack
  family. The Vegiemon-to-Vademon route and two face-down sources are a legal
  stack path, supporting 2/2.
- **Snapshot note:** The generated snapshot has only a broad mine-side
  `digivolutionCards` scaling filter, omitting the direct IR's self-reference
  and face-down restriction. The protected snapshot was not used as the
  authoritative executable definition.

## Delivery gates and limitations

Per task instruction, this pass did **not** run tests, typecheck, lint,
formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, or
`git diff --check`. The focused tests and source assertions were inspected but
not executed. Consequently, every delivery-gate score is 0/2 and every total
above is provisional; no collection-completion or workspace-completed claim is
made.

The main remaining evidence limitations are unexecuted natural event coverage
for several alternate timings and inherited effects, the manually assembled
BT22-069 stack fixture, and the primitive-driven BT22-065 simultaneous
deletion fixture. No further confirmed semantic defect was found in BT22-061,
062, 063, 064, 065, 066, 067, 068, or 070 after the catalog, KB, IR, runtime,
peer, stack, and snapshot comparison.
