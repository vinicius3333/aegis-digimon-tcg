## Scope and authority

This audit covers BT22-001 through BT22-010, in ascending card-ID order, and does not assess any card outside that range. The immutable catalog authority was read from Git blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; its object hash is also the hash of `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, confirming that the catalog data used here is the committed catalog data.

For each card, the local rules knowledge base was queried exactly once with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <cardId>`. The direct TypeScript module, its compiled IR, the interpreter/runtime seams, registration, focused source assertions, related legal peers, legal stack records, and the generated snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were then read. The generated snapshot and `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` were treated as read-only; neither it nor `BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was edited.

Tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check` were explicitly prohibited by the assignment and were not run. Consequently, the Executed delivery gates component is `0/2` for every card and every score below is provisional, with a maximum of `8/10`.

## Scoring

Each card receives five independent two-point components:

1. Catalog/rules evidence
2. IR trace and runtime seam
3. Behavioral proof in source
4. Peer and legal-stack proof
5. Executed delivery gates

Structural assertions and manually injected timing/events/primitives are useful regression evidence but do not receive full Behavioral credit where a natural card or engine origin is feasible. A card can therefore have a complete IR trace and still have a provisional reduction for source-only behavioral or legal-stack limitations.

## Runtime and registration findings

The inspected registration path requires each audited module to export compiled IR and call only `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. BT22-001 through BT22-010 satisfy that requirement after the changes below; no duplicate `registerCard` registration exists in the audited modules or their focused tests.

The interpreter’s `onAddDigivolutionCards` sub-trigger has a dedicated effect-provenance gate. The `placeUnder` and top-to-bottom stack-rotation primitives emit the originating effect seat when run inside effect resolution, while ordinary/manual placement does not. This is the runtime distinction required by the catalog wording “when effects place”. The `whenLinked`, deletion, play, security, replacement, DNA, keyword, cost, and continuous-DP seams were also traced to the corresponding compiled actions.

The generated snapshot is stale in multiple places, including omitted or broad filters, the old BT22-004 direct-digivolve representation, a raw BT22-006 entry, incomplete BT22-007 replacement data, a single-target BT22-008 DNA representation, missing BT22-009 linking data, and the missing BT22-010 payment abort. It was consulted only as a read-only comparison artifact and was not used to override the direct modules or runtime behavior.

## Card-by-card audit

### BT22-001 — Puyoyomon

Catalog/rules evidence: The immutable catalog identifies a blue level-2 Digi-Egg with the inherited `[Your Turn][Once Per Turn]` effect that draws when an effect places a Digimon card with an `[Aqua]` or `[Sea Animal]` trait in this Digimon’s digivolution cards. The one permitted KB query returned no card-specific entry; the general placement and inherited-effect rules were covered by the rules source read.

IR trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/BT22-001.ts` uses inherited `YourTurn`/`OncePerTurn`, a self-hosted `onAddDigivolutionCards` sub-trigger, and the Aqua/Sea Animal trait union. The source filter now includes `byEffect: true`, in addition to the controller and self-stack constraints, so ordinary card placement cannot satisfy the printed “effects place” condition. The `subTrigger.ts` provenance gate and `primitives.ts` effect-context payload provide the matching runtime seam.

Behavioral proof: `BT22-001.test.ts` contains a structural assertion for the effect-provenance filter, positive placement through the real `placeUnder` primitive inside an effect-resolution context, once-per-turn behavior, and negative nonmatching/other-stack/opponent-turn cases. The positive origin is still a primitive-driven source fixture rather than a natural originating card effect, so Behavioral remains `1/2`; no test was executed.

Peer and stack proof: Aqua/Sea Animal trigger patterns in the inspected BT22-024, BT22-027, and BT22-028 peer modules agree with the trait-union shape. The focused fixture uses BT6-030 as a hand-laid level-7 host over the Digi-Egg, which is not a legal evolution sequence, so the legal-stack component is `1/2`.

Correction: Added `byEffect: true` to the inherited source filter and added source assertions plus an effect-context placement helper in the focused test.
