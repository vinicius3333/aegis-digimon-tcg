# BT22-011–020 Card Audit

## Scope and scoring policy

This audit covers exactly BT22-011 through BT22-020 in ascending order. The
authoritative catalog text was read from immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. The local card rules KB was queried
exactly once for each card. For every card, the direct TypeScript module,
compiled IR and runtime seams, registration, source assertions, legal peers and
evolution/stack routes, and the read-only generated
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot were inspected.

The protected `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and
`BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were not edited. The audit stayed within the assigned
BT22-011–020 range, and every direct module uses exclusive
`registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)` registration; no duplicate `registerCard`
registration was added or retained.

Every card is scored on exactly five two-point components: Catalog/rules, IR
trace, Behavioral proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates.
The delivery-gate component is fixed at 0/2 by task instruction, so every
result is provisional and capped at 8/10. Structural assertions and manually
injected timing or primitive calls are supplemental and do not receive full
behavioral credit where a natural originating event is feasible.

executable `AllTurns` replacement rather than a keyword-only marker; the

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT22-011 — BlueMeramon

- **Catalog/rules:** Red/Purple level 5 Ultimate Virus Flame/CS, play cost 7,
  7000 DP, with red or purple level-4 evolution for 4 and an alternate level-4
  Flame/CS evolution for 3. The Main Once Per Turn clause pays 3, may play one
  play-cost-5-or-lower Flame Digimon from trash for free, then may attack. The
  inherited Your Turn clause grants Alliance only to a Flame/CS host. Q4868 and
  Q4869 confirm the payment gate and its effect on the follow-up attack.
- **IR trace:** `BT22-011.ts` uses the real pay-memory cost, trash-only free
  play, exact Flame and play-cost filters, abort-on-decline behavior, and a
  separate optional self-attack. Its inherited effect is a real turn-scoped
  Alliance grant with the Flame/CS qualifier.
- **Behavioral proof:** The source test uses public Main activation with memory
  5, plays the eligible BT22-010 from trash, leaves the nonmatching BT1-009,
  and verifies memory 2. It also observes inherited Alliance on a CS host and
  its off-turn removal. The optional follow-up attack is only asserted
  structurally, so behavioral credit is 1/2.
- **Peer and stack proof:** The BT22-013 host carrying BT22-011 exercises the
  inherited stack path, while BT22-010 is a legal Flame/CS level-4 peer for the
  source-play and evolution route.

## Generated snapshot and registration findings

The generated `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` snapshot was read only
and was not treated as an authority over corrected direct modules. The target
snapshot is stale in several places: BT22-012 has the old Tamer condition and
over-constrained color/CS filter; BT22-013 omits the paid hand-digivolution

the corrected self-scoped replacement. BT22-011, BT22-014, BT22-017, and
BT22-020 are materially aligned for the audited clauses, subject to direct IR
being the executable source.

All ten direct modules were inspected for registration. Each ends with exactly
one `registerIrCard("BT22-0XX", compiled)` call, and no target module has a
second `registerCard` registration.
