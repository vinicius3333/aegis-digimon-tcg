## Outcome and authority

This audit covers all ten cards in strict ascending card-ID order. The
immutable catalog authority was read from catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`, which is the committed blob for
`packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`. Exactly one local rules-KB query
was made for each card. The direct TypeScript module, compiled IR/runtime
seams, source assertions, legal peers and stack fixtures, BT22 index, and the
protected generated effects snapshot were inspected.

Four definite semantic mismatches were corrected:

- BT22-075 Fakemon now limits hosted link material to this Digimon's own
  digivolution cards while retaining the separate trash source.
- BT22-076 ShinMonzaemon now expresses its Ver.1 reduction as a hand-resident
  digivolution `CostModifier`, so the destination card can actually provide
  the reduction.
- BT22-077 Dianamon now pools its four selected digivolution cards across all
  matching opponent Digimon stacks.
- BT22-080 Eater (Human Form) now limits Species Form placement to its own
  stack and gates the security-check effect to this attacker during your turn.

The atomic correction commits, in ascending card order, are:

- `3ab5d453c` — `fix(bt22-075): scope link material to this Digimon`
- `5e17e1ff0` — `fix(bt22-076): make ShinMonzaemon reduction hand resident`
- `e78c88882` — `fix(bt22-077): pool four cards across opponent stacks`
- `1c70850c5` — `fix(bt22-080): scope Eater triggers to their source`

No semantic change was required for BT22-071, BT22-072, BT22-073, BT22-074,
BT22-078, or BT22-079. All ten direct modules expose `coverage: "full"`, an
empty `residual` array, and exactly one `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`
registration. The BT22 index imports all ten modules at
`apps/api/src/cards/BT22/index.ts:72-81`; the individual registrations are at
the ends of the ten direct modules. No audited module has a second legacy
`registerCard` registration.

The generated snapshot
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was inspected read-only at committed
blob `86ec3ff83ca6458c357c14cd63bde5061c63f9d6`; it was not edited. The
protected `BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md` was also not edited.
