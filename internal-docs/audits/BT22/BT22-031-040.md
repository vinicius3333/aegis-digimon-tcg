## Scope and authority

This report covers exactly BT22-031 through BT22-040 in ascending card-ID order. The
immutable catalog authority was read from committed blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. For each card, the local rules knowledge
base was queried exactly once with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD_ID>`; zero-result
queries are recorded below. The review also covered each direct TypeScript module and
colocated source test, compiled IR and runtime seams, registration, legal peers and
stacks, and the read-only generated snapshot at
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`.

All ten direct modules have full compiled coverage, empty residual coverage, and
executable registration exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. No
audited module has a duplicate `registerCard` registration. The direct module is the
executable authority when the generated snapshot is stale or lossy; the snapshot was
not edited. Neither protected file, `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` nor
`BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, was modified.

This is a source/static audit. The task explicitly prohibited tests, typecheck, lint,
formatting, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff --check`.
Consequently `Executed delivery gates` is fixed at `0/2` for every card and every score
is provisional, capped at `8/10`. Structural assertions and manually fired timing or
primitive fixtures are supplemental and do not earn behavioral `2/2` where a natural
card/engine origin is feasible.

## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive
   registration, with generated IR recorded only as comparison evidence.
3. **Behavioral proof** — observable source-test evidence, prioritizing public/natural
   card origins over direct timing injection.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — useful peer comparison and legal evolution/link/inherited
   stack coverage where applicable.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch boundary.

  the same condition as a raw expression and is therefore a lossy comparison only.
- **Behavioral proof — 1/2.** `BT22-031.test.ts` naturally performs the alternate CS
  evolution and observes the Security Attack reduction, and naturally proves the
  inherited cost reduction through two realistic stacks. The positive same-level
  PlatinumNumemon branch is entered through a direct `OnPlay` timing helper, although
  a public play/evolution origin is feasible; the refusal and failed-condition cases
  are also timing-helper driven. That limitation prevents full behavioral credit.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** BT22-019 supplies the CS evolution base, BT22-065 and
  BT14-066 supply legal PlatinumNumemon destinations, and BT22-037/BT22-031 stacks
  exercise inherited reduction and the Q4879 top/under level pairing. The runtime
  `sourceStackHasSameLevelCards` seam includes the top card as required.
