## Scope and authority

This report covers exactly BT22-081 through BT22-090 in ascending card-ID order. The
immutable catalog authority was read from committed blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. For each card, the local rules knowledge
base was queried exactly once with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD_ID>`; the
per-card results are listed below. The review also covered each direct TypeScript
module and colocated source test, compiled IR and runtime seams, exclusive
registration, the BT22 index, legal peers and stacks, and the read-only generated
snapshot at `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`.

All ten direct modules have `coverage: "full"`, empty `residual` arrays, and
executable registration exclusively through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. The
BT22 index imports the cards in order at `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/index.ts:82-91`.
No audited module has a duplicate `registerCard` registration. The direct module is
the executable authority where the generated snapshot is stale or lossy; the
snapshot was not edited. Neither protected file, `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json`
nor `BT22-STATIC-AUDIT.md`, was modified.

This is a source/static audit. The task explicitly prohibited tests, typecheck, lint,
formatting, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, and `git diff
--check`. Consequently `Executed delivery gates` is fixed at `0/2` for every card
and every score is provisional, capped at `8/10`. Existing source tests and
assertions were inspected but not executed. Structural assertions and manually
fired timing or primitive fixtures are supplemental and do not earn behavioral
`2/2` where a natural card/engine origin is feasible.

## Fixed scoring model

Each card receives exactly five two-point components:

1. **Catalog/rules** — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable KB rulings.
2. **IR trace** — direct compiled trigger/action/requirement trace and exclusive
   registration, with generated IR recorded only as comparison evidence.
3. **Behavioral proof** — observable source-test evidence, prioritizing public/natural
   card origins over direct timing or primitive injection.
4. **Peer and stack proof** — useful peer comparison and legal evolution/link/inherited
   stack coverage where applicable.
5. **Executed delivery gates** — fixed at `0/2` by the dispatch boundary.

## Card findings

### BT22-081 Eater Eve — 7/10 provisional

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** The immutable record identifies a white level-6 Eater/CS
  Digimon with alternate `[Yuuko Kamishiro]: Cost 3` evolution and Raid. Its entry
  effects restrict one opposing Digimon from suspending through the opponent's turn,
  then may place a Yuuko from hand or trash as this Digimon's bottom evolution card
  when its stack is empty. Its All Turns replacement may play one Yuuko from its own
  evolution cards without paying. The single KB query returned Q4948 and Q6693-Q6697,
  including the relevant Eater/CS and placement interpretation records.
- **IR trace — 2/2.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/BT22-081.ts:10-148` contains Raid,
  both `OnPlay` and `WhenDigivolving` Restrict/PlaceUnder sequences, structured
  `selfHasNoDigivolutionCards`, hand/trash sourcing, bottom placement, the self-scoped
  `wouldLeavePlay` replacement, and the Yuuko evolution requirement. The outer leave
  replacement is now explicitly optional. The generated snapshot retains the
  condition as a raw expression and omitted the outer optional gate; direct IR is
  authoritative.
- **Behavioral proof — 1/2.** `BT22-081.test.ts:39-59` naturally plays Eve publicly
  with Yuuko in trash and observes the Yuuko card placed underneath. The leave and
  evolution paths are only source assertions or lack a natural public-origin proof in
  this audit; the source test was not run. That limitation prevents full behavioral
  credit.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** BT22-080 supplies the adjacent Eater stack/leave
  pattern, BT22-083 is the legal Yuuko Tamer, and BT22-091 provides the related CS
  Tamer stack context. The source requirement and self-stack anchor agree with the
  direct runtime hosted-card targeting seam.
- **Executed delivery gates — 0/2.** Deferred by task instruction.
- **Implementation finding.** Commit `53216f3e3` adds `optional: true` to the outer
  leave replacement and a focused source assertion. `runReplacement` prompts on the
  outer replacement; a nested `PlayWithoutCost.optional` alone does not make the
  replacement declinable. This was a definite semantic mismatch and is corrected.
