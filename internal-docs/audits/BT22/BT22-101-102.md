Q4977 defines “2 or more same-level cards” across all cards in the stack, including the top card and digivolution cards, which is the interpretation used by the corrected live matcher.

The local rules index and manual were also checked for the relevant procedures: comprehensive rules 8-1/8-1-2/8-1-3 require the destination to be a Digimon card whose digivolution requirements are met, preserve the stack through digivolution, and draw after placement; 15-5 through 15-7 cover trigger timing, processing conditions, and optional processing. The manual's digivolution section states that the field card must meet one of the revealed card's requirements and that the revealed card is stacked on the chosen card.

## Card findings

### BT22-101 — Kyoko Kuremi

Catalog behavior is three clauses: set memory to 3 at the start of your turn when memory is 2 or less; on all turns, when one of your level 4-or-higher CS Digimon is deleted, optionally suspend this Tamer to return one CS Digimon from your trash to hand; and once per your turn, when this Tamer unsuspends, optionally digivolve it into an Alphamon in hand for a cost reduced by 2 when no Alphamon is present. Security plays the card without paying its cost.

The direct module in `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/BT22-101.ts:11-143` carries all three timings and the Security action. It registers only with `registerIrCard("BT22-101", compiled)` at line 150; no legacy `registerCard` registration exists. The BT22 index imports it at `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/index.ts:102`.

Two definite semantic mismatches were corrected:

1. The Alphamon destination filter did not explicitly restrict the destination to the controller's hand or to Digimon cards. The loose-card resolver treats an unset controller scope as both seats, so the effect could inspect an opponent's hand; an unrestricted trait/name destination also failed to encode the printed “Digimon card” noun. The destination now carries `controllerDefault: "mine"` and `kind: ["Digimon"]` (`BT22-101.ts:105-110`).
2. Q4976 requires the destination Alphamon's digivolution requirements to be met. The effect-driven candidate resolver currently skips requirement filtering for a level-less Tamer base, and the effect primitive does not consume an alternate requirement's live `whileCondition`. Under the immutable catalog, BT22-063 is the only Alphamon with a Kyoko Kuremi route. The destination therefore now carries the exact current legal `cardId: "BT22-063"`, while the watcher applies that route's three-or-fewer Security gate before offering the optional action (`BT22-101.ts:89-112`). This excludes other current Alphamon cards that have no Kyoko route and avoids consuming the optional activation when BT22-063's live requirement is unavailable. The generic level-less candidate-resolver gap remains an engine limitation outside this card-specific correction.

Focused assertions in `BT22-101.test.ts` now verify the controller/kind/exact-card destination and live security gate, retain the existing memory and reduced-cost checks, and add natural `advance(...).runTurn(0)` flows for an eligible three-security BT22-063 evolution, an ineligible four-security board, and BT6-111 Alphamon as a negative destination with no Kyoko route. The natural tests were added but intentionally not executed during this audit.

Peer and stack evidence includes BT22-063's named Kyoko Kuremi alternate requirement with its three-security `whileCondition`, all cataloged Alphamon destinations, the shared digivolution legality consumers, and the surrounding BT22 CS/Alphamon evolution line. No other current Alphamon has a Kyoko Kuremi route. The test board's BT22-063 destination and the suspended-to-unsuspended turn-start path match the printed stack and orientation rules.

### BT22-102 — Sayo

Catalog behavior is a Start of Your Main Phase memory gain of 1 when the opponent has a Digimon, plus a Your Turn attack watcher: when one of your Digimon whose full stack contains at least two cards sharing a level attacks, optionally suspend this Tamer to digivolve that attacking Digimon into a qualifying Night Claw, Light Fang, Galaxy, or CS Digimon card in your trash for a cost reduced by 2. Security plays the card without paying its cost.

The direct module in `apps/api/src/cards/BT22/BT22-102.ts:12-95` carries the start-main, attack watcher, and Security action. Its attack destination already correctly restricts the loose card to `controllerDefault: "mine"`, `kind: ["Digimon"]`, and `from: ["trash"]` (`BT22-102.ts:48-60`), and the target is the triggering attacker (`isTriggerSource: true`). It registers only with `registerIrCard("BT22-102", compiled)` at line 101; no legacy `registerCard` registration exists. The BT22 index imports it at `BT22/index.ts:103`.

One definite runtime mismatch was corrected. The direct IR correctly expressed `stackHasSameLevelCards: 2` in the `whenAttacking` source filter (`BT22-102.ts:32-38`), but that predicate existed only as a Condition in the shared IR and was not implemented by `permanentMatchesFilter`. Consequently, the watcher source filter was ignored at runtime and could fire for every qualifying own Digimon attack, even when no two cards in the stack shared a level. The fix adds the live-board predicate to `BoardPredicates` and evaluates it against the candidate's top card plus every digivolution card before stripping it for ordinary definition matching (`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/filters/boardPredicates.ts:30-33`; `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/permanent.ts:88-110,246-250`). The existing source-level condition helper now delegates to the same reusable implementation.

Focused assertions in `BT22-102.test.ts` retain the existing IR and reduced-cost checks and add natural public `attack` flows. The positive fixture has BT22-069 and BT22-068 at level 3 in the stack and evolves into BT22-072; the negative fixture has BT22-069 level 3 over BT22-071 level 4 and verifies that Sayo stays unsuspended, memory is unchanged, and the trash card remains (`BT22-102.test.ts:92-161`). These tests were added but intentionally not executed during this audit.

Peer and stack evidence includes BT22-072/BT22-073's same-level Conditions and same-level-pair costs, BT22-093's natural same-level watcher proof and negative control, and the BT22-069/BT22-071/BT22-072 stack used by the focused assertions. Q4977's all-stacked-cards interpretation agrees with the new matcher, which counts the top card and all cards beneath it.

The canonical `effects.json` entry for BT22-102 remains a partial/raw `whenAttacking` record with a missing primitive, as it was read-only in this audit. The direct module remains the executable IR authority and now has the runtime matcher needed by its already-compiled structured predicate.
