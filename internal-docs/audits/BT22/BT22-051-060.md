## Scope and authority

## Scoring model

Each card receives exactly five independent two-point components:

1. Catalog/rules evidence
2. IR trace and runtime seam
3. Behavioral proof in source
4. Peer and legal-stack proof
5. Executed delivery gates

Structural assertions and manually injected timing/events/primitives are recorded as useful source evidence but do not receive full Behavioral credit where a natural card-origin path is feasible. Source tests were inspected but not executed in this dispatch.

## Runtime and registration findings

The inspected `registerIrCard` path normalizes each direct compiled module into the executable registry and timing collections. `WhenDigivolving`, `OnPlay`, `WhenLinking`, `AllTurns`, `YourTurn`, `OpponentsTurn`, `EndOfOpponentsTurn`, and static keyword entries map to the corresponding interpreter seams. Once-per-turn scope is carried onto reactive SubTrigger/Replacement registrations, and self-reference is resolved against the permanent carrying an inherited or linked source card.

The `onAddDigivolutionCards` seam distinguishes effect-driven placement from ordinary digivolution and matches the newly added card through its live card definition. The same-level stack condition counts any two or more cards with the host's top-card level, matching Q4903/Q4908. The leave-prevention seam evaluates the leaving permanent against the replacement source filter, including the corrected battle-area gate. Linking, App Fusion, Blast Digivolve, forced attack, DP scaling, return restriction, and deletion/token seams were also traced for the cards where applicable.

The generated snapshot is stale or weaker than several direct modules, including older broad placement filters, missing linked behavior, raw stack predicates, and incomplete self/turn/target metadata. It was consulted only for comparison and was not changed or allowed to override the direct modules.

The inherited `whenDeletesInBattle` watcher is self-anchored and trashes the opponent's top security card; the combat deletion seam excludes a vanished/equal-DP host as required by Q4904. Registration is IR-only.
- Behavioral proof: The focused source contains structural assertions plus a natural evolution path, repeated-level condition, opponent suspension/lowest-DP return, natural attack security trash, and simultaneous-deletion negative evidence. This supports full source behavioral credit, but the source was not executed here.
- Peer/stack: The BT22-052 level-6 CS host over BT22-051 is a legal evolution edge, and the inspected same-level/lowest-DP/security peers agree with the typed predicates.
- Correction: No new semantic correction was required in this dispatch.
