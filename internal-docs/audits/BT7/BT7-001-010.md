## Shared rules and interpreter evidence

- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:216-223` distinguishes bracket-only card-name references (exact name, §2-3-1-2) from “with [XX] in its name” substring references (§2-3-1-3). This is load-bearing for BT7-008 versus BT7-009.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:660-663` states that a Digimon gains inherited effects from cards under it. The inherited flags in this range are therefore required, not optional metadata.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:1429-1432` says digivolution is not considered adding a digivolution card. This is the Q1504 boundary for BT7-005.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2052-2060` gives the exact effect-placement example for a `[Your Turn][Once Per Turn]` inherited trigger, matching BT7-005.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2705-2719` defines the On Play, When Digivolving, On Deletion, and When Attacking timing points used by BT7-008 and BT7-010.
- `data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md:2952-2959` defines Digi-Burst as an effect activated by trashing the host’s specified digivolution cards and makes its processing optional. The Digi-Burst event ordering inspected in the engine matches Q1503 for BT7-003.

The following engine seams were inspected as executable support for the direct IR:

- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/trigger.ts:46-51` gates BT7-002’s `fromDigivolution` restriction using the emitted source zone; the same file’s controller/kind matching enforces “your Digimon”.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/subTrigger.ts:297-308` has the dedicated `byEffect` gate for `onAddDigivolutionCards`, while lines 715-755 anchor inherited discard events to the receiving host/source. This supports BT7-003 and BT7-005.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/effect.ts:379-403` carries Your Turn, Once Per Turn, and inherited timing state into SubTrigger processing.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:973-992` emits effect-driven plays with `playedFromZone: "digivolutionCards"`; lines 1617-1654 emit `onAddDigivolutionCards` with effect provenance only inside an effect-resolution context; lines 2189-2265 establish Digi-Burst discard event ordering.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/actions/reveal.ts:108-121`, `202-220`, `277-299`, and `515-551` implement reveal counts, candidate filters, mandatory versus optional selection, and top/bottom remainder placement used by BT7-004, BT7-006, BT7-008, and BT7-009.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/interpreter/matching/definition.ts:272-341` implements `name` as substring and `nameExact` as normalized equality. `effectiveStaticNames` is included for cards with explicit Rule Name aliases; this keeps an actual exact-name alias such as BT18-088 valid without weakening the bracket-only rule.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/builders.ts:26-30` and `151-168` distinguish `attackScope: "ally"` (an observer effect) from the default host/self attack watcher. This proved BT7-004’s former `attackScope: "ally"` was over-broad.
- `apps/api/src/engine/effects/primitives.ts:625+` and `apps/api/src/engine/effects/modifiers.ts:195-225` provide DP modifier and turn-duration support for BT7-001, BT7-003, and BT7-010.

Representative peer checks covered the same seams: BT17-002/BT17-082 for plays from digivolution cards, EX5-001/ST13-14/BT17-003 for placement watchers, ST13-16/EX5-071 for reveal remainder placement, EX2-060 for ally attack observers, BT10-015/BT10-069/ST15-08 for exact named references, and BT6-082/BT7-009 for substring Sistermon-name searches. No peer justified retaining the three corrected gaps.

## Card-by-card findings

### BT7-001 — Kapurimon

**Catalog clause.** Red level-2 Digi-Egg, In-Training/Lesser, DP 0; inherited: `[Your Turn] While you have a Tamer in play, this Digimon gets +1000 DP.`

**Direct implementation.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT7/BT7-001.ts:8-44` is a full inherited Aura. It targets only the host (`isSelfRef`, `isSelf`), checks the battle area for one of the controller’s Tamers (`controllerDefault: "mine"`, `kind: ["Tamer"]`), and applies +1000 DP during Your Turn. This exactly reflects Q1502: no Tamer color restriction was invented.

**Static behavior proof.** `BT7-001.test.ts` covers an own Tamer of the accepted color, an own Tamer of another color, opponent-only Tamer exclusion, and the opponent-turn boundary. The host is a legal red level-3 Digimon over the red BT7-001 egg.

**Snapshot and score.** Snapshot `effects.json:112860-112880` matches the direct module. Score: catalog 2/2, direct IR 2/2, behavior 2/2, peers/boundaries 2/2, executed gates 0/2 = **8/10 provisional**.

### BT7-004 — Koromon

**Catalog clause.** Green level-2 Digi-Egg, In-Training/Lesser; inherited: `[When Attacking] Reveal the top card of your deck, and place it at the top or bottom of your deck.`

**Correction.** `apps/api/src/cards/BT7/BT7-004.ts:5-17` had `attackScope: "ally"`, which makes an inherited host watcher fire when any allied Digimon attacks. The shared builder contract reserves that scope for observer text such as “when one of your Digimon attacks”; this card says simply “when attacking” and must watch the host. The correction removes `attackScope`, leaving the default self/host scope while retaining `RevealAdd(revealCount: 1, rest: "deckTopOrBottom")`.

**Static behavior proof.** `BT7-004.test.ts` now uses a legal green BT1-066 over the green egg and covers top-or-bottom placement. A new negative case attacks an unrelated allied BT1-066 and asserts the deck’s top card remains in place. This is the direct proof for the corrected scope.

**Snapshot drift.** `effects.json:112919-112935` still has a legacy generic `Search` reveal action and cannot represent top-or-bottom placement; it also predates the direct host-scope correction. No snapshot edit was made.

**Score.** Catalog 2/2, direct IR 2/2, behavior 2/2, peers/boundaries 2/2, executed gates 0/2 = **8/10 provisional**.
