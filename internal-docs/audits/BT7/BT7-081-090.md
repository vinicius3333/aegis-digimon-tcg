## Rules, rulings, errata, and restrictions

The committed KB references read for this range were the Official Rule Manual web ver. 6.0 (`data/kb/rules/manual.md`) and Comprehensive Rules ver. 4.2, updated 2026-08-18 (`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`). The applicable clauses were:

- Comprehensive §§2-3 define exact bracket-only name references, substring name references, exact traits, substring trait references, and multi-trait matching. In particular, §§2-3-1-2/3 require `nameExact` for clauses that specify only `[Sistermon Blanc]`, `[Sistermon Ciel]`, `[Eosmon]`, `[EmperorGreymon]`, and `[MagnaGarurumon]`, while “with [X] in its name” remains a substring match; §§2-3-2-3/4 require the corresponding exact or substring trait mode. These govern Hybrid/Ten Warriors searches, Eosmon/Huckmon/Sistermon substring filters, exact exclusion of each Awakened card from its own placement/recovery, and X Antibody trait searches.
- Comprehensive §§4-8 cover card areas, Tamers, digivolution cards, deletion/trashing, ordinary digivolution, and stack transitions. §4-8 distinguishes a card in a digivolution stack from a card on the field; §8-1-2-7 says ordinary digivolution is not “adding” a card to the stack; §8-1-3 fixes the ordinary reveal/pay/place/draw sequence.
- Comprehensive §§11-2 and 13-14 cover attack declaration, security checks, and battle outcomes. The BT7-086 inherited restriction is attached to the selected opponent Digimon and remains active for its stated duration even if that Digimon later gains sources (Q1656 and §15-11-1-3-2).
- Comprehensive §§15-1, 15-3, 15-4, 15-7, 15-8, and 15-9 cover ordered processing, inherited effects, trigger state, optional processing, trigger/immediate timing, and mandatory versus optional actions. Q1652/Q1659 and §15-7 establish that Takuya/Koji may place exactly five cards and decline the follow-on evolution.
- Comprehensive §§15-10 and 15-11 distinguish individual target selection from overall/all processing and preserve the identity of an individually selected card. Comprehensive §15-13 covers the state of gained effects, relevant to inherited Tamer effects.

`data/kb/errata.json` contains no BT7-081 through BT7-090 entry. `data/kb/banlist.json` contains a historical BT7-086 Tommy Himi restriction effective 2022-08-01 and a corresponding lift effective 2023-11-17; no current restriction remains. The historical banlist record does not alter the card's executable effect. No other assigned card appears in the restriction data.

- Card-data Tamer evolution support in `apps/api/src/engine/cards/cardData.ts` derives the alternate level/color route and actual evolution cost from the Tamer-onto action. It does not treat a stale generated requirement cost marker as permission to ignore the printed destination requirement.

### BT7-081 — Bokomon

**Catalog and clauses.** The committed catalog identifies White level 3, play cost 3, 2000 DP, Rookie / Vaccine / Mutant. On Play reveals five, adds one Hybrid or Ten Warriors trait card and one Tamer among them, and bottom-decks the remainder. During your turn once per turn, when one of your Tamers digivolves, it gains 2 memory.

**Direct and correction.** The direct module maps the first clause to `RevealAdd` with two distinct category slots and `deckBottom` remainder. Its second clause is Your Turn, once per turn, and watches a Digimon with a Tamer in its stack through `whenOneOfYoursDigivolves`, gaining 2 memory. The Hybrid/Ten Warriors slot was corrected from exact `trait` matching to `traitContains`, because the catalog says the qualifying traits occur “in its traits” (Comprehensive §2-3-2-4). Q1645 supports resolving whichever category is present rather than inventing a requirement that both must be present.

**Snapshot drift, tests, peers, and stack.** The snapshot uses exact `trait` matching and omits the direct `kind: ["Digimon"]` source boundary; both are recorded as drift and the snapshot remains read-only. `BT7-081.test.ts` proves both reveal categories, visible loose-card decisions, bottom ordering, the Tamer evolution gain, and the once-per-turn limit across two evolving Tamers; a new static proof asserts the substring trait mode. The BT7-085/087 Tamer-onto modules and tests were inspected as same-mechanism peers. The legal stack fixture evolves a Takuya Tamer into a Hybrid Digimon and confirms the Tamer remains in the stack while Bokomon gains memory.

**Boundaries.** Hybrid and Ten Warriors are trait-substring matches; Tamer is a card-kind match. The two selected instances cannot be the same revealed card because the reveal primitive tracks consumed instances. No inherited or Security clause is present.

**Score:** 2/2 clause coverage, 2/2 timing/frequency, 2/2 category/source boundaries, 2/2 reveal/stack behavior, 0/2 executed gates = **8/10 provisional**.
