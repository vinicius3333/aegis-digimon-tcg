## Rules, rulings, errata, and restrictions

The committed KB references read for this range were the Official Rule Manual web ver. 6.0 (`data/kb/rules/manual.md`) and Comprehensive Rules ver. 4.2, updated 2026-08-18 (`data/kb/rules/comprehensive.md`). The applicable clauses were:

- Comprehensive §§2-3 define exact bracket-only name references, substring name references, exact traits, and substring trait references. The standalone `[Lucemon]` reference on BT7-111 is an exact name gate (§2-3-1-2), whereas this range has no printed trait-search clause; BT7-112's `[Hybrid]` text is a trait reference and its `Tamer cards` branch is a card-kind boundary (§2-3-2-3/4).
- Comprehensive §§4-8 cover card areas, Tamers, digivolution cards, deletion/trashing, and stack transitions. §§8-1-2-7 and 8-1-3 distinguish ordinary digivolution from adding a card and establish reveal/pay/place/draw ordering. The BT7-112 placement cost therefore removes loose hand/trash cards to the deck bottom before the evolving card is placed onto the chosen Tamer.
- Comprehensive §§15-1, 15-3, 15-4, 15-7, 15-8, and 15-9 cover ordered processing, inherited effects, trigger state, optional processing, trigger/immediate timing, and mandatory versus optional actions. Q1678 confirms that BT7-111's shared timing declaration is two trigger windows, while Q1681 confirms BT7-112's declared alternate path must complete its required selection and evolution.
- Comprehensive §§15-10 and 15-11 distinguish individual target selection from overall processing and preserve the identity of the selected card. This governs each card's “1” delete target and BT7-112's chosen Tamer/ten-card payment.
- Comprehensive §15-13 covers gained and inherited effects. Q6534/Q6535 are relevant to the legal BT7-112 stack: Tamer Security effects do not transfer under the stack, while inherited effects do; Q1690 also requires Tamer-as-Digimon trigger behavior during the alternate evolution.

`data/kb/errata.json` contains no BT7-111 or BT7-112 entry. `data/kb/banlist.json` contains no restriction record for either card. No errata or restriction changes the direct implementation.

## Card-by-card evidence

### BT7-111 — Lucemon: Chaos Mode

**Catalog and clauses.** The committed catalog identifies Purple level 5, play cost 14, 12000 DP, Ultimate / Virus / Demon Lord / Seven Great Demon Lords. It has no printed ordinary evolution cost. Its text gives a `[Lucemon]` an alternate hand evolution for memory cost 7 while ignoring the card's ordinary digivolution requirements; reduces the hand play cost by 3 for every 10 cards in your trash; and has `[On Play][When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Tamers or level 6 or lower Digimon.`

**Direct and correction.** The direct module maps the play reduction to a Static `Replacement` on `wouldBePlayed`, with amount 3 per 10 cards in your trash. Both On Play and When Digivolving have one Delete action whose primary opponent-Tamer filter is OR'd with an opponent-Digimon filter at level ≤6, preserving the text's asymmetric boundary. The alternate hand evolution is cost 7 and source-zone hand; its source-name field was corrected from substring `names: ["Lucemon"]` to exact `namesExact: ["Lucemon"]` because the printed bracket-only reference is exact under Comprehensive §2-3-1-2. The shared override already carried the exact mode, but executable direct-module authority now agrees.

**Snapshot drift, tests, peers, and stack.** The generated snapshot retains a flattened `{ kind: ["Digimon", "Tamer"], levelComparison: ≤6 }` target that incorrectly applies the level bound to Tamers, and it retains `names: ["Lucemon"]` without `sourceZones: ["hand"]`; this drift was recorded and the snapshot was not edited. `BT7-111.test.ts` proves the module registration path, exact alternate requirement, and On Play Tamer deletion; the When Digivolving route and cost-scaling behavior were inspected through the direct module and shared interpreter paths but no test was run. BT2-111 and BT18-102 were inspected as same-mechanism alternate-requirement peers; BT18-102 demonstrates direct local requirement metadata, while BT2-111 documents the legacy shared-override seam. The legal stack is a `[Lucemon]` permanent in the battle area with BT7-111 in hand, paying 7 memory to place BT7-111 onto that Lucemon; the alternate source card must be in hand, and the delete trigger then operates at the appropriate On Play or When Digivolving timing.

**Boundaries.** The alternate base name is exact Lucemon, not a name-family substring. The play-cost reduction counts your trash only and scales in complete groups of 10; it does not create a trash-to-evolution path. The delete target is one opponent Tamer of any level or one opponent Digimon with level ≤6, not a level-bounded Tamer or an unrestricted Digimon. Q1679 supports the hand evolution timing, while Q4999/Q5002/Q5041 rule out inventing a trash-based or requirement-ignoring alternative.

**Score:** 2/2 clause coverage, 2/2 timing/frequency, 2/2 target/name boundaries, 2/2 cost/zone/stack behavior, 0/2 executed gates = **8/10 provisional**.

### BT7-112 — Susanoomon

**Catalog and clauses.** The committed catalog identifies White level 7, play cost 15, 15000 DP, Mega / Vaccine / Shaman, with every color's level-6 evolution cost listed as 7. Its first clause may digivolve this card from hand onto one of your Tamers as if that Tamer were a level 6 Digimon by placing 10 Tamer cards and/or cards with `[Hybrid]` in their traits from hand and/or trash at the bottom of your deck in any order. It grants Security Attack +2 and has `[When Digivolving] Delete 1 of your opponent's Digimon.`

**Direct and correction.** The direct module contains the Security Attack +2 keyword and one opponent-Digimon Delete action at When Digivolving. It previously omitted the first clause's executable requirement and therefore relied only on the shared `ALTERNATE_DIGIVOLUTION_OVERRIDES` fallback; this was corrected by adding a direct requirement with cost 7, `isAlternate: true`, `baseIsTamer: true`, `sourceZones: ["hand"]`, and a placement cost of exactly 10 from `hand`/`trash`, matching either kind Tamer or trait Hybrid. The explicit hand source applies to the evolving BT7-112 card; placement candidates intentionally come from both hand and trash. The direct requirement now makes the Tamer base, hand-only evolving card, OR matching of Tamer kind/Hybrid trait, exact count, and alternate memory cost executable in the card module itself.

**Snapshot drift, tests, peers, and stack.** The snapshot already contains the alternate requirement but omits `sourceZones: ["hand"]`; it also represents Security Attack +2 as a generated GainKeyword action, while the direct module uses the equivalent keyword declaration. `BT7-112.test.ts` now statically proves the direct requirement shape and existing tests inspect registration, When Digivolving-only timing, exact-one deletion, absence of the old replacement at None timing, and a 5-hand/5-trash Hybrid stack scenario. `BT7-112.digivolve.test.ts` inspects Tamer-base matching, rejection below 10 or with nonmatching cards, alternate cost 7, actual payment, stacking, and draw; `BT7-112.engine.test.ts` inspects player-selected ten-card identity and bottom-deck order. BT7-021, BT7-035, BT18-102, and BT2-111 were inspected as same-mechanism Tamer-base/alternate-requirement peers. The legal stack is a chosen Tamer base plus BT7-112 from hand: select exactly 10 qualifying cards from the union of hand and trash, reveal/order them as required, place them at deck bottom, pay 7, put BT7-112 on the Tamer as if it were level 6, draw for digivolution, and then resolve the When Digivolving delete. Q1682/Q1683 permit any qualifying mix (for example five Tamers and five Hybrids), Q1691 preserves player-selected order, and Q1692 confirms the draw.

**Boundaries.** The evolving card itself must be in hand; a BT7-112 in trash cannot use this alternate hand clause. The base is one of your Tamers, treated as level 6, and not an arbitrary Digimon for this requirement. The ten payment cards are exactly 10 and may be any mix of Tamer-kind cards and cards whose traits contain Hybrid, from hand and/or trash; nonmatching cards do not count, and nine cards are insufficient. The opponent target is exactly one Digimon, not a Tamer; the When Digivolving trigger does not fire when the card is merely trashed (Q2048). Under the resulting stack, the Tamer's inherited effects remain relevant while its Security effect does not transfer (Q6534/Q6535).

**Score:** 2/2 clause coverage, 2/2 timing/optionality, 2/2 target/source/trait boundaries, 2/2 cost/zone/stack behavior, 0/2 executed gates = **8/10 provisional**.
