## Scope and evidence

This audit covers BT24-071 through BT24-080 in ascending card order. The
catalog comparison used the immutable committed catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. For every card, the catalog
identity, colors, kind, level, play cost, DP, forms, attribute, types, and
printed evolution costs were checked. Each colocated source test now anchors
those identity fields through `getCardDefinition`.

The local rules KB was queried exactly once for each card. The results were
Q5647-Q5648 for BT24-071, no entries for BT24-072, Q5649-Q5651 for BT24-073,
Q5652 for BT24-074, no entries for BT24-075, Q5653 for BT24-076, Q5654 for
BT24-077, Q5655-Q5658/Q5775/Q6028 for BT24-078, Q5659-Q5660 for BT24-079,
and Q5661-Q5662 for BT24-080. The cited rulings are applied in the findings
below; the two cards without KB entries record that absence rather than
inventing a ruling.

Direct TypeScript modules, compiled IR definitions, matching and action
interpreters, source assertions, peer cards, evolution-stack behavior, the
BT24 index, and top-level registration were inspected. Every module in this
range has `coverage: "full"` and an empty `residual`; each executable module
registers exactly once through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`. No target
module has a duplicate `registerCard` registration.

The shared interpreter trace covered `registration/module.ts` and
`registeredCompiledCards.ts` (direct modules are the runtime authority),
`matching/definition.ts` and `matching/permanent.ts` (levels, names, traits,
source and stack filters), `actions/play.ts` (free play and dynamic budgets),
`actions/digivolution.ts` (free and paid evolution), `actions/link.ts` (link
eligibility and host/recipient separation), `actions/removal.ts` (deletion),
`actions/resources.ts` (deck trashing), `actions/costs.ts` (stack placement),
`actions/controlFlow.ts` (subtriggers), and `actions/meta.ts`
(`ReactivateEffect` re-firing the recorded trigger). The BT24 index imports
BT24-071 through BT24-080 consecutively at lines 72-81, and the top-level
cards index imports the BT24 index.

## Corrections and limitations

The additive source changes are catalog identity assertions in all ten
colocated tests. BT24-075 also received a semantic correction: its two
deletion clauses previously used numeric `level: 3` and `level: 4` fields.
The live matcher consumes typed `levels` (or an explicit `levelComparison`);
the remaining legacy `level` branch only handles a special object shape, not
these numeric values. Both triggers now use `levels: [3]` and `levels: [4]`,
and the structural source assertion proves those typed filters.

The task explicitly prohibited running focused, mechanism, collection, or
other tests; typecheck; lint; formatter; browser/UI checks; and
`git diff --check`. Consequently all scores below are provisional and the
Executed delivery gates component is fixed at 0/2. The source fixtures do
contain natural public play, evolution, attack, and turn-flow origins, but
the relevant coverage also relies on manually fired timing/subtrigger windows
or direct primitive origins for deletion, linking, stack, and reactivation
branches where a feasible natural origin exists. Under the audit rule those
fixtures do not earn Behavioral proof 2/2, so Behavioral proof is held to
1/2 for every card.

  and a linked host, preserving distinct card and stack origins.
* Score: **2 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 0 = 7/10**.
