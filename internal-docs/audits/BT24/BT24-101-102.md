## Card findings

### BT24-101 Jupitermon — 8/10 provisional

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** Yellow level-6 Mega/Vaccine Digimon, play cost 12,
  13000 DP, with Shaman/Olympos XII/Iliad/TS traits. Normal yellow level-5
  evolution costs 5; alternate level-5 TS costs 3; alternate level-5
  Aegiochusmon-in-name costs 1 per own security card. On Play/When Digivolving
  trashes the top own security, gives one opposing Digimon -13000 DP through
  the opponent’s turn end, then recovers 2 when at one or fewer security.
  Each All Turns effect is Once Per Turn: own security removal trashes the
  opponent’s top security, and all simultaneous own TS Digimon/Tamers leaving
  the battle area may be prevented by one top-security payment. Q5714-Q5718
  above resolve the zero-security, DP0 timing, security-trigger ordering, and
  batch-prevention edges.
- **IR trace — 2/2.** `BT24-101.ts:8-181` contains the hand-resident scaled
  Digivolve `CostModifier`, separate On Play and When Digivolving three-action
  sequences, the own-seat security-removal `SubTrigger`, and the
  `wouldLeavePlay` replacement with `affectsAll: true`, TS Digimon/Tamer
  source filter, optional aborting `Prevent`, and a top-security cost target.
  The alternate requirements are explicit at `:168-180`; exclusive
  `registerIrCard` is at `:184`. Runtime tracing covered
  `actions/resources.ts` for the zero-to-N security cost, `security.ts` for
  trash/recovery ordering and empty-security no-op behavior,
  `actions/subTrigger.ts` for seat-gated security removal and stable Once Per
  Turn keys, and `replacement.ts`/leave prevention for all-target simultaneous
  protection and payment.
- **Behavioral proof — 2/2.** Existing source assertions prove the scaled
  Aegiochusmon cost, zero-security DP/recovery, Once Per Turn security watcher,
  and simultaneous TS Digimon/Tamer protection. The additive source-proof
  commit `a11566365` adds natural public play and Digivolve paths through the
  complete entry sequences, a natural opponent attack that removes own
  security and triggers the opponent-security trash, and a natural opponent
  battle that exercises the TS leave replacement. The manual timing,
  subtrigger, and primitive cases remain supplemental controls rather than the
  basis for this score.
- **Peer and stack proof — 2/2.** BT24-014 Aegiochusmon supplies the legal
  level-5 named evolution stack. BT24-009 Shamanmon and BT24-088 Asuna provide
  positive TS Digimon/Tamer replacement peers; non-TS controls distinguish the
  source filter. BT24-040 Venusmon and BT24-051 Merukimon corroborate the
  Olympos XII/TS combined entry-timing conventions. The tests compare own and
  opponent security seats, use the Aegiochusmon stack, and exercise a mixed
  TS Digimon/Tamer simultaneous leave event.
- **Corrections/limitations.** No new production correction was justified in

### BT24-102 Homeros — 8/10 provisional

- **Catalog/rules — 2/2.** White Tamer, play cost 5, with Iliad/TS
  traits. Its Security effect plays it without paying cost. At the start of
  the controller’s Main phase it gains 1 memory, then, if memory is at least
  5, suspends itself and draws 1. All of the controller’s TS Digimon get
  +1000 DP. At the end of the controller’s turn, by suspending itself, it may
  activate one On Play or When Digivolving effect of one own Olympos XII
  Digimon. Q5719-Q5721, Q6029, Q6251, and Q6945 cover memory direction,
  mandatory draw after an unpaid suspension, disabled lender timing, lender
  Once Per Turn budgets, and borrowed Succession effects.

  threshold-gated actions, uses a source-bound `Aura` for all own TS Digimon,
  represents End-of-Turn borrowing with battle-area/Olympos XII filters,
  On Play/When Digivolving source timings, count 1, optionality, and a self
  suspend cost, and models Security as `PlayWithoutCost` from security with
  `payCost: false`. Exclusive registration is at `:57`. Runtime tracing covered
  `statics.ts` for source-bound Aura removal, `borrowed.ts` for lender timing
  disablement, Once Per Turn usage budgets, optional borrowed resolution, and
