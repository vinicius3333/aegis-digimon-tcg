## Scope and authority

The immutable catalog authority was the committed catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. Each card's identity, color, form,
level, play cost, DP, traits, effect text, inherited text, and evolution/link/
App Fusion recipes were compared with that blob. The local rules KB was queried
card by card with `node tools/kb/query.mjs card <CARD-ID>`; the applicable results
are recorded below.

The review also traced each direct TypeScript module, its compiled IR, the shared
compiled-IR runtime and filters, source-level behavior tests, legal peers, and
realistic evolution/link stacks. The read-only generated snapshot at
`packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was compared but deliberately not
edited. The direct card modules are the executable authority: every card in this
range has `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`, exactly one
`registerIrCard("BT24-<ID>", compiled)` call, and no duplicate `registerCard`
registration.

No tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI check, focused/mechanism/
collection gate, or `git diff --check` was run, as required by the dispatch. The
fifth score component is therefore `0/2` for every card, and every total below is
provisional with a maximum of `8/10`. Structural assertions alone and manually
fired timing or primitive-injected behavior where a natural game origin exists
do not receive behavioral `2/2`.

as comparison evidence only. The direct modules preserve the required behavior
where the snapshot omits it: BT24-051 adds the structured three-Digimon cost
condition and the mandatory attack after the optional buff; BT24-053 adds linked

hand-or-under-host destination branch; BT24-059 preserves the two alternate
level-4 trait-substring routes and suspended TS play; and BT24-060 represents
reveal-plus-free-digivolution as one decision and models Q5782's all-target
replacement. BT24-052's host filter was compared with Q5642 separately from its
payment filter: Q5642 broadens the host condition's “in its text” scope, while
the payment's printed “another [Diaboromon]” remains an exact-name selector
under CR/manual 2-3-1-2.

The final surviving additions from this dispatch are source-proof changes only:

- BT24-051 gained a natural `playCard` source assertion for the Q5641 buff,
  mandatory attack, reduced cost, and observable opponent state.
- BT24-052 retains `namesExact: ["Diaboromon"]` for its optional protection
  payment, and its source proof uses the valid exact-name `BT17-059` payment.
  Q5642 applies only to the host condition requiring `[Diaboromon]` in this
  Digimon's text; it does not broaden the separate payment selector.

Surviving semantic module corrections: **0**. The BT24-052 exact-name
restoration returns its module to the pre-dispatch baseline; the lasting
BT24-052 addition is the exact-filter source assertion, and BT24-051 is
proof-only. The interim semantic broadening and variant fixture are retained
only as superseded chronology below.

No generated snapshot, collection ledger, shared effects catalog, or generic
