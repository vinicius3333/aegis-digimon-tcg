## Scope and authority

This audit covers BT24-001 through BT24-010 in ascending card order. The immutable
catalog authority was the committed catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; printed identity, colors, forms, levels,
costs, traits, effect text, and evolution recipes were checked against that blob.

compiled-card runtime and interpreter primitives, source-level assertions, legal peers
and stack fixtures, the BT24 index, and the read-only snapshot in

## Card findings

### BT24-001 Gigimon

- Catalog/rules: Red level-2 Digi-Egg, Lesser/LIBERATOR. Its inherited Your Turn,
  once-per-turn effect may delete one opponent Digimon at 3000 DP or less when the
  opponent's security stack is removed.
- IR trace: The direct module uses an inherited Your Turn `whenSecurityRemoved`
  watcher, `sourceFilter.controller: "opponent"`, and
  `triggerRemovedSecuritySeat: "opponent"`; its optional opponent Digimon deletion
  uses the correct `dp <= 3000` boundary. The compiled definition is full and
  residual-free, and the runtime gate compares the removed security seat rather than
  blindly matching any security event.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover the opponent-seat gate, 3000/4000 DP
  boundary, once-per-turn limit, and optional decline. The trigger is manually fired,
  so the score is 1/2 pending a natural security-battle proof and execution.
- Peer and stack proof: The fixtures place Gigimon under a legal host and include
  matching, over-boundary, and non-triggering security-seat peers. Relevant correction
  history: `089ca42fd`, `e3fda0a16`, and `2e84a7e6f`.

### BT24-002 Bukamon

- Catalog/rules: Blue level-2 Digi-Egg, Lesser/Iliad/TS. Its inherited End of Your
  Turn once-per-turn effect pays 1 memory to unsuspend this blue TS Digimon.
- IR trace: The direct module binds the self target, requires Blue and TS, and uses an
  optional one-memory cost with `abortOnDecline`. Runtime turn-scope and source-self
  checks were traced; the compiled definition is full and residual-free.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover the legal blue TS host, blue non-TS and
  red TS peers, payment, unsuspend, once-per-turn behavior, and declined cost. End of
  turn is manually injected, so the score is 1/2 pending natural turn-boundary proof
  and execution.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures use a suspended host carrying Bukamon and contrasting
  color/trait peers. Relevant correction history: `fc95694fb` and `39ef4109d`.

### BT24-003 Tsunomon

- Catalog/rules: Yellow level-2 Digi-Egg, Lesser/Iliad/TS. Its inherited Your Turn,
  once-per-turn effect may evolve this Digimon into a Shaman from hand for 1 less when
  its security stack is removed.
- IR trace: The direct module uses a `whenSecurityRemoved` watcher gated to the
  controller's removed security, targets the self stack, selects a Shaman in hand,
  enables the reduced alternate evolution cost, and charges the reduced cost. The
  compiled definition is full and residual-free. Q5576/Q5585 timing implications were
  considered.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover own versus opposing security removal,
  reduced memory payment, stack replacement, and optional decline, but the security
  event is manually fired; score 1/2 pending a natural security-battle proof and
  execution.
- Peer and stack proof: The positive fixture uses a real inherited stack, a legal
  Shaman hand card, and a security-seat negative case. Relevant correction history:
  `a872868df`, `f5e703e46`, and `d2a2b52f9`.

  handling were traced; the compiled definition is full and residual-free.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover own Iliad, own non-Iliad, opposing Iliad,
  and once-per-turn outcomes, but all play events are manually supplied; score 1/2
  pending a natural public play path and execution.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures place Wanyamon under a host and compare friendly
  matching, friendly nonmatching, and opposing matching play peers. The prior source
  proof commit is `e7292112d`; the trigger proof history also includes `7b546d3d2`.

### BT24-005 Kyokyomon

- Catalog/rules: Black level-2 Digi-Egg, Lesser/X Antibody/DigiPolice/SEEKERS. Its
  inherited Your Turn, once-per-turn effect reacts when a Tamer is placed in its

- IR trace: The direct module uses `onAddDigivolutionCards` with a self trigger filter,

- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover exactly three cards, no hand addition,
  deck-card preservation, and non-Tamer/wrong-host negatives. Stack placement is
  manually injected, so score 1/2 pending a natural Tamer placement proof and
  execution.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures use Kyokyomon under a host, a Tamer and Digimon
  placement peer, and a different host. Relevant history: `6f462fd1d`, `bf9cce5b3`,
  `fe6a98782`, and `a2f3a0785`.

### BT24-007 Tsunomon

- Catalog/rules: Purple level-2 Digi-Egg, Lesser/Titan/TS. Its inherited Your Turn,
  once-per-turn effect may play a level-4-or-higher Demon or Titan from the triggering
  hand-trash action with play cost reduced by 2.
- IR trace: The direct module listens for the controller's hand-trash seat, filters to
  level 4+ Demon/Titan Digimon in trash, binds candidates to the triggering hand-trash
  IDs, pays the reduced printed play cost, and keeps the play optional. The compiled
  definition is full and residual-free; runtime `fromTriggerHandTrash` behavior was
  traced.
- Behavioral proof: The source proof reaches the positive and ineligible paths through
  the hand-trash primitive and checks the played card, unrelated trash, and memory
  reduction. Because it still injects the trash primitive rather than using a natural
  discard action, score 1/2 pending a fully natural source path and execution.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures use Tsunomon under a host, a qualifying level-4 Titan,
  an unrelated trash card, and an ineligible level-3 Demon. Relevant history:
  `bf5d9813d`, `bd335de87`, and `9b6de1d8e`.

### BT24-009 Shamanmon

- Catalog/rules: Red/Purple level-3 Digimon, Demon/Titan/TS, with a zero-cost Tsunomon
  or level-2 TS evolution route. Its On Play effect trashes a Demon, Shaman, or Titan
  from hand to draw two; its inherited Your Turn once-per-turn effect may evolve this
  Demon/Titan into Titamon or another Titan from trash for 1 less when the controller's
  hand is trashed.
- IR trace: The direct module uses the optional On Play trash cost and aborts Draw 2
  on decline. The inherited watcher defaults to the controller's hand-trash event,
  requires the self source to remain Demon/Titan, targets the self stack, selects a
  legal trash evolution, enables alternate evolution, and charges the reduced cost.
  The compiled definition is full and residual-free. Q5579's attack-declaration timing
  consequence was considered.
- Behavioral proof: Source assertions cover the On Play cost/decline and the inherited
  reduced evolution after a hand-trash primitive. Since both trigger origins are
  manually driven rather than reached through public natural play/discard actions,
  score 1/2 pending natural-path proof and execution.
- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures use Shamanmon under a Titan host, a P-209 Titamon in
  trash, a Demon cost card, and memory assertions for the reduced evolution. Relevant
  history: `dccda03c0`, `24007db08`, and `64c4a9653`.

### BT24-010 Greymon

- Catalog/rules: Red/Black level-4 Digimon, Dinosaur/Titan/TS, with Blocker, a

- IR trace: The direct module registers Blocker, uses an opponent Digimon target with

- Peer and stack proof: Fixtures include two opposing evolution stacks, verify exactly

  history: `45f373a9a`, `2826436d4`, and `4b763ee46`.
