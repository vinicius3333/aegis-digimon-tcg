## Scope and evidence

This audit covers BT24-021 through BT24-030 in ascending card order. The
catalog comparison used the immutable committed catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`; each card's identity, colors,
kind, level, play cost, DP, form, attribute, types, and printed evolution
costs were checked against that blob. Each colocated source test now anchors
those catalog fields through `getCardDefinition`.

The local rules KB was queried for every card. It has Q5602 for BT24-021,
Q5603-Q5605 for BT24-025, Q5606-Q5607 for BT24-026, Q5608 for BT24-028,
Q5609 for BT24-029, and Q5610 for BT24-030; BT24-022, BT24-023, BT24-024,
and BT24-027 have no KB entries. The cited rulings were applied where
present, and the absence of a KB entry is recorded rather than treated as an
unresolved rule.

Direct modules, the compiled IR, interpreter behavior, source assertions,
peer modules, and stack fixtures were inspected. Every card module has
`coverage: "full"`, an empty `residual`, and exactly one executable
registration through `registerIrCard(cardId, compiled)`; no card in this
range has a duplicate `registerCard` registration.

## Corrections and limitations

The existing IR implementations already contained the semantic corrections
needed by this pass, so no production card module or shared engine seam was
changed. The additive changes are catalog identity assertions in the ten
colocated tests, committed one card at a time below. Existing source-level
behavioral assertions remain in place for positive paths, boundaries,
negative paths, costs, optionality, durations, inherited effects, and stack
origins.

The task explicitly prohibited running focused, mechanism, collection, or
other tests; typecheck; lint; formatter; browser/UI checks; and
`git diff --check`. Consequently, all scores below are provisional, and the
Executed delivery gates component is fixed at 0/2. Behavioral proof is also
held to 1/2 for every card: the source tests assert observable state, but the
available fixtures rely on manually fired timing/subtrigger windows or direct
primitive origins in places where a natural game origin exists, which cannot
earn 2/2 under this audit's rule.
