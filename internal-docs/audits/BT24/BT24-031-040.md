## Scope and scoring

This is a provisional card-by-card audit of BT24-031 through BT24-040 in strict ascending
card-ID order. The immutable catalog source was the committed catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` (SHA-256
`d9c3892e26c3bee5f800557f6619eb413d39cdc1e7460fa70c291af5ac135ca8`). The generated IR
snapshot `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` was inspected read-only (SHA-256
`fb2f278d4a77b940be0bda05141b308a07a3787dfcd54abdc53d00340bf661cf`) and was not edited.
The rules knowledge base and shared interpreter/runtime source were also inspected read-only.

Each card is scored on exactly five two-point dimensions:

1. Catalog/rules — immutable catalog fidelity and applicable rules-KB interpretation.
2. IR trace — direct executable trigger/action/requirement trace and registration.
3. Behavioral proof — behavior-driving source proof from observable state changes. Structural
   assertions alone and manually injected timing or primitive events where a natural origin is
   available do not receive 2/2.
4. Peer and stack proof — comparable implementations, source-zone behavior, target binding,
   and legal stack/requirement coverage.
5. Executed delivery gates — tests and delivery gates executed for this dispatch. This is fixed
   at 0/2 because the task explicitly prohibited all test, typecheck, lint, formatter,
   browser/UI, focused/mechanism/collection-gate, and `git diff --check` commands.

Every total is therefore provisional and capped at 8/10. No collection-completion or completed-
worktree claim is made.

All ten direct modules under `apps/api/src/cards/BT24` have `coverage: "full"`, `residual: []`,
and exactly one `registerIrCard("BT24-<id>", compiled)` registration. No target module contains
`registerCard`. Direct TypeScript modules are the executable registration authority; the
generated snapshot remains comparison evidence only.
