## Scope and method

This audit covers BT24-091 through BT24-100 in ascending card order. Catalog
identity and printed clauses were checked against the immutable catalog blob
`efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a`. For every card, I inspected the
catalog entry, exactly one local rules-KB query, the direct TypeScript module,
compiled IR, relevant interpreter paths, colocated source assertions, nearby
peers, stack behavior, the BT24 index, and registration.

The five fixed two-point components are Catalog/rules, IR trace, Behavioral
proof, Peer and stack proof, and Executed delivery gates. The final component
is `0/2` for this static pass because the task prohibited tests, typecheck,
lint, formatter, browser/UI checks, focused/mechanism/collection gates, other
execution gates, and `git diff --check`. Structural assertions and manually
fired timing, subtrigger, or primitive events are not counted as `2/2`
Behavioral proof when a natural game origin is available. Every total below is
therefore provisional and capped at `8/10`.

The read-only files `packages/shared/src/effects/effects.json` and
`BT24-STATIC-AUDIT.md` were not modified. The historical effects snapshot is
less precise than several direct modules in this range; executable behavior is
defined by the direct IR and shared interpreter, not by editing that snapshot.
