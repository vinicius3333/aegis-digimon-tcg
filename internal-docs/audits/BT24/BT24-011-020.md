## Scope and method

This audit covers BT24-011 through BT24-020 in card-number order. The catalog source was checked against the immutable blob `efbecc002fb9000789123e2f91f201466e1e5b0a` (the current `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json` hashes to that blob). For each card, I inspected the direct TypeScript module, compiled IR shape, shared IR action types, the relevant interpreter paths, source-level card tests, nearby card implementations, registration, and the rules knowledge base.

The five scored components are exactly two points each:

1. Catalog/rules
2. IR trace
3. Behavioral proof
4. Peer and stack proof
5. Executed delivery gates

The requested delivery gates were intentionally not executed: no tests, typecheck, lint, formatter, browser/UI check, focused/mechanism/collection gate, or `git diff --check` was run. Therefore every card is provisional, every gate score is `0/2`, and no card can exceed `8/10` in this pass. Structural source assertions and manually fired primitive events were not counted as full behavioral proof when a natural game origin exists.

## Registration and runtime findings

BT24-011 through BT24-020 all register their target card behavior with `registerIrCard("BT24-<id>", compiled)`. No target module contains a `registerCard` registration. BT24-017 also has a separate `registerIrCard` registration for the explicitly required Petrification Token card; it is a distinct card ID, not a duplicate registration of BT24-017.

The runtime trace was checked through the shared IR action definitions and interpreter. In particular, `PlayWithoutCostAction.fromOwnDigivolutionStack` is required for Decode: generic `from: ["digivolutionCards"]` can scan every permanent stack, while the dedicated field anchors selection to the source permanent's own stack. The replacement, cost-gated block, security manipulation, sub-trigger, token, and lowest-DP paths used by this range were all traced to their interpreter implementations.
