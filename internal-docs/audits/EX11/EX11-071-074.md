### EX11-072 Unique Emblem: Guardian Vortex (Option, Green, play 3, [Vortex Warriors]/[LIBERATOR])

**Clauses.** (1) [Main] "You may play 1 [Pteromon], [Muchomon] or [Shoto Kazama] from your hand or
trash without paying the cost. Then, place this card in the battle area." (2) "[Your Turn] When any
of your [Shoto Kazama]s suspend, ＜Delay＞ ・1 of your Digimon with [Avian] or [Bird] in any of its
traits or the [Vortex Warriors] trait may digivolve into a Digimon card with the [Bird Dragon] and
[LIBERATOR] trait in the hand with the digivolution cost reduced by 3." (3) [Security] activate
this card's [Main] effects.

**Defect 1 — the ＜Delay＞ was free and repeatable (the big one).**

*Before:* a `YourTurn` SubTrigger granting `GainKeyword(Delay, permanent)` to itself, plus a
SEPARATE `trigger: "Main", keywords: [Delay]` effect whose `Digivolve` carried
`requiresDelayArmed: true`.

*Why it is wrong:* the interpreter has two ＜Delay＞ models.
`registration/module.ts` routes a Delay-keyworded effect on a continuous-window trigger
(`YourTurn` maps to `EffectTiming.None`) through `withIntrinsicDelayGate`, which stamps
`delayArmedIntrinsic` onto the SubTrigger. `subTrigger.ts` then enforces CR §16-17 in full: the
turn-guard (`enterFieldTurnCount === turnCount` ⇒ return, §16-17-3), a `canAttemptDigivolve`
legality pre-check, the optional ask (§16-17-2), and `deletePermanent([delaySource])` as the
activation cost (§16-17-1). But `withIntrinsicDelayGate` explicitly **skips** any action already
flagged `requiresDelayArmed` — that flag selects the other model (P-243/EX5-069: a *different*
clause grants ＜Delay＞). In that model `runAction.ts` only revokes the granted keyword; it never
trashes the source. So the emblem was never paid: it sat in the battle area and re-armed on every
Shoto Kazama suspension, digivolving for free once per activation window, forever. The turn-guard
never ran either. `BT17-097.ts` documents the mirror-image error (a `requiresDelayArmed` flag
waiting on a grant nothing made) and the fix there was the same: delete the flag.

*After:* one `trigger: "YourTurn", keywords: [{ keyword: "Delay" }]` effect whose only action is
the `whenSuspended` SubTrigger, with the `Digivolve` as its payload. The extra `[Main]` clause is
deleted. This also removes a second problem: `[Security] Activate this card's [Main] effects`
reached a second Main clause it must never see.

**Defect 2 — `match: "traitAll"` is not a match mode (Q5944).** `matchNameOrTrait` handles
`name`, `nameExact`, `trait`, `traitContains`, and then falls through to a final `return` that is
the `any` branch: name ∪ trait ∪ **effect text**, for *either* token. So
`{ tokens: ["Bird Dragon", "LIBERATOR"], match: "traitAll" }` accepted any Digimon card that merely
had "LIBERATOR" anywhere in its printed text — the opposite of Q5944, which requires **both**
traits. `definitionMatchesFilter` treats a multi-entry `nameOrTrait` array as a UNION (the
`orPrevious` flag agrees with, rather than switches, that default), so the conjunction cannot be
expressed inside one array. Fixed by pairing the array with the sibling `traits` predicate, which
ANDs with it:

```ts
into: {
  controllerDefault: "mine",
  kind: ["Digimon"],
  nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Bird Dragon"], match: "trait" }],
  traits: ["LIBERATOR"],
}
```

The base-side filter keeps its two `nameOrTrait` entries, because there the union is what the card
prints ("[Avian] or [Bird] in any of its traits **or** the [Vortex Warriors] trait"), with
`traitContains` for the substring half.

**Defect 3.** `match: "name"` → `nameExact` on the [Pteromon]/[Muchomon]/[Shoto Kazama] play list,
for the same reason as EX11-071.

**Type errors resolved.** Line 94, `match: "traitAll"`. It was not a typing chore: the value was a
live semantic defect that the type system had been correctly rejecting.

**Tests rewritten.** The old suite asserted `match: "traitAll"` as if it were correct and drove the
Delay through an `activateEffect` intent, which is the wrong window. New suite:
- one-[Main]-clause + Delay-on-YourTurn IR assertion, plus `requiresDelayArmed` absent anywhere;
- Q5944 IR assertion on the `into` conjunction and the base union;
- Security → ActivateMain plays EX11-026 and places the emblem;
- positive Delay: emblem aged, Shoto suspends → emblem in **trash**, EX11-028 becomes EX11-032,
  memory unchanged at 2 (printed cost 3, reduced by 3);
- [Vortex Warriors]-only base: EX8-074 (Warrior/Witchelny/Vortex Warriors, no Avian/Bird)
  digivolves into EX11-074;
- Q5944 negative: EX11-033 (green Lv.5, [LIBERATOR], no [Bird Dragon]) is a legal evolution off
  EX11-028 but must be refused, and the emblem must NOT be trashed;
- base negative: EX11-029 (green Lv.4, [Beast]/[LIBERATOR]) could legally reach EX11-032 but is
  refused by the printed trait gate;
- §16-17-3 turn-guard: emblem placed this turn does nothing;
- wrong subject: suspending the Galemon (a Digimon, not a [Shoto Kazama]) does nothing;
- wrong controller: suspending the **opponent's** [Shoto Kazama] does nothing.

**Fixture correction (found by collection run).** The first version of the
"wrong subject" case kept EX11-062 Shoto Kazama on the board and suspended the Galemon. It passed
in isolation and failed in the collection, where EX11-062's module is registered. That is not a
defect in EX11-072: EX11-062 prints "[All Turns] When any Digimon suspend, **by suspending this
Tamer**, ...", so suspending any of your Digimon really does suspend a [Shoto Kazama] and really
does arm this emblem. The fixture, not the card, was wrong — a genuine peer interaction the
isolated run could not see. The case now drops EX11-062 from the board (documented in the
`armedBoard` helper), and a second negative was added that keeps a [Shoto Kazama] in play but on
the OPPONENT's side, which pins `sourceFilter.controller: "mine"` without touching EX11-062's own
clause. This is also the batch's one confirmed cross-card interaction: EX11-062 + EX11-072 is a
real two-card engine, and it now has a passing assertion.

**Mutation check.** `traitAll` → EX11-033 negative test (under the old value it passed the
`LIBERATOR` text branch and digivolved). Base union → EX11-029 negative and EX8-074 positive.
`traitContains` vs `trait` → EX11-028 is [Bird Dragon], which only a substring test reaches.
Intrinsic Delay model → the positive test's "emblem is in trash / not in battle area" assertions
and the turn-guard test; both fail under the old `requiresDelayArmed` encoding. `reduceCost: 3` →
`memory === 2`. `payCost: true` → same assertion (a free digivolve would also leave memory at 2, so
this one is pinned by the IR assertion plus the EX8-074 case, where the residual cost is 1).
SubTrigger `sourceFilter` name → the wrong-subject test; its `controller: "mine"` → the
opponent's-Shoto test.

**Open ambiguity.** None. §16-17 is explicit that the trigger clause states *when* the ＜Delay＞
may be activated and that trashing the card is the cost.

### EX11-073 ExMaquinamon (Digimon, Green/Black, Lv.7, 15/15000, [Unique]/[LIBERATOR])

**Clauses.** ＜Security A. +1＞, ＜Blocker＞, ＜Link +2＞; [When Digivolving] if DNA digivolving,
you may link up to 3 [Maquinamon] from hand, trash, or this Digimon's digivolution cards to this
Digimon without paying the cost; [End of Opponent's Turn] [Once Per Turn] for each of this
Digimon's link cards, trash your opponent's top security card and return 1 of their Digimon to the
bottom of the deck.

**IR trace.**
- Three empty-action Static effects carrying the keywords. `runEffect`'s
  `actions.length === 0 && keywords.length > 0` branch converts each into a self-`GainKeyword` with
  `duration: "permanent"`; `board.ts` routes `Link`/`LinkMax` to `ctx.fx.grantLinkMax(id, 2)`, and
  `mindLink.ts`'s `linkMax` returns `BASE_LINK_MAX + delta`. ＜Link +2＞ is therefore executable and
  the persisted record's `RawUnparsed`/`coverage: "partial"` is stale.
- `Link { target: { nameOrTrait: [{ tokens: ["Maquinamon"], match: "nameExact" }], count: 3,
  upTo: true, source: "thisDigimon" }, from: ["hand", "trash", "digivolutionCards"],
  payCost: false, optional: true, condition: { kind: "isDnaDigivolving" } }`. `source:
  "thisDigimon"` is the ZoneRef pseudo-selector "hosted candidates must belong to the resolving
  Digimon", which is what scopes `digivolutionCards` to this card's own stack.
  `nameExact` is load-bearing here: **"ExMaquinamon" contains "Maquinamon"**, so a substring match
  would let the card link copies of itself.
- Two `RepeatPerCount { countScaling: selfLinkScale }` groups. `board.ts` prefers `countScaling`
  over `countSource`, and `scaleFactor`'s `linkCards` unit calls `countLinkCards`, which sums
  `permanent.linked.length` over filter matches; `isSelfRef: true` is honored by
  `permanentMatchesFilter`, so the count is this Digimon's own link cards. Two separate groups is
  exactly Q5947's ordering: every security trash first, then every deck-bottom return.

**Defects corrected.** None in behaviour — the module was already right and the persisted record
was the wrong side. Comments were added recording the Q5947 ordering rationale and why
`countSource` is inert.

**Type errors resolved.** Lines 55 and 61. The rejected field was not `isSelfRef` (which is a real
`BoardPredicates` member) — it was the *unannotated const*: `unit: "linkCards"` widened to `string`
and `kind: ["Digimon"]` to `string[]`, so `selfLinkScale` stopped satisfying `Scaling`. Annotating
`const selfLinkScale: Scaling` fixes both call sites, and `scaleFactor` does read `filter`
(`countLinkCards(ctx, filter)`), so the field is not decorative.

**Tests added.**
- ExMaquinamon-excluded-from-its-own-[Maquinamon]-pool (the `nameExact` mutation check);
- non-DNA digivolution links nothing (the `isDnaDigivolving` gate);
- zero link cards at [End of Opponent's Turn] does nothing (the scaling's `0` boundary).

**Mutation check.** ＜Link +2＞ amount → existing `linkMaxDelta === 2` assertion. `nameExact` → the
new self-copy test. `source: "thisDigimon"` → the existing test asserting the other host's stack
copy is untouched. `count: 3, upTo: true` → the existing "exactly three links" assertion.
`isDnaDigivolving` → the new non-DNA test. `linkCards` scaling and Q5947 ordering → the existing
two-link test (security −2, battle area −2, deck +2) plus its event-index ordering assertion.
Zero-count boundary → the new empty-link test.

**Open ambiguity.** Q5946 (＜Mind Link＞ cards are not link cards) is a property of the engine's
`linked` array rather than of this card's IR; it is asserted indirectly by the existing DNA test
and not re-proved here.

＜Piercing＞ ＜Vortex＞ ＜Blocker＞. [When Digivolving] [When Attacking] you may suspend 1 Digimon;

don't affect this Digimon and it gets +6000 DP. [All Turns] [Once Per Turn] when any Digimon
suspend, this Digimon may unsuspend; then this Digimon may battle 1 of your opponent's Digimon.

  the substring `names` field would make "Shoto Kazama" a legal base.
- The shared `suspendAndProtect` array is reused for both [When Digivolving] and [When Attacking],
  which is exactly what the printed double-trigger header means.
  `Restrict { restriction: "beAffected", fromSourceKind: ["Digimon"], byOpponentEffectsOnly: true }`
  is the precise encoding of "their Digimon's effects don't affect this Digimon" (Q5949–Q5954), and
  `condition: { kind: "lastSuspendedIsMine" }` gates both the immunity and the +6000 on the
  printed "if this effect suspended **your** Digimon".
- The [All Turns] clause is one `SubTrigger { event: "whenSuspended", sourceFilter: { controller:
  "any", kind: ["Digimon"] } }` (Q5948: "when **any** Digimon suspend") whose payload is
  `Unsuspend(self)` then `Battle { attacker: self, defender: opponentDigimon }`. `Battle` is a
  direct DP comparison (Q5955/Q5956) rather than `Attack`, so no security check happens
  (Q5957–Q5959).

**Defects corrected.** None in behaviour; the module was already the correct side of the drift.

**Type errors resolved.** Lines 39, 40 and 51. The module hoisted four object literals into
un-annotated consts, so `kind` widened to `string` and `controller` to `string`, and the arrays
widened to `{...}[]` instead of `Action[]`. Annotated as `Target`, `Condition` and `Action[]`. No
field was silently ignored — every field on the widened literals (`fromSourceKind`,
`byOpponentEffectsOnly`, `duration`, `condition`, `attacker`, `defender`) is a real member of the
corresponding IR interface, which I confirmed against `restrictions.ts`, `combat.ts` and
`base.ts`. Line 50's `Unsuspend` did not error only because every field of `self` happens to widen
to a type still assignable to `Target`.

**Tests added.** Three evolution-stack cases the suite was missing:
- alternate route accepted: EX11-032 GrandGalemon base + EX11-062 Shoto Kazama in play, memory
  8 → 2, `stack === ["EX11-032"]`;
- alternate route refused without the Tamer (`{ ok: false, reason: "invalid-evolution" }`);
- alternate route refused from EX11-033 (same colour and level, [LIBERATOR], Tamer present), which
  isolates `namesExact` as the only thing rejecting it.

## 6. Seam requests

**1. `CardPredicates.nameOrTrait`'s doc comment contradicts the interpreter.**
`packages/shared/src/effects/ir/filters/cardPredicates.ts` says "Entries AND by default. One entry carrying
`orPrevious` switches the WHOLE array to OR". `definitionMatchesFilter` does the opposite — it is
`filter.nameOrTrait.some(...)` unconditionally, and its own comment says so. That contradiction is
what produced EX11-072's `traitAll`: an author who believed the doc had no reason to look for
another way to express a conjunction. Proposed minimal diff (documentation only, no behaviour
change):

```diff
-   * Entries AND by default. One entry carrying `orPrevious` switches the WHOLE array to OR
-   * ("[Data] or [Witchelny] trait" — BT19-029, BT19-055, BT21-054, BT21-080).
+   * Entries are a UNION (OR): a card qualifies if it matches ANY entry. `orPrevious` is a
+   * redundant explicit marker of that intent, not a switch. To require SEVERAL traits at once,
+   * pair this with the sibling `traits` / `traitContains` predicates, which AND with it
+   * (EX11-072, KB Q5944).
```

The failing test this needs is `EX11-072.test.ts`'s "rejects a destination carrying only
[LIBERATOR]" case, which already exercises the union semantics through a real board.
