## Exact-name boundary correction

The interpreter's `match: "name"` is substring matching, while
`match: "nameExact"` compares normalized card names exactly. The bracketed
identity references in this range now use `nameExact` for the linked Maquinamon
gate on EX11-006 (the catalog contains EX11-073 ExMaquinamon), Ryutaro Williams
on EX11-009, Suzune Kazuki on EX11-014/015/017, Shoemon and Kyaromon on
EX11-020, and Mirai Kinosaki on EX11-021. The Koromon, Hiyarimon, and Kyaromon
alternate routes use `namesExact` in EX11-007/014/020. Tyrannomon remains
substring matching in EX11-001/007/011 because those texts explicitly say
“in its name.” EX11-006's focused test `does not fire when the only link card
is ExMaquinamon (exact-name boundary)` proves the previously over-broad case;
the affected focused suites pass after updating their structural assertions.
