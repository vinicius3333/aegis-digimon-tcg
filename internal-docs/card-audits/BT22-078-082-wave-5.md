## BT22-082 — Eater Adam — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** White cost-8/10000 DP CS Eater, limit 4: evolve Arata cost 4; Security A. +1; On Play/When Digivolving delete opposing cost ≤7, then if source-less may bottom hand/trash Arata; leave-play may free-play an Arata source.
2. **KB.** Q4949 and Q6698–Q6702 establish Tamer-evolution summoning sickness, Tamer-not-Digimon evolution triggers, evolution draw, Tamer source status, no Security inheritance, and inherited-effect inheritance.
3. **Clauses.** Keyword, paired Delete/PlaceUnder timings, self-bound leave replacement, and alternate requirement map all text.
4. **Requirements.** Delete boundary is inclusive 7; exact Arata source and alternate evolution name; placement only with no current sources.
5. **Costs/failures.** Placement and leave-play are optional/free; normal alternate requirement remains cost 4 and cannot attack if originating Tamer entered this turn.
6. **Controllers.** Adam's owner chooses deletion, Arata placement, and replacement play; opponent owns deletion candidates.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Opponent battle→trash, Arata hand/trash→bottom source, and source→battle on leave are explicit.
8. **Timing/OPT.** On Play and When Digivolving are distinct; leave replacement is all turns; Tamer evolution follows all six rulings.
9. **UI.** Generic target/source choices and inherited display apply.
10. **Gates.** Public play proof observes deletion and Arata under the same physical Adam; focused and wave gates pass.
