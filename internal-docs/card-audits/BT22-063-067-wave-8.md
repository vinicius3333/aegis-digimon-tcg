Scope: BT22-067 through BT22-063, audited independently in descending order on 2026-08-20. Each numbered item is a mandatory evidence point.

## BT22-067 — LordKnightmon — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** Black/red level 6, cost 12/12000, Holy Warrior/Royal Knight/CS; level-5 CS cost 3 and conditional Rie Kishibe cost 5; Raid, Reboot, paired entry/evolution buff-attack, and player-attack reveal.
2. **KB.** Q4925 preserves summoning sickness after Tamer evolution. Q4926/Q4927 allow Raid and the all-turns player-attack trigger in either order. Q6688–Q6692 define Tamer evolution, bonus draw, source status, and inherited/security-text handling.
3. **Clauses.** Hand-written keyword statics, paired buff/forced-attack effects, player-attack watcher, reveal/play/trash resolution, and shared alternate requirements map all clauses.
4. **Requirements.** CS path needs level 5. Rie path requires the named base and at most three security. Reveal candidate is black or red, permanent-playable, and cost 4 or lower.
5. **Costs/failures.** Rie costs 5; CS costs 3. DP choice is mandatory, attack and reveal play are optional, and all unplayed reveals are trashed.
6. **Controllers.** Owner selects buff target, optional attacker, and optional reveal card.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Buff precedes attack; top three are revealed, selected card enters battle free, and every remainder moves deck→trash.
8. **Timing/OPT.** Entry/evolution clauses are separate. Player-directed attacks on either turn trigger the reveal once per turn; Raid target changes remain compatible.
9. **UI.** Generic target, attack, reveal, and cost-label paths apply; the shared requirement gate keeps server/client availability aligned.
10. **Gates.** Public play proves buff→attack→reveal→free play→trash; shared assertion proves the security gate; focused test passes.
