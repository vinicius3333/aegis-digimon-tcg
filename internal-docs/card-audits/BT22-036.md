# BT22 implementation audit — BT22-036

## BT22-036 — Chaperomon — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** Yellow level 5, play cost 7, 7000 DP, Virus/Puppet/LIBERATOR; its hand Main effect places a ShoeShoemon from trash under a Shoemon and effect-digivolves Chaperomon for cost 3 while ignoring requirements. Overclock is Puppet-qualified; the inherited clause is All Turns OPT leave-play prevention.
2. **KB and Q&A.** The exact query returned Q4882 and Q4883. Q4882 confirms the cost becomes 2 when combined with EX7-024's reduction; Q4883 confirms this hand Main effect cannot be activated simultaneously with another effect-driven digivolution such as P-105. No additional local ruling was found.
3. **Clauses.** The compiled card maps the Arisa condition, bottom trash placement, host Shoemon filter, fixed paid digivolution, Puppet Overclock attack, and inherited leave replacement.
4. **Requirements and identity.** Arisa Kinosaki is controller-scoped; the host must be a Shoemon and the material must be a ShoeShoemon from that controller's trash. The destination is this hand instance and ignores printed evolution requirements.
5. **Costs and failures.** The effect pays exactly 3 memory; missing Arisa, host, or ShoeShoemon prevents activation. Q4882's external reduction seam remains available to reduce the fixed cost to 2.
6. **Targets and controllers.** The payment deletes one of the controller's other Puppet Digimon or Token; the attack targets a player without suspending. The leave replacement excludes the controller's own effects.
7. **Zones, order, and face state.** ShoeShoemon leaves trash and is placed as the host's bottom evolution card before Chaperomon enters on top; the hand card becomes face-up in the evolution stack.
8. **Timing and OPT.** The hand effect is Main-only, Overclock is end of the owner's turn, and inherited prevention is All Turns once per turn. The cost/placement path is atomic through the shared DigivolveViaPlacement action.
9. **Engine seams and peers.** The DigivolveViaPlacement interpreter was reviewed for placement order, host filtering, fixed cost, requirement bypass, and effect timing. Peer EX10-032 uses the same placement seam; EX9-032 covers the same inherited delete-own-Puppet replacement shape.
10. **Public proof.** `BT22-036.test.ts` now exercises real hand activation, exact memory payment, trash-to-bottom-stack transition, final top identity, and the missing-Arisa failure path, alongside structural proof for Overclock and inherited OPT. Focused Vitest was attempted with the mandated fork/worker settings but remains blocked by unavailable registry dependencies.

**Implementation result:** no card-code change was required; public behavioral coverage was added and the existing compiled implementation was independently mapped to the catalog and Q&A.
