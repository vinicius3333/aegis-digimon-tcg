# BT22 implementation audit ledger — wave 2

Scope: BT22-097 through BT22-093, audited individually in descending order on 2026-08-20. Each numbered entry is one mandatory rubric point; a score is awarded only when catalog text, exact KB evidence, executable behavior, and focused proof agree.

## BT22-097 — Music of the Heart — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** Yellow Option, cost 2, `Appmon`, limit 4: Appmon field color waiver; Main draws 1 then places self; when an owned Appmon Digimon is played, Delay may link an Appmon Digimon from hand free; Security places self.
2. **KB.** Q4967 defines “on the field” as battle or breeding area. Q4968 says the Delay cannot link a card without Link. These map to both field zones and `hasLinkRequirement: true`.
3. **Clause/code map.** Static waiver, ordered Main `Draw`/`PlaceInBattleAreaSelf`, `whenPlayed` SubTrigger containing the Link action, and Security placement cover every clause.
4. **Requirements/traits.** The field waiver accepts Appmon Digimon or Tamers; the event source and linked card are Appmon Digimon; the recipient is an owned Digimon. No evolution text applies.
5. **Costs/failures.** Link is optional and free, but still requires a printed Link requirement; Delay consumes the battle-area Option and cannot be used on its entry turn.
6. **Controllers.** The owner chooses activation, the eligible Link card, and recipient; opponent cards never satisfy owned filters.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Main draws deck top before moving the same used Option to battle; Link moves hand to a recipient stack through the engine Link path; Security places the checked card.
8. **Timing/OPT/interactions.** Audit fixed the prior free-standing Main Delay action: it is now nested under the Appmon-play event and cannot activate without that event. Delay supplies its standard one-shot trash cost.
9. **UI.** Generic optional/card/recipient decisions apply; there is no card-specific presentation behavior.
10. **Gates.** Focused structural and public play-intent proof covers draw and physical-card placement; exact five-card suite, typecheck, formatting/lint, and diff gates are recorded in the wave commit.

## BT22-096 — Unique Emblem: Poseidia Lagoon — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** Blue Option, cost 3, `LIBERATOR`, limit 4: optionally play Sangomon/Yao from hand or trash free, then place self; owned Yao suspension enables Delay evolution of an Aqua/Sea Animal into an Aquatic+LIBERATOR hand Digimon at cost -3; Security activates Main.
2. **KB.** Q4966 explicitly requires both Aquatic and LIBERATOR, mapping to the conjunctive `and` destination filter.
3. **Clause/code map.** Main optional play plus mandatory placement, Your Turn `whenSuspended` SubTrigger with intrinsic Delay and nested Digivolve, and Security `ActivateMain` cover all text.
4. **Requirements/traits.** Source accepts either exact name. Evolution target matches Aqua or Sea Animal in any trait; destination must be a Digimon with both required traits and must satisfy normal evolution legality.
5. **Costs/failures.** Play is free and optional; failure/refusal does not suppress “Then” placement. Delay is paid by trashing self; reduction is 3, never an evolution-legality waiver.
6. **Controllers.** Owner selects source card, target stack, and destination/refusal. Trigger is any owned Yao suspension.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Candidate moves hand/trash to battle, then the physical Option enters battle; evolution moves hand to stack; Security runs the same ordered Main actions.
8. **Timing/OPT/interactions.** The useful pre-existing fix correctly nests digivolution under Yao suspension; no free-standing Delay action remains. Mixed Aquatic-only/LIBERATOR-only cards fail Q4966.
9. **UI.** All decisions use generic server choices; no unique UI state is required.
10. **Gates.** Focused IR boundaries and public play-intent proof cover Sangomon plus same-Option placement; wave gates are recorded in the commit.

## BT22-095 — Akemi Suedou — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** White cost-4 CS Tamer, limit 4: Security free play; owned Eater play may suspend self to gain 1, then draw at hand size 7 or less; Main puts self under Mother Eater; inherited Mother Eater gains Rush, Alliance, and Scapegoat.
2. **KB.** Q4965 says the post-“then” draw cannot occur unless the “by suspending” condition is met, matching the SubTrigger cost that gates both actions.
3. **Clause/code map.** Security free self-play, Your Turn Eater `whenPlayed`, Main bottom `PlaceUnder`, and inherited conditional keyword auras map every printed clause.
4. **Requirements/traits.** Trigger requires an owned Digimon with Eater; Main host is owned Mother Eater; inherited condition tests the host's exact name. No evolution requirement is printed.
5. **Costs/failures.** Suspension is an optional activation cost; an already suspended Tamer cannot pay it. Draw checks hand count after memory gain and is inclusive at 7.
6. **Controllers.** Owner chooses whether to pay and which Mother Eater receives Akemi; opponent has no choices.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Main moves this Tamer from battle to the bottom of the selected evolution stack; Security moves the checked physical card to battle without memory payment.
8. **Timing/OPT/interactions.** Trigger is Your Turn and per owned Eater play, with no printed OPT. Inherited keywords exist only while the resulting host is Mother Eater.
9. **UI.** Generic optional/host selection and inherited keyword presentation apply; no special UI path.
10. **Gates.** Focused mappings plus observable Security zone and unchanged-memory proof cover the contract; wave gates are recorded in the commit.

## BT22-094 — Yuugo Kamishiro — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** White cost-3 CS Tamer, limit 4: On Play reveal 3/add one CS/rest bottom; Your Turn may bottom self to reduce an owned CS Digimon/Tamer play by 2; Security free play.
2. **KB.** Exact card query returns no entries; no extra ruling or erratum modifies the printed clauses.
3. **Clause/code map.** `RevealAdd`, a `wouldBePlayed` replacement filtered to owned CS Digimon/Tamers, return-to-bottom cost and -2 mode, and Security self-play cover all clauses.
4. **Requirements/traits.** Reveal may add any CS card kind; replacement is intentionally narrower to Digimon or Tamer. No evolution requirements.
5. **Costs/failures.** Returning this exact Tamer is the optional cost and declining aborts reduction; reduction bottoms at engine-valid play cost. Reveal handles fewer than three and no-hit cases.
6. **Controllers.** Owner selects one CS reveal hit and whether to use Yuugo for an owned qualifying play; opponent has no selection.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Reveal goes deck top to one hand/rest deck bottom; replacement moves Yuugo battle to deck bottom before the discounted play; Security moves checked self to battle.
8. **Timing/OPT/interactions.** Replacement is Your Turn and applies at the would-play cost window, not after play. It matches CS exactly and excludes Options.
9. **UI.** Reveal and optional replacement use generic decisions; no card-specific UI behavior.
10. **Gates.** Public play-intent mixed-deck proof observes the CS hit in hand and both misses excluded; structural, focused, and wave gates are recorded in the commit.

## BT22-093 — Ami Aiba — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** White cost-4 CS Tamer, limit 4: start-main gains 1 if opponent has a Digimon; Your Turn owned Digimon evolution into CS with a same-level source may suspend Ami and evolve it into hand CS free; Security free play.
2. **KB.** Q4964 says each Ami rechecks the evolved Digimon's current level against its sources; after the first chain evolution, a second copy cannot trigger without a source matching the new top level.
3. **Clause/code map.** Hand-written start-main timing, evolution-enter timing with subject rebinding/recheck/free evolution, and Security skill cover every clause.
4. **Requirements/traits.** Subject is owned CS Digimon and must contain a source whose level equals the current top level; destination is a hand CS Digimon that passes the shared evolution path.
5. **Costs/failures.** Unsuspended battle-area Ami must pay suspension. Selection is optional; missing same-level source or candidate produces no evolution and no illicit result.
6. **Controllers.** Ami's owner pays and chooses the CS destination for the triggering owned stack; the opponent has no choice.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Destination moves hand to the same subject stack for free; Security uses the checked physical Ami and places it in battle.
8. **Timing/OPT/interactions.** Trigger and resolution both recheck the Q4964 state, preventing stale chained triggers. Start-main checks opposing battle-area Digimon and is independent.
9. **UI.** Standard optional card selection exposes the only choice; no custom UI behavior applies.
10. **Gates.** Behavioral tests cover positive/negative start-main, positive/negative same-level chain, paid suspension, hand-to-stack movement, and Security physical-card play; wave gates are recorded in the commit.
