## BT22-072 — Lekismon — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** Purple/blue level 4, cost 5/6000, Wizard/Night Claw/CS; standard purple/blue level-3 cost 3 and alternate Night Claw/Light Fang/CS cost 2; evolution Tamer play and inherited deletion prevention.
2. **KB.** Q4930 defines the condition as any two stacked cards sharing a level. Q4931 confirms EX5-064 Koh & Sayo does not block BT22-102 Sayo because their names differ.
3. **Clauses.** Conditional optional free Tamer play, exact-name uniqueness check, alternate evolution, and inherited replacement map every printed clause.
4. **Requirements.** The stack needs any same-level pair; the Tamer must have Night Claw or Light Fang and a name absent from all of the owner's Tamers. The prevention host must have Night Claw, Light Fang, or Galaxy.
5. **Costs/failures.** Tamer play is free and optional. Prevention requires trashing exactly two same-level sources; refusal or no pair allows deletion.
6. **Controllers.** Lekismon's owner chooses the Tamer and the prevention pair.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Tamer moves hand→battle; selected sources move stack→trash while the host stays in battle. Bounce is not deletion and is not replaced.
8. **Timing/OPT.** Tamer play is When Digivolving. Prevention applies on either turn to battle/effect deletion and is once per turn.
9. **UI.** Generic optional card/source selection applies; no card-specific UI is required.
10. **Gates.** Public evolution proves Sayo play with the same-level pair; effect deletion, battle deletion, bounce, and second-deletion tests prove replacement boundaries; focused test passes.
