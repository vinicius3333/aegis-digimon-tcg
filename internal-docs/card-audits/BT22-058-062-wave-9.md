## BT22-062 — MetalTyrannomon (X Antibody) — 10/10

1. **Catalog.** Black/green level 5, cost 8/8000, Cyborg/X Antibody/Dinosaur; alternate non-X level-5 Tyrannomon cost 1; Collision, evolution DP/lock, and inherited forced opponent attack.
2. **KB.** Q4916 makes opponent selection optional. Q4917 requires the chosen Digimon to be able to attack. Q4918 bars a nested attack. Q4919 says the choice affects the player, so effect immunity does not prevent selection/attack.
3. **Clauses.** Collision, self ModifyDP, stack-gated evolution Restrict, alternate requirement, and inherited optional opponent-chosen Attack map every clause.
4. **Requirements.** Alternate base is level 5, Tyrannomon-named, and lacks X Antibody. Lock requires MetalTyrannomon by name or X Antibody by trait in the stack.
5. **Costs/failures.** Alternate evolution costs 1. Attack may be declined and no-ops for an unable attacker or an already-open attack.
6. **Controllers.** Owner selects the evolution-lock target; opponent chooses whether and which own Digimon attacks at end of turn.
7. **Zones/order/face.** Evolution card moves hand→stack; no other zone move. DP gain precedes the conditional restriction.
8. **Timing/OPT.** DP/lock is When Digivolving through opponent turn end. Inherited end-opponent-turn attack is once per turn and drains its timing window during combat.
9. **UI.** Generic alternate-cost, restriction-target, and opponent attack-decision paths apply.
10. **Gates.** Public cost-1 evolution proves 12000 DP and opponent evolution lock; inherited ruling boundaries are structurally locked; focused test passes.
