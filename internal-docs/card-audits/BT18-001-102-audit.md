## Per-card rubric status

Every card was checked against its committed catalog entry, its direct module, and its runtime
representation where applicable. The local KB has no card-specific entry for most BT18 cards;
where Q&A exists, it was used as a constraint rather than treated as printed text. No card is
awarded 10/10: runtime, Vitest, and typecheck were unavailable in this workspace, so the final
behavioral/runtime verification field is **not verified** for BT18-001..BT18-102.

The ten rubric dimensions were recorded for each card as: catalog fields, KB/rulings, direct
module, runtime IR, clause mapping, timing, cost, target/filter, zone/face/order, and OPT/
duration. Static evidence is present in the source paths above; the runtime-dependent score is
not verified rather than inferred.
